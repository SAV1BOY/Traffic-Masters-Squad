# Budget Allocation and Distribution Quality Gate

> Quality gate for media budget allocation and distribution across platforms and campaigns. Must pass before budgets are committed in ad platforms.

## Section 1: Budget Planning Foundation
- [ ] Total media budget is confirmed and signed off by the client or finance team
- [ ] Budget period is clearly defined (monthly, quarterly, or campaign flight)
- [ ] Budget currency matches the billing currency of each ad platform account
- [ ] Historical performance data has been analyzed to inform allocation decisions (CPA, ROAS, CAC by platform)
- [ ] Business objectives and KPI targets are documented and tied to budget amounts

## Section 2: Platform Allocation Strategy
- [ ] Budget is allocated across platforms based on historical performance data and strategic objectives
- [ ] Allocation rationale is documented for each platform (e.g., "60% Meta due to proven ROAS of 4.2x, 25% Google for search intent capture, 15% TikTok for awareness testing")
- [ ] New/unproven platforms receive no more than 10-15% of total budget for testing
- [ ] Platform allocation accounts for minimum viable spend thresholds (e.g., LinkedIn requires higher CPMs, TikTok needs $50+/day per ad group)
- [ ] Budget flexibility is built in: 10-15% reserve for scaling winners or mid-flight reallocation

## Section 3: Campaign-Level Distribution
- [ ] Budget is distributed across funnel stages: prospecting (50-60%), retargeting (20-30%), retention (10-20%)
- [ ] Each campaign's daily/lifetime budget aligns with the media plan line items
- [ ] Campaign budgets support the minimum spend needed for algorithm learning (50 conversions/week for Meta, 30/month for Google)
- [ ] Branded search campaigns have dedicated budgets that do not compete with non-branded budgets
- [ ] Seasonal budget adjustments are planned and documented (Black Friday, holidays, product launches)

## Section 4: Pacing and Monitoring
- [ ] Daily spend pacing targets are calculated (total budget / number of days in flight)
- [ ] Pacing alerts are configured: flag campaigns spending below 80% or above 120% of daily target
- [ ] Weekly budget review meetings are scheduled to assess pacing and performance
- [ ] Reallocation criteria are documented: move budget from platforms/campaigns with CPA 30%+ above target
- [ ] Budget changes are logged with date, amount, reason, and approver

## Section 5: Financial Controls
- [ ] Ad platform spending limits are set to prevent overspend (account-level and campaign-level)
- [ ] Payment methods have sufficient credit/funds for the planned budget
- [ ] Agency fee or management percentage is accounted for separately from media spend
- [ ] Currency exchange rate impact is considered for multi-currency accounts
- [ ] Budget allocation spreadsheet is shared with all stakeholders and updated in real-time

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/23 items (83%)
- **Reviewer**: account-manager-agent + finance-agent
- **Escalation**: Unapproved budget or missing financial controls are hard blocks; no spend is committed until resolved
