# Fiscal Compliance Quality Gate (Brazil Focus)

> Quality gate for fiscal compliance including Brazilian tax requirements, nota fiscal, and regulatory obligations. Must pass before invoices are issued or payments are processed.

## Section 1: Tax Registration and Documentation
- [ ] Company CNPJ is active and in good standing with the Receita Federal
- [ ] Company is registered for the correct tax regime: Simples Nacional, Lucro Presumido, or Lucro Real
- [ ] Municipal registration (Inscricao Municipal) is current for ISS (Imposto Sobre Servicos) obligations
- [ ] State registration (Inscricao Estadual) is current if applicable (for companies with ICMS obligations)
- [ ] Tax classification of digital advertising services is documented (CNAE code for advertising and marketing services)

## Section 2: Nota Fiscal Issuance
- [ ] Nota Fiscal de Servicos (NFS-e) is issued for all agency services rendered
- [ ] NFS-e includes correct service description, CNAE code, and ISS rate for the municipality
- [ ] NFS-e is issued within the legally required timeframe (before the 5th business day of the following month, or as per municipal regulation)
- [ ] Digital service invoices for foreign ad platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok) include proper IOF and IRRF withholding calculations
- [ ] Client CNPJ or CPF is correctly listed on all issued notas fiscais

## Section 3: Tax Withholding and Obligations
- [ ] ISS (Imposto Sobre Servicos) is calculated at the correct municipal rate (2% to 5% depending on municipality)
- [ ] IRRF (Imposto de Renda Retido na Fonte) withholding is applied to agency service invoices when required (typically 1.5% for services)
- [ ] PIS/COFINS contributions are calculated correctly based on the company's tax regime
- [ ] CSLL (Contribuicao Social sobre o Lucro Liquido) is accounted for in service invoicing
- [ ] IOF (Imposto sobre Operacoes Financeiras) is accounted for on international ad platform payments (credit card transactions in foreign currency)
- [ ] Tax withholding responsibilities (client vs. agency) are documented per contract

## Section 4: International Platform Payments
- [ ] Payments to international ad platforms (Google Ireland, Meta Platforms Inc.) comply with Central Bank regulations for international remittances
- [ ] Exchange rate documentation is maintained for all foreign currency transactions
- [ ] IOF on international credit card transactions (currently 4.38% for foreign purchases) is accounted for in cost reporting
- [ ] IRRF on international service payments (withholding on remittances to non-residents) is applied when required
- [ ] Transfer pricing documentation is maintained if the agency is part of a multinational group

## Section 5: Compliance Monitoring and Audit Readiness
- [ ] All tax documents (notas fiscais, DARFs, payment receipts) are archived digitally for the legally required retention period (5 years minimum)
- [ ] Monthly tax obligations calendar is maintained with deadlines for ISS, IRRF, PIS/COFINS, and CSLL payments
- [ ] DARF (Documento de Arrecadacao de Receitas Federais) payment receipts are filed for all federal tax payments
- [ ] Annual DIRF (Declaracao do Imposto de Renda Retido na Fonte) includes all withholding information
- [ ] Accounting reconciliation between ad platform spend, bank statements, and tax filings is completed monthly
- [ ] Legal/tax advisor is consulted for any new service types or unusual billing arrangements

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/23 items (83%)
- **Reviewer**: finance-agent + compliance-agent
- **Escalation**: Missing nota fiscal issuance, incorrect tax withholding, or expired CNPJ are critical compliance failures; all financial operations are paused until resolved
