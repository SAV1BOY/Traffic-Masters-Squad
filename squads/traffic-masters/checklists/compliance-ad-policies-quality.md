# Compliance and Ad Policies Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Compliance & Legal
> **Reviewed by**: Compliance Reviewer

## Purpose
Ensures all advertising materials and landing pages comply with platform policies, legal requirements, and industry regulations. Policy violations result in ad rejections, account restrictions, or legal liability.

## Checklist

### Claim Substantiation
- [ ] Every claim made in ad copy has supporting proof or documentation
- [ ] Income or earnings claims include realistic disclaimers
- [ ] Performance claims are based on verifiable data
- [ ] Testimonial results are representative or marked as atypical
- [ ] Superlative claims (best, fastest, cheapest) can be defended
- [ ] No misleading or deceptive claims are present in any creative

### Prohibited Content
- [ ] No prohibited products or services are being advertised
- [ ] No discriminatory language or targeting is used
- [ ] Content does not promote violence, hate speech, or illegal activity
- [ ] No misleading or sensationalist content is present
- [ ] Adult content guidelines are followed where applicable
- [ ] Alcohol, gambling, and pharmaceutical ads follow platform-specific rules

### Health and Finance Disclaimers
- [ ] Health-related ads include required medical disclaimers
- [ ] Financial product ads include risk disclosures
- [ ] Weight loss ads follow platform-specific restrictions
- [ ] Supplement ads do not make unapproved health claims
- [ ] Insurance and credit ads meet regulatory disclosure requirements

### Before/After Guidelines
- [ ] Before/after images follow platform-specific policies
- [ ] Results shown are realistic and achievable
- [ ] Disclaimers accompany before/after imagery
- [ ] No body-shaming or negative self-image language is used
- [ ] Transformations are accurately represented without manipulation

### Targeting Restrictions
- [ ] Special ad categories (housing, employment, credit) use appropriate targeting
- [ ] Age restrictions are applied where legally required
- [ ] Geographic targeting restrictions are followed for regulated products
- [ ] Custom audience usage complies with platform data use policies
- [ ] Sensitive interest targeting is avoided where prohibited

### Landing Page Compliance
- [ ] Landing page content matches ad claims and does not escalate them
- [ ] Terms of service and refund policies are accessible
- [ ] Contact information is visible and functional
- [ ] No auto-play audio or deceptive design patterns are present
- [ ] Landing page does not contain prohibited content or pop-ups
- [ ] Data collection forms comply with applicable regulations

### Privacy and Data
- [ ] Privacy policy is current and covers all data collection practices
- [ ] Cookie consent is implemented for GDPR and applicable jurisdictions
- [ ] Data processing agreements are in place with third-party tools
- [ ] User data opt-out mechanisms are functional
- [ ] CCPA compliance is addressed for California users where applicable
- [ ] Email and SMS marketing consent is properly obtained

## Pass/Fail Criteria
All checklist items must pass. Any compliance violation blocks ad launch and requires remediation before resubmission.

## If Failed
Immediately flag the violation with specific details. Revise the offending materials. Obtain compliance sign-off before resubmitting. Document the issue to prevent recurrence.
