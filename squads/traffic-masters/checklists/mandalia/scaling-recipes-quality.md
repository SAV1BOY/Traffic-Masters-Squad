# Scaling Recipes Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Depesh Mandalia's scaling recipes for taking winning campaigns from test budgets to significant spend. Must pass before any scaling action is executed.

## Section 1: Scale Readiness Assessment
- [ ] Campaign has demonstrated consistent performance for at least 5-7 consecutive days
- [ ] CPA is at or below target with no more than 15% daily variance
- [ ] ROAS meets minimum profitability threshold including all costs (not just ad spend)
- [ ] Campaign has exited the learning phase on the ad platform
- [ ] Creative performance shows no signs of fatigue (stable or improving CTR)

## Section 2: Vertical Scaling (Budget Increases)
- [ ] Budget increases are capped at 20% per increment per day
- [ ] Performance is monitored for 48 hours after each budget increase before the next
- [ ] CPA ceiling is defined (the maximum CPA acceptable during scaling turbulence)
- [ ] If CPA exceeds ceiling for 48+ hours, budget is rolled back to the last stable level
- [ ] Vertical scaling limit is identified (the budget level beyond which CPA degrades)

## Section 3: Horizontal Scaling (Duplication & Expansion)
- [ ] Winning ad sets are duplicated into new campaigns with fresh budgets
- [ ] Duplicated campaigns target new audience segments (not the same audience)
- [ ] New lookalike percentages are tested (expanding from 1% to 2-5%)
- [ ] Geographic expansion is tested with separate campaigns per new region
- [ ] Cross-platform duplication is considered (Meta to TikTok, Google, etc.)

## Section 4: Creative Scaling
- [ ] Winning creative concepts are iterated into 5-10 new variations
- [ ] New formats are produced from winning concepts (static > video, video > carousel)
- [ ] Influencer/UGC versions of winning concepts are commissioned
- [ ] Creative production pipeline is established to sustain the scaling pace
- [ ] Fresh creatives are launched every 7-14 days at scale to combat fatigue

## Section 5: Infrastructure for Scale
- [ ] Landing page can handle increased traffic without performance degradation
- [ ] Fulfillment and customer service teams are notified of expected volume increases
- [ ] Payment processing can handle increased transaction volume
- [ ] Email/SMS sequences are optimized to handle increased lead flow
- [ ] Reporting dashboards update in near-real-time for rapid decision-making at scale

## Section 6: Risk Controls
- [ ] Daily spend caps are set at the account level as a safety net
- [ ] Automated rules pause campaigns if CPA exceeds 2x target
- [ ] Weekend and holiday scaling adjustments are planned
- [ ] Rollback protocol is documented (specific steps to revert if scaling fails)
- [ ] Scaling log tracks every change with date, action, and result

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 21/25 items (84%)
- **Reviewer**: mandalia-scaling-strategist
- **Escalation**: If scale readiness assessment fails, do not proceed; continue optimizing at current budget until criteria are met
