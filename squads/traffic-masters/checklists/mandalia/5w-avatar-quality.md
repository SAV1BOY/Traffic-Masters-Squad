# 5W Avatar Framework Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Depesh Mandalia's 5W Avatar framework (Who, What, Why, When, Where). Must pass before the avatar is used in campaign targeting or creative development.

## Section 1: WHO - Target Identity
- [ ] Demographic profile is specific (age range, gender, location, income, education)
- [ ] Psychographic profile covers values, beliefs, aspirations, and fears
- [ ] Professional identity is defined (job title, industry, career stage)
- [ ] Social identity is mapped (peer groups, communities, influencers they follow)
- [ ] At least 3 distinct "who" sub-segments are identified within the broader avatar

## Section 2: WHAT - Problem & Solution
- [ ] The primary problem the avatar faces is stated in their own words
- [ ] Secondary problems and adjacent frustrations are documented
- [ ] The avatar's current solution (what they are doing now instead) is identified
- [ ] The ideal solution from the avatar's perspective is described
- [ ] The gap between current state and desired state is quantified where possible

## Section 3: WHY - Motivation & Emotion
- [ ] Surface-level motivations are identified (save money, save time, look better)
- [ ] Deeper emotional drivers are uncovered (status, security, belonging, freedom)
- [ ] The "why now" trigger is defined (what makes this problem urgent today?)
- [ ] Objections and reasons NOT to buy are documented with counterarguments
- [ ] The emotional transformation (before vs. after) is articulated in vivid detail

## Section 4: WHEN - Timing & Context
- [ ] The buying cycle length is estimated (impulse, days, weeks, months)
- [ ] Seasonal or cyclical demand patterns are identified
- [ ] The trigger moments that initiate the search for a solution are documented
- [ ] Time-of-day and day-of-week patterns for engagement are noted
- [ ] Life events that correlate with purchase intent are mapped

## Section 5: WHERE - Channels & Platforms
- [ ] Primary digital platforms where the avatar spends time are ranked
- [ ] Content consumption preferences are documented (video, text, audio, social)
- [ ] Specific communities, forums, and groups where the avatar gathers are listed
- [ ] Offline touchpoints and environments are considered (events, stores, workplaces)
- [ ] Channel-specific behavior differences are noted (how they use Instagram vs. YouTube vs. Google)

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/24 items (79%)
- **Reviewer**: mandalia-scaling-strategist
- **Escalation**: If any of the 5W dimensions is missing or based purely on assumption, conduct targeted research for that dimension before proceeding
