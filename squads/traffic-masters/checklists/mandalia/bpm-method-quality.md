# BPM (Brand Performance Marketing) Method Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Depesh Mandalia's BPM Method combining brand building with direct-response performance marketing. Must pass before a BPM campaign strategy is approved for execution.

## Section 1: Brand Foundation
- [ ] Brand positioning statement is clearly articulated (who we are, who we serve, what makes us different)
- [ ] Brand voice and tone guidelines are documented and applied to all ad copy
- [ ] Visual brand identity (colors, fonts, imagery style) is consistent across all creatives
- [ ] Brand story is distilled into a 30-second narrative that can be used in video ads
- [ ] Brand values are mapped to customer values (alignment between what the brand stands for and what the audience cares about)

## Section 2: Performance Layer Integration
- [ ] Direct-response elements are embedded within brand messaging (CTA, urgency, offer)
- [ ] Brand awareness campaigns include measurable conversion actions (not just reach)
- [ ] Performance campaigns maintain brand consistency (no off-brand creative for clicks)
- [ ] Full-funnel attribution connects brand touchpoints to eventual conversions
- [ ] Brand lift and conversion lift studies are planned or in progress

## Section 3: Audience Architecture (BPM Style)
- [ ] Top-of-funnel audiences are targeted with brand-first messaging
- [ ] Mid-funnel audiences receive content that blends brand storytelling with product education
- [ ] Bottom-funnel audiences get direct-response messaging wrapped in brand context
- [ ] Audience segments are built from real behavioral data, not just demographics
- [ ] Retargeting sequences tell a progressive brand story (not just "you forgot something")

## Section 4: Creative Strategy
- [ ] Each creative piece serves dual purpose: builds brand equity AND drives measurable action
- [ ] Video content follows the BPM structure: brand hook, value delivery, performance CTA
- [ ] Static ads use branded templates that are instantly recognizable in the feed
- [ ] Testimonials and social proof are presented in a brand-consistent format
- [ ] Creative testing includes both brand metrics (recall, sentiment) and performance metrics (CPA, ROAS)

## Section 5: Measurement Framework
- [ ] Brand metrics are tracked alongside performance metrics in a unified dashboard
- [ ] Share of voice is monitored relative to competitors
- [ ] Customer acquisition cost is tracked with and without brand campaign contribution
- [ ] Long-term LTV trends are correlated with brand investment levels
- [ ] Monthly brand health check includes aided recall, consideration, and preference metrics
- [ ] BPM efficiency ratio is calculated (total revenue / total brand + performance spend)

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/25 items (80%)
- **Reviewer**: mandalia-scaling-strategist
- **Escalation**: If brand and performance layers are disconnected (running independently), halt and redesign as an integrated BPM strategy
