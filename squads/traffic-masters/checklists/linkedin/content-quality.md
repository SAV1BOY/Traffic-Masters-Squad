# LinkedIn Ad Content Quality Gate

> Quality gate for LinkedIn ad content quality (copy, visuals, tone, compliance). Must pass before ads are submitted for review in Campaign Manager.

## Section 1: Ad Copy Quality
- [ ] Introductory text leads with a compelling hook relevant to the target professional audience
- [ ] Copy addresses a specific pain point or opportunity the target audience faces in their role
- [ ] Tone is professional yet conversational (not overly corporate or salesy)
- [ ] Value proposition is clear: what the audience gets, why it matters, and why now
- [ ] Copy includes a specific, action-oriented CTA (not generic "click here")
- [ ] Text is free of jargon, buzzwords, or cliches that reduce credibility ("synergy", "game-changer", "disrupt")

## Section 2: Headline and Description
- [ ] Headline is benefit-driven and under 70 characters for full display
- [ ] Headline complements (not repeats) the introductory text
- [ ] Description (for Text Ads) adds context and reinforces the value proposition
- [ ] Dynamic ad personalization tokens (%FIRSTNAME%, %COMPANYNAME%) are used correctly in Message/Conversation ads
- [ ] All copy variations have been proofread for grammar, spelling, and punctuation

## Section 3: Visual Asset Quality
- [ ] Images are high-resolution (1200x628px for Single Image, 1080x1080px for Carousel)
- [ ] Visuals are professional and relevant to the B2B audience (no generic stock photos of handshakes)
- [ ] Images contain minimal text overlay (LinkedIn does not enforce 20% rule, but less text performs better)
- [ ] Brand colors, logo, and visual identity are consistent with the brand guidelines
- [ ] Carousel ads tell a sequential story or present a logical progression across cards (3-5 cards recommended)
- [ ] Video ads include captions/subtitles (most LinkedIn feed consumption is sound-off)

## Section 4: Content Relevance and Value
- [ ] Content offer (whitepaper, webinar, demo, case study) is genuinely valuable to the target audience
- [ ] Content is gated appropriately (Lead Gen Form for high-value assets, ungated for awareness content)
- [ ] Landing page content matches the ad promise (no bait-and-switch)
- [ ] Content is timely and relevant to current industry trends or challenges
- [ ] Social proof is included where possible (client logos, testimonials, data points, awards)

## Section 5: Compliance and Policy
- [ ] Ad content complies with LinkedIn Advertising Policies (no misleading claims, prohibited categories)
- [ ] Financial, employment, or housing ads include required disclaimers
- [ ] Personal data collection (Lead Gen Forms) includes privacy policy and consent disclosures
- [ ] No competitor disparagement or unsubstantiated comparative claims
- [ ] All claims are factual and can be substantiated if challenged

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/23 items (83%)
- **Reviewer**: creative-strategist-agent + account-manager-agent
- **Escalation**: Policy violations or misleading claims are hard blocks; content must be revised and re-reviewed before submission
