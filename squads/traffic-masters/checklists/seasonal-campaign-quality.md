# Seasonal Campaign Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Campaign Planning
> **Reviewed by**: Traffic Strategist

## Purpose
Ensures seasonal and event-driven campaigns are planned, produced, and launched with sufficient lead time and strategic rigor. Last-minute seasonal campaigns underperform and cost more due to increased competition.

## Checklist

### Timeline Planning
- [ ] Campaign timeline is created at least 6-8 weeks before the seasonal event
- [ ] Key milestones are defined: strategy, creative production, build, QA, launch
- [ ] Each milestone has an owner and deadline
- [ ] Buffer time is built in for revisions and unexpected delays
- [ ] Launch date allows for platform learning phase before peak traffic days
- [ ] Post-event wind-down and campaign deactivation dates are scheduled

### Budget Pre-Allocation
- [ ] Seasonal budget is separated from evergreen campaign budgets
- [ ] Budget accounts for increased CPMs during competitive seasonal periods
- [ ] Budget is allocated by phase (warm-up, peak, wind-down)
- [ ] Budget contingency is available for extending high-performing campaigns
- [ ] Budget approval is obtained well in advance of the campaign start
- [ ] Year-over-year seasonal spend is compared for realistic planning

### Creative Production
- [ ] Seasonal creative concepts are developed and approved at least 4 weeks early
- [ ] Multiple creative variations are produced for testing during the warm-up phase
- [ ] Creative reflects the seasonal theme while maintaining brand identity
- [ ] Platform-specific formats and specs are addressed in production
- [ ] Creative assets are organized and ready for upload before the build phase
- [ ] Backup creative concepts are available if initial versions underperform

### Historical Review
- [ ] Previous year's seasonal campaign performance is analyzed
- [ ] Top-performing creatives, audiences, and offers from past seasons are identified
- [ ] Mistakes and learnings from previous seasons are documented and referenced
- [ ] Year-over-year trends in CPMs, conversion rates, and ROAS are noted
- [ ] Historical data informs budget allocation and audience targeting decisions

### Competitor Monitoring
- [ ] Key competitor seasonal campaigns are monitored using ad libraries
- [ ] Competitor offers, messaging angles, and creative approaches are documented
- [ ] Opportunities to differentiate from competitor approaches are identified
- [ ] Competitor launch timing is noted to inform own campaign timing
- [ ] Post-season competitor analysis is planned for future improvement

### Contingency Plan
- [ ] Alternative plan exists if primary creative or offer underperforms
- [ ] Budget reallocation protocol is defined for mid-campaign pivots
- [ ] Backup audiences are identified if primary audiences saturate quickly
- [ ] Platform outage or disapproval contingencies are addressed
- [ ] Decision criteria for activating the contingency plan are predefined
- [ ] Communication plan exists for stakeholders if performance deviates from targets

### Post-Season Analysis
- [ ] Post-mortem review is scheduled within one week of campaign end
- [ ] Performance data is captured and archived before platform data expires
- [ ] Learnings are documented in the team knowledge base
- [ ] Creative performance data is stored for next season's reference
- [ ] Recommendations for next year's seasonal campaign are drafted
- [ ] Financial reconciliation for the seasonal budget is completed

## Pass/Fail Criteria
All seven sections must pass. No seasonal campaign should launch without completed timeline planning and pre-allocated budget at minimum.

## If Failed
Assess whether the campaign can be delayed to meet quality standards. If the seasonal window is fixed, prioritize the highest-impact items and document compromises for post-mortem review.
