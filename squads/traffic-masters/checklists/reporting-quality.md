# Reporting Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Reporting & Communication
> **Reviewed by**: Analytics Lead

## Purpose
Ensures every report delivered to stakeholders is accurate, actionable, and clearly communicated. Reports that lack context or recommendations fail to drive decisions and undermine trust.

## Checklist

### Data Source Verification
- [ ] All data sources used in the report are identified and documented
- [ ] Data has been pulled from the correct date range
- [ ] Platform data has been cross-referenced with backend or CRM data
- [ ] Any data discrepancies are noted and explained
- [ ] Data freshness and lag are accounted for (e.g., attribution delays)

### KPI Alignment
- [ ] KPIs in the report match the strategic objectives of the campaign
- [ ] Primary KPIs are highlighted and given prominence
- [ ] Secondary and diagnostic metrics provide supporting context
- [ ] No vanity metrics are presented without business context
- [ ] KPI definitions are consistent with how they were defined in the strategy

### Comparative Analysis
- [ ] Week-over-week (WoW) comparisons are included for key metrics
- [ ] Month-over-month (MoM) comparisons are included for trend analysis
- [ ] Performance is compared against stated targets or benchmarks
- [ ] Seasonal or external factors are noted when they affect comparisons
- [ ] Historical performance context is provided for new campaigns

### Actionable Insights
- [ ] Each data point is accompanied by an interpretation or insight
- [ ] Insights explain the why behind the numbers, not just the what
- [ ] Root causes for significant performance changes are identified
- [ ] Insights are prioritized by business impact
- [ ] Positive trends are reinforced with recommendations to continue

### Decision Recommendations
- [ ] Specific actions are recommended based on the data
- [ ] Recommendations include expected impact and timeline
- [ ] Trade-offs of recommended actions are acknowledged
- [ ] Recommendations are prioritized (must-do, should-do, could-do)
- [ ] Budget reallocation recommendations are supported by data

### Next Steps
- [ ] Clear next steps are listed with owners and deadlines
- [ ] Next steps are achievable within the stated timeframe
- [ ] Dependencies between next steps are identified
- [ ] Next steps tie back to the strategic objectives
- [ ] Follow-up reporting date is confirmed

### Communication Quality
- [ ] Language is clear, concise, and appropriate for the audience
- [ ] Technical jargon is minimized or explained for non-specialist readers
- [ ] Visualizations are clean, labeled, and easy to interpret
- [ ] Executive summary is provided for longer reports
- [ ] Report format is consistent with established templates

## Pass/Fail Criteria
All seven sections must pass before the report is delivered to stakeholders. No report should be sent without actionable insights and clear next steps.

## If Failed
Revise the report addressing the flagged sections. Have a second reviewer validate accuracy and clarity before resubmitting to stakeholders.
