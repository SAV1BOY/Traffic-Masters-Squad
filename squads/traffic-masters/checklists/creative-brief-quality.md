# Creative Brief Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Creative Production
> **Reviewed by**: Creative Strategist

## Purpose
Ensures every creative brief provides sufficient direction for producing high-performing ad creatives. An incomplete brief leads to off-strategy creative and wasted production cycles.

## Checklist

### Audience Definition
- [ ] Target audience segment is clearly identified with demographics
- [ ] Psychographic profile is included (beliefs, values, fears, desires)
- [ ] Awareness level is specified (unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, most aware)
- [ ] Specific language and phrases the audience uses are documented
- [ ] Key objections the audience holds are listed

### Angles (Minimum Three)
- [ ] At least three distinct messaging angles are provided
- [ ] Each angle attacks a different pain point, desire, or objection
- [ ] Angles are prioritized based on expected performance or testing order
- [ ] Each angle includes a brief rationale for why it should resonate
- [ ] Angles are differentiated enough to provide meaningful test variation

### Hooks
- [ ] At least two hooks are written per angle
- [ ] Hooks are designed to stop the scroll within the first 3 seconds
- [ ] Hooks use pattern interrupts, curiosity, or bold claims
- [ ] Hook formats vary (question, statement, statistic, story opener)
- [ ] Hooks are platform-appropriate in tone and length

### Proof Points
- [ ] Specific proof elements are assigned to each angle
- [ ] Testimonials, statistics, or case studies are provided with source references
- [ ] Proof is formatted for easy integration into creative (quotes, screenshots, data)
- [ ] Before-and-after examples are included where applicable

### Policy Compliance
- [ ] All claims are substantiated and can withstand ad platform review
- [ ] No prohibited or restricted language is used
- [ ] Health, finance, or income claims include required disclaimers
- [ ] Content follows platform-specific advertising policies

### Specs and Format
- [ ] Correct dimensions and aspect ratios are specified per platform
- [ ] File format and size requirements are documented
- [ ] Duration limits for video are noted
- [ ] Safe zone guidelines for text and elements are included

### Call to Action
- [ ] Primary CTA is defined for each creative
- [ ] CTA matches the funnel destination and offer
- [ ] CTA language is tested or aligned with best-performing historical CTAs

### Brand Guidelines
- [ ] Brand colors, fonts, and logo usage rules are referenced
- [ ] Tone of voice guidelines are included or linked
- [ ] Any brand restrictions or non-negotiables are clearly stated
- [ ] Approval process and stakeholders are identified

## Pass/Fail Criteria
All eight sections must be complete. No creative production should begin without a fully approved brief.

## If Failed
Return the brief to the strategist with notes on missing or weak sections. Revise and resubmit before any creative work begins.
