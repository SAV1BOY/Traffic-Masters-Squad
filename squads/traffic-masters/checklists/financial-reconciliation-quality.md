# Financial Reconciliation Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Financial Operations
> **Reviewed by**: Finance Lead

## Purpose
Ensures advertising spend is accurately tracked, reconciled against invoices, and properly allocated. Financial discrepancies erode trust, distort ROI calculations, and create accounting issues.

## Checklist

### Spend vs Invoice Matching
- [ ] Platform-reported spend is compared against invoices for each billing period
- [ ] Variance between reported spend and invoiced amount is within 2%
- [ ] Discrepancies exceeding 2% are flagged and investigated immediately
- [ ] Currency conversion rates are verified for international accounts
- [ ] Multiple ad accounts are reconciled individually, not just in aggregate
- [ ] Invoice dates and billing periods align with reporting periods

### Cost Center Tagging
- [ ] Every campaign is tagged to the correct cost center or project code
- [ ] Cost center assignments are verified monthly
- [ ] New campaigns are tagged before activation
- [ ] Cross-departmental campaigns have agreed-upon cost allocation splits
- [ ] Cost center tagging is consistent across all ad platforms

### Tax Documentation
- [ ] Tax amounts on invoices are verified against expected rates
- [ ] VAT, GST, or sales tax is correctly applied based on jurisdiction
- [ ] Tax-exempt status documentation is current where applicable
- [ ] Tax documents are filed and accessible for audit purposes
- [ ] Tax withholding requirements for international platforms are met

### Refund and Credit Tracking
- [ ] Platform credits from outages or errors are tracked and applied
- [ ] Refund requests for policy violations or billing errors are documented
- [ ] Credits received are reconciled against the original discrepancy
- [ ] Pending refunds are tracked with expected resolution dates
- [ ] Promo credits and coupons are applied correctly and tracked to expiration

### Budget Reconciliation
- [ ] Actual monthly spend is reconciled against the approved budget
- [ ] Variance explanations are documented for over and under budget items
- [ ] Year-to-date spend tracking is current and accurate
- [ ] Budget forecasts for remaining periods are updated based on actuals
- [ ] Rollover budget policies are applied correctly across periods

### Anomaly Investigation
- [ ] Unusual spend spikes are investigated within 24 hours
- [ ] Sudden drops in spend that may indicate billing issues are reviewed
- [ ] Double charges or duplicate invoices are identified and disputed
- [ ] Unauthorized charges or unrecognized campaigns are flagged immediately
- [ ] Root cause analysis is completed for every financial anomaly
- [ ] Corrective actions are documented and implemented to prevent recurrence

## Pass/Fail Criteria
All checklist items must pass at each monthly reconciliation. Any invoice discrepancy exceeding 2% requires immediate investigation and resolution.

## If Failed
Escalate discrepancies to the finance team and platform account representative. Freeze discretionary spend increases until reconciliation is complete. Document resolution and update processes to prevent recurrence.
