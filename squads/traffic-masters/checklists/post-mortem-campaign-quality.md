# Post-Mortem Campaign Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Learning & Improvement
> **Reviewed by**: Traffic Strategist

## Purpose
Ensures every completed campaign is systematically reviewed for results, learnings, and actionable improvements. Without structured post-mortems, teams repeat mistakes and fail to compound their knowledge.

## Checklist

### Results vs Goals
- [ ] Final results are compared against the original campaign objectives
- [ ] Primary KPIs (CPA, ROAS, CPL, revenue) are reported against targets
- [ ] Secondary metrics are reviewed for additional context
- [ ] Results are broken down by platform, audience, and creative
- [ ] Timeline of performance is mapped showing ramp-up, peak, and decline phases
- [ ] Budget utilization is reported (actual spend vs planned spend)

### What Worked
- [ ] Top-performing creatives are identified with specific metrics
- [ ] Best-performing audiences are documented with performance data
- [ ] Successful strategies or tactics are noted with supporting evidence
- [ ] Unexpected wins or positive surprises are captured
- [ ] Winning elements are categorized for reuse (hooks, angles, formats, audiences)
- [ ] Quantified impact of what worked is documented (not just qualitative)

### What Did Not Work
- [ ] Underperforming creatives are identified with specific metrics
- [ ] Audiences that did not convert are documented
- [ ] Strategies or tactics that failed are noted honestly
- [ ] Budget wasted on non-performing elements is quantified
- [ ] Timing or market factors that negatively impacted performance are noted

### Root Cause Analysis
- [ ] For each major success, the underlying reason is identified
- [ ] For each major failure, the root cause is investigated
- [ ] External factors (seasonality, competition, market changes) are considered
- [ ] Internal factors (execution quality, timing, resource constraints) are assessed
- [ ] Root causes are supported by data rather than assumptions
- [ ] Preventable issues are distinguished from uncontrollable factors

### Learnings Registry
- [ ] Key learnings are documented in the team's centralized knowledge base
- [ ] Learnings are tagged by category (creative, audience, strategy, tracking, budget)
- [ ] Learnings include context so they are useful to team members who were not involved
- [ ] Contradictions with previous learnings are noted and reconciled
- [ ] Learnings are actionable and specific (not vague generalizations)

### Next Steps
- [ ] Specific follow-up actions are listed with owners and deadlines
- [ ] Winning elements are queued for scaling or replication
- [ ] Failed elements have clear do-not-repeat flags or improvement plans
- [ ] Testing hypotheses for the next campaign are derived from learnings
- [ ] Budget recommendations for future similar campaigns are documented
- [ ] Process improvements are identified and assigned for implementation

### Team Sharing
- [ ] Post-mortem findings are shared with all relevant team members
- [ ] A debrief meeting or presentation is scheduled and conducted
- [ ] Stakeholders outside the immediate team receive an executive summary
- [ ] Creative team receives specific feedback on creative performance
- [ ] Learnings are accessible and searchable for future reference
- [ ] Questions and additional insights from the team are captured

## Pass/Fail Criteria
All seven sections must be completed for the post-mortem to be considered done. No campaign is truly finished until its post-mortem is filed and shared.

## If Failed
Schedule a dedicated session to complete the missing sections. Assign responsibility and set a deadline within one week of the campaign end date. Incomplete post-mortems degrade team learning velocity.
