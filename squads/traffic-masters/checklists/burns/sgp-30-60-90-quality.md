# Strategic Growth Plan (30-60-90 Day) Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Charley T. Burns' Strategic Growth Plan covering 30-60-90 day planning horizons. Must pass before the growth plan is approved for execution.

## Section 1: 30-Day Phase (Foundation & Testing)
- [ ] Current account audit is completed with documented findings
- [ ] Top 3 immediate opportunities are identified from audit data
- [ ] Testing budget is allocated (typically 20-30% of total monthly budget)
- [ ] Minimum of 3 creative concepts are queued for testing in the first 30 days
- [ ] Conversion tracking and attribution are validated end-to-end
- [ ] Baseline metrics are recorded for all active campaigns (CPA, ROAS, CTR, CVR)
- [ ] Week-by-week milestones are defined for the first 30 days

## Section 2: 60-Day Phase (Optimization & Validation)
- [ ] Winners from 30-day testing phase are identified and ready for scaling
- [ ] Losing campaigns from the test phase are documented with root cause analysis
- [ ] Budget reallocation plan shifts spend from losers to winners
- [ ] New audience segments are tested based on 30-day learnings
- [ ] Creative refresh cycle is initiated based on Kaizen Kreative findings
- [ ] Funnel optimization opportunities are identified (landing page, email, checkout)
- [ ] 60-day CPA and ROAS targets are defined based on 30-day actuals

## Section 3: 90-Day Phase (Scaling & Expansion)
- [ ] Proven campaigns are scaled with documented scaling methodology (20% increments)
- [ ] New channels or platforms are evaluated for expansion based on audience overlap data
- [ ] LTV-based NCAC targets replace initial CPA targets for mature campaigns
- [ ] Creative production pipeline is established for ongoing content creation
- [ ] Automation rules are implemented for bid management and budget pacing

## Section 4: Cross-Phase Requirements
- [ ] Weekly reporting cadence is established with standardized templates
- [ ] Stakeholder check-in meetings are scheduled (weekly for 30-day, bi-weekly for 60-90)
- [ ] Risk register identifies top 3 risks per phase with mitigation plans
- [ ] Resource requirements (team, tools, creative assets) are documented per phase
- [ ] Success criteria for graduating from one phase to the next are defined

## Section 5: Plan Documentation & Alignment
- [ ] SGP document is shared with all stakeholders before execution begins
- [ ] Budget projections are approved by finance or the client
- [ ] Team roles and responsibilities are assigned for each phase
- [ ] Contingency plans exist for underperformance scenarios at each phase gate
- [ ] Final 90-day review meeting is pre-scheduled to assess overall plan performance

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 21/25 items (84%)
- **Reviewer**: burns-performance-strategist
- **Escalation**: If 30-day phase milestones are missed by more than 1 week, convene an emergency review to adjust the 60-90 day plan
