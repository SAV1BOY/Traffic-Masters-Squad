# NCAC Method Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Charley T. Burns' NCAC (New Customer Acquisition Cost) method. Must pass before customer acquisition efficiency is considered optimized.

## Section 1: NCAC Baseline Calculation
- [ ] Total ad spend for the measurement period is accurately recorded
- [ ] Total number of NEW customers (not repeat) is isolated from total conversions
- [ ] NCAC is calculated as total ad spend / total new customers acquired
- [ ] NCAC is segmented by campaign, channel, and audience type
- [ ] Historical NCAC trend is plotted (at least 30 days of data for baseline)

## Section 2: NCAC Target Setting
- [ ] Target NCAC is derived from unit economics (not arbitrary benchmarks)
- [ ] Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) is calculated or estimated with documented methodology
- [ ] LTV:NCAC ratio target is set (minimum 3:1 for sustainable growth)
- [ ] Payback period is defined (how many days/months to recoup acquisition cost)
- [ ] Target NCAC accounts for all costs, not just ad spend (landing pages, tools, creative production)

## Section 3: New vs. Returning Customer Separation
- [ ] Tracking distinguishes between first-time and returning customers
- [ ] CRM or backend data is used to validate new customer status (not just platform reporting)
- [ ] Retargeting campaigns to existing customers are excluded from NCAC calculations
- [ ] Brand search campaigns are separated from prospecting in NCAC reporting
- [ ] Organic and referral conversions are excluded from paid NCAC calculations

## Section 4: NCAC Optimization Levers
- [ ] Top-of-funnel efficiency is optimized (lower CPM and higher CTR reduce NCAC)
- [ ] Landing page conversion rate improvements are tested as an NCAC lever
- [ ] Audience quality is prioritized over audience size (better audiences = lower NCAC)
- [ ] Creative performance directly impacts NCAC and is tracked accordingly
- [ ] Offer strength is evaluated as the primary NCAC lever (a better offer beats better ads)

## Section 5: Reporting & Decision Framework
- [ ] NCAC is reported weekly with trend direction (improving, stable, declining)
- [ ] Campaigns exceeding NCAC target by 30%+ for 7 days are flagged for review
- [ ] Campaigns below NCAC target are prioritized for budget increases
- [ ] NCAC dashboard is accessible to all stakeholders
- [ ] Monthly NCAC review meeting is scheduled with documented action items

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 18/22 items (82%)
- **Reviewer**: burns-performance-strategist
- **Escalation**: If NCAC exceeds 1.5x target for 14+ consecutive days, trigger a full campaign audit
