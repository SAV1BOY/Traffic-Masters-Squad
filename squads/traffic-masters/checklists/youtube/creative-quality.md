# YouTube Video Ad Creative Quality Gate

> Quality gate for YouTube video ad creative quality and effectiveness. Must pass before video assets are approved for campaign use.

## Section 1: Hook and Attention (First 5 Seconds)
- [ ] Opening frame grabs attention immediately (question, bold visual, unexpected element, or direct address)
- [ ] Brand name or logo is visible within the first 5 seconds
- [ ] The viewer understands what the ad is about within the first 5 seconds
- [ ] No slow intros, title cards, or generic stock footage in the opening
- [ ] Audio hook is compelling (music, voiceover, or sound effect that stops the scroll)

## Section 2: Story Structure and Messaging
- [ ] Video follows a clear narrative arc: Hook > Problem > Solution > Proof > CTA
- [ ] Key message or value proposition is communicated within the first 15 seconds
- [ ] Only one primary message per video (avoid message overload)
- [ ] Social proof elements (testimonials, stats, reviews) are included where relevant
- [ ] Emotional appeal matches the target audience (aspiration, fear of missing out, humor, empathy)

## Section 3: Production Quality
- [ ] Video resolution is 1080p minimum (4K preferred for YouTube)
- [ ] Audio is clear, well-mixed, and free of background noise
- [ ] Lighting is professional or intentionally styled (no accidental dark/overexposed shots)
- [ ] On-screen text is legible on mobile devices (minimum 24pt equivalent)
- [ ] Color grading is consistent throughout the video
- [ ] Subtitles/captions are accurate, properly timed, and available in the target language

## Section 4: Call-to-Action Effectiveness
- [ ] CTA is clear, specific, and appears both verbally and visually
- [ ] CTA appears at least twice: mid-video and end of video
- [ ] End screen elements (subscribe button, related video, website link) are configured
- [ ] Companion banner reinforces the CTA and is visually consistent with the video
- [ ] The viewer knows exactly what to do next after watching

## Section 5: Format-Specific Requirements
- [ ] Skippable ads: most critical content is front-loaded before the 5-second skip point
- [ ] Bumper ads (6s): single focused message with brand and CTA compressed effectively
- [ ] Shorts ads: vertical format (9:16), native feel, fast-paced editing
- [ ] Non-skippable (15s): complete story arc within 15 seconds, no filler

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/23 items (83%)
- **Reviewer**: creative-strategist-agent
- **Escalation**: Weak hooks (first 5 seconds fail to engage) or missing CTAs require creative revision before deployment
