# YouTube Targeting Quality Gate

> Quality gate for YouTube ad targeting configuration (audiences, placements, topics, demographics). Must pass before ad groups are activated.

## Section 1: Audience Targeting
- [ ] Audience strategy is documented: prospecting (In-market, Affinity) vs. retargeting (website visitors, video viewers)
- [ ] In-market audiences are selected based on active purchase intent signals relevant to the product
- [ ] Affinity audiences match the brand's ideal customer profile (lifestyle and interest alignment)
- [ ] Custom segments are created with relevant search terms (10-15 keywords) and URLs (competitor sites)
- [ ] Customer Match lists are uploaded for retargeting and suppression of existing customers
- [ ] Video remarketing lists are created: viewers of specific videos, channel subscribers, channel visitors

## Section 2: Demographic Targeting
- [ ] Age range targeting matches the buyer persona (excluding "Unknown" only with justification)
- [ ] Gender targeting is applied only when the product is genuinely gender-specific
- [ ] Household income targeting is used for luxury/high-ticket products (available in select markets)
- [ ] Parental status targeting is applied only when product relevance requires it
- [ ] Demographic exclusions are documented with business rationale

## Section 3: Placement and Content Targeting
- [ ] Managed placements (specific channels or videos) are used for brand-safety-sensitive campaigns
- [ ] Topic targeting is relevant and not overly broad (e.g., "Running Shoes" not just "Sports")
- [ ] Content exclusions are configured: DL-MA content, embedded videos, live streaming (as appropriate)
- [ ] Inventory type is set to "Standard" or "Limited" for brand safety (not "Expanded" without justification)
- [ ] Sensitive content categories are excluded (tragedy, conflict, profanity, sexually suggestive)

## Section 4: Targeting Combinations and Layering
- [ ] Targeting layers (audience + topic, audience + placement) are intentionally combined or separated
- [ ] Observation audiences are added alongside targeting audiences to gather data without restricting reach
- [ ] Targeting is not overly narrow (estimated impressions should support the budget)
- [ ] Frequency capping is set: 3-5 impressions per week for awareness, 7-10 for retargeting
- [ ] Device targeting includes mobile, desktop, tablet, and connected TV (unless exclusion is justified)

## Section 5: Targeting Validation
- [ ] Audience size estimates support the planned budget and CPV/CPM targets
- [ ] No conflicting targeting settings that would result in zero or near-zero reach
- [ ] Targeting settings have been reviewed in the Google Ads forecast/reach planner
- [ ] Excluded audiences (converters, existing customers) are correctly applied to prospecting campaigns

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/23 items (83%)
- **Reviewer**: media-buyer-agent
- **Escalation**: Missing brand safety exclusions are a hard block; must be configured before any video campaign goes live
