# YouTube Measurement and Attribution Quality Gate

> Quality gate for YouTube campaign measurement, attribution, and reporting setup. Must pass before campaign performance is reported to stakeholders.

## Section 1: Conversion Tracking Setup
- [ ] Google Ads conversion tracking is active on the destination landing page
- [ ] Video engagement conversions are configured: 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% view milestones
- [ ] Engaged-view conversions (10s+ watch then convert within 3 days) are tracked as a separate action
- [ ] View-through conversion window is set appropriately (default 1 day; extended to 7-30 days for consideration campaigns)
- [ ] Click-through conversion window matches the product consideration cycle

## Section 2: Brand Lift Study Setup (if applicable)
- [ ] Brand Lift study is activated for campaigns with sufficient budget (minimum $10K-$50K depending on market)
- [ ] Survey questions are configured: Ad Recall, Brand Awareness, Consideration, Purchase Intent, Favorability
- [ ] Control and exposed groups are properly isolated
- [ ] Brand Lift study results are available within 7-14 days of reaching sufficient responses
- [ ] Lift results are benchmarked against industry averages

## Section 3: Audience and Engagement Metrics
- [ ] View rate (VR) benchmarks are established by ad format: >15% for skippable in-stream, 100% for non-skippable/bumpers
- [ ] Cost-per-view (CPV) targets are set based on historical data or industry benchmarks
- [ ] Audience retention curve is analyzed to identify drop-off points (especially around 5s skip)
- [ ] Earned actions (shares, likes, subscribes) are tracked as secondary engagement metrics
- [ ] View-through rate by audience segment is compared to identify top-performing targets

## Section 4: Cross-Channel Attribution
- [ ] YouTube campaign data is integrated in Google Analytics 4 for cross-channel path analysis
- [ ] Assisted conversions from YouTube are tracked (YouTube may assist Search/Shopping conversions)
- [ ] Data-driven attribution model is used in Google Ads (not last-click) for accurate YouTube credit
- [ ] YouTube's contribution to branded search lift is measured (compare brand search volume during/after campaign)
- [ ] Incrementality testing or geo-based experiments are planned for campaigns above $50K spend

## Section 5: Reporting and Insights
- [ ] Reporting template includes: video-level metrics, audience-level metrics, and placement-level metrics
- [ ] Frequency distribution report is included (how many users saw 1x, 2x, 3x+)
- [ ] Creative performance comparison includes view rate, engagement rate, and cost efficiency
- [ ] YouTube-specific insights are separated from overall Google Ads reporting
- [ ] Report includes actionable recommendations for creative iteration and targeting adjustments

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 18/23 items (78%)
- **Reviewer**: analytics-agent + media-buyer-agent
- **Escalation**: Missing conversion tracking or incorrect attribution windows are hard blocks; must be fixed before reporting begins
