# YouTube Video Ad Campaign Setup Quality Gate

> Quality gate for YouTube video ad campaign setup and configuration. Must pass before the video campaign is enabled in Google Ads.

## Section 1: Campaign Configuration
- [ ] Campaign type is set to Video (or Performance Max with YouTube assets)
- [ ] Campaign subtype matches the goal: Video Views, Video Reach, Ad Sequence, Audio, or Drive Conversions
- [ ] Bidding strategy aligns with the objective: CPV for awareness, Target CPA for conversions, Target CPM for reach
- [ ] Budget type (daily or total) and amount match the approved media plan
- [ ] Campaign start and end dates match the flight schedule

## Section 2: Ad Format Selection
- [ ] Ad format is appropriate for the objective: Skippable in-stream, Non-skippable (15s), Bumper (6s), In-feed, Shorts
- [ ] Skippable in-stream ads are at least 12 seconds long (recommended 15-60s; charged after 30s or full view)
- [ ] Non-skippable ads are exactly 15 seconds (or 20s in select markets)
- [ ] Bumper ads are exactly 6 seconds or less
- [ ] YouTube Shorts ads are vertical (9:16) and under 60 seconds
- [ ] Video ad sequence steps are ordered logically (awareness hook > consideration > CTA)

## Section 3: Video Asset Requirements
- [ ] Videos are uploaded to the client's YouTube channel (not an unlisted third-party channel)
- [ ] Video resolution is at least 1080p (1920x1080 for landscape, 1080x1920 for vertical/Shorts)
- [ ] Audio quality is clear with no background noise or distortion
- [ ] The first 5 seconds contain a compelling hook (critical for skippable ads)
- [ ] Brand logo or name appears within the first 5 seconds
- [ ] End screen and CTA overlay are configured (companion banner for in-stream ads)

## Section 4: Landing Page and Tracking
- [ ] Final URL is live, mobile-optimized, and loads within 3 seconds
- [ ] UTM parameters are appended to the destination URL
- [ ] Google Ads conversion tracking is firing on the landing page
- [ ] Video engagement actions (watch 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) are tracked as secondary conversions

## Section 5: Compliance and Review
- [ ] Video content complies with YouTube Advertising Policies (no prohibited content)
- [ ] Companion banner image is 300x60px and meets creative guidelines
- [ ] All ads have been previewed in Google Ads preview tool across desktop and mobile
- [ ] Campaign has been reviewed by a second team member before activation

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/22 items (86%)
- **Reviewer**: media-buyer-agent
- **Escalation**: Policy violations or missing video hooks are hard blocks; must be revised before launch
