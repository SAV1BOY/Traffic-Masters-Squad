# GECO & ANA Methodology Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Pedro Sobral's GECO (Gerir, Estruturar, Criar, Otimizar) and ANA (Analisar, Nutrir, Ativar) methodology for traffic management. Must pass before the traffic management framework is considered complete.

## Section 1: G - Gerir (Manage)
- [ ] Account structure is organized with clear naming conventions following Sobral's taxonomy
- [ ] Business Manager and ad accounts are properly configured with correct permissions
- [ ] Payment methods are verified and backup payment is configured
- [ ] Team roles and access levels are defined (admin, editor, analyst)
- [ ] Client communication cadence is established (weekly reports, monthly strategy reviews)

## Section 2: E - Estruturar (Structure)
- [ ] Campaign structure follows the recommended hierarchy for the business type
- [ ] Campaigns are separated by objective (engagement, traffic, conversion, messages)
- [ ] Ad sets are organized by audience segment with clear naming
- [ ] Budget allocation across campaigns follows strategic priorities
- [ ] Pixel and conversion events are properly structured and tested
- [ ] Audience exclusion strategy prevents overlap and wasted spend

## Section 3: C - Criar (Create)
- [ ] Ad creatives are developed based on the offer and audience research
- [ ] Multiple creative formats are tested (image, video, carousel, Stories)
- [ ] Copy variations follow the PAS (Problem-Agitation-Solution) or similar framework
- [ ] Creative assets are produced in all required dimensions (1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9)
- [ ] At least 3-5 ad variations per ad set for meaningful testing

## Section 4: O - Otimizar (Optimize)
- [ ] Optimization schedule is defined (daily monitoring, weekly adjustments)
- [ ] Kill criteria for underperforming ads are documented (CPA threshold, minimum impressions)
- [ ] Budget reallocation follows performance data (increase winners, decrease losers)
- [ ] Creative fatigue is monitored through frequency and CTR trends
- [ ] A/B testing cadence is maintained for continuous improvement

## Section 5: A - Analisar (Analyze - ANA Framework)
- [ ] Performance data is analyzed at campaign, ad set, and ad levels
- [ ] Key metrics are tracked against defined KPIs (CPA, ROAS, CPL, CPC)
- [ ] Funnel analysis identifies drop-off points from impression to conversion
- [ ] Audience performance analysis reveals which segments drive the best results
- [ ] Weekly analysis report is generated with insights and recommended actions

## Section 6: N - Nutrir (Nurture) & A - Ativar (Activate)
- [ ] Nurture sequences are in place for leads who do not convert immediately
- [ ] Remarketing campaigns are structured to nurture warm audiences with progressive messaging
- [ ] Activation campaigns are designed to convert nurtured audiences with direct offers
- [ ] Engagement campaigns maintain brand presence between purchase cycles
- [ ] The full ANA cycle (Analyze > Nurture > Activate) is documented and repeatable

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 21/25 items (84%)
- **Reviewer**: sobral-br-traffic-strategist
- **Escalation**: If the Gerir (Management) or Estruturar (Structure) phases fail, stop all campaign creation until the foundation is solid
