# Filosofia do Medio Consistente Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Pedro Sobral's Filosofia do Medio Consistente (Philosophy of Consistent Average) for sustainable traffic management. Must pass before declaring a campaign strategy mature and sustainable.

## Section 1: Consistency Over Peaks
- [ ] Campaign performance is evaluated over 14-30 day windows, not single days
- [ ] Daily performance fluctuations are expected and documented as normal variance
- [ ] Decision-making is based on rolling averages, not isolated data points
- [ ] Panic-driven changes are prohibited when a single day underperforms
- [ ] The team understands that consistent average results beat inconsistent spikes

## Section 2: Baseline Establishment
- [ ] A performance baseline (average CPA, ROAS, CTR) is established with 30+ days of data
- [ ] Acceptable variance range is defined (e.g., +/- 20% from baseline is normal)
- [ ] Seasonal patterns and cyclical trends are identified and accounted for
- [ ] Baseline is recalculated quarterly or after significant strategy changes
- [ ] Baseline metrics are documented and shared with all stakeholders

## Section 3: Sustainable Budget Management
- [ ] Budget is set at a level that can be sustained consistently (no boom-bust cycles)
- [ ] Budget increases are gradual and performance-validated (not aspirational)
- [ ] Budget is not cut reactively after a bad day or week
- [ ] Monthly budget pacing is tracked to prevent over/under-spending
- [ ] Reserve budget exists for opportunities without disrupting campaign consistency

## Section 4: Creative & Audience Sustainability
- [ ] Creative refresh cycle is proactive, not reactive (planned before fatigue hits)
- [ ] Audience pools are large enough to sustain campaign duration without saturation
- [ ] New audience and creative tests run alongside proven performers (never replace all at once)
- [ ] A "always-on" creative pipeline ensures fresh assets are available on schedule
- [ ] The ratio of proven to experimental campaigns maintains overall consistency (70/30 minimum)

## Section 5: Emotional Discipline
- [ ] Optimization decisions follow a documented protocol, not gut reactions
- [ ] Bad days do not trigger wholesale strategy changes
- [ ] Good days do not trigger premature scaling
- [ ] Data review cadence is consistent (same day, same time, same metrics)
- [ ] The team celebrates consistent performance, not just record-breaking days

## Section 6: Long-Term Growth Mindset
- [ ] Month-over-month improvement targets are modest and achievable (5-10% improvement)
- [ ] Annual performance trends are tracked to demonstrate compounding improvements
- [ ] Short-term sacrifices for long-term gains are discussed and documented
- [ ] Customer lifetime value is prioritized over immediate conversion metrics
- [ ] The philosophy of "medio consistente" is embedded in team culture and reporting

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/25 items (80%)
- **Reviewer**: sobral-br-traffic-strategist
- **Escalation**: If reactive decision-making overrides the consistent average philosophy, conduct a team alignment session before resuming optimization
