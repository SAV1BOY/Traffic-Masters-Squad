# Metodo Subido Lancamentos Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Pedro Sobral's Metodo Subido for launch traffic (lancamentos). Must pass before traffic strategy for any digital product launch is considered ready.

## Section 1: Pre-Lancamento (Pre-Launch Phase)
- [ ] Launch calendar is defined with all key dates (captacao, CPL/evento, carrinho aberto, carrinho fechado)
- [ ] Pixel is installed and tested on all launch pages (captura, obrigado, vendas, checkout)
- [ ] Pre-launch audiences are being built through content campaigns (video views, engajamento)
- [ ] Lead capture campaign is configured with optimized forms and landing pages
- [ ] Email/WhatsApp list from previous launches is loaded as custom audience
- [ ] Lookalike audiences are built from previous buyers and engaged leads

## Section 2: Fase de Captacao (Lead Capture Phase)
- [ ] Lead capture campaigns target cold audiences with broad interests and lookalikes
- [ ] Cost per lead (CPL) target is defined based on historical conversion rates
- [ ] Landing page conversion rate is above 30% for cold traffic (optimize if below)
- [ ] Multiple creative angles are tested (curiosity, authority, transformation, urgency)
- [ ] Budget ramp-up schedule follows the launch timeline (increase daily as launch approaches)
- [ ] Lead magnet or event registration offer is compelling and clearly communicated

## Section 3: Fase de Evento (Event/CPL Phase)
- [ ] Reminder campaigns ensure registered leads attend the live event or consume CPLs
- [ ] Engagement campaigns maintain excitement during the content delivery period
- [ ] Retargeting audiences are segmented by event attendance level (watched all, partial, no-show)
- [ ] Social proof from event engagement is captured and used in subsequent ads
- [ ] CTA during event drives to the sales page at the right moment

## Section 4: Fase de Carrinho (Cart Open Phase)
- [ ] Cart-open campaigns launch immediately when the offer goes live
- [ ] Retargeting audiences are tiered by intent level (event attendees > leads > engagers)
- [ ] Ad creative emphasizes urgency, bonuses, and transformation proof
- [ ] Countdown timers and scarcity elements are accurate and tested
- [ ] Cart abandoner retargeting activates within 1-6 hours of abandonment
- [ ] Budget peaks during cart-open phase (highest daily spend of the entire launch)

## Section 5: Pos-Lancamento (Post-Launch Phase)
- [ ] Post-launch analysis documents total spend, leads captured, sales, and overall ROAS
- [ ] Winning audiences and creatives are archived for future launch replication
- [ ] Lost leads receive nurture sequences for future launches or evergreen offers
- [ ] Pixel data is preserved and audiences are maintained for future campaigns
- [ ] Lessons learned are documented (what worked, what failed, what to test next time)

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 21/25 items (84%)
- **Reviewer**: sobral-br-traffic-strategist
- **Escalation**: If pre-launch audience building or pixel setup is incomplete, delay launch traffic activation until these foundations are ready
