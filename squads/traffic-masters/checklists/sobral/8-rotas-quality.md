# 8 Rotas de Aprendizado Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Pedro Sobral's 8 Rotas de Aprendizado (8 Learning Routes) framework for traffic specialization. Must pass before a traffic manager selects and commits to a specialization route.

## Section 1: Route Selection Prerequisites
- [ ] All 8 routes are understood at a conceptual level before choosing a specialization
- [ ] Self-assessment of strengths and interests has been completed honestly
- [ ] Market demand for each route in the target geography has been evaluated
- [ ] At least 2-3 routes have been tested with basic campaigns before committing
- [ ] A primary route is selected with a secondary route identified for future expansion

## Section 2: Rota 1 - Trafego para Lancamentos (Launch Traffic)
- [ ] Launch funnel structure is understood (captacao > evento > carrinho)
- [ ] Budget pacing for launch cycles is mastered (ramp-up, peak, wind-down)
- [ ] Pre-launch audience building techniques are practiced
- [ ] Launch-specific metrics are tracked (CPL, show-up rate, cart conversion rate)

## Section 3: Rota 2 - Trafego para Perpetuo (Evergreen Traffic)
- [ ] Always-on funnel management is understood (consistent daily leads/sales)
- [ ] Evergreen creative refresh cycles are established to prevent fatigue
- [ ] Long-term audience development strategy is documented
- [ ] Evergreen budget optimization maintains stable CPA over months

## Section 4: Rota 3 - Trafego para E-commerce (E-commerce Traffic)
- [ ] Product catalog advertising is mastered (Shopping, dynamic product ads)
- [ ] E-commerce funnel metrics are tracked (add to cart rate, checkout rate, AOV)
- [ ] Seasonal and promotional traffic strategies are planned
- [ ] Retargeting and cross-sell/upsell campaigns are structured

## Section 5: Rota 4 - Trafego para Negocio Local (Local Business Traffic)
- [ ] Local targeting features are mastered (radius, city, zip code targeting)
- [ ] Google Business Profile integration is optimized for local campaigns
- [ ] Lead generation for local businesses (calls, messages, directions) is configured
- [ ] Local market competitive analysis is performed

## Section 6: Additional Routes (5-8) Assessment
- [ ] Rota 5 (Trafego para Apps) fundamentals are understood if applicable
- [ ] Rota 6 (Trafego para Eventos) event promotion strategies are known
- [ ] Rota 7 (Trafego para Afiliados) affiliate traffic management basics are covered
- [ ] Rota 8 (Trafego para Servicos) service-based business lead gen is understood
- [ ] The selected route's unique challenges and opportunities are documented

## Section 7: Specialization Depth
- [ ] At least 3 clients or projects within the chosen route have been completed
- [ ] Route-specific case studies are documented with metrics and learnings
- [ ] Industry benchmarks for the chosen route are known and referenced
- [ ] A portfolio of results demonstrates competence in the chosen specialization
- [ ] Continuous education plan includes route-specific advanced training

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 18/22 items (82%)
- **Reviewer**: sobral-br-traffic-strategist
- **Escalation**: If route selection is made without testing multiple routes first, require a 2-week exploration period with small budgets across at least 3 routes
