# Estrutura de Campanha para Mercado BR Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Pedro Sobral's campaign structure optimized for the Brazilian market. Must pass before any campaign targeting the BR market is approved for launch.

## Section 1: Account & Business Manager Configuration
- [ ] Business Manager is configured with Brazilian business documentation (CNPJ)
- [ ] Ad account currency is set to BRL (Brazilian Real)
- [ ] Time zone is set to the correct Brazilian time zone (Brasilia, Manaus, etc.)
- [ ] Payment method is configured with a Brazilian payment option (credit card, boleto if available)
- [ ] Account spending limit is set as a safety measure

## Section 2: Campaign Structure (BR Best Practices)
- [ ] Campaigns are separated by objective aligned with the Brazilian customer journey
- [ ] Campaign naming follows a standardized Portuguese-language taxonomy
- [ ] Separate campaigns exist for prospeccao (prospecting) and remarketing
- [ ] Budget is allocated considering Brazilian CPM rates (typically lower than US/EU)
- [ ] Campaign schedule accounts for Brazilian time zones and consumption patterns

## Section 3: Audience Configuration (BR Market)
- [ ] Geographic targeting is precise (state-level or city-level, not just "Brazil")
- [ ] Regional differences in purchasing power are reflected in targeting (Sudeste vs. Norte/Nordeste)
- [ ] Portuguese-language interests and behaviors are used for targeting
- [ ] Brazilian influencers, media outlets, and brands are used as interest targets
- [ ] Lookalike audiences are built from Brazilian customer data specifically
- [ ] Age and demographic settings reflect Brazilian market realities

## Section 4: Creative & Copy (BR Cultural Alignment)
- [ ] All ad copy is in Brazilian Portuguese (not European Portuguese or translated from English)
- [ ] Cultural references, humor, and tone match Brazilian communication style
- [ ] Pricing is displayed in BRL with parcelamento (installment) options when applicable
- [ ] Visual creative features Brazilian people, locations, and cultural context
- [ ] Social proof includes Brazilian customer testimonials and results
- [ ] WhatsApp CTA is included where relevant (predominant messaging platform in Brazil)

## Section 5: Platform-Specific BR Considerations
- [ ] Instagram receives significant budget allocation (highest penetration in Brazil)
- [ ] WhatsApp Business integration is configured for lead capture campaigns
- [ ] Mobile-first creative is prioritized (Brazil is predominantly mobile)
- [ ] Video content accounts for lower bandwidth in some regions (shorter, compressed formats)
- [ ] Stories and Reels formats are emphasized (high engagement in BR market)

## Section 6: Compliance & Reporting
- [ ] Ads comply with Brazilian advertising regulations (CONAR guidelines)
- [ ] Consumer protection requirements are met (CDC - Codigo de Defesa do Consumidor)
- [ ] LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados) compliance is verified for data collection
- [ ] Reporting metrics are benchmarked against Brazilian market averages, not global
- [ ] Reports are generated in Portuguese for Brazilian clients and stakeholders

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 21/25 items (84%)
- **Reviewer**: sobral-br-traffic-strategist
- **Escalation**: If cultural alignment or compliance items fail, do not launch in the Brazilian market until these are corrected by a native BR market specialist
