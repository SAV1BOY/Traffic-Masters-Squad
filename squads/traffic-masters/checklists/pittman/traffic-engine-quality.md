# Traffic Engine 9-Step Methodology Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Molly Pittman's Traffic Engine 9-step methodology. Must pass before any paid traffic campaign launch is considered complete.

## Section 1: Foundation & Offer Clarity
- [ ] Business goal is clearly defined with specific KPIs (revenue target, CPA goal, ROAS target)
- [ ] The offer has been articulated in one sentence that a stranger could understand
- [ ] Offer value proposition passes the "So What?" test at least three times
- [ ] Landing page or destination URL is live, tested, and loads in under 3 seconds
- [ ] Conversion tracking pixels are installed and firing correctly on all key pages

## Section 2: Customer Avatar (Step 1-2)
- [ ] Primary customer avatar is fully developed with demographics, psychographics, and behavioral data
- [ ] At least 2-3 avatar variations have been identified for testing
- [ ] Pain points and desires are mapped to specific ad messaging angles
- [ ] Avatar's awareness level (unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, most-aware) is classified

## Section 3: Traffic Temperature Alignment (Step 3-4)
- [ ] Cold, warm, and hot traffic segments are defined with specific audience criteria
- [ ] Messaging is tailored to each traffic temperature (not one-size-fits-all)
- [ ] Cold traffic campaigns lead with value, not direct sales pitches
- [ ] Warm traffic retargeting audiences are built from engagement and site visitors
- [ ] Hot traffic campaigns include urgency and social proof elements

## Section 4: Ad Grid Construction (Step 5-6)
- [ ] Ad Grid is built with at least 3 hooks x 3 avatars minimum
- [ ] Each grid cell has a unique hook-avatar combination
- [ ] Ad copy variations cover emotional, logical, and fear-based angles
- [ ] Creative assets (images/video) are prepared for each grid cell
- [ ] Ad Grid spreadsheet is documented and shared with the team

## Section 5: Campaign Architecture (Step 7-8)
- [ ] Campaign objective aligns with the business goal (conversions, leads, traffic)
- [ ] Budget allocation follows the 70/20/10 rule (proven/testing/experimental)
- [ ] Ad sets are structured to isolate variables for clear performance attribution
- [ ] Naming conventions follow a consistent taxonomy for reporting

## Section 6: Optimization & Scale (Step 9)
- [ ] Success metrics and kill criteria are defined before launch
- [ ] Optimization schedule is set (when to check, what to adjust, thresholds for action)
- [ ] Scaling plan is documented (vertical scaling limits, horizontal expansion targets)
- [ ] Post-launch review cadence is established (24h, 72h, 7-day checkpoints)

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/25 items (80%)
- **Reviewer**: pittman-traffic-strategist
- **Escalation**: If fewer than 20 items pass, return to the failing section and rework before resubmission
