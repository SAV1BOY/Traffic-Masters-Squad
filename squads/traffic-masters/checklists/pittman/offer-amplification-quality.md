# Offer Amplification Strategy Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Molly Pittman's offer amplification strategy. Must pass before scaling spend on any validated offer.

## Section 1: Offer Validation Prerequisites
- [ ] The core offer has been tested with a minimum viable budget and shows positive signals
- [ ] Cost per acquisition (CPA) is at or below the target threshold for at least 72 hours
- [ ] The offer has achieved statistical significance (minimum 30-50 conversions in the test phase)
- [ ] Landing page conversion rate meets or exceeds industry benchmarks for the vertical
- [ ] Customer feedback on the offer is positive (no high refund rates or complaints)

## Section 2: Amplification via Audience Expansion
- [ ] New lookalike audiences are created from the best-performing seed lists (purchasers, high-value leads)
- [ ] Lookalike ranges are expanded incrementally (1% to 3%, then 3% to 5%)
- [ ] New interest-based audiences are tested to find fresh cold traffic pools
- [ ] International markets are evaluated for expansion if domestic results are strong
- [ ] Broad targeting (no interests, no lookalikes) is tested as a scaling lever

## Section 3: Amplification via Creative Expansion
- [ ] Winning hooks from the Ad Grid are expanded with new creative formats (static to video, carousel to UGC)
- [ ] New hook angles are developed from the Ad Grid to avoid creative fatigue
- [ ] Ad copy is refreshed with new social proof, testimonials, or updated stats
- [ ] Creative refresh cycle is scheduled (every 2-4 weeks depending on frequency metrics)

## Section 4: Amplification via Platform Expansion
- [ ] Primary platform results are documented as the baseline before expanding
- [ ] At least one secondary platform is identified for cross-platform amplification (e.g., Meta to Google, TikTok, YouTube)
- [ ] Messaging is adapted to the secondary platform's native style (not copy-pasted)
- [ ] Budget allocation across platforms follows a lead/support model (primary gets majority)

## Section 5: Amplification via Funnel Depth
- [ ] Upsell or cross-sell offers are added to increase average order value
- [ ] Email/SMS follow-up sequences are active for leads who do not convert immediately
- [ ] Retargeting sequences are expanded with new angles for non-converters
- [ ] Customer lifetime value (LTV) is factored into CPA targets to allow higher acquisition costs
- [ ] Referral or viral loops are considered to amplify organic reach alongside paid

## Section 6: Risk Management
- [ ] Daily spend increase does not exceed 20% to maintain algorithmic stability
- [ ] Performance is monitored at 24-hour intervals during scaling
- [ ] Rollback plan exists if CPA exceeds 1.5x target for more than 48 hours
- [ ] Account-level spend caps are in place to prevent budget overruns

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/25 items (80%)
- **Reviewer**: pittman-traffic-strategist
- **Escalation**: If offer validation prerequisites are not met, do not amplify; return to testing phase
