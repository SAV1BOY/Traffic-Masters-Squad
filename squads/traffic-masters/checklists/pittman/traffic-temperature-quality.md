# Traffic Temperature Classification Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Molly Pittman's traffic temperature classification system. Must pass before audience segmentation and campaign mapping is considered complete.

## Section 1: Cold Traffic Definition
- [ ] Cold audiences are defined as people with zero prior brand interaction
- [ ] Interest-based targeting parameters are documented with rationale for each interest
- [ ] Lookalike audiences are built from high-value seed lists (purchasers, high LTV customers)
- [ ] Lookalike percentage ranges are specified (1%, 1-3%, 3-5%) with strategic reasoning
- [ ] Cold traffic messaging leads with education, entertainment, or value (not a hard sell)
- [ ] Cold traffic offers are low-commitment (free content, lead magnet, quiz, blog post)

## Section 2: Warm Traffic Definition
- [ ] Warm audiences are segmented by engagement type (video viewers, page engagers, site visitors)
- [ ] Engagement windows are defined (7-day, 14-day, 30-day, 60-day, 180-day)
- [ ] Website visitor audiences exclude converters to avoid wasted spend
- [ ] Email list custom audiences are uploaded and matched (match rate verified)
- [ ] Warm traffic messaging acknowledges prior interaction and deepens the relationship
- [ ] Warm traffic offers introduce the core product or tripwire offer

## Section 3: Hot Traffic Definition
- [ ] Hot audiences include cart abandoners, pricing page visitors, and past purchasers
- [ ] Recency windows for hot audiences are tight (1-7 days for cart abandoners)
- [ ] Past purchaser audiences are segmented by product line for cross-sell/upsell
- [ ] Hot traffic messaging uses urgency, scarcity, and social proof
- [ ] Hot traffic offers are direct (buy now, limited time, exclusive deal)

## Section 4: Temperature-to-Funnel Mapping
- [ ] Each temperature tier maps to a specific funnel stage (TOFU, MOFU, BOFU)
- [ ] Budget allocation across temperatures follows strategy (e.g., 60% cold, 25% warm, 15% hot)
- [ ] Frequency caps are set per temperature to avoid ad fatigue
- [ ] Exclusion audiences prevent overlap between temperature tiers
- [ ] Audience flow is documented: cold feeds warm, warm feeds hot

## Section 5: Measurement & Progression
- [ ] KPIs are defined per temperature (cold = CPM/CTR, warm = CPC/CPL, hot = CPA/ROAS)
- [ ] Audience progression tracking is in place (how many cold contacts become warm)
- [ ] Retargeting pool sizes are monitored to ensure warm/hot audiences are growing
- [ ] Temperature classification is reviewed and updated at least bi-weekly

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/25 items (80%)
- **Reviewer**: pittman-traffic-strategist
- **Escalation**: If temperature tiers are poorly defined or overlapping, pause campaign setup and reclassify all audiences
