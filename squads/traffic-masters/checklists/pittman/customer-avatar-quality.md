# Customer Avatar Development Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Molly Pittman's customer avatar development process. Must pass before avatar is used in Ad Grid construction or campaign targeting.

## Section 1: Demographic Foundation
- [ ] Age range is specified (not just "adults" but a meaningful bracket like 28-42)
- [ ] Gender distribution is defined with primary and secondary targets
- [ ] Geographic targeting is documented (countries, regions, cities, or radius)
- [ ] Income level or economic bracket is identified
- [ ] Education level and professional background are noted

## Section 2: Psychographic Depth
- [ ] Core values and beliefs are articulated (what does this person stand for?)
- [ ] Daily routine and lifestyle patterns are described
- [ ] Media consumption habits are documented (podcasts, YouTube channels, blogs, influencers)
- [ ] Brand affinities are listed (what brands does this person already love?)
- [ ] Self-identity statements are written ("I am the kind of person who...")

## Section 3: Pain Points & Desires
- [ ] Top 3 pain points are listed in the avatar's own language (from real research)
- [ ] Top 3 desires/goals are listed in the avatar's own language
- [ ] The emotional state before and after using the product/service is described
- [ ] Objections and hesitations to purchasing are documented (minimum 5)
- [ ] The "trigger event" that makes this person start searching for a solution is identified

## Section 4: Behavioral Signals
- [ ] Online behavior patterns are mapped (search queries, social media behavior, content engagement)
- [ ] Purchase behavior is documented (impulse vs. research-heavy, price-sensitive vs. value-driven)
- [ ] Device usage preferences are noted (mobile-first, desktop, tablet)
- [ ] Time-of-day and day-of-week activity patterns are estimated

## Section 5: Validation & Source Quality
- [ ] Avatar is built from actual customer data (surveys, interviews, reviews), not guesswork
- [ ] At least 3 real customer quotes are included as voice-of-customer references
- [ ] Avatar has been cross-referenced with analytics data (GA4, CRM, social insights)
- [ ] Avatar document includes a named persona with a photo for team alignment
- [ ] Avatar has been reviewed by someone with direct customer interaction (sales, support)

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/24 items (79%)
- **Reviewer**: pittman-traffic-strategist
- **Escalation**: If avatar lacks real customer data or relies entirely on assumptions, halt and conduct primary research before proceeding
