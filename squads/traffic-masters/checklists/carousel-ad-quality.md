# Carousel Ad Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Creative Production
> **Reviewed by**: Creative Strategist

## Purpose
Ensures every carousel ad tells a cohesive story across cards while each card also functions independently. Carousels that lack flow or clarity waste swipes and ad spend.

## Checklist

### Story Flow Across Cards
- [ ] The carousel tells a complete narrative from first card to last
- [ ] Each card builds logically on the previous one
- [ ] Transitions between cards feel natural and maintain momentum
- [ ] The story arc follows a clear structure (hook, problem, solution, proof, CTA)
- [ ] Visual continuity elements connect the cards (color, layout, design motifs)
- [ ] The sequence rewards the user for swiping through all cards

### Individual Card Strength
- [ ] Each card can be understood independently without seeing the others
- [ ] Each card delivers a complete thought or message
- [ ] Cards that appear in preview (first 1-3) are the strongest performers
- [ ] No card feels like filler or padding
- [ ] Each card provides a reason to continue swiping

### CTA Integration
- [ ] Every card includes a call-to-action element (visual or text)
- [ ] CTAs escalate in urgency or specificity across the carousel
- [ ] CTA is visible and does not compete with the card content
- [ ] CTA language is consistent with the offer and funnel destination
- [ ] The final card CTA is the strongest and most direct

### First Card Impact
- [ ] The first card is the strongest hook in the sequence
- [ ] The first card creates curiosity or urgency to swipe
- [ ] The first card clearly communicates the topic or benefit
- [ ] The first card works as a standalone image ad if users do not swipe
- [ ] Text on the first card is minimal and impactful

### Last Card CTA
- [ ] The last card delivers a clear, compelling call-to-action
- [ ] The last card summarizes the key benefit or offer
- [ ] The last card provides a reason to click now
- [ ] The last card includes the brand or product identifier
- [ ] The last card feels like a satisfying conclusion to the story

### Visual Consistency
- [ ] Color palette is consistent across all cards
- [ ] Font choices and sizes are uniform throughout the carousel
- [ ] Image style or illustration style is consistent
- [ ] Layout structure is cohesive (not visually jarring between cards)
- [ ] Brand elements appear consistently without being obtrusive
- [ ] Visual quality is high across all cards (no pixelation or low-res images)

### Mobile Text Readability
- [ ] All text is legible on mobile screens without zooming
- [ ] Font size is a minimum of 24px for headlines on card images
- [ ] Text contrast against backgrounds meets accessibility standards
- [ ] Text does not extend into platform UI overlap zones
- [ ] Amount of text per card is limited to prevent cognitive overload
- [ ] Primary headline text within the card uses six words or fewer

## Pass/Fail Criteria
A minimum of 6 out of 7 sections must pass. First Card Impact and CTA Integration are mandatory and cannot be the failing section.

## If Failed
Identify weak cards and revise. Test first-card variants as they determine swipe rate. Ensure CTA clarity before relaunching.
