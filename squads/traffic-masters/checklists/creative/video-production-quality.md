# Video Ad Production Quality Gate

> Quality gate for video ad production quality across all platforms. Must pass before video assets are approved for campaign use.

## Section 1: Technical Specifications
- [ ] Video resolution is at least 1080p (1920x1080 for landscape, 1080x1920 for vertical, 1080x1080 for square)
- [ ] Frame rate is 30fps minimum (24fps acceptable for cinematic style)
- [ ] Video codec is H.264 with AAC audio (MP4 container preferred)
- [ ] File size is optimized per platform: under 4GB for Meta/TikTok, under 256GB for YouTube
- [ ] Bitrate is appropriate for the resolution (8-12 Mbps for 1080p)
- [ ] All required aspect ratios are delivered: 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 as specified in the brief

## Section 2: Audio Quality
- [ ] Voiceover is recorded professionally with no background noise, echo, or distortion
- [ ] Music is properly licensed (royalty-free, platform music library, or purchased commercial license)
- [ ] Audio levels are balanced: voiceover is clearly audible over background music (-6dB to -3dB for voice, -18dB to -12dB for music)
- [ ] Audio does not clip or distort at any point in the video
- [ ] Sound design (effects, transitions) enhances the viewing experience without being distracting

## Section 3: Visual Quality
- [ ] Lighting is professional and consistent throughout the video
- [ ] Color grading is applied consistently and matches the brand aesthetic
- [ ] No unintentional camera shake or focus issues
- [ ] Transitions are smooth and intentional (not jarring or random)
- [ ] On-screen text is legible on mobile screens (minimum 24pt equivalent, high contrast)

## Section 4: Subtitles and Accessibility
- [ ] Subtitles/captions are included for all spoken content (mandatory for social media where 80%+ watch without sound)
- [ ] Subtitles are accurately transcribed with correct spelling and punctuation
- [ ] Subtitle timing is synchronized with speech (no more than 0.5s offset)
- [ ] Subtitle font is legible against all backgrounds (use text shadow or background box)
- [ ] Subtitles are provided in the target market language (localized if serving multiple markets)

## Section 5: Content and Pacing
- [ ] Video opens with a strong hook in the first 2-3 seconds
- [ ] Pacing matches the platform: fast cuts for TikTok/Reels (1-2s per scene), moderate for YouTube, professional for LinkedIn
- [ ] Key message is delivered within the first 15 seconds
- [ ] CTA appears at least twice (mid-video and end) with clear visual and verbal direction
- [ ] End frame includes brand logo, CTA, and contact/URL information

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 21/25 items (84%)
- **Reviewer**: creative-strategist-agent
- **Escalation**: Audio quality failures, missing subtitles, or resolution below 1080p are hard blocks; video must be re-produced or re-edited before deployment
