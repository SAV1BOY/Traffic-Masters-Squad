# Creative Brief to Production Handoff Quality Gate

> Quality gate for creative brief completeness and production handoff readiness. Must pass before the brief is sent to the design/production team.

## Section 1: Brief Completeness
- [ ] Campaign objective is clearly stated with measurable KPIs (e.g., "Drive 500 leads at $25 CPA")
- [ ] Target audience is described with demographics, psychographics, pain points, and motivations
- [ ] Key message hierarchy is defined: primary message, secondary messages, supporting proof points
- [ ] Tone and voice guidelines are specified (e.g., professional, friendly, urgent, aspirational)
- [ ] Mandatory brand elements are listed: logo usage, color palette, fonts, tagline, legal disclaimers

## Section 2: Deliverables Specification
- [ ] All required ad formats and sizes are listed with exact pixel dimensions per platform
- [ ] Number of creative variants needed is specified (e.g., 3 static images + 2 videos per ad set)
- [ ] File format requirements are documented: JPG/PNG for images, MP4/MOV for video, GIF if applicable
- [ ] Maximum file size constraints per platform are noted in the brief
- [ ] Aspect ratios needed are listed: 1:1 (Feed), 4:5 (Feed vertical), 9:16 (Stories/Reels/Shorts), 16:9 (YouTube/Display)
- [ ] Safe zone specifications are included for platforms that overlay UI elements (TikTok, Stories)

## Section 3: Content and Messaging
- [ ] Headline options are provided or requested (number of variants specified)
- [ ] Primary text / body copy is drafted or a copy brief is included
- [ ] CTA options are specified and matched to each platform's available buttons
- [ ] Value proposition is clearly articulated and differentiated from competitors
- [ ] Content references or mood board are included for visual direction

## Section 4: Production Timeline
- [ ] Brief delivery date to production team is documented
- [ ] First draft due date is set (minimum 5 business days from brief delivery for complex assets)
- [ ] Review and feedback round dates are scheduled (allow 2 rounds of revision)
- [ ] Final asset delivery deadline is at least 3 business days before campaign launch
- [ ] Stakeholder approval workflow is documented (who approves at each stage)

## Section 5: Handoff Quality
- [ ] Brief is shared in the approved format (template, project management tool, or shared document)
- [ ] Reference assets (existing ads, competitor examples, inspiration) are attached
- [ ] Platform-specific requirements that affect creative decisions are highlighted (e.g., TikTok native style vs. LinkedIn professional)
- [ ] Contact person for creative questions is identified with response time SLA
- [ ] Production team has confirmed receipt and understanding of the brief (kickoff meeting or acknowledgment)

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/24 items (83%)
- **Reviewer**: creative-strategist-agent
- **Escalation**: Incomplete briefs (missing deliverable specs or timeline) are returned to the requestor; production does not begin until the brief is complete
