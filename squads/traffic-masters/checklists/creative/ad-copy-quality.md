# Ad Copy Quality Gate

> Quality gate for ad copy across all platforms (headlines, primary text, descriptions, CTAs). Must pass before copy is finalized in ad creatives.

## Section 1: Headline Quality
- [ ] Headlines are benefit-driven (what the user gains) rather than feature-driven (what the product does)
- [ ] Headlines respect platform character limits: Meta (40 chars visible), Google RSA (30 chars), LinkedIn (70 chars)
- [ ] At least 3 headline variants are written per ad group/ad set for testing
- [ ] Headlines include power words that drive action: "Free", "New", "Proven", "Exclusive", "Limited"
- [ ] Headlines are scannable and understandable in under 2 seconds

## Section 2: Primary Text / Body Copy
- [ ] Opening line hooks the reader with a question, bold statement, statistic, or pain point
- [ ] Copy addresses the target audience's specific problem before presenting the solution
- [ ] Copy follows the AIDA framework: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action (or similar proven structure)
- [ ] Sentences are short and punchy (15-20 words max per sentence for ad copy)
- [ ] Copy uses "you" language (second person) to directly address the reader
- [ ] Platform-specific text limits are respected: Meta (125 chars visible; 2,200 max), LinkedIn (150 chars visible; 600 max)

## Section 3: Call-to-Action (CTA)
- [ ] CTA is specific and action-oriented: "Get Your Free Trial", "Download the Guide", "Book a Demo" (not "Click Here" or "Learn More" without context)
- [ ] CTA matches the campaign objective and landing page action
- [ ] CTA creates urgency or scarcity when appropriate: "Limited Time", "Ends Friday", "Only 50 Spots"
- [ ] CTA button selection matches the platform's available options and aligns with the ad copy
- [ ] Only one primary CTA per ad (avoid competing actions)

## Section 4: Tone, Voice, and Brand Alignment
- [ ] Copy tone matches the brand voice guidelines (formal, casual, humorous, authoritative)
- [ ] Copy is consistent across all ads within the same campaign (unified messaging)
- [ ] Industry jargon is used only when the target audience is professionals in that field
- [ ] Copy avoids cliches, buzzwords, and filler phrases that add no value
- [ ] Emotional triggers are appropriate for the audience and product (trust for finance, excitement for retail, etc.)

## Section 5: Compliance and Proofreading
- [ ] All copy has been proofread by a second person for grammar, spelling, and punctuation
- [ ] No platform policy violations: no misleading claims, no before/after implications (Meta), no superlatives without proof
- [ ] Legal disclaimers are included where required (financial products, health claims, promotions with terms)
- [ ] Dynamic keyword insertion or personalization tokens are tested for edge cases (long names, special characters)
- [ ] Copy is localized correctly for the target market (Brazilian Portuguese for BR, not European Portuguese)

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/24 items (83%)
- **Reviewer**: creative-strategist-agent + account-manager-agent
- **Escalation**: Policy violations or brand misalignment are hard blocks; copy must be revised before creative production proceeds
