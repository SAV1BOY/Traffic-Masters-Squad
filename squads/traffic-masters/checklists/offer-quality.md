# Offer Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Offer Design
> **Reviewed by**: Traffic Strategist

## Purpose
Validates that the offer is compelling, credible, and conversion-ready before it enters any funnel or campaign. A weak offer cannot be saved by great media buying.

## Checklist

### Promise Clarity
- [ ] The core promise is stated in one sentence a fifth-grader could understand
- [ ] The promise addresses a specific, urgent pain or desire of the ICP
- [ ] The outcome is concrete and measurable (not vague or aspirational)
- [ ] The promise is believable given the product and market context

### Mechanism
- [ ] The unique mechanism (how the result is delivered) is clearly explained
- [ ] The mechanism differentiates this offer from competitors
- [ ] The mechanism is named or branded for memorability
- [ ] Technical or process details are simplified for the audience

### Proof Stack
- [ ] At least three distinct types of proof are included (testimonials, data, case studies, credentials, demos)
- [ ] Proof directly supports the core promise
- [ ] Proof is specific with numbers, names, and timeframes where possible
- [ ] Proof sources are verifiable and authentic

### Guarantee
- [ ] A guarantee is present and clearly stated
- [ ] The guarantee reduces or reverses perceived risk
- [ ] Guarantee terms and conditions are transparent
- [ ] The guarantee duration is appropriate for the product

### Price Justification
- [ ] Price is anchored against a higher reference point
- [ ] Value-to-price ratio is explicitly demonstrated
- [ ] Price is framed in relatable terms (per day, per result, vs alternative)
- [ ] Payment options or plans are available if appropriate

### Bonuses
- [ ] Bonuses enhance and complement the core offer
- [ ] Each bonus has a stated value
- [ ] Bonuses address secondary objections or desires
- [ ] Bonuses create a sense of added value without diluting the core offer

### Scarcity and Urgency
- [ ] Scarcity element is real and verifiable (not fabricated)
- [ ] Urgency driver has a legitimate reason (deadline, capacity, event)
- [ ] Scarcity or urgency is communicated without being manipulative

### Call to Action
- [ ] CTA is clear, specific, and tells the prospect exactly what to do next
- [ ] CTA language matches the commitment level of the offer
- [ ] CTA is visually prominent and repeated at key decision points

## Pass/Fail Criteria
A minimum of 7 out of 8 sections must pass. The Promise Clarity section is mandatory and cannot be the failing section.

## If Failed
Identify which sections are weak. Revise the offer with the strategist and copywriter. Re-evaluate before any creative production begins.
