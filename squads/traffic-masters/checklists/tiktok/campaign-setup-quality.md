# TikTok Campaign Setup Quality Gate

> Quality gate for TikTok campaign setup and structure. Must pass before the campaign is enabled in TikTok Ads Manager.

## Section 1: Campaign Configuration
- [ ] Advertising objective matches the business goal: Awareness (Reach, Video Views), Consideration (Traffic, App Install), Conversion (Website Conversions, Lead Generation, Product Sales)
- [ ] Campaign budget optimization (CBO) vs. ad group budget is selected with documented rationale
- [ ] Campaign budget aligns with the approved media plan (minimum $50/day per ad group recommended for learning)
- [ ] Campaign name follows naming convention: [Client]_[Objective]_[Funnel]_[Date]_[Version]
- [ ] A/B test is configured if testing audiences, creatives, or placements

## Section 2: Ad Group Settings
- [ ] Placement selection is intentional: TikTok only, or automatic placement (Pangle, News Feed apps)
- [ ] Pangle (TikTok Audience Network) is excluded unless specifically approved for reach extension
- [ ] Targeting includes: location, age (13+ minimum; 18+ for most advertisers), gender, language, and interests
- [ ] Interest and behavior targeting categories are relevant to the product/audience
- [ ] Custom audiences (website traffic, engagement, customer file) are applied for retargeting campaigns
- [ ] Lookalike audiences are based on high-value seed audiences with appropriate expansion level (Narrow, Balanced, Broad)

## Section 3: Bidding and Optimization
- [ ] Optimization goal is correct: Click, Conversion, Value, or Video View depending on objective
- [ ] Bid strategy matches campaign maturity: Cost Cap for controlled spend, Maximum Delivery for volume
- [ ] Target CPA is set based on historical data or realistic industry benchmarks
- [ ] Learning phase requirements are understood: 50 conversions within 7 days per ad group
- [ ] Daily budget per ad group is at least 20x the target CPA to support the learning phase

## Section 4: Schedule and Delivery
- [ ] Ad group start and end dates match the campaign flight plan
- [ ] Dayparting is configured if audience activity data supports specific time windows
- [ ] Delivery type (Standard vs. Accelerated) is selected with documentation
- [ ] Frequency capping is considered for awareness campaigns (TikTok manages this less granularly)

## Section 5: Compliance and Pre-Launch
- [ ] All ad content complies with TikTok's Advertising Policies (no prohibited industries or content)
- [ ] Ad account is in good standing with no active policy violations
- [ ] Landing page URL is live, mobile-optimized, and loads in under 3 seconds
- [ ] UTM parameters are appended to all destination URLs
- [ ] Campaign has been reviewed by a second team member before activation

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/23 items (83%)
- **Reviewer**: media-buyer-agent
- **Escalation**: Policy violations or insufficient budget for learning phase are hard blocks; must be resolved before launch
