# TikTok Creative Quality Gate

> Quality gate for TikTok ad creative quality, native feel, and trend alignment. Must pass before creatives are uploaded to TikTok Ads Manager.

## Section 1: Native Feel and Authenticity
- [ ] Creative looks and feels like organic TikTok content (not a polished TV commercial)
- [ ] Video uses vertical format (9:16) at 1080x1920px resolution
- [ ] Creator or presenter speaks directly to camera (talking-head style preferred for authenticity)
- [ ] Content uses native TikTok features: text overlays, stickers, transitions, trending sounds
- [ ] Production style matches the target audience (Gen Z = raw/authentic; Millennials = slightly polished)

## Section 2: Hook and Engagement (First 3 Seconds)
- [ ] First frame is visually arresting (pattern interrupt, bold text, unexpected action)
- [ ] Hook is delivered within the first 1-3 seconds (TikTok users decide to skip faster than other platforms)
- [ ] Opening line or text creates curiosity, urgency, or relatability ("POV:", "Wait for it", "Things I wish I knew")
- [ ] No brand logo or title card in the opening (breaks the native feel; save branding for mid/end)
- [ ] Sound/music starts immediately and matches the energy of the content

## Section 3: Content Structure and Storytelling
- [ ] Video follows a fast-paced structure: Hook > Value/Entertainment > CTA (no slow builds)
- [ ] Video duration is optimized: 15-30 seconds for awareness, 30-60 seconds for consideration/conversion
- [ ] One clear message per video (avoid cramming multiple value propositions)
- [ ] Text overlays reinforce key points (many viewers watch without sound or scan visually)
- [ ] Transitions are dynamic and maintain viewer attention throughout

## Section 4: Trend and Sound Alignment
- [ ] Trending sounds or music are incorporated when relevant and properly licensed via TikTok Commercial Music Library
- [ ] Creative leverages current TikTok trends or formats (challenges, duets, stitches, memes) when brand-appropriate
- [ ] Hashtags in ad copy are relevant and not excessive (2-3 max)
- [ ] Creator-driven content (UGC-style or actual creator partnerships) is prioritized over brand-produced content

## Section 5: Technical and Compliance Requirements
- [ ] Video resolution is 720p minimum (1080p recommended)
- [ ] File size is under 500MB; format is MP4 or MOV
- [ ] Safe zones are respected: top 20% (username area) and bottom 20% (CTA/description area) kept clear of key content
- [ ] CTA is clear and integrated naturally (not a forced sales pitch)
- [ ] Content complies with TikTok Advertising Policies (no misleading claims, prohibited content, or clickbait)
- [ ] At least 3-5 creative variants are prepared per ad group (TikTok creative fatigue is faster than other platforms)

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/23 items (83%)
- **Reviewer**: creative-strategist-agent
- **Escalation**: Creatives that look like traditional ads (non-native) or violate safe zones must be reworked before launch
