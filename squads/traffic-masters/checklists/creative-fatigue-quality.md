# Creative Fatigue Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Creative Management
> **Reviewed by**: Creative Strategist

## Purpose
Ensures creative fatigue is detected early and managed proactively so campaign performance does not degrade from overexposed ads. Stale creative is one of the top causes of rising costs.

## Checklist

### Frequency Monitoring
- [ ] Ad frequency is tracked at the ad set and ad level
- [ ] Frequency thresholds are defined per platform (e.g., 2.5 for Meta prospecting)
- [ ] Frequency is segmented by audience tier (TOF vs retargeting)
- [ ] High-frequency alerts are configured and monitored
- [ ] Frequency trends are included in weekly reporting

### CTR Trend Tracking
- [ ] Click-through rate is tracked daily for all active ads
- [ ] A declining CTR trend over 3-5 consecutive days triggers a review
- [ ] CTR benchmarks are established per creative type and audience
- [ ] CTR decline is cross-referenced with frequency to confirm fatigue
- [ ] Historical CTR patterns are used to predict fatigue onset

### Hook Rate Trend
- [ ] Hook rate (3-second video views / impressions) is tracked for video ads
- [ ] Declining hook rate is an early indicator of creative fatigue
- [ ] Hook rate benchmarks are established per video format and placement
- [ ] Hook rate decline precedes CTR decline and is used as an early warning
- [ ] Hook rate data informs which creative elements need refreshing first

### Rotation Schedule
- [ ] A creative rotation schedule is defined and documented
- [ ] New creatives are introduced before fatigue indicators reach critical levels
- [ ] Rotation cadence is adjusted based on spend level and audience size
- [ ] A/B rotation between proven and new creatives maintains stability
- [ ] Rotation decisions are logged with dates and performance context

### Refresh Cadence
- [ ] Creative refresh cadence is defined (e.g., every 2-4 weeks at scale)
- [ ] Refresh cadence is faster for high-spend campaigns
- [ ] Refresh includes variation of hooks, visuals, and copy elements
- [ ] Full creative overhauls are scheduled quarterly or as needed
- [ ] Refresh cadence is reviewed monthly based on fatigue data

### Variation Backlog
- [ ] A minimum of 3 ready-to-launch creative variations are always available
- [ ] Backlog includes different hooks, formats, and visual styles
- [ ] Creative production pipeline has adequate lead time built in
- [ ] Backlog status is reviewed weekly in team meetings
- [ ] Emergency creative production process exists for unexpected fatigue

### Retirement Criteria
- [ ] Criteria for retiring a creative are defined (CTR below X, frequency above Y)
- [ ] Retired creatives are documented with performance history
- [ ] Retired creatives are archived for potential future revival or iteration
- [ ] Retirement decisions are data-driven and not based on subjective opinion
- [ ] Top-performing retired creatives are analyzed for learnable patterns

## Pass/Fail Criteria
A minimum of 6 out of 7 sections must pass. Frequency Monitoring and Variation Backlog are mandatory and cannot be the failing section.

## If Failed
Audit current creative health across all active campaigns. Prioritize production of new variations. Reduce spend on fatigued creatives immediately.
