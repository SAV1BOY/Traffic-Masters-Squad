# Meta Creative Specifications Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Meta ad creative specifications (sizes, aspect ratios, text limits, file requirements). Must pass before creatives are uploaded to Ads Manager.

## Section 1: Image Ad Specifications
- [ ] Feed images are 1080x1080px (1:1) or 1200x628px (1.91:1) at minimum
- [ ] Stories/Reels images are 1080x1920px (9:16) full-screen vertical format
- [ ] Image file size is under 30MB and uses JPG or PNG format
- [ ] Images contain less than 20% text overlay (checked via Meta's text overlay guidelines)
- [ ] No cropped or pixelated visuals; all images are high-resolution and properly framed

## Section 2: Video Ad Specifications
- [ ] Feed videos meet minimum resolution of 1080x1080px (1:1) or 1080x1350px (4:5)
- [ ] Stories/Reels videos are 1080x1920px (9:16) with safe zones respected (top 14% and bottom 20% clear of key content)
- [ ] Video file format is MP4 or MOV with H.264 compression
- [ ] Video file size is under 4GB; duration is between 5-60 seconds (15s or less recommended for Reels)
- [ ] Video includes captions/subtitles for sound-off viewing (85% of feed video is watched without sound)
- [ ] Thumbnail is custom-selected (not auto-generated) and visually compelling

## Section 3: Text and Copy Limits
- [ ] Primary text is under 125 characters for optimal display (max 2,200; truncation occurs at ~125)
- [ ] Headline is under 40 characters (max 255; truncation on mobile at ~40)
- [ ] Description is under 30 characters (max 255; often hidden on mobile)
- [ ] CTA button selection matches the campaign objective (Shop Now, Learn More, Sign Up, etc.)
- [ ] No use of prohibited language (misleading claims, all-caps abuse, excessive punctuation)

## Section 4: Placement-Specific Compliance
- [ ] Creatives are reviewed in all active placements using the Ads Manager preview tool
- [ ] Carousel ads have 2-10 cards, each meeting individual image/video specs
- [ ] Collection ads have a cover image/video plus at least 4 product images
- [ ] Instant Experience (Canvas) loads correctly and all links function
- [ ] Advantage+ placements asset customization is configured per placement group (Feed, Stories, Reels, Right Column)

## Section 5: Brand and Policy Compliance
- [ ] All creatives comply with Meta Advertising Standards (no prohibited content)
- [ ] Brand logos, colors, and fonts are consistent with the brand guidelines document
- [ ] Landing page URL matches the ad content (no bait-and-switch)
- [ ] Disclaimers and legal text are included where required (financial, health, alcohol)

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/24 items (83%)
- **Reviewer**: creative-strategist-agent
- **Escalation**: Any policy compliance failure is a hard block; creative must be revised and re-submitted
