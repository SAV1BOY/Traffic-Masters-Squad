# Cross-Platform Consistency Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Multi-Channel Strategy
> **Reviewed by**: Traffic Strategist

## Purpose
Ensures messaging, branding, tracking, and audience management are consistent and coordinated across all advertising platforms. Inconsistency confuses prospects, inflates costs, and corrupts data.

## Checklist

### Messaging Consistency
- [ ] Core value proposition is identical in meaning across all platforms
- [ ] Key claims and promises do not contradict between platforms
- [ ] Messaging is adapted for platform tone without changing substance
- [ ] Promotional offers and prices are identical across all active platforms
- [ ] Call-to-action language is consistent in intent across platforms
- [ ] Messaging updates are deployed simultaneously across all channels
- [ ] Platform-specific character limits are respected without losing core message

### Visual Identity Consistency
- [ ] Brand colors are consistent across all platform creatives
- [ ] Logo usage follows brand guidelines on every platform
- [ ] Typography is consistent or appropriately adapted per platform specs
- [ ] Image style and photography direction are cohesive
- [ ] Video production quality is consistent across platforms
- [ ] Creative templates maintain brand identity while respecting platform norms
- [ ] Ad formats unique to each platform still feel like the same brand

### Offer Alignment
- [ ] The same offer is presented consistently across all platforms
- [ ] Pricing and discount structures match across all touchpoints
- [ ] Offer expiration dates and scarcity elements are synchronized
- [ ] Landing page destinations are consistent regardless of traffic source
- [ ] Upsell and cross-sell offers do not conflict across platforms
- [ ] Promotional calendars are aligned across all channels

### Unified Tracking (UTMs)
- [ ] UTM naming conventions are standardized across all platforms
- [ ] utm_source correctly identifies each platform
- [ ] utm_medium is consistent for similar traffic types across platforms
- [ ] utm_campaign naming enables cross-platform campaign grouping
- [ ] UTM parameters are validated before launch on each platform
- [ ] A master UTM document is maintained and shared with all team members

### Attribution Reconciliation
- [ ] Cross-platform attribution overlap is identified and quantified
- [ ] Platform-reported conversions are reconciled against actual totals
- [ ] A blended view of performance is maintained alongside platform-specific data
- [ ] Assisted conversions and cross-platform paths are analyzed
- [ ] Attribution discrepancies are documented with explanations

### Audience Deduplication
- [ ] Audience lists are compared across platforms to identify overlap
- [ ] Retargeting audiences are coordinated to prevent excessive cross-platform frequency
- [ ] Suppression lists (converters, unsubscribes) are applied across all platforms
- [ ] Lookalike or similar audience seeds are consistent where applicable
- [ ] Total user exposure across all platforms is estimated and monitored
- [ ] Platform-specific audience strategies complement rather than compete with each other

## Pass/Fail Criteria
A minimum of 5 out of 6 sections must pass. Unified Tracking is mandatory and cannot be the failing section.

## If Failed
Audit all active platforms for the failing areas. Align teams and update shared documentation. Implement fixes across all platforms simultaneously to restore consistency.
