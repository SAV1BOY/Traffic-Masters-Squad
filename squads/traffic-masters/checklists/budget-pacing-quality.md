# Budget Pacing Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Financial Management
> **Reviewed by**: Media Buyer

## Purpose
Ensures budget pacing is monitored, controlled, and aligned with targets across all active campaigns. Unchecked pacing leads to overspend, underspend, or missed performance targets.

## Checklist

### Daily Targets
- [ ] Daily spend target is calculated from the monthly budget
- [ ] Daily targets account for expected performance fluctuations by day of week
- [ ] Daily spend is monitored by midday and end of day
- [ ] Daily targets are documented in the pacing tracker
- [ ] Weekend vs weekday pacing differences are anticipated and planned

### Weekly Targets
- [ ] Weekly spend target is set as a checkpoint against the monthly plan
- [ ] Week-over-week pacing is compared every Monday
- [ ] Weekly variance from target is calculated and documented
- [ ] Corrective actions are taken if weekly pacing deviates by more than 10%
- [ ] Weekly targets allow for ramp-up periods on new campaigns

### Monthly Targets
- [ ] Monthly budget is confirmed and approved before the month begins
- [ ] Month-to-date spend is tracked against the prorated target
- [ ] End-of-month projection is updated weekly
- [ ] Monthly budget includes contingency for testing and opportunistic spend

### Alert Thresholds
- [ ] Alert is configured when spend reaches 80% of period budget
- [ ] Alert is configured when spend reaches 100% of period budget
- [ ] Alerts are sent to the media buyer and account manager
- [ ] Alert response protocol is documented and followed
- [ ] Platform-level spending limits are set as a safety backstop

### Reallocation Triggers
- [ ] Criteria for reallocating budget between campaigns are defined
- [ ] Reallocation requires documented justification
- [ ] Minimum performance period before reallocation is established (typically 72 hours)
- [ ] Reallocation amounts follow incremental rules (no more than 20% at a time)
- [ ] Reallocation decisions are logged with before and after figures

### Underspend Plan
- [ ] Root causes for underspend are investigated promptly
- [ ] Underspend reallocation options are predefined (other campaigns, channels, savings)
- [ ] Audience expansion or bid increase protocols exist for delivery issues
- [ ] Underspend is reported to stakeholders with explanation and plan
- [ ] Chronic underspend triggers a strategy or audience review

### Overspend Protocol
- [ ] Immediate action steps for overspend are documented
- [ ] Campaign or ad set pause criteria for overspend are defined
- [ ] Overspend root cause analysis is conducted within 24 hours
- [ ] Financial impact of overspend is reported to stakeholders
- [ ] Preventive measures are implemented after any overspend incident

### Billing Verification
- [ ] Ad platform invoices are reviewed monthly against tracked spend
- [ ] Discrepancies between platform reporting and invoices are investigated
- [ ] Payment methods and billing thresholds are current and correct
- [ ] Tax and currency conversion impacts are accounted for

## Pass/Fail Criteria
All checklist items must be in place before campaign activation and maintained throughout the campaign lifecycle.

## If Failed
Immediately review budget controls and alert configurations. Implement missing safeguards within 24 hours. Escalate any active overspend to the account manager.
