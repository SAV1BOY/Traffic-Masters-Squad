# 4Ms Framework Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Nicholas Kusmich's 4Ms framework (Market, Message, Magnet, Mechanism). Must pass before any campaign strategy is approved.

## Section 1: Market (M1) - Audience Definition
- [ ] Target market is defined with specificity beyond basic demographics
- [ ] Market size is estimated and confirmed large enough to sustain the campaign
- [ ] Market's primary pain point is identified and validated through research
- [ ] Market's purchasing power and willingness to pay are assessed
- [ ] Market is reachable through the chosen advertising platform's targeting options
- [ ] Competitive landscape within the market is documented

## Section 2: Message (M2) - Communication Clarity
- [ ] Core message can be stated in one clear sentence
- [ ] Message speaks directly to the market's primary pain point or desire
- [ ] Message differentiates from competitors (not generic industry language)
- [ ] Message passes the "friend test" (could you say this to a friend without sounding weird?)
- [ ] Message is adapted for each traffic temperature (cold, warm, hot)
- [ ] Emotional resonance is tested (does the message make the reader feel something?)

## Section 3: Magnet (M3) - Lead Magnet Quality
- [ ] Lead magnet is irresistible to the defined market (they would feel foolish saying no)
- [ ] Lead magnet solves a specific, urgent problem (not a vague promise)
- [ ] Lead magnet is consumable quickly (under 15 minutes for reports, under 30 minutes for video)
- [ ] Lead magnet delivers immediate value (actionable within hours, not weeks)
- [ ] Lead magnet naturally leads to the core paid offer (logical next step)
- [ ] Lead magnet format matches audience preference (PDF, video, quiz, template, tool)

## Section 4: Mechanism (M4) - Funnel & Delivery
- [ ] Delivery mechanism is automated and reliable (no manual steps required)
- [ ] Opt-in process is frictionless (minimal form fields, clear CTA)
- [ ] Thank-you page includes a next-step offer or engagement opportunity
- [ ] Email/SMS delivery sequence is built and tested
- [ ] Follow-up sequence nurtures the lead toward the core offer over 5-7 days
- [ ] Technical infrastructure (email platform, landing page, CRM) is tested end-to-end

## Section 5: 4M Alignment Check
- [ ] Market and Message are aligned (the message resonates with this specific market)
- [ ] Message and Magnet are aligned (the magnet delivers on the message's promise)
- [ ] Magnet and Mechanism are aligned (the delivery system matches the magnet's format)
- [ ] All 4Ms work together as a cohesive system (no weak link in the chain)
- [ ] The 4M strategy has been reviewed by at least one team member before launch

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/25 items (80%)
- **Reviewer**: kusmich-social-advertising-strategist
- **Escalation**: If any single M is fundamentally weak, it will undermine the other three; fix the weakest M first before proceeding
