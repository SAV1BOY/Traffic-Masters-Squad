# High-Ticket Lead Generation Campaign Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Nicholas Kusmich's high-ticket lead generation campaign strategy. Must pass before any high-ticket lead gen campaign is launched.

## Section 1: Offer & Positioning
- [ ] The high-ticket offer ($2,000+) is clearly defined with deliverables, timeline, and price
- [ ] Offer positioning targets a specific, affluent audience with an urgent, high-value problem
- [ ] The transformation or outcome the offer delivers is specific and measurable
- [ ] Competitive differentiation is clear (why this over alternatives at a similar price point)
- [ ] Risk reversal is built into the offer (guarantee, trial period, or milestone-based payment)

## Section 2: Lead Qualification Strategy
- [ ] Lead qualification criteria are defined before campaign launch (budget, authority, need, timeline)
- [ ] Application or booking form includes qualifying questions (not just name and email)
- [ ] Disqualification criteria are set to filter out unqualified leads automatically
- [ ] Lead scoring system assigns points based on responses to prioritize follow-up
- [ ] The application process itself acts as a commitment device (longer form = more committed leads)

## Section 3: Funnel Architecture
- [ ] Funnel follows the give-before-ask principle (value delivery before application request)
- [ ] Webinar, VSL, or long-form content educates and pre-sells before the CTA
- [ ] Application page communicates exclusivity (not everyone is accepted)
- [ ] Thank-you/confirmation page sets expectations for next steps and timeline
- [ ] Calendar booking is integrated for qualified leads (Calendly, OnceHub, or equivalent)

## Section 4: Ad Creative for High-Ticket
- [ ] Ad creative targets the decision-maker, not a general audience
- [ ] Messaging emphasizes ROI and transformation, not price
- [ ] Case studies and specific client results are featured prominently
- [ ] Video content positions the founder/expert as a trusted authority
- [ ] Ad copy length is longer than typical (high-ticket requires more persuasion)

## Section 5: Follow-Up System
- [ ] Automated email sequence nurtures leads who apply but do not book
- [ ] Speed-to-lead protocol ensures follow-up within 5 minutes of application
- [ ] Phone/video call script is prepared for the sales team
- [ ] No-show follow-up sequence is automated (email + SMS reminders)
- [ ] Lead nurture for non-qualifiers redirects to a lower-ticket offer

## Section 6: Metrics & Economics
- [ ] Target cost per qualified lead (CPQL) is defined based on close rate and deal value
- [ ] Cost per booked call is tracked separately from cost per lead
- [ ] Show rate for booked calls is monitored (target: 70%+)
- [ ] Close rate from call to sale is tracked and benchmarked
- [ ] Total campaign ROI accounts for ad spend, sales team cost, and fulfillment cost

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 21/25 items (84%)
- **Reviewer**: kusmich-social-advertising-strategist
- **Escalation**: If lead qualification is weak (too many unqualified leads booking calls), tighten the application process before increasing ad spend
