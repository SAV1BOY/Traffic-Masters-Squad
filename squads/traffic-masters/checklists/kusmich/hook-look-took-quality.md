# Hook-Look-Took Framework Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Nicholas Kusmich's Hook-Look-Took ad creation framework. Must pass before any ad creative is approved for launch.

## Section 1: HOOK - Attention Capture
- [ ] Hook is the very first thing the audience sees or reads (first line of copy, first frame of video)
- [ ] Hook calls out the target audience directly ("Attention [specific person]..." or equivalent)
- [ ] Hook addresses a specific pain, desire, or curiosity gap relevant to the avatar
- [ ] Hook is concise (under 10 words for headlines, under 3 seconds for video)
- [ ] Hook stops the scroll by being unexpected, bold, or emotionally provocative
- [ ] At least 5 hook variations are written for testing

## Section 2: LOOK - Engagement & Story
- [ ] Look section expands on the hook's promise with a compelling narrative or explanation
- [ ] Content delivers genuine value (insight, education, entertainment, or inspiration)
- [ ] Look section is formatted for scanability (short paragraphs, line breaks, bullet points)
- [ ] The reader/viewer can extract value from the Look section even without clicking through
- [ ] Social proof or credibility is woven into the Look section naturally
- [ ] Look section maintains the emotional tone established by the hook

## Section 3: TOOK - Call to Action
- [ ] CTA is crystal clear (the audience knows exactly what to do and what they will get)
- [ ] CTA uses action-oriented language ("Download", "Watch", "Claim", "Get")
- [ ] CTA includes a reason to act now (urgency, scarcity, or time-sensitivity)
- [ ] CTA friction level matches the traffic temperature (low commitment for cold, higher for hot)
- [ ] Landing page URL is included and functional
- [ ] CTA button text on the platform matches the ad copy CTA

## Section 4: Hook-Look-Took Flow
- [ ] The three sections flow logically (hook grabs > look educates > took converts)
- [ ] Transition between hook and look is seamless (no jarring topic change)
- [ ] Transition between look and took feels earned (the CTA is a natural next step)
- [ ] The entire ad can be consumed in the appropriate time for the platform (30s-2min for social)
- [ ] The ad works as a complete unit (removing any section would break it)

## Section 5: Format-Specific Adaptation
- [ ] Hook-Look-Took is adapted to the creative format (video, static, carousel, long-form)
- [ ] Video: Hook is visual + text overlay, Look is spoken content, Took is end card + voiceover
- [ ] Static: Hook is headline/image, Look is body copy, Took is CTA button
- [ ] Carousel: Hook is first card, Look is middle cards, Took is final card
- [ ] Long-form copy: Hook is first 2 lines (above "see more"), Look is expanded body, Took is final paragraph

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/25 items (80%)
- **Reviewer**: kusmich-social-advertising-strategist
- **Escalation**: If the Hook fails to stop the scroll in testing (CTR below platform average), rewrite hooks before spending more budget
