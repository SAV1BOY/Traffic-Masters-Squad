# Give Before You Ask Strategy Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Nicholas Kusmich's "Give Before You Ask" philosophy for Facebook advertising. Must pass before any lead generation or sales campaign is approved for launch.

## Section 1: Value-First Content Assessment
- [ ] Lead magnet or value offer delivers genuine, actionable value (not a teaser)
- [ ] The free content solves a complete sub-problem, not just identifies the problem
- [ ] Value content can stand alone as useful even if the prospect never buys
- [ ] Content quality matches or exceeds what competitors charge for
- [ ] The value piece demonstrates expertise and builds trust simultaneously

## Section 2: Reciprocity Architecture
- [ ] The giving sequence precedes the asking sequence in the funnel (value before pitch)
- [ ] At least 2-3 value touchpoints occur before any sales request
- [ ] Each value touchpoint escalates the relationship (awareness > trust > desire)
- [ ] The transition from giving to asking feels natural, not abrupt
- [ ] The ask is proportional to the value given (small gives = small asks, big gives = bigger asks)

## Section 3: Content-to-Offer Bridge
- [ ] The free content logically leads to the paid offer (content is related, not random)
- [ ] The gap between what the free content delivers and what the paid offer solves is clear
- [ ] Content creates an "aha moment" that naturally generates desire for the next step
- [ ] The bridge is explicit ("Now that you know X, the next step is Y")
- [ ] Multiple bridge paths exist for different audience segments

## Section 4: Ad Creative Alignment
- [ ] Ad creative leads with value, not with a pitch (education, entertainment, or inspiration)
- [ ] Ad copy follows the give-give-give-ask cadence even within a single ad
- [ ] Headlines promise a specific deliverable or insight, not just a product
- [ ] Visuals show the value being delivered (screenshots, previews, results)
- [ ] Social proof in ads highlights the value received, not just the product purchased

## Section 5: Measurement of Giving Impact
- [ ] Lead magnet download/access rate is tracked as a leading indicator
- [ ] Content engagement metrics are monitored (open rates, watch time, completion rates)
- [ ] Time from first value touch to first purchase is measured
- [ ] Customers who received value content are compared to those who did not (LTV, retention)
- [ ] The "give" cost (content production + distribution) is factored into total CAC

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/24 items (79%)
- **Reviewer**: kusmich-social-advertising-strategist
- **Escalation**: If the value-first content fails to deliver genuine value or the ask comes too early, redesign the funnel sequence before launching
