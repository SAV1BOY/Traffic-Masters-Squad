# Contextual Congruence Principle Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Nicholas Kusmich's Contextual Congruence principle ensuring ads match the platform context and user intent. Must pass before any ad is published on any platform.

## Section 1: Platform Context Alignment
- [ ] Ad format matches what users expect to see on the platform (social = social content, not billboards)
- [ ] Ad creative looks native to the feed (not overly polished or corporate in social contexts)
- [ ] Ad tone matches platform culture (casual on Facebook/Instagram, professional on LinkedIn)
- [ ] Content type aligns with platform strengths (video on YouTube/TikTok, images on Instagram, text on LinkedIn)
- [ ] Ad does not feel like an interruption but like content the user would willingly consume

## Section 2: User Intent Alignment
- [ ] The ad acknowledges the user's current mindset (browsing, searching, learning, connecting)
- [ ] Messaging matches the user's awareness level on the platform (social = problem-aware, search = solution-aware)
- [ ] The ask in the ad is congruent with the user's readiness (don't ask for a purchase from someone just browsing)
- [ ] The landing page experience matches the promise and tone of the ad (no bait and switch)
- [ ] Post-click experience continues the conversation started in the ad (headline, visuals, offer match)

## Section 3: Message-to-Market Match
- [ ] Ad copy speaks the language of the target audience (vocabulary, slang, jargon level)
- [ ] Pain points referenced in the ad are real and current (validated through research)
- [ ] The solution presented is relevant to the audience seeing the ad (segmented by avatar)
- [ ] Cultural references, humor, and tone are appropriate for the audience demographic
- [ ] The offer timing is congruent with the audience's buying cycle

## Section 4: Visual Congruence
- [ ] Images or video feature people/scenarios the audience relates to (age, lifestyle, environment)
- [ ] Color palette and design style match audience expectations (not the advertiser's preference)
- [ ] Product or service is shown in context of use (not isolated on white background for social)
- [ ] Thumbnail (video) or hero image (static) is optimized for the specific platform placement
- [ ] Mobile-first design ensures the ad looks great on the primary consumption device

## Section 5: Congruence Across Touchpoints
- [ ] Ad > Landing page > Thank you page > Email follow-up all maintain consistent messaging
- [ ] Visual style is consistent from ad through the entire post-click experience
- [ ] The emotional tone remains consistent (if the ad is warm and personal, the landing page is too)
- [ ] CTAs escalate logically through the journey (watch > learn more > try > buy)
- [ ] Retargeting ads reference the specific content the user previously engaged with

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/24 items (79%)
- **Reviewer**: kusmich-social-advertising-strategist
- **Escalation**: If platform context or user intent alignment fails, the ad will feel like spam; redesign creative to be platform-native before launch
