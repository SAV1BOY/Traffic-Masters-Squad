# Acquisition Strategy Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Strategy & Planning
> **Reviewed by**: Traffic Strategist

## Purpose
Ensures every acquisition strategy is complete, measurable, and economically viable before any campaign launches. Prevents wasted spend from incomplete strategic foundations.

## Checklist

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- [ ] ICP is documented with demographics, psychographics, and behavioral traits
- [ ] Pain points and desires are explicitly listed with supporting evidence
- [ ] Awareness level of the target audience is identified (unaware to most aware)
- [ ] ICP has been validated against existing customer data or research

### Offer Clarity
- [ ] Core offer is stated in one clear sentence
- [ ] Value proposition differentiates from top three competitors
- [ ] Offer-to-audience fit has been assessed and documented

### Funnel Mapping
- [ ] Full funnel is mapped from first touch to conversion
- [ ] Each funnel stage has defined entry and exit criteria
- [ ] Drop-off points are anticipated with mitigation strategies
- [ ] Post-conversion flow is documented (onboarding, upsell, retention)

### Channel Selection
- [ ] Primary and secondary channels are selected with rationale
- [ ] Channel selection aligns with where the ICP spends time
- [ ] Channel-specific constraints and opportunities are noted

### Budget Allocation
- [ ] Total budget is defined with breakdown by channel
- [ ] Budget split between testing and scaling phases is documented
- [ ] Minimum viable budget per channel meets platform recommendations

### Metas and Targets
- [ ] Revenue or lead targets are set for the campaign period
- [ ] Target CPA, ROAS, or CPL is defined and realistic
- [ ] Interim KPIs are set for each funnel stage

### Unit Economics
- [ ] Customer lifetime value (LTV) is calculated or estimated
- [ ] Maximum allowable CPA is derived from LTV and margin
- [ ] Break-even timeline is projected
- [ ] Payback period is acceptable per business constraints

### Measurement Plan
- [ ] Primary and secondary success metrics are defined
- [ ] Reporting cadence and format are agreed upon
- [ ] Data sources and tools for measurement are confirmed
- [ ] Baseline metrics are captured before launch

## Pass/Fail Criteria
All checklist items must be completed and verified. Any incomplete section blocks campaign launch.

## If Failed
Return the strategy to the strategist with specific items flagged. Schedule a review session within 48 hours. Do not proceed to campaign build until all items pass.
