# Account Audit Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Account Management
> **Reviewed by**: Traffic Strategist

## Purpose
Ensures every account audit is thorough, systematic, and produces actionable findings. A superficial audit misses critical optimization opportunities and perpetuates wasted spend.

## Checklist

### Campaign Review
- [ ] All active campaigns have been reviewed for performance against objectives
- [ ] Paused campaigns have been reviewed for reactivation potential or cleanup
- [ ] Campaign objectives are verified as still aligned with current business goals
- [ ] Campaign structure is assessed for efficiency and scalability
- [ ] Historical performance trends are analyzed (minimum 90-day lookback)

### Waste Identification
- [ ] Underperforming ad sets and ads are identified with specific metrics
- [ ] Audience overlap between campaigns is measured and quantified
- [ ] High-spend low-return segments are flagged for action
- [ ] Irrelevant placements or search terms consuming budget are documented
- [ ] Total estimated monthly waste is calculated in dollar terms

### Gap Analysis
- [ ] Missing funnel stages in the account structure are identified
- [ ] Untapped audience segments are documented with estimated opportunity
- [ ] Missing retargeting layers are identified
- [ ] Creative format gaps are noted (e.g., no video, no carousel)
- [ ] Platform feature gaps are flagged (e.g., not using CAPI, no lookalikes)

### Tracking Verification
- [ ] Pixel installation is verified across all relevant pages
- [ ] Conversion events are firing correctly and matching backend data
- [ ] CAPI integration is checked for proper configuration
- [ ] UTM parameter consistency is audited across all active URLs
- [ ] Attribution settings are reviewed and documented

### Structure Assessment
- [ ] Campaign naming conventions are consistent and logical
- [ ] Account structure supports clear reporting and optimization
- [ ] Ad set count per campaign is within best-practice ranges
- [ ] Budget distribution across campaigns is intentional and justified
- [ ] Audience segmentation is clean with minimal overlap

### Creative Health
- [ ] Active creatives are assessed for fatigue indicators
- [ ] Top-performing creatives are identified with their key attributes
- [ ] Creative diversity is evaluated (formats, angles, hooks)
- [ ] Ad copy quality and relevance are reviewed
- [ ] Creative testing velocity is assessed against best practices

### Quick Wins
- [ ] Immediate optimization opportunities are listed with expected impact
- [ ] Quick wins are prioritized by effort vs impact
- [ ] Each quick win has a clear implementation step
- [ ] Quick wins can be implemented within 1-2 weeks
- [ ] Expected performance lift from quick wins is estimated

### 30/60/90 Day Plan
- [ ] 30-day actions focus on fixes and quick wins
- [ ] 60-day actions focus on structural improvements and testing
- [ ] 90-day actions focus on scaling and strategic expansion
- [ ] Each phase has measurable success criteria
- [ ] Resource requirements for each phase are identified
- [ ] Plan is documented and shared with all stakeholders

## Pass/Fail Criteria
All eight sections must be completed. An audit missing any section is considered incomplete and should not be presented to the client or stakeholders.

## If Failed
Complete the missing sections before presenting the audit. If data access limitations prevented completion, document the gaps and request access immediately.
