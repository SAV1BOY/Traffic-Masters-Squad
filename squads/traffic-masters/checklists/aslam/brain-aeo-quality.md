# BRAIN/AEO Methodology Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Kasim Aslam's BRAIN (Brand, Reach, Awareness, Intent, Nurture) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) methodology for AI-era search. Must pass before the BRAIN/AEO strategy is approved for implementation.

## Section 1: Brand Authority Foundation
- [ ] Brand presence is established across all major platforms (website, social, YouTube, podcasts)
- [ ] Brand mentions and citations exist on authoritative third-party sites
- [ ] Structured data (schema markup) is implemented on all key website pages
- [ ] Google Business Profile is fully optimized with complete information and regular posts
- [ ] Brand entity is recognized by Google's Knowledge Graph (or steps are taken to establish it)

## Section 2: AEO Content Strategy
- [ ] Content is structured to directly answer common questions in the niche
- [ ] FAQ schema markup is applied to relevant pages for AI snippet eligibility
- [ ] Content covers topics at all awareness levels (problem-aware through most-aware)
- [ ] Long-form authoritative content establishes topical authority in the primary subject area
- [ ] Content is optimized for featured snippets, People Also Ask, and AI Overviews
- [ ] Voice search patterns are considered in content optimization (conversational queries)

## Section 3: BRAIN - Reach & Awareness Campaigns
- [ ] Top-of-funnel campaigns are designed to build brand recognition at scale
- [ ] YouTube, Display, and Demand Gen campaigns support brand awareness objectives
- [ ] Reach campaigns use broad targeting to introduce the brand to new audiences
- [ ] Awareness metrics (impressions, reach, video views) are tracked separately from conversion metrics
- [ ] Brand lift studies are planned to measure awareness impact

## Section 4: BRAIN - Intent Capture
- [ ] High-intent search campaigns capture demand generated by awareness efforts
- [ ] Brand search volume is monitored as a leading indicator of awareness campaign effectiveness
- [ ] Non-brand search campaigns target queries that indicate solution-seeking behavior
- [ ] Shopping and PMax campaigns capture commercial intent from aware audiences
- [ ] Intent campaigns are structured to receive the audience built by reach/awareness campaigns

## Section 5: BRAIN - Nurture & Conversion
- [ ] Remarketing campaigns nurture audiences who engaged with awareness content
- [ ] Email/SMS sequences complement ad-based nurturing
- [ ] Nurture sequences deliver progressive value (education > proof > offer)
- [ ] Customer match audiences are used to nurture leads across Google's ecosystem
- [ ] Conversion campaigns only target audiences who have been through the awareness/intent stages

## Section 6: AEO Performance Measurement
- [ ] AI Overview appearances are tracked for target queries
- [ ] Featured snippet ownership is monitored for priority keywords
- [ ] Organic traffic from AI-assisted search is measured separately
- [ ] Brand search volume trends are correlated with BRAIN campaign activity
- [ ] Total cost of BRAIN/AEO is measured against total revenue influenced (not just last-click)

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 19/24 items (79%)
- **Reviewer**: aslam-google-ads-strategist
- **Escalation**: If brand authority foundation is weak, prioritize brand building before investing heavily in AEO content or BRAIN campaigns
