# 4 Core Campaign Types Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Kasim Aslam's 4 Core Campaign Types framework for Google Ads. Must pass before the campaign portfolio is considered complete.

## Section 1: Campaign Type 1 - Brand Search
- [ ] Brand search campaign captures all branded keyword variations (misspellings, abbreviations)
- [ ] Brand campaign uses exact and phrase match (not broad match for brand terms)
- [ ] Competitor conquesting terms are separated into their own campaign, not mixed with brand
- [ ] Brand campaign has its own dedicated budget (not shared with non-brand)
- [ ] Brand CPC is monitored for competitor bidding intrusion
- [ ] Ad copy in brand campaigns reinforces brand authority and includes sitelinks

## Section 2: Campaign Type 2 - Non-Brand Search
- [ ] Non-brand keywords target high-intent commercial and transactional queries
- [ ] Keywords are organized into tightly themed ad groups (single theme ad groups)
- [ ] Negative keywords prevent cannibalization between brand and non-brand campaigns
- [ ] Ad copy matches keyword intent with relevant headlines and descriptions
- [ ] Landing pages are specific to the keyword theme (not generic homepage)
- [ ] Quality Score is monitored and optimized (target 7+ for primary keywords)

## Section 3: Campaign Type 3 - Shopping / Performance Max
- [ ] Product feed is optimized with accurate titles, descriptions, and attributes
- [ ] Shopping campaign structure separates top performers from the rest
- [ ] Performance Max asset groups are organized by product category or theme
- [ ] Search themes in PMax are configured with strategic intent (not left empty)
- [ ] PMax is not cannibalizing branded search traffic (brand exclusions applied)

## Section 4: Campaign Type 4 - Remarketing / Display
- [ ] Remarketing audiences are built from meaningful site interactions (not just "all visitors")
- [ ] Audience segments include cart abandoners, product viewers, and past purchasers
- [ ] Frequency caps prevent ad fatigue in remarketing campaigns
- [ ] Display placements are curated or excluded to avoid low-quality inventory
- [ ] Remarketing ad creatives are refreshed regularly to prevent banner blindness

## Section 5: Portfolio Balance & Integration
- [ ] All 4 campaign types are active and allocated budget proportionally
- [ ] Budget follows priority: Brand Search > Non-Brand Search > Shopping/PMax > Remarketing
- [ ] Cross-campaign cannibalization is monitored via auction insights and search term overlap
- [ ] Each campaign type has distinct KPIs appropriate to its role in the funnel
- [ ] Campaign types work together as a system (remarketing catches leakage from search and shopping)
- [ ] Monthly portfolio review assesses balance and reallocates budget based on performance

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 21/25 items (84%)
- **Reviewer**: aslam-google-ads-strategist
- **Escalation**: If any of the 4 core campaign types is missing, the account has a structural gap; build the missing campaign type before optimizing existing ones
