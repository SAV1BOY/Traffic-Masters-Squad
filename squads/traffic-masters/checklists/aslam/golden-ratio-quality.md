# Golden Ratio Budget Allocation Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Kasim Aslam's Golden Ratio budget allocation framework for Google Ads. Must pass before monthly budget distribution is finalized.

## Section 1: Budget Ratio Foundation
- [ ] Total monthly Google Ads budget is confirmed and approved
- [ ] Budget allocation follows the Golden Ratio principle: highest-intent campaigns get the largest share
- [ ] Brand search receives sufficient budget to capture 90%+ impression share on branded terms
- [ ] Non-brand search receives budget proportional to available high-intent keyword volume
- [ ] Shopping/PMax receives budget proportional to product catalog size and margin targets
- [ ] Remarketing receives budget based on retargeting pool size (not a fixed percentage)

## Section 2: Intent-Based Prioritization
- [ ] High-intent campaigns (brand search, high-commercial-intent non-brand) are fully funded first
- [ ] Mid-intent campaigns (broad non-brand, shopping) receive remaining budget after high-intent
- [ ] Low-intent campaigns (display prospecting, discovery) receive only surplus budget
- [ ] Budget is never taken from high-intent campaigns to fund low-intent campaigns
- [ ] Intent tiers are reviewed monthly as keyword performance data accumulates

## Section 3: Performance-Based Reallocation
- [ ] Weekly budget review compares actual spend vs. planned allocation
- [ ] Campaigns exceeding ROAS/CPA targets receive incremental budget from underperformers
- [ ] Campaigns missing targets by 30%+ for 2 consecutive weeks trigger budget reduction
- [ ] Budget reallocation follows a documented process (not ad hoc decisions)
- [ ] No single budget change exceeds 20% of a campaign's current budget per adjustment

## Section 4: Seasonal & Promotional Adjustments
- [ ] Seasonal demand patterns are factored into monthly budget plans
- [ ] Promotional periods (sales, launches) have pre-allocated incremental budget
- [ ] Post-promotion budget normalization plan is documented
- [ ] Competitor activity spikes (detected via auction insights) trigger budget defense allocations
- [ ] Budget flexibility reserves (10-15% of total) are maintained for unexpected opportunities

## Section 5: Reporting & Accountability
- [ ] Budget utilization report is generated weekly (planned vs. actual vs. optimal)
- [ ] Cost per conversion by campaign type is tracked to validate the allocation strategy
- [ ] Marginal CPA analysis identifies the point of diminishing returns per campaign
- [ ] CFO/client budget report shows spend efficiency and ROI by allocation tier
- [ ] Quarterly Golden Ratio review recalibrates allocation based on accumulated data

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 18/22 items (82%)
- **Reviewer**: aslam-google-ads-strategist
- **Escalation**: If high-intent campaigns are underfunded while low-intent campaigns are overfunded, immediately rebalance the budget before the next billing cycle
