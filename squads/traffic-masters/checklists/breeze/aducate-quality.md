# ADUCATE YouTube Ad Framework Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Tom Breeze's ADUCATE (Aim, Difficulty, Understanding, Credibility, Action Plan, Teach, Exit) YouTube ad framework. Must pass before any YouTube ad script is approved for production.

## Section 1: A - Aim (Target Audience Call-Out)
- [ ] The ad opens by identifying the specific target viewer within the first 5 seconds
- [ ] The call-out uses language the audience uses to describe themselves
- [ ] The aim creates an immediate "this is for me" response in the right viewer
- [ ] Non-target viewers are naturally filtered out by the specificity of the aim

## Section 2: D - Difficulty (Problem Identification)
- [ ] A specific, relatable difficulty or pain point is presented clearly
- [ ] The difficulty is stated in the viewer's own words (not clinical or jargon-heavy)
- [ ] The emotional weight of the problem is conveyed (frustration, overwhelm, fear)
- [ ] The difficulty feels urgent and current (not a someday problem)

## Section 3: U - Understanding (Empathy & Rapport)
- [ ] The speaker demonstrates genuine understanding of the viewer's situation
- [ ] Empathy is shown through shared experience or acknowledgment ("I know how it feels")
- [ ] The viewer feels validated, not judged, for having this difficulty
- [ ] Understanding section builds trust before any solution is introduced

## Section 4: C - Credibility (Proof & Authority)
- [ ] Credentials or relevant experience are stated concisely (not a long bio)
- [ ] Specific results or case studies are referenced (numbers, outcomes, client names if permitted)
- [ ] Credibility is delivered naturally within the story, not as a brag
- [ ] Third-party validation is included where possible (media, awards, testimonials)

## Section 5: A - Action Plan (Clear Path Forward)
- [ ] A simple, clear plan of action is outlined (3 steps or fewer)
- [ ] The plan feels achievable and not overwhelming
- [ ] Each step logically follows the previous one
- [ ] The action plan bridges from the viewer's current state to the desired outcome

## Section 6: T - Teach (Value Delivery)
- [ ] At least one actionable insight or tactic is taught in the ad itself
- [ ] The teaching moment provides immediate value (the viewer learns something useful)
- [ ] Teaching demonstrates expertise without giving away everything
- [ ] The insight creates a desire to learn more (opens a curiosity loop)

## Section 7: E - Exit (Call to Action)
- [ ] CTA is specific and tells the viewer exactly what to do ("Click the link below to...")
- [ ] CTA includes a compelling reason to act now
- [ ] The exit feels like a natural conclusion, not an abrupt pitch
- [ ] CTA matches the offer and the traffic temperature (soft for cold, direct for warm)
- [ ] End screen and cards are configured to support the CTA

## Section 8: Overall ADUCATE Script Quality
- [ ] All 7 ADUCATE elements are present in sequence
- [ ] Total script length is appropriate for the ad format (15s bumper, 30s pre-roll, 2-5min long-form)
- [ ] Script is written conversationally (sounds natural when spoken aloud)
- [ ] Script has been read aloud and timed before production
- [ ] Pacing allows for visual b-roll and text overlay integration

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/25 items (80%)
- **Reviewer**: breeze-youtube-strategist
- **Escalation**: If the Aim or Exit sections are weak, rewrite those first as they determine targeting effectiveness and conversion respectively
