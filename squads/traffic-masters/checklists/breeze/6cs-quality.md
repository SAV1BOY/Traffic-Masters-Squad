# 6Cs ADucational Content Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Tom Breeze's 6Cs framework for creating ADucational (advertising + educational) YouTube content. Must pass before content is approved for production.

## Section 1: C1 - Captivate (Attention)
- [ ] Opening visual or statement is designed to stop the viewer from scrolling or skipping
- [ ] Captivation technique is relevant to the topic (not generic clickbait)
- [ ] First 3 seconds include movement, a face, or bold text to capture attention
- [ ] The viewer's curiosity is activated through a question, statistic, or provocative claim
- [ ] Captivation element has been A/B tested in thumbnail and opening frame

## Section 2: C2 - Connect (Relationship)
- [ ] Speaker establishes rapport by demonstrating understanding of the viewer's world
- [ ] Personal story or shared experience creates an emotional connection
- [ ] Language mirrors the viewer's vocabulary and communication style
- [ ] The connection moment makes the viewer think "this person gets me"

## Section 3: C3 - Content (Value Delivery)
- [ ] Substantive educational content is delivered (not just surface-level tips)
- [ ] Content follows a logical structure that is easy to follow
- [ ] Key teaching points are supported by examples, data, or demonstrations
- [ ] Content is genuinely useful even if the viewer never clicks the CTA
- [ ] Information hierarchy is clear (most important points are emphasized)
- [ ] Content length is appropriate for the depth of the topic (not padded)

## Section 4: C4 - Credibility (Trust)
- [ ] Expert credentials are established naturally within the content delivery
- [ ] Specific results, case studies, or data points are cited
- [ ] Testimonials or social proof from recognizable sources are included
- [ ] Production quality reflects the level of expertise being claimed
- [ ] Claims are verifiable and not exaggerated

## Section 5: C5 - Call-to-Action (Conversion)
- [ ] CTA is clear, specific, and actionable ("Click the link to download your free guide")
- [ ] CTA is presented as a logical next step from the educational content
- [ ] Only one primary CTA is given (not multiple competing actions)
- [ ] CTA is reinforced visually (on-screen text, graphics, end card)
- [ ] CTA timing is at the peak of viewer engagement, not just at the end

## Section 6: C6 - Continue (Retention & Nurture)
- [ ] A reason to continue the relationship is provided (subscribe, follow, join community)
- [ ] Next content recommendation keeps the viewer in the brand ecosystem
- [ ] End screen promotes related content for continued engagement
- [ ] Post-view nurture sequence is planned (email, retargeting ad, next video)
- [ ] Viewer is given a path to consume more content regardless of whether they convert now

## Section 7: 6Cs Integration Quality
- [ ] All 6 Cs are present in the content piece
- [ ] The flow between each C feels natural and conversational
- [ ] No single C dominates at the expense of the others (balanced delivery)
- [ ] The content works as both education and advertisement simultaneously
- [ ] Content has been reviewed by someone unfamiliar with the topic to confirm clarity

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/25 items (80%)
- **Reviewer**: breeze-youtube-strategist
- **Escalation**: If Content (C3) or Call-to-Action (C5) sections are weak, the ADucational approach fails; rework those elements before production
