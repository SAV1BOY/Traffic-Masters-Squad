# 3 Acts Video Structure Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Tom Breeze's 3 Acts video structure for YouTube ads. Must pass before any video ad script is approved for filming.

## Section 1: Act 1 - The Setup (First 5-15 Seconds)
- [ ] Act 1 immediately hooks the viewer with a bold statement, question, or visual
- [ ] The target audience is identified within the first 5 seconds
- [ ] The core problem or opportunity is introduced quickly and clearly
- [ ] Viewer retention is prioritized (the ad earns the right to continue)
- [ ] Act 1 creates enough intrigue for the viewer to watch past the skip button (5-second mark)
- [ ] Tone and energy level are calibrated to the audience (not generic hype)

## Section 2: Act 2 - The Confrontation (Core Content)
- [ ] Act 2 deepens the problem or opportunity introduced in Act 1
- [ ] Conflict or tension is introduced (obstacles, misconceptions, common mistakes)
- [ ] Value is delivered through teaching, storytelling, or demonstration
- [ ] Credibility elements are woven naturally into the narrative
- [ ] The viewer's worldview is expanded or challenged (paradigm shift moment)
- [ ] Pacing varies to maintain engagement (mix of high-energy and reflective moments)
- [ ] Act 2 accounts for 60-70% of the total video length

## Section 3: Act 3 - The Resolution (Final 15-30 Seconds)
- [ ] Act 3 resolves the tension created in Act 2 with a clear solution path
- [ ] The solution is positioned as the natural next step (not a hard pivot to a pitch)
- [ ] CTA is delivered with confidence and clarity
- [ ] A reason to act immediately is provided (urgency, bonus, limited availability)
- [ ] The resolution ties back to the setup (narrative closure)
- [ ] End screen elements support the CTA (subscribe, link click, companion banner)

## Section 4: Narrative Flow & Transitions
- [ ] Transition from Act 1 to Act 2 is seamless (not "but first, let me tell you about...")
- [ ] Transition from Act 2 to Act 3 is earned (the viewer wants to know the next step)
- [ ] Emotional arc progresses logically (curiosity > understanding > motivation > action)
- [ ] No section feels rushed or unnecessarily padded
- [ ] The 3 Acts work together as a complete story, not three disconnected segments

## Section 5: Production Alignment
- [ ] Script timing matches the target video length when read aloud
- [ ] Visual direction notes are included for each act (b-roll, graphics, demonstrations)
- [ ] Speaker delivery notes indicate energy level and tone shifts between acts
- [ ] Music or sound design supports the emotional arc of the 3 acts
- [ ] Captions/subtitles are planned for silent viewing compatibility

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/25 items (80%)
- **Reviewer**: breeze-youtube-strategist
- **Escalation**: If Act 1 fails to hook (high drop-off in first 5 seconds), rewrite the opening before investing in full production
