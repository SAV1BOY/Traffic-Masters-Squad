# 5A's Readiness Assessment Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Tom Breeze's 5A's Readiness framework for assessing whether a business is ready for YouTube advertising. Must pass before committing budget to YouTube campaigns.

## Section 1: A1 - Assets (Creative & Content Readiness)
- [ ] Video production capability exists (in-house or outsourced partner identified)
- [ ] A library of at least 3-5 video ad concepts is ready for testing
- [ ] Thumbnail creation process is established for click-through optimization
- [ ] Brand guidelines are documented for consistent video production
- [ ] Existing video content (organic YouTube, webinars, testimonials) can be repurposed for ads

## Section 2: A2 - Audience (Targeting Readiness)
- [ ] Target audience is clearly defined and present on YouTube in significant numbers
- [ ] YouTube search behavior of the target audience is researched (what they search for)
- [ ] Competitor channels and content the audience watches are identified
- [ ] Custom intent audiences can be built from relevant search terms
- [ ] Customer match lists are available for YouTube targeting (minimum 1,000 emails)
- [ ] Remarketing audiences from website and YouTube channel are populated

## Section 3: A3 - Analytics (Measurement Readiness)
- [ ] Google Ads conversion tracking is set up and verified
- [ ] Google Analytics 4 is configured with YouTube campaign UTM parameters defined
- [ ] Conversion actions are defined (lead, purchase, sign-up, phone call)
- [ ] View-through conversion window is set appropriately for the sales cycle
- [ ] Attribution model accounts for YouTube's role in the customer journey (often upper-funnel)
- [ ] Baseline metrics exist from other channels for comparison

## Section 4: A4 - Allocation (Budget Readiness)
- [ ] YouTube test budget is allocated separately from other channels (not cannibalized)
- [ ] Budget is sufficient for a meaningful test (minimum 30 days of data collection)
- [ ] Daily budget allows for at least 50-100 views per day per ad group
- [ ] Budget accounts for the learning period (first 2 weeks may show elevated costs)
- [ ] Long-term budget commitment is secured (YouTube requires patience, not just 1-week tests)

## Section 5: A5 - Ability (Team & Expertise Readiness)
- [ ] Team member or agency partner has YouTube Ads experience (not just Meta/Google Search)
- [ ] Understanding of YouTube ad formats exists (skippable in-stream, bumper, discovery, Shorts)
- [ ] Bid strategy knowledge covers Target CPV, Target CPA, and Maximize Conversions
- [ ] Creative iteration process is established for ongoing optimization
- [ ] Reporting and analysis cadence is defined (weekly reviews, monthly strategy sessions)
- [ ] Escalation path exists for underperformance (who decides to adjust, pause, or scale?)

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 20/25 items (80%)
- **Reviewer**: breeze-youtube-strategist
- **Escalation**: If any of the 5 A's scores below 60% readiness, address that dimension before launching YouTube campaigns
