# Master Ad Plan (MAP) Quality Gate

> Quality gate for Tom Breeze's Master Ad Plan for YouTube advertising strategy. Must pass before the YouTube ad plan is approved for execution.

## Section 1: Strategic Foundation
- [ ] Business objectives are translated into specific YouTube campaign goals
- [ ] Revenue target is defined with reverse-engineered metrics (views > clicks > leads > sales)
- [ ] Customer journey role of YouTube is defined (awareness, consideration, conversion, or full-funnel)
- [ ] YouTube's relationship to other channels is mapped (does YouTube feed Meta retargeting? Does Google Search feed YouTube remarketing?)
- [ ] Timeline for the MAP is defined (30-day sprint, 90-day plan, or ongoing)

## Section 2: Audience Strategy
- [ ] Primary audience segments are defined with YouTube-specific targeting parameters
- [ ] Custom intent audiences are built from high-intent search keywords
- [ ] Affinity and in-market audiences are selected based on avatar alignment
- [ ] Placement targeting identifies specific YouTube channels and videos for ads
- [ ] Audience layering strategy combines multiple targeting methods for precision
- [ ] Exclusion lists prevent showing ads to existing customers or irrelevant viewers

## Section 3: Creative Strategy
- [ ] Ad creative matrix maps different scripts to different audience segments
- [ ] Multiple ad lengths are planned (6s bumper, 15s non-skip, 30-60s skippable, 2-5min long-form)
- [ ] ADUCATE framework is applied to all long-form scripts
- [ ] 3 Acts structure is used for storytelling-based ads
- [ ] Thumbnail strategy is defined for TrueView discovery/in-feed ads
- [ ] Creative testing rotation schedule is documented (new creatives every 2-4 weeks)

## Section 4: Campaign Architecture
- [ ] Campaign structure separates prospecting from remarketing
- [ ] Ad groups are organized by audience segment or creative theme
- [ ] Bid strategy is selected based on campaign maturity and data volume
- [ ] Frequency capping is applied to prevent viewer fatigue
- [ ] Device targeting preferences are set based on conversion data

## Section 5: Budget & Pacing
- [ ] Total YouTube budget is defined as a percentage of overall media spend
- [ ] Budget is split between testing (30%) and scaling (70%) campaigns
- [ ] Daily pacing is set to distribute budget evenly (not front-loaded)
- [ ] Budget flexibility exists for reallocating from underperforming to outperforming campaigns
- [ ] Break-even analysis determines the maximum acceptable cost per view and cost per conversion

## Section 6: Measurement & Optimization Plan
- [ ] KPIs are defined by campaign type (awareness = CPM/view rate, conversion = CPA/ROAS)
- [ ] Reporting dashboard is configured before launch
- [ ] Optimization triggers are documented (when to adjust bids, audiences, or creatives)
- [ ] Weekly optimization review process is established
- [ ] Monthly MAP review assesses overall strategy effectiveness and adjusts the plan

## Approval
- **Minimum pass rate**: 21/25 items (84%)
- **Reviewer**: breeze-youtube-strategist
- **Escalation**: If strategic foundation or audience strategy sections fail, the entire MAP needs reworking before any budget is committed
