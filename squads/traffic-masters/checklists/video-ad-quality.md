# Video Ad Quality
> **Type**: Quality Gate
> **Domain**: Creative Production
> **Reviewed by**: Creative Strategist

## Purpose
Ensures every video ad is crafted for maximum attention, engagement, and conversion within platform-specific constraints. Video is the highest-performing format when executed well and the most wasteful when executed poorly.

## Checklist

### Hook (First 3-5 Seconds)
- [ ] The opening frame or statement is designed to stop the scroll
- [ ] The hook creates curiosity, surprise, or emotional engagement
- [ ] The hook is relevant to the target audience and their pain or desire
- [ ] No logos, intros, or brand bumpers appear before the hook
- [ ] The hook works with sound off (visual pattern interrupt)
- [ ] Multiple hook variations have been produced for testing

### Problem and Solution
- [ ] The problem is clearly stated and relatable to the target audience
- [ ] The problem agitates the pain enough to create urgency
- [ ] The solution is presented as the logical answer to the problem
- [ ] The transition from problem to solution feels natural, not forced
- [ ] The unique mechanism or differentiator is communicated

### Credibility Elements
- [ ] At least one credibility element is present (testimonial, data, expert, social proof)
- [ ] Credibility is specific and verifiable (not vague claims)
- [ ] The credibility element appears within the first half of the video
- [ ] Authority signals match the audience's expectations
- [ ] Results or proof are shown rather than just stated

### Call to Action
- [ ] CTA is clearly visible and stated (verbal and visual)
- [ ] CTA appears at least twice (middle and end of the video)
- [ ] CTA tells the viewer exactly what to do next
- [ ] CTA language matches the offer and funnel destination
- [ ] CTA creates a reason to act now rather than later

### Audio Quality
- [ ] Audio is clear, well-leveled, and free of background noise
- [ ] Voiceover is professional and matches the brand tone
- [ ] Music supports the message without overpowering speech
- [ ] Audio levels are consistent throughout the video
- [ ] Sound design enhances key moments (transitions, reveals)

### Captions and Text
- [ ] Captions are present for the entire spoken content
- [ ] Captions are accurately timed and easy to read
- [ ] Text overlays reinforce key messages without cluttering
- [ ] Font size is readable on mobile devices
- [ ] Caption style is consistent with brand guidelines

### Safe Zones and Specs
- [ ] Critical elements are within platform safe zones
- [ ] Video is rendered in the correct aspect ratio for the target placement
- [ ] Multiple aspect ratios are produced (1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9 as needed)
- [ ] File size and resolution meet platform requirements
- [ ] No critical text or visuals are cut off on any placement

### Thumbnail
- [ ] Custom thumbnail is compelling and represents the video content
- [ ] Thumbnail works at small sizes on mobile feeds
- [ ] Thumbnail includes a clear visual subject or text hook
- [ ] Thumbnail does not use clickbait that misrepresents the content

### Length and Pacing
- [ ] Video length is appropriate for the platform and placement
- [ ] Pacing maintains engagement throughout (no slow sections)
- [ ] The core message is delivered within the first 15 seconds
- [ ] Longer videos (60s+) have justified rationale and engagement data
- [ ] Scene changes or visual variety prevent monotony

## Pass/Fail Criteria
A minimum of 8 out of 9 sections must pass. Hook and Call to Action sections are mandatory and cannot be the failing section.

## If Failed
Identify the weak sections and revise. Prioritize hook and CTA improvements as they have the highest impact on performance. Re-review before launching.
