# Headline Formulas for Ads

## Purpose
Proven headline formulas adapted for advertising across platforms.

## Result-Based Headlines
- "[Get/Achieve] [Specific Result] in [Timeframe]"
- "[Number] [People] Got [Result]. You Can Too."
- "From [Before State] to [After State] in [Time]"
- "How [Persona] [Achieved Result] Without [Objection]"

## Curiosity Headlines
- "The [Adjective] [Method] That [Unexpected Result]"
- "[Number] [Things] Your [Competitor/Industry] Doesn't Want You to Know"
- "Why [Common Belief] Is Costing You [Amount]"
- "The [Hidden/Secret/Unexpected] Reason Your [Area] Isn't Working"

## How-To Headlines
- "How to [Achieve Goal] Without [Pain Point]"
- "How to [Outcome] Even If You [Common Objection]"
- "[Number] Steps to [Desired Outcome]"
- "The [Audience]'s Guide to [Achieving Goal]"

## Social Proof Headlines
- "Why [Number]+ [Audience] Choose [Product/Brand]"
- "Trusted by [Recognizable Names/Companies]"
- "[Star Rating] Rated by [Number] [Customers/Users]"
- "The [Category] [Audience] Recommends Most"

## Urgency Headlines
- "[Offer] Ends [Date/Time] — Don't Miss Out"
- "Only [Number] Left at This Price"
- "Last Chance: [Benefit] Before [Deadline]"
- "[Season/Event] Special: [Offer] for [Audience]"
