# Benefit vs Feature Phrases

## Purpose
Transform features into benefits for more compelling ad copy.

## Conversion Formula
Feature: "What the product HAS or DOES"
Benefit: "What the customer GETS or FEELS"
Bridge: "...which means..." or "...so you can..."

## Examples by Category

### Software/SaaS
| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Cloud-based platform | Access from anywhere, never lose your work |
| Real-time analytics | Make decisions in minutes, not days |
| API integrations | Works with tools you already use — no switching |
| Automated reporting | Get your mornings back — reports build themselves |

### Ecommerce/Product
| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Organic ingredients | Feel good about what you put on your skin |
| 30-day battery life | Charge once a month and forget about it |
| Free 2-day shipping | Get it before the weekend |
| Machine washable | Less time doing laundry, more time living |

### Service/Consulting
| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Dedicated account manager | One person who knows your business inside out |
| Weekly strategy calls | Never feel lost — get expert guidance every week |
| Custom reporting | See exactly how your investment is performing |
| 24/7 support | Help when you need it, not when it's convenient for us |

## Writing Exercise
For every feature, ask "So what?" three times:
1. Feature: "We have a 98% uptime guarantee"
2. So what? "Your site won't go down"
3. So what? "You won't lose sales to downtime"
4. So what? "You can sleep at night knowing revenue is protected"

The deepest "so what" is usually the best benefit.
