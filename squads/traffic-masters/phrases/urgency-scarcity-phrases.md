# Urgency & Scarcity Phrases (Ethical)

> Ethical urgency and scarcity phrases for ad copy and campaigns. All phrases should be backed by real constraints — never fabricate urgency.

---

## Time-Based Urgency

### Deadline-Driven
- "This offer closes [specific date] — no extensions."
- "You have [X] days left to lock in this rate."
- "Registration closes at midnight on [date]."
- "Early-bird pricing ends [date]. Standard pricing applies after."
- "The window to join this cohort closes [date]."
- "Act before [date] — this pricing won't be available again until [next availability]."
- "Final day to claim your [offer/bonus/rate]."
- "Enrollment for [program/service] opens [date] and closes [date]."

### Season / Event-Driven
- "Get set up before [holiday/season] — your busiest period is [X] weeks away."
- "Q4 planning starts now. Don't enter peak season unprepared."
- "With [event/season] approaching, the time to act is now."
- "Brands that prepare for [season] now outperform those that wait."
- "The [industry event] is [timeframe] away — launch before your competitors do."

### Momentum-Based
- "The sooner you start, the sooner you see results."
- "Every week without optimization is a week of lost revenue."
- "Start today and you'll be fully operational by [future milestone]."
- "The algorithm rewards early movers — get in before the auction heats up."
- "Campaigns launched this week will be optimized in time for [upcoming period]."

## Quantity-Based Scarcity

### Limited Capacity
- "We accept [X] new clients per [month/quarter] to ensure quality."
- "Only [X] spots remain in this [cohort/program/tier]."
- "[X] of [Y] spots have been filled."
- "Our team can only onboard [X] accounts this month."
- "Due to demand, we're capping enrollment at [X] participants."

### Availability Windows
- "This program runs [X] times per year. Next cohort: [date]."
- "We open enrollment [frequency]. The next window is [date]."
- "Availability is first-come, first-served."
- "Once all spots are filled, the waitlist opens."
- "Current availability: [X] openings. Waitlist expected by [date]."

### Inventory / Access
- "Limited release — [X] units/licenses available."
- "This offer is available to the first [X] respondents."
- "Access is limited to maintain [quality/exclusivity/performance]."
- "We're releasing this in batches. Batch [X] is now open."
- "When it's gone, it's gone — we won't be restocking this [offer/tier]."

## Price-Based Urgency

### Introductory / Launch Pricing
- "Launch pricing: [X]% off for founding members. Regular price takes effect [date]."
- "Lock in the current rate before our scheduled price increase on [date]."
- "This introductory rate is only available during [period]."
- "Founding member pricing will never be offered again after [date]."
- "Secure today's price — our rates increase with each new tier of clients."

### Bonus-Driven
- "Sign up by [date] and receive [specific bonus] — valued at [amount]."
- "Early action bonus: [bonus item] included free when you start before [date]."
- "This week only: your subscription includes [bonus] at no extra cost."
- "The first [X] to enroll get [exclusive bonus]."
- "Claim your bonus bundle — only available with orders placed before [date]."

### Grandfathered Rates
- "Lock in this rate for life — new subscribers after [date] pay the updated price."
- "Your rate stays the same as long as you remain a member."
- "Current pricing is grandfathered for existing clients. New rates start [date]."
- "Subscribe now and you'll never pay the higher rate."
- "Early adopters keep their founding rate, guaranteed."

## Social Urgency

### Demand Signals
- "[X] people are currently viewing this page."
- "[X] [users/teams] signed up in the last [timeframe]."
- "This [item/program] was fully booked within [timeframe] last time."
- "Demand has [increased X%] since [time period]."
- "Our last [launch/cohort] sold out in [timeframe]."

### Competitive Framing
- "Your competitors are already using this. The gap widens every day you wait."
- "[X] companies in your industry adopted this in [time period]."
- "The brands moving on this now will have a significant head start."
- "Early adopters are already seeing [specific results]."
- "The market is shifting — those who act first gain the advantage."

## Consequence-Based Urgency

### Cost of Inaction
- "Every day without this costs you approximately [amount] in [waste/lost revenue]."
- "The cost of waiting: [specific quantified loss per time period]."
- "Delaying this decision doesn't make it cheaper — it makes it more expensive."
- "That inefficient campaign is burning [amount] per [time period] while you decide."
- "The opportunity cost of inaction compounds daily."

### Risk of Missing Out
- "This [market condition/algorithm change/trend] won't last — capitalize while you can."
- "The window for [specific opportunity] is closing as [market condition changes]."
- "CPMs are at seasonal lows right now. They historically [increase X%] by [month]."
- "Audience data from this [period] has a shelf life. Act while it's fresh."
- "Platform changes take effect on [date] — optimize before the landscape shifts."

---

## Ethical Guidelines

### Do
- Use urgency based on real constraints (actual deadlines, genuine capacity limits, real price changes)
- Be specific with dates, numbers, and availability
- Honor every deadline and limit you state — no silent extensions
- Make the consequence of inaction clear and honest
- Remove or update urgency messaging when the constraint has passed

### Do Not
- Fabricate countdown timers that reset on page refresh
- Claim limited availability when supply is unlimited
- Create false deadlines that get quietly extended
- Use "last chance" messaging repeatedly for the same offer
- Manufacture scarcity for digital products with no actual supply constraint
- Use fear-based urgency that manipulates vulnerable audiences

### Platform-Specific Compliance
- **Meta/Facebook:** Avoid "limited time" claims without substantiation
- **Google Ads:** Countdown customizers must reflect real deadlines
- **Email:** CAN-SPAM and GDPR require honest representation
- **Landing Pages:** FTC guidelines require truthful urgency claims
