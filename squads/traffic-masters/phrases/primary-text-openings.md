# Primary Text Openings

## Purpose
First lines of ad body copy that drive readthrough and clicks.

## Pain Openings
- "Tired of [painful situation] with no end in sight?"
- "If you're still [frustrating activity], read this."
- "Every [time period], you're losing [amount] because of [problem]."
- "[Pain point] is costing [audience] more than they realize."

## Story Openings
- "Last year, I was exactly where you are now."
- "When [name] first tried [approach], they expected to fail."
- "Nobody thought it would work. But then..."
- "I want to share something that changed everything for me."

## Data Openings
- "[Percentage]% of [audience] struggle with [problem]. Here's why."
- "After analyzing [number] [campaigns/accounts], we found..."
- "The average [metric] in [industry] is [number]. Ours is [better number]."
- "New research shows [surprising finding]."

## Direct Openings
- "Here's the truth about [topic] that nobody talks about."
- "You don't need [common belief]. You need [actual solution]."
- "I'm going to show you exactly how to [specific outcome]."
- "This is the [method] that [specific audience] uses to [result]."

## Social Proof Openings
- "[Number] [people] have already [achieved result] using this."
- "[Name] went from [before] to [after] in just [timeframe]."
- "Here's what [audience] are saying about [product/service]:"
- "Over [number] five-star reviews can't be wrong."

## Rules for First Lines
1. Must be visible without "See More" click (125 chars on Meta)
2. Should create reason to keep reading
3. Avoid starting with brand name (unless authority hook)
4. Match the ad creative's visual message
