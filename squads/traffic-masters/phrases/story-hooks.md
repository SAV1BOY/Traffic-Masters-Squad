# Story-Based Hook Openers

> Story hooks leverage narrative to create emotional engagement. These openers draw the audience into a story before delivering the message.

---

## Origin Story Hooks

Begin with how a discovery, product, or method came to be.

- "It started when I was staring at a dashboard full of red numbers at 2 AM..."
- "Three years ago, I was about to give up on paid ads entirely. Then I discovered something that changed everything."
- "I didn't set out to build a framework. I was just trying to save a failing campaign."
- "The first campaign I ever ran lost $3,000 in a week. That failure taught me the most important lesson of my career."
- "This approach was born from desperation — a client was ready to fire us, and we had 72 hours to turn things around."
- "I started in this industry with zero budget, zero connections, and zero idea what I was doing. Here's what I learned."
- "Every system we use today exists because something went catastrophically wrong in the past."
- "I spent two years doing everything the 'experts' recommended. My results were mediocre at best. Then I tried the opposite."

---

## Client Story Hooks

Begin with a client scenario that the audience can relate to.

- "When [Client Name / Descriptor] came to us, they were spending $[X]/month on ads and couldn't tell if any of it was working."
- "Our client was 60 days from shutting down their ad program entirely. Here's what we did in those 60 days."
- "She'd been through three agencies. Each one promised the world. None of them delivered. By the time she found us, she barely trusted anyone."
- "They were doing everything right on paper — the targeting, the creative, the landing pages. But something wasn't clicking. It took us one audit to find the problem."
- "A $2M brand came to us frustrated: 'We know ads should work for us. We just can't figure out why they don't.'"
- "He told me, 'I've never seen a positive ROAS month in two years of advertising.' Fourteen days later, he saw his first one."
- "The founder called us on a Friday afternoon. 'We launch Monday. Nothing is set up. Can you help?' Here's what happened."
- "They had the best product in their category. Their competitors had the best ads. Guess who was winning?"

---

## Personal Failure / Lesson Hooks

Begin with a personal mistake that leads to a valuable insight.

- "I once burned through $15,000 of a client's budget in 10 days because I ignored one setting. Here's the setting."
- "My biggest career mistake wasn't a bad campaign — it was running a good campaign for the wrong audience."
- "I spent six months obsessing over the wrong metric. By the time I realized it, the damage was done."
- "I used to think great creative was all that mattered. Then I ran the best ad I'd ever made — and it bombed."
- "The campaign was perfect. The targeting was precise. The offer was irresistible. And it completely failed. Here's why."
- "I learned more from one disastrous launch than from a year of successful campaigns."
- "The worst advice I ever followed: 'Just increase the budget and the algorithm will figure it out.'"
- "I almost got fired over a reporting error that misrepresented three months of data. It's the reason I now triple-check everything."

## "Day in the Life" Hooks

Begin with a relatable moment from the audience's daily experience.

- "You open your laptop Monday morning. Notifications are piling up. CPA is up 40% since Friday. Sound familiar?"
- "It's budget review season. Your boss wants to know exactly what the last quarter of ad spend produced. Can you answer confidently?"
- "You're scrolling through your ad manager, and every campaign has a different cost per result. Some are great. Some are terrible. Most are somewhere in the middle. What do you do?"
- "The CEO walks into your office and asks, 'Are our ads working?' You have three seconds to answer."
- "Picture this: You launch a campaign on Monday. By Wednesday, your phone is ringing with qualified leads. By Friday, your sales team is booked for the next two weeks."
- "Your competitor just launched a campaign that's everywhere — your feed, your inbox, your YouTube pre-roll. What's your move?"
- "You've been testing for weeks. Nothing is winning. The team is losing confidence. The budget review is next Thursday."
- "You check your phone at 7 AM. Three alerts. Spend is pacing 200% above target. An ad was disapproved overnight. And someone changed the campaign settings without telling you."

## Transformation Arc Hooks

Begin with a before state, hint at the journey, and promise the after.

- "Twelve months ago, this account was hemorrhaging money. Today, it's their most profitable marketing channel. Here's what changed."
- "From 'turn off all ads' to 'double the budget' — the 90-day journey that changed how this brand thinks about paid media."
- "She went from dreading her ad account to checking it with excitement. The difference wasn't talent — it was process."
- "They were a team of two, managing six platforms with spreadsheets. Now they run a streamlined operation that outperforms teams three times their size."
- "A year ago, they couldn't break $50K/month in revenue. Today, they consistently hit $300K. The ads didn't change — the strategy did."
- "He used to spend Sunday nights worrying about Monday's campaign performance. Now he spends Sunday nights planning his team's growth."
- "From 'advertising doesn't work for our industry' to 'advertising is our primary growth engine' — the full story."
- "We took an account from negative ROAS to 4.5x in 45 days. But the real story isn't the result — it's the three decisions that made it possible."

---

## Contrarian / Against-the-Grain Hooks

Begin with a statement that goes against conventional wisdom, framed as a story.

- "Everyone told me to narrow my targeting. So I did the opposite. I went as broad as possible. Here's what happened."
- "The day I stopped following 'best practices' was the day my campaigns actually started performing."
- "I told a client to cut their best-performing campaign. They thought I was crazy. Three weeks later, they understood."
- "Every agency was telling this brand to spend more on Meta. I told them to stop spending on Meta entirely. Here's why."
- "The conventional wisdom says to test one variable at a time. I tested everything at once. And I'd do it again."
- "I walked into a pitch meeting and told the CMO their entire ad strategy was wrong. I didn't have another meeting with them for three months. Then they called me back."
- "When the algorithm changed, everyone panicked. We saw it as the biggest opportunity of the year."
- "They fired their last agency for being too conservative. They almost fired us for the same thing — until month three."

---

## Micro-Story Hooks (Ultra-Short)

For platforms with limited space or attention — convey a story in one to two sentences.

- "Last Tuesday, a single creative change added $12K in revenue. Here's the change."
- "We found a $4,000/month leak in a client's account. It took 5 minutes to fix."
- "One audience exclusion. That's all it took to cut their CPA by 35%."
- "A client asked me the simplest question: 'Where do our best customers come from?' Nobody on their team could answer it."
- "Day 1: Launched the campaign. Day 3: First sale. Day 7: Profitable. Day 30: Scaled to 5 figures."
- "We paused 60% of their campaigns. Revenue went up."
- "The ad that outperformed everything else? Plain text on a white background."
- "Three words in the headline. That was the only difference between the winner and the loser."

---

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Truth is non-negotiable** — Every story must be true or clearly presented as a hypothetical scenario
2. **Get to the point** — Story hooks create engagement, but meandering loses it; open the loop fast
3. **One story per asset** — Multiple stories dilute each other
4. **Match story to audience** — A founder relates to founder stories; a media buyer relates to media buyer stories
5. **Emotional specificity** — "I was terrified" is more compelling than "I was worried"; use precise emotional language
6. **Include sensory details** — "Staring at a dashboard at 2 AM" is more vivid than "having a bad day at work"
7. **Close the loop** — Every story hook must deliver a satisfying resolution or insight
8. **Video advantage** — Story hooks are especially powerful in video formats where tone of voice adds dimension
