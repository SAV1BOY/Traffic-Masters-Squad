# CTAs Library

## Purpose
Call-to-action phrases organized by funnel stage and intent level.

## TOFU — Low Commitment CTAs
- "Learn More"
- "See How It Works"
- "Watch the Free Training"
- "Discover the [Method/System]"
- "Get the Guide"
- "Take the Quiz"

## MOFU — Medium Commitment CTAs
- "Download Free [Resource]"
- "Save Your Seat"
- "Start Your Free Trial"
- "See the Case Studies"
- "Compare Plans"
- "Get Your Free Audit"

## BOFU — High Commitment CTAs
- "Start Now"
- "Get Started Today"
- "Buy Now — Save [%]"
- "Claim Your [Offer]"
- "Book Your Call"
- "Apply Now"
- "Join [Number]+ [People] Today"

## Ecommerce-Specific CTAs
- "Shop Now"
- "Add to Cart"
- "Complete Your Order"
- "Get Yours Before They're Gone"
- "Shop the Collection"
- "Grab the Bundle"

## Urgency-Enhanced CTAs
- "Act Now — [Offer] Ends [Date]"
- "Don't Miss Out — [Number] Left"
- "Claim Before [Deadline]"
- "Limited Time: [Offer]"

## CTA Matching Guide

| Funnel Stage | Best CTA Type | Example |
|-------------|--------------|---------|
| Cold/Aware | Curiosity | "See How It Works" |
| Warm/Consider | Value Exchange | "Get Your Free Guide" |
| Hot/Decide | Direct Action | "Start Your Free Trial" |
| Customer/Retain | Loyalty | "Unlock VIP Access" |
