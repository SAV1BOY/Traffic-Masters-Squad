# Objection Reframes

## Purpose
Ready-to-use phrases that reframe common buyer objections.

## "It's too expensive"
- "It costs less than [relatable daily expense] per day"
- "Think of it as an investment that pays back [X]x"
- "The real cost? Not solving [problem] for another [timeframe]"
- "Compared to [alternative], you're saving [amount]"
- "Our [guarantee] means zero risk to try it"

## "I don't have time"
- "Set it up in [specific short time]. That's it."
- "You'll save [X hours/week] once it's running"
- "Takes less time than your morning coffee"
- "We handle everything — you just approve"
- "5 minutes now saves you [X hours] later"

## "Will it work for me?"
- "If you're a [specific persona], this was built for you"
- "[Name] was skeptical too. Then they got [specific result]"
- "It works for [audience] even if they've [common objection]"
- "Our [guarantee] means if it doesn't work, you pay nothing"
- "[Number]+ [people like you] have seen [result]"

## "I need to think about it"
- "The best time to start was yesterday. The next best? Today."
- "[Number] people joined while you were thinking about it"
- "Every day without [solution] costs you [specific amount]"
- "This offer won't be available at this price after [date]"

## "I've tried similar things before"
- "This isn't like [old solution]. Here's what's different:"
- "We built this because everything else failed us too"
- "The key difference is [specific mechanism]"
- "[Name] tried 3 alternatives before finding this one"

## Usage Notes
- Use in retargeting copy for warm/hot audiences
- Match objection to audience awareness stage
- Test different reframes to find what resonates
- Pair with proof to maximize effectiveness
