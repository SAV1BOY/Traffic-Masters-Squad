# Hooks & Headlines Library

> A curated library of 50+ proven ad hooks and headline formulas, organized by psychological angle. Adapt these templates to your product, audience, and platform.

---

## Pain Angle

| # | Formula | Example |
|---|---------|---------|

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Test in batches** — Run 3-5 hook variations per ad set to find winners
2. **Match hook to audience temperature** — Pain hooks for cold, desire hooks for warm, proof hooks for hot
3. **Adapt the formula** — Never copy verbatim; tailor specifics (numbers, industry, platform) to your context
4. **Pair with strong visuals** — The hook gets attention; the visual holds it
5. **Rotate monthly** — Even winning hooks fatigue; refresh with new angles regularly
