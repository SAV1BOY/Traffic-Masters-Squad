# Words That Convert

## Purpose
High-performance words proven to increase engagement and conversion in ad copy.

## Action Words
- Get, Start, Join, Discover, Unlock, Build, Create, Try, Grab, Claim

## Value Words
- Free, Proven, Exclusive, Premium, Guaranteed, Instant, Bonus, Save

## Urgency Words
- Now, Today, Limited, Last, Final, Before, Hurry, Deadline, Only

## Trust Words
- Trusted, Certified, Verified, Secure, Official, Endorsed, Backed

## Emotional Words
- Transform, Breakthrough, Secret, Insider, Powerful, Life-changing

## Specificity Words
- Exactly, Step-by-step, Blueprint, Roadmap, Framework, System

## Curiosity Words
- Hidden, Surprising, Unexpected, Little-known, Controversial, Revealed

## Community Words
- Join, Community, Member, Insider, Together, Exclusive, VIP

## Results Words
- Results, Outcome, Impact, Growth, Revenue, Profit, Return

## Sensory Words
- Feel, Experience, Imagine, Picture, Taste, Touch, Hear

## Performance by Context

| Word Category | Best For | Platform |
|--------------|----------|----------|
| Action | CTAs | All |
| Value | Headlines | All |
| Urgency | BOFU copy | Meta, Email |
| Trust | B2B | LinkedIn, Google |
| Emotional | TOFU | Meta, TikTok |
| Specificity | Lead gen | Google, LinkedIn |
| Curiosity | Video hooks | YouTube, TikTok |

## Usage Tips
- Combine categories: "Get your free blueprint" (action + value + specificity)
- Test power words in headlines — small word changes yield big CTR shifts
- Platform compliance: some power words trigger policy review (e.g., "guaranteed")
