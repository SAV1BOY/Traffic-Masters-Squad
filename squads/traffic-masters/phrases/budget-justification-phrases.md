# Budget Justification Phrases

## Purpose
Phrases for justifying budget requests, increases, and allocation decisions.

## Requesting Budget Increase
- "At current efficiency ($[CPA] CPA), every additional $1K generates $[X]K in revenue"
- "We're leaving an estimated $[X] in revenue on the table at current spend levels"
- "Scaling from $[X] to $[Y] would add approximately [number] conversions at [CPA]"
- "Our marginal CPA analysis shows room to scale [%] before hitting diminishing returns"
- "Competitor analysis shows they're outspending us [X]x in [channel]"

## Defending Current Budget
- "Current spend is generating [ROAS]x return — well above the [target]x target"
- "Reducing budget would sacrifice [number] profitable conversions per month"
- "LTV analysis shows our CPA of $[X] pays back within [Y] months"
- "Budget efficiency has improved [%] over [period]"

## Justifying Reallocation
- "[Platform A] is delivering [X]x ROAS vs [Platform B] at [Y]x — reallocation improves blended performance"
- "Shifting [%] from [underperformer] to [outperformer] projects [impact]"
- "Testing budget of [%] is necessary to avoid creative fatigue in [timeframe]"

## Justifying New Platform Spend
- "Test budget of $[X] will validate opportunity with statistical significance in [Y] weeks"
- "Early signals from [platform] show [metric] at [value] vs [benchmark]"
- "Platform diversification reduces risk of [single platform dependency]"

## Justifying Spend During Losses
- "Learning phase investment of $[X] is expected to stabilize within [timeframe]"
- "Historical pattern shows campaigns reach target CPA by week [Y]"
- "Initial losses are within acceptable range for [test type] testing"

## ROI Framing
- "For every $1 invested in paid traffic, we return $[X] in revenue"
- "Blended CAC of $[X] against LTV of $[Y] gives [ratio] LTV:CAC ratio"
- "Traffic investment generates [%] of total company revenue"
