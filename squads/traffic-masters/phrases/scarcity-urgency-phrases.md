# Scarcity & Urgency Phrases

## Purpose
Ethical urgency and scarcity phrases for ad copy and landing pages.

## Time-Based Urgency
- "Offer ends [specific date/time]"
- "Last day to get [offer]"
- "Price increases on [date]"
- "[Hours/days] left at this price"
- "This [week/month] only"

## Quantity-Based Scarcity
- "Only [number] spots remaining"
- "[Number] left in stock"
- "Limited to [number] per [customer/order]"
- "We only accept [number] new [clients/members] per [period]"
- "Batch [number] — once they're gone, they're gone"

## Demand-Based Urgency
- "[Number] people are viewing this right now"
- "[Number] purchased in the last [timeframe]"
- "This sold out last time in [timeframe]"
- "Waitlist is growing — secure your spot now"

## Seasonal Urgency
- "[Holiday/Event] sale — [dates] only"
- "Pre-[season] pricing ends [date]"
- "Early bird pricing available until [date]"
- "[Year] pricing locked in if you join by [date]"

## Soft Urgency (Evergreen)
- "The sooner you start, the sooner you see results"
- "Every day without [solution] costs you [amount]"
- "Don't let another [week/month] go by without [action]"
- "Your competitors aren't waiting. Why are you?"

## Ethical Guidelines
1. Only use real scarcity (actual limited inventory or time)
2. Fake countdown timers erode trust and may violate policies
3. Evergreen urgency is acceptable if the opportunity cost is real
4. Be transparent — if the offer returns, don't call it "last chance"
5. Platform policies prohibit misleading urgency tactics
