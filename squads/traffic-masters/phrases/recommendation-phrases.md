# Standard Phrases for Strategic Recommendations

> Professional language for presenting recommendations to clients and stakeholders, organized by recommendation type.

---

## Recommendation Structure

Every recommendation should follow this pattern:

> **[Action Verb] + [Specific Tactic] + [Expected Outcome] + [Rationale]**

Example: "We recommend reallocating 30% of [Campaign A]'s budget to [Campaign B], which is expected to reduce blended CPA by approximately 15%, based on the current performance differential between the two campaigns."

---

## Budget Recommendations

### Increase Budget
- "We recommend increasing [Campaign/Channel] budget from $[X] to $[Y] per [period], based on consistent [metric] performance above target for [X] consecutive [periods]."
- "The data supports a [X]% budget increase on [Campaign], which is currently constrained by budget and leaving conversions on the table."
- "Given current efficiency levels, scaling spend by $[X] per [period] is projected to yield an additional [Y] [conversions/revenue] at a CPA within [Z]% of current levels."
- "We recommend a phased budget increase of [X]% per [period] to scale while monitoring for diminishing returns."

### Decrease Budget
- "We recommend reducing [Campaign/Channel] budget by [X]%, redirecting those funds to [higher-performing area]."
- "Current performance does not justify the current spend level. A reduction to $[X] would improve overall account efficiency."
- "Diminishing returns are evident above $[X] daily spend on [Campaign]. We recommend capping at that level."

### Reallocate Budget
- "We recommend shifting $[X] from [Source] to [Destination], where marginal ROAS is [Y]x higher."
- "Budget reallocation across channels based on marginal CPA analysis would yield an estimated [X]% efficiency improvement."
- "Cross-campaign reallocation is recommended to balance spend with performance, prioritizing [top performers]."

## Creative Recommendations

### New Creative
- "We recommend developing [X] new creative assets focused on [angle/theme] to combat fatigue indicators on current assets."
- "Testing [creative format — e.g., UGC, static, carousel] is recommended based on platform trend data and competitor analysis."
- "A new creative batch is needed by [date] to maintain performance ahead of [season/event]."
- "We recommend producing [X] hook variations for the current body creative to extend asset lifespan."

### Creative Rotation
- "Current top-performing creative has been running for [X] days and is showing early fatigue signals. We recommend introducing [Y] new variants while the performer still delivers."
- "We recommend a structured rotation schedule: introduce [X] new creatives per [period] while retiring assets below [CTR/engagement] thresholds."
- "Pausing [Creative A] and replacing with a refreshed version that maintains the winning concept but updates the visual treatment."

### Creative Testing
- "We recommend an A/B test comparing [Variable A] vs. [Variable B] on [Campaign], with a minimum test duration of [X] days."
- "A multivariate test across [X] headline variants and [Y] visual variants is recommended to identify the optimal combination."
- "We recommend isolating [specific element] in the next test to determine its impact on [metric]."

---

## Audience Recommendations

### Expansion
- "We recommend expanding targeting to include [new audience segment], based on customer data analysis showing strong overlap."
- "Testing a broader audience is recommended to allow the algorithm more room for optimization within the $[X] daily budget."
- "Adding [demographic/interest/behavioral] targeting layers is expected to increase qualified reach by [X]%."

### Refinement
- "We recommend narrowing the audience by excluding [segment], which data shows converts at [X]% the rate of other segments."
- "Applying [exclusion criteria] would remove an estimated [X]% of non-converting impressions."
- "Audience segmentation analysis suggests splitting [current audience] into [Segment A] and [Segment B] for tailored messaging."

### Refresh
- "Seed audiences for lookalikes should be refreshed using the last [X] days of converter data to reflect current buyer profiles."
- "We recommend rebuilding custom audiences using updated CRM data synced as of [date]."
- "Retargeting windows should be shortened from [X] days to [Y] days based on time-to-conversion analysis."

---

## Campaign Structure Recommendations

- "We recommend consolidating [X] campaigns into [Y] to improve algorithmic learning and reduce audience fragmentation."
- "Campaign restructuring from [current structure] to [proposed structure] is recommended to align with platform best practices."
- "Separating prospecting and retargeting into distinct campaigns would provide clearer budget control and performance visibility."
- "Adding a dedicated testing campaign with [X]% of total budget would enable structured experimentation without impacting core performance."
- "We recommend implementing [campaign structure name — e.g., CBO, ABO, Advantage+] based on current account maturity and objectives."

---

## Platform & Channel Recommendations

### Add Channel
- "Based on audience analysis, we recommend piloting [Platform] with an initial budget of $[X] per [period] for [X] weeks."
- "Adding [Channel] to the media mix is projected to reach an incremental [X] unique users not currently reached through existing channels."
- "The competitive landscape on [Platform] shows lower CPMs and fewer active advertisers in [category], presenting a first-mover opportunity."

### Optimize Channel Mix
- "We recommend shifting [X]% of spend from [Channel A] to [Channel B] based on cross-channel incrementality analysis."
- "Channel-level analysis indicates [Channel] delivers the highest marginal efficiency, warranting increased investment."
- "Diminishing returns on [Channel] suggest the budget ceiling has been reached; diversifying to [alternative] is recommended."

---

## Testing Recommendations

- "We recommend the following test: [Test Name] — comparing [Variant A] vs. [Variant B], measuring [Primary Metric], over [Duration], with a minimum sample of [X] conversions per variant."
- "This test is designed to answer the question: [Specific Question]. The result will inform [strategic decision]."
- "We propose a sequential testing roadmap: Test 1: [Description], Test 2: [Description], Test 3: [Description]."
- "Based on the results of [Previous Test], we recommend proceeding with [winning approach] and testing [next variable]."
- "We recommend holding [winning variant] as the new control and introducing [X] challenger variants."

---

## Tracking & Measurement Recommendations

- "We recommend implementing [tracking solution] to address the current attribution gap in [area]."
- "Server-side tracking implementation is recommended to recover an estimated [X]% of lost conversion data."
- "A UTM audit and standardization across all active campaigns would improve attribution accuracy significantly."
- "We recommend adding [event/conversion action] to the tracking setup to capture [funnel stage] data."
- "Switching from [current attribution model] to [recommended model] would provide a more accurate view of channel contribution."

---

## Risk Caveats

Include appropriate caveats with recommendations to set expectations.

- "Note: This recommendation is based on current data trends. Results may vary as market conditions change."
- "Expected impact is an estimate based on historical performance patterns and may differ from actual outcomes."
- "We recommend a [X]-day monitoring period after implementation to validate the expected impact."
- "This recommendation carries [low/moderate/elevated] risk due to [specific factor]. Mitigation: [specific safeguard]."
- "Results of this change may take [X] days to materialize as the platform re-enters the learning phase."

---

## Priority & Sequencing Phrases

- "We rank this recommendation as [Priority 1 / High / Critical] based on expected impact and ease of implementation."
- "Recommended implementation order: (1) [Action], (2) [Action], (3) [Action] — sequenced by dependency and expected impact."
- "Quick wins (implementable this week): [List]. Strategic initiatives (require planning): [List]."
- "This recommendation should be implemented before [dependent action] to maximize the combined effect."
- "We suggest addressing [foundational issue] first, as other optimizations depend on it being resolved."

---

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Be specific and actionable** — "Improve creative" is not a recommendation; "Launch 3 new UGC-style video ads focused on pain-point messaging by [date]" is
2. **Quantify expected impact** — Include projected improvement ranges with confidence levels
3. **Explain the reasoning** — Clients and stakeholders need to understand why, not just what
4. **Include timelines** — Every recommendation should have a proposed implementation date
5. **Acknowledge risks** — Transparent risk assessment builds trust more than unbounded confidence
6. **Prioritize clearly** — When presenting multiple recommendations, rank them by impact and urgency
