# Pain-Agitation Phrases for Ad Copy

> Pain-agitation phrases organized by industry vertical. These phrases call out specific frustrations and amplify them to motivate action.

---

## E-Commerce / DTC

### Awareness of Problem
- "You're spending more on ads every month, but revenue isn't keeping up."
- "Your product pages get traffic, but your cart abandonment rate tells the real story."
- "You keep launching new creatives, yet nothing moves the needle."
- "Every month, your customer acquisition cost creeps higher — and your margins shrink."
- "You're competing with brands that have 10x your budget, and it shows."

### Agitation
- "Meanwhile, your competitors are locking in customers at half the cost you're paying."
- "That $50 CPA used to be $30 — and it's only going up."
- "If you can't profitably acquire customers, growth isn't growth — it's a cash burn."
- "The longer you run inefficient campaigns, the more runway you lose."
- "Every abandoned cart is a customer who wanted to buy — but something stopped them."

## SaaS / B2B Technology

### Awareness of Problem
- "Your pipeline looks full, but your close rate tells a different story."
- "You're generating leads, but sales says they're unqualified."
- "Your demo requests dropped 30% this quarter and nobody knows why."
- "You've invested in marketing automation, but your funnel still leaks."
- "MQL to SQL conversion is stalling, and finger-pointing between teams has started."

### Agitation
- "Every unqualified lead that enters your pipeline wastes your sales team's time and your budget."
- "Your CAC payback period is stretching past 18 months — investors are noticing."
- "That 'full pipeline' is an illusion when 80% of leads go dark after first contact."
- "Your competitors are booking demos while your prospects are stuck in a nurture loop."
- "Without attribution clarity, you're flying blind — and budget season is approaching."

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Lead with pain, follow with solution** — Never leave the audience in agitation without offering a path forward
2. **Be specific** — Vague pain is ignorable; specific, quantified pain is undeniable
3. **Match intensity to awareness** — Problem-unaware audiences need lighter pain statements; problem-aware audiences respond to direct agitation
4. **Stay ethical** — Agitate real problems, never fabricate or exaggerate pain
5. **Test pain vs. desire** — Some audiences respond better to aspiration than agitation; A/B test both angles
6. **Customize the numbers** — Replace placeholder metrics with data relevant to your client's industry benchmarks
