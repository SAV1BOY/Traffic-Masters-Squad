# Email Subject Line Formulas

> Email subject line templates for traffic-related communications including campaign updates, performance reports, client outreach, and internal communications.

---

## Performance Report Subject Lines

### Weekly Reports
- "Weekly Performance Report: [Date Range] — [Account/Brand Name]"
- "[Account] Weekly Recap: [Key Metric] [Direction] [Amount]%"
- "Your Week in Numbers: [Headline Metric] | [Date Range]"
- "Campaign Performance Update — Week of [Date]"
- "[Account] Ads: This week's wins, watchpoints, and next steps"

### Monthly Reports
- "Monthly Performance Summary: [Month] [Year] — [Account]"
- "[Month] Results: [Headline Number] at [Key Metric]"
- "Your [Month] Ad Performance — Full Breakdown Inside"
- "[Account] — [Month] in Review: [One-Line Summary]"
- "End-of-Month Report: Here's where [Account] stands"

### Quarterly / Annual Reports
- "Q[X] [Year] Performance Review — [Account]"
- "[Year] Annual Performance Summary — [Account/Brand]"
- "Quarterly Business Review: Key Findings & Recommendations"
- "[Quarter] Wrap-Up: Trends, Results, and the Road Ahead"

## Alert & Notification Subject Lines

### Performance Alerts
- "ALERT: [Metric] [exceeded/dropped below] threshold — [Account]"
- "Action Needed: [Campaign/Ad Set] performance anomaly detected"
- "Heads up: [Metric] changed [X]% in the last [timeframe]"
- "Budget Alert: [Campaign] pacing [X]% [above/below] target"
- "Urgent: [Platform] ad disapprovals on [Account] — [X] ads affected"

### Positive Alerts
- "New milestone: [Account] hit [achievement]"
- "Winner found: [Creative/Audience] outperforming by [X]%"
- "Good news: [Metric] reached best-ever level this [period]"
- "Scaling opportunity identified in [Campaign/Account]"

---

## Strategy & Recommendation Subject Lines

- "Recommended: [Specific Action] for [Account] — Expected Impact: [X]%"
- "Strategy Update: Proposed changes for [Account] — [Month/Quarter]"
- "Optimization Opportunity: [Brief Description] — [Account]"
- "New Test Proposal: [Test Description] for [Account]"
- "[Account]: 3 changes we recommend making this week"
- "Scaling Plan: How to take [Account] from $[X] to $[Y]/month"
- "Creative Refresh Needed: [Account] fatigue indicators rising"
- "Audience Expansion Proposal — [Account]"

## Client Communication Subject Lines

### Onboarding
- "Welcome to [Service/Squad] — Here's what happens next"
- "Getting Started: Your onboarding checklist for [Account]"
- "Account Setup Complete — [Account] is ready to launch"
- "Your [Service] kick-off: Key dates and deliverables"
- "Access Granted: Here's how to view your dashboard"

### Check-In / Relationship
- "Quick check-in: How are things going with [Campaign/Account]?"
- "Catching up: [Account] progress and upcoming priorities"
- "Thinking about [Account]'s next phase — a few ideas"
- "Before our meeting: Key talking points for [Date]"
- "Following up on our conversation about [Topic]"

### Renewal / Upsell
- "Your [Service] contract renewal — options inside"
- "Unlocking the next level: New capabilities available for [Account]"
- "What's next for [Account]? A few growth paths to consider"
- "Your results so far — and how we can build on them"

## Internal Team Communication Subject Lines

### Status Updates
- "Daily Standup: [Date] — Campaign Status Across Accounts"
- "Weekly Team Sync Notes: [Date Range]"
- "Platform Update: [Platform] changed [Feature] — impact assessment"
- "Account Handoff: [Account] moving from [Person A] to [Person B]"

### Knowledge Sharing
- "Insight: [Discovery] — applicable to [Account Type]"
- "Test Result: [Test Description] — [Outcome Summary]"
- "New Benchmark Data: [Category] — [Timeframe]"
- "Playbook Update: [Framework/Process] has been revised"
- "Learning: What worked (and didn't) on [Account/Campaign]"

### Escalation
- "Escalation: [Account] — [Issue Summary] — Action needed by [Date]"
- "Priority: [Account] requires immediate attention — [Issue]"
- "Risk Flag: [Account/Campaign] — [Brief Description]"

## Prospecting / Outreach Subject Lines

### Cold Outreach
- "Quick question about [Company]'s ad strategy"
- "I noticed something about [Company]'s [Platform] campaigns"
- "[Mutual Connection] suggested I reach out"
- "Idea for [Company] — [specific, concise benefit]"
- "Saw [Company]'s [ad/campaign/post] — had a thought"

### Follow-Up
- "Following up: [Original Subject]"
- "Re: [Company]'s ad performance — one more thought"
- "Did you get a chance to review [Resource/Proposal]?"
- "Last note on this: [Brief Value Statement]"
- "Before I close the loop — [One-Sentence Value Prop]"

### Re-Engagement
- "It's been a while — here's what's changed in [Area]"
- "New [feature/capability/data] — thought of [Company]"
- "Quick update since we last spoke"
- "[Company] — are you still looking to [Original Goal]?"

## Formula Patterns That Work

### Number-Based
- "[X] [things] you need to know about [topic]"
- "[X]% [improvement/change] — here's how"
- "[X] minutes to read. [Y] hours of insight."

### Question-Based
- "Are you making this [topic] mistake?"
- "What would [X]% better [metric] mean for [Company]?"
- "Have you seen [this trend/data point/change]?"

### Urgency-Based
- "Before [date/event]: [Action/Recommendation]"
- "Time-sensitive: [Opportunity/Issue] — [Account]"
- "This week only: [Offer/Availability]"

### Personalization-Based
- "[First Name], your [Account/Campaign] update is ready"
- "For [Company]: [Specific Recommendation]"
- "[First Name] — a quick note about [Relevant Topic]"

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Keep it under 50 characters** — Subject lines over 50 characters get cut off on mobile
2. **Front-load the important words** — The first 3-4 words determine whether it gets opened
3. **Avoid spam triggers** — Words like "FREE," "GUARANTEED," excessive caps, and multiple exclamation marks can trigger filters
4. **Be specific over clever** — Clarity drives opens more reliably than cleverness
5. **Test subject lines** — A/B test subject lines in email campaigns; small variations can produce large differences
6. **Match subject to content** — Misleading subject lines erode trust and increase unsubscribes
7. **Use preview text** — The preview text (preheader) is a second subject line; use it to complement, not repeat
8. **Personalize where possible** — Including the recipient's name or company can increase open rates
