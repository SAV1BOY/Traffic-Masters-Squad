# CTA Phrases by Funnel Stage

> Call-to-action phrases organized by funnel stage. Select the appropriate CTA based on where the prospect sits in the buyer journey.

---

## TOFU — Top of Funnel (Awareness)

Goal: Generate initial engagement. Low commitment, high curiosity.

### Educational CTAs
- "Learn more"
- "See how it works"
- "Discover the difference"
- "Find out why"
- "Explore the approach"
- "Watch the breakdown"
- "Read the full guide"

### Content-Driven CTAs
- "Watch the free training"
- "Get the free guide"
- "Download the playbook"
- "Access the resource library"
- "See the full report"
- "Watch the 2-minute explainer"
- "Browse the collection"

### Engagement CTAs
- "Take the quiz"
- "See where you stand"
- "Calculate your score"
- "Compare your results"
- "Find your fit"
- "Test your knowledge"
- "See if you qualify"

### Social / Share CTAs
- "Share this with your team"
- "Tag someone who needs this"
- "Save this for later"
- "Follow for more insights"
- "Join the conversation"

- "Start today"
- "Claim your plan"
- "Activate your account"
- "Choose your package"

## CTA Selection Matrix

| Audience Temperature | Commitment Level | Recommended CTA Type |
|---------------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Cold (unaware) | Very low | Educational, content-driven |
| Cool (problem-aware) | Low | Quiz, calculator, guide |
| Warm (solution-aware) | Medium | Demo, trial, consultation |
| Hot (product-aware) | High | Purchase, claim, activate |
| Customer (post-purchase) | Variable | Upsell, referral, review |

---

## Best Practices

1. **One CTA per asset** — Multiple CTAs create decision paralysis; pick the single most appropriate action
2. **Match CTA to ad objective** — If your campaign optimizes for leads, use lead CTAs, not purchase CTAs
3. **Use first person where possible** — "Get my free guide" often outperforms "Get your free guide"
4. **Add specificity** — "Download the 12-page playbook" outperforms "Download now"
5. **Test verb variations** — "Get" vs "Claim" vs "Access" vs "Unlock" can yield different results
6. **Button vs. text link** — BOFU benefits from buttons; TOFU can use softer in-text links
7. **Mobile context** — Shorter CTAs perform better on mobile placements
