# Social Proof Phrases for Ads

## Purpose
Ready-to-use social proof copy elements for ad creative.

## Crowd Proof
- "Join [number]+ [audience] who already [action/result]"
- "[Number] people signed up this week alone"
- "The [category] that [number] [professionals/customers] trust"
- "Why are [number] [people] making the switch?"

## Rating Proof
- "Rated [score]/5 by [number] verified [reviewers]"
- "[Number] five-star reviews on [platform]"
- "#1 rated [category] on [review site]"
- "[Score]% customer satisfaction rate"

## Named Proof
- "'[Short powerful quote]' — [Full Name], [Title]"
- "See why [Name] at [Company] chose [product]"
- "[Name] went from [X] to [Y] using [product]"
- "As recommended by [Name], [Credential]"

## Logo/Brand Proof
- "Trusted by teams at [Company], [Company], and [Company]"
- "Used by [industry] leaders including..."
- "Partnered with [recognizable brand]"

## Media Proof
- "As seen in [Publication]"
- "Featured by [Media Outlet]"
- "[Award Name] winner, [Year]"

## Momentum Proof
- "[Number] new customers this month"
- "Fastest-growing [category] in [market]"
- "Growing [X]% year over year"
- "[Number] [results] delivered and counting"

## Placement Tips
- Above the fold for immediate trust
- Near CTA to reduce last-second hesitation
- In ad copy first line for scroll-stopping power
- As headline for awareness campaigns
- In creative (logo bars, star ratings, quote overlays)
