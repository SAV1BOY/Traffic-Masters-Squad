# Hooks Library

## Purpose
Ready-to-use hook templates organized by type.

## Question Hooks
- "What if you could [outcome] without [pain]?"
- "Are you still [frustrating activity] every [timeframe]?"
- "Why do 90% of [audience] fail at [goal]?"
- "What would change if you could [specific result] by [date]?"

## Bold Claim Hooks
- "We [achieved result] in [timeframe] using only [method]"
- "[Specific number] [people] have already [result]"
- "This one change added [amount] to our [metric]"
- "The [#1/fastest/only] way to [outcome] in [year]"

## Story Hooks
- "Six months ago, I was [relatable painful situation]..."
- "I almost gave up on [goal] until I discovered..."
- "Nobody believed it would work. Then we saw the results."
- "Here's what happened when we tried [unexpected approach]..."

## Curiosity Hooks
- "The secret behind every [successful outcome] is..."
- "There's a reason [common approach] stopped working..."
- "Nobody's talking about this [platform] feature..."
- "[Industry] insiders use this strategy. Now you can too."

## Social Proof Hooks
- "[Number]+ [audience] trust [brand/product] for [outcome]"
- "Rated [score] by [number] verified [users/customers]"
- "[Name] went from [before] to [after] in [time]"
- "The most [superlative] [product category] of [year]"

## Pattern Interrupt Hooks
- "STOP. This will save you [amount/time]."
- "Everything you know about [topic] is wrong."
- "I got [fired/rejected/banned] for sharing this."
- "[Unexpected opening] ...and that's when everything changed."
