# Standard Phrases for Performance Reports

> Consistent, professional language for communicating campaign performance to clients and stakeholders.

---

## Executive Summary Phrases

### Overall Performance — Positive
- "Campaign performance exceeded targets across all primary KPIs during [period]."
- "The account delivered strong results in [period], with [metric] outperforming the target by [X]%."
- "Overall, [period] was a high-performing period marked by improvements in [metric 1], [metric 2], and [metric 3]."
- "Results this [period] reflect the impact of [strategic change], with measurable gains in [key areas]."
- "Performance remained consistently above benchmark throughout [period]."

### Overall Performance — Mixed
- "Performance during [period] was mixed, with strong results in [area] offset by softness in [area]."
- "[Metric A] improved [X]%, while [Metric B] saw a [Y]% decline — both are addressed in the recommendations section."
- "The account showed positive momentum in [area], though [area] requires attention heading into [next period]."
- "While top-line [metric] remained healthy, efficiency metrics suggest opportunities for optimization."
- "Results met expectations in most areas; however, [specific area] underperformed relative to target."

### Overall Performance — Challenging
- "[Period] presented performance challenges driven primarily by [root cause]."
- "Results fell short of target in [area], which we attribute to [specific factors]."
- "The account experienced headwinds from [external factor / market condition / platform change], impacting [metrics]."
- "Performance declined in [area], prompting a strategic review outlined in the recommendations section."
- "While [period] was below expectations, the corrective actions detailed below are designed to restore performance."

## Metric Commentary Phrases

### Spend & Budget
- "Total spend for [period] was $[X], representing [Y]% of the allocated budget."
- "Budget utilization was [X]%, [within / above / below] the target range of [Y-Z]%."
- "Spend paced [X]% [ahead of / behind] plan, driven by [reason]."
- "Budget was reallocated from [underperforming area] to [higher-performing area] mid-period."
- "We recommend [increasing / maintaining / decreasing] budget to $[X] for [next period] based on current efficiency."

### Revenue & ROAS
- "The account generated $[X] in attributed revenue at a [Y]x ROAS."
- "Revenue [increased / decreased] [X]% period-over-period, driven by [factor]."
- "ROAS of [X] represents a [Y]% [improvement / decline] from [previous period]."
- "Blended ROAS across all channels stood at [X], with [Channel] leading at [Y]."
- "Revenue per conversion improved to $[X], up from $[Y] in the prior period."

### Cost Metrics (CPA, CPL, CPC)
- "Cost per acquisition came in at $[X], [above / below / in line with] the $[Y] target."
- "CPA [decreased / increased] [X]% compared to [previous period], reflecting [reason]."
- "Cost per lead averaged $[X] across all campaigns, with top performers at $[Y]."
- "CPC [rose / fell] to $[X], influenced by [competitive pressure / targeting changes / creative refresh]."
- "Efficiency improved with CPA declining from $[X] to $[Y] over the reporting period."

### Volume Metrics
- "The account generated [X] [conversions / leads / purchases] during [period]."
- "Lead volume [increased / decreased] [X]% period-over-period."
- "Total impressions reached [X], with [Y] clicks at a [Z]% CTR."
- "Conversion volume scaled [X]% while maintaining CPA within [Y]% of target."
- "Total reach expanded to [X] unique users, up [Y]% from [previous period]."

### Engagement Metrics
- "Click-through rate averaged [X]%, [above / below] the [Y]% platform benchmark."
- "Engagement rate on creative assets averaged [X]%, with top-performing creative at [Y]%."
- "Video completion rate was [X]%, indicating [strong / moderate / weak] audience retention."
- "Landing page conversion rate held steady at [X]%, consistent with prior periods."
- "Bounce rate on destination pages was [X]%, suggesting [alignment / misalignment] between ad messaging and page content."

## Trend & Change Commentary

### Positive Trends
- "This marks the [Xth] consecutive [period] of improvement in [metric]."
- "[Metric] has trended positively since [event/change], confirming the effectiveness of [strategy]."
- "The upward trajectory in [metric] is expected to continue as [reason]."
- "Month-over-month improvement of [X]% in [metric] demonstrates sustained momentum."
- "Performance stabilized at a higher level following the [optimization/change] implemented on [date]."

### Negative Trends
- "[Metric] has declined for [X] consecutive [periods], warranting investigation."
- "The downward trend in [metric] appears correlated with [specific factor]."
- "We are monitoring the [X]% decline in [metric] closely and have implemented [countermeasure]."
- "Performance softened in [area], consistent with [seasonal patterns / market conditions / platform changes]."
- "This decline is within expected range given [context]; however, if it persists beyond [timeframe], we recommend [action]."

### Stable / Flat Trends
- "[Metric] remained stable at [value], consistent with historical averages."
- "Performance held steady during [period], with no significant fluctuations."
- "While [metric] did not improve, maintaining current levels during [challenging condition] represents a positive outcome."
- "Flat performance in [metric] is expected given [context]; growth initiatives are planned for [next period]."

## Attribution & Methodology Notes

- "All figures are based on [platform-reported / blended / MTA / last-click] attribution unless noted otherwise."
- "Revenue figures reflect a [X]-day attribution window as configured in [Platform]."
- "Discrepancies between platform-reported and analytics-reported data are addressed in the appendix."
- "We apply a [X]-day click / [Y]-day view attribution model for consistency across platforms."
- "Note: [Platform] attribution may overstate results by approximately [X]% based on our internal validation."

---

## Forward-Looking Statements

- "Based on current trajectory, we project [metric] of [value] for [next period]."
- "The optimizations implemented this period are expected to yield measurable results within [timeframe]."
- "We anticipate [seasonal / competitive / market] pressure on [metric] in [upcoming period]."
- "The planned [test / launch / expansion] in [next period] is designed to address the [area] identified above."
- "Our focus for [next period] will be [priority 1], [priority 2], and [priority 3]."

---

## Closing Report Phrases

- "Full data tables and supporting analysis are available in the attached appendix."
- "We welcome any questions on this report and are available to discuss at your convenience."
- "The next report will be delivered on [date], covering [period]."
- "Recommendations for [next period] are detailed in the following section."
- "Please review and share any feedback ahead of our [next meeting/call] on [date]."

---

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Be precise** — Always include specific numbers, dates, and percentages
2. **Lead with the headline** — Start every section with the most important finding
3. **Contextualize every metric** — A number without context is meaningless; always compare to target, benchmark, or prior period
4. **Separate observation from interpretation** — State what happened, then explain why
5. **Maintain consistent terminology** — Use the same metric names throughout the report
6. **Avoid jargon overload** — Match language complexity to the audience; executives need summaries, practitioners need details
