# Retargeting Copy Phrases

## Purpose
Copy phrases specifically for retargeting audiences at different intent levels.

## Cart Abandonment (1-3 days)
- "Looks like you left something behind..."
- "Your cart is waiting — and so is free shipping"
- "Still thinking about it? Here's why [number] others said yes"
- "Complete your order before your [product] sells out"
- "We saved your cart. Ready to finish?"

## Site Visitors (3-7 days)
- "Hey, you checked us out. Here's what you might have missed."
- "Still looking for [solution]? Here's why we're different."
- "You visited. You browsed. Now here's the proof it works."
- "Remember us? Here's what's new since your last visit."

## Engaged but Not Converted (7-14 days)
- "Still on the fence? Here's what [Name] decided — and the result."
- "We know you're researching. Let us make the decision easy."
- "The most common question we get? [FAQ answer]"
- "Here's the one thing that convinces most [audience]:"

## Lead Nurture (14-30 days)
- "Enjoyed our [lead magnet]? Here's the logical next step."
- "You took the first step. Ready for [next step]?"
- "Since downloading [resource], have you tried [action]?"

## Win-Back (30+ days)
- "It's been a while! Here's what's changed since you last visited."
- "We've improved [specific thing] based on feedback like yours."
- "We miss you. Come back for [special offer]."
- "A lot has changed. Give us another look."

## Customer Reactivation
- "It's been [X days] — time for a reorder?"
- "Your favorite [product] is back in stock!"
- "New arrivals you'll love based on your last purchase"
- "Exclusive for returning customers: [offer]"

## Usage Notes
- Match urgency level to recency (hotter = more urgent)
- Rotate copy every 2 weeks to prevent fatigue
- Pair with dynamic product ads for ecommerce
