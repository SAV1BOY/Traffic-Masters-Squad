# Compliance-Safe Claims

## Purpose
Pre-approved claim phrasings that are policy-safe across major platforms.

## Health & Wellness
- "Supports overall wellness" (not "cures" or "treats")
- "May help with [benefit]" (not "will fix")
- "Designed to support [goal]" (not "guaranteed to")
- "Many users report [benefit]" (not "you will experience")
- "Formulated with [ingredient] known for [property]"

## Financial & Income
- "Our clients have achieved [result]" (not "you will make")
- "Results depend on individual effort and circumstances"
- "Build skills to grow your income potential"
- "Framework designed to support business growth"
- "Past performance does not guarantee future results"

## Results & Outcomes
- "Individual results may vary"
- "Based on [specific study/sample]"
- "Typical results range from [X] to [Y]"
- "When implemented as directed, users have seen..."
- "Results shown are from real users; your experience may differ"

## Comparison Claims
- "An alternative to [category]" (not "better than [specific brand]")
- "Designed to address limitations of traditional [approach]"
- "A different approach to [problem]"

## Disclaimers to Include
- Earnings: "Results not typical. Individual effort and circumstances vary."
- Health: "Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."
- Before/After: "Individual results may vary. Images show actual users."
- Testimonials: "Testimonial reflects personal experience of the individual."

## Platform-Specific Notes
- **Meta:** Cannot assert or imply personal attributes
- **Google:** Requires substantiation for comparative claims
- **TikTok:** Strict on body image and unrealistic expectations
- **All:** Avoid absolute claims, guarantees, and superlatives without proof
