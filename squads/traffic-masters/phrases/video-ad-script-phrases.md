# Video Ad Script Phrases

## Purpose
Ready-to-use phrases for video ad scripts organized by section.

## Opening Lines (0-3 seconds)
- "Stop scrolling — this will save you [amount/time]"
- "I have to tell you something nobody else will"
- "Watch this before you [common mistake]"
- "In the next [X] seconds, I'll show you [outcome]"
- "This is the [thing] that changed everything"

## Problem Setup
- "If you're like most [audience], you're probably dealing with..."
- "Here's the thing about [problem] — it only gets worse"
- "I used to [relatable struggle] every single [day/week]"
- "The worst part about [problem]? [Specific consequence]"

## Solution Introduction
- "That's exactly why we created [product/service]"
- "After [time/effort], we finally found the answer"
- "Here's what actually works..."
- "The solution is simpler than you think"
- "Meet [product] — the [category] designed for [specific need]"

## Proof Section
- "Don't just take my word for it..."
- "Here are the numbers: [specific metrics]"
- "[Name] was in the same boat. Now [result]."
- "We've helped [number] [people] achieve [outcome]"

## CTA Section
- "Click the link below to [specific action]"
- "Tap [CTA button] to get started — it takes [X] minutes"
- "Visit [URL] for your free [offer]"
- "Comment [word] and I'll send you the link"
- "The link is in my bio — grab it before [deadline]"

## Transition Phrases
- "But here's where it gets interesting..."
- "Now, you might be thinking [objection]..."
- "Let me show you exactly how this works..."
- "Here's the part most people miss..."
