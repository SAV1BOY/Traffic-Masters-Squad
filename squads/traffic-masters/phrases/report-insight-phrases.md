# Report Insight Phrases

## Purpose
Phrases for communicating performance insights in reports and dashboards.

## Positive Insights
- "Performance exceeded target by [%], driven by [cause]"
- "[Metric] improved [%] WoW, the strongest gain in [timeframe]"
- "New [creative/audience/strategy] outperformed control by [%]"
- "Efficiency gains in [area] offset [challenge]"
- "We're on track to exceed [period] targets by [%]"

## Negative Insights
- "[Metric] declined [%] due to [specific cause]"
- "Performance fell below guardrail; corrective action taken"
- "[Area] underperformed expectations — investigating [hypothesis]"
- "External factors ([factor]) impacted [metric] by [amount]"

## Neutral/Contextual Insights
- "[Metric] remained stable despite [market condition]"
- "Performance was within expected range for [seasonal period]"
- "Insufficient data to draw conclusions; continuing test"
- "Results are directionally positive but not yet significant"

## Causal Analysis
- "The primary driver was [specific change/event]"
- "This is attributed to [cause] based on [evidence]"
- "Correlation observed between [A] and [B]; testing causation"
- "Three factors contributed: [1], [2], and [3]"

## Recommendation Phrases
- "Based on this data, we recommend [action]"
- "Next steps: [1], [2], [3]"
- "We're implementing [change] to address [issue]"
- "Opportunity identified: [description], estimated impact [%]"
- "No action required — monitoring continues"

## Transition Phrases
- "Looking ahead to next [period]..."
- "Building on this momentum..."
- "To address this trend..."
- "In response to these findings..."
