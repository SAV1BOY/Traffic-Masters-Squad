## Usage Guidelines

1. **Lead with the transformation** — Focus on the end state, not the process
2. **Be vivid and specific** — "Watch your CPA drop 40%" outperforms "Save money on ads"
3. **Use second person** — "You" statements create personal connection
4. **Pair with proof** — Desire phrases gain power when followed by evidence
5. **Match aspiration to audience** — A CMO aspires differently than a solo founder; adjust accordingly
6. **Balance aspiration with believability** — Overpromising creates skepticism; ground desire in reality
