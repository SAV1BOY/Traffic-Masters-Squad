# Proof Phrases

## Purpose
Ready-to-use social proof and credibility phrases for ad copy.

## Number-Based Proof
- "Trusted by [number]+ [audience] worldwide"
- "[Number] customers served since [year]"
- "[Number] five-star reviews and counting"
- "[Percentage]% of users report [positive outcome]"
- "Over [number] [results] delivered to date"

## Result-Based Proof
- "Average customer sees [result] within [timeframe]"
- "Our clients average [metric] of [impressive number]"
- "[Name] achieved [specific result] in just [timeframe]"
- "From [before state] to [after state] — documented results"

## Authority-Based Proof
- "As featured in [publication/media]"
- "Recommended by [expert/organization]"
- "[Certification/award] recognized"
- "Used by teams at [recognizable company names]"
- "Developed by [credentials]"

## Testimonial Phrases
- "'[Quote]' — [Name], [Title/Company]"
- "Don't take our word for it. Here's what [Name] says:"
- "[Name] was skeptical. Now they [positive outcome]."
- "Hear from real [customers/users/clients]:"

## Comparison Proof
- "[X]x [faster/better/cheaper] than [alternative]"
- "Voted #1 in [category] by [source]"
- "Outperforms [competitor/old way] by [metric]"

## Risk Reversal Proof
- "[Number]-day money-back guarantee"
- "Try free for [timeframe] — cancel anytime"
- "If you don't see [result], we'll [guarantee action]"
- "Zero risk. Full refund if not satisfied."

## Usage Guidelines
- Always use real, verifiable numbers
- Include names/photos when possible (with permission)
- Match proof type to audience stage (data for B2B, stories for B2C)
- Comply with platform policies on claims and testimonials
