# Words to Avoid in Ads

## Purpose
Words and phrases that hurt performance or trigger policy violations.

## Policy-Triggering Words

| Word/Phrase | Risk | Alternative |
|-------------|------|-------------|
| "Cure" / "Treat" | Health policy violation | "Support" / "Help with" |
| "Guaranteed income" | Financial claims policy | "Potential to earn" |
| "You are [attribute]" | Personal attributes policy (Meta) | "People who [behavior]" |
| "Best" / "#1" | Unsubstantiated superlative | "Top-rated" / "Leading" |
| "Miracle" | Misleading claims | "Effective" / "Powerful" |
| "Risk-free" | Misleading guarantee | "Try with confidence" |

## Performance-Killing Words

| Word/Phrase | Why It Hurts | Alternative |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| "Buy" (cold traffic) | Too direct for unaware audiences | "Discover" / "Explore" |
| "Cheap" | Implies low quality | "Affordable" / "Great value" |
| "Submit" (CTAs) | Creates friction | "Get" / "Send me" |
| "Honestly" / "Trust me" | Signals untrustworthiness | Let proof speak |
| "Very" / "Really" | Weakens the statement | Use stronger adjectives |
| "Basically" | Undermines authority | Be direct |

## Overused Phrases to Freshen
- "Game-changer" — replace with specific impact
- "Synergy" — replace with specific benefit
- "Cutting-edge" — replace with specific feature
- "World-class" — replace with specific credential
- "Revolutionary" — replace with specific improvement

## Platform-Specific Words to Avoid
- **Meta:** Personal attribute assertions, "you" + negative state
- **Google:** Unverified superlatives, click-bait phrasing
- **TikTok:** Body-shaming language, unrealistic expectations
- **LinkedIn:** Overly casual slang, hard-sell language

## General Rule
If a word makes a claim you can't prove, find a provable alternative.
