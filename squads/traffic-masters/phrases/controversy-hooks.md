# Controversy & Contrarian Hook Formulas

> Contrarian hooks challenge widely held beliefs to provoke curiosity and engagement. Use responsibly — the goal is to educate, not just to shock.

---

## "Myth-Busting" Hooks

Challenge common industry myths with evidence-backed alternatives.

- "Myth: You need a huge budget to run profitable ads. Reality: Strategy beats budget every time."
- "Everyone says to start with lookalike audiences. Here's why that's often the worst place to start."
- "The 'best practice' of testing one variable at a time is slowing you down. Here's a better approach."
- "You've been told ROAS is the metric that matters. It's not — and here's the metric you should watch instead."
- "A/B testing is overrated. Structured creative testing is what actually moves the needle."
- "The funnel is dead. Here's the model that replaced it for top-performing brands."
- "No, you don't need a complicated attribution model. You need a clear one."
- "The idea that 'creative is king' has done more damage than any algorithm update."
- "Broad targeting doesn't mean lazy targeting. It means trusting the algorithm — strategically."
- "The '3-second rule' for video ads is wrong. Here's what the data actually shows."

---

## "Unpopular Opinion" Hooks

State a position that most people disagree with, then build the case.

- "Unpopular opinion: Most ad agencies are optimizing for their own convenience, not your performance."
- "Unpopular opinion: You should pause your best-performing campaign right now."
- "Unpopular opinion: Brand campaigns are more profitable than performance campaigns — if you measure correctly."
- "Unpopular opinion: Your creative team should report to your media buyer, not the other way around."
- "Unpopular opinion: Facebook Ads are getting easier, not harder. Most people just haven't adapted."
- "Unpopular opinion: The best ad strategy involves turning off most of your ads."
- "Unpopular opinion: Manual bidding still beats automated bidding in specific, predictable scenarios."
- "Unpopular opinion: Your landing page matters more than every ad setting combined."
- "Unpopular opinion: You should spend 80% of your testing budget on copy, not visuals."
- "Unpopular opinion: Most marketers optimize too frequently and it's hurting their results."

---

## "What They Won't Tell You" Hooks

Position the insight as suppressed or overlooked knowledge.

- "What ad platforms don't want you to know about automated bidding."
- "Your ad rep will never tell you this about campaign structure."
- "The agencies charging $10K/month don't want you to know how simple this actually is."
- "Here's what nobody mentions about 'scaling' campaigns."
- "The truth about those ROAS screenshots everyone posts on social media."
- "What the algorithm actually rewards — and it's not what you've been told."
- "The real reason your costs went up after the last platform update."
- "Why your ad platform recommends settings that benefit them, not you."
- "The metric the platform hides that tells you more than any other."
- "What happens behind the scenes when you click 'Advantage+ Shopping Campaign.'"

---

## "Stop Doing This" Hooks

Command-style hooks that challenge current behavior.

- "Stop obsessing over CPC. It's the most misleading metric in your dashboard."
- "Stop duplicating winning ad sets. It's destroying your account structure."
- "Stop changing your bids every day. You're resetting the learning phase."
- "Stop blaming the algorithm. The problem is almost always your creative."
- "Stop creating new campaigns for every test. Your account doesn't need 47 campaigns."
- "Stop running traffic to your homepage. Send them where they can convert."
- "Stop adding audiences to fix performance. Try removing them instead."
- "Stop using interest-based targeting as your primary strategy. There's a better way."
- "Stop looking at daily ROAS. The data you need lives in longer windows."
- "Stop copying your competitor's ads. What works for them won't work for you."

---

## "The Real Reason" Hooks

Offer a deeper, often uncomfortable explanation for common problems.

- "The real reason your ads aren't working isn't your targeting — it's your offer."
- "The real reason CPMs are rising has nothing to do with competition."
- "The real reason agencies use complicated reporting: it's harder for you to question."
- "The real reason your scaling attempts fail: you're scaling spend, not strategy."
- "The real reason your retargeting campaigns underperform: your awareness campaigns are weak."
- "The real reason your 'winning' campaign eventually stops working — and it's not ad fatigue."
- "The real reason your creative tests are inconclusive: your testing framework is flawed."
- "The real reason you can't attribute revenue to ads: your tracking setup, not the platform."
- "The real reason your conversion rate dropped: your traffic quality changed, not your page."
- "The real reason most brands can't scale past $[X]/month: they optimize too narrowly."

---

## "Hot Take" Hooks

Short, provocative statements that demand engagement.

- "90% of what passes as 'strategy' in paid media is just organized guessing."
- "Your ad account doesn't need more optimization. It needs fewer campaigns."
- "The best ad you'll ever run will be ugly."
- "Attribution is a story we tell ourselves to feel in control."
- "If your ads need more than 5 seconds of explanation, they're too complicated."
- "Most ad budgets are set by gut feeling disguised as 'experience.'"
- "The brands spending the most on ads aren't the brands winning with ads."
- "Your creative brief is more important than your media plan."
- "Reporting isn't proof of work. Results are proof of work."
- "If AI could replace your media buying approach, your approach isn't good enough."

---

## "Everything You Know Is Wrong" Hooks

Maximum disruption — use sparingly and only when you have strong evidence.

- "Everything you've been taught about ad frequency is wrong. Here's the research that proves it."
- "The campaign structure your agency uses is outdated by at least 2 years."
- "That 'complete guide to Facebook Ads' you read? Half of it no longer applies."
- "The targeting framework that worked in [year] is actively harming your results today."
- "Your competitor's strategy isn't better — it's just different. And different isn't always better."
- "The industry's obsession with new customer acquisition is leaving the most profitable strategy on the table."
- "Most of what gets shared as 'data-driven insights' is cherry-picked survivorship bias."
- "The playbook everyone follows was written for a version of the platform that no longer exists."

## Ethical Boundaries

### Acceptable Controversy
- Challenging ineffective best practices with evidence
- Questioning platform recommendations that serve the platform more than the advertiser
- Presenting a contrarian approach backed by data or experience
- Exposing common misconceptions that lead to wasted spend
- Advocating for approaches that the audience hasn't considered

### Unacceptable Controversy
- Attacking specific individuals, competitors, or companies by name for engagement
- Making claims you can't substantiate
- Manufacturing outrage with no educational payoff
- Disparaging entire professions or groups
- Being provocative solely for attention without delivering genuine insight
- Spreading misinformation even if it's engaging

---

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Always deliver the substance** — A contrarian hook without a solid argument behind it is clickbait
2. **Back it up** — Every controversial claim needs evidence, experience, or data to support it
3. **Be specific about what you oppose** — Vague contrarianism is unconvincing; target specific practices
4. **Offer the alternative** — Don't just tear down; build up the better approach
5. **Know your audience** — Beginners need gentler contrarianism; experienced practitioners appreciate bolder takes
6. **Use sparingly** — If every piece of content is controversial, none of it is; save this angle for when it matters
7. **Accept disagreement gracefully** — Contrarian content invites debate; be prepared to engage respectfully
