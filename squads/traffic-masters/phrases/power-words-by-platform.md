# Power Words by Platform

## Purpose
Platform-specific power words that resonate with each audience.

## Meta (Facebook / Instagram)
- **Top performers:** Free, New, You, Discover, Limited, Exclusive
- **Engagement drivers:** Comment, Share, Tag, DM, Link in bio
- **Emotional:** Love, Amazing, Obsessed, Life-changing, Finally
- **Avoid:** Buy (cold), Click here (policy), Spam triggers

## Google Search
- **CTR boosters:** Free, Official, Best, Near me, [Year], How to
- **Trust signals:** Certified, Verified, Guaranteed, Trusted, Secure
- **Action:** Get, Compare, Find, Calculate, Start, Try
- **Avoid:** Superlatives without proof, excessive caps

## YouTube
- **Hook words:** Secret, Never, Mistake, Truth, Shocking, Finally
- **Retention words:** Wait, But, Here's the thing, The real reason
- **CTA words:** Click, Subscribe, Link below, Comment
- **Avoid:** Clickbait that doesn't deliver, misleading thumbnails

## TikTok
- **Native phrases:** POV, Storytime, GRWM, Wait for it, Part [X]
- **Engagement:** Stitch this, Duet, Comment for, Share with someone
- **Trending:** Whatever's current (check Creative Center weekly)
- **Avoid:** Corporate language, overly polished phrasing

## LinkedIn
- **Authority:** Insights, Strategy, Framework, Research, Data shows
- **Professional:** Opportunity, Growth, Leadership, Innovation
- **Engagement:** Agree?, Thoughts?, What would you add?
- **Avoid:** Slang, hard-sell language, excessive emojis

## Cross-Platform Universal Power Words
- **Always strong:** Free, New, You, How, Why, Secret, Proven
- **Always test:** Numbers, questions, negative framing
- **Always localize:** Adapt to platform culture and norms
