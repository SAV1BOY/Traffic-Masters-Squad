# Application Pages — Swipe File

## Purpose
High-converting application/qualification pages for high-ticket offers.

## Page Structure

### Section 1: Positioning
- Headline: "Apply to [Program Name]"
- Sub-headline: Exclusivity framing — not for everyone
- Brief description of transformation/outcome

### Section 2: Qualification
- "This is for you if..." (3-5 qualifying criteria)
- "This is NOT for you if..." (2-3 disqualifying criteria)
- Builds perceived value through selectivity

### Section 3: Social Proof
- Client results (specific numbers)
- Video testimonials from successful applicants
- Logo bar or client photos

### Section 4: Application Form
- Multi-step form (reduces friction)
- Qualifying questions (revenue, timeline, commitment)
- Contact info collected last (after investment in form)

### Section 5: Next Steps
- "What happens after you apply" — sets expectations
- Timeline for review and response

## Swipe Examples

| # | Niche | Offer Price | Conv Rate | Key Element |
|---|-------|------------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | Coaching | $10K | 15% | Multi-step form |
| 2 | Agency | $25K | 8% | Case study video |
| 3 | Mastermind | $50K | 5% | Exclusivity framing |

## Key Principles
1. Application = qualification — higher close rates on calls
2. Multi-step forms convert 20-30% better than single page
3. Disqualification language increases perceived value
4. Expected close rate: 20-40% of qualified applicants
