# Advertorial Pages — Swipe File

## Purpose
High-converting advertorial/pre-sell pages that warm traffic before the offer.

## Page Structure

### Editorial-Style Layout
- Looks like a blog post or news article, not a sales page
- Byline with author name and date
- Engaging headline (curiosity or news angle)

### Content Flow
1. **Hook:** Story or news angle that draws reader in
2. **Problem:** Establish the pain or challenge
3. **Discovery:** Introduce the solution naturally within the story
4. **Evidence:** Data, studies, testimonials woven into narrative
5. **Transition:** Natural bridge to product/offer
6. **CTA:** Soft CTA within content + stronger CTA at end

## Key Principles
1. Must provide genuine value — not just a disguised sales page
2. Compliance: clearly label as sponsored/advertorial where required
3. Warm traffic converts 2-5x better than cold direct-to-offer
4. Average time on page should be 2-4 minutes (indicates reading)
5. In-content CTAs outperform bottom-only CTAs

## Compliance Notes
- FTC guidelines require clear disclosure
- Platform policies on advertorial landing pages vary
- Include "Advertisement" or "Sponsored" label
