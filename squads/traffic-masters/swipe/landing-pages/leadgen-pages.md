# Lead Gen Landing Pages — Swipe File

## Purpose
High-converting lead generation landing page examples and patterns.

## Page Structure Template

### Above the Fold
- Headline matching ad promise
- Sub-headline with specific benefit
- Lead form or CTA button
- Trust indicators (logos, rating, secure badge)

### Body
- 3-5 bullet points of what they get
- Social proof section (testimonials, numbers)
- What's inside preview (mockup, sample content)
- FAQ addressing top 3 objections

### Footer
- Final CTA repeat
- Privacy statement
- Contact info

## Swipe Examples

| # | Industry | Headline Formula | Conv Rate | Key Element |
|---|----------|-----------------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | SaaS | "Get [Result] Without [Pain]" | 32% | 3-field form |
| 2 | Coaching | "Free [Resource]: [Specific Outcome]" | 28% | Video + form |
| 3 | Finance | "[Number] [Professionals] Trust [Product]" | 22% | Logo bar |

## Optimization Patterns
1. Single CTA focus — one action per page
2. Form fields: fewer = higher conversion (sweet spot: 3-5)
3. Mobile-first design (70%+ traffic is mobile)
4. Page load under 3 seconds or lose 40% of visitors
5. Message match between ad and headline is non-negotiable

## Testing Priority
1. Headline (highest impact)
2. CTA button text and color
3. Form length
4. Social proof placement
5. Image/video choice
