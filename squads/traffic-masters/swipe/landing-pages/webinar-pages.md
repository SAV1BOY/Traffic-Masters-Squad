# Webinar Registration Pages — Swipe File

## Purpose
High-converting webinar registration page examples and patterns.

## Page Structure

### Essential Elements
- **Headline:** "Free Training: How to [Outcome] in [Timeframe]"
- **Date/Time:** With timezone and "can't make it? register for replay"
- **Speaker Photo + Credentials:** Builds authority
- **3-5 Bullet Points:** What they'll learn (benefit-driven)
- **Registration Form:** Name + email minimum
- **Urgency Element:** "Limited seats" or countdown timer

### Optional Boosters
- Short video teaser (60-90s)
- Testimonials from past attendees
- "Who this is for" qualifier section
- Bonus for live attendance

## Optimization Patterns
1. Registration pages should convert 30-50% of visitors
2. Countdown timers increase urgency by 15-20%
3. "Register for replay" option captures 10-15% more leads
4. Speaker photo with credentials builds necessary trust
5. Mobile-optimized form is critical (60%+ mobile traffic)
