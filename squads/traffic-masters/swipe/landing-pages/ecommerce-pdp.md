# Ecommerce Product Detail Pages — Swipe File

## Purpose
High-converting PDP (Product Detail Page) examples for ecommerce.

## PDP Structure

### Above the Fold
- Product images (5-8, including lifestyle and detail shots)
- Product title with key attributes
- Price (with anchor if on sale)
- Star rating + review count
- Add to Cart button (prominent, contrasting color)
- Trust badges (shipping, returns, secure checkout)

### Below the Fold
- Product description (benefits > features)
- Size/variant selector
- Social proof section (reviews, UGC photos)
- FAQ / common questions
- Cross-sell / "Frequently bought together"

## Swipe Examples

| # | Category | Conv Rate | Key Element |
|---|----------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | Beauty | 4.2% | Before/after gallery |
| 2 | Supplements | 3.8% | Ingredient breakdown + studies |
| 3 | Fashion | 3.1% | Size guide + fit photos |
| 4 | Electronics | 2.9% | Comparison chart vs competitors |

## Optimization Patterns
1. Product images are the #1 conversion driver
2. Reviews with photos increase trust and conversion 15-25%
3. Urgency elements: low stock, recent purchases, shipping deadline
4. Sticky Add to Cart on mobile increases conversion 10-15%
5. Trust badges near checkout button reduce abandonment
