# Pain Angles — Swipe File

## Purpose
Ad angles that lead with the audience's pain points and frustrations.

## Pain Angle Framework
1. Identify the specific pain (not generic)
2. Agitate with consequences of inaction
3. Bridge to solution as relief

## Angle Templates

### Financial Pain
- "Tired of wasting $X/month on [thing that doesn't work]?"
- "Every day you wait costs you $X in lost [revenue/time/customers]"

### Time Pain
- "Still spending X hours/week on [manual task]?"
- "What would you do with X extra hours every week?"

### Status Pain
- "Your competitors are already doing X. Are you?"
- "While you're stuck on [old way], leaders are [new way]"

### Emotional Pain
- "The frustration of [specific scenario] ends today"
- "If [painful situation] keeps you up at night..."

### Opportunity Cost Pain
- "Every month without [solution] = $X left on the table"
- "You're one [action] away from [desired outcome]"

## Swipe Examples

| # | Angle | Platform | Industry | Result |
|---|-------|----------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Financial pain — wasted ad spend | Meta | Agency | 2.1% CTR |
| 2 | Time pain — manual reporting | LinkedIn | SaaS | $45 CPL |
| 3 | Status pain — competitor fear | Google | B2B | 3.8% CVR |

## When to Use Pain Angles
- Cold audiences who don't know the solution exists
- Problem-aware stage of awareness
- High-pain industries (health, finance, business)
