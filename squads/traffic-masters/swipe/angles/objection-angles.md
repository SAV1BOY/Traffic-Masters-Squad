# Objection Angles — Swipe File

## Purpose
Ad angles that preemptively address and overcome common buyer objections.

## Common Objections & Counter-Angles

### "It's too expensive"
- "Costs less than your daily coffee — saves you $X/month"
- "The real cost is NOT having [solution]"
- ROI framing: "For every $1 you invest, get $X back"

### "I don't have time"
- "Set up in 5 minutes. Results by tomorrow."
- "Takes less time than [relatable activity]"
- "We do it for you" angle

### "It won't work for me"
- "Works for [specific persona] even if [common excuse]"
- Case study of someone just like them
- "Guaranteed or your money back"

### "I need to think about it"
- Urgency: "Only X spots left" / "Price increases [date]"
- Risk reversal: "Try free for 30 days"
- Social proof: "10,000 people didn't wait — here's what happened"

### "I've tried something like this before"
- "This isn't like [old solution]. Here's what's different."
- Mechanism angle: explain WHY this works when others don't
- "We built this because [old solutions] failed us too"

## Swipe Examples

| # | Objection | Counter-Angle | Platform | Result |
|---|-----------|--------------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Too expensive | ROI calc | Meta | -22% CPA |
| 2 | Won't work for me | Case study | YouTube | +35% VTR |
| 3 | Tried before | Mechanism | Google | +18% CVR |

## When to Use
- Mid-funnel and retargeting audiences
- High-consideration purchases
- After initial interest but before conversion
