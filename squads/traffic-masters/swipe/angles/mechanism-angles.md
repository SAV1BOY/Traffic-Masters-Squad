# Mechanism Angles — Swipe File

## Purpose
Ad angles that explain the unique mechanism — WHY the solution works differently.

## Mechanism Framework
1. Name the mechanism (proprietary term if possible)
2. Explain how it works (simply)
3. Show why alternatives lack this mechanism
4. Prove the mechanism delivers results

## Angle Templates

### Named Mechanism
- "Our [Proprietary Name] technology does what [old way] can't"
- "The [Name] Method: why 93% of users see results in week 1"

### Process Mechanism
- "3-step system that [outcome] without [objection]"
- "Here's the exact process we use to [result]"

### Scientific Mechanism
- "Backed by [research/study] from [institution]"
- "The science behind why [product] works when others don't"

### Contrarian Mechanism
- "Everyone thinks [common belief]. The truth? [mechanism]"
- "The real reason your [area] isn't working has nothing to do with [assumed cause]"

### Ingredient/Component Mechanism
- "Contains [specific ingredient] — the key to [benefit]"
- "Built with [technology] that [specific function]"

## Swipe Examples

| # | Mechanism Type | Example | Platform | Result |
|---|---------------|---------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Named | "The Cascade Method" | YouTube | 52% VTR |

## When to Use
- Solution-aware audiences comparing options
- Saturated markets where differentiation is key
- Products with genuine technical advantages
