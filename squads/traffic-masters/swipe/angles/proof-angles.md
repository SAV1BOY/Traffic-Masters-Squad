# Proof Angles — Swipe File

## Purpose
Ad angles that lead with evidence, results, and social proof to build credibility.

## Proof Types

### 1. Data Proof
- "247% increase in conversions in 30 days"
- Specific numbers are more believable than round numbers
- Before/after metrics with timeframe

### 2. Social Proof
- "Join 50,000+ marketers who use [product]"
- Logo bars of recognizable clients
- User count, review count, rating

### 3. Testimonial Proof
- Named person + photo + specific result
- Video testimonials outperform text by 2-3x
- Include role/company for B2B credibility

### 4. Authority Proof
- "As featured in [Forbes/TechCrunch/etc.]"
- Certifications, awards, partnerships
- Expert endorsements

### 5. Demonstration Proof
- Show the product working in real-time
- Screen recordings, live demos, unboxings
- "Watch me do X in Y minutes"

### 6. Case Study Proof
- Mini case study within the ad
- Problem > Solution > Result arc
- Named client with permission

## Swipe Examples

| # | Proof Type | Example | Platform | Result |
|---|-----------|---------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Data | "312% ROAS in 60 days" | Meta | 2.4% CTR |
| 2 | Testimonial | Video review | TikTok | -30% CPA |
| 3 | Demo | Live screen recording | YouTube | +45% VTR |

## When to Use
- All funnel stages (adjust proof type to stage)
- High-skepticism niches
- Premium/high-ticket offers
