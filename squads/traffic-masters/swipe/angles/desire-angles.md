# Desire Angles — Swipe File

## Purpose
Ad angles that lead with aspirational outcomes and desired future states.

## Desire Angle Framework
1. Paint the desired outcome vividly
2. Make it feel attainable
3. Show the path (product/service as vehicle)

## Angle Templates

### Lifestyle Desire
- "Imagine waking up to [desired scenario] every morning"
- "This is what your [life/business] looks like after [solution]"

### Achievement Desire
- "Join 5,000+ [role] who've already [achievement]"
- "From [current state] to [dream state] in [timeframe]"

### Freedom Desire
- "Fire your [thing they hate] and replace it with [solution]"
- "What if you never had to [painful task] again?"

### Status Desire
- "Become the go-to [role] in your industry"
- "The tool that [aspirational peers] use to [outcome]"

### Security Desire
- "Never worry about [fear] again"
- "Predictable [revenue/results/growth] month after month"

## Swipe Examples

| # | Angle | Platform | Industry | Result |
|---|-------|----------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Lifestyle — freedom | Meta | Coaching | 1.8x ROAS |
| 2 | Achievement — peers | LinkedIn | SaaS | $38 CPL |
| 3 | Security — predictable | Google | Finance | 4.2% CVR |

## When to Use Desire Angles
- Solution-aware audiences
- Aspirational product categories
- Warm/retargeting audiences who need motivation
