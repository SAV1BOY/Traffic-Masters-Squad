# Harmon Brothers — Iconic Campaigns Swipe

## Agency Overview
- **Agency:** Harmon Brothers
- **Specialty:** Long-form direct response video ads
- **Notable Clients:** Squatty Potty, Purple, Chatbooks, FiberFix, Lume
- **Combined Results:** $1B+ in attributable revenue

## Signature Formula
1. **Pattern Interrupt Hook** (0-5s): Unexpected visual or statement
2. **Problem Establishment** (5-30s): Relatable pain point with humor
3. **Product Introduction** (30-60s): Solution reveal with demonstration
4. **Mechanism Explanation** (60-120s): Why it works (often with props)
5. **Social Proof** (120-150s): Testimonials or results
6. **CTA + Offer** (final 30s): Clear action with incentive

## Key Campaigns Analyzed

### Squatty Potty (Unicorn)
- 170M+ views, $33M in sales in first year
- Humor + demonstration + science = viral DR

### Chatbooks
- Mom humor, highly relatable, story-driven
- Proved DTC subscriptions work with video ads

### Lume Deodorant
- Founder-led, science-backed, taboo topic
- Brave creative direction in sensitive category

## Takeaways for Media Buyers
1. Production investment in one hero ad can outperform hundreds of cheap ads
2. Humor must serve the message, not distract from it
3. Demonstration is the most persuasive element
4. Long-form works when entertainment value justifies length
5. Every Harmon Brothers ad follows the same core structure

## Swipe Elements to Reuse
- The 6-part formula above
- Prop-based demonstrations
- Humor + education hybrid
- Character-driven narratives
