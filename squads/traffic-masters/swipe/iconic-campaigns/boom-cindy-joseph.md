# BOOM! by Cindy Joseph — Iconic Campaign Swipe

## Campaign Overview
- **Brand:** BOOM! by Cindy Joseph (cosmetics for older women)
- **Founder:** Ezra Firestone
- **Years:** 2014-present
- **Result:** Built to $30M+/year primarily through Facebook ads

## What Made It Work

### Audience & Positioning
- Underserved market: women 50+ who feel ignored by beauty industry
- "Pro-age" positioning against anti-aging messaging
- Cindy Joseph as authentic face of the brand

### Ad Strategy
- Content-first approach: blog posts, videos, education
- Advertorial funnel: ad > blog/article > product page
- Heavy use of UGC and customer stories
- Retargeting sequences with testimonial content

### Creative Approach
- Real women (not models) in creative
- Before/after with authentic lighting
- Educational content about skincare for mature skin
- Ezra's transparency about ad strategies built industry authority

## Key Takeaways for Media Buyers
1. Underserved audiences have lower CPMs and higher engagement
2. Content-first funnels build trust before selling
3. Authentic representation outperforms aspirational for some markets
4. Advertorial pre-sell pages warm traffic effectively
5. Consistent brand voice across all touchpoints compounds results

## Swipe Elements to Reuse
- Content > advertorial > product funnel
- Underserved audience targeting
- Authentic representation in creative
- Customer story-driven ads
