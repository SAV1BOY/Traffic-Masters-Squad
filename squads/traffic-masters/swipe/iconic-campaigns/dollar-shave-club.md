# Dollar Shave Club — Iconic Campaign Swipe

## Campaign Overview
- **Brand:** Dollar Shave Club
- **Year:** 2012
- **Format:** YouTube video ad (1:33)
- **Result:** 12,000 orders in 48 hours, acquired by Unilever for $1B

## What Made It Work

### Hook (First 5s)
- "Hi, I'm Mike, founder of DollarShaveClub.com"
- "Our blades are f***ing great" — pattern interrupt through boldness

### Creative Approach
- Low-budget feel = authenticity
- Humor throughout maintains engagement
- Founder as spokesman = trust + personality
- Walking tour format keeps visual interest

### Messaging Strategy
- Direct attack on overpriced competitors (Gillette)
- Simple value prop: great blades, $1/month, delivered
- Handles objections through humor ("Are they any good?")

### Funnel
- Video ad > landing page > subscription signup
- Viral organic sharing amplified paid reach

## Key Takeaways for Media Buyers
1. Personality-driven creative can outperform polished production
2. Humor + clear value prop = shareability
3. Contrarian positioning against category leader works
4. Sometimes one great ad can build an entire brand
5. Low production cost does not mean low performance

## Swipe Elements to Reuse
- Founder-as-spokesperson format
- Walking tour / continuous shot
- Bold opening statement
- Humor to handle objections
