# Dr. Squatch — Iconic Campaign Swipe

## Campaign Overview
- **Brand:** Dr. Squatch (natural men's soap)
- **Year:** 2018-present
- **Format:** YouTube + Meta video ads
- **Result:** Scaled to $100M+ revenue, Super Bowl ad in 2021

## What Made It Work

### Hero Ad: "You're Not a Dish"
- Hook: "You're not a dish. You're a man." — instant identity play
- Humor throughout keeps viewer engaged for 4+ minutes
- Attacks competitor (chemical soap) while positioning as natural

### Creative Approach
- Spokesperson character with strong personality
- Comedic demonstrations and comparisons
- High production value with authentic feel
- Consistent character across all ads (brand recognition)

### Messaging Strategy
- Masculine identity angle (not health/eco angle)
- Simple mechanism: natural ingredients vs chemicals
- Sensory language: smell, feel, experience
- Subscription model with easy entry point

### Scaling Strategy
- One hero video tested and iterated
- Cut into 15s, 30s, 60s versions for different placements
- UGC variations of the same core message
- Seasonal and limited edition angles for retargeting

## Key Takeaways for Media Buyers
1. Identity-based messaging resonates deeper than feature-based
2. A strong spokesperson character creates brand moat
3. Long-form hero ad + short-form cuts = full platform coverage
4. Humor makes ads shareable, reducing effective CPM
5. Consistent creative universe builds brand over time

## Swipe Elements to Reuse
- Identity hook ("You're not a...")
- Competitor attack through humor
- Hero creative cut into multiple formats
- Character continuity across campaigns
