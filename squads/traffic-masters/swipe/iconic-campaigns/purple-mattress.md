# Purple Mattress — Iconic Campaign Swipe

## Campaign Overview
- **Brand:** Purple Mattress
- **Year:** 2016-2017
- **Format:** YouTube long-form (2-4 min) by Harmon Brothers
- **Result:** $75M+ in revenue attributed to the Raw Egg Test video

## What Made It Work

### The Raw Egg Test
- Drops raw eggs on mattress from height — none break
- Visual demonstration of product's unique technology
- Proof-based selling at its finest

### Creative Approach
- Professional production with comedic tone
- Goldilocks character as relatable protagonist
- Educational + entertaining (edutainment)
- Demonstrates the "Hyper-Elastic Polymer" mechanism

### Messaging Strategy
- Named mechanism: "Hyper-Elastic Polymer" grid
- Physical demonstration > verbal claims
- Comparison to competitors through testing
- Clear call to action with offer

## Key Takeaways for Media Buyers
1. Product demonstration is the most persuasive ad format
2. Named mechanisms create differentiation and memorability
3. Long-form video works when content is genuinely engaging
4. Investing in one hero creative can outperform 50 average ones
5. Visual proof > written testimonials for physical products

## Swipe Elements to Reuse
- Physical product demonstration / stress test
- Named proprietary mechanism
- Character-driven narrative
- Edutainment format
