# Offer Positioning Winners — LinkedIn

## Purpose
How top B2B advertisers position their offers on LinkedIn for maximum response.

## Positioning Frameworks

### 1. Authority Position
- Lead with credentials, client logos, or industry recognition
- "Trusted by 500+ enterprise teams including [logos]"

### 2. Data-Led Position
- Open with proprietary data or surprising stat
- "78% of CFOs report this as their #1 challenge in 2026"

### 3. Contrarian Position
- Challenge conventional wisdom in the industry
- "Stop doing X. Here's what top performers do instead."

### 4. Peer Proof Position
- Show what similar companies/roles are doing
- "How [Company] reduced churn by 34% in one quarter"

### 5. Exclusivity Position
- Limited access, application-based, or invite-only framing
- "We work with 20 new companies per quarter. Apply here."

## Key Principles
1. LinkedIn audiences respond to peer validation over celebrity
2. Specificity beats generality (exact numbers, named companies)
3. Professional tone with edge outperforms corporate-safe copy
4. Offer must match seniority level of targeting
