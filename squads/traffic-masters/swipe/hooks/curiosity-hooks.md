# Curiosity Hooks — Swipe File

## Purpose
Hooks that open information gaps the viewer must close.

## Curiosity Mechanisms

### 1. The Incomplete Loop
- "There's one thing killing your ad performance and it's not what you think..."
- "I just discovered why 90% of campaigns fail at this stage..."

### 2. The Secret/Hidden
- "The strategy Meta doesn't want you to know about"
- "Hidden feature that top advertisers use daily"

### 3. The Unexpected Result
- "We tested 100 ads. The winner shocked us."
- "I tried the opposite of best practices. Here's what happened."

### 4. The Specific Mystery
- "This $3 change doubled our ROAS overnight"
- "One line of ad copy generated $2.3M in revenue"

### 5. The Contradiction
- "The worst-looking ad outperformed our best creative by 400%"
- "We scaled by cutting our budget in half"

## Swipe Collection

| # | Hook | Mechanism | Platform | Result |
|---|------|-----------|----------|--------|
| 1 | "Nobody talks about this Meta glitch" | Hidden | Meta | 3.1% CTR |
| 2 | "We ran the ugliest ad ever and..." | Unexpected | TikTok | 44% hook |
| 3 | "This one setting changed everything" | Specific | YouTube | 55% VTR |

## Guidelines
1. Always deliver on the curiosity promise — never bait-and-switch
2. Specificity increases curiosity (exact numbers > vague claims)
3. Combine curiosity with relevance to target audience
4. Works best for awareness and consideration stages
