# Social Proof Hooks — Swipe File

## Purpose
Hooks that leverage crowd behavior and peer validation to stop the scroll.

## Social Proof Types

### 1. Numbers-Led
- "50,000+ marketers already use this strategy"
- "Rated 4.9/5 by 12,000 customers"

### 2. Testimonial-Led
- "[Real Name]: 'This changed everything about how we run ads'"
- Video clip of customer sharing specific result

### 3. Trend-Led
- "Everyone's switching to [solution]. Here's why."
- "The #1 tool in [category] for the 3rd year running"

### 4. Peer-Led
- "Your competitors are already doing this"
- "See why [Role] at [Company Type] chose [product]"

### 5. FOMO-Led
- "1,200 people joined this week alone"
- "We had to close enrollment early last time"

## Guidelines
1. Real numbers always beat vague claims
2. Named people/companies > anonymous testimonials
3. Recency matters — "this month" > "all time"
4. Match social proof to audience (peers, not celebrities for B2B)
