# Authority Hooks — Swipe File

## Purpose
Hooks that establish immediate credibility and expertise.

## Authority Mechanisms

### 1. Credential Lead
- "After managing $50M+ in ad spend..."
- "As a Google Ads certified partner since 2015..."

### 2. Result Lead
- "We generated 10,000 leads last month. Here's how."
- "312% ROAS across 47 accounts. The secret?"

### 3. Client/Logo Lead
- "We help brands like [Name], [Name], and [Name]..."
- "Trusted by Fortune 500 marketing teams"

### 4. Media Lead
- "As featured in Forbes / AdAge / Search Engine Journal..."
- "What I shared at [Conference] changed how teams run ads"

### 5. Experience Lead
- "After running ads for 12 years and $100M spent..."
- "I've launched 500+ campaigns. Here's the #1 lesson."

## Swipe Collection

| # | Hook | Type | Platform | Result |
|---|------|------|----------|--------|
| 1 | "After $30M in Meta spend..." | Result | Meta | 2.8% CTR |
| 2 | "Google invited me to share this" | Media | YouTube | 48% VTR |
| 3 | "Our clients average 5x ROAS" | Result | LinkedIn | $52 CPL |

## Guidelines
1. Authority must be verifiable and relevant
2. Pair authority with value — don't just brag
3. Specifics beat generalizations ("$30M" > "millions")
4. Best for B2B, coaching, high-ticket, and education verticals
