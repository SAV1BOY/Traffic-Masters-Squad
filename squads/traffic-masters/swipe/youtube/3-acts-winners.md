# 3-Act Structure Winners — YouTube

## Purpose
YouTube ads using classic 3-act storytelling structure for maximum engagement.

## The 3-Act Framework
- **Act 1 — Setup (0-30s):** Hook + establish the problem/character
- **Act 2 — Conflict (30s-2min):** Tension, failed attempts, deeper pain
- **Act 3 — Resolution (final 30-60s):** Solution reveal, proof, CTA

## Winners Catalog

### 1. [Example — DTC Supplement]
- **Duration:** 2:30
- **Act 1:** Relatable morning routine gone wrong (fatigue, brain fog)
- **Act 2:** Tries coffee, energy drinks, naps — nothing works long-term
- **Act 3:** Discovers product, shows transformation, social proof montage
- **Emotional Arc:** Frustration > hope > confidence
- **Why It Works:** Viewer sees themselves in Act 1, stays for resolution

## Key Patterns
1. Act 1 must hook AND create identification
2. Act 2 builds investment — longer = higher intent at CTA
3. Act 3 proof must match Act 2 promises
4. Humor in Act 2 keeps viewers through the middle
