## Winners Catalog

### 1. [Example — Online Course $997]
- **Format:** In-stream skippable, 4 min
- **Hook:** "I spent $2M on ads last year. Here's what actually works."
- **Body:** 3 key lessons > each with mini proof > builds to offer
- **CTA:** Verbal at 2:00 and 3:45, end card with URL
- **Landing Page:** VSL opt-in
- **Why It Works:** Authority hook, value delivery, double CTA placement

## DR YouTube Principles
1. Front-load value — earn the right to pitch
2. Multiple CTA placements (early skip-catchers + end)
3. Companion banners drive 10-15% of clicks
4. Custom intent audiences outperform affinity for DR
5. Test 15s, 30s, 60s, and long-form versions of same script
