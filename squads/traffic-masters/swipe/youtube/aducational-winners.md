## Winners Catalog

### 1. [Example — Marketing Course]
- **Format:** In-stream skippable, 3 min
- **Teaching Element:** Shares one actionable tactic viewers can use immediately
- **Hook:** "Most marketers waste 60% of their budget. Here's the fix."
- **Structure:** Problem > Quick teach > "Want all 7 strategies?" > CTA
- **Why It Works:** Delivers real value, earns trust, natural transition to offer

## Educational Ad Patterns
1. Teach one thing well > covering many topics
2. Specificity in hook prevents skips
3. "Value-first" earns 40-60% higher VTR
4. Bridge from lesson to offer must feel natural
5. Whiteboard/screen share formats perform well for B2B
