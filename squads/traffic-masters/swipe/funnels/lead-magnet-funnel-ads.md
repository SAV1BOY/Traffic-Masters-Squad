# Lead Magnet Funnel Ads — Swipe File

## Purpose
Winning ads for lead magnet funnels (PDF, checklist, template, quiz).

## Funnel Stages

### Stage 1: Lead Magnet Acquisition (Cold)
- **Angle:** Immediate value exchange — specific resource for email
- **Copy:** "Free [Resource Type]: The [Outcome] [Format]"
- **CTA:** "Download Free" / "Get Instant Access"
- **Formats:** Carousel showing pages, video walkthrough, static with mockup

### Stage 2: Nurture Sequence Ads (Leads)
- **Angle:** Extend value, build relationship, introduce offer
- **Copy:** "Did you grab the [Lead Magnet]? Here's how to use it."
- **CTA:** "Watch the Tutorial" / "Read the Guide"

### Stage 3: Tripwire/Core Offer (Nurtured Leads)
- **Angle:** Upgrade from free to paid
- **Copy:** "The [Lead Magnet] showed you what. [Offer] shows you how."
- **CTA:** "Get Started" / "Buy Now"

## Lead Magnet Types & Performance

| Type | Avg CPL | Lead Quality | Best Platform |
|------|---------|-------------|---------------|
| Checklist/Cheatsheet | $2-5 | Medium | Meta |
| Template/Swipe File | $3-7 | Medium-High | Meta |
| Quiz/Assessment | $4-8 | High | Meta/TikTok |
| Mini-Course | $5-12 | High | YouTube |
| Industry Report | $8-20 | Very High | LinkedIn |

## Best Practices
1. Show the lead magnet visually (mockup, preview pages)
2. Specificity in title drives higher opt-in rates
3. Carousel ads showing internal pages boost CTR 25%
4. Quiz funnels segment leads for personalized follow-up
