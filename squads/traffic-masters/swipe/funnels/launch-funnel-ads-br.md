# Launch Funnel Ads — Brazil Market (Lancamento)

## Purpose
Winning ads for Brazilian-style product launch funnels (Formula de Lancamento).

## Funnel Stages (Lancamento Model)

### Stage 1: CPL (Conteudo Pre-Lancamento)
- **Angle:** Free content series building anticipation
- **Copy:** "Aula gratuita: Como [resultado] em [prazo]"
- **CTA:** "Quero Participar" / "Inscreva-se Gratis"
- **Format:** Video teaser, carousel, founder story

### Stage 2: Engagement / Lives
- **Angle:** Community building, value delivery, anticipation
- **Copy:** "AULA 2 LIBERADA — Hoje voce vai aprender..."
- **CTA:** "Assistir Agora"

### Stage 3: Carrinho Aberto (Cart Open)
- **Angle:** Urgency, social proof, bonus stacking
- **Copy:** "Vagas abertas por tempo limitado + bonus exclusivos"
- **CTA:** "Garantir Minha Vaga"
- **Urgency:** Countdown, limited spots, bonus deadline

### Stage 4: Carrinho Fechando (Cart Closing)
- **Angle:** Last chance, FOMO, final testimonials
- **Copy:** "Ultimas horas — depois disso, so no proximo lancamento"
- **CTA:** "Entrar Agora"

## Brazil-Specific Considerations
- WhatsApp integration is essential for engagement
- Instagram Reels and Stories outperform Feed for CPL phase
- Payment: boleto and Pix options increase conversion 20-30%
- Compliance: follow CONAR advertising standards

## Key Metrics
- CPL (custo por lead), taxa de comparecimento, taxa de conversao
- Meta: 3-5% conversion rate on launch list
