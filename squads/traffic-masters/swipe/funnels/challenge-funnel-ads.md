# Challenge Funnel Ads — Swipe File

## Purpose
Winning ads for challenge-based funnels (3-day, 5-day, 7-day).

## Funnel Stages

### Stage 1: Challenge Registration (Cold)
- **Angle:** Free challenge promise with specific outcome
- **Copy:** "Join the free 5-Day [Outcome] Challenge"
- **CTA:** "Join Free" / "Reserve Your Spot"
- **Key:** Emphasize what they'll build/achieve by end of challenge

### Stage 2: Day-by-Day Engagement (Participants)
- **Angle:** Daily nudges with value previews
- **Copy:** "Day 2 is LIVE — today you'll learn [specific thing]"
- **CTA:** "Watch Day 2" / "Complete Today's Task"

### Stage 3: Community Building (Active Participants)
- **Angle:** Peer momentum and group energy
- **Copy:** "500 people just completed Day 3 — have you?"
- **CTA:** "Join the Discussion"

### Stage 4: Offer (Challenge Completers)
- **Angle:** Natural next step after challenge wins
- **Copy:** "You did [challenge result]. Ready for the full system?"
- **CTA:** "Enroll Now" / "Apply"

## Swipe Examples

| Stage | Hook | Platform | Metric |
|-------|------|----------|--------|
| Registration | "5 days to your first [result]" | Meta | $5 CPL |
| Day nudge | "Don't fall behind — Day 3 unlocks..." | Meta | 62% open |
| Offer | "Challenge grads get $500 off" | Meta | $220 CPA |

## Challenge Funnel Best Practices
1. Challenge must deliver a quick win by Day 2
2. Daily engagement ads keep completion rates high
3. Offer feels like natural next step, not hard pivot
4. Urgency on enrollment window post-challenge
