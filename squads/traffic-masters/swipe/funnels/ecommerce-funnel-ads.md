# Ecommerce Funnel Ads — Swipe File

## Purpose
Winning ads at each stage of the ecommerce purchase funnel.

## Funnel Stages

### Stage 1: Awareness / Prospecting (Cold)
- **Angle:** Brand story, problem awareness, lifestyle aspiration
- **Formats:** Video (brand story), Carousel (product range), UGC
- **CTA:** "Shop Now" / "Learn More"
- **Targeting:** Broad, interest-based, lookalikes

### Stage 2: Consideration (Warm — Site Visitors)
- **Angle:** Product education, social proof, comparison
- **Formats:** Testimonial video, review carousel, demo
- **CTA:** "See Why 10,000+ Choose Us"
- **Window:** 7-14 day website visitors

### Stage 3: Conversion (Hot — ATC/Checkout)
- **Angle:** Urgency, incentive, objection removal
- **Formats:** Dynamic product ads, reminder with offer
- **CTA:** "Complete Your Order" / "Get 10% Off"
- **Window:** 1-7 day cart abandoners

### Stage 4: Retention (Customers)
- **Angle:** Cross-sell, replenishment, loyalty
- **Formats:** New arrivals, complementary products, VIP offers
- **CTA:** "Shop New Arrivals" / "Reorder"

## Benchmark Metrics by Stage

| Stage | Avg ROAS | Avg CPA | Key Metric |
|-------|----------|---------|------------|
| Prospecting | 1-3x | $25-50 | nCAC |
| Consideration | 3-6x | $15-30 | CTR |

| Retention | 5-10x | $8-20 | LTV/CAC |
