# Webinar Funnel Ads — Swipe File

## Purpose
Winning ads at each stage of the webinar funnel.

## Funnel Stages & Ad Types

### Stage 1: Registration Ads (Cold)
- **Angle:** Value promise + curiosity about what they'll learn
- **Copy:** "Free training: How to [outcome] in [timeframe]"
- **CTA:** "Save Your Seat" / "Register Free"
- **Format:** Video teaser or static with speaker photo

### Stage 2: Reminder Ads (Registered)
- **Angle:** Anticipation building + show rate optimization
- **Copy:** "Starting in X hours — don't miss this"
- **CTA:** "Add to Calendar" / "Join Now"
- **Frequency:** 3 reminders (24hr, 1hr, live now)

### Stage 3: Replay Ads (No-Shows)
- **Angle:** FOMO + highlight best moment
- **Copy:** "You missed it — but the replay is live for 48hrs"
- **CTA:** "Watch Replay"

### Stage 4: Offer Ads (Attendees)
- **Angle:** Reinforce webinar content + extend offer
- **Copy:** Testimonials from attendees, offer deadline
- **CTA:** "Enroll Now" / "Book Your Call"
