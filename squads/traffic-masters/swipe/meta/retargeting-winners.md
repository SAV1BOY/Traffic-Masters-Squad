## Purpose
Winning retargeting ads organized by window and intent level.

## Winners by Window

### 1-3 Day (Hot)
- Messaging: Cart reminder, "Still thinking about it?"
- Urgency: Medium — "Selling fast"
- Offer: Same + free shipping nudge

### 7-14 Day (Warm)
- Messaging: Objection handling, testimonials, FAQ
- Urgency: Soft — social proof driven
- Offer: Same or small bonus

### 30+ Day (Cool)
- Messaging: New angle, fresh creative, re-education
- Urgency: None — value-led
- Offer: May include discount or new lead magnet

## Key Learnings
1. Frequency cap at 3-4/week prevents fatigue
2. Sequenced messaging outperforms single ad
3. Dynamic product ads best for ecom ATC retargeting
4. Testimonial ads best for mid-funnel consideration
