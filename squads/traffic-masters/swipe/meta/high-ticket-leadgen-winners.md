# High-Ticket Lead Gen Winners — Meta

## Purpose
Winning ads for high-ticket offers ($2K+) focusing on qualification and value.

- **Funnel:** (webinar > call / VSL > application / challenge > offer)
- **Ad Format:** (Video / Static / Carousel)
- **Metrics:** (CPL, lead-to-call rate, close rate)
- **Why It Works:**

## Winners Catalog

### 1. [Example — Business Coaching $10K Program]
- **Qualification Approach:** Self-select with "If you're doing $X/mo..."
- **Value Proposition:** "Add $50K/mo in 90 days" with case study proof
- **Lead Magnet:** Free training webinar
- **Funnel:** Webinar > application > call
- **Why It Works:** Pre-qualifies in hook, stacks proof, positions exclusivity

## Patterns in High-Ticket
1. Qualification language in first line filters tire-kickers
2. Case study proof outperforms testimonials
3. Application funnels yield higher close rates
4. Video ads outperform static for $5K+ offers
