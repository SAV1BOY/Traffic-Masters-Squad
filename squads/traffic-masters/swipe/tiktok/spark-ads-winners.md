## Spark Ads Best Practices
1. Boost content that already has organic traction
2. Organic engagement (likes, comments) persists — builds social proof
3. Creator handle shows instead of brand — higher trust
4. Let top organic posts run 48-72hrs before deciding to Spark
5. Use identity: creator post (not brand repost) for authenticity

## Selection Criteria
- Hook rate > 30% in first 3s organically
- Positive comment sentiment > 80%
- Completion rate above platform average
