## Winners Catalog

### 1. [Example — Beauty Brand]
- **Creator Type:** Micro-influencer, authentic feel
- **Hook:** "OK so I wasn't going to post this but..."
- **Structure:** Storytime > product reveal > before/after
- **Duration:** 45s
- **Sound:** Trending audio, low volume under voiceover
- **Why It Works:** Feels like organic content, curiosity-driven hook

## Native Principles
1. Vertical 9:16, shot on phone, no polish
2. First frame must not look like an ad
3. Trending sounds boost distribution 20-40%
4. Caption hooks work as secondary hook layer
5. Comment section engagement signals boost delivery
