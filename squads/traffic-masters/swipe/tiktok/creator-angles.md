# Creator Angles — TikTok

## Purpose
Proven creator content angles that drive performance on TikTok ads.

## Angle Categories

### 1. The Honest Review
- Creator gives genuine-feeling review, mentions one minor "con"
- Authenticity drives trust, the con makes the pros believable

### 2. The Unexpected Discovery
- "I can't believe I just found out about this..."
- Curiosity-driven, positions product as hidden gem

### 3. The Routine Integration
- Product shown within daily routine (GRWM, morning routine, desk setup)
- Native placement, low resistance

### 4. The Comparison
- "I tried [competitor] vs [product] and here's what happened"
- Direct comparison builds perceived value

### 5. The Transformation
- Before/after with authentic reaction
- Works best for beauty, fitness, home, organization

### 6. The POV
- "POV: You finally found a [product] that actually works"
- Relatable scenario, viewer self-inserts

### 7. The Stitched Response
- Responds to trending question or comment with product as answer
- Leverages existing conversation

## Creator Brief Template
1. Hook instruction (first 2 seconds)
2. Key talking points (3 max)
3. CTA instruction
4. Do's and don'ts
5. Reference videos for tone
