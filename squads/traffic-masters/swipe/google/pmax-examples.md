## Examples Catalog

### 1. [Example — DTC Ecommerce]
- **Asset Group Theme:** Best sellers grouped by category
- **Audience Signals:** Purchase lookalikes + competitor custom segments
- **Creative Assets:** 15 images, 5 videos, 15 headlines, 5 descriptions
- **Final URL Expansion:** Off (controlled landing pages)
- **Bidding:** tROAS 400%
- **Key Insight:** Separate asset groups by product margin tier

## PMax Patterns Observed
1. More creative assets = more placements = lower CPA
2. Audience signals guide but don't restrict targeting
3. Brand exclusions prevent cannibalization of branded search
4. Video assets prevent auto-generated low-quality videos
5. Asset group theming by intent > by product
