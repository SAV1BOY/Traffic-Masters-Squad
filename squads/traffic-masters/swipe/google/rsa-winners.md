## RSA Best Practices Observed
1. Pin brand name in H1 or H2 for branded campaigns
2. Include numbers/stats in at least 5 headlines
3. Mix benefit, feature, and CTA headlines
4. Test emotional vs. rational descriptions
5. Ad strength "Excellent" correlates with 10-15% better CTR
