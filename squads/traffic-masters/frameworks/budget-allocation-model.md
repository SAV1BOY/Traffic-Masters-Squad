# Budget Allocation Model
> **Type**: Media Planning Framework
> **Used by agents**: Media Buyer, Traffic Chief, Performance Analyst

## Overview
A framework for allocating ad spend across proven, promising, and experimental initiatives using the 70/20/10 rule. Includes channel allocation by funnel stage, seasonal adjustments, and minimum viable budgets per channel. Prevents both over-concentration and over-diversification of marketing investment.

## When to Use
- Planning monthly or quarterly ad budgets
- Allocating budget across multiple channels or campaigns
- Deciding how much to invest in testing vs scaling
- When budget is limited and prioritization is critical

## The Framework

### 70/20/10 Rule
- **70% Proven**: Channels, campaigns, and creatives with demonstrated ROI
- **20% Promising**: Initiatives showing early positive signals, ready to scale
- **10% Experimental**: New channels, audiences, or approaches being tested
- Rebalance monthly as experimental graduates to promising to proven

### Channel Allocation by Funnel
- TOFU (awareness): 30-40% — prospecting, broad audiences, video views
- MOFU (consideration): 25-35% — engagement, retargeting warm audiences
- BOFU (conversion): 25-35% — purchase, lead gen, hot retargeting

### Minimum Viable Budget (per channel)
- Meta Ads: $20-50/day minimum per ad set
- Google Search: $30-50/day minimum per campaign
- YouTube Ads: $50-100/day minimum per campaign
- TikTok Ads: $20-50/day minimum per ad group
- LinkedIn Ads: $50-100/day minimum per campaign
- Below MVB, data is too sparse for optimization

### Seasonal Adjustments
- Q4 (Oct-Dec): CPMs rise 20-50%; increase budget or accept reduced reach
- January: CPMs drop; opportunity for efficient acquisition
- Industry-specific seasons: Plan budget spikes 4-6 weeks in advance
- Event-based: Allocate separate budget for launches, promotions, events

### Scaling Rules
- Scale by 20-30% per increase, wait 3-5 days between increases
- Monitor CPA/ROAS after each increase — diminishing returns are normal
- If CPA increases >20% after scaling, hold and let algorithm stabilize

## Key Concepts
- Budget allocation is a strategic decision, not just a math exercise
- Under-spending on a channel is worse than not being on it at all
- The 70/20/10 ratio ensures stability while enabling growth
- Budget flexibility (10-15% reserve) allows capitalizing on opportunities

## Decision Rules
- IF total budget < $3,000/month — focus on one channel only
- IF budget $3,000-$10,000/month — 2 channels maximum
- IF budget $10,000+/month — 2-3 channels with clear allocation ratios
- IF channel hits diminishing returns — redistribute to next-best channel
- IF seasonal CPMs spike — maintain proven spend, reduce experimental

## Integration
- Feeds into: All campaign-level budget decisions
- Pairs with: LTV-CAC Unit Economics (profitability gates for allocation)
- Complements: Omnichannel Media Strategy (channel selection before allocation)

## Output
- Monthly budget allocation plan with 70/20/10 distribution
- Channel-level budget assignments with minimum viable thresholds
- Seasonal adjustment calendar for the upcoming quarter
- Scaling rules and triggers for budget increases per channel
