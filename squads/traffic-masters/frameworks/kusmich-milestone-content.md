# Milestone Content Framework
> **Author**: Nicholas Kusmich / Content Strategy
> **Domain**: Nurture Sequences, Content Planning, Funnel Strategy
> **Used by agents**: content-strategist-agent, funnel-architect-agent, email-sequence-agent

## Overview
A journey-based content framework that maps content types and CTAs to the prospect's
awareness level. Instead of blasting the same message to everyone, content is sequenced
by milestones the prospect must pass through before they are ready to buy.

## When to Use
- Planning email nurture sequences or drip campaigns
- Designing content calendars that align with the buyer journey
- Structuring retargeting sequences by awareness stage
- When conversion rates are low because content does not match readiness

## The Framework
### Milestone 1: Problem Awareness
- Prospect knows they have a problem but cannot name it clearly
- Content types: Educational blog posts, "Do you struggle with X?" ads, quizzes
- CTA: Engage (like, comment, share, take the quiz)
- Goal: Get the prospect to self-identify with the problem

### Milestone 2: Solution Awareness
- Prospect knows solutions exist but has not evaluated them
- Content types: How-to guides, comparison posts, case studies, webinars
- CTA: Consume (watch, read, download, register)
- Goal: Position your approach as a viable and differentiated solution

### Milestone 3: Product Awareness
- Prospect knows your product exists and is evaluating it
- Content types: Product demos, testimonials, FAQ content, free trials
- CTA: Experience (try, demo, sample)
- Goal: Remove friction and objections through direct experience

### Milestone 4: Decision Support
- Prospect is ready to buy but needs the final push
- Content types: ROI calculators, guarantees, limited offers, social proof stacks
- CTA: Buy (purchase, enroll, subscribe, book a call)
- Goal: Make the decision feel safe and urgent simultaneously

## Key Concepts
- Each milestone requires its own content — never skip stages
- Prospects self-select their milestone through behavior (clicks, views, downloads)
- Retargeting audiences map directly to milestones
- The framework is cyclical: post-purchase customers re-enter at Milestone 2 for upsells
- Content at the wrong milestone creates friction, not conversion

## Decision Rules
- IF prospect has not engaged with any content → serve Milestone 1
- IF prospect consumed educational content but not product content → serve Milestone 2
- IF prospect visited product pages or watched demos → serve Milestone 3
- IF prospect added to cart or started checkout → serve Milestone 4
- IF unsure of milestone → default to Milestone 1 and let behavior sort

## Common Mistakes
- Serving Milestone 4 content (buy now) to Milestone 1 audiences (just learning)
- Creating content only for Milestone 4 and wondering why cold traffic does not convert
- Not tracking which milestone each prospect has reached
- Treating all email subscribers as the same awareness level
- Skipping Milestone 3 (product experience) in high-ticket funnels

## Integration
- Feeds into: Kusmich Give-Before-You-Ask (Milestone 1-2 content is the "give")
- Pairs with: Traffic Temperature Framework (cold=M1, warm=M2-3, hot=M4)
- Complements: Customer Journey Mapping (milestone = journey stage)
- Source data: Mandalia 5W Avatar, ICP-and-Avatar frameworks

## Output
- A milestone-mapped content plan with content types, CTAs, and audience triggers
- Retargeting audience definitions per milestone
- Email sequence structure aligned to awareness progression
