# Offer Formula - Structuring Irresistible Offers

## Overview

The Offer Formula is Molly Pittman's system for structuring offers that convert. The core insight: the offer is the number one lever in any campaign. Before touching your ads, your targeting, or your creative, fix the offer. A mediocre ad with a great offer will outperform a great ad with a mediocre offer every time. This framework provides 9+ funnel templates and a universal offer structure.

## When to Use

- Launching a new product or service and need to position the offer
- Campaign CPA is too high despite good traffic metrics (the offer is the bottleneck)
- Building a funnel from scratch and need the right offer at each stage
- Testing new price points, bonuses, or positioning for an existing product
- Diagnosing why a landing page is not converting

## The Framework

### The Universal Offer Structure

Every offer, regardless of funnel position, should contain these seven elements:

1. **Promise**: What specific outcome will the buyer achieve? Be concrete and measurable. "Learn Facebook ads" is weak. "Get your first 100 leads in 30 days" is strong.

2. **Proof**: Why should they believe the promise? Testimonials, case studies, data, credentials, demonstrations. Stack multiple forms of proof.

3. **Price**: What does it cost? Frame the price against the value delivered. Anchor against alternatives. Show the math.

4. **Bonuses**: What additional value do they get? Bonuses should overcome objections or accelerate results. Each bonus needs its own mini-offer (name, description, value).

5. **Guarantee**: What happens if it does not work? Risk reversal is one of the most powerful conversion levers. 30-day, 60-day, results-based, or "better than money back."

6. **Scarcity**: Why act now? Genuine scarcity only -- limited seats, deadline-based pricing, limited bonuses. Never manufacture fake urgency.

7. **CTA**: What exactly do they do next? One clear action. "Click here to enroll" not "Learn more about our programs."

### The 9+ Funnel Offer Templates

#### 1. Lead Magnet Offer
- **Purpose**: Convert cold traffic into leads
- **Price**: Free (email in exchange for value)
- **Examples**: PDF guide, checklist, template, quiz, mini-course, swipe file
- **Key**: Must solve one specific problem immediately

#### 2. Tripwire Offer
- **Purpose**: Convert leads into first-time buyers
- **Price**: $1-$47 (irrationally low for the value)
- **Examples**: Mini-course, toolkit, templates bundle, trial
- **Key**: The goal is a buyer, not profit. Acquiring a $7 buyer is more valuable than a free lead.

#### 3. Core Offer
- **Purpose**: Primary revenue driver
- **Price**: $97-$2,000+ depending on market
- **Examples**: Full course, coaching program, software subscription, done-for-you service
- **Key**: This is where the real transformation lives

#### 4. Profit Maximizer Offer
- **Purpose**: Increase average order value and lifetime value
- **Price**: Higher than core offer
- **Examples**: Upsell, cross-sell, premium tier, VIP access, done-for-you upgrade
- **Key**: Offered after the core purchase when trust is highest

#### 5. Webinar Funnel Offer
- **Purpose**: Educate and sell in a single event
- **Structure**: Registration page, webinar content, pitch, replay sequence
- **Key**: The webinar is the proof; the offer closes the deal

#### 6. Challenge Funnel Offer
- **Purpose**: Build engagement and commitment over 5-7 days
- **Structure**: Registration, daily content/tasks, community, offer at end
- **Key**: Participation creates investment and reciprocity

#### 7. Application Funnel Offer
- **Purpose**: Qualify high-ticket prospects
- **Structure**: Sales page, application form, call booking, sales call
- **Key**: The application filters for serious buyers and positions the offer as exclusive

#### 8. Free-Plus-Shipping Offer
- **Purpose**: Acquire buyers at breakeven with a physical product
- **Structure**: Free product, shipping fee, order bump, upsell sequence
- **Key**: The backend offers generate the profit

#### 9. Return/Reactivation Offer
- **Purpose**: Bring back past customers or dormant leads
- **Structure**: Personalized message, exclusive offer, deadline
- **Key**: Past buyers convert at 3-5x the rate of cold traffic

## Key Concepts

- **The offer is the lever**: A 10% improvement in the offer often outperforms a 50% improvement in ads
- **Stack value, not features**: Bonuses and proof create perceived value far beyond the price
- **Risk reversal converts**: The guarantee removes the final objection -- "What if it doesn't work for me?"
- **Temperature determines offer type**: Cold gets lead magnets, warm gets tripwires/webinars, hot gets core offers

## Decision Rules

- IF landing page converts below 20% for a lead magnet THEN the promise is not specific or compelling enough
- IF tripwire conversion is below 2% THEN the price-to-value ratio is not irrationally good enough
- IF core offer refund rate exceeds 10% THEN the promise and delivery are misaligned
- IF average order value is flat THEN you are missing profit maximizer offers in your funnel
- IF cold traffic will not convert THEN test a different lead magnet before changing ads

## Common Mistakes

- Trying to sell the core offer to cold traffic -- the temperature mismatch kills conversion
- Weak or missing guarantees -- leaving risk on the buyer is a conversion killer
- Generic bonuses that do not address specific objections
- Fake scarcity that erodes trust when buyers discover it is not real
- Too many CTAs on one page -- one offer, one action
- Skipping the tripwire -- the gap between free and $497 is too wide for most buyers
