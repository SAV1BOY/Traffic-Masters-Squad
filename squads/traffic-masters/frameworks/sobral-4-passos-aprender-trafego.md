# 4 Passos para Aprender Trafego Framework
> **Author**: Pedro Sobral / Traffic Learning Methodology
> **Domain**: Education, Skill Development, Traffic Management Training
> **Used by agents**: training-agent, onboarding-agent, skill-assessment-agent

## Overview
Four sequential steps for learning paid traffic management: Biblia (study platform
documentation), Clicar (click every button), Investir (spend real money), Cursos
(then take courses). The order is non-negotiable. Most people start with courses
and skip the fundamentals. Sobral insists on hands-on platform knowledge first.

## When to Use
- Training new traffic managers or media buyers
- Onboarding team members who are new to a platform
- Self-assessment of traffic management skill gaps
- When someone is struggling with campaigns despite taking many courses

## The Framework
### Step 1: Biblia (Study the Documentation)
- Read the official platform documentation cover to cover
- Google Ads Help Center, Meta Business Help Center, etc.
- Understand every campaign type, objective, targeting option, and metric
- This is the "bible" — the source of truth about how the platform works
- Duration: 1-2 weeks of dedicated study per platform
- Output: A personal reference document summarizing key platform capabilities

### Step 2: Clicar (Click Every Button)
- Open the ads manager and explore every single feature
- Create test campaigns without publishing — explore all settings
- Navigate every menu, report, and configuration option
- Build muscle memory with the platform interface
- Duration: 1 week of daily hands-on exploration
- Output: Familiarity with every interface element and setting location

### Step 3: Investir (Spend Real Money)
- Launch real campaigns with real budget, even if small
- Experience the learning phase, optimization, and real results
- Learn from actual data, not theoretical scenarios
- Start with small budgets ($5-20/day) and real business objectives
- Duration: Ongoing — minimum 30 days of active campaign management
- Output: Real campaign data, performance history, and practical learnings

### Step 4: Cursos (Then Take Courses)
- Now take courses, workshops, and training programs
- With Steps 1-3 complete, courses make 10x more sense
- You can evaluate course quality because you have hands-on context
- Apply course learnings immediately to your running campaigns
- Duration: Continuous learning alongside active management
- Output: Advanced strategies layered onto a solid practical foundation

## Key Concepts
- The order is non-negotiable: documentation → exploration → spending → courses
- Most beginners go straight to Step 4 and lack the foundation to apply learnings
- Steps 1-2 are free and can be done before spending any money
- Step 3 with real money teaches lessons no course can replicate
- By Step 4, you have enough context to filter good advice from bad
- This process repeats for each new platform or major platform update

## Decision Rules
- IF new to a platform → start at Step 1, no shortcuts
- IF struggling with campaign setup → you skipped Step 2, go back
- IF courses are not helping → you probably skipped Steps 1-3
- IF experienced but moving to new platform → restart from Step 1
- IF platform has a major update → repeat Steps 1-2 for new features

## Common Mistakes
- Starting with courses and skipping documentation study
- Reading documentation without hands-on exploration (theory only)
- Taking courses without having spent real money (no context for the advice)
- Rushing through Steps 1-2 to get to the "fun" part of spending
- Not repeating the cycle when platforms release major updates
