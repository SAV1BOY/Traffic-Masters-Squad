# CaAMP - Customer Acquisition Amplification Process

> **Author**: Ralph Burns
> **Domain**: Holistic Customer Acquisition Strategy
> **Used by agents**: traffic-strategist, operations-analyst, funnel-architect
> **Checklists**: caamp-diagnostic-checklist, funnel-leak-audit

## Overview

CaAMP (Customer Acquisition Amplification Process) is Ralph Burns' holistic approach to customer acquisition. The core insight: traffic, after-the-click experience, and business operations must be aligned. Most advertisers operate in silos -- the media buyer focuses on ads, the designer focuses on the landing page, and nobody looks at fulfillment, email sequences, or customer support. CaAMP breaks down these silos. "If your funnel leaks, more traffic just means more waste."

## When to Use

- Onboarding a new client or account and need a full diagnostic
- CPA is rising but nobody can pinpoint the cause
- Scaling campaigns but revenue is not increasing proportionally
- Multiple teams (media buying, CRO, operations) are not communicating
- Preparing for a Strategic Growth Plan (burns-sgp-30-60-90.md)

## The Framework

### The CaAMP Principle

The customer acquisition process is not a funnel -- it is a pipeline with three connected systems. If any one system fails, the entire pipeline breaks.

### System 1: Traffic (Getting the Click)

This is where most advertisers focus. It includes:

- Platform selection and account structure
- Audience targeting and segmentation
- Ad creative (hooks, copy, visuals, video)
- Bid strategy and budget allocation
- Campaign optimization and testing

**CaAMP lens**: Traffic is the fuel. But fuel without an engine is useless. Before increasing traffic spend, verify that Systems 2 and 3 are functioning.

### System 2: After-the-Click (Converting the Click)

This is where most advertisers lose money. It includes:

- Landing page experience (speed, design, messaging, congruence with ad)
- Offer presentation (value stack, pricing, guarantee, scarcity)
- Checkout or form experience (friction, trust signals, payment options)
- Thank you page and immediate post-conversion experience
- Email sequences (welcome, nurture, abandoned cart, post-purchase)
- Retargeting sequences aligned with funnel stage

**CaAMP lens**: The click is a promise. After-the-click must deliver on that promise. Ad-to-page congruence is non-negotiable. If the ad says one thing and the page says another, trust is broken instantly.

### System 3: Operations (Fulfilling the Promise)

This is where most advertisers are blind. It includes:

- Product/service delivery quality
- Customer support response time and satisfaction
- Refund and chargeback rates
- Customer onboarding and activation
- Upsell and cross-sell execution
- Customer feedback loops back to marketing

**CaAMP lens**: A refund is a negative acquisition. High refund rates mean your traffic is working but your operations are failing. CaAMP treats operations data as marketing data.

### The CaAMP Diagnostic Process

1. **Map the pipeline**: Document every step from ad impression to customer delivery
2. **Identify the leaks**: Where are people dropping off? Use data at every stage.
3. **Quantify the cost of each leak**: A 10% drop at checkout on 1,000 visitors = 100 lost customers
4. **Prioritize fixes by impact**: Fix the biggest leak first, not the most visible one
5. **Align teams**: Traffic, CRO, and operations must share data and goals
6. **Measure the full journey**: Track from first click to 90-day customer value

### CaAMP Metrics That Matter

- **Click-to-Lead Rate**: Are visitors becoming leads?
- **Lead-to-Customer Rate**: Are leads becoming buyers?
- **Customer-to-Advocate Rate**: Are buyers becoming repeat buyers and referrers?
- **Refund/Chargeback Rate**: Are customers keeping and using the product?
- **Time-to-Value**: How quickly does a customer experience the promised result?
- **nCAC (New Customer Acquisition Cost)**: True cost including all pipeline inefficiencies

## Key Concepts

- **No silos**: Media buying, CRO, and operations are one system, not three departments
- **Leaks compound**: A 10% leak at 3 stages means you are losing 27% of potential customers
- **More traffic amplifies problems**: If the funnel leaks, scaling traffic scales the waste
- **Operations is marketing**: Refund rates, support tickets, and chargeback rates are marketing metrics
- **Full-journey thinking**: The customer relationship does not end at the purchase confirmation page

## Decision Rules

- IF CPA is rising but CTR and CVR are stable THEN the leak is post-conversion (System 3)
- IF high traffic but low leads THEN the leak is in System 2 (landing page)
- IF high leads but low sales THEN the nurture sequence or sales process is broken (System 2)
- IF high sales but high refunds THEN the product or onboarding is broken (System 3)
- IF all three systems are performing THEN scale traffic confidently
- IF you cannot identify the leak THEN run the full CaAMP diagnostic before spending more

## Common Mistakes

- Blaming the ads when the funnel is broken -- ads get audited, funnels do not
- Scaling traffic into a leaking funnel -- spending more to waste more
- Ignoring refund and chargeback data -- these are signals of systemic problems
- Treating media buying as the entire acquisition strategy
- Not measuring post-purchase metrics -- the journey continues after checkout
- Having traffic, CRO, and operations teams with different dashboards and no shared metrics
