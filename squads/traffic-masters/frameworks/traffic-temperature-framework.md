# Traffic Temperature Framework
> **Type**: Audience Strategy Framework
> **Used by agents**: Media Buyer, Ad Midas, Traffic Chief

## Overview
Categorizes all traffic into three temperature levels — Cold, Warm, and Hot — based on the audience's awareness and relationship with the brand. Traffic temperature determines everything: the message, the creative, the offer, the landing page, and the expected conversion rate. Treating all traffic the same is the most common and expensive mistake in paid media.

## When to Use
- Planning campaign architecture and funnel structure
- Writing ad copy and creating creative for different audiences
- Setting conversion rate expectations and CPA targets
- Designing landing pages and post-click experiences
- Allocating budget across funnel stages

## The Framework

### Cold Traffic (Awareness Stage)
- **Definition**: Never heard of you. No prior interaction with brand.
- **Expected CVR**: 0-3% (direct response), higher for lead magnets
- **Audiences**: Interests, lookalikes, broad targeting, prospecting
- **Message Strategy**: Education, problem awareness, curiosity, value-first content
- **Creative Approach**: Long-form video, educational content, story-driven ads, UGC
- **Offer Type**: Free resource, low-commitment entry point, content, quiz, webinar
- **Landing Page**: Long-form, heavy on proof and education, address objections
- **Goal**: Move to Warm. Build awareness and initial trust.

### Warm Traffic (Consideration Stage)
- **Definition**: Aware of you. Has interacted but not converted.
- **Expected CVR**: 3-10%
- **Audiences**: Website visitors, video viewers, engagers, email subscribers
- **Message Strategy**: Proof, social validation, case studies, differentiation, nurture
- **Creative Approach**: Testimonial ads, case study videos, comparison content, retargeting
- **Offer Type**: Demo, trial, consultation, discount, bundle
- **Landing Page**: Medium length, proof-heavy, clear CTA, address remaining objections
- **Goal**: Move to Hot. Build confidence and desire.

### Hot Traffic (Decision Stage)
- **Definition**: Ready to buy. High intent. Returning visitors, cart abandoners.
- **Expected CVR**: 10-25%+
- **Audiences**: Cart abandoners, pricing page visitors, past customers, high-intent search
- **Message Strategy**: Urgency, scarcity, final objection handling, direct offer, reminder
- **Creative Approach**: Dynamic product ads, testimonial reminders, offer-focused, countdown
- **Offer Type**: Full offer with guarantee, limited-time bonus, free shipping, direct purchase
- **Landing Page**: Short, direct, minimal friction, prominent CTA, trust badges
- **Goal**: Convert. Remove all remaining barriers.

## Key Concepts
- Temperature determines everything — message, creative, offer, and expectations
- Cold traffic is expensive but builds the pipeline; hot traffic is cheap but limited
- Most businesses over-invest in BOFU and under-invest in TOFU
- The funnel warms traffic over time through sequential touchpoints
- Never send cold traffic to a hot-traffic landing page (and vice versa)

## Decision Rules
1. Classify every audience by temperature before writing creative
2. Set CPA targets by temperature — cold CPA will always be higher than hot
3. If cold traffic is not converting, check message-temperature alignment first
4. Allocate minimum 30% of budget to cold traffic to fill the funnel
5. Retargeting windows define temperature transitions (see Retargeting Architecture)

## Integration
- Feeds into: Full-Funnel Ads Strategy, Retargeting Architecture, Creative Layer
- Pairs with: Offer and Proof Stack (offer intensity matches temperature)
- Informs: Budget Allocation Model (budget split by temperature)

## Output
- Traffic temperature classification for all active audiences
- Message and creative guidelines per temperature level
- CVR benchmark expectations per temperature
- Temperature-aligned landing page specifications
- Budget allocation recommendation by temperature
