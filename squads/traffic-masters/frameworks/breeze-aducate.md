# ADUCATE/ADUCATES Framework
> **Author**: Tom Breeze / Video Ad Strategy
> **Domain**: YouTube Ads, Video Scripts, Creative Production
> **Used by agents**: creative-strategist-agent, video-script-agent, youtube-ads-agent

## Overview
A 7+1 step video ad scripting framework designed for YouTube ads. ADUCATE stands for
Aim, Difficulty, Understand, Credibility, Action Plan, Teach, Exit — with an optional
Stack step. The first 5 seconds (Aim) determine whether the viewer watches or skips.
Every subsequent step earns the next few seconds of attention.

## When to Use
- Scripting YouTube in-stream (skippable) video ads
- Creating educational or "aducational" video content that sells
- When video completion rates are low and viewers skip early
- Structuring any video ad longer than 30 seconds

## The Framework
1. **A — Aim (0-5s)**
   - Hook the exact target viewer in the first 5 seconds
   - Call out the audience directly: "If you're a [role] struggling with [problem]..."
   - The viewer must think "this is for me" instantly or they skip
2. **D — Difficulty (5-15s)**
   - Name the specific problem or pain point
   - Agitate: show the consequences of inaction
   - Make the difficulty feel urgent and personal
3. **U — Understand (15-25s)**
   - Show empathy — demonstrate you truly get their situation
   - Use their language, reference their experience
   - Build emotional connection before offering anything
4. **C — Credibility (25-35s)**
   - Prove you have the authority to solve this problem
   - Use results, credentials, client outcomes, media features
   - Keep it brief — just enough to earn trust
5. **A — Action Plan (35-50s)**
   - Present your solution as a clear, simple plan
   - 3 steps or fewer — complexity kills conversion
   - Make it feel achievable and logical
6. **T — Teach (50-65s)**
   - Deliver genuine value — teach something useful right now
   - This is the "aducational" moment: education inside an ad
   - The viewer should feel they gained something even without buying
7. **E — Exit (65-80s)**
   - Clear, specific CTA: what to do, where to go, what happens next
   - Create urgency or scarcity if authentic
   - Repeat the core promise one final time
8. **S — Stack (optional, 80-90s)**
   - Add bonus value: "And when you sign up, you also get..."
   - Increases perceived value before the final CTA
   - Use only when the offer benefits from stacking

## Key Concepts
- The first 5 seconds are everything — test multiple Aim hooks per script
- "Aducational" = educational + advertising: teach to earn trust, then convert
- Each step earns the viewer's attention for the next step
- The Teach phase differentiates you from competitors who only pitch
- Stack is optional but powerful for high-ticket or complex offers

## Decision Rules
- IF YouTube skippable ad → use full ADUCATES structure
- IF ad is under 30 seconds → compress to A-D-U-E (hook, problem, empathy, CTA)
- IF audience is warm/retargeting → shorten A and D, extend C and T
- IF product is simple → skip Stack, keep Exit concise
- IF completion rate is low → rewrite Aim and Difficulty first

## Common Mistakes
- Spending the first 5 seconds on brand logos instead of audience callout
- Making the Credibility section too long (becomes bragging)
- Skipping the Teach phase and going straight to pitch
- Weak Exit with no specific CTA or next step
- Writing for readers instead of speakers — scripts must sound natural
