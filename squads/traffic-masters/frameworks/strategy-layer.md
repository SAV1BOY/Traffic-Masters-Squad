# Strategy Layer
> **Type**: Stack Layer
> **Used by agents**: Traffic Chief, Pittman, Burns

## Overview
The Strategy Layer transforms Discovery Layer intelligence into an actionable campaign architecture. This is where the funnel is designed, channels are selected, budgets are allocated, measurement is planned, creative strategy is defined, and timeline is set. Every decision at this layer cascades through the entire stack. Duration: approximately 1 week.

## When to Use
- Immediately following completed Discovery Layer
- Quarterly strategic reviews and pivots
- Significant budget changes (up or down 30%+)
- New channel or platform expansion decisions
- When performance plateaus despite optimization efforts

## The Framework

### 1. Funnel Architecture
- Design the conversion path from first touch to purchase/conversion
- Select funnel type: Direct Response, Content, Webinar/VSL, or Ecommerce
- Map funnel stages: Awareness, Consideration, Conversion, Retention
- Define content type and primary channel per stage

### 2. Channel Strategy
- Select channels based on discovery insights and audience location
- Primary channel first (Meta for B2C, Google for B2B/services)
- Channel sequencing: master one, then add the complement
- Never launch on 4+ channels simultaneously

### 3. Budget Allocation
- Testing Phase (Month 1-2): 60% primary channel, 30% testing, 10% secondary
- Growth Phase (Month 3-6): 50% proven, 25% scaling, 15% new channel, 10% testing
- Scale Phase (Month 6+): 40% core, 30% scaling, 20% diversification, 10% innovation
- Budget floor rules: minimum $50/day per ad set (Meta), $30/day per campaign (Google)

### 4. Measurement Plan
- Define north star metric (Target CPA, Target ROAS, or Blended CAC)
- Business metrics (monthly): Revenue, profit, LTV, CAC payback
- Campaign metrics (weekly): ROAS, CPA, conversion volume, spend
- Ad-level metrics (daily during testing): Hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CVR

### 5. Creative Strategy
- Primary angles to test first (top 3-5 from creative angle matrix)
- Format mix: % video, % static, % carousel, % UGC
- Volume targets based on budget level
- Platform-specific adaptations

### 6. Timeline
- Weeks 1-2: Discovery
- Week 3: Strategy document approved
- Weeks 4-5: Creative production + account setup (parallel)
- Week 6: Launch
- Weeks 6-8: Learning phase
- Weeks 8-10: First optimization cycle
- Week 10+: Scaling decision

## Key Concepts
- Strategy debt compounds: launching without strategy creates waste that grows monthly
- Channel-market fit: not every channel works for every offer
- Measurement plan precedes media plan — if you cannot measure it, you cannot improve it
- Start simple, add complexity only as data justifies it

## Decision Rules
1. Strategy document requires Traffic Chief sign-off before campaign builds begin
2. Never launch on more than 2 channels simultaneously for new clients
3. Budget allocation must include testing budget — minimum 10% always reserved
4. North star metric must be agreed upon before launch
5. Creative strategy must include minimum 3 distinct angles
6. Timeline must be realistic — rushing strategy is prohibited

## Integration
- Receives inputs from Discovery Layer (all strategy is evidence-based)
- Outputs feed Creative Layer for production direction
- Budget allocation guides Media Buying Layer campaign setup
- Measurement plan defines Tracking Layer implementation requirements

## Output
- Strategy document: funnel, channel plan, budget, measurement, creative, timeline
- North star metric definition with target ranges
- Channel-specific tactical briefs for Media Buyer
- Creative strategy brief for Ad Midas and Breeze
- Client-facing strategy presentation for alignment and approval
