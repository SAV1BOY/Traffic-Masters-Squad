# UGC Creator Framework

> **Type**: Internal
> **Domain**: Creative Operations — User-Generated Content
> **Used by agents**: Ad Midas, Creative Analyst, Breeze, Media Buyer

## Overview

A comprehensive framework for sourcing, briefing, managing, and measuring user-generated content creators for paid advertising. UGC-style ads consistently outperform polished brand content in direct response campaigns because they inherit the trust and authenticity of organic content. This framework industrializes UGC production while maintaining the authenticity that makes it effective.

## When to Use

- Launching direct response campaigns on Meta, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts
- When polished brand creative is underperforming (CTR below benchmark)
- Building social proof assets for retargeting sequences
- Testing new angles or offers with low production overhead
- Scaling creative volume beyond internal production capacity

## The Framework

### Phase 1: Sourcing

**Platforms for Finding Creators**:
- Dedicated UGC platforms: Billo, Insense, JoinBrands, Trend
- Social media direct outreach: TikTok, Instagram (micro-influencers 1K-50K followers)
- Customer base: Actual users willing to create content
- Creator networks and agencies
- Fiverr / Upwork for budget-conscious options

**Selection Criteria**:
| Criteria | Minimum Standard |
|----------|-----------------|
| On-camera presence | Natural, conversational, not overly rehearsed |
| Demographic match | Aligns with target audience avatar |
| Content quality | Good lighting, clear audio (phone camera acceptable) |
| Reliability | Responsive communication, meets deadlines |
| Portfolio | 3+ examples of previous ad-style content |
| Authenticity | Does not look/sound like a paid spokesperson |

**Rate Guidelines**:
- Micro creators (1K-10K): $100-300 per video
- Mid-tier (10K-50K): $300-750 per video
- Established UGC specialists: $500-1,500 per video
- Raw footage only (no editing): 40-60% of full rate
- Bulk pricing: 15-25% discount for 5+ videos/month

### Phase 2: Briefing

**Brief Template for Creators**:

```
Project: [Client / Product Name]
Creator Name: [Name]
Due Date: [Date]
Deliverables: [Number of videos, lengths, formats]

PRODUCT INFO:
- What it is: [Description]
- Key benefits: [Top 3]
- Target customer: [Avatar description]
- Unique selling point: [What makes it different]

VIDEO GUIDELINES:
- Length: [15s / 30s / 60s]
- Format: [9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 minimum]
- Hook: [Specific opening line or concept — from hook library]
- Body: [Key talking points — not a script, talking points only]
- CTA: [Desired closing action]
- Tone: [Conversational / Excited / Skeptic-turned-believer / Educational]

STYLE NOTES:
- Film in natural setting (home, office, outdoors) — NOT a studio
- Natural lighting preferred
- Look at camera, not off to the side
- No visible branding of competitors
- Authenticity over polish — imperfect is good

DO NOT:
- Make medical, financial, or guaranteed outcome claims
- Use competitor brand names negatively
- Film in bathroom or inappropriate settings
- Use copyrighted music
- Include any content that could be considered offensive

DELIVERABLES:
- [X] raw video files (no editing, no text overlays)
- Filmed horizontally AND vertically if possible
- Multiple takes of hook section (minimum 3 different hooks)
- B-roll footage of product use (15-30 seconds)
```

### Phase 3: Production

**Formats and Lengths**:
- **Testimonial**: 30-60s. Personal experience with product. Story arc: problem, discovery, result.
- **Unboxing**: 15-30s. First impressions, genuine reaction, product reveal.
- **Tutorial/How-To**: 30-60s. Demonstrate product use. Educational angle.
- **Problem/Solution**: 15-30s. State pain point, introduce product as answer.
- **Day-in-the-Life**: 30-60s. Product integrated into daily routine. Lifestyle context.
- **Comparison**: 30-45s. Before/after or old way vs new way. Policy-compliant framing.

**Hook Requirements**:
- First 3 seconds must capture attention — reference `hook-library-system.md`.
- Multiple hook takes required — minimum 3 different openings per video.
- Hooks should feel spontaneous, not scripted.

**Authenticity Markers**:
- Natural environment (not a studio)
- Casual clothing appropriate to audience
- Minor imperfections accepted (slight camera movement, natural pauses)
- Genuine facial expressions and vocal tone
- Product shown in real use context

### Phase 4: Review

**Review Checklist**:
- [ ] Hook is compelling and within first 3 seconds
- [ ] Audio is clear and intelligible
- [ ] Lighting allows product and creator to be clearly seen
- [ ] No policy violations in claims or visual content
- [ ] Talking points covered naturally (not read from script)
- [ ] Multiple takes provided for editing flexibility
- [ ] Raw files delivered in correct format and resolution
- [ ] No competitor branding visible
- [ ] Tone matches brief guidelines

**Revision Policy**: One round of revisions included in base rate. Additional revisions at 25% of original rate. Major reshoots at 50% of original rate.

### Phase 5: Rights and Usage

**Usage Rights Agreement Must Include**:
- Paid advertising usage rights (platform-specific or all platforms)
- Duration of usage: minimum 6 months, ideally 12 months or perpetual
- Whitelisting rights: ability to run ads from creator's social profile
- Editing rights: permission to cut, overlay text, add music, modify
- Exclusivity clause: creator cannot promote direct competitors for X days
- Testimonial authenticity: creator confirms genuine experience or discloses paid nature

**Whitelisting**:
- Running ads from creator's profile increases authenticity and often improves performance.
- Requires creator to grant Ads Manager access to their page/profile.
- Whitelisted ads should be labeled as paid partnership where required.
- Track whitelisted vs brand-account performance separately.

### Phase 6: Performance Tracking

| Metric | Benchmark | Action if Below |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| Hook rate (3s views) | 25%+ | Test new hook takes |
| CTR | 1.5%+ (Meta) | Revise CTA or offer framing |
| CVR | Matches or exceeds brand creative | Scale creator, order more |
| CPV | Below brand creative average | Continue — efficient awareness |
| Creative lifespan | 2-4 weeks before fatigue | Rotate to new creator or angle |

**Creator Scorecard**: Track each creator's aggregate performance across all deliverables. Top performers get priority for future briefs and higher rates.

## Key Concepts

- **The Authenticity Paradm**: UGC works because it does not look like advertising. Over-polishing destroys the advantage.
- **Creator Diversity**: Rotate creators to prevent audience fatigue. Minimum 3 different creators active per client.
- **Hook Banking**: Request extra hook takes beyond what is needed — build a hook variation library.
- **Scaling UGC**: At $50K+/month spend, target 8-12 new UGC assets per month from 4-6 creators.

## Decision Rules

1. Every UGC creator signs usage rights agreement before any content is used in ads.
2. No UGC goes live without policy review per `policy-risk-classification.md`.
3. Creator briefs must include specific hooks from `hook-library-system.md` — never leave hooks to creator discretion.
4. Whitelisting is default for top-performing creators — discuss during sourcing.
5. Replace underperforming creators after 2 batches of below-benchmark content.
6. Pay creators within 7 days of approved delivery — fast payment builds loyalty.

## Common Mistakes

- Sending creators a full script — they sound unnatural reading it.
- Not specifying hook requirements — getting buried leads instead of front-loaded hooks.
- Skipping usage rights — legal exposure when scaling successful UGC.
- Using one creator for everything — audience recognizes the same face and tunes out.
- Not providing product — creator cannot speak authentically about something they have not used.
- Over-editing UGC — adding heavy graphics and transitions defeats the purpose.
