# BRAIN AEO Framework
> **Author**: Kasim Aslam / Answer Engine Optimization
> **Domain**: SEO, AI Search, Content Strategy, Paid+Organic Integration
> **Used by agents**: seo-agent, content-strategist-agent, strategist-agent

## Overview
BRAIN stands for Build authority, Rank for intent, Adapt to AI search, Integrate
paid+organic, Nurture through content. It is a framework for Answer Engine
Optimization (AEO) — optimizing for AI-powered search experiences (Google SGE,
ChatGPT, Perplexity) alongside traditional search. The future of search is answers,
not just links.

## When to Use
- Developing an organic search strategy that accounts for AI search
- When organic traffic is declining due to AI-generated answers
- Integrating SEO with paid search for maximum visibility
- Building content that serves both traditional and AI search engines

## The Framework
### B — Build Authority
- Establish topical authority through comprehensive content clusters
- E-E-A-T signals: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness
- Build author profiles, cite sources, earn backlinks from authoritative sites
- Create content depth: pillar pages + supporting articles + FAQ pages
- Goal: Be the source AI models reference and cite

### R — Rank for Intent
- Target search queries by intent: informational, navigational, transactional
- Match content format to intent: guides for informational, comparisons for commercial
- Optimize for featured snippets and position zero
- Structure content with clear headings, lists, and direct answers
- Goal: Win the information architecture battle

### A — Adapt to AI Search
- Structure content for AI extraction: clear Q&A format, schema markup
- Provide concise, authoritative answers in the first paragraph
- Use structured data (FAQ schema, How-to schema, Product schema)
- Monitor AI search results to see if your content is being cited
- Goal: Be the answer, not just a result

### I — Integrate Paid + Organic
- Use paid search data to inform organic keyword strategy
- Use organic ranking data to optimize paid bid strategy
- Fill organic gaps with paid coverage and vice versa
- Unified reporting: see paid + organic together for total search share
- Goal: Maximize total search visibility at optimal cost

### N — Nurture Through Content
- Create content sequences that move prospects through awareness stages
- Email capture from organic traffic for continued engagement
- Retarget organic visitors with paid ads for conversion
- Build a content library that compounds in value over time
- Goal: Turn search traffic into relationships, not just visits

## Key Concepts
- AI search changes the game: being on page 1 is not enough if AI answers directly
- Authority is earned through depth, consistency, and citations
- Intent matching is more important than keyword volume
- Paid and organic are not separate channels — they are one search strategy
- Content nurture converts informational searches into eventual customers

## Decision Rules
- IF AI search is answering your queries directly → improve AEO signals
- IF organic traffic is declining → audit for AI search displacement
- IF paid CPC is rising → invest more in organic for those terms
- IF organic ranks well but does not convert → add paid retargeting
- IF content is not being cited by AI → improve structure and authority

## Common Mistakes
- Ignoring AI search and optimizing only for traditional results
- Building content without topical authority clusters
- Separating paid and organic teams without shared data
- Creating thin content that cannot compete for AI citations
- Not using structured data and schema markup
