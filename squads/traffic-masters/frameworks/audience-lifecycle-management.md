# Audience Lifecycle Management
> **Type**: Strategy Framework
> **Used by agents**: Media Buyer, CRM Specialist, Traffic Chief

## Overview
Framework para gerenciar audiences ao longo de todo o lifecycle — de cold prospect a cliente recorrente. Define como construir, segmentar, progredir e reativar audiences de forma sistematica, maximizando LTV e reduzindo dependencia de prospeccao constante.

## When to Use
- Planejamento de audience strategy para qualquer account
- Quando CAC esta subindo e prospeccao pura nao escala
- Implementacao de estrategia de retencao e recompra via paid media
- Estruturacao de campanhas por lifecycle stage

## The Framework

### Lifecycle Stages

```
[Cold] -> [Engaged] -> [Lead] -> [Customer] -> [Repeat] -> [Advocate]
   |           |          |           |             |            |
 Broad    Interacted   Captured   Purchased    Recomprou    Referiu
```

### Stage 1: Cold Audience (Prospeccao)
- **Definicao:** Nunca interagiu com a marca
- **Sources:** Broad targeting, lookalikes, interest-based, contextual
- **Objetivo:** Awareness e primeiro engajamento
- **Creative:** Educativo, problem-aware, hooks fortes
- **Metricas:** CPM, ThruPlay rate, hook rate, CTR
- **Exclusoes:** Todos os stages seguintes (evitar sobreposicao)

### Stage 2: Engaged Audience
- **Definicao:** Interagiu mas nao deu informacao de contato
- **Sources:** Video viewers (25%, 50%, 75%, 95%), page engagers, website visitors, ad clickers
- **Objetivo:** Deepening interest, mover para lead capture
- **Creative:** Social proof, product demo, comparison
- **Metricas:** Engagement rate, CTR, page views, time on site
- **Janela:** 7-30 dias de engajamento (testar janelas)

### Stage 3: Lead
- **Definicao:** Deu informacao de contato (email, telefone, form)
- **Sources:** Email subscribers, form submitters, quiz completers, free trial signups
- **Objetivo:** Nurturing e conversao para primeira compra
- **Creative:** Case studies, ofertas, demos, objection handling
- **Metricas:** CPL, Lead-to-Customer rate, time to conversion
- **Coordenacao:** Email marketing + paid media em sync

### Stage 4: Customer (First Purchase)
- **Definicao:** Realizou a primeira compra
- **Sources:** Purchase events, CRM data (custom audience upload)
- **Objetivo:** Onboarding, satisfacao, preparar para recompra
- **Creative:** Thank you, how-to-use, complementary products, review request
- **Metricas:** NPS, product usage, time to second purchase
- **Exclusoes:** Excluir de campanhas de prospeccao (waste de budget)

### Stage 5: Repeat Customer
- **Definicao:** Comprou 2+ vezes
- **Sources:** CRM segmented by purchase frequency
- **Objetivo:** Aumentar frequency e AOV
- **Creative:** Bundles, upsells, loyalty offers, early access
- **Metricas:** Purchase frequency, AOV, LTV, retention rate

### Stage 6: Advocate
- **Definicao:** Recomenda ativamente (referrals, reviews, UGC)
- **Sources:** Referral program participants, high-NPS customers, UGC creators
- **Objetivo:** Amplificar word-of-mouth e gerar UGC
- **Creative:** Referral incentives, UGC campaigns, community building
- **Metricas:** Referral rate, UGC volume, organic share of voice

### Reativacao (Cross-stage)
- **Win-back 30d:** Customers que nao compraram em 30 dias
- **Win-back 60d:** Escalar incentivo (desconto maior)
- **Win-back 90d+:** Ultimo esforco com oferta agressiva ou pesquisa de churn

## Budget Allocation por Lifecycle
| Stage | % do Budget | Justificativa |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| Cold | 40-50% | Growth engine principal |
| Engaged | 15-20% | Warm audience com alta conversao |
| Lead | 10-15% | Nurturing para conversao |
| Customer/Repeat | 10-15% | LTV maximization |
| Win-back | 5-10% | Recuperar churned customers |

## Integration
- Feeds into: Campaign Structure, Budget Allocation, Creative Strategy
- Receives from: CRM Data, Purchase Data, Engagement Data
- Pairs with: First-Party Data Activation, Privacy-First Targeting

## Output
- Audience map com stages, sizes, e targeting rules
- Campaign structure por lifecycle stage
- Exclusion lists para evitar sobreposicao entre stages
- Lifecycle progression dashboard (flow rates entre stages)
