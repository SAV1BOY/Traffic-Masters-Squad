# Customer Journey Mapping Framework
> **Author**: Universal / Customer Experience
> **Domain**: Funnel Strategy, Customer Experience, Touchpoint Planning
> **Used by agents**: strategist-agent, funnel-architect-agent, content-strategist-agent

## Overview
Maps the customer's path from first awareness to post-purchase advocacy. Five stages,
each with defined touchpoints, emotions, content types, and KPIs. Reveals gaps where
prospects drop off and opportunities where engagement can be deepened. The journey is
from the customer's perspective, not the company's.

## When to Use
- Planning a full-funnel marketing strategy
- Diagnosing where prospects drop off in the conversion path
- Aligning content, ads, and communication across the journey
- Onboarding a new client to understand their customer experience

## The Framework
### Stage 1: Awareness
- Customer state: Realizes they have a problem or need
- Touchpoints: Social media, search, word-of-mouth, PR, content
- Emotions: Curiosity, frustration, hope
- Content: Educational articles, social posts, awareness ads, podcasts
- KPIs: Impressions, reach, brand search volume, new visitors

### Stage 2: Consideration
- Customer state: Actively researching solutions
- Touchpoints: Website, comparison sites, reviews, webinars, demos
- Emotions: Evaluation, skepticism, excitement
- Content: Case studies, comparisons, webinars, product pages, FAQs
- KPIs: Engagement rate, time on site, lead captures, webinar registrations

### Stage 3: Purchase
- Customer state: Ready to buy, selecting provider
- Touchpoints: Sales calls, checkout, pricing pages, proposals
- Emotions: Anxiety, urgency, decision fatigue
- Content: Testimonials, guarantees, pricing clarity, onboarding previews
- KPIs: Conversion rate, AOV, cart abandonment rate, close rate

### Stage 4: Loyalty
- Customer state: Using the product, forming opinions
- Touchpoints: Onboarding, support, email sequences, product experience
- Emotions: Satisfaction, frustration (if experience is poor), engagement
- Content: Onboarding guides, tips, check-ins, loyalty programs
- KPIs: Retention rate, NPS, support tickets, product usage

### Stage 5: Advocacy
- Customer state: Loves the product, willing to recommend
- Touchpoints: Review platforms, referral programs, social sharing
- Emotions: Pride, belonging, enthusiasm
- Content: Referral incentives, share prompts, community, UGC campaigns
- KPIs: Referral rate, review volume, social mentions, UGC created

## Key Concepts
- The journey is non-linear — customers can skip or revisit stages
- Each stage has different emotional needs requiring different messaging
- Gaps between stages are where revenue is lost
- The journey continues post-purchase — retention and advocacy drive LTV
- Map the journey from the customer's perspective, not internal processes
- Different customer segments may have different journey maps

## Decision Rules
- IF awareness is low → invest in Stage 1 content and top-of-funnel ads
- IF leads are high but sales are low → fix Stage 2-3 transition
- IF churn is high → invest in Stage 4 loyalty and onboarding
- IF referrals are low → build Stage 5 advocacy programs
- IF unsure where the problem is → map the full journey and measure each stage

## Common Mistakes
- Mapping only Stages 1-3 and ignoring loyalty and advocacy
- Creating the journey map from the company's perspective instead of the customer's
- Assuming all customers follow the same linear path
- Not assigning KPIs to each stage, making gaps invisible
- Building the map once and never updating it as the business evolves
