# 6 Cs of Aducational Video Framework
> **Author**: Tom Breeze / Video Content Quality
> **Domain**: YouTube Ads, Video Quality Assurance, Creative Review
> **Used by agents**: creative-strategist-agent, video-script-agent, qa-review-agent

## Overview
A quality checklist for "aducational" video content — videos that educate and advertise
simultaneously. The 6 Cs ensure every video ad delivers value while driving conversion.
Use as a review lens after scripting and before production.

## When to Use
- Reviewing video ad scripts before production
- Auditing existing video ads for improvement opportunities
- Training creative teams on what makes aducational content work
- Scoring video concepts during creative ideation sessions

## The Framework
### 1. Context
- Does the video establish relevance in the first 5 seconds?
- Is the viewer's situation clearly acknowledged?
- Does the viewer immediately know this content is for them?
- Score: Is context established before any pitch begins?

### 2. Claim
- Is there a clear, specific, believable promise?
- Does the claim differentiate from competitors?
- Is the claim stated early enough to maintain attention?
- Score: Would the viewer want this outcome enough to keep watching?

### 3. Credibility
- Is there proof the claim is achievable?
- Types: results, credentials, testimonials, demonstrations, data
- Is credibility delivered without arrogance?
- Score: Does the viewer believe the claim after this section?

### 4. Content
- Does the video teach something genuinely useful?
- Would the viewer feel they learned something even without buying?
- Is the content specific enough to demonstrate expertise?
- Score: Is this the "aducational" heart of the video?

### 5. CTA (Call to Action)
- Is the next step crystal clear and simple?
- Does the CTA feel like a natural conclusion to the content?
- Is there a reason to act now rather than later?
- Score: Does the viewer know exactly what to do and why?

### 6. Continue
- Does the video create a reason to maintain the relationship?
- Is there a follow-up sequence, retargeting, or next piece of content?
- Does the viewer want more from this brand after watching?
- Score: Is the relationship designed to continue beyond this video?

## Key Concepts
- Each C builds on the previous — skipping one weakens the chain
- Content (C4) is the differentiator: most ads skip teaching entirely
- Continue (C6) is often forgotten but drives long-term ROI
- The 6 Cs can be used as a 1-6 scoring rubric (6 = perfect, <4 = revise)
- Videos scoring below 4 on any C should be revised before production

## Decision Rules
- IF Context score < 4 → rewrite the hook and opening
- IF Claim score < 4 → sharpen the promise or add specificity
- IF Credibility score < 4 → add proof elements or social proof
- IF Content score < 4 → add a genuine teaching moment
- IF CTA score < 4 → simplify the next step and add urgency
- IF Continue score < 4 → design post-view retargeting or follow-up

## Common Mistakes
- Treating the 6 Cs as optional guidelines rather than requirements
- Strong Context and Claim but weak Content (all sizzle, no substance)
- CTA that contradicts the educational tone of the content
- Ignoring Continue — treating each video as an isolated event
- Over-indexing on Credibility and sounding boastful
