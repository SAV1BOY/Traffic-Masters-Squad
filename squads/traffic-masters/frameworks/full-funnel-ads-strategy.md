# Full-Funnel Ads Strategy
> **Type**: Campaign Architecture Framework
> **Used by agents**: Traffic Chief, Media Buyer, Ad Midas

## Overview
A framework for structuring paid media campaigns across the full funnel: Top of Funnel (TOFU), Middle of Funnel (MOFU), and Bottom of Funnel (BOFU). Defines the objective, audiences, creatives, and KPIs for each stage. Budget allocation follows the 40/30/30 split as a starting point, adjusted by business maturity and data.

## When to Use
- Building campaign architecture from scratch
- Restructuring underperforming accounts
- Expanding from single-stage campaigns to full-funnel approach
- Aligning creative production to funnel needs

## The Framework

### TOFU — Top of Funnel (Awareness)
- **Budget Allocation**: 40% of total
- **Objective**: Awareness, reach, video views, traffic
- **Audiences**: Broad, interests, lookalikes (1-5%), cold prospecting
- **Creatives**: Educational content, brand story, problem-awareness videos, UGC
- **KPIs**: CPM, reach, video view rate, CTR, cost per ThruPlay
- **Goal**: Generate awareness and build retargeting pools

### MOFU — Middle of Funnel (Consideration)
- **Budget Allocation**: 30% of total
- **Objective**: Consideration, engagement, lead generation
- **Audiences**: Video viewers (25-95%), website visitors (7-30d), engagers, email subscribers
- **Creatives**: Testimonials, case studies, product demos, comparison content, FAQ
- **KPIs**: CTR, engagement rate, cost per engagement, landing page views
- **Goal**: Build trust, differentiate from competitors, nurture toward conversion

### BOFU — Bottom of Funnel (Conversion)
- **Budget Allocation**: 30% of total
- **Objective**: Conversions, purchases, leads, sign-ups
- **Audiences**: Cart abandoners, pricing page visitors, hot retargeting (1-7d), high-intent search
- **Creatives**: Offer-focused, urgency/scarcity, testimonial reminders, DPA, direct CTA
- **KPIs**: CPA, ROAS, conversion rate, cost per purchase/lead
- **Goal**: Convert warm and hot audiences into customers

### Cross-Funnel Rules
- Each stage feeds the next: TOFU fills MOFU audiences, MOFU fills BOFU audiences
- Exclusions are critical: exclude converters from BOFU, exclude BOFU audiences from TOFU
- Frequency caps prevent audience fatigue across stages
- Creative must be stage-appropriate — never use BOFU creative for TOFU audiences

## Key Concepts
- Full-funnel is not optional at scale — single-stage campaigns eventually plateau
- TOFU is an investment in future conversions, not immediate ROI
- MOFU is the most neglected stage and often the highest-leverage improvement
- The 40/30/30 split is a starting point; adjust based on funnel health data
- Measure TOFU by pipeline contribution, not direct ROAS

## Decision Rules
1. New accounts start with BOFU, then add MOFU, then TOFU as budget allows
2. If BOFU is underperforming, check MOFU audience quality before cutting TOFU
3. If retargeting pools are too small, increase TOFU investment
4. Adjust allocation quarterly based on funnel stage performance
5. Never cut TOFU entirely — it feeds the entire funnel

## Integration
- Feeds into: Campaign builds across all platforms
- Pairs with: Traffic Temperature Framework (temperature maps to funnel stage)
- Complements: Retargeting Architecture (retargeting windows per stage)
- Informed by: Budget Allocation Model (budget splits)

- Funnel architecture document with stages, audiences, and objectives
- Campaign structure map per platform aligned to funnel stages
- Creative brief per funnel stage
- KPI targets and alert thresholds per stage
- Budget allocation by stage with quarterly adjustment plan
