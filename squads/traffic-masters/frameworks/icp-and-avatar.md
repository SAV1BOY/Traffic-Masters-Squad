# ICP and Avatar Framework
> **Author**: Universal / Audience Research
> **Domain**: Targeting, Audience Research, Customer Profiling
> **Used by agents**: strategist-agent, targeting-agent, creative-strategist-agent

## Overview
A dual-layer audience definition framework. ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) defines the
company or account level: industry, size, revenue, tech stack. Avatar defines the
person level: role, pain points, goals, behaviors, objections. Together they create a
complete picture of who to target, what to say, and where to find them.

## When to Use
- Starting any new campaign or entering a new market
- When targeting is too broad and campaigns lack focus
- Building creative briefs that require audience understanding
- Aligning sales, marketing, and product teams on who they serve

## The Framework
### ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) — Company Level
- Industry/vertical and sub-segments
- Company size (employees, revenue, locations)
- Technology stack and tools they use
- Geographic focus and market maturity
- Budget range and buying process
- Disqualifiers: who is NOT an ideal customer

### Avatar (Buyer Persona) — Person Level
- Job title, role, and responsibilities
- Daily challenges and pain points (in their words)
- Goals, aspirations, and desired outcomes
- Information sources: where they learn, who they trust
- Buying behavior: research process, decision factors, objections
- Emotional triggers: fears, frustrations, aspirations
- Demographics: age, income, education (where relevant)

### Data Sources
- Customer interviews (5-10 ideal customers)
- Review mining (Amazon, G2, Trustpilot, Reddit)
- Analytics data (GA4 demographics, CRM patterns)
- Sales team insights (common questions, objections)
- Social listening (comments, forums, groups)
- Surveys (post-purchase, prospect qualification)

## Key Concepts
- ICP = the company you sell to; Avatar = the person you persuade
- B2C may only need Avatar; B2B needs both ICP and Avatar
- Use actual customer language, not marketing language
- Update ICP and Avatar quarterly as the business evolves
- Multiple avatars may exist within one ICP (buyer vs. user vs. champion)
- The best targeting and copy come from deep avatar research

## Decision Rules
- IF B2B → define ICP first, then Avatar(s) within that ICP
- IF B2C → focus primarily on Avatar with light ICP (if applicable)
- IF multiple products → create separate Avatar per product line
- IF targeting is not working → revisit ICP/Avatar with fresh research
- IF creative is not resonating → mine more customer language for copy

## Common Mistakes
- Creating fictional avatars without real customer research
- Using demographics alone without psychographics and behaviors
- Having one avatar when the business serves multiple distinct segments
- Never updating the ICP/Avatar as the market and product evolve
- Writing marketing copy in company language instead of avatar language
