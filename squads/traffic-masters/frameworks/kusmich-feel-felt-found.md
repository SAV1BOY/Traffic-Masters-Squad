# Feel-Felt-Found Framework
> **Author**: Nicholas Kusmich / Empathy-Based Copywriting
> **Domain**: Ad Copy, Landing Pages, Testimonials
> **Used by agents**: copywriter-agent, creative-strategist-agent, landing-page-agent

## Overview
A three-phase empathetic copy structure that mirrors the prospect's emotional journey.
Acknowledges their current pain, shares a relatable experience, then introduces the
solution as a discovery rather than a pitch. Rooted in the principle that people buy
from those who understand them.

## When to Use
- Writing testimonial-style ad copy or video scripts
- Landing page hero sections where emotional connection is critical
- Warm and cold audiences who need to feel understood before they act
- Any context where the prospect is skeptical or has been burned before

## The Framework
1. **FEEL** — "I know how you feel..."
   - Validate the prospect's current emotional state
   - Name the specific frustration, fear, or desire
   - Use their exact language (mine reviews, surveys, comments)
2. **FELT** — "I felt the same way..."
   - Share a relatable moment of struggle
   - Demonstrate you are not above them — you were there too
   - Build trust through vulnerability and specificity
3. **FOUND** — "Then I found..."
   - Introduce the solution as a discovery, not a sales pitch
   - Describe the transformation: before state vs. after state
   - Transition naturally into the offer or CTA

## Key Concepts
- Empathy precedes persuasion — always lead with understanding
- Specificity beats generality: "I felt overwhelmed by 47 ad metrics" > "I felt confused"
- The FOUND phase must feel like a revelation, not a commercial
- Works in first person (founder story) or third person (customer testimonial)
- Can be compressed into 3 sentences or expanded into a full VSL

## Decision Rules
- IF audience is cold AND skeptical → use third-person testimonial version
- IF audience is warm → use founder/brand first-person version
- IF product is complex → extend the FOUND phase with mechanism explanation
- IF offer is simple → keep all three phases to 1-2 sentences each
- NEVER skip the FEEL phase — it is the trust anchor

## Common Mistakes
- Rushing to the FOUND phase without earning emotional trust
- Using generic emotions instead of specific, researched pain points
- Making the FELT phase sound inauthentic or performative
- Turning the FOUND phase into a feature dump instead of a transformation story
- Forgetting to include a clear CTA after the FOUND phase

## Integration
- Feeds into: Kusmich Give-Before-You-Ask (FOUND phase can be a value offer)
- Pairs with: Kusmich 3Cs Invisible Influence (congruence in emotional arc)
- Complements: Mandalia 5W Avatar (source of FEEL language)
- Output flows to: Ad copy, video scripts, email sequences, landing pages

## Output
- A copy block or script following the Feel → Felt → Found arc
- Includes specific emotional triggers, relatable story, and discovery moment
- Ready for deployment in ads, LPs, emails, or testimonial compilations
