# Governance Layer
> **Type**: Stack Layer
> **Used by agents**: Traffic Chief, Fiscal

## Overview
The oversight and control layer of the traffic stack. Governs compliance, financial reconciliation, audits, cross-squad synchronization, and quarterly reviews. Ensures operations remain compliant, financially accurate, and strategically aligned. Ongoing.

## When to Use
- Continuously — governance is a permanent function
- Monthly financial reconciliation
- Quarterly strategic reviews
- When compliance issues arise on any platform
- When coordinating with other squads

## The Framework

### 1. Compliance Management
- Ensure all ads comply with Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn policies
- Track rejection rates. Target: under 5%.
- Maintain Green/Yellow/Red policy risk classification per offer
- Verify all performance claims are substantiated
- GDPR, CCPA, ATT adherence verified per market
- Weekly policy scan, immediate response to rejections

### 2. Financial Reconciliation
- Platform spend vs invoiced spend vs budget. Monthly.
- Platform conversions vs CRM/backend revenue. Monthly.
- Platform ROAS vs true ROAS (backend data). Monthly.
- Budget vs actual with variance analysis
- Track all costs beyond ad spend for true MER

### 3. Audits
- **Tracking Audit**: Full stack verification. Quarterly.
- **Account Structure Audit**: Naming, organization, overlap. Quarterly.
- **Creative Audit**: Active creative performance review. Monthly.
- **Permissions Audit**: Access control review. Quarterly.
- **Vendor Audit**: Tools, agencies, cost justification. Semi-annually.

### 4. Cross-Squad Synchronization
- Sales: Lead quality feedback loop
- Product: Launches, pricing changes, inventory
- Brand: Messaging consistency across paid and organic
- Finance: Budget approvals, spend reporting, ROI
- Cadence: Bi-weekly sync, monthly written report

### 5. Quarterly Reviews
- Performance vs target on all KPIs (CPA, ROAS, MER, LTV:CAC)
- Strategy assessment: what worked, what did not, pivots needed
- Budget reallocation recommendations for next quarter
- Competitive landscape refresh
- Creative intelligence: top learnings and patterns
- Next quarter plan: strategy, pipeline, budget, timeline

### Governance Calendar
- **Daily**: Compliance monitoring, rejection response
- **Weekly**: Policy scan, spend pacing review
- **Monthly**: Reconciliation, creative audit, cross-squad report
- **Quarterly**: Full audits, strategic review
- **Annually**: Full strategic review, next-year planning

## Key Concepts
- Governance prevents small issues from becoming expensive problems
- Financial reconciliation catches discrepancies before they compound
- Cross-squad alignment prevents siloed decision-making
- Compliance is cheaper than remediation

## Decision Rules
1. Ad rejections resolved within 4 hours of detection
2. Financial reconciliation completed by the 5th of each month
3. Spend variance >10% triggers investigation within 24 hours
4. Quarterly reviews are mandatory — no exceptions
5. Cross-squad sync cannot be skipped for more than 2 consecutive cycles
6. Any compliance violation escalated immediately to Traffic Chief

## Integration
- Oversees: Discovery, Strategy, Creative, Media Buying, Tracking, Optimization, Scaling
- Financial data from all ad platforms and finance systems
- Cross-squad data from sales CRM, product, brand team

## Output
- Monthly compliance report with rejection rates and remediation log
- Monthly financial reconciliation with variance analysis
- Quarterly audit reports (tracking, account, creative, permissions)
- Quarterly strategic review document with next-quarter plan
- Cross-squad sync meeting minutes and action items
