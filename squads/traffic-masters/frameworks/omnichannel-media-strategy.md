# Omnichannel Media Strategy
> **Type**: Media Planning Framework
> **Used by agents**: Traffic Chief, Media Buyer, Performance Analyst

## Overview
A framework for how Meta, Google, YouTube, TikTok, and LinkedIn work together in a cohesive media strategy. Defines channel selection criteria, the role each channel plays, and the recommended expansion sequence. Expansion rule: validate 1 channel, test a 2nd, then optimize both before adding more.

## When to Use
- Planning a multi-channel media strategy from scratch
- Deciding when and how to expand from one channel to multiple
- Allocating budget across channels based on strategic role
- When single-channel performance is declining and diversification is needed

## The Framework

### Channel Roles and Synergies
- **Meta (FB/IG)**: Primary demand generation and retargeting. All funnel stages. Best for B2C, ecommerce, lead gen.
- **Google Search**: Demand capture — intercept active intent. MOFU/BOFU. Best for services, SaaS, high-ticket.
- **YouTube**: Storytelling, education, awareness at scale. TOFU/MOFU. Best for complex products, brand building.
- **TikTok**: Discovery and viral awareness. TOFU primarily. Best for B2C, trend-driven, younger demographics.
- **LinkedIn**: B2B targeting precision. TOFU/MOFU for B2B. Best for SaaS, professional services.

### Channel Selection Criteria
1. Where does the target audience spend time?
2. Does the channel match the offer type and price point?
3. Does the team have creative capabilities for the format?
4. Is the minimum viable budget available?
5. Does this channel fill a gap in the current strategy?

### Expansion Sequence
1. **Validate 1**: Start with ONE channel. Master it. Achieve profitable, consistent results.
2. **Test 2**: Add the complement. Meta + Google or vice versa.
3. **Optimize**: Get both profitable before adding a third.
4. Add YouTube or TikTok as warranted by audience and creative capability.
5. Add LinkedIn if B2B targeting is needed.
6. Never add a channel until the previous one is stable and profitable.

### Cross-Channel Synergies
- Meta generates demand; Google captures the search it creates
- YouTube educates; Meta retargets the educated audience
- TikTok drives discovery; Meta and Google capture downstream intent

## Key Concepts
- Channels are not competitors — they serve different funnel roles
- Multi-channel creates reinforcement effects greater than the sum of parts
- Expand only when current channels are profitable and stable
- Cross-channel attribution is imperfect — use MER for strategic decisions

## Decision Rules
- IF B2C product — start with Meta, add Google Shopping
- IF B2B/SaaS — start with Google Search, add LinkedIn
- IF budget < $5k/month — one channel only until profitable
- IF adding a channel — allocate minimum 10% of budget for 90-day test

## Integration
- Feeds into: Budget Allocation Model (channel-level allocation)
- Pairs with: Attribution and Incrementality (cross-channel measurement)
- Complements: Full-Funnel Ads Strategy (channels fill funnel stages)

## Output
- Channel strategy document with roles and funnel assignments
- Channel expansion timeline with profitability gates
- Cross-channel creative adaptation guidelines
- Budget allocation by channel with minimum viable thresholds
