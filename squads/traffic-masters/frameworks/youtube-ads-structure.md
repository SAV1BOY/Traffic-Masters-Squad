# YouTube Ads Structure

> **Type**: Internal
> **Domain**: Media Buying — YouTube / Video Advertising
> **Used by agents**: Media Buyer, Breeze, Creative Analyst, Traffic Chief

## Overview

Defines the strategic framework for YouTube advertising across all ad formats. YouTube operates as both a search engine and a content platform, requiring a hybrid approach that combines intent-based targeting with audience-based discovery. This framework connects to Breeze methodology for creative excellence and the broader Google account structure.

## When to Use

- Brand awareness campaigns requiring video reach
- Top-of-funnel prospecting for products that benefit from demonstration
- Retargeting website visitors with video content
- Scaling beyond Meta into a secondary video platform
- Building remarketing audiences for cross-platform use

## The Framework

### Ad Formats

| Format | Length | Billing | Best For |
|--------|--------|---------|----------|
| In-Stream Skippable | 15s-3min+ | CPV (after 30s or interaction) | Consideration, conversions |
| In-Stream Non-Skippable | 15s max | CPM | Awareness, reach |
| Bumper | 6s max | CPM | Frequency reinforcement |
| In-Feed (Discovery) | Thumbnail + text | CPC (on click) | Engagement, subscribers |
| Shorts Ads | Up to 60s vertical | CPM/CPV | Mobile-first, younger demos |

### Campaign Objectives Mapping

- **Awareness**: Non-skippable + Bumper sequence. Maximize reach. Broad targeting.
- **Consideration**: Skippable in-stream. Custom intent + in-market audiences. Optimize for views.
- **Conversion**: Skippable in-stream with TrueView for Action. Remarketing + custom intent. Optimize for conversions.

### Targeting Layers

1. **Custom Intent Audiences**: Built from Google search terms related to product/service. Highest intent on YouTube. Create audiences of 10-15 tightly themed keywords.
2. **Affinity Audiences**: Broad lifestyle/interest categories. Use for awareness.
3. **In-Market Audiences**: Users actively researching a purchase category. Strong for consideration.
4. **Remarketing**: Website visitors (30/60/90/180 day windows), video viewers (watched 25%/50%/75%/100%), channel subscribers.
5. **Customer Match**: Upload CRM lists for suppression or lookalike expansion.
6. **Placement Targeting**: Specific channels or videos. Use for competitor conquesting or contextual alignment. Monitor closely — can limit scale.
7. **Topic/Keyword Targeting**: Contextual. Videos must appear alongside relevant content. Layer with audience targeting for precision.

## Key Concepts

- **Breeze Connection**: All video creatives follow Breeze methodology — hook in first 5 seconds, pattern interrupt, clear CTA. Creative quality is the primary lever on YouTube.
- **The 5-Second Rule**: If you do not capture attention in 5 seconds, the viewer skips. Front-load the hook, the brand, or the disruption.
- **Sequencing**: Use video ad sequencing to tell a story — Awareness (bumper) then Consideration (skippable long-form) then Conversion (CTA-heavy short-form).
- **View Rate Benchmarks**: Skippable in-stream 20-35% is healthy. Below 15% signals creative fatigue or targeting mismatch.

## Decision Rules

1. Always start with Custom Intent audiences — they bridge search intent to video format.
2. Minimum 3 creative variations per campaign at launch. Rotate weekly based on view rate and CTR.
3. Non-skippable reserved for brands with 6-second assets ready. Do not cut longer ads to fit.
4. In-feed ads only for content/education plays — not direct response unless product is highly visual.
5. Shorts ads must be native vertical content, not cropped horizontal. Repurpose UGC-style assets from Meta.
6. Frequency cap: 3 impressions per user per week for awareness. 5 for retargeting.

## Common Mistakes

- Using the same creative across all funnel stages without adaptation.
- Targeting too broadly without layering audiences plus demographics.
- Ignoring Brand Safety settings — ads appearing next to inappropriate content.
- Not setting frequency caps, leading to audience fatigue and complaints.
- Judging YouTube on last-click attribution only — it is an assist channel.
- Running conversion campaigns without sufficient pixel data (need 50+ conversions/month).

## Brand Safety

- Enable inventory type: "Limited Inventory" minimum. "Standard" preferred.
- Exclude sensitive content categories: tragedy, conflict, mature content.
- Create placement exclusion lists for known problematic channels.
- Review "Where Ads Showed" report weekly during first month.
- Use Ads Transparency Center to verify ad placement context.
