# Metodo Subido para Lancamentos Framework
> **Author**: Pedro Sobral / Launch Traffic Strategy
> **Domain**: Product Launches, Infoproduct Traffic, Campaign Phasing
> **Used by agents**: launch-agent, media-buyer-agent, campaign-planner-agent

## Overview
An 8-stage traffic method for product launches (lancamentos), particularly for
infoproducts and online courses. Each stage has specific objectives, budget allocation,
audience targeting, and creative requirements. Covers the full launch cycle from
pre-launch through post-launch, ensuring traffic strategy adapts to each phase.

## When to Use
- Planning traffic for product launches (especially infoproducts)
- Running webinar or challenge-based launch funnels
- Any time-bound campaign with a defined open/close period
- When launches underperform due to poor traffic phasing

## The Framework
### Stage 1: Pre-Lancamento (Pre-Launch) — Weeks -4 to -2
- Objective: Build anticipation and grow the audience
- Budget: 10-15% of total launch budget
- Audience: Cold — lookalikes, interests, broad targeting
- Creative: Content ads, value posts, brand awareness
- KPI: Cost per engagement, audience growth rate

### Stage 2: Captacao (Lead Capture) — Weeks -2 to -1
- Objective: Build the launch list (email/WhatsApp registrations)
- Budget: 25-30% of total launch budget
- Audience: Warm (engaged) + cold (prospecting)
- Creative: Registration ads with strong hooks and urgency
- KPI: Cost per lead, registration rate

### Stage 3: Aquecimento (Warm-Up) — Days -7 to -1
- Objective: Engage and warm up the registered list
- Budget: 5-10% of total launch budget
- Audience: Registered leads only (custom audiences)
- Creative: Reminder content, testimonials, social proof
- KPI: Email open rates, content consumption, show-up rate

### Stage 4: Abertura (Opening) — Days 1-2
- Objective: Drive immediate sales at cart open
- Budget: 15-20% of total launch budget (concentrated)
- Audience: Registered leads, website visitors, engaged audiences
- Creative: Launch announcement, urgency, bonuses, testimonials
- KPI: Sales, conversion rate, revenue

### Stage 5: Sustentacao (Sustain) — Days 3-5
- Objective: Maintain momentum after initial rush
- Budget: 10-15% of total launch budget
- Audience: Leads who have not purchased, cart abandoners
- Creative: Objection handling, case studies, FAQ content
- KPI: Sales velocity, objection resolution

### Stage 6: Ultimas Horas (Final Hours) — Day 6-7
- Objective: Create urgency for cart close
- Budget: 10-15% of total launch budget (spike)
- Audience: All warm audiences, especially cart abandoners
- Creative: Countdown, last chance, final testimonials, scarcity
- KPI: Final day sales, total conversion rate

### Stage 7: Fechamento (Close)
- Objective: Close the cart, finalize sales
- Budget: Minimal — last push
- Audience: Final retargeting push
- Creative: "Cart closed" or "last chance" messaging
- KPI: Total launch revenue, total ROAS

### Stage 8: Pos-Lancamento (Post-Launch) — Week +1 to +2
- Objective: Nurture buyers and non-buyers for next launch
- Budget: 5% of total launch budget
- Audience: Buyers (onboarding) + non-buyers (nurture)
- Creative: Thank you content, onboarding, waitlist for next launch
- KPI: Customer satisfaction, waitlist growth

## Key Concepts
- Budget is not evenly distributed — it follows the launch energy curve
- Captacao (lead capture) gets the largest share — no list, no launch
- Aquecimento prevents "cold" registrants from ghosting the launch event
- The Abertura-Sustentacao-Ultimas Horas arc creates a conversion wave
- Post-launch is not optional — it seeds the next launch cycle

## Decision Rules
- IF lead cost is too high in Captacao → narrow targeting or improve creative
- IF show-up rate is low → increase Aquecimento budget and frequency
- IF Day 1 sales are strong → maintain Sustentacao budget
- IF Day 1 sales are weak → shift budget to Ultimas Horas urgency
- IF non-buyer list is large post-launch → nurture for evergreen or next launch

## Common Mistakes
- Spending all budget on lead capture and nothing on warm-up
- Not having distinct creative for each stage
- Treating launch traffic like evergreen campaign traffic
- Ignoring post-launch nurture and losing future launch potential
- Under-budgeting the Ultimas Horas phase where urgency drives most sales
