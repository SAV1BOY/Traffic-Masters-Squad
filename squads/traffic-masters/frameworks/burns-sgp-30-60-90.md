# SGP - Strategic Growth Plan (111-Point Audit + 30/60/90 Day Plan)

> **Author**: Ralph Burns
> **Domain**: Strategic Planning & Account Auditing
> **Used by agents**: account-strategist, traffic-strategist, operations-analyst, cro-specialist
> **Checklists**: 111-point-audit-checklist, 30-60-90-action-plan-template

## Overview

The SGP (Strategic Growth Plan) is Ralph Burns' comprehensive approach to transforming an underperforming or stagnating ad account into a growth engine. It begins with a 111-point audit covering account structure, tracking, creative, funnel, and operations, then lays out a phased 30/60/90-day action plan. The SGP is not a quick fix -- it is a systematic overhaul that addresses root causes, not symptoms.

## When to Use

- Onboarding a new client or taking over an existing account
- Account performance has plateaued or declined for 30+ days
- Preparing to scale significantly (2x+ budget increase)
- After a major business change (new product, new market, rebrand)
- Annual strategic planning for advertising operations

## The Framework

### Phase 1: The 111-Point Audit (Days 1-30)

Audit everything before changing anything. The first 30 days are diagnostic -- no major changes to live campaigns until the audit is complete.

#### Category A: Account Structure (Points 1-20)

1. Campaign naming convention (consistent, descriptive, scalable)
2. Campaign objective alignment (traffic objectives match business goals)
3. Ad set structure (proper segmentation without overlap)
4. Audience overlap analysis (campaigns not competing against each other)
5. Budget distribution (aligned with funnel stages and performance)
6. Bid strategy appropriateness (correct for campaign maturity)
7. Placement optimization (or proper broad placement with data)
8. Geographic targeting accuracy
9. Dayparting analysis (if applicable)
10. Device performance analysis
11. Platform account health (policy compliance, account standing)
12. Ad account structure cleanliness (inactive campaigns, test remnants)
13. Audience size analysis (not too narrow, not too broad)
14. Lookalike audience freshness (source audience updated recently)
15. Custom audience configuration (website visitors, engagement, lists)
16. Exclusion audiences (purchasers excluded from prospecting)
17. Retargeting window analysis (recency and frequency)
18. Campaign consolidation opportunities (reducing fragmentation)
19. Learning phase management (ad sets exiting learning phase)
20. A/B test history and learnings documentation

#### Category B: Tracking & Data (Points 21-40)

21. Pixel installation verification (all pages)
22. Conversion event configuration (standard and custom events)
23. Conversions API (CAPI) setup and event matching
24. Event deduplication (no double-counting)
25. UTM parameter consistency
26. Google Analytics 4 configuration
27. Attribution model selection and understanding
28. Cross-platform attribution reconciliation
29. Offline conversion tracking (if applicable)
30. Revenue tracking accuracy (reported vs. actual)
31. Return/refund tracking integration
32. Customer data platform integration
33. Reporting dashboard accuracy audit
34. Data freshness (real-time vs. delayed reporting)
35. Conversion value accuracy (dynamic vs. static)
36. iOS 14.5+ impact assessment and mitigation
37. Server-side tracking evaluation
38. Third-party tracking tool assessment
39. Data retention and privacy compliance
40. Cohort tracking infrastructure (for LTV analysis)

#### Category C: Creative (Points 41-65)

41. Active creative inventory audit (how many unique concepts live)
42. Creative age analysis (percentage of spend on creative older than 30 days)
43. Hook diversity (are all three hook types represented)
44. Format diversity (static, video, carousel, UGC, testimonial)
45. Creative-to-audience alignment (right creative for right temperature)
46. Ad copy quality audit (clarity, specificity, CTA strength)
47. Visual quality standards (professional, on-brand, platform-appropriate)
48. Video view-through analysis (where do viewers drop off)
49. Creative testing velocity (concepts tested per week over last 90 days)
50. Winner identification and iteration history
51. Creative brief quality (research-backed vs. assumption-based)
52. UGC pipeline assessment (is user-generated content being sourced)
53. Testimonial and social proof in creative
54. Competitor creative benchmarking
55. Ad fatigue indicators (rising frequency, declining CTR)
56. Creative compliance (platform policies, legal requirements)
57. Offer clarity in creative (is the value proposition obvious)
58. Brand consistency across creative variations
59. CTA consistency and strength across all ads
60. Creative naming convention (trackable, meaningful)
61. Ad-to-landing-page congruence audit
62. Emotional resonance assessment
63. Specificity audit (vague claims vs. concrete promises)
64. Format-to-platform optimization (vertical video for Stories/Reels)
65. Creative production workflow documentation

#### Category D: Funnel & CRO (Points 66-90)

66-89: Run the full 24-point Conversion Architecture checklist (burns-conversion-architecture.md) across all active funnels, plus:
- Funnel mapping documentation
- Multi-step funnel drop-off analysis
- Email sequence performance audit
- Upsell and cross-sell conversion rates
- Cart abandonment recovery effectiveness
90. Overall funnel conversion rate vs. industry benchmark

#### Category E: Operations & Business (Points 91-111)

91. Customer refund rate by acquisition source
92. Chargeback rate and trends
93. Customer support ticket volume and themes
94. Product/service delivery timeline and quality
95. Customer onboarding completion rate
96. Net Promoter Score or customer satisfaction metric
97. Customer retention and churn rate
98. Repeat purchase rate by cohort
99. Average order value trends
100. Revenue per customer trends
101. Fulfillment cost per customer
102. Customer lifetime value by source and cohort
103. Payback period by channel
104. Cash flow impact of current acquisition pace
105. Team capacity assessment (can operations handle 2x volume?)
106. Competitive landscape changes
107. Market seasonality analysis
108. Pricing strategy competitiveness
109. Partnership and affiliate channel assessment
110. Organic traffic and SEO baseline
111. Overall nCAC calculation and target setting

### Phase 2: Fix Fundamentals (Days 31-60)

Based on audit findings, fix the foundation before optimizing.

**Priority 1: Tracking (Week 5-6)**
- Fix all tracking issues identified in Category B
- Implement CAPI if not configured
- Verify conversion event accuracy
- Set up proper attribution reporting
- Establish baseline nCAC

**Priority 2: Account Structure (Week 6-7)**
- Consolidate fragmented campaigns
- Fix audience overlap issues
- Configure proper exclusion audiences
- Align bid strategies with objectives
- Clean up inactive campaigns and ad sets

**Priority 3: Creative Pipeline (Week 7-8)**
- Run Creative Lab research (burns-creative-lab.md)
- Establish weekly creative production cadence
- Kill all fatigued creative (frequency above 4, declining CTR)
- Launch first Kaizen Kreative sandbox test cycle
- Implement creative naming convention

**Priority 4: Funnel Quick Wins (Week 8)**
- Fix all failed Conversion Architecture points
- Improve page speed to under 3 seconds
- Fix ad-to-page congruence issues
- Optimize forms to 3 or fewer fields
- Add trust signals and proof near CTAs

### Phase 3: Optimize and Scale (Days 61-90)

With fundamentals fixed, shift to growth.

**Week 9-10: Apply Kaizen Kreative at full velocity**
- Run weekly sandbox tests with 5-15 new concepts
- Identify unicorn ads and begin scaling
- Iterate on winners (3-5 variations each)
- Establish ongoing creative refresh cadence

**Week 11-12: Scale winners**
- Increase budget on winning campaigns 20-30% every 3-5 days
- Expand to new audiences with proven creative
- Evaluate platform diversification opportunities
- Monitor nCAC weekly against targets

**Week 12+: Diversify and compound**
- Test new traffic sources with validated creative and funnels
- Implement advanced testing (incrementality, holdout tests)
- Build LTV cohort tracking for ongoing nCAC refinement
- Document SOPs for all recurring processes

## Key Concepts

- **Audit before action**: The first 30 days are diagnostic. Resist the urge to change things before understanding them.
- **Fundamentals before optimization**: Tracking, structure, and funnel health must be solid before scaling.
- **Phased approach prevents chaos**: Trying to fix everything at once creates more problems than it solves.
- **The 111 points are comprehensive by design**: Leaving blind spots means leaving money on the table.
- **The SGP is a living document**: Update it quarterly as the business and market evolve.

## Decision Rules

- IF more than 30 audit points fail THEN extend Phase 2 by 2 weeks before moving to Phase 3
- IF tracking issues exceed 5 failed points THEN tracking is Priority 0 -- fix before everything else
- IF creative inventory has fewer than 10 active concepts THEN creative production is the bottleneck
- IF funnel conversion rate is below 50% of benchmark THEN do not increase ad spend until CRO is fixed
- IF operations points reveal refund rate above 10% THEN the product/service must be addressed alongside marketing
- IF the account is fundamentally sound (fewer than 15 failures) THEN compress Phase 2 and move to Phase 3 faster

## Common Mistakes

- Skipping the audit and jumping straight to "optimization" (you optimize the wrong things)
- Fixing visible problems (ads) while ignoring invisible problems (tracking, operations)
- Trying to scale during Phase 2 -- fundamentals must be solid before adding volume
- Not documenting audit findings -- the audit is useless if results are not captured and shared
- Treating the SGP as a one-time event instead of a recurring strategic planning tool
- Allowing Phase 1 to drag beyond 30 days -- analysis paralysis prevents progress
