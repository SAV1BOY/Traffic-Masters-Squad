# Offer and Proof Stack
> **Type**: Conversion Strategy Framework
> **Used by agents**: Ad Midas, Traffic Chief, Mandalia

## Overview
A framework for constructing irresistible offers by stacking eight core components and backing them with layered proof. The offer is the single biggest lever in paid traffic. Better offers beat better ads every time. An offer without proof is just a claim. Proof without an offer is just content.

## When to Use
- Creating or refining the core offer before campaign launch
- When conversion rates are below benchmark despite good traffic
- Developing landing page copy and ad creative
- Competitive differentiation and positioning

## The Framework

### The Offer Stack (8 Components)
1. **Promise**: The specific outcome the customer will achieve. Clear, measurable, believable.
2. **Mechanism**: How the product or service delivers the promise. The unique method or system.
3. **Proof**: Evidence that the promise is real and achievable (see Proof Stack below).
4. **Price**: The investment required. Frame against value, not cost. Use anchoring and contrast.
5. **Bonuses**: Additional value that increases perceived worth without significant cost increase.
6. **Guarantee**: Risk reversal that removes the fear of making a wrong decision. Stronger guarantee = higher conversion.
7. **Scarcity**: Legitimate limitations on availability (time, quantity, access). Must be real, never fabricated.
8. **CTA (Call to Action)**: The specific next step. Clear, urgent, low-friction. One CTA per page.

### The Proof Stack (6 Types)
1. **Testimonials**: Customer quotes, video testimonials, star ratings. Specificity beats generality.
2. **Case Studies**: Detailed before/after stories with context, process, and results.
3. **Data and Statistics**: Numbers, percentages, research findings supporting the promise.
4. **Expert Endorsements**: Authority figures, industry experts, professional credentials.
5. **Media Mentions**: Press coverage, publications, awards, certifications.
6. **Before/After**: Visual or narrative transformation evidence.

### Proof Hierarchy
- Strongest: Video testimonials with specific results
- Strong: Case studies with data and named individuals
- Moderate: Written testimonials, expert endorsements
- Baseline: Statistics, media mentions, credentials
- Layer multiple proof types for maximum credibility

## Key Concepts
- The offer is not the product — it is the framing of the product
- A strong offer with weak creative outperforms weak offer with strong creative
- Every component of the stack multiplies the others
- Proof must match the size of the claim — bigger promises need bigger proof
- The guarantee is the most underused conversion lever

## Decision Rules
1. Every offer must include at minimum: Promise, Mechanism, Proof, Price, CTA
2. If conversion rate is below target, audit the offer stack before changing ads
3. If proof is thin, invest in collecting testimonials before scaling spend
4. Guarantees should always be tested — even small risk reversal lifts conversion
5. Scarcity must be legitimate; fake urgency destroys trust long-term
6. Test different offer stack configurations before declaring the offer "weak"

## Integration
- Feeds into: Creative Layer (ad copy and landing page content)
- Pairs with: Traffic Temperature Framework (offer intensity matches temperature)
- Informed by: Discovery Layer (avatar research, competitor offers)
