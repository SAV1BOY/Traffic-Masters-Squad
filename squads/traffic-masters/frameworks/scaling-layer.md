# Scaling Layer
> **Type**: Stack Layer
> **Used by agents**: Scale Optimizer, Mandalia

## Overview
Governs the strategic expansion of proven campaigns beyond initial parameters. Addresses when to scale, which direction, how to maintain efficiency during growth, and when to diversify. Activates once campaigns demonstrate repeatable, profitable performance. Ongoing.

## When to Use
- Campaigns achieving target CPA/ROAS consistently for 2+ weeks
- Client growth mandate requiring increased volume
- Market opportunity window (seasonal, competitive exit, trending demand)
- Single-channel dependency creating business risk

## The Framework

### Scaling Readiness Assessment
- Performance stability: CPA within target for 14+ consecutive days
- Creative depth: 5+ winning ads active across 3+ formats
- Audience headroom: current reach < 50% of total addressable
- Tracking reliability: platform vs CRM variance < 20%
- Creative pipeline: can produce 15+ new concepts/month
- Budget ceiling: client approved increased spend

### Dimension 1: Vertical Scaling (Budget Depth)
- Scale existing winning campaigns with increased budgets
- Track marginal CPA: cost of each additional conversion, not just average
- When marginal CPA exceeds 130% of average, vertical is at ceiling
- Shift to horizontal dimensions before forcing past ceiling

### Dimension 2: Horizontal Scaling (New Audiences)
- Sequence: next LAL% > untested interests > broad > new demographics
- Test one new audience per campaign per week maximum
- Give each 7 days and $500 minimum before evaluation
- Do not retire winners to fund tests — add budget incrementally

### Dimension 3: Channel Diversification
- Add when primary produces consistent results for 30+ days
- Add when single channel represents >70% of total spend
- Priority: Meta > Google (or reverse) > YouTube > TikTok > LinkedIn
- Start new channel at 15-20% of total budget

### Dimension 4: Creative System Scaling
- $10-25K/month: 10-12 concepts, 2-3 formats
- $25-50K/month: 15-20 concepts, 3-4 formats
- $50-100K/month: 20-30 concepts, 4-5 formats
- $100K+/month: 30+ concepts, 5+ formats

### Dimension 5: Geographic / International Expansion
- Start same-language countries (US > UK > AU > CA)
- Separate campaigns per country — never mix geos
- Localize creative — domestic ads do not work in new markets
- Allow 2-4 weeks per new geo for baseline

### Stop-Loss at Scale
- Daily dollar limit: max loss = 2x daily target spend
- If blended CPA exceeds 120% of target for full week, halt and revert
- New channel fails 80% of target within 30 days — pause
- No single campaign > 40% of total spend at scale

## Key Concepts
- Scale is not linear: doubling budget yields 60-80% proportional return
- Creative velocity = scaling velocity
- Diversification reduces risk across platforms, audiences, and creative

## Decision Rules
1. Readiness assessment must pass before entering this layer
2. Vertical first, horizontal when vertical plateaus
3. Never scale without proportional creative pipeline increase
4. Channel diversification mandatory once primary exceeds 70% of spend
5. Geographic expansion requires localized assets

## Integration
- Tactical execution follows scaling playbook
- Creative velocity through Creative Layer pipeline
- Performance monitoring with scaling-adjusted thresholds
- Financial oversight via Governance Layer

## Output
- Scaling readiness assessment document
- Scaling roadmap with prioritized dimensions and timeline
- Channel diversification plan with budget per channel
- Creative velocity plan aligned to spend trajectory
- Weekly scaling report with marginal CPA tracking
