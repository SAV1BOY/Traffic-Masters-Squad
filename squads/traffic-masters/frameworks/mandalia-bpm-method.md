# BPM Method (Brand-Performance-Marketing Trinity)

> **Author**: Depesh Mandalia
> **Domain**: Strategic Advertising Architecture
> **Used by agents**: strategy-orchestrator, media-buyer, brand-strategist
> **Checklists**: campaign-launch-checklist, account-audit-checklist

## Overview

The BPM Method is Depesh Mandalia's foundational framework for building sustainable advertising systems. Most advertisers focus exclusively on Performance (direct response, measurable ROI), which creates a race to the bottom. BPM argues that lasting success requires three interconnected pillars: Brand, Performance, and Marketing — working in concert.

## When to Use

- Setting up a new advertising account or strategy from scratch
- Auditing an underperforming account that has plateaued
- Client onboarding to establish strategic foundation
- When scaling hits a ceiling and performance-only tactics are exhausted
- Transitioning from short-term acquisition mindset to sustainable growth

## The Framework

### Pillar 1: Brand (Long-Term Positioning)
1. Define the emotional connection the brand creates with its audience
2. Establish distinctive brand assets (visual, verbal, tonal)
3. Build long-term positioning that differentiates from competitors
4. Create brand campaigns that build mental availability
5. Measure: brand recall, sentiment, share of voice, organic search volume

### Pillar 2: Performance (Short-Term Acquisition)
1. Set up direct response campaigns with clear, measurable KPIs
2. Optimize for immediate ROI — CPA, ROAS, CPL
3. Test creative, audiences, and offers relentlessly
4. Scale winners, cut losers — the engine of acquisition
5. Measure: ROAS, CPA, conversion rate, revenue attributed

### Pillar 3: Marketing (The Bridge)
1. Develop deep customer understanding — who they are, what they need
2. Craft messaging that resonates at every stage of the journey
3. Define the strategy that ties Brand and Performance together
4. Ensure creative and targeting reflect genuine customer insight
5. Measure: engagement rates, message resonance, funnel throughput

### Integration Loop
- Brand feeds Performance by reducing CPMs and increasing CTR (people click what they recognize)
- Performance feeds Brand by generating data and customer stories
- Marketing feeds both by ensuring message-market fit

## Key Concepts

- **Performance without Brand** is a race to the bottom — rising CPAs, creative fatigue, audience exhaustion
- **Brand without Performance** is vanity — beautiful campaigns with no measurable impact
- **Marketing is the bridge** — it ensures Brand and Performance speak to the same customer truth
- **Budget allocation**: Start 80/20 Performance/Brand, shift toward 60/40 as scale increases
- **Time horizons**: Performance = days/weeks, Marketing = weeks/months, Brand = months/years

## Decision Rules

- IF account is new with no data THEN start with Performance-heavy BPM (80/20) to generate learnings
- IF CPA is rising despite optimization THEN invest in Brand to reduce reliance on direct response alone
- IF brand awareness is high but conversions are low THEN the Marketing pillar is weak — fix messaging and funnel
- IF scaling has plateaued THEN the Brand pillar is likely underdeveloped — cold audiences do not trust you yet
- IF creative fatigue is constant THEN Brand assets are not strong enough to sustain repeated exposure

## Common Mistakes

- Running only Performance campaigns and wondering why CPAs keep rising
- Treating Brand as a luxury that only big companies can afford
- Ignoring the Marketing pillar — great Brand and great Performance but messaging does not connect them
- Expecting Brand campaigns to deliver immediate ROAS
- Not giving Brand campaigns enough time to show impact (minimum 90-day measurement window)
- Over-indexing on one pillar and neglecting the others
