# Discovery Layer
> **Type**: Stack Layer
> **Used by agents**: Mandalia, Kusmich

## Overview
The foundational layer of the traffic stack. Before any campaign is built, creative produced, or budget allocated, the Discovery Layer generates the strategic intelligence that informs every downstream decision. This layer answers: Who is the customer? What is the market? What are competitors doing? What is the offer? What are the risks? Duration: 1-2 weeks.

## When to Use
- New client onboarding (mandatory, no exceptions)
- New product or service launch for existing client
- Quarterly strategic review and recalibration
- When performance degrades without clear cause — return to discovery

## The Framework

### Research Workstreams

#### 1. Market Research
- Market size, growth trajectory, industry benchmarks (CPA, CVR, ROAS, CPM)
- Seasonal patterns and demand cycles
- Platform-specific market dynamics (Meta vs Google dominance in niche)

#### 2. Avatar / ICP Development
- Demographics: age, gender, location, income, education
- Psychographics: values, fears, desires, frustrations, aspirations
- Behavioral: buying patterns, media consumption, device usage
- Language mining: exact words they use to describe problem and solution
- Objection inventory: top 5-10 reasons they do NOT buy
- Sources: customer interviews, reviews, Reddit/forums, support tickets, surveys

#### 3. Competitor Analysis
- Active competitors on paid channels (Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency)
- Creative analysis: formats, angles, hooks, offers being used
- Landing page and funnel mapping: what happens after the click
- Gaps: what competitors are NOT saying or doing

#### 4. Offer Analysis
- Core offer, value proposition, pricing structure
- Risk reversal: guarantees, trials, return policies
- Upsells, cross-sells, and post-purchase revenue opportunities
- Offer-market fit assessment

#### 5. Policy Risk Assessment
- Classify offer category by platform policy (Green/Yellow/Red)
- Identify claims needing softening or removal
- Document required certifications or authorizations

### Input Required
- Business brief: product documentation, pricing, brand guidelines
- Existing customer data (CRM, reviews, testimonials)
- Historical advertising data (if any)
- Business goals and KPI targets

## Key Concepts
- Depth over speed: two weeks of research saves two months of wasted spend
- Language mining produces the highest-converting ad copy
- Competitive intelligence informs positioning; copying guarantees mediocrity
- If the offer is weak, no amount of traffic will fix it

## Decision Rules
1. Discovery is mandatory for every new client — no campaign launches without it
2. Minimum 2 avatar documents per product
3. Competitor analysis must include at least 5 direct competitors
4. Policy risk assessment completed before any creative brief is written
5. If discovery reveals a weak offer, escalate to Traffic Chief before proceeding

## Integration
- Outputs feed directly into Strategy Layer for planning
- Avatar documents inform audience building and seed audiences
- Competitor analysis feeds creative angle matrix gap identification
- Market benchmarks set baselines for pacing and guardrails

## Output
- Market intelligence brief with benchmarks and seasonal calendar
- Avatar/ICP documents (minimum 2) with psychographic depth
- Competitive landscape document with ad library and gap analysis
- Offer assessment with strengths, weaknesses, and recommendations
- Policy risk report with classification and mitigation plan
- Discovery synthesis document for Strategy Layer handoff
