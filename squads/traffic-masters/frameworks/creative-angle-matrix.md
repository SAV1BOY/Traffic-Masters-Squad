# Creative Angle Matrix

> **Type**: Internal
> **Domain**: Creative Strategy
> **Used by agents**: Ad Midas, Creative Analyst, Breeze, Traffic Chief

## Overview

A systematic grid for generating ad concepts by crossing angle types with audience segments. Eliminates creative block and ensures comprehensive coverage of persuasion vectors across all target personas. Every campaign launch requires a minimum populated matrix before creative production begins.

## When to Use

- Campaign launch creative planning
- Creative refresh cycles (monthly or when fatigue detected)
- New audience segment expansion
- When winning angle is identified and needs variation mining
- Quarterly creative strategy reviews

## The Framework

### Angle Types (Rows)

| # | Angle Type | Core Mechanism | Example Trigger |
|---|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Pain** | Agitate the problem they live with daily | "Tired of..." / "Sick of..." |
| 2 | **Desire** | Paint the outcome they crave | "Imagine..." / "What if..." |
| 3 | **Objection** | Preemptively address why they hesitate | "You might think..." / "I know what you're thinking" |
| 4 | **Proof** | Evidence that it works — data, results, case studies | "We tested..." / "After 90 days..." |
| 5 | **Mechanism** | Explain HOW/WHY it works differently | "The secret is..." / "Here's why this works" |
| 6 | **Authority** | Credentials, endorsements, expertise | "Recommended by..." / "As seen in..." |
| 7 | **Social Proof** | Others like them succeeded | "Join 10,000+" / "See what [name] said" |
| 8 | **Curiosity** | Open a loop that demands closure | "Nobody talks about this..." / "The #1 mistake..." |
| 9 | **Controversy** | Challenge a common belief | "Stop doing X" / "Everything you know about X is wrong" |
| 10 | **Story** | Narrative arc — struggle to transformation | "I used to..." / "6 months ago I was..." |

### Audience Segments (Columns)

Define 3-5 audience segments per client. Standard archetypes include:

- **Segment A**: Problem-aware, never tried a solution
- **Segment B**: Solution-aware, comparing options
- **Segment C**: Product-aware, needs final push
- **Segment D**: Past customers, upsell/cross-sell
- **Segment E**: Skeptics, high objection load

### Matrix Population

Each cell contains a concept brief:
- **Hook** (first line/5 seconds)
- **Angle execution** (how the angle manifests)
- **CTA alignment** (what action this drives)
- **Format suggestion** (video, static, carousel, UGC)

Minimum: 3 concepts per angle-segment intersection for primary segments (A, B, C). Secondary segments (D, E) require 1-2 concepts minimum.

## Key Concepts

- **Angle Stacking**: Combine two angles in one ad (e.g., Pain + Proof, Curiosity + Story). Stacked angles outperform single-angle ads in most tests.
- **Angle Fatigue**: Each angle has a performance shelf life. Track when CTR drops 30%+ from peak — time to rotate.
- **Segment-Angle Fit**: Not all angles work for all segments. Pain resonates with problem-aware. Proof resonates with solution-aware. Story works broadly.
- **Temperature Mapping**: Cold traffic responds to Curiosity, Pain, Controversy. Warm traffic responds to Proof, Social Proof, Mechanism. Hot traffic responds to Objection, Authority, Desire.

## Decision Rules

1. Never launch a campaign with fewer than 3 distinct angles across at least 2 segments.
2. Prioritize angle-segment combinations by estimated audience size times likelihood of resonance.
3. If an angle wins in one segment, test it across other segments before declaring it universal.
4. Controversy and Curiosity angles require policy review before production — see `policy-risk-classification.md`.
5. Story angles require UGC or founder-led content — do not attempt in static format.
6. Refresh the matrix every 30 days. Archive retired concepts with performance data attached.

## Common Mistakes

- Fixating on one angle type that worked once and ignoring the rest of the matrix.
- Creating angles in isolation without mapping to specific audience segments.
- Skipping Objection angles — these are critical for mid-funnel conversion.
- Using Controversy for the sake of provocation without strategic intent.
- Not testing Mechanism angles — they differentiate commoditized offers.
- Populating the matrix but never revisiting it with performance data.
