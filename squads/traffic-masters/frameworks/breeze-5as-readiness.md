# 5 As Readiness Framework
> **Author**: Tom Breeze / YouTube Ads Readiness
> **Domain**: YouTube Ads, Campaign Readiness, Client Onboarding
> **Used by agents**: strategist-agent, onboarding-agent, audit-agent

## Overview
A diagnostic framework to assess whether a business is ready to run profitable YouTube
ads. Five dimensions are scored 1-5 each for a maximum of 25. Below 15 means the
business is not ready and should address gaps first. Prevents wasted ad spend on
premature campaigns.

## When to Use
- Before launching any YouTube ad campaign
- During client onboarding to set expectations and identify gaps
- Quarterly reviews to reassess readiness as the business evolves
- When YouTube campaigns are underperforming — diagnose root causes

## The Framework
### 1. Audience (Score 1-5)
- 1: No clear target audience defined
- 2: Basic demographics only
- 3: Audience defined with psychographics and pain points
- 4: Detailed avatar with validated data from customer research
- 5: Deep audience understanding with behavioral data and segmentation

### 2. Ads (Score 1-5)
- 1: No video assets or experience with video ads
- 2: Basic video content exists but not optimized for ads
- 3: Ad scripts following proven frameworks (ADUCATE, 3 Acts)
- 4: Multiple ad variations ready for testing with strong hooks
- 5: Proven ad creative with performance data and iteration history

### 3. Acquisition (Score 1-5)
- 1: No funnel or landing page exists
- 2: Basic landing page with unclear conversion path
- 3: Tested funnel with known conversion rates
- 4: Optimized funnel with multiple stages and retargeting
- 5: High-converting funnel with predictable unit economics

### 4. Accounts (Score 1-5)
- 1: No Google Ads account or tracking setup
- 2: Account exists but tracking is incomplete or broken
- 3: Full tracking stack (pixel, GTM, GA4, conversions)
- 4: Verified tracking with conversion data flowing correctly
- 5: Mature account with historical data and optimized structure

### 5. Attitude (Score 1-5)
- 1: Expects instant results, no tolerance for testing
- 2: Willing to test but impatient with learning phase
- 3: Understands testing is required, committed to 90-day horizon
- 4: Data-driven mindset, treats ads as investment not expense
- 5: Long-term growth focus, embraces iteration and learning

## Key Concepts
- Total score out of 25 determines readiness tier
- Score < 15: NOT READY — address gaps before spending
- Score 15-20: CONDITIONALLY READY — launch with caveats and gap plan
- Score 21-25: READY — launch with confidence
- Attitude is often the most underestimated dimension
- A single score of 1 in any dimension is a blocker regardless of total

## Decision Rules
- IF total < 15 → do not launch; create a readiness improvement plan
- IF Audience < 3 → prioritize avatar research before any ad creation
- IF Ads < 3 → script and produce video assets before launch
- IF Acquisition < 3 → build and test the funnel before driving traffic
- IF Accounts < 3 → fix tracking infrastructure first
- IF Attitude < 3 → set expectations and agree on testing protocol

## Common Mistakes
- Launching at score 12 because the client is eager to spend
- Ignoring Attitude and then facing pressure to cut campaigns early
- Perfect Ads score but weak Acquisition — great ads to a broken funnel
- Treating readiness as binary when it is a spectrum
- Not re-scoring after addressing gaps to confirm improvement
