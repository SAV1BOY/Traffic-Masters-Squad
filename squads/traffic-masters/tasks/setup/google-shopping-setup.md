# Google Shopping Setup

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: setup
> **Agents**: Aslam, Media Buyer
> **Frameworks**: Shopping Campaign Architecture, Merchant Center Optimization
> **Checklists**: google-shopping-setup-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/google-shopping-build-sheet.md

## Objective
Set up Google Merchant Center, configure product feeds, and build Shopping campaigns with proper product group segmentation, bidding strategy, and feed optimization for e-commerce product advertising.

## Inputs
- Product catalog with titles, descriptions, images, prices, and GTINs
- Google Merchant Center account access
- Google Ads account linked to Merchant Center
- Conversion tracking setup with revenue values
- Budget allocation for Shopping campaigns
- Shipping and tax configuration details

## Steps
1. Set up or audit Google Merchant Center account settings and verification
2. Configure product feed: source type (API, file, content API, Shopify connector)
3. Optimize product titles with search-relevant keywords and attributes
4. Optimize product descriptions with benefit-driven copy and key specifications
5. Verify product images meet quality requirements and showcase the product clearly
6. Set up shipping settings and tax configurations correctly
7. Submit feed and resolve any disapprovals or warnings
8. Create Standard Shopping campaign with product group segmentation by category, brand, or margin
9. Set bidding strategy: manual CPC to start, then transition to target ROAS
10. Apply negative keywords to filter irrelevant search queries
11. Create campaign priority structure if running multiple Shopping campaigns
12. Set up supplemental feeds for custom labels (margin tiers, best sellers, seasonal)

## Output
Live Google Shopping campaigns with: configured Merchant Center, optimized product feed, campaign structure documentation, product group segmentation, bidding settings, negative keywords, and custom label strategy.

## Quality Gate
- Google shopping setup checklist confirms Merchant Center and feed are healthy
- All products approved with no critical disapprovals
- Aslam validates product group segmentation and bidding approach

## Duration
4-8 hours for initial setup; 2-3 hours for feed optimization and QA
