# Performance Max (PMax) Build

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: setup
> **Agents**: Aslam, Media Buyer
> **Frameworks**: PMax Guardrails Framework, Asset Group Strategy
> **Checklists**: pmax-build-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/pmax-build-sheet.md

## Objective
Build Performance Max campaigns with proper guardrails, asset group strategy, audience signals, and feed optimization to leverage Google's full inventory while maintaining control over performance and brand safety.

## Inputs
- Acquisition plan with PMax strategy and objectives
- Creative assets: images, videos, logos, headlines, descriptions
- Product feed from Merchant Center (for e-commerce)
- Audience signals: custom segments, remarketing lists, demographics
- Budget allocation for PMax campaigns
- Conversion tracking with value assignment

## Steps
1. Define PMax campaign objective: sales, leads, or store visits with proper conversion goals
2. Create asset groups organized by theme, product category, or audience segment
3. Upload creative assets per group: minimum 20 images, 5 videos, 5 logos per asset group
4. Write headlines (up to 15) and descriptions (up to 5) per asset group
5. Configure audience signals: custom intent segments, remarketing lists, customer match, demographics
6. Set up URL expansion settings: enable or restrict based on landing page quality
7. Apply brand exclusions and negative keywords (where available) to protect brand searches
8. Configure final URL options and URL tracking templates
9. Set bidding strategy: maximize conversions or maximize conversion value with targets
10. Apply budget at campaign level with daily cap monitoring
11. Set up search themes to guide PMax toward relevant queries
12. Document known PMax limitations and monitoring workarounds

## Output
Live PMax campaigns with: asset group documentation, audience signal configuration, URL expansion settings, brand protection measures, bidding strategy, and monitoring plan for opaque reporting.

## Quality Gate
- PMax build checklist confirms all guardrails applied
- Asset strength rating of "Good" or "Excellent" for all asset groups
- Aslam validates audience signals and brand protection measures

## Duration
3-5 hours for build; 1-2 hours for asset preparation and QA
