# Frequency Cap Optimization

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: optimization
> **Agents**: Media Buyer, Performance Analyst
> **Frameworks**: Frequency Management Framework, Fatigue Detection Model
> **Checklists**: frequency-optimization-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/frequency-cap-adjustments.md

## Objective
Optimize frequency caps across all campaigns to reduce ad fatigue, maintain positive brand perception, and maximize the efficiency of each impression by finding the optimal exposure level per audience segment.

## Inputs
- Campaign performance data with frequency metrics
- Platform frequency reports: Meta delivery insights, Google reach/frequency
- Creative performance trends correlated with frequency levels
- Audience size data for each campaign and ad set
- Industry benchmarks for optimal frequency by funnel stage
- Negative feedback metrics: hide rates, negative comments, ad relevance scores

## Steps
1. Export frequency distribution data for all active campaigns
2. Analyze the relationship between frequency and key metrics: CTR, CPA, conversion rate
3. Identify the frequency sweet spot: the point where incremental impressions stop adding value
4. Compare frequency across funnel stages: prospecting should have lower caps than retargeting
5. Review negative feedback signals correlated with high-frequency ad sets
6. Check creative-level frequency: are specific creatives fatiguing faster than others
7. Analyze audience size versus budget to identify campaigns with forced high frequency
8. Set frequency caps per campaign type: prospecting (2-3/week), retargeting (4-7/week)
9. Configure platform-specific frequency controls: Meta ad set caps, Google campaign caps
10. Plan creative rotation to support frequency management: more creatives = lower per-creative frequency
11. Set up frequency monitoring alerts for campaigns approaching fatigue thresholds
12. Document cap settings with rationale and planned review dates

## Output
Frequency cap adjustments containing: frequency analysis per campaign, optimal frequency findings, updated cap settings, creative rotation recommendations, monitoring alert configuration, and review schedule.

## Quality Gate
- Frequency optimization checklist confirms all campaigns analyzed
- Cap settings justified by data showing the frequency-performance relationship
- Performance Analyst validates methodology and projected efficiency improvements

## Duration
1-3 hours for analysis and cap setting; 30 minutes for monitoring setup
