# Launch Traffic Strategy

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: strategy
> **Agents**: Sobral, Traffic Chief, Media Buyer
> **Frameworks**: Sobral 8 Stages of Launch Traffic
> **Checklists**: launch-strategy-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/launch-traffic-plan.md

## Objective
Design the launch traffic strategy using Sobral's 8-stage framework to systematically warm audiences, build anticipation, and maximize conversions during the launch window with precise budget pacing and creative sequencing.

## Inputs
- Launch date and duration
- Offer details and launch mechanism (webinar, VSL, cart open/close, etc.)
- Available audience assets: email list, pixel audiences, social followers
- Total launch budget
- Creative assets available or in production
- Funnel and landing pages for the launch

## Steps
1. Define the launch timeline and map Sobral's 8 stages to specific date ranges
2. Stage 1 - Content: Plan value-first content ads to warm cold audiences
3. Stage 2 - Anticipation: Design anticipation-building ads with countdown elements
4. Stage 3 - Pre-launch: Create pre-launch registration or waitlist campaigns
5. Stage 4 - Launch: Build launch day campaigns with urgency and social proof
6. Stage 5 - Objection handling: Plan objection-specific ads for mid-launch
7. Stage 6 - Social proof: Deploy testimonial and case study ads
8. Stage 7 - Scarcity: Create deadline and scarcity campaigns for close
9. Stage 8 - Last call: Design final push campaigns with maximum urgency
10. Set budget allocation per stage with daily pacing guidelines
11. Define audience targeting per stage: cold, warm, hot, buyer segments
12. Map creative assets to each stage and identify production gaps

## Output
Launch traffic plan containing: 8-stage timeline with dates, campaign structure per stage, budget allocation and pacing, audience targeting per stage, creative asset mapping, email/ad coordination plan, and contingency scenarios.

## Quality Gate
- Launch strategy checklist confirms all 8 stages defined with campaigns
- Budget pacing allows sufficient spend in each stage for statistical validity
- Traffic Chief validates overall launch coordination with business team

## Duration
4-6 hours for strategy development; 2 hours for documentation and coordination
