# Budget Allocation

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: strategy
> **Agents**: Traffic Chief, Performance Analyst
> **Frameworks**: Budget Allocation Model, Traffic Engine
> **Checklists**: budget-allocation-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/budget-allocation.md

## Objective
Allocate the total media budget across channels, campaign types, and funnel stages using data-driven allocation models that balance proven performance with testing and innovation.

## Inputs
- Total available monthly media budget
- Acquisition plan with channel strategy
- Historical performance data by channel (if available)
- Platform minimum spend requirements
- Business seasonality and cash flow constraints
- KPI targets: CPA, ROAS, volume goals

## Steps
1. Define budget allocation tiers: proven channels (70%), testing (20%), experimental (10%)
2. Calculate minimum viable budget per channel based on platform requirements and statistical significance
3. Allocate within each channel by campaign type: prospecting, retargeting, retention
4. Set daily and weekly spend caps per campaign to control pacing
5. Define the testing budget carve-out for new creatives, audiences, and offers
6. Model expected results per channel at allocated spend levels
7. Build a budget flexibility framework: rules for shifting budget between channels
8. Plan for seasonal adjustments: when to increase or decrease allocation
9. Set break-even thresholds that trigger budget reallocation decisions
10. Create a budget tracking spreadsheet with actuals vs planned columns
11. Define the budget review cadence and reallocation decision process

## Output
Budget allocation document containing: channel-level allocation table, campaign-type breakdown, daily/weekly caps, testing budget plan, expected results model, reallocation rules, seasonal adjustment plan, and tracking template.

## Quality Gate
- Budget allocation checklist confirms all channels have justified allocations
- Expected results model validated against industry benchmarks
- Traffic Chief approves allocation aligns with strategic priorities

## Duration
2-3 hours for modeling and allocation; 1 hour for documentation
