# Platform Feasibility Assessment

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: research
> **Agents**: Traffic Chief, Ads Analyst
> **Frameworks**: Platform Selection Matrix, Traffic Engine
> **Checklists**: platform-feasibility-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/platform-recommendation.md

## Objective
Assess which advertising platforms are viable and optimal for the business based on audience presence, creative requirements, budget thresholds, policy constraints, and expected performance benchmarks.

## Inputs
- ICP and avatar document with demographic and behavioral data
- Business model details: product type, price point, funnel type
- Available budget range for paid media
- Creative production capabilities
- Industry vertical and any known policy restrictions

## Steps
1. Map avatar demographics and behaviors to platform user bases (Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Snapchat, X)
2. Evaluate each platform's targeting capabilities against ICP requirements
3. Assess minimum viable budget for each platform based on auction dynamics
4. Review creative format requirements versus production capabilities
5. Check platform policies for the specific vertical and offer type
6. Research industry benchmarks for CPM, CPC, CPA on each platform
7. Evaluate attribution and measurement capabilities per platform
8. Score each platform on: audience fit, targeting precision, cost efficiency, creative fit, policy risk, measurement quality
9. Rank platforms by composite score and classify as primary, secondary, or not recommended
10. Document rationale for each recommendation with supporting data

## Output
Platform recommendation report containing: platform scoring matrix, ranked recommendations with rationale, minimum budget requirements per platform, creative format requirements, policy risk notes, and suggested launch sequence.

## Quality Gate
- Platform feasibility checklist confirms all platforms evaluated on all criteria
- Scoring methodology documented and consistently applied
- Traffic Chief approves final platform selection and launch sequence

## Duration
2-3 hours for assessment; 1 hour for documentation and review
