# Hook and Angle Research

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: research
> **Agents**: Ad Midas, Creative Analyst
> **Frameworks**: Hook Classification System, Angle Mining Framework
> **Checklists**: hook-angle-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/hook-angle-bank.md

## Objective
Mine and catalog high-performing hooks and angles from competitor ads, customer reviews, forums, and social conversations to build a comprehensive hook/angle bank that fuels creative production.

## Inputs
- Completed swipe file from competitor ads mining
- ICP and avatar document with real customer language
- Access to review sites, Reddit, Quora, Facebook groups
- Product or service USPs and differentiators
- Existing ad performance data (if available)

## Steps
1. Extract hooks from top-performing competitor ads identified in swipe mining
2. Classify each hook by type: question, statistic, story, controversy, demonstration, social proof
3. Mine customer reviews for language patterns that suggest natural hooks
4. Scan Reddit threads, Quora answers, and Facebook groups for recurring questions and frustrations
5. Identify angles by mapping distinct motivations, fears, and desires from avatar research
6. Cross-reference angles with hooks to create hook-angle combinations
7. Rate each combination on novelty, emotional impact, and platform fit
8. Organize the bank by angle category and hook type for easy retrieval
9. Flag top 10 hook-angle combinations as priority for first creative sprint
10. Document the source and context for each hook to maintain authenticity

## Output
Hook/angle bank containing: categorized hooks with type labels, angle definitions with emotional drivers, hook-angle combination matrix, priority rankings, source documentation, and platform suitability notes per combination.

## Quality Gate
- Hook angle checklist confirms minimum 30 hooks and 10 distinct angles documented
- Each hook classified by type with source attribution
- Ad Midas validates top 10 combinations for creative viability

## Duration
3-4 hours for mining; 1-2 hours for classification and prioritization
