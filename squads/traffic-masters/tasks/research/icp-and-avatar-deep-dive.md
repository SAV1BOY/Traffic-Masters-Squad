# ICP and Avatar Deep Dive

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: research
> **Agents**: Mandalia, Kusmich, Traffic Chief
> **Frameworks**: Mandalia 5W Avatar, Kusmich Trifecta
> **Checklists**: research-checklist, avatar-validation-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/icp-avatar-document.md

## Objective
Build a comprehensive Ideal Customer Profile and detailed avatar using the Mandalia 5W framework and Kusmich Trifecta methodology, capturing real language, emotional triggers, and behavioral patterns that drive ad targeting and messaging decisions.

## Inputs
- Business description and product/service details
- Existing customer data (if available)
- Access to reviews, forums, and social media conversations
- Competitor customer analysis
- Sales call transcripts or support tickets

## Steps
1. Define the 5W avatar dimensions: Who they are, What they want, Where they congregate, When they buy, Why they act now
2. Apply Kusmich Trifecta to identify the three core emotional drivers: pain, desire, and fear
3. Mine real customer language from reviews, Reddit, Quora, Facebook groups, and forums
4. Document exact phrases, complaints, and aspirations in the customer's own words
5. Map demographic and psychographic attributes with specificity
6. Identify the awareness level of the avatar (Schwartz scale)
7. Validate avatar hypotheses against actual customer data or interviews
8. Create targeting implications for each platform based on avatar attributes
9. Compile the ICP document with all findings and recommended targeting layers

## Output
ICP document containing: demographic profile, psychographic profile, real language swipe, emotional triggers map, awareness level assessment, platform-specific targeting recommendations, and avatar summary card for creative briefing.

## Quality Gate
- Research checklist must pass with all 5W dimensions fully documented
- Avatar validation checklist confirms real language sourced from at least three channels
- Traffic Chief reviews and approves before use in campaign strategy

## Duration
3-5 hours for initial research; 1-2 hours for validation and document assembly
