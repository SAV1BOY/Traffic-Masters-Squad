# Competitor Ads Swipe Mining

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: research
> **Agents**: Creative Analyst, Ads Analyst
> **Frameworks**: Creative Analysis Framework, Competitive Intelligence
> **Checklists**: swipe-file-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/swipe-file.md

## Objective
Systematically mine competitor advertisements from Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, and Google Ads Transparency Center to build an annotated swipe file that informs creative strategy and identifies market gaps.

## Inputs
- List of direct and indirect competitors
- Access to Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, Google Ads Transparency Center
- Industry vertical and product category
- Current market positioning of each competitor

## Steps
1. Identify top 10-15 competitors and adjacent brands to research
2. Search Meta Ad Library for all active ads from each competitor, filter by region and date
3. Search TikTok Creative Center for top-performing ads in the category
4. Search Google Ads Transparency Center for competitor search and display ads
5. Capture screenshots, video links, and ad copy for each notable ad
6. Annotate each ad with: hook type, angle, offer, CTA, format, estimated run time
7. Identify patterns in hooks, angles, and formats that appear across multiple competitors
8. Flag ads with long run times as likely winners worth deeper analysis
9. Identify creative gaps where no competitor is addressing a specific angle or audience
10. Organize swipe file by category: hooks, angles, formats, offers, CTAs
11. Write summary of key findings and recommended creative directions

## Output
Annotated swipe file containing: competitor ad screenshots and links, annotations per ad (hook, angle, offer, CTA, format, run time), pattern analysis, creative gap identification, and recommended directions for original creative.

## Quality Gate
- Swipe file checklist confirms minimum 50 ads captured across platforms
- Each ad annotated with all required fields
- Creative Analyst validates pattern analysis and gap identification

## Duration
4-6 hours for mining and annotation; 1-2 hours for analysis and summary
