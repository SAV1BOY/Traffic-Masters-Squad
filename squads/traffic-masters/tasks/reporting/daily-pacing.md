# Daily Pacing Check

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: reporting
> **Agents**: Media Buyer, Performance Analyst
> **Frameworks**: Pacing Management Framework, Budget Delivery Model
> **Checklists**: daily-pacing-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/pacing-report.md

## Objective
Perform daily pacing checks to verify campaigns are delivering on budget targets, identify delivery issues early, and provide a quick performance snapshot that enables timely intervention.

## Inputs
- Daily budget targets by channel and campaign
- Monthly budget plan with daily pacing schedule
- Access to all ad platform dashboards
- Previous day's pacing report for trend comparison
- Seasonal or promotional adjustments to pacing targets

## Steps
1. Pull spend data from all active platforms for yesterday and month-to-date
2. Calculate daily delivery rate: actual spend versus daily target per channel
3. Calculate month-to-date pacing: actual cumulative spend versus planned cumulative spend
4. Flag underpacing channels: spending less than 90% of daily target
5. Flag overpacing channels: spending more than 110% of daily target
6. Identify delivery issues: ad rejections, budget caps hit, learning phase stalls
7. Check conversion volume pacing: are conversions on track for weekly and monthly targets
8. Calculate projected month-end spend at current daily run rate
9. Note any planned budget changes or promotional periods affecting pacing
10. Document recommended actions for off-pace campaigns
11. Update the pacing tracker spreadsheet with actuals
12. Distribute pacing summary to relevant stakeholders

## Output
Pacing report containing: daily spend by channel, MTD pacing percentage, delivery issue flags, conversion volume pacing, projected month-end totals, recommended actions, and stakeholder summary.

## Quality Gate
- Daily pacing checklist confirms all channels checked
- All off-pace campaigns have documented reasons and recommended actions
- Pacing tracker updated before the daily optimization review

## Duration
15-30 minutes for standard daily check
