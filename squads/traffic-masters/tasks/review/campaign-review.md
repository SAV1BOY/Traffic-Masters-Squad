# Campaign Review

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: review
> **Agents**: Traffic Chief
> **Frameworks**: Campaign Review Protocol, Performance Assessment
> **Checklists**: campaign-review-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/review-notes.md

## Objective
Conduct a structured campaign review led by the Traffic Chief to evaluate campaign performance, validate strategic alignment, assess execution quality, and make decisions on continuation, optimization, or termination of campaigns.

## Inputs
- Campaign performance data for the review period
- Original campaign strategy and objectives
- Creative performance breakdowns
- Audience performance breakdowns
- Budget pacing and delivery data
- Notes from Media Buyer's daily optimization logs

## Steps
1. Review campaign-level metrics against original KPI targets
2. Assess strategic alignment: is the campaign still serving its intended role in the acquisition plan
3. Evaluate ad set performance: identify top and bottom performers with clear separation
4. Review creative performance: which creatives are driving results vs dragging down averages
5. Check audience health: frequency, reach, saturation, and overlap metrics
6. Validate bid strategy effectiveness: is the current approach optimal or should it change
7. Review budget delivery: is the campaign spending efficiently within its allocation
8. Assess tracking integrity: are reported numbers trustworthy for this campaign
9. Make decisions: scale, optimize, pause, or kill for each campaign and its components
10. Document specific optimization actions with owners and deadlines
11. Identify learnings that should inform other campaigns or strategy updates
12. Set the next review date and define what data is needed

## Output
Review notes containing: performance assessment per campaign, strategic alignment evaluation, decision log (scale/optimize/pause/kill), specific action items with owners, learnings extracted, and next review schedule.

## Quality Gate
- Campaign review checklist confirms all active campaigns reviewed
- Every campaign has a clear decision with documented rationale
- Action items have assigned owners and deadlines

## Duration
1-2 hours depending on number of active campaigns
