# Compliance Review

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: review
> **Agents**: Fiscal, Ads Analyst
> **Frameworks**: Advertising Compliance Framework, Regulatory Compliance Checklist
> **Checklists**: compliance-review-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/compliance-report.md

## Objective
Review all active advertising for compliance with platform policies, advertising regulations, privacy laws, and industry-specific requirements to prevent ad rejections, account suspensions, legal issues, and reputational damage.

## Inputs
- All active ad creatives and copy across platforms
- Landing page URLs currently receiving traffic
- Platform advertising policies (current versions)
- Applicable regulations: FTC, GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, industry-specific rules
- Previous compliance review findings and remediation status
- Any recent policy updates from advertising platforms

## Steps
1. Review all active ad copy for prohibited claims: unsubstantiated health, income, or results claims
2. Check creative assets for policy violations: before/after images, personal attributes, sensationalism
3. Verify all required disclosures are present: sponsored content, affiliate relationships, material connections
4. Audit landing pages for: privacy policy, terms of service, refund policy, contact information
5. Check data collection practices: consent forms, opt-in mechanisms, data handling disclosures
6. Verify email and SMS marketing compliance: CAN-SPAM, TCPA, opt-out mechanisms
7. Review targeting for special ad category compliance: housing, credit, employment restrictions
8. Check for trademark usage issues in ad copy and keywords
9. Verify tax compliance on digital advertising spend (Brazilian ISS, CIDE, etc.)
10. Review influencer and UGC content for proper disclosure requirements
11. Document all compliance issues with severity levels and remediation requirements
12. Update the compliance log with current review findings and status

## Output
Compliance report containing: issue inventory by category and severity, specific violations with evidence, remediation requirements with deadlines, platform policy update summary, regulatory compliance status, and updated compliance log.

## Quality Gate
- Compliance review checklist confirms all active ads and pages reviewed
- All high-severity issues have immediate remediation plans
- Fiscal confirms tax and financial compliance elements are current

## Duration
2-4 hours for comprehensive review; recurring monthly or upon policy changes
