# Tax and Compliance Log

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: finance
> **Agents**: Fiscal
> **Frameworks**: Brazilian Tax Compliance Framework, Digital Advertising Tax Guide
> **Checklists**: tax-compliance-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/compliance-log.md

## Objective
Maintain a comprehensive tax and compliance log for all advertising expenditures, ensuring proper documentation of tax obligations, withholding requirements, and regulatory compliance for Brazilian and international digital advertising operations.

## Inputs
- Monthly advertising invoices from all platforms
- Platform billing details with tax breakdowns
- Brazilian tax calendar and filing deadlines
- Current tax rates: ISS, CIDE, IOF, IRRF, PIS/COFINS
- International service payment documentation
- Previous period compliance log for continuity

## Steps
1. Log all advertising expenditures by platform, amount, date, and currency
2. Document ISS (Imposto Sobre Servicos) obligations on digital advertising services
3. Calculate and record CIDE (Contribuicao de Intervencao no Dominio Economico) on foreign platform payments
4. Track IOF (Imposto sobre Operacoes Financeiras) charges on international credit card transactions
5. Document IRRF (Imposto de Renda Retido na Fonte) withholding on payments to foreign platforms
6. Calculate PIS/COFINS obligations on imported digital services
7. Verify tax treaty applicability for payments to platforms headquartered in treaty countries
8. Maintain documentation for potential input tax credit recovery
9. Record all tax payments made with proof of payment and filing references
10. Track compliance deadlines and set alerts for upcoming filing dates
11. Document any regulatory changes affecting digital advertising taxation
12. Prepare period-end summary with total tax burden analysis

## Output
Compliance log containing: expenditure register, tax obligation calculations per type, payment proof records, filing deadline tracker, regulatory change notes, tax credit documentation, and period-end tax burden summary.

## Quality Gate
- Tax compliance checklist confirms all tax types documented and calculated
- All filing deadlines tracked with no missed submissions
- Fiscal verifies calculations against current tax rates and regulations

## Duration
2-4 hours monthly for log maintenance and calculations; additional time during filing periods
