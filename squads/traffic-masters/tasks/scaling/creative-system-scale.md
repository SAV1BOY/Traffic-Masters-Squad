# Creative System Scale

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: scaling
> **Agents**: Ad Midas, Creative Analyst, Burns
> **Frameworks**: Kaizen Kreative at Scale, Creative Operations Model
> **Checklists**: creative-system-scale-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/production-pipeline.md

## Objective
Scale the creative production system to support increased ad spend by expanding production capacity, systemizing workflows, implementing modular creative approaches, and building a sustainable pipeline that prevents creative bottlenecks.

## Inputs
- Current creative production capacity: volume per week, formats, turnaround time
- Projected creative demand based on scaling plans and refresh cadence
- Creative analysis data: which formats and angles have highest ROI
- Available production resources: internal team, freelancers, agencies, AI tools
- Budget for creative production scaling
- Brand guidelines and quality standards

## Steps
1. Audit current production capacity against projected demand at target spend levels
2. Identify the capacity gap: how many additional creatives per week are needed
3. Design modular creative templates: swap hooks, visuals, CTAs independently
4. Build a component library: reusable headlines, images, video clips, CTAs, overlays
5. Systemize the production workflow: brief template, approval flow, asset management
6. Evaluate production scaling options: hire, outsource, use AI tools, or hybrid
7. Set up a creator network for UGC at scale: vetting, briefing, quality control
8. Implement creative versioning: systematic variant creation from winning templates
9. Build automated reporting on creative pipeline health: backlog, in-progress, ready, live
10. Define quality control gates at each production stage to maintain standards
11. Calculate unit economics of creative production: cost per asset, cost per winner
12. Document the scaled production pipeline with roles, tools, and timelines

## Output
Production pipeline document containing: capacity analysis, modular template system, component library structure, workflow design, scaling approach, creator network plan, pipeline health metrics, quality gates, and unit economics.

## Quality Gate
- Creative system scale checklist confirms capacity meets projected demand
- Burns validates creative production cost aligns with overall unit economics
- Ad Midas approves quality standards are maintained at increased volume

## Duration
4-6 hours for system design; ongoing implementation over 2-4 weeks
