# Onboard New Account

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: operations
> **Agents**: Traffic Chief, Ads Analyst, Pixel Specialist
> **Frameworks**: Account Onboarding Framework, Discovery Process
> **Checklists**: onboarding-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/onboarding-document.md

## ROUTING

> **Agents**: traffic-chief, ads-analyst
> **Escalation**: Traffic Chief if quality gate fails 2x → see `config.yaml > rework_loop`
> **Rework**: Max 3 iterations per gate → team lead → Traffic Chief


## Objective
Onboard a new client or account into the Traffic Masters Squad by conducting discovery, auditing existing assets, setting up access and tracking, and building the initial campaign plan through a structured onboarding process.

## Inputs
- Client or account intake form with business details
- Access credentials for ad platforms, analytics, and website
- Existing campaign data and historical performance (if available)
- Business goals, budget, and timeline expectations
- Brand assets, guidelines, and existing creative library
- Current tracking setup documentation

## Steps
1. Conduct discovery session: business model, products, customers, goals, constraints
2. Collect all access credentials and verify permissions for ad platforms, analytics, GTM
3. Audit existing ad accounts: campaign structure, performance history, current settings
4. Audit existing tracking: pixel health, event accuracy, analytics configuration
5. Audit existing creative assets: inventory, performance data, brand guidelines
6. Run the ICP and avatar deep dive research task for the new account
7. Conduct platform feasibility assessment based on business type and audience
8. Perform a policy risk scan for the specific vertical and offer
9. Build the initial measurement plan and tracking setup
10. Develop the acquisition plan with channel strategy and budget allocation
11. Create the initial campaign build plan with timeline and milestones
12. Present the onboarding findings and campaign plan to the client or stakeholder

## Output
Onboarding document containing: discovery summary, access inventory, audit findings, ICP research results, platform recommendations, tracking setup plan, acquisition strategy, campaign build timeline, and stakeholder presentation.

## Quality Gate
- Onboarding checklist confirms all discovery and audit steps completed
- All access credentials verified and documented securely
- Traffic Chief, Ads Analyst, and Pixel Specialist sign off on readiness to proceed

## Duration
8-16 hours spread across 3-5 days for complete onboarding process
