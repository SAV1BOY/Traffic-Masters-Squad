# Cross-Squad Sync

> **Type**: Task
> **Category**: operations
> **Agents**: Traffic Chief
> **Frameworks**: Cross-Functional Coordination Framework
> **Checklists**: cross-squad-sync-checklist
> **Output template**: templates/sync-notes.md

## Objective
Coordinate with the Copy Squad, Brand Squad, and other relevant teams to ensure alignment on messaging, creative direction, offers, landing pages, and upcoming campaigns that affect traffic performance.

## Inputs
- Current traffic campaign priorities and upcoming launches
- Copy Squad deliverables: ad copy, email sequences, landing page copy
- Brand Squad deliverables: brand guidelines, visual assets, campaign themes
- Shared campaign calendar with cross-squad deadlines
- Performance data relevant to other squads: copy performance, brand lift
- Open requests or dependencies between squads

## Steps
1. Prepare sync agenda: items requiring alignment, updates to share, requests to make
2. Share traffic performance highlights relevant to other squads
3. Review Copy Squad deliverables: ad copy alignment with current angles and hooks
4. Review Brand Squad deliverables: visual assets and brand guideline updates
5. Align on upcoming campaign launches: timing, assets needed, dependencies
6. Discuss landing page changes: copy updates, design changes, new pages needed
7. Share creative learnings: which messages, angles, and styles perform in ads
8. Request assets or deliverables needed from other squads with deadlines
9. Resolve any conflicts: messaging inconsistencies, brand guideline interpretation, priorities
10. Update the shared campaign calendar with agreed timelines
11. Document action items with owners and deadlines across squads
12. Set the next sync date and preliminary agenda items

## Output
Sync notes containing: performance updates shared, alignment decisions made, cross-squad action items with owners, updated campaign calendar, dependency tracker, and next sync schedule.

## Quality Gate
- Cross-squad sync checklist confirms all agenda items addressed
- All cross-squad dependencies have clear owners and deadlines
- Traffic Chief confirms no misalignment between traffic messaging and brand/copy direction

## Duration
30-60 minutes for sync meeting; 30 minutes for notes and action item documentation
