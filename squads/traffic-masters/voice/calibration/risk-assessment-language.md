# Risk Assessment Language Calibration Guide

## Purpose
Standard framework for communicating risk levels associated with paid traffic decisions, campaign changes, and strategic recommendations.

---

## Risk Scale

| Level | Label | Definition | Language Marker |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Minimal | Negligible chance of negative impact | "This carries minimal risk." |
| **2** | Low | Small chance of minor impact, easily reversible | "This is a low-risk change." |
| **3** | Moderate | Reasonable chance of noticeable impact, manageable | "There is moderate risk here." |
| **4** | High | Significant chance of meaningful negative impact | "This carries significant risk." |
| **5** | Critical | High chance of severe impact, difficult to reverse | "This is a high-risk decision." |

---

## Risk Categories in Paid Traffic

### Performance Risk
- Impact on CPA, ROAS, or conversion volume
- "There is a [LEVEL] risk that this change will increase CPA by [X-Y]%."

### Budget Risk
- Potential for overspending, underspending, or misallocation
- "If this approach does not work, the budget exposure is $[AMOUNT] over [PERIOD]."

### Tracking Risk
- Potential for data loss or measurement inaccuracy
- "Changing the tracking setup carries risk of a [PERIOD] gap in conversion data."

### Platform Risk
- Risk of ad disapproval, account restriction, or policy violation
- "This copy approach is within policy but on the edge -- there is a moderate risk of disapproval."

### Competitive Risk
- Risk from competitor actions or market dynamics
- "If competitors increase spend during this period, our costs may rise [X-Y]%."

### Brand Risk
- Risk to brand perception or reputation
- "This creative angle is aggressive and could generate negative sentiment if not executed carefully."

## Risk Communication Templates

### Low-Risk Changes
```
"This is a low-risk optimization. The change is small, easily reversible, and has a well-understood impact profile."
"We have executed this type of change [X] times previously with consistent positive results."
"The downside is limited to [SPECIFIC_DOWNSIDE]. The upside is [SPECIFIC_UPSIDE]."
```

### Moderate-Risk Changes
```
"This carries moderate risk. We expect [POSITIVE_OUTCOME], but there is a [X]% chance of [NEGATIVE_OUTCOME]."
"We mitigate this risk by [MITIGATION_1] and [MITIGATION_2]."
"If results are negative, we will [ROLLBACK_PLAN], limiting exposure to $[AMOUNT]."
```

### High-Risk Changes
```
"This is a high-risk decision that requires explicit approval. The potential impact is [DESCRIPTION]."
"The worst-case scenario is [WORST_CASE], which we estimate has a [X]% probability."
"We recommend proceeding only if [CONDITION], and with the following safeguards: [SAFEGUARDS]."
```

---

## Risk Mitigation Language

### Pre-Action Mitigation
- "Before implementing this, we will [PRECAUTION] to reduce the risk of [OUTCOME]."
- "We have a rollback plan ready: if [METRIC] exceeds [THRESHOLD] for [DURATION], we will [ACTION]."
- "We are limiting the initial exposure to [AMOUNT/PERCENTAGE] of total budget."

### During-Action Monitoring
- "We will monitor [METRICS] at [FREQUENCY] during the transition period."
- "An alert has been set for [CONDITION], which will trigger immediate review."

### Post-Action Assessment
- "After [PERIOD], we will assess whether the risk materialized and adjust accordingly."
- "The risk window is [PERIOD]. After that, the change will be fully absorbed."

---

## Risk-Reward Framing

### When Risk is Justified
- "The potential reward of [OUTCOME] outweighs the moderate risk of [DOWNSIDE]. We recommend proceeding."
- "The risk-reward ratio is favorable: limited downside of [X] against potential upside of [Y]."
- "This is a calculated risk. Even if results are 30% below projection, the campaign will still be profitable."

### When Risk is Not Justified
- "The potential improvement does not justify the risk. A [X]% chance of [NEGATIVE_OUTCOME] is too high given the marginal upside of [Y]%."
- "We recommend a lower-risk alternative that achieves [Z]% of the upside with significantly less exposure."

## Common Risk Scenarios and Language

| Scenario | Risk Level | Standard Language |
|---|---|---|
| Increasing budget 15-20% | Low | "Standard scaling increment with minimal risk to performance." |
| Increasing budget 50%+ | High | "Aggressive budget increase carries significant risk of CPA spike and learning phase reset." |
| Launching new creative | Low | "Adding creative carries minimal risk -- existing winners remain active." |
| Pausing all old creative | Moderate | "Removing proven creative carries moderate risk if new variants underperform." |
| Testing new platform | Low-Moderate | "Limited budget exposure. Risk is primarily in the opportunity cost of the test budget." |
| Changing conversion event | High | "Changing the optimization event will reset the algorithm's learning. Expect 1-2 weeks of instability." |
| Changing landing page | Moderate | "If the new page underperforms, CPA will increase. We recommend a split test rather than a full switch." |
| Restructuring campaigns | High | "Campaign restructure will reset learning across all campaigns. Plan for 7-14 days of elevated CPA." |
| Ignoring a declining trend | High | "Not acting on the declining performance trend carries risk of continued deterioration." |

## Escalation Language

### When to Escalate
- "This risk level exceeds my decision authority. Escalating to [PERSON] for approval."
- "Given the [AMOUNT] budget exposure, this decision requires [CLIENT/LEADERSHIP] approval before proceeding."
- "I am flagging this risk for visibility. No immediate action is required, but [STAKEHOLDER] should be aware."

### How to Escalate
```
RISK ALERT: [BRIEF_DESCRIPTION]
Risk Level: [1-5]
Potential Impact: [QUANTIFIED]
Deadline for Decision: [DATE]
Recommended Action: [ACTION]
Alternatives: [ALTERNATIVES]
```
