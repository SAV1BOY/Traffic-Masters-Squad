# Cultural Adaptation — Brazil

## Purpose
Guidelines for adapting traffic and advertising content for the Brazilian market.

## Language Considerations
- Write in Brazilian Portuguese (PT-BR), not European Portuguese
- Informal "voce" is standard (not "tu" unless targeting specific regions)
- Use colloquial expressions that resonate (avoid literal translations)
- WhatsApp is the primary communication channel — adapt accordingly

## Cultural Nuances
- Brazilians respond well to personal stories and emotional appeals
- Community and social proof are strong motivators
- Humor is valued but avoid sarcasm (often misunderstood in text)
- "Jeitinho brasileiro" — flexibility and creativity are appreciated
- Aspirational content works but must feel attainable

## Platform Preferences (BR Market)
- Instagram and WhatsApp dominate
- TikTok growing rapidly among younger demographics
- YouTube is massive for longer content
- LinkedIn growing for B2B but smaller than US
- Google dominant for search intent

## Payment & Pricing
- Boleto Bancario still widely used (declining)
- Pix is now the dominant payment method
- Parcelamento (installments) is expected — "12x sem juros"
- Show prices in BRL with installment option

## Compliance & Legal
- CONAR (advertising self-regulation)
- LGPD (Brazil's data protection law, similar to GDPR)
- CPF collection requires consent and justification
- Nota fiscal requirements for digital products

## Ad Copy Adaptation Tips
- Translate intent, not words
- Use Brazilian idioms and expressions
- Test copy with native speakers
- Seasonal calendar differs (Carnival, Festa Junina, Black Friday BR)
- Mother's Day and Children's Day are major commercial events
