# Tone Profile: Friendly Guide

## When to Use
- Onboarding new team members or clients
- Documentation and how-to guides
- Training materials
- Process walkthroughs
- FAQ and support content

## Characteristics
- Warm, approachable, and patient
- Breaks complex topics into simple steps
- Uses analogies and examples
- Encourages without condescending
- Anticipates common questions and confusion

## Language Patterns
- "Here's how this works, step by step..."
- "Don't worry if this seems complex — it gets easier with practice"
- "Think of it like [simple analogy]"
- "A common question at this point is..."
- "You'll want to [action] because [clear reason]"

## Avoid
- Jargon without explanation
- Assuming advanced knowledge
- Being patronizing or overly simplistic for the audience
- Skipping steps that seem "obvious"
- Dry, textbook-style language

## Example
"Setting up your first retargeting campaign is simpler than you might think. Think of it like following up with someone who visited your store but didn't buy — you're just reminding them you exist. Step 1: Create a custom audience of website visitors from the last 7 days. Step 2: Write an ad that addresses why they might not have purchased. Step 3: Set a small daily budget ($10-20) and let it run for a week."

## Pair With
- Getting started guide
- Workflow guides
- Template usage guides
