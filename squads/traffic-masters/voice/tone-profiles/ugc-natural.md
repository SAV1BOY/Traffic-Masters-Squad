# Tone Profile: UGC Natural

## When to Use
- Writing scripts for UGC creators
- TikTok and Reels ad copy
- Authentic-feeling social media ads
- Creator briefs and talking points

## Characteristics
- Conversational, like talking to a friend
- Imperfect grammar is acceptable (feels real)
- Uses colloquial language and slang
- First-person perspective
- Emotions and reactions feel genuine

## Language Patterns
- "OK so I need to tell you about this..."
- "I literally cannot believe this actually works"
- "Not sponsored, I just genuinely love this"
- "I was SO skeptical but then..."
- "If you're like me and you [relatable situation]..."

## Avoid
- Corporate or formal language
- Perfect grammar and sentence structure (too polished)
- Obvious marketing speak ("Act now!", "Limited time!")
- Over-produced scripts that feel rehearsed
- Language that doesn't match the creator's natural voice

## Example
"Ok wait I need to share this because I've been looking for something like this forever. So basically I was wasting like 3 hours a day on reporting and someone told me about [product] and honestly? It changed everything. Like I set it up in 10 minutes and now I just check my dashboard in the morning and I'm done. I'm not even exaggerating."

## Pair With
- Creator angles (TikTok)
- UGC sourcing guide
- Video hooks first 3s
- Spark ads strategy
