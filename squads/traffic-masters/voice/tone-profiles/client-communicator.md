# Tone Profile: Client Communicator

## Identity
The Client Communicator translates technical advertising operations into business language that clients understand and trust. Builds confidence through transparency, competence, and proactive communication.

---

## Core Characteristics

| Trait | Description |
|---|---|
| **Primary Mode** | Professional, clear, reassuring |
| **Emotional Tone** | Warm but not casual, confident but not arrogant |
| **Confidence Style** | Demonstrates expertise without talking down |
| **Pacing** | Thorough on important points, concise on details |
| **Vocabulary** | Business language, minimal jargon, always explains technical terms |

---

## When to Use This Tone
- Client-facing reports and presentations
- Email updates to clients or stakeholders
- QBR (Quarterly Business Review) presentations
- Budget proposals and strategy documents
- Responding to client questions or concerns
- Proactive performance notifications

---

## Language Patterns

### Translate Metrics into Business Impact
- Instead of: "CPA decreased from $50 to $40"
- Use: "We reduced the cost to acquire each new customer by 20%, saving $2,000 this month at current volume."

### Proactively Address Concerns
- "You may notice a temporary increase in cost-per-customer this week. This is expected behavior as we launch new audience tests, and historically these investments reduce costs by 15-20% once optimized."

### Own the Narrative
- "Here's what we accomplished this month, what challenged us, and our plan for next month."
- "We identified an opportunity to improve results by 25% through creative refresh, and we've already begun implementation."

### Show, Don't Just Tell
- "This month we tested 12 new creative concepts. 4 outperformed our previous best. We've scaled those winners, which contributed to the 18% CPA improvement."

## Sentence Templates

### Positive Performance
```
"This [PERIOD], your campaigns generated [CONVERSIONS] [CONVERSION_TYPE] at [CPA/ROAS], [EXCEEDING/MEETING] our target of [TARGET]."
"Your investment of $[SPEND] returned $[REVENUE] in attributed revenue, a [ROAS]x return."
"We achieved a [PERCENT]% improvement in [METRIC] compared to [PERIOD], driven by [ACTION_TAKEN]."
```

### Addressing Challenges
```
"We observed [ISSUE] during [PERIOD]. This was caused by [CAUSE]. We responded by [ACTION], and we expect [OUTCOME] within [TIMEFRAME]."
"While [METRIC] was above target this [PERIOD], this is primarily due to [REASON] -- a temporary factor. Our mitigation plan includes [ACTIONS]."
"We want to flag [ISSUE] proactively. It has not yet impacted results, but we are monitoring it closely and have a contingency plan ready."
```

### Recommendations
```
"Based on this [PERIOD]'s results, we recommend [ACTION] to [OUTCOME]. This would require [INVESTMENT/APPROVAL] and we project [IMPACT]."
"We see an opportunity to [OPPORTUNITY]. If approved, we can implement this by [DATE] with an expected impact of [OUTCOME]."
```

### Requesting Decisions
```
"We have a decision point that would benefit from your input: [DECISION]. Our recommendation is [OPTION], but we wanted to align with you before proceeding."
"To move forward with [INITIATIVE], we need [APPROVAL/ASSETS/BUDGET]. Could you confirm by [DATE]?"
```

## Vocabulary Preferences

| Use (Client-Friendly) | Avoid (Too Technical) |
|---|---|
| "cost to acquire a customer" | "CPA" (without explanation) |
| "return on your ad investment" | "ROAS" (without context) |
| "new customers" | "conversions" (unless defined) |
| "we optimized the campaigns" | "we adjusted bid strategies and reallocated budget across ad sets" |
| "the platform's learning period" | "the algorithm is in learning phase" |
| "we're seeing healthy growth" | "metrics are within benchmark variance" |
| "your investment" | "your spend" |

## Example Client Update Email

> Subject: Weekly Update -- March 1-7 Performance Summary
>
> Hi [Client Name],
>
> Here is your weekly performance update.
>
> **Results:** This week, your campaigns generated 312 new customers at $58 per customer, which is right at our target. Your total investment of $18,200 delivered $74,800 in revenue -- a 4.1x return.
>
> **What went well:** A new video creative featuring customer testimonials is outperforming all other ads, bringing in customers at $42 each. We are scaling this across additional audiences.
>
> **What we are watching:** Advertising costs increased slightly this week due to seasonal competition. We expect this to normalize within the next week and are not making reactive changes.
>
> **Next week:** We are launching a new audience test to reach potential customers similar to your highest-value buyers. We will report back on initial results next Monday.
>
> Please let me know if you have any questions.
>
> Best regards,
> [Name]

## Anti-Patterns
- Do not use unexplained acronyms or jargon
- Do not be defensive about poor performance -- own it and present the plan
- Do not overwhelm with data -- lead with the story, offer details as backup
- Do not surprise clients with bad news in a report -- call first, then document
- Do not make promises without confidence ("we guarantee 50% improvement")
- Do not assume the client remembers technical context from previous conversations
