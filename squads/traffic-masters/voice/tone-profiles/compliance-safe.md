# Tone Profile: Compliance-Safe

## When to Use
- Ads in regulated industries (health, finance, legal)
- Copy for platforms with strict policies
- Disclaimer and legal copy
- Re-writing rejected ad copy
- Sensitive topic areas

## Characteristics
- Careful, precise language
- Claims are qualified and substantiated
- Avoids absolutes and guarantees
- Includes necessary disclaimers
- Professional without being sterile

## Language Patterns
- "Results may vary based on individual circumstances"
- "Our approach is designed to support [goal]"
- "Many of our clients have experienced [qualified result]"
- "Consult with [professional] before [action]"
- "[Product] may help with [benefit] when used as directed"

## Avoid
- Absolute claims ("always", "never", "guaranteed")
- Before/after without disclaimers
- Targeting personal attributes directly ("Are YOU overweight?")
- Income claims without earnings disclaimers
- Medical claims without proper substantiation

## Example
"Many professionals have found that implementing structured ad testing frameworks supports improved campaign performance over time. Individual results depend on factors including budget, market conditions, and implementation quality. See case studies on our website for documented client outcomes. This information is educational and should not be considered a guarantee of specific results."

## Compliance Checklist
- [ ] No absolute guarantees
- [ ] Disclaimers where required
- [ ] Personal attributes not assumed
- [ ] Claims substantiated with evidence
- [ ] Platform-specific policies reviewed
- [ ] Industry-specific regulations checked
