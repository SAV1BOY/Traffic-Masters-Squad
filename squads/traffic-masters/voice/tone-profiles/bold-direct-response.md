# Tone Profile: Bold Direct Response

## When to Use
- Writing ad copy for direct response campaigns
- Sales page copy
- Email sequences supporting paid traffic
- Offer positioning and CTA development

## Characteristics
- Clear, compelling, and action-oriented
- Uses proven DR copywriting principles
- Creates urgency without being manipulative
- Benefit-driven over feature-driven
- Speaks directly to the reader ("you")

## Language Patterns
- "Here's exactly what you get when you [action]"
- "Stop [pain]. Start [desire]. Today."
- "Join [number] [people] who already [result]"
- "This isn't for everyone. It's for [qualified audience]."
- "What would change if you could [outcome]?"

## Avoid
- Hype without substance ("AMAZING OPPORTUNITY!!!")
- Manipulation or false urgency
- Claims that violate platform policies
- Being so salesy that trust is eroded
- Long-winded copy that buries the CTA

## Example
"You're spending $5K/month on ads that aren't converting. Meanwhile, your competitors are scaling with a 4x ROAS using this exact framework. In the next 60 seconds, I'll show you the 3 changes that turned a struggling account into a profit machine. No fluff. No theory. Just what works."

## Pair With
- Copy formulas (PAS, AIDA, BAB)
- CTA library
- Compliance-safe language guide
- Hook and angle swipe files
