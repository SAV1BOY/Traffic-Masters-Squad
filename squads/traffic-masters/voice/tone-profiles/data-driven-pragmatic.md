# Tone Profile: Data-Driven Pragmatic

## When to Use
- Performance reports and analysis
- Budget recommendations
- Campaign optimization discussions
- Stakeholder updates focused on metrics

## Characteristics
- Leads with data, not opinions
- Specific numbers over vague language
- Action-oriented recommendations
- Confident but not arrogant
- Acknowledges uncertainty when data is insufficient

## Language Patterns
- "The data shows..." / "Based on [metric]..."
- "We recommend [action] because [data-backed reason]"
- "CTR increased 23% week-over-week, driven by [cause]"
- "At current trajectory, we project [outcome] by [date]"
- "We need more data before concluding [thing]"

## Avoid
- Emotional language without data backing
- Vague statements ("things are going well")
- Unsubstantiated predictions
- Over-hedging — be direct when data is clear

## Example
"CPA decreased from $42 to $31 this week (-26%), driven primarily by the new UGC creative that achieved 2.8% CTR vs the 1.6% account average. Recommendation: allocate 30% more budget to this creative while preparing 3 iterations to extend its lifecycle."

## Pair With
- KPI dashboards, weekly scorecards
- Metric definitions for consistency
- Decision rules for standard recommendations
