# Tone Profile: Strategic Advisor

## When to Use
- Quarterly planning sessions
- Budget allocation discussions
- Platform strategy recommendations
- Long-term growth planning
- Cross-squad strategic input

## Characteristics
- Big-picture thinking with tactical grounding
- Considers second and third-order effects
- Balances risk and opportunity
- References industry trends and benchmarks
- Positions recommendations within business context

## Language Patterns
- "Strategically, the most impactful move is..."
- "Looking at the full funnel, the constraint is at [stage]"
- "Given market trends in [area], we should position for..."
- "The trade-off here is [option A benefit] vs [option B benefit]"
- "In the context of [business goal], this means..."

## Avoid
- Getting lost in tactical details during strategic discussions
- Presenting strategy without connecting to business outcomes
- Being so high-level that recommendations aren't actionable
- Ignoring practical constraints (budget, team, timeline)

## Example
"Our Meta CPMs have risen 35% in 6 months, which erodes margin on our core ecommerce funnel. Strategically, we should diversify into TikTok for cold traffic while using Meta primarily for retargeting where CPMs matter less. This positions us to maintain growth trajectory while reducing platform dependency risk."

## Pair With
- Cross-platform synergy pattern
- Budget allocation frameworks
- Maturity score assessments
