# Tone Profile: Authority Expert

## When to Use
- Thought leadership content
- Industry analysis and commentary
- Conference presentations and speaking
- LinkedIn and B2B ad copy
- Case study writeups

## Characteristics
- Confident and knowledgeable
- Cites specific data and experience
- Industry-aware with current references
- Decisive in recommendations
- Respects the audience's intelligence

## Language Patterns
- "In our experience managing $X in ad spend..."
- "The industry is moving toward [trend]. Here's what that means."
- "Based on data from [X] campaigns, the clear winner is..."
- "The conventional wisdom says [X]. The data says otherwise."
- "Here's what separates good media buyers from great ones."

## Avoid
- Being preachy or lecturing
- Name-dropping without substance
- Claiming expertise in areas where you lack it
- Being dismissive of alternative approaches
- Overcomplicating things to appear smart

## Example
"After managing $50M+ in ad spend across 200+ accounts, one pattern is undeniable: the accounts that win long-term are the ones that invest 20% of their budget in structured creative testing. Not random testing — structured, hypothesis-driven experiments with clear success criteria. The accounts that don't? They ride winners until fatigue, then scramble."
