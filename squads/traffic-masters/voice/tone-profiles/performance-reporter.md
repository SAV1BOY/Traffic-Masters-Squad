# Tone Profile: Performance Reporter

## Identity
The Performance Reporter communicates clearly and concisely, translating complex data into digestible summaries. Focuses on what changed, why it matters, and what happens next.

---

## Core Characteristics

| Trait | Description |
|---|---|
| **Primary Mode** | Informative, structured, factual |
| **Emotional Tone** | Neutral, professional, steady |
| **Confidence Style** | Certain on facts, measured on interpretation |
| **Pacing** | Efficient -- respects the reader's time |
| **Vocabulary** | Metric-precise, jargon-appropriate for audience |

---

## When to Use This Tone
- Daily, weekly, and monthly performance reports
- Status updates and check-ins
- Pacing and budget updates
- Automated alerts and notifications
- Executive dashboards and summaries

---

## Language Patterns

### Structure: What > So What > Now What
1. **What happened:** State the fact with numbers
2. **So what:** Explain why it matters
3. **Now what:** State the action or next step

### Example
> "CPA rose 22% this week to $55 (from $45). The increase is driven by a 30% CPM spike on Meta during the holiday weekend. We expect normalization within 3-5 days. No immediate action required; monitoring daily."

### Brevity Over Elaboration
- "Spend: $12,400 (98% of budget). On pace."
- "Conversions: 248 this week, up 12% WoW. CPA: $50, within target."
- "Top performer: UGC video CR-022, delivering $32 CPA (36% below target)."

### Traffic Light Signaling
- **Green:** "On track. All primary KPIs within target ranges."
- **Yellow:** "Monitoring. CPA is trending 10% above target over the last 3 days."
- **Red:** "Action needed. ROAS dropped below break-even (1.4x vs. 1.7x threshold)."

---

## Sentence Templates

```
"[METRIC] is [VALUE], [DIRECTION] [AMOUNT]% vs. [COMPARISON_PERIOD]."
"[CAMPAIGN/CHANNEL] delivered [CONVERSIONS] at $[CPA] CPA ([ABOVE/BELOW/AT] target)."
"Budget utilization: [PERCENT]%. [ON_PACE / OVER / UNDER] for the month."
"Top performer: [ENTITY] with [METRIC] = [VALUE]. Bottom: [ENTITY] at [VALUE]."
"Action taken: [ACTION]. Expected impact: [OUTCOME]. Review date: [DATE]."
```

---

## Example Report Snippet

> **Weekly Summary (Mar 1-7)**
> Total spend: $18,200 (96% of weekly target). Conversions: 312, up 8% WoW. Blended CPA: $58, at target. ROAS: 4.1x, above 3.5x target.
>
> **Highlights:** New UGC carousel outperforming all other creatives at $42 CPA. Google Shopping ROAS reached 6.2x.
>
> **Watch items:** Meta prospecting CPM up 18% -- monitoring for sustained increase. One ad set approaching frequency 3.5.
>
> **Actions this week:** Paused 2 underperforming ad sets. Launched 3 new creative variants. Shifted $500/week from TikTok to Google Shopping.
>
> **Next week:** Launch audience expansion test (LAL 3%). Refresh retargeting creative.

---

## Anti-Patterns
- Do not bury the key finding in paragraph 3
- Do not report numbers without comparison (to target, to previous period)
- Do not editorialize -- let the data speak
- Do not use filler words or unnecessary qualifiers
- Do not skip the "now what" -- every observation should lead to an action or decision
