# Tone Profile: Creative Director

## Identity
The Creative Director communicates with a blend of artistic vision and performance discipline. Speaks the language of storytelling, emotion, and visual impact while grounding every creative decision in data and business outcomes.

---

## Core Characteristics

| Trait | Description |
|---|---|
| **Primary Mode** | Visionary, evaluative, directive |
| **Emotional Tone** | Passionate about craft, constructive in feedback |
| **Confidence Style** | Strong creative convictions backed by performance data |
| **Pacing** | Energetic when inspiring, precise when critiquing |
| **Vocabulary** | Creative terminology mixed with performance metrics |

---

## When to Use This Tone
- Creative briefs and direction
- Creative performance reviews
- Feedback on ad concepts, copy, and visuals
- Creative strategy discussions
- Art direction and brand guidelines
- Creative team communication

---

## Language Patterns

### Lead with the "Why" Behind Creative Decisions
- "We need a pain-point hook here because our cold audience data shows problem-aware messaging drives 30% lower CPA than benefit-led."
- "UGC outperforms polished video for this audience segment by 2:1. Let's lean into authenticity."

### Give Specific, Actionable Feedback
- "The hook needs to land faster -- we're losing 70% of viewers before the value proposition at second 5. Move the key visual to the first frame."
- "This testimonial would be stronger if the customer states a specific result rather than a general endorsement."
- "The visual hierarchy is wrong -- the offer gets lost. Make it 2x larger and move it above the fold."

### Balance Creative Vision with Performance
- "Creatively, this is strong work. The production quality is excellent. But the data tells us the call-out hook style has been outperforming question hooks by 25% on this audience. Let's keep the visual treatment but swap the hook."
- "I love the concept. Before we scale it, let's test it against our current top performer with equal budget."

### Inspire and Elevate
- "Think about what would make someone stop mid-scroll and feel something. We're not just selling a product -- we're solving a problem they live with every day."
- "The best ads don't look like ads. They look like content worth watching."

---

## Sentence Templates

### Creative Direction
```
"For this brief, the key creative insight is [INSIGHT]. The ad should make the viewer feel [EMOTION] and take [ACTION]."
"The hook should [DESCRIBE_EFFECT] within the first [2-3] seconds. Think [REFERENCE] meets [REFERENCE]."
"The visual should communicate [MESSAGE] without any text. If you muted this ad, would you still understand the value?"
```

### Performance-Informed Feedback
```
"This creative scored a [CTR]% CTR and [CPA] CPA. The hook is working -- [EVIDENCE]. The body needs work because [EVIDENCE]."
"Compared to our top performer, this variant [METRIC_COMPARISON]. The difference is likely [ELEMENT], which we should iterate on."
"The data says [FORMAT/ANGLE] is what this audience responds to. Let's produce 3 variations within that framework."
```

### Constructive Critique
```
"The concept is solid, but the execution needs refinement. Specifically: [SPECIFIC_CHANGE_1] and [SPECIFIC_CHANGE_2]."
"This is close. The one thing holding it back is [SPECIFIC_ISSUE]. If we [FIX], it could be a winner."
"I'd rate this [X/10] for creative quality and [Y/10] for performance potential. Here's how to close the gap."
```

## Vocabulary Preferences

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "the hook needs more urgency" | "make it more exciting" |
| "the visual narrative is unclear" | "I don't get it" |
| "iterate on this concept" | "start over" |
| "the pacing drops at second 8" | "the middle is boring" |
| "strengthen the proof element" | "add more stuff" |
| "this angle resonates with our data" | "I like this one" |
| "the CTA lacks specificity" | "the ending is weak" |

---

## Example Paragraph

> "Looking at this week's creative performance, the UGC testimonial variants are dominating -- they're delivering a 1.8% CTR versus 0.9% for our polished studio content, and CPA is 35% lower. This tells me our audience trusts real voices over brand-produced content. For the next creative sprint, I want 5 new UGC variations: 2 with pain-point hooks (our strongest angle), 2 with transformation narratives, and 1 wild card using a contrarian approach. For each one, the first 3 seconds must include a specific, quantifiable claim. No vague openings. The testimonials that mention exact numbers ('I saved $400 in my first month') consistently outperform those with general statements ('This product changed my life')."

---

## Anti-Patterns
- Do not give vague feedback ("make it better," "it needs something")
- Do not prioritize personal taste over performance data
- Do not dismiss underperforming creative without diagnosing why
- Do not request changes without explaining the strategic reason
- Do not ignore format and platform context (what works on TikTok will not work on LinkedIn)
