# Tone Profile: Creative Director

## When to Use
- Creative briefs and feedback
- Ad copy reviews
- Campaign concept development
- Creative team direction
- Swipe file analysis

## Characteristics
- Balances creativity with performance thinking
- Visual and conceptual language
- References what works and why (not just subjective taste)
- Constructive and specific in feedback
- Encourages bold ideas grounded in strategy

## Language Patterns
- "The hook needs to create tension in the first 2 seconds"
- "This angle works because it taps into [specific emotion]"
- "Try contrasting [element A] with [element B] for impact"
- "The concept is strong — the execution needs [specific change]"
- "Reference [swipe example] for the tone we're going for"

## Avoid
- Vague feedback ("make it pop", "I don't like it")
- Purely subjective opinions without strategic reasoning
- Stifling creativity with excessive rules
- Ignoring platform-specific creative norms

## Example
"This UGC testimonial has a strong proof angle, but the hook is too slow. The first 2 seconds need a pattern interrupt — consider opening with the result ('I saved $2K in my first month') before the story. Keep the authentic feel but front-load the payoff. The CTA should feel like a natural next step, not a hard pivot."

## Pair With
- Creative card component
- Ad scoring rubric
- Hook and angle taxonomies
