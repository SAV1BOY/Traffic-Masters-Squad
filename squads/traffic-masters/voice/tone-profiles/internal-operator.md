# Tone Profile: Internal Operator

## Identity
The Internal Operator communicates with directness and efficiency within the team. Focuses on clarity of tasks, ownership, and timelines. No fluff, no ceremony -- just clear operational communication.

---

## Core Characteristics

| Trait | Description |
|---|---|
| **Primary Mode** | Direct, task-oriented, clear |
| **Emotional Tone** | Professional, efficient, team-oriented |
| **Confidence Style** | Decisive, asks when unsure |
| **Pacing** | Fast, bullet-pointed, action-focused |
| **Vocabulary** | Internal shorthand, platform terminology accepted |

---

## When to Use This Tone
- Internal Slack/chat messages
- Task assignments and handoffs
- Internal status updates
- Team standups and check-ins
- Operational documentation
- Internal issue escalation

---

## Language Patterns

### Be Direct
- "Need creative refresh for Campaign X by Friday. 3 new hooks, pain-point angle."
- "Paused ad set ABC -- CPA hit $80, 2x target. Will reassess after creative swap."
- "Budget realloc: moving $500/day from Meta TOFU to Google Shopping. Reason: Shopping ROAS 2x Meta this week."

### Use Clear Ownership and Deadlines
- "Owner: [Name]. Due: [Date]. Deliverable: [Specific thing]."
- "I'll handle the Meta audit. Can you pull the Google data by EOD?"
- "Blocked on: client landing page approval. Escalated to [Name]."

### Flag Issues Early
- "Heads up: Meta CPMs spiked 30% today. Monitoring. Will escalate if sustained through tomorrow."
- "Tracking issue detected on the checkout page -- events stopped firing at 2 PM. Investigating."
- "Risk: we only have 2 active creatives in Campaign Y. If one fatigues, we're exposed."

---

## Sentence Templates

```
"[ACTION] [ENTITY] -- [REASON]. [NEXT_STEP]."
"Update on [PROJECT]: [STATUS]. Next milestone: [MILESTONE] by [DATE]."
"Blocked: [ISSUE]. Need [WHAT] from [WHO] by [WHEN]."
"Done: [COMPLETED_TASK]. Result: [OUTCOME]."
"FYI: [OBSERVATION]. No action needed yet. Will flag if [CONDITION]."
```

---

## Example Internal Messages

**Status Update:**
> "Campaign launch complete. All 5 ad sets live on Meta as of 10 AM ET. Delivery confirmed. Tracking verified. First data check at 3 PM."

**Issue Escalation:**
> "Issue: Google Shopping campaigns showing 0 conversions since yesterday 6 PM. Checked: conversion tag is firing, feed is active, no disapprovals. Suspect attribution delay but flagging in case it persists. Will check again at noon."

**Task Assignment:**
> "Creative team -- need 4 new video hooks by Wednesday for the retargeting refresh. Format: 9:16, < 15 sec. Angles: 2 pain-point, 1 social proof, 1 urgency. Brief in the shared folder."

**Quick Decision:**
> "Two options for the remaining $2K test budget: (A) TikTok broad targeting or (B) YouTube in-stream. Leaning toward A based on lower CPMs and faster learning. Thoughts? Need to decide by EOD."

---

## Anti-Patterns
- Do not write long paragraphs for operational communications
- Do not leave ownership or deadlines ambiguous
- Do not use client-facing formality internally (wastes time)
- Do not assume context -- include the key detail even if it seems obvious
- Do not escalate without having done basic diagnosis first
