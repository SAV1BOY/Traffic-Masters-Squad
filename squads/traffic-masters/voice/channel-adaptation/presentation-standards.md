# Presentation Standards

## Purpose
Standards for traffic strategy and performance presentations.

## Slide Design Rules
- One key message per slide
- Maximum 5 bullet points per slide
- Use charts/graphs instead of tables when possible
- Dark text on light background (professional readability)
- Include source/date on every data slide

## Presentation Structure

### Performance Review (15-20 min)
1. Executive summary (1 slide)
2. Key metrics dashboard (1-2 slides)
3. What worked (1-2 slides)
4. What needs attention (1-2 slides)
5. Creative performance (1 slide)
6. Experiment results (1 slide)
7. Next period plan (1-2 slides)
8. Questions (1 slide)

### Strategy Proposal (20-30 min)
1. Current state and context (2 slides)
2. Opportunity/problem (1-2 slides)
3. Proposed strategy (3-5 slides)
4. Expected outcomes and KPIs (1-2 slides)
5. Budget and timeline (1-2 slides)
6. Risks and mitigations (1 slide)
7. Next steps and approval needed (1 slide)

## Data Visualization Rules
- Bar charts for comparisons
- Line charts for trends over time
- Pie charts only for 2-4 segments (avoid 5+)
- Always include axis labels and legend
- Highlight the insight, not just the data

## Speaking Notes
- Lead with the insight, then show the data
- Prepare for "so what?" after every slide
- Have backup slides for detailed questions
- Practice the transition between slides
- Time yourself — respect the meeting length
