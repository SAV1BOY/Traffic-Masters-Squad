# Voice Adaptation: Formal Reports

## Purpose
Guidelines for adapting the Traffic Masters voice for formal written reports (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly). Optimizes for clarity, professionalism, and completeness.

---

## Core Principles for Reports

1. **Structure is king.** Follow consistent section ordering.
2. **Lead with the conclusion.** Executive summary first, details after.
3. **Every number needs context.** Compare to target, previous period, or benchmark.
4. **Data tables should be scannable.** Use consistent formatting and highlight key values.
5. **Recommendations are mandatory.** No report should end without clear next steps.

---

## Report Voice Characteristics

| Aspect | Guideline |
|---|---|
| **Tone** | Professional, objective, measured |
| **Person** | Third person for observations ("The campaign delivered..."), first person plural for recommendations ("We recommend...") |
| **Tense** | Past tense for results, present tense for current status, future tense for recommendations |
| **Length** | Proportional to cadence: daily (1-2 pages), weekly (3-5 pages), monthly (5-10 pages) |
| **Jargon** | Acceptable for internal reports; translate for client reports |

---

## Section-by-Section Voice Guide

### Executive Summary
- **Voice:** Confident, concise, decisive
- **Length:** 2-5 sentences (daily), 1-2 paragraphs (weekly/monthly)
- **Pattern:** What happened + why it matters + what we are doing about it
- **Example:** "This week's campaigns delivered 312 conversions at $58 CPA, meeting our $60 target. The primary driver was a strong-performing UGC creative that reduced prospecting CPA by 22%. We recommend scaling this creative across additional audiences next week."

### KPI Scorecard
- **Voice:** Neutral, factual, data-forward
- **Formatting:** Table with consistent column order: Metric | Current | Previous | Change | Target | Status
- **Highlight:** Bold or color-code values that exceed or miss targets
- **Note:** Always include the comparison period and attribution model in a footnote

### Analysis Section
- **Voice:** Analytical, evidence-based
- **Pattern:** Observation + Evidence + Interpretation
- **Example:** "CPA increased 15% week-over-week. This coincides with a 20% CPM increase across Meta campaigns, likely driven by increased auction competition during the Presidents' Day holiday period. Historical data from the same period last year shows a similar 18% CPM spike that normalized within 5 days."

### Recommendations Section
- **Voice:** Assertive, action-oriented
- **Pattern:** Recommendation + Rationale + Expected Impact + Priority + Timeline
- **Example:** "P1: Launch 4 new UGC creative variants by Wednesday. Rationale: Current top performer is approaching fatigue (CTR down 15% from peak). Expected impact: Maintain or improve CPA efficiency while sustaining volume."

### Next Steps
- **Voice:** Clear, specific, assigned
- **Pattern:** Task + Owner + Due Date
- **Example:** "Launch audience expansion test (LAL 3%) -- [Name] -- March 12"

## Formatting Standards

### Numbers
- Currency: $1,234.56 (two decimals for < $100, no decimals for larger)
- Percentages: 12.3% (one decimal place)
- Large numbers: 125,000 or 125K
- ROAS: 4.2x (one decimal with "x")
- Changes: +15.2%, -$3.50 (include sign)

### Tables
- Use header rows with bold text
- Right-align numerical columns
- Include totals row where applicable
- Maximum 8-10 columns before readability suffers

### Narrative Sections
- Maximum paragraph length: 4-5 sentences
- Use bullet points for lists of 3+ items
- Use bold for first mention of key terms or metrics
- Use subheadings to break up long sections

---

## Audience-Specific Adaptations

### Internal Team Reports
- Technical jargon acceptable
- Include granular data (ad set level, creative level)
- Include "what we are testing" and "what we learned"
- Action items with specific owners

### Client Reports
- Translate all jargon (see Client Reporting Language Guide)
- Focus on business outcomes (revenue, customers, ROI)
- Include "what this means for your business"
- Recommendations framed as proposals requiring feedback

### Executive / Leadership Reports
- Maximum 2 pages
- Lead with 3-5 key metrics in a dashboard format
- Include trend charts rather than raw data tables
- Recommendations framed as strategic decisions
- Include financial impact of recommendations

---

## Report Quality Checklist

- [ ] Executive summary can stand alone (reader understands the full picture without reading the rest)
- [ ] Every metric has a comparison point (target, previous period, or benchmark)
- [ ] No unexplained acronyms
- [ ] Recommendations are specific and actionable
- [ ] Next steps have owners and deadlines
- [ ] Numbers have been spot-checked against source data
- [ ] Report follows the standard template for its cadence
- [ ] Tone is consistent throughout (no shift from professional to casual)
- [ ] Sent on schedule
