# Email — Traffic Communications

## Purpose
Standards for traffic-related email communications.

## Email Types

### Weekly Report Email
- **Subject:** "[Client] Traffic Report — Week of [Date]"
- **Structure:** Summary > Key Metrics Table > Wins > Challenges > Next Steps
- **Length:** 300-500 words max
- **Attachments:** Dashboard link, not screenshots

### Budget Approval Email
- **Subject:** "[Client] Budget Recommendation — [Month/Quarter]"
- **Structure:** Recommendation > Rationale > Expected Outcome > Alternatives
- **Tone:** Confident, data-backed

### Strategy Proposal Email
- **Subject:** "[Client] Proposed Strategy Change — [Topic]"
- **Structure:** Current State > Proposed Change > Expected Impact > Timeline
- **Tone:** Strategic advisor

### Incident Notification Email
- **Subject:** "[URGENT] [Client] — [Issue Description]"
- **Structure:** What Happened > Impact > Actions Taken > Next Steps
- **Tone:** Direct, transparent, solution-focused

## Formatting Rules
- Use headers and bullet points (not walls of text)
- Bold key numbers and decisions
- Include one clear CTA (what you need from the recipient)
- Keep paragraphs to 2-3 sentences
- Use tables for metric comparisons

## Subject Line Formulas
- Status: "[Client] Traffic Update — [Period]"
- Request: "[Client] Approval Needed — [Topic]"
- Alert: "[URGENT] [Client] — [Issue]"
- Win: "[Client] Traffic Win — [Achievement]"

## Response Expectations
- Set clear timelines for needed approvals
- "Please confirm by [date] so we can [action]"
- Follow up if no response within 48 hours
