# Dashboard Narration Guide

## Purpose
How to write narrative summaries for dashboard reports and data visualizations.

## Narration Framework
1. **State the headline:** What is the most important takeaway?
2. **Provide context:** How does this compare to target/prior period?
3. **Explain the cause:** Why did this happen?
4. **Recommend action:** What should we do about it?

## Example Narrations

### For a KPI That Improved
"CPA improved to $24 (-18% WoW), driven by the new UGC creative batch launched Monday. This puts us 20% below our $30 target. Recommendation: Scale the top 2 creatives by 30% and prepare iterations."

### For a KPI That Declined
"ROAS declined from 3.8x to 3.1x this week. Primary cause: CPM increased 22% due to increased auction competition (seasonal). Still above our 3.0x guardrail. Monitoring closely — will adjust bid strategy if trend continues."

### For a Stable KPI
"Conversion rate held steady at 4.2% for the third consecutive week. The landing page is performing consistently. Focus testing efforts elsewhere (creative and targeting) for incremental gains."

## Dashboard Section Narration

| Section | Narration Style |
|---------|----------------|
| Executive Summary | 2-3 sentences, headline + context |
| Platform Performance | Comparative, highlight leader and laggard |
| Creative Performance | Spotlight top/bottom, fatigue signals |
| Budget Pacing | Status + explanation if off-track |
| Experiments | Result + learning + next step |

## Tone
- Data-driven pragmatic (see tone profile)
- Concise — readers scan dashboards quickly
- Actionable — every narration ends with a recommendation or status
- Honest — don't spin bad data

## Anti-Patterns
- Data without narration (forces reader to interpret)
- Narration without data (claims without evidence)
- Overly long narration (defeats purpose of dashboard)
