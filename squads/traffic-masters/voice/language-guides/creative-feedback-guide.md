# Creative Feedback Guide

## Purpose
How to give clear, actionable feedback on ad creative.

## Feedback Framework: SBI+A
1. **Situation:** Which creative, which context
2. **Behavior:** What specifically works or doesn't
3. **Impact:** Why it matters for performance
4. **Action:** What to change or keep

## Good Feedback Examples
- "The hook (first 3s) opens too slowly — viewers will scroll. Open with the result instead of the backstory. This should improve hook rate from ~15% to 25%+."
- "The testimonial section is strong — specific numbers and genuine delivery build credibility. Keep this exact approach in future variations."
- "CTA feels disconnected from the ad body. The copy talks about 'learning' but the CTA says 'Buy Now.' Match the CTA to the intent level."

## Bad Feedback Examples
- "I don't like it" (no specifics)
- "Make it better" (no direction)
- "Can we make it more punchy?" (vague)
- "This doesn't feel right" (subjective without reasoning)

## Feedback by Creative Element

| Element | What to Evaluate | Good Feedback Pattern |
|---------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Hook | Speed, relevance, curiosity | "The hook should [specific change] because [reason]" |
| Body | Clarity, flow, persuasion | "The message gets lost at [point]. Simplify to [suggestion]" |
| CTA | Clarity, urgency, match | "The CTA should match [funnel stage] intent" |
| Visual | Quality, platform-native, attention | "The visual works/doesn't work because [specific reason]" |
| Copy | Length, tone, compliance | "Shorten to [length] and adjust tone to [profile]" |

## Rules
1. Always be specific — point to exact moments or elements
2. Explain the "why" behind every piece of feedback
3. Balance positive and constructive feedback
4. Reference performance data when available
5. Suggest alternatives, not just problems
