# Client Reporting Language Guide

## Purpose
Standard language for client-facing reports and communications. Ensures reports are clear, professional, jargon-free, and focused on business outcomes rather than technical details.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Lead with business impact**, not platform metrics
2. **Translate jargon** into plain business language
3. **Tell the story**, then provide the data as evidence
4. **Be honest about challenges** while demonstrating competence
5. **Every number needs context** -- compared to what?

---

## Metric Translation for Clients

| Technical Term | Client-Friendly Language |
|---|---|
| CPA | "Cost to acquire each new customer" or "Cost per new customer" |
| ROAS | "Return on your advertising investment" or "For every $1 invested, you received $X back" |
| CTR | "The percentage of people who saw the ad and clicked through" |
| CPM | "The cost to reach 1,000 potential customers" |
| CPC | "The cost for each website visit from the ad" |
| CVR | "The percentage of website visitors who completed a purchase/action" |
| Impressions | "The number of times your ads were displayed" |
| Reach | "The number of unique people who saw your ads" |
| Frequency | "On average, each person saw your ad [X] times" |
| Pixel / CAPI | "Our tracking technology" or "our measurement tools" |
| Learning Phase | "The platform's initial optimization period" |
| Creative Fatigue | "The ad has been seen too many times by the same people" |
| Audience Saturation | "We've reached most of the available target audience at this budget level" |
| A/B Test | "We tested two versions to determine which performs better" |

## Reporting Performance

### Strong Performance
- "Your campaigns delivered strong results this [PERIOD]. You acquired [X] new customers at $[CPA] each, with every dollar invested returning $[ROAS] in revenue."
- "This month's results exceeded our targets across the board. Highlights include a [X]% improvement in cost efficiency and [Y]% increase in customer acquisition volume."
- "The creative refresh we implemented two weeks ago is paying off -- customer acquisition costs have decreased [X]%, saving you $[AMOUNT] this month."

### Steady Performance
- "Performance this [PERIOD] was consistent with expectations. Your campaigns generated [X] customers at $[CPA], right in line with our $[TARGET] goal."
- "Results are stable and on-track. No major changes needed -- we continue to optimize within the current strategy."

### Challenging Performance
- "This [PERIOD] presented some challenges, with customer acquisition costs rising [X]% to $[CPA]. We have identified the cause and have already implemented corrective measures."
- "While results were below target this [PERIOD], the actions we have taken -- including [SPECIFIC_ACTIONS] -- are already showing early signs of improvement."
- "We want to be transparent: [METRIC] did not meet our target this [PERIOD]. Here is what happened, what we learned, and our plan to get back on track."

---

## Explaining Changes

### Positive Changes
- "We made strategic adjustments that resulted in [OUTCOME]. Specifically, [ACTION] led to a [X]% improvement in [METRIC]."
- "Our optimization work this month -- including [ACTION_1] and [ACTION_2] -- contributed to [QUANTIFIED_IMPROVEMENT]."

### Negative Changes
- "Advertising costs in your industry increased this [PERIOD] due to [REASON -- seasonal competition, market dynamics, etc.]. This impacted costs across the industry, not just your campaigns."
- "We identified that [SPECIFIC_ISSUE] was contributing to higher costs. We have addressed this by [ACTION] and expect to see improvement within [TIMEFRAME]."

### Explaining External Factors
- "This [PERIOD] included [HOLIDAY/EVENT] which historically increases advertising competition and costs. Our costs were impacted, but to a lesser degree than the industry average."
- "A platform update changed how ads are delivered, which temporarily affected performance. We have adapted our approach accordingly."

## Budget and Investment Language

### Describing Spend
| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| "We spent $X" | "Your advertising investment of $X..." |
| "Ad spend" | "Your media investment" or "advertising budget" |
| "We burned through the budget" | "The full budget was deployed across campaigns" |
| "Budget left over" | "Budget available for reallocation" |

### Discussing Budget Changes
- "Based on current performance, we recommend increasing your monthly investment from $[X] to $[Y]. At this level, we project an additional [Z] customers per month while maintaining current efficiency."
- "We identified an opportunity to achieve your goals more efficiently. By reallocating $[AMOUNT] from [CHANNEL_A] to [CHANNEL_B], we project a [X]% improvement in overall return."

## Recommendation Language for Clients

### Standard Format
```
"Based on [EVIDENCE], we recommend [ACTION].
This should [EXPECTED_OUTCOME].
The investment required is [AMOUNT/EFFORT].
If approved, we can implement by [DATE]."
```

### Presenting Options
- "We see two paths forward and would like your input. Option A [DESCRIPTION] with [EXPECTED_RESULT]. Option B [DESCRIPTION] with [EXPECTED_RESULT]. We recommend [OPTION] because [REASON], but both are viable."

### Requesting Decisions
- "To proceed with [INITIATIVE], we need your approval on [SPECIFIC_ITEM] by [DATE]. This will allow us to [LAUNCH/IMPLEMENT] in time for [GOAL/EVENT]."

## Narrative Templates

### Monthly Summary Narrative
> "In [MONTH], your advertising campaigns generated [X] new [CUSTOMERS/LEADS] at an average cost of $[CPA], representing a [X]% [IMPROVEMENT/CHANGE] compared to last month. Your total investment of $[SPEND] returned $[REVENUE] in attributed revenue -- a [ROAS]x return on investment. [HIGHLIGHT_SENTENCE]. Looking ahead, our priorities for [NEXT_MONTH] include [PRIORITY_1] and [PRIORITY_2]."

### Quarterly Review Narrative
> "Over the past quarter, your paid advertising program has [ACHIEVED/PROGRESSED_TOWARD] [GOAL]. Key metrics: [X] total [CUSTOMERS/LEADS] acquired at $[CPA], with $[REVENUE] in total revenue. Compared to last quarter, [KEY_IMPROVEMENT]. The primary driver of [POSITIVE_RESULT] was [STRATEGY/ACTION]. For Q[NEXT], we recommend [STRATEGIC_DIRECTION] to continue this momentum."

## Tone Calibration

### Confident but Not Arrogant
- Use: "We are confident this strategy will deliver results."
- Avoid: "We guarantee a 50% improvement."

### Transparent but Not Alarming
- Use: "We identified a challenge and have a clear plan to address it."
- Avoid: "Performance is in freefall and we're not sure what to do."

### Professional but Not Cold
- Use: "We're pleased to share strong results this month."
- Avoid: "Performance metrics exceeded predetermined KPI thresholds."

### Knowledgeable but Not Condescending
- Use: "For context, this metric measures how efficiently we're acquiring customers."
- Avoid: "As you may not know, CPA stands for cost per acquisition."
