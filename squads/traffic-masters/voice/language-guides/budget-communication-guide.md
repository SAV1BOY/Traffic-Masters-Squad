# Budget Communication Guide

## Purpose
How to discuss budget, spending, and financial decisions clearly and confidently.

## Presenting Budget Recommendations

### Framework
1. State the recommendation clearly
2. Provide the data supporting it
3. Show expected outcome
4. Acknowledge risks and mitigations

### Example
"We recommend increasing the Meta budget from $5K to $7K/month (+40%). Current CPA is $22 (target $30), and marginal CPA analysis shows we can scale to $7K before hitting diminishing returns. Expected result: ~90 additional conversions/month. Risk: CPM increases during Q4; we'll monitor weekly and adjust."

## Discussing Overspend
- Lead with the cause, not just the number
- Present corrective action already taken
- Provide prevention plan going forward

## Discussing Underspend
- Explain why budget was not fully deployed
- Usually: audience saturation, quality controls, or delivery issues
- Present plan to resolve (creative refresh, audience expansion)

## Justifying Spend Increases
- "Every additional $1 at current efficiency returns $X"
- "We're leaving $X in revenue on the table at current spend"
- "Competitor [data] suggests market opportunity at higher spend"

## Presenting Budget Cuts
- Frame as optimization, not failure
- "Reallocating from [underperforming] to [outperforming]"
- Show how the cut improves blended efficiency

## Tone Rules
- Always connect budget to business outcomes
- Never apologize for smart spending decisions
- Be transparent about waste — then show the fix
- Present options when the path isn't clear
- Use round numbers for budgets, precise numbers for metrics
