# Creative Feedback Language Guide

## Purpose
Standard language for providing constructive, actionable feedback on ad creative assets. Ensures feedback is specific, respectful, and performance-oriented.

---

## Feedback Principles

1. **Be specific** -- Point to the exact element, not the whole piece
2. **Be constructive** -- Every critique should include a suggested improvement
3. **Be data-informed** -- Reference performance data when available
4. **Be respectful** -- Critique the work, not the person
5. **Be actionable** -- The creator should know exactly what to change

---

## Feedback Structure

### The SBI+R Framework
```
SITUATION: Where/when is this creative being used?
BEHAVIOR: What specific element are you addressing?
IMPACT: What is the effect on performance or perception?
RECOMMENDATION: What should change?
```

## Hook Feedback

### Positive
- "The hook is strong -- it creates an immediate information gap that compels the viewer to keep watching."
- "This question-based hook tested well; the CTR is 1.8%, 50% above our benchmark."
- "The opening stat is attention-grabbing and relevant to the target audience's pain point."

### Constructive
- "The hook does not land until second 4. We need the key tension point in the first 2 seconds to prevent scroll-past."
- "The current hook is too generic ('Are you looking for...?'). Make it more specific to the audience segment: 'Still spending 10 hours a week on manual reporting?'"
- "The visual hook is strong, but the text overlay is competing with it. Remove the text and let the visual do the work in the first 3 seconds."

---

## Visual and Design Feedback

### Positive
- "The visual hierarchy is clear -- the eye naturally moves from hook to benefit to CTA."
- "The color contrast makes the CTA button impossible to miss."
- "The product demonstration is compelling -- you see the value instantly."

### Constructive
- "The visual is too cluttered. There are 4 competing messages on screen. Reduce to one primary message and one supporting element."
- "The text is too small to read on mobile at standard viewing distance. Increase headline size by at least 50%."
- "The product is not visible until the 8-second mark. For a product-focused ad, show the product within the first 3 seconds."
- "The thumbnail does not work at small sizes -- the detail is lost. Simplify the composition for feed display."

---

## Copy Feedback

### Positive
- "The copy is concise and benefit-driven. Every sentence earns its place."
- "The social proof element ('trusted by 10,000+') adds credibility exactly where the reader might hesitate."
- "The CTA is specific and value-oriented ('Get your free analysis' vs. 'Learn more')."

### Constructive
- "The primary text is too long for this placement -- it gets truncated after 125 characters on mobile. Front-load the key message."
- "The copy lists features, not benefits. Change 'AI-powered dashboard' to 'See your most important metrics in 10 seconds, powered by AI.'"
- "The headline is generic ('Best solution for you'). Make it specific: 'Cut your reporting time from 4 hours to 15 minutes.'"
- "The CTA is weak ('Submit'). Replace with an action-oriented CTA that reinforces value ('Get my free report')."

---

## Video-Specific Feedback

### Pacing
- "The pacing drops in the middle section (seconds 8-15). Tighten the edit -- cut 3-4 seconds of dead time."
- "The pacing is too fast -- the viewer doesn't have time to absorb the key benefit before the next point."
- "Strong pacing throughout. The transitions maintain energy and the CTA lands at the right moment."

### Audio
- "The music overpowers the voiceover between seconds 5-10. Lower the music bed by 30% during dialogue."
- "The voiceover tone is too corporate for TikTok. Re-record with a more conversational delivery."
- "Add captions -- 85% of mobile viewers watch without sound."

### Structure
- "The video follows the problem-solution-proof-CTA structure well. Clean execution."
- "The proof section is missing. After presenting the solution, add a testimonial or data point before the CTA."
- "The CTA comes too late (second 28 of 30). Move it to second 20 and repeat it at the end."

---

## Performance-Based Feedback

### Using Data to Guide Creative Decisions
- "This creative has a 2.1% CTR (strong) but $65 CPA (above target). The hook is working, but the post-click experience or audience alignment may be the issue -- not the creative itself."
- "CTR is 0.4%, well below our 1.0% benchmark, suggesting the hook is not resonating. Test a different opening approach."
- "The thumb-stop rate is 35% (excellent), but the hold rate drops to 15% -- viewers are stopping but not staying. The content after the hook needs to deliver on the promise faster."
- "This creative has been running for 21 days and CTR has declined 30% from peak. It is time for a refresh, not an iteration on this exact concept."

## Feedback Calibration

| Element | Strong | Acceptable | Needs Work |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | Stops the scroll, creates curiosity, relevant | Gets attention but could be sharper | Generic, slow, or unclear |
| **Message Clarity** | Value prop understood in 3 seconds | Value prop understood but takes too long | Confusing or buried message |
| **Visual Quality** | Professional, platform-appropriate, eye-catching | Adequate quality, nothing distracting | Low resolution, cluttered, off-brand |
| **Copy** | Concise, benefit-driven, specific | Clear but could be more compelling | Vague, feature-heavy, too long |
| **CTA** | Specific, urgent, value-reinforcing | Clear but generic | Missing, weak, or confusing |
| **Brand Consistency** | On-brand, recognizable | Acceptable brand representation | Off-brand or inconsistent |
| **Platform Fit** | Feels native to the platform | Acceptable for the platform | Clearly repurposed from another platform |

---

## Language to Avoid

| Avoid | Use Instead |
|---|---|
| "I don't like it." | "This element is not aligned with what the data shows works for this audience." |
| "It's boring." | "The pacing could be more dynamic -- consider tighter edits and a stronger hook." |
| "Start over." | "The core concept needs rethinking. Here is a new direction to explore." |
| "Make it pop." | "Increase the contrast between the headline and background. Use a bolder color for the CTA." |
| "This is bad." | "This has potential, but [specific element] needs refinement." |
| "I would never click on this." | "The hook may not resonate with our target audience because [reason]. Consider [alternative]." |
