# Scaling Language Guide

## Purpose
Standard language for discussing budget scaling decisions, growth strategies, and capacity discussions in paid traffic operations.

---

## Scaling Decision Language

### When Recommending Scale-Up

| Confidence Level | Language |
|---|---|
| **Ready to scale** | "Performance data supports scaling. CPA has been stable at $[X] for [PERIOD], below our $[Y] target. We recommend increasing budget by [Z]%." |
| **Cautiously optimistic** | "Early indicators are promising. We suggest a gradual increase of [X]% to validate that performance holds at higher spend." |
| **Testing phase** | "We need more data before scaling. Current sample size is [X] conversions over [Y] days. We recommend maintaining current budget for [Z] more days." |

### When Recommending Against Scaling

| Situation | Language |
|---|---|
| **Not enough data** | "We do not have sufficient data to scale confidently. We need [X] more conversions to establish a reliable baseline." |
| **Diminishing returns** | "Marginal CPA has increased to $[X], compared to average CPA of $[Y]. We are approaching the efficiency ceiling at this spend level." |
| **Performance instability** | "CPA has been volatile over the past [PERIOD], ranging from $[X] to $[Y]. We recommend stabilizing performance before increasing investment." |
| **Audience saturation** | "Frequency has reached [X] on our primary audience, indicating saturation. We recommend expanding audiences before increasing budget." |

---

## Describing Scale Dynamics

### Diminishing Returns
- "As we increase spend beyond $[AMOUNT], each incremental dollar becomes less efficient. Marginal CPA rises from $[A] to $[B] between the $[LOW] and $[HIGH] spend tiers."
- "We are seeing diminishing returns -- the last 20% of budget increase yielded only a 10% increase in conversions."
- "The efficiency curve suggests an optimal monthly spend of approximately $[AMOUNT], beyond which marginal returns decline significantly."

### Learning Phase
- "After the budget increase, the campaign will enter a learning phase. We expect performance to be volatile for 3-5 days before the algorithm re-optimizes."
- "The learning phase typically requires approximately 50 conversion events. At the new budget level, we estimate this will take [X] days."

### Capacity and Ceiling
- "Based on audience size and current frequency, this campaign has capacity for approximately $[AMOUNT] in monthly spend before saturation."
- "We estimate there is $[AMOUNT] of additional scalable spend available across all channels before hitting efficiency ceilings."

---

## Scaling Pace Language

### Conservative Scaling
- "We recommend a conservative approach: 10-15% budget increases every 5-7 days with performance verification between each increment."
- "Given the account's sensitivity to budget changes, we suggest scaling gradually to preserve the algorithm's optimization."

### Moderate Scaling
- "We recommend moderate scaling: 20% budget increases every 3-4 days, with daily monitoring during the adjustment period."
- "This pace balances growth urgency with performance stability."

### Aggressive Scaling
- "If rapid growth is the priority, we can increase budget by 30-40% in a single move, accepting a temporary CPA increase of 15-25% during the recalibration period."
- "Aggressive scaling is viable here because [REASON: large audience, strong creative pipeline, proven funnel]."

---

## Horizontal vs. Vertical Scaling

### Vertical Scaling (Increasing budget on existing campaigns)
- "We recommend vertical scaling -- increasing the daily budget on [CAMPAIGN] from $[A] to $[B]."
- "Vertical scaling is appropriate here because the audience is large enough to absorb additional spend without saturation."

### Horizontal Scaling (Launching parallel campaigns)
- "We recommend horizontal scaling -- launching new campaigns targeting [NEW_AUDIENCES] with the proven creative set."
- "Horizontal scaling reduces the risk of resetting the learning phase on our best-performing campaigns."

### Combined
- "Our scaling plan combines both approaches: vertical scaling on Campaign A (+20%) and horizontal scaling via a new Campaign B targeting [AUDIENCE]."

---

## Risk Language for Scaling

### Acknowledging Risk
- "The primary risk of scaling at this pace is a temporary CPA increase during the learning recalibration. Based on historical data, we expect this to normalize within [PERIOD]."
- "Scaling introduces the risk of audience saturation. We mitigate this by monitoring frequency daily and having audience expansion ready."

### Mitigation Language
- "We have a rollback plan: if CPA exceeds $[THRESHOLD] for 48+ hours post-increase, we will revert to the previous budget level."
- "To de-risk this scaling move, we are simultaneously launching new creative to ensure freshness at higher exposure levels."

---

## Communicating Scale Results

### Positive Outcome
- "The budget increase from $[A] to $[B] has been absorbed well. CPA is $[X], within [Y]% of the pre-scale baseline, and conversion volume increased [Z]%."
- "Scaling has been successful. We have increased spend [X]% while maintaining CPA within target. Ready for the next increment."

### Negative Outcome
- "The recent budget increase has resulted in a CPA increase from $[A] to $[B]. We have implemented [ACTION] and are monitoring for stabilization."
- "We have reached the efficiency ceiling at $[AMOUNT]/day. Further increases produce marginal CPA above $[THRESHOLD]. We recommend capping spend here and focusing on efficiency optimization."

### Mixed Outcome
- "Volume increased [X]% as planned, but CPA rose [Y]% -- higher than our expected [Z]% tolerance. The trade-off may be acceptable if volume is the priority. We recommend holding at this level for [PERIOD] before deciding on the next increment."

---

## Example Scaling Communication

> "Based on the last 14 days of stable performance (CPA: $42, target: $50, ROAS: 4.5x), we are confident this campaign can absorb additional spend efficiently. Our recommendation is to increase the daily budget from $200 to $240 (a 20% increase). At this level, we project an additional 4-5 daily conversions, maintaining a CPA in the $42-$48 range. The risk of temporary CPA elevation during the 3-5 day learning adjustment is manageable, and we have a rollback threshold set at $55 CPA for 48 consecutive hours. If this increment succeeds, we plan to execute a second 20% increase the following week."
