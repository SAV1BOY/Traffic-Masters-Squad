# Report Writing Guide

## Purpose
Standards for writing performance reports and client communications.

## Report Structure
1. **Executive Summary** (2-3 sentences): Overall performance and key takeaway
2. **Key Metrics Table**: Numbers-first, comparison to target and prior period
3. **What Worked**: Top 2-3 wins with data
4. **What Needs Attention**: Top 2-3 challenges with data
5. **Actions Taken**: What we did this period
6. **Next Steps**: What we plan to do next period

## Writing Rules
- Lead with the most important finding
- Use data first, interpretation second
- Include comparison context (vs target, vs prior period, vs benchmark)
- Keep paragraphs to 2-3 sentences maximum
- Use bullet points for lists of 3+ items
- Bold key numbers and insights

## Tone
- Confident but honest
- Proactive — present problems with solutions
- Concise — respect the reader's time
- Professional — no slang or overly casual language

## Avoid
- Burying bad news at the end
- Reporting without analysis (data dump)
- Using metrics without definitions
- Making excuses without action plans
- Overly long reports (1-2 pages max for weekly, 3-5 for monthly)

## Example Opening
"This week, blended CPA improved to $28 (-12% WoW), driven by the new UGC creative batch that outperformed our control by 35%. Total spend was $12,400, pacing at 98% of target. ROAS held steady at 3.4x, exceeding our 3.0x guardrail."

## Templates
- Weekly report: 1 page, key metrics + actions
- Monthly report: 2-3 pages, deeper analysis + strategy
- Quarterly report: 3-5 pages, strategic review + planning
