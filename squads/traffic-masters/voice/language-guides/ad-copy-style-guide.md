# Ad Copy Style Guide

## Purpose
Standards for writing ad copy across all platforms.

## Core Principles
1. **Clarity over cleverness** — The reader should understand immediately
2. **Benefit over feature** — What it does for them, not what it is
3. **Specific over vague** — "Save 3 hours/week" not "save time"
4. **Active voice** — "We built" not "It was built"
5. **Second person** — "You" and "Your" for direct connection

## Copy Length by Platform
| Platform | Ideal Length | Max |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| Meta Primary Text | 100-150 chars | 500+ chars |
| Meta Headline | 25-30 chars | 40 chars |
| Google Headline | 25-30 chars | 30 chars |
| Google Description | 80-90 chars | 90 chars |
| TikTok Text | 50-80 chars | 100 chars |
| LinkedIn Text | 150-300 chars | 600 chars |

## Formatting Rules
- Use line breaks to improve readability (Meta, LinkedIn)
- Emojis: sparingly on Meta/TikTok, never on Google/LinkedIn B2B
- Numbers: use digits ("3 steps") not words ("three steps")
- Capitalization: Title Case for headlines, sentence case for body

## Power Words
- Action: get, start, join, discover, unlock, build
- Urgency: now, today, limited, last chance, before
- Value: free, save, proven, guaranteed, exclusive
- Emotion: transform, breakthrough, secret, insider

## Words to Avoid
- "Buy" in cold traffic copy (too direct)
- "Cheap" (use "affordable" or "value")
- Superlatives without proof ("best", "fastest", "#1")
- Jargon the audience won't understand

## CTA Best Practices
- Match CTA to funnel stage (Learn More for TOFU, Buy Now for BOFU)
- Start with a verb
- Create micro-commitment ("See how" vs "Buy now")
- Be specific ("Get Your Free Audit" vs "Submit")
