# Influencer & UGC Sourcing — Guide

## Purpose
How to find, vet, and brief creators for UGC ad content.

## Sourcing Platforms
- **TikTok Creator Marketplace:** Official platform, verified metrics
- **Insense:** UGC marketplace with ad rights included
- **Billo:** Quick UGC videos from vetted creators
- **Trend.io:** Curated creator network
- **Collabstr:** Marketplace for influencer partnerships
- **Manual outreach:** DM creators whose style fits your brand

## Vetting Criteria

| Criteria | What to Check |
|----------|--------------|
| Authenticity | Engagement rate > 3%, real comments |
| Content quality | Lighting, audio, energy, storytelling |
| Audience fit | Demographics match your ICP |
| Past brand work | Check for competitor conflicts |
| Responsiveness | Communication speed and professionalism |
| Ad rights | Willing to grant usage rights for paid ads |

## Brief Template
1. Brand overview (1 paragraph)
2. Product/service description
3. Target audience description
4. Content requirements (format, duration, deliverables)
5. Key talking points (3 max)
6. Mandatory mentions (CTA, promo code, URL)
7. Do's and don'ts
8. Reference videos (2-3 examples of desired style)
9. Deadline and compensation

## Budget Ranges (2025-2026)
- Nano (1-10K followers): $100-300/video
- Micro (10-100K): $300-1,000/video
- Mid (100K-500K): $1,000-5,000/video
- Macro (500K+): $5,000-25,000/video
