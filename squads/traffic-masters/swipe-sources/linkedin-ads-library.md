# LinkedIn Ads Library — Mining Guide

## How to Access
- Visit any company's LinkedIn page
- Click "Posts" > "Ads" tab
- Shows all active sponsored content

## Mining Process
1. List target competitors and industry leaders
2. Visit each company page on LinkedIn
3. Navigate to Posts > Ads tab
4. Screenshot and catalog each active ad
5. Note format, copy, CTA, and creative approach
6. Repeat monthly to track rotation

## What to Catalog
- **Ad format:** Single image, carousel, video, document, message
- **Copy length:** Short (1-2 lines) vs long (paragraph+)
- **Tone:** Professional, thought-leadership, direct response
- **CTA:** Lead gen form vs website vs message
- **Offer:** Content download, demo, webinar, free trial
- **Creative style:** Branded, data-driven, people-focused

## Key Patterns to Watch
- Which formats dominate in your industry
- Copy length trends (LinkedIn tends toward longer copy)
- Whether competitors use Lead Gen Forms or drive to site
- Document/carousel ads gaining traction
- Thought leadership vs direct selling ratio

## Limitations
- Only shows currently active ads
- No performance data visible
- Limited historical data
- Some companies restrict ad visibility

## Pro Tips
- Follow competitor company pages for feed-level ad viewing
- Check both company page ads AND individual exec sponsored posts
- LinkedIn Document ads (PDF carousels) often have highest engagement
- Note if competitors use conversation ads (message format)
