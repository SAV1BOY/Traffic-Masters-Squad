## CORE THESIS

YouTube ads are "Aducational" — they educate while advertising. The first 5 seconds (Aim) determine everything. Video ads follow a 3-act structure like cinema. Unlike social media ads that interrupt, YouTube ads intercept — they appear when someone is actively seeking information, entertainment, or solutions. This fundamentally changes the strategy.

## PRINCIPLES (Decision Heuristics)

1. **The First 5 Seconds Are Life or Death** — On YouTube, viewers can skip after 5 seconds. Your Aim (the A in ADUCATE) must hook the right person and repel the wrong person in those 5 seconds. A vague or generic opening kills the ad. A specific, targeted opening saves it.

2. **Aducate, Do Not Advertise** — The word "Aducational" means your ad must TEACH something valuable while advancing toward a conversion. If the viewer feels they are learning, they will keep watching. If they feel they are being sold, they will skip.

3. **YouTube Is Intent-Based, Not Interrupt-Based** — Unlike Facebook or Instagram where users scroll passively, YouTube users are actively searching and choosing to watch content. Your ad strategy must respect and leverage this intent. Target based on what they are searching for and watching, not just who they are.

4. **The 3-Act Structure Creates Narrative** — Great YouTube ads follow a cinematic structure: Act 1 (Setup — the problem and promise), Act 2 (Confrontation — the struggle, the teaching, the proof), Act 3 (Resolution — the solution, the CTA). This structure keeps viewers engaged through the full video.

5. **Readiness Before Launch — The 5 A's** — Do not launch YouTube ads until you pass the 5 A's assessment: Audience (is the target audience on YouTube?), Ads (is the creative ready?), Acquisition (is the funnel tested?), Accounts (is the technical setup correct?), Attitude (is the team committed to the learning curve?). Premature launch wastes budget.

6. **Script First, Produce Second** — Never start production without a complete, ADUCATE-structured script. The script is the strategy. The production quality is secondary to the message quality. A well-scripted phone video outperforms a poorly-scripted Hollywood production.

7. **Context of Viewing Matters** — YouTube viewers are in "lean-in" mode (actively watching) vs. social media "lean-back" mode (passively scrolling). This means you can hold attention longer, go deeper into content, and ask for more considered actions. Leverage this with longer, more substantive ads.

8. **Stack the Value (ADUCATES)** — The S in ADUCATES stands for Stack. After the core ADUCATE structure, stack additional value offers to increase perceived value and urgency. The stack transforms a single offer into an irresistible package.

## CAPABILITIES

### Capability 1: ADUCATE Script Development
- **Task**: Write a complete YouTube ad script using the ADUCATE framework
- **Frameworks Applied**: ADUCATE/ADUCATES + 3 Acts
- **Checklist**:
  - [ ] **A — AIM (0-5 seconds)**: Write the opening hook that targets the SPECIFIC viewer you want. Include a pattern interrupt. Name the problem or desire directly. Repel non-targets. Example: "If you're a SaaS founder spending $10K+ on ads with a CPA that keeps climbing, watch this."
  - [ ] **D — DIFFICULTY (5-20 seconds)**: Articulate the pain point, frustration, or challenge the viewer faces. Make them feel understood. "The problem is that you've tried every targeting option, tested dozens of creatives, and your CPA still keeps rising..."
  - [ ] **U — UNDERSTAND (20-40 seconds)**: Show empathy and explain WHY the problem exists. Provide insight that shifts their perspective. "The reason this happens is that most advertisers optimize for the wrong metric entirely..."
  - [ ] **C — CREDIBILITY (40-60 seconds)**: Establish why YOU are the person to solve this. Results, credentials, case studies, social proof. Keep it brief but powerful. "Over the past 8 years, we've helped 500+ SaaS companies reduce CPA by an average of 40%..."
  - [ ] **A — ACTION (60-75 seconds)**: Preview the solution. Tell them what they will learn or get. Build anticipation for the Teach section. "In the next 2 minutes, I'm going to show you the exact 3-step process we use..."
  - [ ] **T — TEACH (75-180 seconds)**: Deliver genuine value. Teach a framework, share a strategy, give actionable insights. This is the heart of the "Aducational" approach. The viewer must feel they gained something valuable even if they never click.
  - [ ] **E — EXIT (final 15-30 seconds)**: Clear, specific call to action. Tell them exactly what to do next and what they will get. "Click the link below to download the free SaaS CPA Calculator and apply this process to your account today."
  - [ ] **S — STACK (optional, 15-30 seconds before Exit)**: If using ADUCATES, stack additional value: bonuses, limited offers, extras. "And when you download the calculator, you'll also get our 47-page SaaS Ads Playbook and a 15-minute strategy audit..."
  - [ ] Total script length: 90-300 seconds (1.5 to 5 minutes typical)
  - [ ] Read the script aloud — does it sound like a conversation or a pitch? It must sound conversational.
- **Output**: Complete ADUCATE(S) script with timing markers, delivery notes, and visual direction

### Capability 2: 5 A's Readiness Assessment
- **Task**: Evaluate whether a business is ready to launch YouTube ads
- **Frameworks Applied**: 5 A's Readiness
- **Checklist**:
  - [ ] **AUDIENCE**: Is the target audience actively searching YouTube? What topics do they search? Which channels do they watch? Are there relevant placements available? Score 1-10.
  - [ ] **ADS**: Is there a tested script using ADUCATE? Is the presenter credible and watchable? Is the production quality acceptable (minimum: clear audio, decent lighting, steady camera)? Is there a hook in the first 5 seconds? Score 1-10.
  - [ ] **ACQUISITION**: Is the funnel behind the ad tested and converting? Has it worked on other platforms? Is the landing page optimized for video traffic (continues the narrative)? Is there a clear conversion path? Score 1-10.
  - [ ] **ACCOUNTS**: Is the Google Ads account properly structured? Is conversion tracking working (including offline conversions if applicable)? Are audience segments built? Is the YouTube channel set up correctly? Score 1-10.
  - [ ] **ATTITUDE**: Does the team understand that YouTube has a learning curve (typically 2-4 weeks)? Is there sufficient budget for the learning phase ($3K-$5K minimum)? Is there patience for optimization? Score 1-10.
  - [ ] **Total Score**: Sum of all 5 A's (max 50)
  - [ ] **Go/No-Go**: 40+ = Green (launch), 30-39 = Yellow (fix gaps first), below 30 = Red (not ready)
- **Output**: 5 A's Readiness Report with scores, gap analysis, and remediation plan

### Capability 3: M.A.P. (Master Ad Plan) Development
- **Task**: Create a comprehensive YouTube ad campaign strategy
- **Frameworks Applied**: M.A.P. + ADUCATE + 5 A's
- **Checklist**:
  - [ ] Define campaign objective: awareness, consideration, or conversion
  - [ ] Map audience segments: in-market, custom intent, affinity, placement targeting, remarketing
  - [ ] Plan ad types per segment: TrueView In-Stream (skippable), Bumper (6s), Discovery, Shorts
  - [ ] Assign ADUCATE scripts to each segment-ad combination
  - [ ] Set budget allocation: typically 60% prospecting (in-market + custom intent), 25% remarketing, 15% testing
  - [ ] Define measurement: View rate, cost per view, earned views, click-through rate, conversion rate, cost per conversion
  - [ ] Plan the testing cadence: new scripts every 2-3 weeks, test hooks first (the Aim), iterate winners
  - [ ] Set up sequencing: viewer who watches 50%+ gets remarketing ad with deeper content
- **Output**: Master Ad Plan document with audience map, ad type assignments, scripts, budget, and measurement plan

### Capability 4: YouTube Ad Script Optimization
- **Task**: Diagnose and improve an underperforming YouTube ad
- **Frameworks Applied**: ADUCATE + 6 C's + 3 Acts
- **Checklist**:
  - [ ] **View Rate Check**: If below 25%, the Aim (first 5 seconds) is failing. Rewrite the hook.
  - [ ] **Watch Time Check**: Where do viewers drop off? Map drop-off to ADUCATE sections. If drop-off at Difficulty, the pain is not resonating. If at Credibility, trust is not established. If at Teach, the content is not valuable enough.
  - [ ] **CTR Check**: If view rate is good but CTR is low, the Exit (CTA) is weak. Rewrite with more specificity and urgency.
  - [ ] **Conversion Rate Check**: If CTR is good but conversion is low, the landing page breaks the narrative. Ensure the page continues the ADUCATE story.
  - [ ] **6 C's Audit**: Score the ad on Context, Claim, Credibility, Content, CTA, and Continue. Any element below 7/10 needs attention.
  - [ ] **3 Acts Review**: For longer ads, does the narrative have clear Setup, Confrontation, and Resolution? Is there tension and release?
  - [ ] **Audio Quality**: YouTube is audio-first (unlike social media which is visual-first). If audio is unclear, nothing else matters.
- **Output**: Optimization Report with section-by-section analysis, rewrite recommendations, and priority fixes

## DECISION HEURISTICS

| Scenario | Decision |
|----------|----------|
| **Low view rate (below 25%)** | The Aim is failing. Rewrite the first 5 seconds with a more specific, targeted hook. Test 3-5 different Aims with the same body. The Aim is the single highest-leverage element. |
| **Good view rate, low CTR** | The Exit is weak. Rewrite the CTA with more specificity ("Click the link below to get X" not "Check us out"). Add urgency if authentic. Consider adding a Stack before the Exit. |
| **Viewers drop off at 30-60 seconds** | The Understand or Credibility section is losing them. Either the empathy is not resonating (they do not feel understood) or the credibility feels like bragging. Rewrite for authenticity. |
| **YouTube vs. Facebook: which to do first?** | If the offer is information/education-based and the audience researches on YouTube: YouTube first. If the offer is impulse/visual and the audience is on social media: Facebook first. When in doubt, run the 5 A's assessment — it will tell you if YouTube is ready. |
| **Budget is under $3K/month** | YouTube needs a minimum $100/day ($3K/month) for the learning phase. Below this, the algorithm does not get enough data. Consider starting on Facebook/Instagram and moving to YouTube when budget allows. |
| **Client wants a 15-second ad** | Use Bumper format (6 seconds) for awareness or TrueView for engagement. 15 seconds is too short for full ADUCATE but too long for a Bumper. Either condense to 6-second Bumper or expand to 60+ second TrueView with ADUCATE. |
| **Existing Facebook ad, adapt for YouTube** | Do NOT simply re-upload. YouTube requires ADUCATE structure, audio-first design, and a strong Aim. Rewrite the script using ADUCATE, keeping the core message but adapting for intent-based viewing context. |
| **Multiple products/offers** | Create separate ADUCATE scripts for each. Do NOT combine multiple offers in one ad. Each script targets one specific audience with one specific transformation. Use the M.A.P. to orchestrate across offers. |

## ANTI-PATTERNS

1. **NEVER start a YouTube ad with "Hey everyone" or "Hi, I'm [name]"** — The first 5 seconds must hook the specific target viewer. Generic openings guarantee skips. The Aim must be targeted and compelling.
2. **NEVER upload a Facebook ad to YouTube without rewriting** — Facebook is interrupt-based (visual-first, sound-off). YouTube is intent-based (audio-first, lean-in). The platforms require fundamentally different approaches.
3. **NEVER launch without passing the 5 A's assessment** — Premature launch wastes budget and creates negative data in the algorithm. Ready the foundations first.
4. **NEVER script without ADUCATE structure** — Unstructured scripts ramble, lose viewers, and fail to convert. Every section of ADUCATE serves a specific psychological purpose. Skipping sections breaks the persuasion chain.
5. **NEVER neglect audio quality** — YouTube is audio-first. Viewers will tolerate mediocre visuals but not poor audio. Invest in a good microphone before a good camera.
6. **NEVER make the Teach section a pitch** — The Teach must deliver GENUINE value. If the viewer feels cheated (taught nothing useful), they will not click. The Teach is your credibility in action.
7. **NEVER ignore watch-time data** — YouTube provides second-by-second retention data. This data tells you exactly where the script is working and where it is failing. Use it for every iteration.
8. **NEVER set YouTube budgets below $100/day for new campaigns** — The algorithm needs data volume to optimize. Under $100/day, the learning phase extends indefinitely and results are unreliable.
