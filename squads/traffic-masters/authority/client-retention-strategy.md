# Client Retention Strategy

## Purpose
Retain clients through exceptional results, communication, and value delivery.

## Retention Pillars

### 1. Consistent Results
- Meet or exceed KPI targets consistently
- Proactive optimization (don't wait for problems)
- Document and communicate wins regularly

### 2. Exceptional Communication
- Weekly scorecards delivered on time
- Proactive alerts (don't let clients discover problems)
- Monthly strategy calls with insights and recommendations
- Respond to questions within 4 business hours

### 3. Strategic Value
- Quarterly strategy reviews with fresh ideas
- Competitive intelligence updates
- Industry trend briefings relevant to their business
- Proactive recommendations beyond current scope

### 4. Relationship Building
- Understand their business beyond just ads
- Know their stakeholders and internal dynamics
- Celebrate wins together
- Be honest about challenges (trust > comfort)

## Churn Risk Indicators

| Signal | Risk Level | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Missed KPI targets 2+ months | High | Strategy review + action plan |
| Client disengaged from calls | Medium | Re-engage with fresh insights |
| Competitor outreach mentioned | High | Demonstrate unique value |
| Budget reduction requested | Medium | Show ROI justification |
| New decision-maker | High | Onboard and build relationship |

## Retention Cadence
- Weekly: Performance scorecard + proactive communication
- Monthly: Strategy call + report + competitive update
- Quarterly: Business review + strategy refresh
- Annually: Year-in-review + next-year planning

## Key Metrics
- Client retention rate (target: 90%+ annual)
- Client satisfaction score (NPS or survey)
- Average client lifetime (target: 12+ months)
- Revenue retention rate (including upsells)
