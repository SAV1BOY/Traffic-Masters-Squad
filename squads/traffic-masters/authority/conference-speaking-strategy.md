# Conference Speaking Strategy

## Purpose
Secure and deliver impactful conference speaking opportunities.

## Target Conferences
- Traffic & Conversion Summit
- Social Media Marketing World
- Google Marketing Live
- INBOUND (HubSpot)
- Ad World
- Affiliate Summit
- Industry-specific conferences (by vertical)
- Brazilian marketing conferences (RD Summit, Fire Festival)

## Speaking Topic Ideas
1. Creative testing frameworks that scale
2. Cross-platform traffic strategies for [year]
3. How to build a $X ad account from scratch
4. The death of targeting: creative-first strategies
5. Attribution in a privacy-first world
6. Scaling lessons from $XM in managed spend

## Application Strategy
- Apply 6-12 months before event
- Include case study data and specific results
- Offer unique angle not covered by other speakers
- Include professional headshot and bio
- Reference past speaking experience

## Presentation Preparation
1. Follow presentation-standards.md
2. Lead with actionable insights, not theory
3. Include real data and case studies
4. Practice timing (respect the slot)
5. Prepare for Q&A with backup data

## Follow-Up
- Share slides and resources post-presentation
- Connect with attendees on LinkedIn
- Create content from presentation (blog, video clips)
- Collect testimonials from audience

## Goal
- 2-4 speaking engagements per year
- Build reputation as go-to expert in traffic strategy
- Generate inbound inquiries from presentations
