# Community Engagement Strategy

## Purpose
Build authority through active participation in industry communities.

## Target Communities

### Online Communities
- Facebook Groups: FBAdsHacks, Media Buyer Society
- Reddit: r/PPC, r/DigitalMarketing, r/FacebookAds
- Slack: Demand Curve, Superside, industry-specific
- Discord: Marketing communities
- X/Twitter: #PPCChat, #FacebookAds, marketing threads

### Professional Networks
- LinkedIn industry groups
- Platform partner communities
- Local marketing meetups
- Industry associations

### Brazilian Communities
- Comunidade Sobral de Trafego
- Grupos de gestores de trafego
- Marketing Digital Brasil communities

## Engagement Strategy

### Daily (10-15 min)
- Answer 1-2 questions in communities
- Share a quick insight or observation
- Engage with industry leaders' content

### Weekly (30 min)
- Write 1 substantial community post
- Share a framework, tool, or finding
- Connect with 3-5 new industry professionals

### Monthly
- Host or participate in community event (AMA, live Q&A)
- Publish community-specific content
- Review community engagement metrics

## Rules of Engagement
1. Provide value first, promote never (or last)
2. Be genuinely helpful, not self-serving
3. Share real data and experience (not theory)
4. Acknowledge when you don't know something
5. Give credit to others' ideas
6. Be consistent — show up regularly

## Metrics
- Community reputation score / karma
- DMs received asking for help/referrals
- Inbound inquiries from community presence
- Relationships built with peers
