## Key Frameworks and Methodologies

### The Solutions 8 PMax Methodology
Kasim's approach to Performance Max campaigns:

**Asset Group Strategy:**
- Organize asset groups by product category or audience intent
- Each asset group should have a coherent theme
- Provide 5+ headlines, 5+ descriptions, 5+ images, and at least 1 video per asset group
- Use high-quality images that showcase products in context
- Create custom video content rather than relying on auto-generated

**Audience Signals:**
- Layer audience signals as directional guides, not constraints
- Use custom segments based on competitor URLs and relevant search terms
- Include first-party data (customer lists, website visitors) as signals
- Add in-market and affinity audiences relevant to the product category
- Understand that signals influence but do not control targeting

**Brand Exclusions:**
- Exclude brand terms from PMax to prevent branded search cannibalization
- Run a separate branded search campaign for full control and accurate measurement
- Monitor search terms insights for branded query leakage

**Feed Optimization for E-commerce:**
- Product feed quality is the most important PMax input for e-commerce
- Optimize titles: include brand, product type, key attributes, and target keywords
- Use high-quality product images (multiple angles, lifestyle images)
- Ensure pricing and availability are accurate and updated
- Implement custom labels for strategic product grouping

### Google Ads Account Architecture
Kasim's recommended account structure for 2025-2026:

| Campaign | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Brand Search | Search (exact match) | Protect branded terms, measure brand demand |
| Non-Brand Search | Search (broad match + Smart Bidding) | Capture non-branded intent |
| Performance Max | PMax | Cross-channel prospecting and remarketing |
| YouTube | Video Action / Demand Gen | Mid-funnel consideration, direct response |
| Demand Gen | Demand Gen | YouTube, Discover, Gmail engagement |

### The ROAS Scaling Framework
Kasim's approach to scaling Google Ads profitably:
1. **Establish baseline ROAS** at current spend level (minimum 2 weeks stable data)
2. **Set target ROAS** slightly below current (accepting marginal efficiency loss for growth)
3. **Increase budget 15-20%** and allow 7-10 days for optimization
4. **Monitor blended account ROAS** (not just individual campaign ROAS)
5. **If ROAS holds:** increase budget again
6. **If ROAS drops below minimum threshold:** hold budget and optimize creative/feeds
7. **Track marginal ROAS** (ROAS of incremental spend, not average ROAS)

### YouTube Ads for Google Advertisers
Kasim advocates for YouTube as a Google Ads extension, not a separate channel:
- Use Google's custom intent audiences (based on Google search data) for YouTube targeting
- Run Video Action campaigns with conversion-focused bidding
- Connect YouTube activity to Google Search demand generation
- Measure YouTube's impact through branded search lift, not just direct attribution
- Create YouTube content that addresses search queries visually

## Relevance to Paid Traffic Management

### Key Takeaways from His Work
1. **PMax requires strategy:** Despite being "automated," PMax performance depends heavily on asset quality, audience signals, and feed optimization
2. **Protect branded search:** Always run branded search separately from PMax
3. **Feed quality is king:** For e-commerce Google Ads, product feed optimization is the highest-leverage activity
4. **Transparency builds trust:** Sharing your methodology openly attracts better clients and elevates the industry
5. **Google Ads deserves primary channel consideration:** For businesses with search demand, Google Ads often offers the highest-intent traffic available
6. **YouTube integrates with Search:** YouTube advertising through Google Ads creates unique synergies not available on standalone video platforms
