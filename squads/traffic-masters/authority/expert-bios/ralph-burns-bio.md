# Ralph Burns - Expert Bio

## Professional Summary

Ralph Burns is the founder and CEO of Tier 11, a digital marketing agency specializing in Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google advertising. He is co-host of the Perpetual Traffic podcast, one of the most influential paid traffic podcasts in the industry. Ralph is known for his agency-operator perspective, bringing real-world campaign management experience to his educational content.

## Key Frameworks and Methodologies

### The Tier 11 Campaign Architecture
Ralph's agency operates with a structured campaign framework:

**Prospecting Tier:**
- Broad targeting campaigns (Advantage+ for e-commerce)
- Interest-based testing campaigns
- Lookalike campaigns (seeded from high-value customers)

**Retargeting Tier:**
- Website visitor retargeting (segmented by recency and page depth)
- Engagement retargeting (video viewers, social engagers)
- Cart and checkout abandonment recovery

**Retention Tier:**
- Customer re-engagement campaigns
- Cross-sell and upsell campaigns
- Loyalty and referral campaigns

### Data-First Media Buying
Ralph emphasizes a data-first approach that includes:
1. **Tracking audit before launch:** Verify pixel, CAPI, and event setup before spending
2. **Baseline establishment:** Document current performance before making changes
3. **Statistical significance:** Require meaningful data before declaring winners or losers
4. **Business-level reporting:** Report in terms of revenue, profit, and LTV, not just CPA and ROAS
5. **Third-party validation:** Cross-reference platform data with business backend

### Account Simplification
In the Advantage+ and PMax era, Ralph advocates for:
- Fewer campaigns with larger budgets
- Broader targeting to give algorithms room to optimize
- Creative volume as the primary optimization lever
- Patience with learning phases and algorithm adjustment periods
- Trusting the algorithm while verifying with external data

## Relevance to Paid Traffic Management

### Why He Matters
Ralph brings the rare perspective of someone who runs an agency (managing real client money and expectations) while also educating the broader market. His insights are grounded in daily campaign management across dozens of accounts, making his recommendations practical and battle-tested.

### Key Takeaways from His Work
1. **Agency operations inform strategy:** The best media buying happens within well-structured agency processes
2. **Data integrity is non-negotiable:** Tracking and measurement must be verified before strategic decisions
3. **Client communication matters:** Translating platform metrics into business outcomes is a core media buyer skill
4. **Adapt quickly:** The Perpetual Traffic podcast documents Ralph's own real-time adaptation to platform changes
5. **Simplify account structure:** In the AI-driven era, simpler campaigns often outperform complex ones
