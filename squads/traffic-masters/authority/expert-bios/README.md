## How to Use These Bios

1. **Learning:** Each bio includes key frameworks, publications, and educational resources. Follow these to deepen expertise in specific areas.
2. **Reference:** Cite expert methodologies when presenting strategies to clients or team members.
3. **Networking:** Understanding industry leaders helps identify speaking opportunities, podcast collaborations, and partnership possibilities.
4. **Benchmarking:** Compare your approaches against frameworks developed by leading practitioners.

---

## Adding New Expert Bios

When adding a new expert bio, include:
- Full professional background and career trajectory
- Specific frameworks or methodologies they have developed
- Notable publications, courses, or educational content
- Key accomplishments with quantifiable results where possible
- Relevance to paid traffic management
- Links to primary resources (website, social, publications)
