## Key Frameworks and Methodologies

### The Sobral Method
Pedro's systematic approach to Facebook Ads management encompasses:

**1. Campaign Structure Philosophy**
- Clear separation of objectives: prospecting, retargeting, and remarketing in distinct campaigns
- Budget allocation rules between campaign types
- Naming conventions for organized account management
- Testing campaigns isolated from scaling campaigns
- Systematic approach to campaign organization that scales across accounts

**2. Creative Testing at Volume**
Pedro emphasizes high-volume creative testing:
- Launch multiple creative variants simultaneously
- Kill underperformers quickly (48-72 hours with sufficient data)
- Document winning patterns (hooks, formats, angles that recur)
- Build a creative library organized by performance tier
- Iterate on winners rather than starting from scratch each time

**3. Metric-Driven Decision Making**
Specific metrics and thresholds for every decision:
- CTR thresholds for creative viability
- CPA thresholds for scaling decisions
- Frequency caps for audience health
- Budget rules for scaling pace
- Clear stop-loss rules to prevent overspending on underperforming campaigns

**4. The Daily Optimization Routine**
Pedro developed a structured daily routine for media buyers:
- Morning: Review overnight performance, check for anomalies
- Mid-day: Evaluate creative performance, pause underperformers
- Afternoon: Launch new tests, adjust budgets based on day's performance
- End of day: Document learnings, plan next day's actions
- Weekly: Full account review, creative production planning, reporting

### The Offer-Creative-Audience Triangle
Pedro's framework for diagnosing performance issues:
- **Offer:** Is the product/service/price compelling to the market?
- **Creative:** Is the ad communicating the offer effectively?
- **Audience:** Are we reaching people who care about this offer?

When performance declines, diagnose in this order:
1. First, check if the offer is still competitive (external factors)
2. Second, check if creative has fatigued (ad-level metrics)
3. Third, check if the audience is saturated (frequency and reach metrics)

Most media buyers jump to audience adjustments first, but Pedro teaches that offer and creative issues are more common causes of decline.

### Retargeting Architecture
Pedro's structured approach to retargeting:

| Audience | Timeframe | Message | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cart abandoners | 1-3 days | Urgency, reminders | Highest |
| Product page viewers | 1-7 days | Benefits, testimonials | High |
| Content engagers | 1-14 days | Deeper education, offers | Medium |
| Social engagers | 1-14 days | Social proof, community | Medium |
| Website visitors (general) | 1-30 days | Brand reinforcement | Lower |

## Relevance to Paid Traffic Management

### Key Takeaways from His Work
1. **Community accelerates learning:** Peer learning among practitioners is often more valuable than one-way education
2. **Process over intuition:** Systematic routines and documented processes outperform ad hoc optimization
3. **Volume testing wins:** Testing many creative variants quickly identifies winners faster than testing a few carefully
4. **Diagnose in order:** Offer, creative, audience (in that order) when troubleshooting performance issues
5. **Execution is the differentiator:** The best strategy means nothing without disciplined daily execution
6. **Accessibility matters:** Making complex concepts simple and actionable creates more impact than sophisticated theory
