## Key Frameworks and Methodologies

### BPM (Business Process of Media buying)
Depesh's signature methodology treats media buying as a structured business process rather than an art. The BPM framework includes:

**1. Business Foundation**
- Define unit economics: COGS, target CPA, breakeven ROAS, LTV
- Establish the financial model before touching the ad platform
- Set clear KPIs tied to business outcomes, not vanity metrics
- Understand the product-market fit and competitive landscape

**2. Creative System**
- Systematic creative production pipeline
- Framework for developing hooks, angles, and creative concepts
- Testing methodology: test hooks first, then bodies, then CTAs
- Creative scoring system for objective performance evaluation
- Continuous creative production at scale

**3. Campaign Architecture**
- Simplified campaign structure optimized for the algorithm
- Testing campaigns for new creative and audiences
- Scaling campaigns for proven performers
- Retargeting campaigns with specific frequency and messaging controls
- Budget allocation rules between testing and scaling

**4. Scaling Protocol**
- Gradual budget increases (20-30% every 3-5 days)
- Performance thresholds for scaling decisions
- Clear triggers for pausing or reducing spend
- Horizontal scaling (new campaigns/ad sets) vs. vertical scaling (budget increases)
- Account-level monitoring to prevent over-spending

**5. Analysis and Optimization**
- Daily, weekly, and monthly review cadences
- Specific metrics to review at each cadence
- Decision frameworks for common scenarios (CPA spike, creative fatigue, platform issues)
- Reporting templates for stakeholders

### The 3x3 Creative Testing Framework
A method for efficiently testing creative concepts:
- **3 hooks** (different opening approaches to the same message)
- **3 bodies** (different middle sections with different proof/story/demonstration)
- **3 CTAs** (different closing actions)
- Test hooks first (highest impact on performance)
- Once winning hook is identified, test body variations
- Finally, test CTA variations
- This sequential approach isolates variables and builds on proven elements

### The Scaling Decision Matrix
A framework for making scaling decisions based on data:

| Metric Status | Action |
|---|---|
| CPA below target, volume increasing | Scale 20-30% budget |
| CPA at target, volume stable | Test new creative, maintain budget |
| CPA above target, volume stable | Analyze creative fatigue, test new creative |
| CPA above target, volume declining | Reduce budget to last profitable level, diagnose |
| CPA well below target (suspicious) | Verify tracking, check for algorithm anomalies |

## Key Contributions to the Industry

### Broad Targeting Advocacy
Depesh was among the early practitioners to document and advocate for broad targeting on Facebook Ads (before it became conventional wisdom). His testing and documentation showed that broad audiences with strong creative consistently matched or outperformed detailed targeting.

### Creative-First Philosophy
Before "creative is the new targeting" became an industry mantra, Depesh was teaching that creative quality and volume were the primary levers for Facebook Ads performance. His creative testing frameworks have been widely adopted.

### Process Over Talent
Depesh's BPM methodology emphasizes that great media buying is a process, not a talent. By codifying decisions, thresholds, and workflows, he made high-performance media buying teachable and replicable across teams.

## Relevance to Paid Traffic Management

### Key Takeaways from His Work
1. **Process is everything:** Build repeatable systems rather than relying on individual talent

3. **Creative is the variable:** In the broad targeting era, creative quality determines performance

5. **Test sequentially:** Isolate variables in creative testing for clear learnings
