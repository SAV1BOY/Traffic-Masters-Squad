# Podcast Guesting Strategy

## Purpose
Secure guest appearances on relevant podcasts to build authority and reach.

## Target Podcasts

### Marketing & Advertising
- Perpetual Traffic (Tier Media)
- Marketing School (Neil Patel & Eric Siu)
- Social Media Marketing Podcast (Michael Stelzner)
- The DigitalMarketer Podcast
- Paid Media Pros

### Business & Entrepreneurship
- My First Million
- The Game (Alex Hormozi)
- Marketing Against the Grain (HubSpot)

### Brazilian Market
- Podcast do Sobral
- Marketing Digital (various)

## Pitch Template
1. Who you are (credentials, results)
2. Topic you'd discuss (unique angle)
3. Why their audience cares (specific value)
4. Social proof (other appearances, audience size)
5. 3 talking points with hooks

## Preparation
- Research the podcast (listen to 3+ episodes)
- Prepare 3-5 key stories with specific data
- Have a lead magnet or CTA ready for listeners
- Test audio setup (quality microphone, quiet room)

## During the Episode
- Lead with stories and data, not theory
- Give actionable takeaways listeners can implement
- Reference the host's previous content (shows respect)
- Mention your lead magnet naturally

## Post-Episode
- Share on all social channels
- Send thank-you note to host
- Repurpose clips for social media content
- Track inbound inquiries from the appearance

## Goal
- 1-2 podcast appearances per month
- Build relationships with hosts for repeat invitations
