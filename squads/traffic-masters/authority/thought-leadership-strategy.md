# Thought Leadership Strategy

## Purpose
Build industry authority through consistent, valuable thought leadership content.

## Content Pillars
1. **Data-Driven Insights:** Share findings from campaign data (anonymized)
2. **Platform Strategy:** Tactical platform-specific expertise
3. **Industry Trends:** Analysis of industry shifts and their implications
4. **Creative Strategy:** Ad creative insights and frameworks
5. **Measurement & Attribution:** Tracking and analytics thought leadership

## Content Calendar

### Weekly
- 1 LinkedIn post (insight, framework, or data point)
- 1 comment/engagement on industry conversations

### Monthly
- 1 in-depth article or guide
- 1 case study or data analysis
- Engagement with industry publications

### Quarterly
- 1 comprehensive industry report or benchmark study
- 1 webinar or live session
- Conference submission (if applicable)

## Content Formats
- LinkedIn posts (insights and frameworks)
- Blog articles (detailed strategy guides)
- Video content (tutorials and analysis)
- Podcast appearances (guest expert)
- Conference presentations (speaking opportunities)
- Data reports (original research)

## Distribution Channels
- LinkedIn (primary — B2B authority)
- YouTube (tutorials and analysis)
- Industry newsletters (guest contributions)
- Podcasts (guest appearances)
- Twitter/X (real-time industry commentary)

## Measurement
- LinkedIn engagement rate and follower growth
- Website traffic from thought leadership content
- Inbound inquiries attributed to content
- Speaking invitation frequency
- Industry publication mentions
