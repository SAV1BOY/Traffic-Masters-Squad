# Playbooks — Index

## Purpose
Collection of step-by-step playbooks for common traffic scenarios.

## Playbook Structure
Each playbook follows this format:
1. Objective and success criteria
2. Prerequisites and requirements
3. Step-by-step execution guide
4. Decision points and criteria
5. Expected timeline
6. Metrics to track
7. Common pitfalls and solutions
