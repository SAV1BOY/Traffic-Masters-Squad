# Agency Brand Building

## Purpose
Build a recognizable and trusted brand for the traffic/media buying practice.

## Brand Positioning
- **What we do:** Drive profitable paid traffic at scale
- **Who we serve:** [Define target client profile]
- **How we're different:** [Unique methodology, results, approach]
- **What we stand for:** Data-driven decisions, creative excellence, transparency

## Brand Elements

### Core Message
- One-line positioning statement
- Elevator pitch (30 seconds)
- Detailed value proposition (1 paragraph)

### Proof Points
- Total ad spend managed
- Average client ROAS
- Client retention rate
- Number of platforms mastered
- Notable client results

### Brand Voice
- Data-driven but accessible
- Confident but not arrogant
- Results-focused
- Transparent about process and methodology

## Brand Building Activities
1. **Content marketing:** Regular thought leadership (see thought-leadership-strategy.md)
2. **Case studies:** Documented results (see case-study-creation-guide.md)
3. **Speaking:** Conference presentations (see conference-speaking-strategy.md)
4. **Community:** Engage in industry communities and forums
5. **Partnerships:** Platform partner programs (Meta, Google, TikTok)

## Platform Partner Programs
- Meta Business Partner
- Google Partner / Premier Partner
- TikTok Marketing Partner
- LinkedIn Marketing Partner

## Measurement
- Brand search volume (Google Trends)
- Inbound inquiry volume and quality
- Social media following and engagement
- Referral rate from existing clients
