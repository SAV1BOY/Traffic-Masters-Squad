# Proofs & Assets — Index

## Purpose
Organize proof assets used for authority building and client acquisition.

## Asset Categories

### Screenshots & Data
- Platform dashboard screenshots (anonymized)
- Before/after metric comparisons
- Scaling timeline visuals
- ROAS and CPA achievement records

### Testimonials
- Written testimonials from clients
- Video testimonials
- LinkedIn recommendations
- Case study quotes

### Media Mentions
- Publication features and articles
- Podcast appearance recordings/links
- Conference presentation recordings
- Industry award certificates

### Certifications
- Platform certification badges
- Course completion certificates
- Partner program status documentation

## File Naming Convention
`[type]_[source]_[date].[ext]`

Examples:
- `screenshot_meta-roas-3x_2026-03.png`
- `testimonial_client-x_2026-02.md`
- `cert_google-ads-search_2026.pdf`

## Usage Guidelines
- Always anonymize client data unless permission granted
- Include context/caption for every proof asset
- Keep screenshots recent (within 6 months)
- Update testimonials quarterly
- Store originals in high resolution
