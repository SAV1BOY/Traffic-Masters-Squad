# Cross-Platform Expansion Workflow
> **Type**: Workflow
> **Duration**: 4-6 weeks per new platform
> **Agents involved**: traffic-chief, media-buyer, ad-midas, pixel-specialist, performance-analyst

## Trigger
Primary platform reaches diminishing returns, or strategic decision to diversify traffic sources.

## Steps
1. Platform Selection → Agent: traffic-chief → Framework: Audience-Platform Fit → Output: Recommended platform based on audience demographics, intent signals, and competition
2. Competitive Research → Agent: traffic-chief → Framework: Ad Library Analysis → Output: Competitor presence, creative patterns, and estimated spend on target platform
3. Account Setup → Agent: media-buyer → Framework: Platform Onboarding → Output: Ad account created, payment configured, business verification complete
4. Tracking Setup → Agent: pixel-specialist → Framework: Cross-Platform Tracking → Output: Platform pixel installed, CAPI configured, UTMs standardized
5. Creative Adaptation → Agent: ad-midas → Framework: Platform-Native Specs → Output: Existing winning creatives adapted to new platform format and culture
6. Test Campaign → Agent: media-buyer → Framework: Minimum Viable Test → Output: Test campaign with 3-5 ad sets, budget for 50+ conversions
7. Performance Comparison → Agent: performance-analyst → Framework: Cross-Platform CPA Analysis → Output: New platform vs primary platform performance comparison
8. Optimization Cycle → Agent: media-buyer → Framework: Platform-Specific Optimization → Output: 2-4 weeks of optimization with platform-native best practices
9. Scale Decision → Agent: traffic-chief → Framework: Go/No-Go Matrix → Output: Decision to scale, iterate, or abandon new platform

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Platform-audience fit validated with data
- [ ] Creatives adapted to platform culture (not just resized)
- [ ] Tracking parity with primary platform
- [ ] Minimum test budget committed (typically 2-4 weeks of spend)
- [ ] Performance compared on equal attribution window
- [ ] Platform-specific policies reviewed and complied with

## Output
Cross-platform performance comparison report.
Decision on expansion with budget allocation recommendation.
Platform-specific playbook for ongoing management.

## Platform Priority Matrix
- High intent: Google Search, YouTube → best for bottom-funnel
- High reach: Meta, TikTok → best for top and mid-funnel
- Professional: LinkedIn → best for B2B
- Visual/shopping: Pinterest, Amazon → best for e-commerce
- Programmatic: DSPs → best for scale and retargeting

## Notes
- Never expand to a new platform before primary platform is profitable
- First 2 weeks on a new platform are learning — do not judge performance
- Each platform has its own creative culture; adapt, do not copy-paste
- Brazilian context: Meta dominates, but TikTok growing fast in younger demos
