# Full Funnel Build Workflow
> **Type**: Workflow
> **Duration**: 7-10 business days
> **Agents involved**: traffic-chief, media-buyer, ad-midas, pixel-specialist

## Trigger
New account strategy requires full-funnel approach, or expansion from single-stage campaigns.

## Steps
1. Funnel Mapping → Agent: traffic-chief → Framework: TOFU-MOFU-BOFU → Output: Funnel stages defined with objectives, audiences, KPIs per stage
2. TOFU Build → Agent: media-buyer → Framework: Awareness/Reach Optimization → Output: Top-of-funnel campaigns targeting cold audiences with educational/value content
3. TOFU Creative → Agent: ad-midas → Framework: Hook-First Content → Output: Video views, blog posts, lead magnets designed for attention and reach
4. MOFU Build → Agent: media-buyer → Framework: Consideration/Engagement → Output: Mid-funnel campaigns targeting engaged users with deeper content
5. MOFU Creative → Agent: ad-midas → Framework: Trust-Building → Output: Testimonials, case studies, comparisons, webinar invites
6. BOFU Build → Agent: media-buyer → Framework: Conversion Optimization → Output: Bottom-funnel campaigns targeting high-intent users with direct offers
7. BOFU Creative → Agent: ad-midas → Framework: Offer-CTA → Output: Direct response ads with strong CTAs, urgency, risk reversal
8. Cross-Stage Tracking → Agent: pixel-specialist → Framework: Funnel Event Chain → Output: Events configured at each stage with audience flow tracking
9. Budget Allocation → Agent: traffic-chief → Framework: 60-30-10 Model → Output: Budget split across funnel (adjust based on funnel maturity)
10. Launch and Calibrate → Agent: media-buyer → Framework: Sequential Launch → Output: BOFU first (quick wins), then MOFU, then TOFU

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Each funnel stage has distinct objective and KPI
- [ ] Audiences properly segmented with exclusions between stages
- [ ] Creative messaging matches funnel temperature
- [ ] Tracking captures user progression through funnel
- [ ] Budget allocation reflects business maturity and goals
- [ ] No audience overlap between stages

## Output
Complete three-stage funnel with campaigns, creatives, tracking, and budget allocation.
Funnel flow diagram with expected conversion rates per stage.
Baseline metrics for each funnel stage.

## Budget Framework
- New accounts: 70% BOFU, 20% MOFU, 10% TOFU (prove ROI first)
- Mature accounts: 40% TOFU, 30% MOFU, 30% BOFU (growth mode)
- Scale mode: 50% TOFU, 25% MOFU, 25% BOFU (pipeline building)

## Notes
- TOFU success metric: CPM and video view rate, not conversions
- MOFU success metric: engagement rate, lead quality score
- BOFU success metric: CPA, ROAS, conversion volume
- Re-evaluate funnel split monthly based on pipeline data
