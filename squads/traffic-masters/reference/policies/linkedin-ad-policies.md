# LinkedIn Advertising Policies Reference

> Quick-reference guide for paid traffic professionals running campaigns on LinkedIn (Sponsored Content, Message Ads, Text Ads, Dynamic Ads, Lead Gen Forms).

---

## 1. Ad Review Process

- **Review time**: Typically within 24 hours; can take up to 48 hours
- **Scope**: Ad creative, copy, landing page, targeting, and advertiser profile
- **Automated + manual**: Initial AI screening, then human review for flagged content
- **Professional context**: LinkedIn's policies are stricter than other platforms due to the professional context of the network

---

## 2. Prohibited Content

| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Illegal products/services** | Drugs, weapons, counterfeit goods |
| **Adult content** | Pornography, dating services, sexual content |
| **Tobacco & e-cigarettes** | All tobacco and vaping products |
| **Weapons & ammunition** | Firearms, explosives, tactical equipment |
| **Gambling** | Online casinos, sports betting, lotteries |
| **Pyramid/MLM schemes** | Multi-level marketing or network marketing opportunities |
| **Fake documents** | Counterfeit diplomas, certifications, IDs |
| **Hate speech** | Content promoting discrimination or violence |
| **Malware/spyware** | Software that damages or surveils systems |
| **Payday loans** | Predatory lending products |
| **Political ads** | Political candidate ads and ballot measure ads (banned globally since 2018) |

## 3. Restricted Content

### Alcohol
- Must comply with local laws
- Must target 21+ in US, legal age elsewhere
- Cannot promote excessive consumption
- Not available in all countries

### Financial Services
- Must be offered by licensed/regulated entities
- Must include appropriate disclaimers and disclosures
- Crypto: Highly restricted; regulated exchanges may qualify with documentation
- Cannot guarantee specific investment returns

### Healthcare
- Cannot make disease cure/prevention claims
- Prescription drug advertising restricted
- Clinical trial recruitment allowed with proper disclosures
- Telehealth services permitted with licensing proof

### Education
- Degree-granting institutions must be accredited
- Cannot guarantee employment outcomes
- Must accurately represent program details and costs
- Coding bootcamps and professional development allowed with honest claims

### Employment & Recruiting
- Must represent real job opportunities
- Cannot discriminate based on protected categories
- Must comply with local employment advertising laws
- Gig economy/freelance opportunities must be clearly labeled

## 4. Creative & Copy Standards

### Professional Tone
- LinkedIn expects a professional tone; overly casual or clickbait content is rejected
- No excessive emojis (1-2 per post acceptable)
- No ALL CAPS headlines or body text
- No sensational or fear-based language
- Superlative claims ("best," "#1," "leading") must be substantiated

### Image Requirements
- **Sponsored Content**: 1200x627px (1.91:1) or 1080x1080 (1:1)
- **Carousel**: 1080x1080px per card (2-10 cards)
- **Video**: MP4, 3 seconds to 30 minutes, 16:9 or 1:1 or 9:16
- No deceptive imagery (fake LinkedIn UI elements, fake notifications)
- No before/after images for health/beauty products
- Stock photos allowed but authentic imagery performs 2-3x better

### Ad Copy Requirements
- Headlines: Max 70 characters (single-image), 45 characters (carousel)
- Description: Max 100 characters (below creative)
- Introductory text: Max 600 characters (150 characters visible before "...see more")
- Must accurately represent the product/service
- CTAs must match the landing page action

### Landing Page Requirements
- Must match the ad offer
- Must be functional and mobile-responsive
- Must include privacy policy
- No auto-playing audio
- No pop-ups that block content immediately
- Gated content must clearly state what's behind the form

## 5. Targeting Guidelines

### Professional Targeting
LinkedIn's targeting strength is professional attributes:
- Job title, function, seniority
- Company name, size, industry
- Skills, groups, degrees
- Years of experience

### Minimum Audience Size
- Sponsored Content/Message Ads: 300 members minimum
- Text Ads: 300 members minimum
- LinkedIn recommends 50,000+ for optimal delivery

### Restricted Targeting
- Cannot target by race, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation
- Cannot combine attributes to create discriminatory targeting (e.g., targeting by age + gender for employment ads)
- Employment ads subject to anti-discrimination requirements
- Cannot use Matched Audiences built from improperly collected data

### Matched Audiences
- Contact lists: Must have legitimate business relationship with contacts
- Retargeting: Pixel-based retargeting must comply with privacy policies
- Lookalike audiences: Source audience must be compliant
- Account-Based Marketing (ABM) lists: Must be sourced from legitimate business data

---

## 6. Message Ads & Conversation Ads Policies

Message Ads (InMail) have additional restrictions:
- **Frequency cap**: LinkedIn enforces a limit; members won't receive competing Message Ads within 45 days
- **Opt-out**: Members can opt out of Message Ads entirely
- **Content**: Must be relevant to professional context
- **Sender**: Must be a real person (not a company page) with a complete LinkedIn profile
- **No attachments**: Cannot include file attachments
- **CTA**: Must have a clear, professional call-to-action

### Conversation Ads
- Decision-tree format with multiple CTAs
- Each branch must lead to compliant content
- Cannot be used for personal or dating purposes
- Maximum 5 CTA buttons per message
- Must include at least one "Not interested" option

---

## 7. Lead Gen Forms Policies

- Must include a link to the advertiser's privacy policy
- Must clearly state how collected data will be used
- Cannot ask for sensitive information (SSN, passwords, health data)
- Pre-filled fields (name, email, job title) use LinkedIn profile data with member consent
- Custom questions must be relevant and non-discriminatory
- Must comply with GDPR/local privacy laws for data handling

---

## 8. Common Disapproval Reasons & Fixes

| Disapproval | Typical Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Unprofessional content** | Overly casual tone, excessive emojis | Adopt professional tone; limit emojis |
| **Unsubstantiated claims** | "#1 platform" without evidence | Add source citation or remove claim |
| **Misleading content** | Exaggerated ROI claims | Use realistic, verifiable metrics |
| **Landing page mismatch** | Ad promises whitepaper, LP sells product | Align LP content with ad promise |
| **Prohibited industry** | MLM opportunity disguised as job | Remove content; LinkedIn actively flags MLM |
| **Fake engagement** | Ad designed to look like organic post | Add clear ad context; avoid mimicking organic UI |
| **Political content** | Any political candidate or issue advocacy | LinkedIn does not allow political ads |
| **Poor image quality** | Blurry, distorted, or misleading images | Use high-resolution, relevant imagery |

## 9. Appeals Process

1. **In Campaign Manager**: Navigate to rejected ad > view policy reason > click "Appeal"
2. **LinkedIn Help Center**: Submit a support ticket for account-level issues
3. **Response time**: 1-3 business days
4. **Resubmission**: Edit the ad to address the issue, then resubmit
5. **Account manager**: Enterprise accounts can escalate through their LinkedIn rep

### Tips
- LinkedIn tends to be stricter on tone than other platforms
- If rejected for "unprofessional," try a more formal approach
- B2B-specific language is well-received
- Case studies and data-driven creative face fewer rejections

## 10. LinkedIn-Specific Benchmarks

| Metric | B2B Average | Top Performers |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| CTR (Sponsored Content) | 0.40-0.65% | 0.8%+ |
| CTR (Message Ads) | 3.0-4.0% open rate | 50%+ open rate |
| CPC | $5.00-$9.00 | $3.00-$5.00 |
| CPL (Lead Gen Forms) | $30-$75 | $15-$30 |
| CPM | $30-$50 | Varies by audience |
| Engagement Rate | 0.5-1.0% | 1.5%+ |

### Cost Optimization Tips
- Minimum bid often delivers well; avoid overbidding
- Use Lead Gen Forms vs. landing pages to reduce CPL by 30-50%
- Carousel ads often achieve 2x engagement at similar CPM
- Document Ads (PDF) can achieve high engagement at lower cost
- Thought Leader Ads (boosting employee posts) achieve 2-3x higher CTR
