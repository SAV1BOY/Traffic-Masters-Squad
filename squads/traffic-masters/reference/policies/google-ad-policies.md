# Google Ads Policies Reference

> Quick-reference guide for paid traffic professionals running campaigns on Google Ads (Search, Display, YouTube, Shopping, Performance Max, Demand Gen).

---

## 1. Ad Review Process

- **Automated + manual**: Most ads reviewed within 1 business day. Complex ads or new accounts may take longer.
- **Review scope**: Ad text, assets, keywords, landing page, targeting, bid strategy, and advertiser identity are all evaluated.
- **Policy enforcement**: Google uses a three-strike system for repeated violations within 90 days.

---

## 2. Prohibited Content

| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Counterfeit goods** | Products that mimic brand features to pass as genuine |
| **Dangerous products** | Recreational drugs, psychoactive substances, weapons, explosives, instructions for making weapons |
| **Dishonest behavior** | Hacking software, ad fraud services, fake documents, academic cheating services |
| **Inappropriate content** | Bullying, intimidation, hate speech, graphic violence, content exploiting sensitive events |
| **Malware & unwanted software** | Ads leading to sites that distribute malicious code |

---

## 3. Restricted Content

### Alcohol
- Must comply with local alcohol laws
- Cannot target minors
- Cannot promote excessive consumption
- Landing page must verify age where required

### Gambling
- Must be licensed in the target jurisdiction
- Must obtain Google certification
- Cannot target minors
- Real-money gambling restricted to certain countries

### Healthcare & Medicines
- **Prescription drugs**: Only certified online pharmacies (varies by country)
- **Over-the-counter drugs**: Allowed in most countries with restrictions
- **Unapproved drugs/supplements**: Cannot make unsubstantiated health claims
- **Addiction services**: Must be certified by LegitScript (US)
- **Clinical trial recruitment**: Allowed with restrictions

### Financial Services
- Must comply with local financial regulations
- **Cryptocurrency**: Allowed for regulated exchanges and wallets in approved regions; must be Google-certified
- **Loan ads**: Must display APR, repayment terms; no payday loans (repayment < 60 days)
- **Complex speculative financial products** (CFDs, spread betting, binary options): Restricted by country

### Political Ads
- Must complete advertiser verification
- Election ads include "Paid for by" disclosure
- Stored in Google's Transparency Report
- Restricted targeting (no microtargeting based on political affiliation)

### Adult Content
- Limited to specific formats (no YouTube or Google Shopping)
- Must not target minors
- Cannot appear alongside family-safe content

## 4. Editorial & Technical Standards

### Ad Text Requirements
- Correct grammar and spelling
- No excessive capitalization (e.g., "FREE SHIPPING" is borderline; "BUY NOW FREE SHIPPING TODAY" will be rejected)
- No repeated punctuation (!!!, ???)
- No gimmicky use of symbols, numbers, or letters (e.g., "F-R-E-E")
- No bullet points or emojis in standard text ads (allowed in some asset types)

### Trademark Policy
- Trademarked terms may be restricted in ad text
- Trademark owners can file complaints via Google's trademark form
- **Resellers, informational sites, and authorized dealers** can request exceptions
- Trademarks in keywords are generally allowed (no restriction on keyword bidding)

### Landing Page Requirements
- Must be functional and load properly
- Content must match the ad's offer
- Must be easy to navigate
- Must include clear contact information or business identification
- Cannot use deceptive "back" button behavior
- Must be mobile-friendly for mobile ads

### Destination Requirements
- Final URL must match the display URL domain
- No bridge/doorway pages (thin pages that only redirect)
- Cannot require app installation to view content (unless app install campaign)
- No auto-download of files

## 5. Three-Strike System

Google implemented a graduated enforcement system:

| Strike | Consequence | Duration |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Warning** | First offense for most violations; ad removed | N/A |
| **Strike 1** | 3-day account hold (cannot create new ads) | Stays on record 90 days |
| **Strike 2** | 7-day account hold | Stays on record 90 days |
| **Strike 3** | Account suspension | Permanent (appealable) |

**Immediate suspension** (no warning): Circumventing systems, egregious violations, coordinated deception.

---

## 6. Common Disapproval Reasons & Fixes

| Disapproval | Typical Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Misleading content** | Exaggerated claims, clickbait headlines | Use verifiable, specific claims |
| **Destination not working** | 404 page, timeout, geo-blocked landing page | Check LP from multiple locations; fix errors |
| **Malicious software** | Third-party scripts flagged on LP | Audit all JS; remove flagged scripts |
| **Trademark violation** | Using competitor brand in ad text | Remove trademarked term or apply for exception |
| **Destination mismatch** | Display URL doesn't match final URL domain | Align domains |
| **Healthcare claim** | Making disease cure/prevention claims | Rephrase; use "may help support" language cautiously |
| **Circumventing systems** | Cloaking, redirect chains, hiding true LP | Use direct, transparent URLs |
| **Insufficient info** | Missing business name, unclear offer | Add business details and specific offer info |

## 7. Advertiser Verification

### Identity Verification (Mandatory for all advertisers)
- Must verify legal business name, address, and documentation
- Deadline imposed; failure to verify results in account pause
- "About this advertiser" info displayed publicly

### Industry-Specific Certifications
| Industry | Certification Required |
|----------|----------------------|
| Gambling | Country-specific gambling license |
| Financial services | Varies by country; some require FCA/SEC registration proof |
| Healthcare (addiction) | LegitScript certification |
| Pharmacy | VIPPS or equivalent certification |
| Political ads | Advertiser identity verification + election ads verification |
| Cryptocurrency | Must be registered with relevant financial authority |

---

## 8. Appeals Process

1. **In Google Ads**: Navigate to the disapproved ad > click "Policy details" > "Request review"
2. **Policy Manager**: Use the Policy Manager in Google Ads for bulk appeals
3. **Response time**: Typically 1-3 business days
4. **Contact support**: Use the Google Ads support chat or phone for complex cases
5. **Escalation**: Request supervisor review through support; file formal appeals via the Help Center

### Appeal Tips
- Fix the issue before appealing if possible (submit compliant version + appeal)
- Be specific about why you believe the ad complies
- Include supporting documentation (licenses, certifications, product details)
- If repeatedly rejected, test different creative approaches rather than repeatedly appealing the same ad

## 9. Platform-Specific Considerations

### Google Search
- Responsive Search Ads: All headline/description combinations must be policy-compliant (including any auto-combination)
- Dynamic Search Ads: Landing pages are auto-selected; ensure all site pages are compliant

### YouTube
- Video content itself is subject to YouTube Community Guidelines AND Google Ads policies
- Sensitive categories may be demonetized or limited in delivery
- TrueView ads: First 5 seconds are critical; no shocking/misleading content in skippable portion

### Google Shopping
- Product data must match landing page exactly (price, availability, title)
- Mismatched data is the #1 disapproval reason in Shopping
- Free listings subject to the same policies
- GTIN/barcode required for branded products

### Performance Max
- All assets (headlines, descriptions, images, videos) reviewed individually and in combination
- Asset-level disapprovals can reduce campaign reach significantly
- More opaque disapprovals; use asset details report to identify issues

### Demand Gen
- Similar policies to YouTube/Discovery
- Image and video assets must meet both editorial and visual standards
- Lifestyle imagery preferred; avoid stock photo appearance
