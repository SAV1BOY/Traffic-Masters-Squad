# LinkedIn Ad Policies Reference
> **Platform**: LinkedIn
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Prohibited Content
- Illegal products/services, weapons, tobacco, recreational drugs
- Adult content, gambling, multilevel marketing schemes
- Discriminatory content, hate speech, harassment
- Fake engagement services (buy followers, fake endorsements)
- Cryptocurrency trading ads (restricted in most markets)

## Restricted Content
- Alcohol (age/geo restrictions, responsibility messaging)
- Financial services (licensing requirements, disclaimers)
- Healthcare (no unsubstantiated claims, professional disclaimers)
- Political ads (prohibited in most markets outside US)
- Job postings (must comply with employment law, no discriminatory requirements)

## Professional Standards
- Content must maintain professional tone appropriate for LinkedIn
- No clickbait headlines or sensationalist claims
- Testimonials must be genuine and verifiable
- Salary and compensation claims must be accurate and documented
- No impersonation of other companies or individuals

## Targeting Restrictions
- No targeting by race, religion, sexual orientation, or disability
- Employment ads cannot exclude by age, gender, or protected class
- Minimum audience size of 300 for campaigns to launch
- Matched Audiences require privacy-compliant data collection

## Creative Requirements
- Images must be relevant to ad content (no stock photo abuse)
- Video must have relevant content throughout (no filler)
- Text must be accurate and free of excessive formatting
- Landing pages must be professional and functional

## Key Takeaways
- LinkedIn has a higher bar for professional quality than other platforms
- B2B advertisers should emphasize credibility and authority
- Employment advertising has specific legal compliance requirements
- LinkedIn's professional audience expects substantive, useful content
- Message Ads must comply with anti-spam regulations
