# TikTok Ad Policies Reference
> **Platform**: TikTok Ads
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Prohibited Content
- Illegal products, weapons, drugs, tobacco, adult content
- Misleading claims, fake endorsements, counterfeit goods
- Content that promotes self-harm, eating disorders, or dangerous behavior
- Discrimination, hate speech, politically sensitive content

## Restricted Content
- Alcohol (21+ targeting, country-specific restrictions)
- Financial services (disclaimers required, no guaranteed returns)
- Health and beauty (no miracle claims, no before/after for weight loss)
- Gambling (certification required, many markets prohibited)
- Dating apps (age restrictions, content guidelines)

## Creative Guidelines
- Content must feel native to TikTok (overly polished ads may be deprioritized)
- No misleading UI elements (fake play buttons, fake notifications)
- Audio must not be jarring or excessively loud
- Text must be readable and not cover critical content areas
- Landing page must be consistent with ad creative messaging

## Community Standards
- Ads must comply with TikTok Community Guidelines in addition to ad policies
- Content promoting unrealistic body standards may be restricted
- Transparency required for sponsored content and paid partnerships
- Political advertising policies vary by country (prohibited in many markets)

## Account and Business Requirements
- Business verification required for advertising
- Payment method verification for all accounts
- Multiple ad accounts require business center setup

## Key Takeaways
- TikTok prioritizes user experience — ads that feel like organic content perform best
- Policy enforcement is evolving rapidly — check for updates frequently
- Health and beauty vertical has strict creative guidelines
- Native, authentic content is both a best practice and a policy advantage
- Spark Ads (boosting organic posts) have fewer creative restrictions
