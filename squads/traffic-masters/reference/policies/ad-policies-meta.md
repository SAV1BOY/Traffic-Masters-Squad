# Meta Ad Policies Reference
> **Platform**: Meta (Facebook + Instagram)
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Prohibited Content
- Illegal products/services, discriminatory practices
- Tobacco, drugs, unsafe supplements, weapons, adult content
- Misleading claims, fake news, sensationalist content
- Before/after images for health and weight loss
- Personal attributes: "Are you [characteristic]?" format prohibited

## Restricted Content (Requires Approval/Special Ad Category)
- Alcohol (age-gated, geo-restricted)
- Financial services (disclaimers required, APR disclosure)
- Health and wellness (no miracle cures, no guaranteed results)
- Political/social issues (Special Ad Category required)
- Housing, employment, credit (Special Ad Category, limited targeting)

## Creative Guidelines
- Text overlay: no hard limit but minimal text recommended
- Landing page must match ad content (ad scent)
- No deceptive buttons or UI elements in images
- No before/after photos implying unrealistic results
- Video: no disruptive audio, no misleading previews

## Account Health
- Policy violations accumulate — repeated violations lead to ad account restriction
- Account Quality score visible in Account Quality dashboard
- Appeals process available but slow — prevention is better than cure
- Business verification recommended for all commercial advertisers

## Brazilian Market Specifics
- Alcohol advertising restricted by CONAR guidelines
- Health claims require ANVISA compliance awareness
- Political advertising has specific Brazilian electoral law requirements
- Consumer protection (CDC) applies to all advertising claims

## Key Takeaways
- Review every creative against policies before launch
- When in doubt, err on the side of compliance
- Document all policy rejections and appeal outcomes
- Stay updated via Meta Business Help Center
