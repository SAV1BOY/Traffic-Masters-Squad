# Financial Services Advertising Rules

> Cross-platform reference for paid traffic professionals advertising banking, lending, insurance, investments, crypto, and fintech products.

---

## 1. Universal Principles

Financial advertising is among the most heavily regulated categories across all platforms and jurisdictions:

1. **No guaranteed returns** on investments
2. **Full disclosure** of terms, fees, rates, and risks
3. **Fair and balanced** presentation of benefits and risks
4. **Licensing/registration** required in target jurisdictions
5. **No predatory lending** promotion
6. **Clear identification** of the advertiser and regulatory status
7. **Risk warnings** mandatory for investment products

## 2. Platform-by-Platform Rules

### Meta (Facebook/Instagram)

| Product | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Credit cards** | Must disclose APR, annual fees; cannot target under 18 |
| **Loans/mortgages** | Must use Special Ad Category (Housing); limited targeting |
| **Insurance** | Must identify licensed provider; no misleading coverage claims |
| **Investments** | No guaranteed returns; risk disclaimers required |
| **Cryptocurrency** | Must obtain prior written permission; no ICOs/unregistered tokens |
| **Trading platforms** | Must be regulated; no "get rich quick" messaging |
| **Buy Now Pay Later** | Must disclose terms; cannot promote irresponsible borrowing |

**Special Ad Category**: Credit-related ads in the US trigger Housing/Credit category, which restricts targeting:
- Cannot target by age, gender, or zip code
- Minimum 15-mile radius for location
- Limited interest and demographic targeting
- Lookalike audiences replaced with "Special Ad Audiences"

### Google Ads

| Product | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Personal loans** | Must display min/max repayment period, APR, total cost example |
| **Payday loans** | Banned (repayment < 60 days or APR > 36% in US) |
| **Mortgage** | Must comply with local regulations; TILA requirements in US |
| **Crypto exchanges** | Must be Google-certified and regulated |
| **NFTs/DeFi** | Generally restricted; policies evolving |
| **Complex speculative products** | CFDs, spread betting, binary options restricted by country |
| **Debt management** | Must be accredited/licensed; no guarantees of debt elimination |
| **Tax services** | Must be qualified; cannot guarantee specific refund amounts |

**Google certification required for**: Crypto exchanges, financial comparison sites, loan modification services, and debt services.

### TikTok

| Product | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Banking/fintech** | Allowed with licensing verification |
| **Investments** | Restricted; must include risk warnings |
| **Crypto** | Highly restricted; only regulated exchanges with prior approval |
| **Lending** | Must disclose APR and terms; no predatory lending |
| **Insurance** | Allowed with proper licensing disclosure |
| **Trading education** | Cannot guarantee profits; must include risk disclaimers |

**TikTok-specific**: Financial content from creators (even Spark Ads) must comply with financial advertising regulations. "Finfluencer" content is scrutinized.

### LinkedIn

| Product | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **B2B financial services** | Allowed; strong performance channel for B2B finance |
| **Investment management** | Must be regulated entity; no guaranteed returns |
| **Fintech** | Allowed with proper licensing |
| **Crypto** | Highly restricted |
| **Financial recruiting** | Standard employment ad rules |
| **Trading courses** | Must include realistic disclaimers |

## 3. Regulatory Framework by Region

### United States

**Securities (SEC/FINRA)**:
- Investment ads must be filed with FINRA within 10 business days of first use
- Performance data must include standardized disclosures
- Past performance disclaimers required: "Past performance does not guarantee future results"
- Testimonials allowed since 2020 SEC rule change but with extensive requirements
- Social media posts by registered reps are considered advertising

**Banking (FDIC/OCC/CFPB)**:
- Truth in Lending Act (TILA): Trigger terms require full APR disclosure
- TILA trigger terms: specific rates, payment amounts, finance charges, number of payments
- Equal Credit Opportunity Act: Cannot discriminate in credit advertising
- CAN-SPAM compliance for financial email marketing
- CFPB enforcement on deceptive financial marketing practices

**Insurance (State-level)**:
- Regulated at state level; must comply with each state where advertising
- Cannot use "guarantee" or "risk-free" for insurance products
- Must identify the insurance company, not just the agency
- Disclaimers about coverage limitations required

**Crypto (SEC/CFTC/FinCEN)**:
- Crypto assets may be classified as securities (SEC jurisdiction)
- Must register or qualify for exemption
- Staking yield ads must include risk disclosures
- DeFi protocols face evolving regulatory scrutiny
- State money transmitter licenses may be required

### European Union (MiFID II / PSD2)

- **MiFID II**: Investment firms must ensure all marketing is "fair, clear, and not misleading"
- **PRIIPs**: Key Information Document required for packaged retail investment products
- **Risk warnings**: Mandatory for CFDs ("X% of retail investor accounts lose money trading CFDs with this provider")
- **PSD2**: Payment services advertising must be transparent about fees
- **Crypto (MiCA)**: Markets in Crypto-Assets regulation requires registration and disclosures
- **Cross-border**: Must comply with regulations in each target country

### Brazil (CVM/BACEN)

- CVM regulates securities advertising
- Must include: "Investimentos envolvem riscos e podem resultar em perdas"
- Influencer crypto/investment promotion must include risk disclaimers
- BACEN regulates banking/payment advertising
- Credit ads must include CET (Custo Efetivo Total)

### United Kingdom (FCA)

- FCA Financial Promotions regime applies to all ad channels
- Crypto ads must include risk warnings: "Don't invest unless you're prepared to lose all the money you invest"
- High-risk investment ads must include specific FCA-mandated warnings
- Cooling-off period disclosures for certain financial products
- BNPL regulation evolving; advertising rules tightening

## 4. Product Category Deep Dive

### Credit Cards & Lending

**Required disclosures (US)**:
When any "trigger term" is used, you must disclose:
- APR (Annual Percentage Rate)
- Fees (annual fee, transaction fees)
- Repayment terms
- Any variable rate conditions

**Trigger terms that require full disclosure**:
- "Low interest"
- "3.9% APR"
- "$0 down"
- "No payments for 12 months"
- "$50/month"
- Any specific credit terms

**Safe phrases that DON'T trigger disclosure**:
- "Competitive rates"
- "Low rates available" (without specifying)
- "Apply now"
- "See if you qualify"

### Investment & Trading

**What you CAN say**:
- "Start investing with as little as $1"
- "Access stocks, ETFs, and crypto in one app"
- "Commission-free trading" (if accurate)
- Reference past performance WITH disclaimers

**What you CANNOT say**:
- "Guaranteed 10% annual returns"
- "Risk-free investing"
- "Make $500/day trading"
- "Never lose money"
- Cherry-picked performance without context

**Required disclaimers**:
- "Past performance is not indicative of future results"
- "Investing involves risk, including potential loss of principal"
- "Securities offered through [registered broker-dealer name], Member FINRA/SIPC"

### Cryptocurrency

**Compliance framework**:
1. Identify whether the token is a security (Howey Test)
2. Verify licensing in each target jurisdiction
3. Include mandatory risk warnings
4. No guaranteed yield/return claims
5. Disclose the nature of the asset (not FDIC insured, volatile)

**Typical required disclaimer**:
"Cryptocurrency is a highly volatile asset. You could lose all of the money you invest. Don't invest unless you're prepared to lose all the money you invest. This is not financial advice."

### Insurance

**Rules across platforms**:
- Must identify the underwriting insurance company
- Cannot use "free" for insurance products (premiums are always paid somehow)
- Must not overstate coverage or understate exclusions
- Comparative claims ("save $500") must be based on verifiable data
- State-specific disclosures may be required

### Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL)

**Emerging regulation area**:
- Must clearly disclose repayment terms
- Must disclose late fees and consequences of missed payments
- Cannot promote as "free money"
- Must include information about credit impact
- Target audience must be 18+

## 5. Compliant Ad Copy Templates

### Credit Card Ad
```
Headline: "Earn Rewards on Every Purchase"
Body: "Apply for the [Card Name] and earn 2% cash back on all purchases.
No annual fee. Variable APR: 18.99%-26.99% based on creditworthiness.
See full terms at [URL]."
Disclaimer: "Terms and conditions apply. Subject to credit approval."
```

### Investment App Ad
```
Headline: "Start Building Your Portfolio Today"
Body: "Invest in stocks, ETFs, and bonds with [App Name].
No minimum balance required. Commission-free trades available."
Disclaimer: "Investing involves risk, including loss of principal.
Past performance does not guarantee future results.
Securities offered through [Broker], Member FINRA/SIPC."
```

### Crypto Exchange Ad
```
Headline: "Trade 200+ Cryptocurrencies"
Body: "Buy, sell, and hold crypto securely with [Exchange Name].
Regulated and licensed. Bank-grade security."
Disclaimer: "Crypto is highly volatile. You may lose all invested capital.
Not FDIC insured. Not investment advice. [Exchange] is registered with FinCEN."
```

## 6. Common Compliance Mistakes & Fixes

| Mistake | Risk Level | Fix |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| Trigger terms without full APR disclosure | HIGH - TILA violation | Add required disclosures or remove trigger terms |
| Guaranteed investment returns | CRITICAL - SEC/FCA action | Remove guarantees; add risk disclaimers |
| Targeting minors for financial products | HIGH - Platform ban | Set age targeting to 18+ (21+ for some products) |
| Missing broker-dealer disclosure | HIGH - FINRA violation | Add "Securities offered through..." disclosure |
| "Risk-free" language for investments | HIGH - Regulatory action | Replace with "low-cost" or "designed to manage risk" |
| Crypto ads without risk warnings | HIGH - FCA/platform rejection | Add mandatory crypto risk disclaimers |
| Using "FDIC insured" for non-bank products | CRITICAL - Regulatory action | Remove; only FDIC-member bank deposits are insured |
| Income claims for trading courses | HIGH - FTC action | Remove specific income claims; use disclaimers |

## 7. Audit Checklist for Financial Ads

Before launching any financial campaign:

- [ ] Advertiser is properly licensed/registered in all target jurisdictions
- [ ] All required disclosures are present and legible (APR, fees, terms)
- [ ] No guaranteed returns or risk-free claims
- [ ] Risk disclaimers are included for investment/crypto products
- [ ] Special Ad Category selected (if credit/housing related, US)
- [ ] Platform-specific certifications obtained (Google, Meta crypto approval)
- [ ] Landing page includes full terms, privacy policy, and regulatory disclosures
- [ ] Age targeting set to 18+ minimum
- [ ] Testimonials include proper disclaimers ("Results not typical")
- [ ] Creative does not use misleading charts or fabricated performance data
- [ ] Comparative claims are substantiated with verifiable data
- [ ] FINRA filing completed (if applicable to securities ads)
