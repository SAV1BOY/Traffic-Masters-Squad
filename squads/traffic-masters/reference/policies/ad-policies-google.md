# Google Ads Policies Reference
> **Platform**: Google Ads (Search, Display, Shopping, YouTube)
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Prohibited Content
- Counterfeit goods, dangerous products, dishonest behavior
- Inappropriate content (hate speech, violence, adult content)
- Malware, unwanted software, destination that does not work

## Restricted Content
- Alcohol (country-specific rules, no targeting minors)
- Gambling (requires certification, geo-restricted)
- Healthcare and medicines (certification required in many countries)
- Financial services (disclaimers, licensing requirements)
- Political content (verification required, transparency reports)

## Editorial Standards
- No excessive capitalization (e.g., "FREE MONEY NOW")
- No repeated punctuation (e.g., "Buy Now!!!")
- No gimmicky use of symbols or characters
- Grammar and spelling must be correct
- Phone numbers not allowed in ad text (use call extension)

## Technical Requirements
- Landing page must be functional, relevant, and easy to navigate
- HTTPS required for all landing pages
- No pop-ups or interstitials that prevent content access
- Mobile-friendly landing pages required
- Page load speed affects Quality Score and ad rank

## Account Structure Policies
- One account per business entity (avoid duplicate accounts)
- Circumventing policies with new accounts leads to permanent suspension
- Sensitive event policies: no exploiting crises, tragedies, or conflicts

## Key Takeaways
- Google enforcement is stricter than Meta — proactive compliance essential
- Healthcare and financial verticals require extra care and often certification
- Editorial standards are actively enforced — follow them exactly
- Account suspension on Google is harder to recover from than Meta
- Keep landing pages fast, secure, and directly relevant to ad content
