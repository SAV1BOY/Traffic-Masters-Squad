# Meta Advertising Policies Reference

> Quick-reference guide for paid traffic professionals running ads on Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Audience Network).

---

## 1. Ad Review Process

- **Automated review**: Most ads are reviewed within 24 hours by Meta's automated systems. Some ads undergo manual review.
- **Review criteria**: Ad creative (image/video), text, targeting, landing page, and the product/service itself are all evaluated.
- **Re-review**: Editing a live ad triggers a new review cycle. Plan edits in batches to minimize downtime.

---

## 2. Prohibited Content (Complete Ban)

| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Illegal products/services** | Drugs, weapons, counterfeit goods |
| **Discriminatory practices** | Ads that discriminate based on race, ethnicity, religion, sex, etc. |
| **Tobacco & related** | Cigarettes, e-cigarettes, vaporizers, tobacco products |
| **Unsafe supplements** | Anabolic steroids, HGH, ephedra-based products |
| **Weapons & ammunition** | Guns, gun parts, ammunition (including paintball/airsoft in some regions) |
| **Surveillance equipment** | Spy cams, phone trackers, hidden recording devices |
| **Payday loans** | Loans requiring repayment within 60 days or less |
| **MLM/pyramid schemes** | Business opportunity ads that resemble pyramid structures |
| **Misinformation** | Content debunked by third-party fact-checkers |
| **Anti-vaccination** | Content discouraging vaccination |

## 3. Restricted Content (Conditional Approval)

### Alcohol
- Must comply with local laws and applicable industry codes
- Targeting must exclude minors (age-gating required)
- Not available in all countries

### Dating Services
- Must receive prior written permission from Meta
- Cannot promote infidelity or sugar-daddy/sugar-baby arrangements
- Must target 18+ audiences

### Gambling & Online Gaming
- Requires prior written permission
- Must target 18+ (or higher where legally required)
- Restricted to authorized jurisdictions only

### Pharmacies & Supplements
- Online pharmacies must be certified by LegitScript
- Supplements cannot make disease cure/prevention claims
- Must not promote anabolic steroids or unsafe substances

### Financial Services
- Crypto ads: Must obtain prior written permission; no ICOs or unregistered securities
- Credit/loan ads: Must clearly disclose terms (APR, fees)
- Insurance ads: Cannot be misleading about coverage

### Healthcare
- Cannot reference specific health conditions in targeting
- Cannot imply knowledge of user's medical condition
- Telehealth services must comply with local regulations

### Political & Social Issue Ads
- Must use Meta's Ad Authorization process
- Require "Paid for by" disclaimer
- Added to Meta's Ad Library for 7 years
- Restricted to authorized advertisers in the relevant country

## 4. Creative & Copy Rules

### Text & Imagery
- **Before/after images**: Prohibited if they imply unrealistic results or create negative self-perception
- **Personal attributes**: Cannot assert or imply knowledge of a user's personal attributes (race, religion, health status, financial situation). Avoid "you" statements like "Are you overweight?"
- **Shocking content**: No graphic violence, gore, or sensationalized imagery
- **Misleading claims**: No fake "play" buttons, fake notifications, non-functional UI elements
- **Text in images**: No longer subject to the 20% text rule (removed in 2021), but heavy text still reduces delivery

### Landing Page Rules
- Must match the ad's offer and product
- Must be functional (no error pages, under construction, or parked domains)
- Cannot auto-download files
- Must not contain malware or deceptive pop-ups
- Must include privacy policy and terms of service

### Video Ads
- Must not contain disruptive or overly shocking content in the first 3 seconds
- Audio cannot be excessively loud or startling
- No flashing effects that could trigger seizures

---

## 5. Targeting Restrictions

### Special Ad Categories (US & EU)
Ads related to **Credit, Employment, Housing, or Social Issues/Elections/Politics** must:
- Use Special Ad Category targeting
- Cannot target by age, gender, zip code, or detailed demographics
- Limited interest/behavior targeting
- Minimum radius of 15 miles for location targeting

### Sensitive Targeting Removed (2022+)
Meta removed targeting options related to:
- Health conditions
- Religious practices
- Political beliefs
- Sexual orientation
- Race/ethnicity

### Custom Audiences
- Must have legitimate basis for contact list data
- Must accept Custom Audience Terms of Service
- Cannot use data obtained from scraping or unauthorized third parties

## 6. Common Disapproval Reasons & Fixes

| Disapproval Reason | Typical Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal attributes** | Using "you" + sensitive topic ("Are you in debt?") | Rephrase to third person or general statements |
| **Misleading claims** | Guaranteed results, fake urgency | Remove absolute guarantees; use realistic language |
| **Non-functional landing page** | 404, slow load, paywall blocking review | Ensure LP loads fully; whitelist Meta's crawler IPs |
| **Prohibited content** | Supplement making disease claims | Remove health claims; focus on lifestyle benefits |
| **Low quality** | Clickbait, engagement bait, withheld information | Be transparent about what user will find after clicking |
| **Circumventing systems** | Cloaking, using text in images to bypass review | Stop cloaking immediately; use compliant creative |

## 7. Appeals Process

1. **In Ads Manager**: Go to the ad, click "Edit", then look for "Request Review" or appeal option
2. **Account Quality Dashboard**: Visit `facebook.com/accountquality` for a centralized view of all policy issues
3. **Response time**: Typically 24-48 hours for first appeal
4. **Escalation**: If first appeal fails, submit through the Business Help Center or contact your Meta rep (if available)
5. **Pattern**: Multiple disapprovals in short succession can trigger account-level restrictions

### Tips for Successful Appeals
- Reference the specific policy you believe was misapplied
- Provide context about your business and the product/service
- If the ad was borderline, submit a slightly modified version simultaneously
- Keep a log of all appeals for pattern analysis

---

## 8. Account Health & Restrictions

### Account Quality Score Factors
- Ad disapproval rate
- User feedback (hide ad, report ad)
- Policy violation severity and recency
- Account age and spending history

### Restriction Levels
1. **Ad-level rejection**: Single ad disabled
2. **Ad set/campaign restriction**: Group of ads paused
3. **Advertising restriction**: Account limited (reduced delivery, higher CPMs)
4. **Account disabled**: Full ban; requires appeal
5. **Business Manager disabled**: All associated accounts banned

### Prevention Best Practices
- Maintain disapproval rate below 5%
- Monitor the Account Quality dashboard weekly
- Use multiple ad accounts within a Business Manager for risk distribution
- Build account history gradually (don't go from $0 to $10K/day overnight)

---

## 9. Industry-Specific Guidance

### E-commerce
- Product must match what is shown in the ad
- Shipping and return policies must be accessible
- Price shown in ad must match landing page price

### Lead Generation
- Lead forms must include a privacy policy link
- Cannot ask for sensitive information (SSN, financial account numbers) in lead forms
- Must clearly state what user is signing up for

### App Install
- App must be live and functional in the relevant app store
- Screenshots must be accurate representations
- In-app purchase disclosures required where applicable
