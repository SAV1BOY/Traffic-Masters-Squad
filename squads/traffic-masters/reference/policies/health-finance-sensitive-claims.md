# Health, Finance, and Sensitive Claims Policy Guide
> **Category**: Cross-Platform Compliance
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Health Claims
- No guaranteed health outcomes ("Cures cancer", "Guaranteed weight loss")
- No before/after images implying guaranteed transformation (Meta, TikTok)
- Disclaimers required: "Results may vary", "Consult a healthcare professional"
- Supplement ads: cannot claim to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease
- Mental health: avoid triggering language, no claims of instant cure
- ANVISA (Brazil): health product advertising must align with approved indications

## Financial Claims
- No guaranteed returns on investment
- Required disclaimers: "Past performance does not guarantee future results"
- Licensing/registration numbers must be displayed where required
- Cryptocurrency: heavily restricted or prohibited on most platforms
- Credit/loan offers: APR, terms, and conditions must be disclosed
- CVM (Brazil): securities advertising requires compliance with CVM rules

## Weight Loss and Body Image
- Meta: no before/after images, no body shaming, no unrealistic expectations
- TikTok: restricted promotion of unrealistic body standards
- Google: weight loss claims must be substantiated with evidence
- All platforms: avoid targeting based on perceived body insecurities

## Testimonials and Results Claims
- Must represent typical results, not exceptional outcomes
- Income claims must include average/typical results
- Celebrity/influencer endorsements must disclose paid relationship
- User testimonials should be genuine and verifiable
- Brazilian CDC (Consumer Defense Code) prohibits misleading advertising

## General Sensitive Categories
- Dating: age restrictions, no explicit content
- Gambling: certification required, responsible gambling messaging
- Alcohol: age gating, no promoting excessive consumption
- Legal services: bar association rules, no guaranteed outcomes

## Key Takeaways
- When in doubt, add a disclaimer — it is easier than appealing a rejection
- Brazilian market has additional regulatory layers (ANVISA, CVM, CONAR, CDC)
- Keep a library of approved disclaimer language by vertical
- Review competitor ads for compliance patterns (not to copy violations)
- Train all creative producers on sensitive claims guidelines
