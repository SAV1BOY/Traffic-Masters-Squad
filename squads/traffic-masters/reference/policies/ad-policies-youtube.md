# YouTube Ad Policies Reference
> **Platform**: YouTube (via Google Ads)
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Content Policies
- All Google Ads policies apply to YouTube ads
- Additional content suitability controls for brand safety
- Advertiser-friendly content guidelines affect placement eligibility
- No shocking, violent, or sexually suggestive content in ad creatives

## Video-Specific Policies
- No misleading thumbnails or titles
- Audio must not be disruptive or excessively loud
- No flashing/strobing content that could trigger seizures
- Claims in video must be substantiated (same as text ad rules)
- Disclaimers must be visible and readable in video ads

## Placement Restrictions
- Brand safety: inventory types (expanded, standard, limited)
- Content exclusions: embedded videos, live streams, specific categories
- Topic and placement exclusions for sensitive advertisers
- Digital content labels for age-appropriate targeting

## Targeting Restrictions
- No personalized ads to users under 18 (limited targeting for minors)
- Sensitive interest categories cannot be used for targeting
- Remarketing lists require minimum size (1,000 users)

## Music and Copyright
- Only use royalty-free or licensed music in video ads
- Copyright strikes can affect channel and ad account standing
- YouTube Content ID may flag copyrighted material in ads

## Key Takeaways
- YouTube ads inherit all Google Ads policies plus video-specific rules
- Brand safety settings matter — configure inventory type per campaign
- Music licensing is a common source of ad rejections
- Keep disclaimers visible and readable at all video resolutions
- Monitor placement reports to ensure ads appear in brand-safe contexts
