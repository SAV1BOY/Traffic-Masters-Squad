# Healthcare & Pharmaceutical Advertising Rules

> Cross-platform reference for paid traffic professionals advertising healthcare, pharma, supplements, telehealth, and wellness products.

---

## 1. Universal Principles Across All Platforms

These rules apply regardless of the advertising platform:

1. **No disease cure/prevention claims** without FDA/regulatory approval
2. **No misleading before/after imagery** for health outcomes
3. **No personal health assertions** ("We know you have diabetes")
4. **Required disclaimers** vary by product category and jurisdiction
5. **Substantiation required** for all health-related claims
6. **Licensed professionals** must be accurately represented
7. **Age-gating** required where applicable (e.g., cannabis in legal markets)

## 2. Platform-by-Platform Rules

### Meta (Facebook/Instagram)

| Category | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Prescription drugs** | Prohibited in most countries; US allows with restrictions |
| **Online pharmacies** | Must be LegitScript-certified |
| **Supplements** | Cannot make drug claims; no steroid/HGH products |
| **Weight loss** | No before/after images; no guaranteed weight loss claims |
| **Cosmetic procedures** | Allowed with restrictions; no graphic surgical imagery |
| **Mental health** | Allowed; cannot imply knowledge of user's condition |
| **Telehealth** | Allowed; must link to licensed provider |
| **Personal attributes** | Cannot reference user's health condition directly |
| **Targeting** | Cannot target based on health conditions (removed 2022) |

**Key Meta restriction**: The "personal attributes" policy is the most common rejection trigger. Avoid:
- "Struggling with back pain?"
- "If you have diabetes..."
- "Tired of your weight?"

Instead use: "Many people experience..." or "For those seeking..."

### Google Ads

| Category | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Prescription drugs** | Certified pharmacies only; varies by country |
| **OTC drugs** | Allowed in most markets with disclaimers |
| **Addiction services** | Must be LegitScript-certified (US) |
| **Clinical trials** | Allowed with proper disclosures |
| **Telehealth** | Allowed; must demonstrate licensing |
| **Healthcare claims** | Must be substantiated by clinical evidence |
| **Remarketing** | Cannot remarket to users based on health-related page visits using health-condition keywords |
| **Personalized ads** | Health-related advertisers may face restrictions on personalized targeting |

**Google-specific**: Healthcare advertisers must complete advertiser verification. Some healthcare keywords are restricted in certain countries (e.g., prescription drug names in keyword targeting).

### TikTok

| Category | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Prescription drugs** | Prohibited |
| **OTC drugs** | Restricted; varies by market |
| **Supplements** | Allowed with no drug claims; no weight loss guarantees |
| **Weight loss** | No before/after; no body shaming; no specific weight loss claims |
| **Mental health** | Allowed with sensitivity; no diagnosis claims |
| **Cosmetic procedures** | Restricted; no graphic content |
| **Body image** | Stricter than other platforms; no ads promoting unrealistic body standards |

**TikTok-specific**: TikTok is particularly strict about body image and weight loss content. Influencer/creator content for healthcare brands must also comply with ad policies.

### LinkedIn

| Category | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Pharma (B2B)** | Allowed for HCP-targeted campaigns |
| **Medical devices** | Allowed with proper regulatory clearance documentation |
| **Healthcare recruiting** | Allowed; standard employment ad rules apply |
| **Patient-facing** | More restricted; no direct health condition targeting |
| **Clinical trial recruitment** | Allowed with IRB-approved content |

## 3. Regulatory Framework by Region

### United States (FDA/FTC)

**FDA Oversight**:
- Prescription drug ads must include fair balance (benefits + risks)
- DTC (Direct-to-Consumer) prescription ads require Major Statement of risks
- "Adequate directions for use" or referral to prescribing information
- Off-label promotion strictly prohibited
- Reminder ads (brand name only, no indication) have fewer requirements

**FTC Oversight**:
- Supplements regulated under DSHEA (1994)
- Claims must be "truthful, not misleading, and substantiated"
- Structure/function claims allowed with disclaimer: "This statement has not been evaluated by the FDA. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."
- Testimonials must represent typical results or include clear disclaimers
- Endorsements by healthcare professionals must reflect genuine opinions

**HIPAA Considerations for Ads**:
- Never use patient data (PHI) for ad targeting without proper authorization
- Pixel/tracking data from healthcare provider websites may constitute PHI
- Conversions API implementations must strip PHI before sending to ad platforms
- Patient testimonials require written authorization

### European Union (EMA + National Authorities)

- **Prescription drugs**: DTC advertising prohibited (only HCP-targeted allowed)
- **OTC drugs**: Allowed with mandatory disclaimers ("Read the leaflet carefully")
- **Supplements**: EU Health Claims Regulation (EC 1924/2006) restricts which claims can be made
- **Medical devices**: Must comply with MDR (Medical Device Regulation)
- **Cross-border**: Must comply with the most restrictive country in the target audience

### Brazil (ANVISA)

- All health product ads must include ANVISA registration number
- Prescription drug DTC advertising prohibited
- OTC ads must include "SE PERSISTIREM OS SINTOMAS, O MEDICO DEVERA SER CONSULTADO"
- Supplement ads heavily regulated; structure/function claims need pre-approval
- Influencer health content subject to same rules as paid advertising

### United Kingdom (MHRA + ASA)

- Post-Brexit, UK follows MHRA guidance (not EMA)
- ASA (Advertising Standards Authority) enforces ad codes
- No prescription drug DTC advertising
- OTC ads must be "legal, decent, honest, and truthful"
- Health claims must be authorized under retained EU law

## 4. Content Category Deep Dive

### Weight Loss & Diet Products

**What you CAN say**:
- "May support your weight management goals as part of a healthy lifestyle"
- "Designed to complement a balanced diet and exercise program"
- Share ingredient information and nutritional facts
- Reference published studies (if they exist and are relevant)

**What you CANNOT say**:
- "Lose 20 pounds in 2 weeks"
- "Burn fat without exercise"
- "Guaranteed weight loss results"
- "Before: 200lbs / After: 150lbs" (with images)
- Any implication that the product alone causes weight loss

### Mental Health & Therapy Services

**What you CAN say**:
- "Connect with licensed therapists online"
- "Support for anxiety, depression, and stress management"
- "Start your mental health journey"
- Share statistics about mental health prevalence (general population)

**What you CANNOT say**:
- "We know you're depressed" (personal attribute)
- "Cure your anxiety forever"
- "Replace your medication with our therapy"
- Target based on mental health condition interest categories

### Supplements & Nutraceuticals

**Allowed claims (US)**:
- Structure/function claims: "Supports bone health," "Promotes digestive balance"
- Nutrient content claims: "High in Vitamin C," "Good source of fiber"
- Health claims authorized by FDA: "Calcium may reduce the risk of osteoporosis"

**Prohibited claims**:
- Disease claims: "Treats arthritis," "Cures cancer," "Prevents heart disease"
- Drug-like claims: "Lowers cholesterol," "Reduces blood pressure"
- Unapproved health claims without scientific consensus

### Telehealth & Online Healthcare

- Must verify licensing in all states/countries where advertising
- Cannot guarantee specific health outcomes
- Prescription services must follow applicable pharmacy regulations
- Must be transparent about consultation process
- Must disclose limitations of telemedicine vs. in-person care

## 5. Compliant Ad Copy Templates

### Supplement Ad (US)
```
Headline: "Support Your Immune System Naturally"
Body: "Our Vitamin C & Zinc formula is designed to support your daily immune function.*
Made with clinically studied ingredients. GMP-certified facility."
Disclaimer: "*These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA.
This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."
```

### Telehealth Ad
```
Headline: "Licensed Therapists, On Your Schedule"
Body: "Connect with board-certified therapists from home.
100% HIPAA-compliant. Insurance accepted. First session available this week."
CTA: "Get Matched Today"
```

### Weight Management Ad
```
Headline: "A Smarter Approach to Wellness"
Body: "Our program combines nutrition guidance, exercise plans,
and ongoing support to help you work toward your health goals.
Consult with registered dietitians. Personalized to your lifestyle."
CTA: "Learn More"
```

## 6. Common Compliance Mistakes & Fixes

| Mistake | Risk Level | Fix |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| Using "cure" or "treat" for supplements | HIGH - FTC/FDA action | Replace with "support" or "promote" + disclaimer |
| Before/after patient photos | HIGH - Platform ban | Use lifestyle imagery; no transformation shots |
| Targeting health condition audiences | MEDIUM - Ad rejection | Use contextual or demographic targeting instead |
| Missing FDA disclaimer on supplements | HIGH - Regulatory action | Add standard FDA disclaimer to all supplement ads |
| Testimonials claiming specific outcomes | HIGH - FTC action | Add "Results not typical" + disclose material connections |
| Using patient data for targeting | CRITICAL - HIPAA violation | Audit data flows; use compliant tracking solutions |
| Off-label drug promotion | CRITICAL - FDA warning letter | Only promote FDA-approved indications |

## 7. Audit Checklist for Healthcare Ads

Before launching any healthcare campaign, verify:

- [ ] All health claims are substantiated with evidence
- [ ] Required disclaimers are present and legible
- [ ] No personal health attribute assertions in ad copy
- [ ] Landing page is compliant and matches ad claims
- [ ] Targeting does not discriminate or use health conditions
- [ ] Tracking implementation does not transmit PHI to ad platforms
- [ ] Testimonials have proper disclosures
- [ ] Creative does not include prohibited before/after imagery
- [ ] Product is properly registered/approved in target markets
- [ ] Privacy policy covers ad-related data collection
- [ ] Professional credentials of endorsers are verified and current
