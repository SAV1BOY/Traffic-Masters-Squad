# TikTok Advertising Policies Reference

> Quick-reference guide for paid traffic professionals running ads on TikTok Ads Manager (In-Feed, TopView, Spark Ads, Search Ads, etc.).

---

## 1. Ad Review Process

- **Review time**: Most ads reviewed within 24 hours; can take up to 48 hours during peak periods
- **Scope**: Creative content (video/image), ad text, landing page/app store page, targeting settings, and brand profile
- **Automated + human**: TikTok uses AI screening followed by human review for flagged content
- **Re-review**: Any edit to a live ad triggers a new review cycle

## 3. Restricted Content

### Alcohol
- Permitted only in authorized countries (must be 21+ in US, 18+ in most other markets)
- Cannot glorify excessive consumption
- Must include responsible drinking messaging where required
- Cannot target users below legal drinking age

### Financial Services
- Must provide license/registration information
- Cannot guarantee returns or profits
- Must include risk disclaimers
- Lending ads must disclose APR and terms
- Crypto exchanges: restricted to licensed entities with prior approval

### Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals
- OTC medications: Allowed with disclaimers in approved markets
- Prescription drugs: Prohibited in most markets
- Supplements: No disease cure/prevention claims
- Weight loss: Cannot use before/after imagery or guarantee specific results
- Mental health services: Allowed with appropriate sensitivity

### Dating Services
- Must target 18+ audiences
- Cannot promote casual hookups or infidelity
- Must comply with local regulations
- Sugar dating platforms prohibited

### Food & Dietary
- Cannot make unsubstantiated health claims
- Must comply with local food advertising regulations
- Children's food advertising restrictions apply

## 4. Creative Standards

### Video Requirements
- **Resolution**: Minimum 720p recommended; 1080p preferred
- **Aspect ratios**: 9:16 (full-screen vertical), 1:1, 16:9
- **Duration**: 5-60 seconds (15-30 seconds optimal for performance)
- **File size**: Up to 500MB
- **Audio**: Required for most placements; ads with audio perform 2-3x better

### Content Quality Standards
- No blurry, pixelated, or stretched imagery
- No excessive text overlays that obstruct the main content
- No screenshots of other apps or platforms (including competitor ad screenshots)
- No static images used as video (slideshow is acceptable with transitions)
- Must feel native to TikTok; overly polished/traditional ads underperform

### Copy Requirements
- No excessive capitalization
- No misleading clickbait
- No excessive use of emojis or special characters
- No profanity or vulgar language
- Claims must be substantiable
- CTA must accurately represent what happens after click

### Landing Page Requirements
- Must match the ad's offer and product
- Must be mobile-optimized (TikTok is mobile-first)
- Must load within 3 seconds
- Must include privacy policy
- Cannot auto-download files or trigger pop-ups
- App download ads must link to official app store

---

## 5. Targeting Restrictions

### Age-Based Restrictions
- TikTok users must be 13+ (platform minimum)
- Advertising targeting minimum: 18+ in most markets
- Alcohol: 21+ in US, legal drinking age elsewhere
- Financial services: 18+ minimum
- Dating: 18+ minimum

### Sensitive Categories
- Cannot target based on health conditions
- Cannot target based on sexual orientation
- Cannot target based on religious beliefs
- Cannot target based on political affiliation
- Retargeting audiences must be sourced from compliant data collection

### Geographic Restrictions
- Some product categories restricted to specific markets
- Advertisers must be authorized to operate in target markets
- Cross-border advertising subject to both origin and destination country rules

---

## 6. Spark Ads Specific Policies

Spark Ads (boosting organic TikTok posts) have additional considerations:
- Original post must comply with ad policies (even if it was compliant as organic content)
- Creator authorization required (via authorization code)
- Creator's profile and other content may be reviewed
- Organic engagement metrics are combined with paid metrics
- Comments on Spark Ads cannot be turned off
- Creator must not be associated with prohibited content

## 7. Common Disapproval Reasons & Fixes

| Disapproval | Typical Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Unsubstantiated claims** | "Best product ever", "#1 in the market" | Add source/substantiation or remove superlative |
| **Before/after imagery** | Weight loss, skincare transformations | Remove transformation imagery; focus on product features |
| **Landing page mismatch** | Ad shows one product, LP shows different | Align ad creative with LP content |
| **Low quality creative** | Blurry video, static image, no audio | Re-shoot or re-edit to meet quality standards |
| **Prohibited product** | Supplement making drug claims | Remove health claims; reposition as lifestyle product |
| **Intellectual property** | Using competitor logos/brand names | Remove third-party IP; create original content |
| **Personal attributes** | Implying knowledge of user's condition | Use third-person language; avoid "you" + health/finance |
| **Non-native creative** | Repurposed TV ad or Instagram Reel | Create TikTok-native content with trends, sounds |

## 8. Appeals Process

1. **In TikTok Ads Manager**: Navigate to the rejected ad > click the policy notification > "Request Review"
2. **TikTok Business Center**: Use the Help section for account-level issues
3. **Response time**: 24-48 hours typically
4. **Resubmission**: Can edit and resubmit the ad after making changes
5. **Support ticket**: File via TikTok Business Help Center for complex issues
6. **Account manager**: If you have a dedicated rep, escalate through them

### Appeal Tips
- TikTok reviewers tend to be stricter than Meta/Google; err on the side of caution
- Provide clear business context and documentation
- If a creative style keeps getting rejected, try a fundamentally different approach
- Test with Spark Ads first (organic posts boosted tend to face fewer restrictions)

---

## 9. Brand Safety & Inventory Filters

### Inventory Tiers
- **Full inventory**: Maximum reach, may appear alongside all content
- **Standard inventory**: Excludes most controversial content (recommended default)
- **Limited inventory**: Most restrictive; excludes all potentially sensitive content

### Category Exclusions
Advertisers can exclude ad placement alongside specific content categories:
- Gambling
- Violent content
- Adult themes
- Political content
- Sensitive social issues

### Brand Safety Partners
TikTok works with IAS, DoubleVerify, and MOAT for third-party brand safety verification.
