# Video Attention Curves
> **Category**: Psychology Applied to Advertising
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Overview
Video attention follows predictable patterns. Understanding these curves helps design video ads that maximize view time, message delivery, and conversion.

## The Typical Attention Curve
- **0-3 seconds**: Critical hook window — 50-70% of viewers decide to stay or leave
- **3-10 seconds**: Rapid drop-off — only viewers who were hooked remain
- **10-30 seconds**: Gradual decline — engaged viewers processing the message
- **30-60 seconds**: Plateau — remaining viewers are highly engaged
- **60+ seconds**: Slow decline — only the most interested viewers continue

## Key Metrics by Time Point
- **Hook rate**: 3-second views / impressions (target: 25%+ for cold traffic)
- **Hold rate**: 15-second views / 3-second views (target: 40%+)
- **ThruPlay rate**: views to 15s or completion / impressions
- **Completion rate**: full views / impressions (varies by video length)

## Designing for the Attention Curve

### First 3 Seconds (The Hook)
- Pattern interrupt: unexpected visual, bold text, direct address
- Curiosity gap: open a question that demands answers
- Emotional trigger: surprise, humor, shock, empathy
- Never start with a logo, intro, or slow build

### 3-15 Seconds (The Bridge)
- Deliver on the hook's promise — do not waste earned attention
- Introduce the problem or desire clearly
- Maintain visual variety (scene changes every 2-3 seconds)
- Add text overlays for sound-off viewers

### 15-30 Seconds (The Body)
- Present the solution, proof, or story arc
- Social proof and credibility elements here
- Keep energy high — any dip loses viewers permanently

### Final 5 Seconds (The CTA)
- Clear, specific call to action
- Visual CTA (text overlay + spoken CTA)
- Urgency or incentive to act now

## Platform-Specific Attention Patterns
- TikTok: fastest decay — hook must land in 0.5-1s
- Instagram Reels: similar to TikTok but slightly more forgiving
- Facebook feed: 1-3s hook window, sound-off dominant
- YouTube: 5s before skip — hook optimized for 5s window

## Key Takeaways
- Front-load your most important message — most viewers will not reach the end
- Multiple hooks throughout the video re-engage declining attention
- Scene changes every 2-3 seconds maintain visual interest
- Always design for sound-off (captions, text overlays)
- Test multiple video lengths: 15s, 30s, 60s to find optimal for your audience
