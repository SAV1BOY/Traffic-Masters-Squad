# Risk Reversal and Trust Psychology
> **Category**: Psychology Applied to Advertising
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Overview
Perceived risk is the primary barrier to conversion. Risk reversal transfers the risk from the buyer to the seller, making the decision feel safe.

## Types of Perceived Risk
1. **Financial risk**: "What if I waste my money?"
2. **Functional risk**: "What if it doesn't work?"
3. **Social risk**: "What if people judge me for this?"
4. **Time risk**: "What if I waste my time?"
5. **Psychological risk**: "What if I regret this decision?"

## Risk Reversal Mechanisms

### Guarantees
- Money-back guarantee (30, 60, 90 days)
- Results guarantee ("If you don't get X, we'll refund")
- "Better than money back" guarantee (refund + bonus)
- Brazilian CDC: 7-day return right is legally required for online purchases

### Social Proof
- Testimonials and reviews reduce social and functional risk
- Numbers ("50,000+ customers") reduce financial risk
- Expert endorsements reduce functional and psychological risk
- Case studies with specific results reduce all risk types

### Trust Signals
- Logos of known partners, media mentions, certifications
- Security badges on checkout pages
- Professional design and production quality
- Transparent pricing (no hidden fees)

### Trial and Low Commitment
- Free trials remove financial risk entirely
- Low-cost tripwires allow testing before major commitment
- "Cancel anytime" removes ongoing financial risk
- Free shipping removes a common friction point

## Application in Ad Creative
- Mention guarantee prominently in ad copy and landing page
- Lead with social proof in cold traffic ads
- Display trust signals above the fold on landing pages
- Address the dominant risk type for your specific audience

## Key Takeaways
- Every purchase has perceived risk — your job is to eliminate it
- The stronger your guarantee, the fewer refunds you actually get (paradox)
- Different audiences have different dominant risk types — identify yours
- Social proof is the most versatile risk reducer across all funnel stages
- Brazilian law requires 7-day right of withdrawal — present it as a feature
