# Behavior Change Models for Advertising
> **Category**: Psychology Applied to Advertising
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Overview
Advertising fundamentally asks people to change behavior — from not buying to buying, from unaware to aware. Understanding behavior change models improves campaign design.

## Key Models

### Fogg Behavior Model (B=MAP)
- Behavior = Motivation + Ability + Prompt (all three must be present simultaneously)
- Motivation: desire for outcome, fear of missing out
- Ability: how easy is the action? (reduce friction)
- Prompt: the trigger to act now (CTA, ad itself)
- Application: if conversions are low, diagnose which element is missing

### AIDA Model
- Attention → Interest → Desire → Action
- Classic advertising funnel model
- Maps to: Hook → Story → Offer → CTA
- Each ad stage must accomplish its AIDA step

### Transtheoretical Model (Stages of Change)
- Pre-contemplation: unaware of need (educational content)
- Contemplation: aware but not ready (value-first content)
- Preparation: planning to act (comparison, proof content)
- Action: making the purchase (offer, CTA, urgency)
- Maintenance: staying engaged (retention, loyalty)

### Loss Aversion Framework
- People are more motivated to avoid losses than to achieve gains
- "Don't miss out" is more motivating than "You could gain"
- Application: frame offers in terms of what user loses by not acting

## Application to Campaign Design
1. Map your audience to their current stage of change
2. Design campaign messaging for that specific stage
3. Use Fogg model to diagnose conversion barriers
4. Apply AIDA structure to individual ad creative

## Key Takeaways
- If conversions are low, check: is motivation present? Is the action easy? Is there a clear prompt?
- Different audiences are at different stages — one message does not fit all
- Loss framing outperforms gain framing for most direct response advertising
- Reducing friction (fewer form fields, faster checkout) often beats increasing motivation
- The prompt (CTA) must be clear, specific, and timely
