# Persuasion Biases for Ads
> **Category**: Psychology Applied to Advertising
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Key Cognitive Biases for Ad Creative

### Anchoring Bias
- First piece of information anchors all subsequent judgments
- Application: Show original price before discount, high-value comparison before your price
- Example: "Courses that cost R$5,000... yours for R$297"

### Bandwagon Effect
- People adopt behaviors that others are adopting
- Application: "Join 50,000+ students", "Best-selling product"
- Strongest when the reference group is similar to the target audience

### Confirmation Bias
- People favor information that confirms existing beliefs
- Application: Lead with statements your audience already agrees with
- Example: "You know that diets don't work..." (for an alternative health product)

### Decoy Effect
- Adding a third option makes one of the other two look better
- Application: Three pricing tiers where the middle tier is the target choice
- The decoy makes the target option appear as the best value

### Framing Effect
- Same information presented differently changes the decision
- Application: "Save R$200" vs "Get 40% off" vs "Pay only R$1.60/day"
- Loss framing often more powerful than gain framing

### Status Quo Bias
- People prefer the current state of affairs
- Application: Overcome by showing the cost of inaction
- Example: "Every day you wait costs you R$X in lost revenue"

### Mere Exposure Effect
- Familiarity breeds preference — repeated exposure increases liking
- Application: Retargeting, frequency management, consistent brand elements

## Application Framework
1. Identify the primary decision barrier for your audience
2. Select 2-3 biases that address that barrier
3. Incorporate into ad creative (visual, copy, structure)
4. Test bias-driven creative against control

## Key Takeaways
- Use biases ethically — manipulative application erodes trust
- Layer multiple biases for stronger persuasion
- Different funnel stages respond to different biases
- Test which biases resonate with your specific audience
