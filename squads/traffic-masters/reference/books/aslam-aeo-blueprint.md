# AEO Blueprint
> **Author**: Kasim Aslam
> **Year**: 2025
> **Category**: Answer Engine Optimization

## Key Concepts
- AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) as the evolution of SEO in an AI-first search landscape
- Optimizing content to be surfaced by AI assistants and answer engines
- The shift from ranking on page one to being the cited answer
- Structured data, authority signals, and content formatting for AI consumption
- Intersection of paid traffic and organic AI visibility

## Core Frameworks
1. **Answer-First Content**: Structure content to directly answer queries
2. **Authority Building**: Signals that AI engines use to determine source credibility
3. **Structured Data Protocol**: Schema markup for AI-readable content
4. **Citation Optimization**: Getting cited by AI-generated summaries
5. **Paid + Organic Synergy**: Using paid data to inform organic AEO strategy

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Organic visibility complements paid traffic efforts
- Paid search data reveals which queries to target with AEO
- Landing pages optimized for AEO also perform better for paid Quality Score
- Understanding AI search behavior informs audience intent mapping

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. AI-powered search is changing how users find information and products
2. Content structured for AI consumption gets more organic visibility
3. Paid traffic data (search terms) is a goldmine for AEO keyword research
4. Landing pages should serve both paid visitors and AI crawlers
5. Authority and trust signals matter more than ever for visibility
6. AEO and paid traffic are complementary, not competing strategies
