# Click Happy
> **Author**: Molly Pittman
> **Year**: 2020
> **Category**: Traffic Systems & Strategy

## Key Concepts
- Traffic is a system, not a tactic — build repeatable processes, not one-off campaigns
- The Customer Journey Map: awareness, engagement, subscription, conversion, excitement, ascension, advocacy
- Traffic temperature framework: cold (strangers), warm (engaged), hot (buyers)
- Every ad campaign should be mapped to a specific stage in the customer journey
- "Traffic is not the goal — profitable customer acquisition is the goal"

## Core Frameworks
1. **Customer Journey Mapping**: Map every campaign to a journey stage
2. **Traffic Temperature**: Match message to audience awareness level
3. **Campaign Architecture**: Structure campaigns by objective, not just audience
4. **Metrics by Stage**: Different KPIs for each journey stage (reach for cold, CTR for warm, CPA for hot)

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Foundation for full-funnel campaign thinking (see: full-funnel-build-workflow.md)
- Traffic temperature informs creative messaging and audience segmentation
- Customer journey mapping aligns with retargeting sequence design
- Systems thinking prevents ad-hoc, reactive campaign management

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Build traffic systems, not individual campaigns
2. Always know what journey stage you are targeting
3. Match message temperature to audience temperature
4. Measure success differently at each funnel stage
5. Traffic without a journey map is just expensive attention
6. Document your systems so they are repeatable and teachable
