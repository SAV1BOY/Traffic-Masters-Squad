# HIRE
> **Author**: Kasim Aslam
> **Year**: 2025
> **Category**: Digital Marketing Team Building

## Key Concepts
- Framework for hiring and evaluating digital marketing professionals
- Skills assessment matrices for media buyers, analysts, and strategists
- Distinguishing between tactical executors and strategic thinkers
- Building teams that can handle platform changes and algorithm shifts
- Onboarding processes that accelerate new hire productivity

## Core Frameworks
1. **HIRE Assessment**: Hunger, Intelligence, Resourcefulness, Experience
2. **Skills Matrix**: Technical skills vs strategic thinking vs communication
3. **Trial Project Protocol**: Real-world test tasks before full hiring commitment
4. **Ramp-Up Timeline**: 30/60/90 day expectations for new media buyers
5. **Retention Framework**: Growth paths that keep top talent engaged

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Informs squad agent role definitions and capability requirements
- Assessment framework helps evaluate team member readiness
- Onboarding aligns with new account onboarding workflow
- Retention concepts ensure squad knowledge continuity

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Hire for adaptability — platforms change, principles endure
2. Test candidates with real scenarios, not just interviews
3. Strategic thinking is harder to teach than platform skills
4. Clear growth paths prevent turnover of trained talent
5. Document institutional knowledge so it survives personnel changes
6. The best media buyers are curious, data-driven, and resilient
