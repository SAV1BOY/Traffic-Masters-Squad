# Ultimate CBO Cookbook
> **Author**: Depesh Mandalia
> **Year**: 2023
> **Category**: Meta Ads Scaling & CBO

## Key Concepts
- Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO) as the primary scaling mechanism on Meta
- CBO structures that allow Meta's algorithm to allocate budget efficiently
- When to use CBO vs ABO and how to transition between them
- Budget scaling recipes: vertical (budget increase) and horizontal (new ad sets)
- Creative testing within CBO frameworks without disrupting delivery

## Core Frameworks
1. **CBO Structure**: Campaign > Ad Sets (audience segments) > Ads (creative variations)
2. **Budget Scaling Recipe**: 15-20% increases every 48-72 hours
3. **Creative Refresh Protocol**: Add new creatives without resetting learning
4. **Ad Set Design**: Broad vs stacked vs lookalike audience combinations
5. **Kill Criteria**: When to pause underperforming ad sets within CBO

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Primary scaling methodology for Meta Ads campaigns
- Directly feeds into scaling-stability-loop workflow
- CBO structure informs campaign build in launch workflows
- Kill criteria align with daily optimization loop decisions

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. CBO works best with 3-5 ad sets per campaign
2. Let the algorithm learn before judging — minimum 50 conversions
3. Scale budget on the campaign, not individual ad sets
4. Add creatives to existing ad sets rather than creating new ones
5. Broad targeting + strong creative often outperforms narrow targeting
6. Monitor at ad set level but optimize at campaign level
7. Never scale a campaign that has not demonstrated 7-day stability
