# Viewability
> **Author**: Todd Breeze (Tommie Powers)
> **Year**: 2018
> **Category**: YouTube Advertising

## Key Concepts
- Viewability as the core metric for YouTube ad success
- YouTube is the second-largest search engine — intent-based video targeting
- In-stream ads: skippable vs non-skippable strategy differences
- Video ad structure: hook (0-5s), story (5-30s), CTA (last 5s)
- Audience targeting on YouTube: custom intent, affinity, life events, placements

## Core Frameworks
1. **Video Ad Structure**: Hook → Problem → Solution → Proof → CTA
2. **Targeting Layers**: Keywords, custom intent, placements, remarketing
3. **View Rate Optimization**: Improving the percentage of viewers who watch past 30s
4. **YouTube Funnel**: Discovery ads → In-stream ads → Retargeting → Conversion
5. **Budget Pacing**: CPV bidding strategies for optimal delivery

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Foundation for YouTube advertising campaigns
- Video ad structure informs creative production workflow
- Targeting frameworks complement Google Ads strategy
- YouTube as a mid-funnel warming channel for full-funnel builds

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. YouTube ads need to hook in the first 5 seconds or lose the viewer
2. Custom intent audiences (based on search behavior) are YouTube's secret weapon
3. View rate above 25% indicates strong creative-audience fit
4. YouTube retargeting audiences are highly valuable for cross-platform campaigns
5. Test skippable vs non-skippable based on campaign objective
6. YouTube ads build brand while driving direct response
