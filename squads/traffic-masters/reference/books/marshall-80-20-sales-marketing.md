# 80/20 Sales and Marketing
> **Author**: Perry Marshall
> **Year**: 2013
> **Category**: Marketing Strategy & Efficiency

## Key Concepts
- The Pareto Principle (80/20) applies fractally to all marketing activities
- 80/20 of 80/20: the top 4% of efforts produce 64% of results
- Power curves in customer value: a tiny fraction of customers are worth exponentially more
- Rack the shotgun: qualify prospects quickly to focus on high-value opportunities
- Traffic quality matters more than traffic quantity

## Core Frameworks
1. **Power Curve Analysis**: Map customer value distribution to find the vital few
2. **Rack the Shotgun**: Quick qualification to separate buyers from browsers
3. **80/20 Budget Allocation**: Invest disproportionately in what works
4. **Star Principle**: Best market + best position = exponential returns
5. **Swiss Army Knife**: One tool (or campaign) that serves multiple purposes

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Budget allocation should follow 80/20 (invest more in proven winners)
- Customer value analysis informs LTV-based bidding strategies
- Qualification thinking improves lead quality from traffic campaigns
- Prevents equal-budget-spread syndrome across campaigns

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Not all traffic is equal — a small fraction drives most value
2. Allocate budget proportionally to proven performance, not equally
3. Qualify early in the funnel to improve downstream conversion
4. Look for the vital few campaigns, audiences, and creatives
5. Diminishing returns are real — know when to stop scaling
6. Apply 80/20 to time management: focus optimization effort where it matters
