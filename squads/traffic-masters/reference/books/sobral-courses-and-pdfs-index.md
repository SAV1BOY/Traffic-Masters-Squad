# Pedro Sobral — Courses and PDFs Index
> **Author**: Pedro Sobral
> **Year**: Ongoing (2018-present)
> **Category**: Traffic Management (Brazilian Market)

## Overview
Pedro Sobral is Brazil's most influential traffic manager educator. His materials form the backbone
of traffic management methodology in the Brazilian and LATAM digital marketing ecosystem.

## Key Courses
1. **Comunidade Sobral de Trafego**: Main community with weekly lives and updated strategies
2. **Curso de Meta Ads**: Comprehensive Meta Ads course from beginner to advanced
3. **Curso de Google Ads**: Google Ads fundamentals through advanced strategies
4. **Gestao de Trafego**: Business of traffic management (pricing, clients, operations)
5. **Lancamentos com Trafego Pago**: Traffic strategy for digital product launches

## Key PDFs and Frameworks
- **Checklist de Campanha**: Pre-launch campaign checklist
- **Estrutura de Campanhas**: Campaign structure templates for Meta and Google
- **Metricas de Trafego**: KPI definitions and benchmark ranges
- **Relatorio de Trafego**: Reporting templates for client communication
- **Funil de Lancamento**: Launch funnel traffic stages (8-phase model)

## Core Methodologies
1. **8-Phase Launch Traffic**: Pre → Capture → Warm → Open → Sustain → Urgency → Close → Post
2. **Campaign Structure**: Organized by objective with clear naming conventions
3. **Daily Optimization Routine**: Systematic daily review process
4. **Audience Temperature Model**: Cold, warm, hot with specific strategies per temperature
5. **Metrics Hierarchy**: Which metrics matter at which funnel stage
