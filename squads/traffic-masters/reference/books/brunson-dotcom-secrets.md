# DotCom Secrets
> **Author**: Russell Brunson
> **Year**: 2015 (Updated 2020)
> **Category**: Funnels and Offers

## Key Concepts
- Sales funnels are the mechanism that turns traffic into revenue
- Value ladders: ascending offers from free to high-ticket
- Attractive character: the persona that makes marketing personal
- Funnel types: lead funnels, webinar funnels, product launch funnels, high-ticket funnels
- Traffic is useless without a converting funnel to send it to

## Core Frameworks
1. **Value Ladder**: Free content → Lead magnet → Low-ticket → Core → High-ticket
2. **Attractive Character**: Leader, adventurer, reporter, or reluctant hero
3. **Soap Opera Sequence**: Email follow-up that builds relationship through story
4. **Funnel Blueprint**: Each funnel type matched to traffic temperature
5. **Epiphany Bridge**: Story structure that creates belief change

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Understanding funnels helps traffic managers optimize for the right conversion event
- Value ladder informs which offers to promote at each funnel stage
- Funnel type determines campaign objective and audience targeting
- Traffic temperature matching to funnel type prevents misalignment

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Never send traffic to a page — send traffic to a funnel
2. The value ladder determines what you promote at each traffic temperature
3. Cold traffic goes to lead magnets; warm traffic goes to webinars; hot goes to sales pages
4. Landing page conversion rate is as important as ad performance
5. Email follow-up sequences multiply the ROI of paid traffic
6. Every funnel element should be tested: headline, copy, CTA, design
