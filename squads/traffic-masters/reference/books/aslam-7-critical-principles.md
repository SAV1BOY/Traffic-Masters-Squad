# 7 Critical Principles of Effective Digital Marketing
> **Author**: Kasim Aslam
> **Year**: 2017
> **Category**: Digital Marketing Strategy

## Key Concepts
- Seven foundational principles that every digital marketer must understand
- Data-driven decision making over gut instinct
- Customer lifetime value as the north star metric
- Multi-channel presence with unified messaging
- Testing as a continuous discipline, not a one-time activity

## Core Frameworks
1. **Principle of Measurement**: Track everything, decide on data
2. **Principle of LTV**: Optimize for customer lifetime value, not just first purchase
3. **Principle of Omnipresence**: Be where your customers are across channels
4. **Principle of Testing**: Always be testing; assume nothing works until proven
5. **Principle of Simplicity**: Simple funnels outperform complex ones
6. **Principle of Speed**: Fast execution beats perfect planning
7. **Principle of Consistency**: Consistent effort compounds over time

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Measurement principle aligns with tracking and attribution workflows
- LTV focus connects business metrics to traffic optimization
- Omnipresence supports cross-platform expansion strategy
- Testing discipline mirrors creative testing sprint workflow

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Decisions based on data, not opinions
2. Optimize for LTV, not just CPA — a higher CPA can be profitable
3. Show up on multiple platforms with consistent messaging
4. Test everything: audiences, creatives, offers, landing pages
5. Simple campaigns that work beat complex campaigns that confuse
6. Speed of implementation is a competitive advantage
