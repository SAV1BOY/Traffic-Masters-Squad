# Ultimate Guide to Facebook Advertising
> **Author**: Perry Marshall, Bob Regnerus, Thomas Meloche, Keith Krance
> **Year**: 2020 (4th Edition)
> **Category**: Facebook/Meta Ads

## Key Concepts
- Facebook advertising as a demand-generation platform (interrupt marketing)
- Audience-first approach: understand who before deciding what to say
- The hook-story-offer framework for Facebook ad creative
- Lookalike audiences and custom audience strategy
- Campaign structure for testing and scaling on Facebook/Meta

## Core Frameworks
1. **Hook-Story-Offer**: Three-part ad creative formula
2. **Audience Layering**: Interest stacking, exclusions, and lookalikes
3. **Campaign Structure**: Testing campaigns vs scaling campaigns
4. **Ad Type Matrix**: When to use image, video, carousel, or collection
5. **Retargeting Waterfall**: Sequential retargeting based on engagement depth

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Core reference for Meta Ads campaign strategy
- Hook-story-offer feeds creative production workflow
- Audience strategy informs retargeting sequence build
- Campaign structure aligns with Mandalia BPM approach

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Facebook is interruption marketing — your ad competes with friends and family
2. The hook is everything: you have 1-3 seconds to stop the scroll
3. Story creates emotional connection; offer creates action
4. Lookalike audiences amplify your best customers at scale
5. Test audiences before creatives; test creatives before offers
6. Retargeting is where Facebook ROI is highest
