# Ogilvy on Advertising
> **Author**: David Ogilvy
> **Year**: 1983
> **Category**: Classic Advertising Principles

## Key Concepts
- Research-driven advertising: understand your audience before writing a word
- The headline is the most important element — five times more people read the headline than the body
- Specificity sells: concrete facts outperform vague adjectives
- Long copy outsells short copy when the product requires explanation
- Brand image is built by every ad, not just brand campaigns

## Core Frameworks
1. **Research First**: Study the product, the competition, and the consumer deeply
2. **Big Idea**: Every great campaign is built around one powerful idea
3. **Headline Dominance**: 80% of ad success is determined by the headline
4. **Specificity Principle**: Specific claims are more believable than general ones
5. **Brand Personality**: Every ad contributes to the long-term brand image

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Headline/hook principles directly apply to digital ad creative
- Research-first approach aligns with strategy phase of all workflows
- Specificity principle improves CTR and conversion rates in ad copy
- Brand consistency reminder: performance ads still build (or erode) brand

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. The headline (or hook) carries 80% of the weight — invest time there
2. Use specific numbers and facts instead of adjectives and superlatives
3. Research the audience deeply before creating any ad
4. Every ad is a brand ad — performance campaigns still shape perception
5. Test headlines obsessively — it is the highest-leverage creative element
6. Simple, clear communication outperforms clever, ambiguous messaging
