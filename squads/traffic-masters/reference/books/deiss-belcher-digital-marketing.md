# Digital Marketing: Integrating Strategy and Tactics
> **Author**: Ryan Deiss, Russ Henneberry
> **Year**: 2020 (2nd Edition)
> **Category**: Digital Marketing Strategy

## Key Concepts
- The Customer Value Journey (CVJ): 8 stages from awareness to advocacy
- Content marketing as the engine that drives the customer journey
- Traffic is the fuel; the funnel is the engine; the offer is the destination
- Multi-channel digital marketing integration
- Metrics and measurement at every journey stage

## Core Frameworks
1. **Customer Value Journey**: Aware → Engage → Subscribe → Convert → Excite → Ascend → Advocate → Promote
2. **Content Mapping**: Right content for each CVJ stage
3. **Traffic Temperature Model**: Cold, warm, hot alignment with CVJ
4. **Offer Architecture**: Lead magnet → tripwire → core offer → profit maximizer
5. **Campaign Planning Canvas**: Visual planning tool for integrated campaigns

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- CVJ provides the strategic backbone for full-funnel traffic
- Traffic temperature model aligns with audience segmentation
- Offer architecture informs what traffic campaigns are driving toward
- Campaign planning canvas useful for complex multi-channel launches

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Traffic without a customer journey is just paid attention
2. Each CVJ stage requires different messaging and metrics
3. The offer architecture determines how traffic converts into revenue
4. Cold traffic campaigns should aim for micro-commitments, not sales
5. Integration across channels amplifies each individual channel's impact
6. Measure at every stage — awareness metrics differ from conversion metrics
