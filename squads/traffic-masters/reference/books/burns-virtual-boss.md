# Virtual Boss
> **Author**: Molly Pittman & John Belcher
> **Year**: 2021
> **Category**: Agency Operations & Management

## Key Concepts
- Building and running a digital marketing team or agency in a virtual environment
- SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures) are the backbone of scalable operations
- Hiring, training, and managing remote media buyers and strategists
- Client management frameworks for traffic agencies
- Performance review systems tied to campaign KPIs

## Core Frameworks
1. **SOP Library**: Document every repeatable process (campaign launches, optimizations, reporting)
2. **Hiring Matrix**: Skills and traits to look for in traffic team members
3. **Client Communication Cadence**: Weekly updates, monthly reviews, quarterly strategy
4. **Performance Dashboards**: Metrics that matter for team management

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Squad operational model benefits from SOP-driven processes
- Agent workflows mirror the documented procedures concept
- Client handoff and reporting workflows align with communication cadence
- Quality gates in workflows reflect the accountability systems described

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Every workflow should have a documented SOP
2. Clear roles and responsibilities prevent dropped balls
3. Client communication should be proactive, not reactive
4. Performance reviews should tie to measurable outcomes
5. Virtual teams need more documentation, not less
6. Build a knowledge base that survives team member turnover
