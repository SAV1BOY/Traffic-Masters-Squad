# Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion
> **Author**: Robert Cialdini
> **Year**: 1984 (Updated 2021)
> **Category**: Psychology of Persuasion

## Key Concepts
- Six (now seven) universal principles of influence that drive human decisions
- These principles are hardwired — they work across cultures and contexts
- Ethical application of influence principles in advertising and marketing
- Understanding persuasion mechanics improves ad creative and landing page design

## Core Frameworks (7 Principles)
1. **Reciprocity**: Give first, receive later — free value creates obligation
2. **Commitment/Consistency**: Small yeses lead to big yeses
3. **Social Proof**: People follow what others do, especially similar others
4. **Authority**: Experts and credentials increase trust and compliance
5. **Liking**: People buy from those they like and relate to
6. **Scarcity**: Limited availability increases perceived value
7. **Unity**: Shared identity creates stronger persuasion (added in 2021 edition)

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Every ad creative should leverage at least 2-3 influence principles
- Social proof: testimonials, numbers, user counts in ad copy
- Scarcity/urgency: limited-time offers, countdown timers
- Reciprocity: lead magnets, free content, value-first approach
- Authority: credentials, media logos, expert endorsements

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Layer multiple influence principles into every ad for maximum impact
2. Social proof is the most powerful principle for cold traffic
3. Scarcity must be genuine — fake urgency destroys trust long-term
4. Reciprocity explains why lead magnets and free content convert
5. Authority signals (logos, certifications) reduce friction on landing pages
6. Unity (shared identity) makes UGC and community-based ads powerful
