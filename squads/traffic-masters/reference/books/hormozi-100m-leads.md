# $100M Leads
> **Author**: Alex Hormozi
> **Year**: 2023
> **Category**: Lead Generation & Customer Acquisition

## Key Concepts
- Lead generation is the lifeblood of every business
- Four core ways to get leads: warm outreach, cold outreach, content, paid ads
- Advertising is buying attention with money; content is buying attention with effort
- Lead magnets must solve a narrow problem completely, not a broad problem partially
- More leads at a lower cost beats fewer leads at a higher quality (volume wins with systems)

## Core Frameworks
1. **Core Four**: Warm outreach, cold outreach, content creation, paid advertising
2. **Lead Magnet Framework**: Solve one narrow problem for free to create obligation
3. **Advertising Math**: LTV > CPA for every channel to be sustainable
4. **Content-to-Ads Pipeline**: Test organically, scale with paid
5. **Lead Quality Scoring**: Not all leads are equal — score and segment

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- Paid advertising is one of the Core Four — understand its role in the ecosystem
- Lead magnet framework informs TOFU campaign offers
- Advertising math connects to budget review and LTV-based optimization
- Content-to-ads pipeline is a creative testing methodology

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Paid ads are the fastest way to generate leads at scale
2. Lead magnets should over-deliver value to create reciprocity
3. If LTV > CPA, scale aggressively — do not optimize for efficiency alone
4. Test organic content first; scale winners with paid budget
5. Volume of leads allows for better optimization and learning
6. Combine paid ads with other lead sources for maximum impact
