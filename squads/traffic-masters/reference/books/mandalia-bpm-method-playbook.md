# BPM Method Playbook
> **Author**: Depesh Mandalia
> **Year**: 2025
> **Category**: Meta Ads Advanced Strategy

## Key Concepts
- BPM (Business, Product, Market) framework for ad strategy
- Start with business economics before touching the ad platform
- Product-market fit validation through ad testing
- Market sophistication levels determine messaging approach
- Unit economics drive campaign structure and bidding

## Core Frameworks
1. **Business Layer**: Define acceptable CPA based on LTV, margins, and payback period
2. **Product Layer**: Identify unique mechanism, transformation, and proof elements
3. **Market Layer**: Map audience awareness (Schwartz levels) and sophistication
4. **Testing Protocol**: Structured testing with statistical rigor at each layer
5. **Scaling Framework**: Scale only after all three layers are validated

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- BPM informs strategy before campaign build (strategy-first approach)
- Business layer connects to budget review and ROI workflows
- Product layer feeds creative angles and hooks
- Market layer determines audience segmentation and messaging
- Testing protocol aligns with creative testing sprint workflow

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Never build campaigns without understanding business economics first
2. Product understanding drives creative differentiation
3. Market sophistication determines whether features or story sells
4. Test at the BPM level, not just the ad level
5. Scaling requires all three layers to be validated
6. CBO structure works best when BPM foundation is solid
