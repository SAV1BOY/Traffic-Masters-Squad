# Contagious: Why Things Catch On
> **Author**: Jonah Berger
> **Year**: 2013
> **Category**: Virality & Word of Mouth

## Key Concepts
- Six principles that make content shareable and ideas spread
- Word of mouth (online and offline) drives more action than advertising
- Social currency: people share things that make them look good
- Practical value: useful content gets shared more than entertaining content
- Triggers: environmental cues that remind people of your brand or message

## Core Frameworks (STEPPS)
1. **Social Currency**: Makes sharer look smart, cool, or in-the-know
2. **Triggers**: Environmental cues that keep brand top-of-mind
3. **Emotion**: High-arousal emotions (awe, anxiety, humor) drive sharing
4. **Public**: Observable behavior is imitable — make consumption visible
5. **Practical Value**: Useful information gets passed along
6. **Stories**: Narratives carry messages as passengers in the story

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- STEPPS framework helps create organic amplification of paid content
- Social currency explains why people share certain ads but not others
- Triggers inform retargeting timing and environmental associations
- Emotion selection for ad creative (high-arousal vs low-arousal)

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Design ads people want to share — this multiplies paid reach organically
2. High-arousal emotions (surprise, humor, awe) generate more shares than calm content
3. Practical value (tips, hacks, how-tos) drives sharing in educational content ads
4. Associate your brand with common environmental triggers for top-of-mind recall
5. Make results visible and shareable (before/after, progress posts)
6. Stories carry your brand message further than direct promotion
