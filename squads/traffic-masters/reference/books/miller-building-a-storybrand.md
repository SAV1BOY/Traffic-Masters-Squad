# Building a StoryBrand
> **Author**: Donald Miller
> **Year**: 2017
> **Category**: Brand Messaging & Clarity

## Key Concepts
- Customers are the hero; your brand is the guide
- The StoryBrand framework clarifies messaging so customers engage
- If you confuse, you lose — clarity beats cleverness in marketing
- The SB7 Framework: character, problem, guide, plan, CTA, failure, success
- Every ad, landing page, and email should follow the story structure

## Core Frameworks
1. **SB7 Framework**:
   - Character: the customer (with a want)
   - Problem: external, internal, philosophical
   - Guide: your brand (with empathy and authority)
   - Plan: simple steps to success
   - Call to Action: direct and transitional CTAs
   - Failure: what happens if they do not act
   - Success: the transformation achieved
2. **One-Liner**: A concise brand pitch using the SB7 structure
3. **Wireframe**: Website/landing page structure following SB7

## How It Applies to Traffic Squad
- SB7 framework is a landing page and ad copy structure
- "Ad scent" — alignment between ad message and landing page message
- Clarity in ad copy improves CTR and reduces bounce rate
- The guide positioning (empathy + authority) improves brand trust

## Key Takeaways for the Squad
1. Position the customer as the hero, not your brand
2. Clarity in ad copy improves every metric downstream (CTR, CVR, bounce)
3. Name the problem clearly — external, internal, and philosophical
4. Show a simple plan: "Step 1, Step 2, Step 3" reduces perceived effort
5. Use both direct CTAs ("Buy Now") and transitional CTAs ("Learn More")
6. Paint both failure (what they lose) and success (what they gain)
