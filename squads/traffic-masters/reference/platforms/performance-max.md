# Performance Max (PMax) Specifications
> **Platform**: Google Ads — Performance Max
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Overview
Performance Max is Google's AI-driven campaign type that serves ads across all Google surfaces: Search, Shopping, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Discover, and Maps.

## Asset Group Requirements
- **Text**: Up to 5 headlines (30 chars), 5 long headlines (90 chars), 5 descriptions (90 chars), 1 business name, 1 short description
- **Images**: Up to 20 images. Landscape (1200x628), square (1200x1200), portrait (960x1200). Min 3 images.
- **Video**: Up to 5 videos. Min 10 seconds. If not provided, Google auto-generates (low quality).
- **Logos**: Landscape (1200x300) and square (1200x1200)
- **Final URL**: Landing page URL. URL expansion on by default.

## Audience Signals
- Custom segments (search terms, URLs, apps)
- First-party data (customer lists, website visitors)
- Interests and demographics
- Signals are suggestions, not hard targeting — PMax will go broader

## Campaign Structure Best Practices
- 1 PMax campaign per conversion goal or product category
- Separate asset groups for distinct product lines or audiences
- Minimum budget: 10x target CPA per day recommended
- Allow 2-4 weeks learning phase before judging performance

## Reporting and Insights
- Asset performance rating: Low, Good, Best
- Placement reporting available (where ads appeared)
- Search terms insights (limited but improving)
- Audience insights showing which signals drive results

## Key Best Practices
- Always upload custom videos — auto-generated videos underperform
- Use audience signals aggressively — better signals improve learning speed
- Monitor URL expansion — may send traffic to unwanted pages
- Exclude brand terms if running separate brand Search campaigns
- Feed quality is critical for retail/e-commerce PMax
- Layer PMax with standard Search for maximum control
