# Google Ads Search Specifications
> **Platform**: Google Ads (Search Network)
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Ad Formats
- **Responsive Search Ads (RSA)**: Up to 15 headlines (30 chars each), 4 descriptions (90 chars each)
- **Dynamic Search Ads (DSA)**: Auto-generated headlines from website content
- **Call Ads**: Mobile-only, click-to-call format

## Character Limits
- Headline: 30 characters each, up to 15 headlines
- Description: 90 characters each, up to 4 descriptions
- Display URL path: 15 characters per path field (2 paths)
- Minimum pinned: at least 3 headlines and 2 descriptions recommended for RSA

## Extensions (Assets)
- Sitelinks: 25 char headline, 2x35 char descriptions
- Callouts: 25 characters each
- Structured snippets: header + values
- Call, Location, Price, Promotion, Image, Lead Form

## Bidding Strategies
- Manual CPC, Enhanced CPC
- Target CPA, Target ROAS, Maximize Conversions, Maximize Conversion Value

## Match Types
- Broad Match: widest reach, AI-driven matching
- Phrase Match: includes meaning of keyword
- Exact Match: close variants of exact keyword

## Key Best Practices
- Use all available headline and description slots
- Pin critical messaging to position 1 headline
- Negative keyword lists updated weekly
- Quality Score optimization: relevance, landing page, expected CTR
- Enhanced Conversions for better signal quality
- Segment campaigns by intent level (brand, non-brand, competitor)
