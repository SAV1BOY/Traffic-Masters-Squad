# Pinterest Ads Specifications
> **Platform**: Pinterest Ads Manager
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Ad Formats
- **Standard Pin**: 1000x1500 (2:3) recommended. JPG/PNG. Max 20MB.
- **Video Pin**: 1:1, 2:3, 9:16. MP4/MOV. 4s-15min (6-15s recommended). Max 2GB.
- **Carousel**: 2-5 cards. 1:1 or 2:3 per card.
- **Shopping Ads**: Product catalog integration with price and availability.
- **Collections**: Hero image/video + 3 secondary product images.
- **Idea Pins (Paid)**: Multi-page story format. Up to 20 pages.

## Text Limits
- Title: 100 characters
- Description: 500 characters
- Overlay text on image: keep minimal, 20% rule of thumb

## Targeting Options
- Interests, Keywords, Demographics
- Customer Lists, Website Visitors, Engagement Audiences
- Actalike Audiences (Pinterest's version of lookalikes)
- Automatic Targeting

## Bidding
- Automatic bid, Custom bid (CPC, CPM)
- Optimization goals: Awareness, Consideration, Conversions, Catalog Sales

## Audience Characteristics
- 60%+ female audience, high purchase intent
- Users come to Pinterest to plan purchases (high commercial intent)
- Longer consideration cycles — pins have long shelf life (months)
- Popular categories: home decor, fashion, food, DIY, beauty, weddings

## Key Best Practices
- 2:3 vertical format performs best in the feed
- Pinterest is a search and discovery platform — keyword targeting is powerful
- Pins have long organic lifespan — unlike other social ads
- Lifestyle and aspirational imagery outperforms product-only shots
- Rich Pins (with real-time product data) improve shopping conversions
- Plan campaigns 2-4 weeks before seasonal moments (users plan ahead)
