# YouTube Ads Specifications
> **Platform**: YouTube (via Google Ads)
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Ad Formats
- **Skippable In-Stream**: Plays before/during/after video. Skip after 5s. Charged at 30s or completion.
- **Non-Skippable In-Stream**: 15-20 seconds. Cannot be skipped. Charged per impression (CPM).
- **Bumper Ads**: 6 seconds max. Non-skippable. CPM-based. Brand awareness.
- **In-Feed (Discovery)**: Thumbnail + text in search results and related videos. Charged on click.
- **Shorts Ads**: Vertical video ads between YouTube Shorts. Up to 60 seconds.
- **Masthead**: Homepage takeover. Reserved buy only.

## Video Specs
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (16:9), 1080x1080 (1:1), 1080x1920 (9:16 for Shorts)
- File size: Max 256GB. Format: MP4, MOV, AVI, WMV
- Duration: Skippable (no max, 15s-3min recommended), Non-skip (15-20s), Bumper (6s)

## Targeting Options
- Keywords, Topics, Placements (specific channels/videos)
- Custom Intent audiences (based on search behavior)
- Affinity and In-Market audiences
- Remarketing (video viewers, channel subscribers)
- Demographics and Life Events

## Bidding Strategies
- CPV (cost per view) for skippable ads
- CPM (cost per thousand) for non-skippable and bumper
- Target CPA for action campaigns (Video Action Campaigns)
- Target ROAS for conversion value optimization

## Key Metrics
- View Rate: percentage of views (30s or completion) vs impressions
- Cost Per View (CPV): average cost per qualified view
- Earned Actions: likes, shares, subscribes from ad exposure

## Key Best Practices
- Hook in first 5 seconds — viewers decide to skip or stay
- Include captions — many viewers watch without sound
- Custom Intent audiences are YouTube's strongest targeting
- Upload multiple video lengths for format flexibility
- Video Action Campaigns for direct response objectives
- Retarget video viewers across Google Ads ecosystem
