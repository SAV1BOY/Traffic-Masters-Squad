# LinkedIn Ads Specifications
> **Platform**: LinkedIn Campaign Manager
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Ad Formats
- **Single Image**: 1200x627 (1.91:1) or 1080x1080 (1:1). JPG/PNG. Max 5MB.
- **Video**: 16:9, 1:1, 9:16. MP4. 3s-30min (15-30s recommended). Max 200MB.
- **Carousel**: 1080x1080 per card. 2-10 cards.
- **Document Ads**: PDF, DOC, PPT displayed as carousel. Lead gen without download.
- **Message Ads**: Direct InMail messages. Subject + body + CTA.
- **Conversation Ads**: Multi-CTA interactive message format.
- **Text Ads**: 25 char headline + 75 char description + 100x100 image.

## Text Limits
- Introductory text: 600 characters (150 recommended before truncation)
- Headline: 200 characters (70 recommended)
- Description: 300 characters

## Targeting Options
- Job title, function, seniority, industry, company size, company name
- Skills, groups, education, years of experience
- Matched Audiences (website retargeting, CRM lists, lookalikes)
- Account-Based Marketing (ABM) with company lists

## Bidding
- CPC, CPM, Cost per Send (message ads)
- Minimum daily budget varies by market (typically $10 USD)

## Key Best Practices
- Professional tone but not boring — authenticity matters on LinkedIn too
- Document Ads generate leads without requiring a landing page
- ABM targeting is LinkedIn's unique advantage for B2B
- Video completion rates are higher on LinkedIn than most social platforms
- Conversation Ads outperform traditional Message Ads
- Audience size sweet spot: 50,000-500,000 for most B2B campaigns
