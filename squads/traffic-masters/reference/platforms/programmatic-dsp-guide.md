# Programmatic DSP Guide
> **Platform**: Demand-Side Platforms (General)
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Overview
Programmatic advertising uses automated technology to buy and optimize display, video, native, and connected TV (CTV) ads across the open internet through real-time bidding (RTB).

## Major DSPs
- Google DV360 (Display & Video 360)
- Amazon DSP
- The Trade Desk
- Meta Audience Network (limited)
- Xandr (Microsoft)

## Ad Formats
- **Display**: 300x250, 728x90, 160x600, 320x50 (mobile), 300x600. IAB standard sizes.
- **Native**: Headline + description + image integrated into publisher content.
- **Video**: Pre-roll, mid-roll, outstream. VAST/VPAID compliant.
- **CTV/OTT**: Full-screen video on connected TV devices. Non-skippable 15s/30s.
- **Audio**: Streaming audio ads on platforms like Spotify, podcasts.

## Targeting Capabilities
- First-party data onboarding (CRM lists matched to device IDs/cookies)
- Third-party data segments (demographics, interests, purchase behavior)
- Contextual targeting (page content, keywords, sentiment)
- Geotargeting and geofencing
- Retargeting across the open web

## Buying Models
- CPM (cost per thousand impressions) — most common
- CPC (cost per click) — performance-focused
- CPCV (cost per completed view) — video specific
- dCPM (dynamic CPM) — algorithm-optimized bidding

## Key Best Practices
- Programmatic excels at retargeting and broad reach at scale
- Contextual targeting is privacy-friendly and gaining importance
- Frequency capping is essential — set at 3-5 impressions per user per day
- Viewability targeting: aim for 70%+ viewable impressions
- Brand safety: use inclusion/exclusion lists and verification vendors
- CTV is growing rapidly as a performance channel, not just branding
