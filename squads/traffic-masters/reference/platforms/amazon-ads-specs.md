# Amazon Ads Specifications
> **Platform**: Amazon Advertising
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Ad Types
- **Sponsored Products**: Keyword/product-targeted ads in search results and product pages. CPC.
- **Sponsored Brands**: Banner ads with brand logo, headline, and product selection. CPC.
- **Sponsored Display**: Retargeting and audience-based display ads on/off Amazon. CPC/CPM.
- **Sponsored Brands Video**: Video ads in search results. Auto-play, muted.
- **Amazon DSP**: Programmatic display and video ads across Amazon and third-party sites. CPM.

## Creative Specs
- Sponsored Products: Uses existing product listing images (no custom creative)
- Sponsored Brands: Logo (400x400, PNG), headline (50 chars), up to 3 products
- Sponsored Brands Video: 6-45s, 1920x1080 or 1280x720, MP4. No letterboxing.
- Sponsored Display: 1200x628 or 600x500. Custom headline (50 chars) and logo.

## Targeting Options
- Keyword targeting (broad, phrase, exact)
- Product targeting (specific ASINs or categories)
- Audience targeting (in-market, lifestyle, remarketing)
- Negative targeting (keyword and product exclusions)

## Bidding
- Dynamic bidding (up and down, down only)
- Fixed bids, rule-based bidding
- Budget: daily budget per campaign, no minimums for Sponsored Products

## Key Best Practices
- Product listing optimization is prerequisite — ads drive to listings
- Use all match types with separate campaigns for control
- Sponsored Brands Video has highest engagement in search results
- Amazon DSP for off-Amazon retargeting and brand awareness
- ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales) is the primary efficiency metric
- Negative targeting prevents wasted spend on irrelevant searches
- Review search term reports weekly for keyword discovery and negatives
