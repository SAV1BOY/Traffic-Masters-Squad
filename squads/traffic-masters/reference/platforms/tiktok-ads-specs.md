# TikTok Ads Specifications
> **Platform**: TikTok Ads Manager
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Ad Formats
- **In-Feed Ads**: Native video ads in the For You page. 5-60s. Full screen vertical.
- **TopView**: First ad seen when opening TikTok. Up to 60s. Premium placement.
- **Spark Ads**: Boost existing organic TikTok posts as ads. Native engagement preserved.
- **Collection Ads**: Video with product catalog integration.
- **Lead Gen Ads**: In-app lead form without leaving TikTok.
- **Shopping Ads**: Product catalog ads for e-commerce.

## Video Specs
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (recommended), 1:1, 16:9
- Resolution: 720x1280 minimum, 1080x1920 recommended
- File size: Max 500MB. Format: MP4, MOV, AVI
- Duration: 5-60 seconds (15-30s recommended for best performance)

## Text Limits
- Ad description: 1-100 characters (varies by language)
- Display name: 2-20 characters
- CTA: Pre-set options (Shop Now, Learn More, Sign Up, etc.)

## Targeting Options
- Demographics, Interests, Behaviors
- Custom Audiences (website, app, customer file, engagement)
- Lookalike Audiences
- Automatic Targeting (TikTok AI-driven)
- Hashtag and creator targeting

## Bidding
- Lowest Cost, Cost Cap, Bid Cap
- Optimization goals: Conversions, Clicks, Reach, Video Views, Lead Gen

## Key Best Practices
- Native content outperforms polished ads — think creator, not advertiser
- Hook in first 1-2 seconds (faster than other platforms)
- Use trending sounds and formats when relevant
- Spark Ads preserve social proof and tend to outperform standard in-feed
- Vertical full-screen (9:16) is mandatory for best performance
- Creative fatigue happens faster on TikTok — refresh every 7-14 days
- UGC-style content significantly outperforms brand-polished content
