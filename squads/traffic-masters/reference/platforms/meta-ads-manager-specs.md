# Meta Ads Manager Specifications
> **Platform**: Meta (Facebook + Instagram)
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Ad Formats and Specs
- **Single Image**: 1080x1080 (1:1), 1080x1350 (4:5), 1200x628 (1.91:1). Max 30MB. JPG/PNG.
- **Video**: 1080x1080, 1080x1920 (9:16), 1920x1080 (16:9). Max 4GB. MP4/MOV. Up to 240min.
- **Carousel**: 1080x1080 per card. 2-10 cards. Image or video per card.
- **Collection**: Cover image/video + product catalog. Instant Experience required.
- **Stories/Reels**: 1080x1920 (9:16). Full-screen vertical. 15-60s for Reels.

## Text Limits
- Primary text: 125 characters recommended (up to 1,000+ allowed)
- Headline: 27 characters recommended (up to 255 allowed)
- Description: 27 characters recommended
- Text overlay: under 20% of image area recommended (no longer penalized but best practice)

## Campaign Objectives
- Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, App Promotion, Sales

## Targeting Options
- Custom Audiences (website, app, CRM, engagement)
- Lookalike Audiences (1%-10% based on source audience)
- Detailed Targeting (interests, behaviors, demographics)
- Advantage+ Audiences (AI-driven broad targeting)

## Bidding and Budget
- CBO or Ad Set Budget. Minimum varies by country.
- Bid strategies: Lowest cost, Cost cap, Bid cap, ROAS target
- Learning phase: approximately 50 conversions per week per ad set

## Key Best Practices
- Mobile-first creative design (90%+ of impressions are mobile)
- Video ads: hook in first 3 seconds, captions always on
- Advantage+ Shopping campaigns for e-commerce
- Broad targeting + strong creative often outperforms narrow targeting
- Refresh creatives every 2-4 weeks to prevent fatigue
