# Paid Traffic Playbook for Info-Product / Course Creators

> Complete playbook for running paid traffic to online courses, digital products, coaching programs, and memberships.

---

## 1. Funnel Architecture

### Common Info-Product Funnels

**Funnel A: Direct to Offer**
```
Ad -> Sales Page -> Checkout -> Thank You
Best for: Low-ticket ($7-$97), warm audiences, simple offers
```

**Funnel B: Lead Magnet -> Nurture -> Offer**
```
Ad -> Lead Magnet LP -> Email Sequence (5-7 emails) -> Sales Page -> Checkout
Best for: Mid-ticket ($97-$497), cold audiences
```

**Funnel C: Webinar Funnel**
```
Ad -> Webinar Registration -> Webinar (live or automated) -> Offer -> Checkout
Best for: Mid-to-high ticket ($297-$2,000+)
```

**Funnel D: Video Sales Letter (VSL)**
```
Ad -> VSL Page (15-45 min video) -> Checkout
Best for: Mid-to-high ticket, direct response
```

**Funnel E: Challenge / Boot Camp**
```
Ad -> Challenge Registration -> 3-5 Day Challenge -> Offer -> Checkout
Best for: High engagement, community building, $497-$5,000
```

## 3. Meta Ads Strategy

### Campaign Structure

```
Cold Traffic (Prospecting)
├── Broad targeting (age + gender only)
├── Interest stacks (industry, topic, competitor interests)
├── Lookalike: Purchasers (1%, 2%, 3-5%)
├── Lookalike: Email list (1%, 2%)
└── Lookalike: Webinar registrants (1%)

Warm Traffic (Retargeting)
├── Video viewers (25%, 50%, 75%, 95%)
├── Page engagers (90 days)
├── Website visitors (7, 14, 30 days)
├── Lead magnet opt-ins (not purchased)
├── Webinar registrants (not attended)
├── Webinar attendees (not purchased)
└── Cart abandoners
```

### Creative Types

| Creative | Description | Best For |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Talking head video | Creator speaking to camera | Trust building, authority |
| Testimonial compilation | Multiple student results | Social proof |
| Screen share + voiceover | Showing the product/content | Product demonstration |
| Lifestyle + results | Creator's lifestyle + student outcomes | Aspiration |
| Carousel: Framework/steps | Teaching a concept in carousel format | Lead magnets |
| UGC: Student results | Students sharing their outcomes | Social proof + authenticity |

### Ad Copy Formula for Info Products

```
Hook: [Bold claim or story opener]
"I went from $0 to $10K/month in 90 days using this exact system."

Proof: [Specific results and credentials]
"After teaching 5,000+ students who've collectively generated $50M..."

Promise: [What they'll get]
"In this free training, you'll learn the 3-step framework to [outcome]."

CTA: [Low-friction next step]
"Click below to watch the free training now."
```

## 4. YouTube Ads Strategy

### Why YouTube Works for Info Products
- Longer attention spans allow for deeper education
- Trust built through extended video content
- Intent signals from YouTube search behavior
- Lower CPV than Meta for many niches

### Campaign Types

| Type | Format | Best For |
|------|--------|----------|
| In-Stream (skippable) | 2-15 min video | Webinar registration, course sales |
| In-Stream (non-skip, 15s) | Short hook | Brand awareness, retargeting |
| Discovery | Thumbnail + title in search | How-to content, topic authority |
| Shorts | <60s vertical | Awareness, younger audiences |

### YouTube Ad Structure (2-5 min for info products)

```
0-5s: Hook that prevents skip
"If you want to [desired outcome], don't skip this."

5-30s: Problem identification
Describe the viewer's current pain/situation

30s-2min: Value delivery (teach something)
Give a genuine insight or framework (builds reciprocity)

2-3min: Introduce the offer
Present the course/program as the full system

3-4min: Social proof
Student results, testimonials, numbers

4-5min: CTA
"Click the link below to [register/enroll/watch]"
```

## 5. Webinar Funnel Optimization

### Registration Page Benchmarks

| Metric | Average | Good | Excellent |
|--------|---------|------|-----------|
| Registration rate (from ad click) | 20-30% | 30-40% | 40-55% |
| Show-up rate (live) | 20-30% | 30-40% | 40-50% |
| Show-up rate (replay) | 40-60% | 60-70% | 70-80% |
| Offer conversion (live) | 5-10% | 10-15% | 15-25% |
| Offer conversion (replay/automated) | 2-5% | 5-8% | 8-15% |

### Webinar Registration Ad Strategy

**Registration campaign optimization**:
- Optimize for "Registration" custom event (not landing page views)
- Test registration page variants (video vs. no video, long vs. short)
- Run reminder sequences (email + Meta retargeting)
- Target webinar registrants who didn't attend with replay ads

**Post-webinar conversion campaign**:
- Cart open/close sequence (3-5 day window)
- Retarget attendees with testimonials and bonuses
- Urgency: countdown timer to cart close
- Objection-handling ads (FAQ, guarantee, payment plan)

## 6. Pricing & Offer Strategy

### Price Point Economics

| Price Range | Funnel Type | Typical CPA | Volume Needed |
|------------|------------|-------------|---------------|
| $7-47 (tripwire) | Direct to offer | $5-20 | High volume |
| $47-197 (low-ticket) | Lead magnet + email | $15-50 | Medium-high |
| $197-497 (mid-ticket) | Webinar or VSL | $50-150 | Medium |
| $497-2,000 (premium) | Webinar + application | $100-500 | Lower volume |
| $2,000+ (high-ticket) | Webinar/VSL + sales call | $200-1,000 | Low volume, high close rate |

### Offer Elements That Increase Conversion

1. **Bonuses**: 3-5 bonuses with individual values > price of the course
2. **Guarantee**: 30-day money-back minimum; 60-90 day is stronger
3. **Payment plan**: 3-6 monthly payments; slightly higher total than one-time
4. **Urgency**: Genuine cart close, early bird pricing, or bonus expiration
5. **Social proof**: Student count, testimonials, income results (with disclaimers)

## 7. Key Metrics & Benchmarks

### Funnel Metrics to Track

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Cost per lead (CPL) | Ad spend / Leads | $2-10 (lead magnet), $10-30 (webinar reg) |
| Cost per acquisition (CPA) | Ad spend / Sales | < 30% of product price |
| Earnings per lead (EPL) | Revenue / Leads | > CPL (profitability indicator) |
| ROAS | Revenue / Ad Spend | 2x+ (immediate), 3-5x (with email nurture) |
| Email list value | Monthly revenue / List size | $1-3 per subscriber per month |

### Industry Benchmarks

| Niche | Avg CPL | Avg CPA (Mid-Ticket) | Typical ROAS |
|-------|---------|---------------------|-------------|
| Business/Marketing | $3-8 | $80-200 | 2-4x |
| Health/Fitness | $2-6 | $50-150 | 2-5x |
| Finance/Investing | $5-15 | $100-300 | 2-4x |
| Personal Development | $2-5 | $60-150 | 2-4x |
| Tech/Coding | $4-10 | $80-200 | 2-3x |

## 8. Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Impact | Fix |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Selling too early to cold traffic | Low conversion, high CPA | Warm up with value-first content |
| No email nurture sequence | Leaving money on the table | Build 7-14 day email sequence after opt-in |
| Only measuring immediate ROAS | Under-valuing campaigns | Track 30/60/90-day ROAS including email sales |
| Ignoring creative fatigue | Rising CPAs over time | Refresh creatives every 2-3 weeks |
| Income claims without disclaimers | FTC risk, platform bans | Always include earnings disclaimers |
| One funnel for all traffic temperatures | Poor conversion at every stage | Separate funnels for cold, warm, and hot traffic |
