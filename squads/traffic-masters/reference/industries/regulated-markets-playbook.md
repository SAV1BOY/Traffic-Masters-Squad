# Regulated Markets Traffic Playbook
> **Industry**: Healthcare, Finance, Legal, Pharma, Insurance
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Key Characteristics
- Strict advertising regulations (government and platform level)
- Higher risk of ad disapprovals and account restrictions
- Longer approval times for ad creative
- Compliance documentation required for all campaigns

## Regulatory Landscape
- **Healthcare/Pharma**: ANVISA (Brazil), FDA (US), platform health policies
- **Finance**: CVM (Brazil), SEC (US), platform financial services policies
- **Legal**: Bar association advertising rules, prohibited guarantee of outcomes
- **Insurance**: SUSEP (Brazil), state regulations, required disclosures

## Recommended Channel Mix
- **Google Search** (40-50%): High-intent queries, easier compliance than social
- **Meta Ads** (20-30%): Awareness and education (careful creative compliance)
- **YouTube** (15-20%): Educational content, thought leadership
- **LinkedIn** (10-15%): Professional audience, B2B services

## Compliance Framework
1. Pre-production: Legal review of all claims and messaging
2. Creative development: Use approved disclaimer templates
3. Pre-launch review: Internal compliance sign-off
4. Monitoring: Watch for platform policy changes affecting regulated content
5. Documentation: Archive all approved creatives and compliance sign-offs

## Creative Best Practices
- Educational content over promotional content
- Avoid testimonials with specific health or financial outcomes
- Use platform-approved disclaimer language
- No before/after imagery for health products on Meta
- Include required regulatory disclosures in all ads

## Key Takeaways
- Compliance is not optional — violations can result in permanent account loss
- Build extra time into production for legal review (add 3-5 days)
- Google Search is generally safer for regulated industries (intent-based)
- Maintain a library of pre-approved copy and disclaimers
- Brazilian regulated industries have specific additional requirements (ANVISA, CVM, CONAR)
- When in doubt, get explicit legal sign-off before launching
