# Crypto and Web3 Traffic Playbook
> **Industry**: Cryptocurrency, DeFi, NFTs, Web3
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Key Characteristics
- Heavily restricted on major ad platforms
- Rapidly changing regulatory landscape
- Community-driven marketing is as important as paid ads
- High audience sophistication — marketing BS is quickly detected

## Platform Restrictions
- **Meta**: Crypto ads require pre-approval, restricted targeting, no ICO/token sale ads
- **Google**: Crypto exchange ads require certification, DeFi/NFT ads restricted
- **TikTok**: Cryptocurrency ads largely prohibited
- **Twitter/X**: More permissive but still requires compliance
- **Native/Programmatic**: Often the most accessible paid channels

## Recommended Channel Mix
- **Twitter/X Ads** (25-30%): Core crypto audience engagement
- **Google Search** (20-25%): Brand defense, educational keywords (with certification)
- **Programmatic/Native** (20-25%): Broader reach with fewer restrictions
- **Community** (20-30%): Discord, Telegram, Reddit (organic + amplified)

## Compliant Campaign Strategies
- Educational content: "Learn about [topic]" rather than "Buy [token]"
- Brand awareness for established, licensed platforms
- Product feature campaigns for regulated exchanges
- Community growth campaigns (Discord/Telegram signups)

## Key Metrics and Benchmarks
- Cost Per Registration: $5-30 (exchange signup)
- Cost Per FTD (First Time Deposit): $50-200
- Depositor-to-trader conversion: 30-50%
- Community member CPA: $1-5

## Creative Best Practices
- Education-first messaging avoids policy triggers
- Avoid hype language ("guaranteed returns", "moon", "get rich")
- Transparency about risks is both ethical and policy-compliant
- Community testimonials over individual financial results
- Platform UI showcases for exchange products

## Key Takeaways
- Compliance is the number one challenge — one violation can ban the account
- Community marketing is not optional — it is the primary growth channel
- Platform certification (Google) should be obtained early
- Brazilian CVM regulations add another compliance layer
- Content marketing and organic social are often more effective than paid for crypto
