# Real Estate Traffic Playbook
> **Industry**: Real Estate (Residential, Commercial, Developments)
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Key Characteristics
- High-value transactions with long consideration periods (months)
- Lead generation focused — conversion happens offline with agents
- Highly local — geo-targeting is essential
- Visual-heavy — photos and video tours drive engagement

## Recommended Channel Mix
- **Meta Ads** (35-45%): Lead gen forms, property showcase, local targeting
- **Google Search** (25-35%): "[type] for sale in [location]", project names
- **Google Display/YouTube** (10-15%): Visual property showcase, virtual tours
- **Portals** (10-20%): ZAP, OLX, Viva Real (Brazil); Zillow, Realtor (US)

## Campaign Structure
- Prospecting: Showcase property listings to geo-targeted audiences
- Lead Gen: In-app forms on Meta, landing pages on Google
- Retargeting: Re-engage visitors who viewed listings but did not inquire
- Brand: Developer/agency brand awareness in target neighborhoods

## Key Metrics and Benchmarks
- Cost Per Lead: R$20-80 (varies by property value and location)
- Lead-to-visit rate: 10-25%
- Visit-to-sale rate: 5-15%
- Time to close: 30-180 days

## Creative Best Practices
- High-quality property photography is non-negotiable
- Video tours and drone footage significantly increase engagement
- Show floor plans and key specifications in carousel format
- Neighborhood lifestyle content (not just the property)
- Price transparency when competitive (or "starting from R$X")

## Key Takeaways
- Lead quality is paramount — unqualified leads waste agent time
- Pre-qualifying questions in lead forms improve lead quality
- WhatsApp integration is essential for Brazilian real estate leads
- Weekend campaigns often perform best (people browse properties on weekends)
- Seasonal patterns exist: beginning of year and post-holidays are peak periods
- Special Ad Category (Housing) on Meta limits targeting options
