# Education and Courses Traffic Playbook
> **Industry**: Online Education, Universities, EdTech
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Key Characteristics
- Enrollment-driven with seasonal peaks (back-to-school, January)
- Long consideration cycles for formal education, shorter for short courses
- Price sensitivity varies: free courses to MBA-level tuition
- Content quality and outcomes are primary selling points

## Recommended Channel Mix
- **Google Search** (35-45%): "best course in X", "[topic] certification", program names
- **Meta Ads** (30-35%): Lead gen, webinar registration, course discovery
- **YouTube** (15-20%): Free lesson previews, instructor introductions, testimonials
- **TikTok** (5-10%): Educational snippets, student life content

## Campaign Structure
- Awareness: Free content, mini-lessons, educational value
- Consideration: Webinar/masterclass registration, curriculum previews
- Decision: Enrollment campaigns with urgency (class start dates, limited spots)
- Retention: Alumni referral programs, upsell to advanced courses

## Key Metrics and Benchmarks
- Cost Per Lead (info request): R$10-40 / $5-25
- Cost Per Enrollment: varies widely (R$50-500 for short courses, R$200-2000 for formal programs)
- Lead-to-enrollment rate: 5-15%
- Webinar show-up rate: 30-50%

## Creative Best Practices
- Instructor credibility is the primary trust builder
- Student success stories with specific career/life outcomes
- Curriculum preview content (demonstrate quality before purchase)
- Community and networking benefits as selling points
- Urgency through enrollment deadlines and cohort start dates

## Key Takeaways
- Free content ads are the most effective TOFU strategy for education
- The instructor's personal brand can make or break enrollment rates
- Enrollment deadlines create natural urgency without artificial scarcity
- Student outcomes and career results are the strongest social proof
- Brazilian education market: ProUni and FIES references may be relevant for formal programs
