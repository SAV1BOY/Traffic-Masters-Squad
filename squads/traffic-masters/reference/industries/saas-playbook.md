# Paid Traffic Playbook for SaaS Companies

> Complete playbook for running paid traffic to SaaS products: trial signups, demo requests, and subscription conversions.

---

## 1. SaaS Funnel Architecture

### Typical SaaS Conversion Path

```
Cold Traffic -> Landing Page -> Free Trial/Demo Request -> Onboarding ->
Activation -> Paid Subscription -> Expansion -> Advocacy
```

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Target Range | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| CAC (Blended) | < LTV/3 | LTV:CAC ratio of 3:1 minimum |
| CAC Payback | < 12 months | How long to recover acquisition cost |
| Trial-to-Paid | 15-25% (no CC) / 40-60% (CC required) | Major lever for optimization |
| Demo-to-Close | 15-30% | For sales-led SaaS |
| MQL-to-SQL | 20-40% | Lead quality indicator |
| Churn Rate | < 5% monthly / < 10% annual | Determines LTV ceiling |

## 3. Google Ads for SaaS

### Campaign Structure

```
Brand Campaigns (always on)
├── Brand exact match
├── Brand + product terms
└── Competitor brand terms

Non-Brand Search
├── Category terms ("project management software")
├── Problem terms ("how to manage remote teams")
├── Feature terms ("gantt chart tool")
├── Comparison terms ("[competitor] alternative")
└── Integration terms ("[tool] + [your product] integration")

Performance Max / Demand Gen
├── Remarketing signals
├── Customer list signals
└── Intent-based audiences
```

### SaaS-Specific Keyword Strategy

| Keyword Type | Example | Intent | Typical CPC | Conversion Rate |
|-------------|---------|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Category | "CRM software" | High | $5-15 | 2-5% |
| Problem | "how to track customer emails" | Medium | $2-8 | 1-3% |
| Comparison | "HubSpot vs Salesforce" | Very High | $8-20 | 5-10% |
| Alternative | "cheaper alternative to Salesforce" | Very High | $6-15 | 4-8% |
| Feature | "email automation tool" | High | $4-12 | 3-6% |

### Bidding Strategy

- **Brand**: Target Impression Share (90%+)
- **Non-brand (high intent)**: Target CPA or Maximize Conversions
- **Non-brand (lower intent)**: Maximize Clicks with bid cap initially, then move to Target CPA
- **Competitor terms**: Manual CPC or Target CPA with conservative targets

## 4. Meta Ads for SaaS

### Campaign Structure

```
Prospecting
├── Broad targeting (let Meta's algorithm optimize)
├── Interest-based (software, business, tools)
├── Lookalike: Free trial signups (1-3%)
├── Lookalike: Paying customers (1-3%)
└── Lookalike: High-LTV customers (1%)

Retargeting
├── Website visitors (7 days, 14 days, 30 days)
├── Video viewers (50%, 75%, 95%)
├── Landing page viewers who didn't convert
├── Free trial users who haven't activated
└── Trial users approaching expiration
```

### Creative Types That Work for SaaS

| Creative Type | Best For | Expected CTR |
|--------------|----------|-------------|
| Product demo video (15-30s) | Feature showcase | 0.8-1.5% |
| Founder/team talking head | Trust building | 1.0-2.0% |
| Customer testimonial video | Social proof | 0.9-1.5% |
| Screen recording with voiceover | Product education | 0.7-1.2% |
| Before/after workflow | Problem-solution | 1.0-1.8% |
| Carousel: Feature walkthrough | Multiple benefits | 0.8-1.3% |
| UGC-style review | Authenticity | 1.2-2.0% |

### SaaS Ad Copy Formula

```
Hook: [Pain point or bold claim]
"Managing projects across 5 tools is killing your productivity."

Body: [Solution + proof]
"[Product] brings everything into one dashboard.
12,000+ teams switched and saw 40% more output in 30 days."

CTA: [Low-friction action]
"Start your free trial. No credit card required."
```

## 5. LinkedIn Ads for SaaS

### When to Use LinkedIn

- B2B SaaS with $1,000+ annual contract value
- Products targeting specific roles (CMOs, CTOs, HR directors)
- Enterprise sales cycles with multiple stakeholders
- Account-based marketing (ABM) for named accounts

### Campaign Types

| Type | Use Case | Typical CPL |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Sponsored Content (single image) | Broad awareness, thought leadership | $30-75 |
| Sponsored Content (video) | Product demos, brand storytelling | $25-60 |
| Sponsored Content (carousel) | Feature walkthroughs, case studies | $25-65 |
| Document Ads | Lead magnets, whitepapers | $20-50 |
| Message Ads (InMail) | Direct offers to decision-makers | $40-100 |
| Lead Gen Forms | Gated content, demo requests | $25-60 |
| Thought Leader Ads | Boosted employee posts | $15-40 |

### LinkedIn Targeting for SaaS

**ABM (Account-Based)**:
- Upload company list -> Target all decision-makers at those companies
- Layer with job title/function for precision
- Ideal for enterprise SaaS with < 1,000 target accounts

**Role-Based**:
- Job function + seniority level (e.g., Marketing + Director+)
- Company size filter (50-500 employees for mid-market)
- Industry filter for vertical SaaS

## 6. Trial Optimization

### Free Trial Best Practices

| Decision | Option A | Option B | Recommendation |
|----------|----------|----------|----------------|
| Credit card required? | No CC (higher signups) | CC required (higher quality) | No CC for PLG; CC for premium |
| Trial length | 7 days | 14 days / 30 days | 14 days is the sweet spot |
| Feature access | Full access | Limited features | Full access (build endowment) |
| Onboarding | Self-serve | Guided | Hybrid (email + in-app) |

### Post-Trial Ad Sequences

**Day 1-3**: Welcome + quick win
- Ad: "You're in! Here's how to get started in 5 minutes."

**Day 4-7**: Value demonstration
- Ad: "[Customer] achieved [result] in their first week."

**Day 8-12**: Feature discovery
- Ad: "Did you try [advanced feature]? Here's what it can do."

**Day 13-14**: Conversion push
- Ad: "Your trial ends tomorrow. Lock in your discount."

## 8. Common SaaS Paid Traffic Mistakes

| Mistake | Impact | Fix |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Optimizing for signups, not activation | High CAC, low revenue | Track and optimize for activation events |
| Ignoring trial-to-paid conversion | Wasteful spending | Invest in onboarding alongside acquisition |
| Running the same creative for months | Ad fatigue, rising CPAs | Refresh creative every 2-4 weeks |
| Targeting too narrow on Meta | High CPMs, limited scale | Start broader; let algorithm optimize |
| Not tracking offline conversions | Google/Meta can't optimize properly | Import CRM conversions (MQL, SQL, Closed Won) |
| Bidding on competitors without LP strategy | Low Quality Score, high CPC | Create comparison LPs for competitor terms |
| LinkedIn without testing content first | Expensive learning phase | Test with organic posts first, then boost winners |
