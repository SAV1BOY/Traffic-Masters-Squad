# B2B SaaS Traffic Playbook
> **Industry**: B2B Software as a Service
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Key Characteristics
- Long sales cycles (30-180 days), multiple decision makers
- Free trial or freemium model common for acquisition
- High LTV justifies higher CPA (LTV:CPA ratio 3:1+)
- Content marketing and thought leadership drive trust

## Recommended Channel Mix
- **Google Search** (35-45%): High-intent bottom-funnel keywords
- **LinkedIn** (20-30%): ABM, decision-maker targeting, thought leadership
- **Meta Ads** (15-20%): Retargeting, lookalikes, content distribution
- **YouTube** (5-10%): Product demos, case studies, educational content
- **Review Sites/Partnerships** (5-10%): G2, Capterra, partner programs

## Campaign Structure
- TOFU: Content ads (guides, reports, webinars) on LinkedIn and Meta
- MOFU: Case study retargeting, product demo ads, comparison content
- BOFU: Free trial/demo request campaigns on Google Search
- ABM: Account-targeted campaigns on LinkedIn for named accounts

## Key Metrics and Benchmarks
- Cost Per MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead): $50-300 (varies by market)
- MQL to SQL conversion: 25-40%
- Trial to paid conversion: 10-25% (product-led growth)
- Demo to close rate: 15-30%
- LTV:CPA target: 3:1 minimum

## Creative Best Practices
- Lead with the business problem, not the software features
- Case studies with specific ROI numbers (logos, results, timeline)
- Product demo videos (2-3 minutes) for mid-funnel
- Thought leadership content builds credibility for long sales cycles
- Social proof: customer logos, G2 ratings, analyst recognition

## Key Takeaways
- Patience is required — B2B sales cycles are long, attribution is complex
- Content is the primary lead magnet (guides, templates, reports)
- LinkedIn ABM is uniquely powerful for targeting specific companies
- Multi-touch attribution is essential — no single ad closes a B2B deal
- Align traffic campaigns with sales team pipeline and quota targets
