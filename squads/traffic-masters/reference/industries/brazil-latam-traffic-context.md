# Brazil and LATAM Traffic Context
> **Region**: Brazil and Latin America
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Brazilian Digital Advertising Landscape
- Brazil is the largest digital ad market in Latin America
- Meta (Facebook + Instagram) dominates social advertising
- Google is the primary search engine (95%+ market share)
- TikTok growing rapidly, especially among 16-34 demographics
- WhatsApp is the primary communication channel (used by 95%+ of internet users)

## Key Brazilian Market Characteristics
- Mobile-first market: 85%+ of social media access is mobile
- Price sensitivity: installment payments (parcelamento) are standard
- Boleto bancario: payment method with 3-day clearing (affects attribution window)
- Pix: instant payment system — growing rapidly for online purchases
- Portuguese language: all creative must be in Brazilian Portuguese (not European)

## Currency and Payment Considerations
- Ad platforms bill in USD — BRL fluctuation affects effective CPA
- Brazilian credit cards have IOF tax on international purchases
- Boleto payments have 40-60% completion rate (many are never paid)
- Pix offers instant confirmation — preferred for low-ticket purchases

## Regulatory Environment
- LGPD: data protection law (similar to GDPR)
- CDC: Consumer Defense Code — 7-day return right for online purchases
- CONAR: advertising self-regulation council
- ANVISA: health product advertising regulation
- CVM: financial product advertising regulation
- Nota Fiscal (NF): required for all commercial transactions

## Seasonal Calendar (Brazil)
- January/February: Back to school, Carnaval
- March: Consumer Week (Semana do Consumidor)
- May: Dia das Maes (Mother's Day)
- June: Dia dos Namorados (Valentine's Day - June 12), Festa Junina
- August: Dia dos Pais (Father's Day)
- October: Dia das Criancas (Children's Day)
- November: Black Friday BR (massive, often extends to full month)
- December: Christmas and New Year

## Key Takeaways
- WhatsApp integration is not optional for Brazilian traffic campaigns
- Installment payment display increases conversion rates significantly
- Boleto attribution requires extended conversion windows (3+ days)
- Brazilian Portuguese localization must be authentic, not translated
- CPMs are generally lower in Brazil than US/EU but rising
- Sobral methodology is the gold standard for BR traffic management
