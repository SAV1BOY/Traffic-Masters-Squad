# E-Commerce Traffic Playbook
> **Industry**: E-Commerce / Online Retail
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Key Characteristics
- Multiple products, multiple price points, catalog-driven
- Purchase intent varies (browsing vs buying)
- High competition for attention, low switching costs
- AOV (Average Order Value) and repeat purchase rate are critical metrics

## Recommended Channel Mix
- **Meta Ads** (40-50%): Prospecting with catalog ads, DPA retargeting
- **Google Shopping/PMax** (30-40%): High-intent product searches
- **Google Search** (10-15%): Brand and non-brand search terms
- **TikTok** (5-10%): Product discovery, UGC, trend-based content

## Campaign Structure
- Prospecting: broad/LAL audiences with best-sellers or hero products
- Retargeting: DPA (Dynamic Product Ads) with viewed/carted products
- Retention: email/SMS synced audiences with upsell/cross-sell offers
- Brand Search: protect brand terms, capture high-intent traffic

## Key Metrics and Benchmarks
- ROAS target: 3-5x (varies by margin)
- CPA: varies by AOV (typically 20-30% of first-order margin)
- CTR: 1.5%+ for feed ads, 3%+ for search
- Cart abandonment rate: 65-75% (retarget aggressively)

## Creative Best Practices
- Lifestyle imagery outperforms product-only for cold traffic
- UGC/unboxing videos drive discovery and trust
- Carousel ads showcase multiple products (good for ASP testing)
- Dynamic creatives for large catalogs
- Seasonal and promotional creative rotation

## Key Takeaways
- Feed quality is your secret weapon — optimize product titles, images, descriptions
- DPA retargeting is the highest-ROI campaign for e-commerce
- Segment products by margin tier for budget allocation (custom labels)
- First-purchase CPA can be higher if LTV justifies it (cohort analysis)
- Free shipping thresholds increase AOV and conversion rate
