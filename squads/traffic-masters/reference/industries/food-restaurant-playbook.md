# Food and Restaurant Traffic Playbook
> **Industry**: Restaurants, Food Delivery, Food Products
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Key Characteristics
- Highly visual — food photography and video drive engagement
- Local targeting (for restaurants), broader for food products
- Low average transaction value, high repeat purchase potential
- Peak times matter (lunch, dinner, weekends)

## Recommended Channel Mix
- **Meta Ads** (40-50%): Local targeting, food visuals, offer promotion
- **Google Ads** (25-30%): "restaurants near me", delivery searches, brand terms
- **Google Business Profile** (organic): Reviews, photos, menu, hours
- **TikTok** (10-15%): Food content, recipe videos, restaurant tours
- **Delivery Apps** (10-20%): iFood, Uber Eats, Rappi (Brazil)

## Campaign Structure (Restaurants)
- Local Awareness: Radius targeting with mouthwatering food imagery
- Offer Campaigns: Happy hour, lunch specials, new menu items
- Event Campaigns: Special occasions, holiday menus, live music
- Delivery: Drive app orders or direct delivery website traffic

## Campaign Structure (Food Products)
- Prospecting: Recipe content, lifestyle imagery with product
- Trial: First-purchase offers, sample packs, bundle deals
- Retention: Subscription models, loyalty program promotion

## Key Metrics and Benchmarks
- Cost Per Visit (restaurant): R$5-20
- Cost Per Order (delivery): R$8-25
- Repeat customer rate: 30-50% target
- Average frequency: 2-4 visits per month for regular customers

## Creative Best Practices
- Food photography: close-up, warm lighting, steam/motion
- Video: preparation process, plating, first-bite reaction
- UGC: customer reviews, food influencer partnerships
- Seasonal menus and limited-time offers create urgency
- Show real food, not stock photos — authenticity drives trust

## Key Takeaways
- Food photography quality directly correlates with ad performance
- Local radius targeting is essential — every kilometer matters
- Google Business Profile with strong reviews is free high-impact marketing
- Dayparting: schedule ads around meal decision times (10-11am, 4-5pm)
- iFood and delivery app integration is critical in Brazilian market
- WhatsApp ordering is increasingly popular for Brazilian restaurants
