# Gaming Traffic Playbook
> **Industry**: Mobile Games, PC Games, Console Games
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Key Characteristics
- Install is free (F2P model) — monetization through IAP or ads
- Creative is the dominant performance variable (more than targeting)
- High creative fatigue — refresh every 5-7 days
- ROAS measurement critical for F2P economics

## Recommended Channel Mix
- **Meta Ads** (25-35%): Broad targeting + creative testing at scale
- **Google UAC/ACe** (25-35%): App campaigns across Google properties
- **TikTok** (15-20%): High engagement, young demographics, viral potential
- **Ad Networks** (15-25%): Unity, ironSource, AppLovin, Vungle
- **Apple Search Ads** (5-10%): High-intent App Store search

## Creative Formats That Win
- **Gameplay Video**: Real gameplay footage, 15-30 seconds
- **Fail/Win Scenarios**: Show satisfying puzzle completion or funny fails
- **Playable Ads**: Interactive mini-game experience within the ad
- **UGC Reactions**: Creators reacting to gameplay
- **Misleading-Prevention**: Show actual gameplay, not misleading renders

## Key Metrics and Benchmarks
- CPI: $0.30-3.00 (casual), $3-10 (mid-core), $10-30 (hardcore)
- D1 Retention: 35-45% (good), 45%+ (excellent)
- D7 Retention: 15-25% (good)
- ROAS D7: 15-25% of target LTV
- IPM (Installs Per Mille): 20+ is strong creative

## Campaign Structure
- Testing: rapid creative testing with small budgets, broad audiences
- Scaling: winners scaled with budget increases, new geos
- Retargeting: lapsed users re-engagement campaigns
- Cross-promotion: promote new games to existing game players

## Key Takeaways
- Creative is king in gaming UA — it matters more than targeting
- Test 10-20 new creatives per week at minimum
- IPM (installs per mille) is the key creative quality metric
- D7 ROAS is the standard decision metric for scaling
- iOS SKAN requires adapted measurement and creative testing approaches
- Brazil is a large gaming market with strong mobile adoption
