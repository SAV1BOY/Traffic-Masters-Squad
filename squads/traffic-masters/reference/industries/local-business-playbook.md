# Local Business Traffic Playbook
> **Industry**: Local Services, Restaurants, Clinics, Gyms
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Key Characteristics
- Geo-restricted audience (city, neighborhood, radius)
- Lower budgets but highly targeted
- Phone calls, walk-ins, and appointments are primary conversions
- Google Maps/local search is critical discovery channel

## Recommended Channel Mix
- **Google Ads** (40-50%): Local search, Google Maps ads, call extensions
- **Meta Ads** (30-40%): Radius targeting, local awareness, lead gen forms
- **Google Business Profile** (organic): Reviews, posts, photos, Q&A
- **Waze Ads** (5%): Navigation-based advertising for drive-by businesses

## Campaign Structure
- Google Search: "[service] near me", "[service] + [city/neighborhood]"
- Google Maps: Local campaigns targeting radius around business
- Meta Lead Gen: In-app lead forms for appointments/consultations
- Meta Awareness: Radius targeting with offers or content

## Key Metrics and Benchmarks
- Cost Per Lead: R$10-50 (varies by service and competition)
- Phone call conversion rate: 15-30% of clicks should generate calls
- Walk-in attribution: use offer codes or "how did you hear about us"
- Google Maps visibility: appear in top 3 local pack results

## Creative Best Practices
- Show real location, real staff, real results (authenticity is paramount)
- Include address, phone number, and operating hours prominently
- Local testimonials from community members
- Offers with clear value ("First visit free", "20% off this week")
- Before/after for service businesses (dentists, salons, home improvement)

## Key Takeaways
- Google Business Profile optimization is free and often more impactful than paid ads
- Reviews are the number one trust signal for local businesses
- Mobile optimization is critical — most local searches happen on phones
- Track phone calls and form submissions separately for accurate CPA
- Brazilian local businesses: integrate with WhatsApp for lead capture
