# High-Ticket Services Traffic Playbook
> **Industry**: Consulting, Coaching, Premium Services ($3,000+)
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Key Characteristics
- High price point requires high trust before purchase
- Sales process involves human interaction (call, meeting, consultation)
- Lead quality matters more than lead volume
- Personal brand of the service provider is a key conversion factor

## Recommended Channel Mix
- **Meta Ads** (40-50%): Lead gen for application/booking funnels
- **YouTube** (20-25%): Long-form content builds authority and trust
- **Google Search** (15-20%): Brand terms and high-intent service keywords
- **LinkedIn** (10-15%): Professional authority, B2B high-ticket offers

## Funnel Architecture
- Cold: Value content ad → Lead magnet (guide, webinar, VSL)
- Warm: Retarget with case studies, testimonials, authority content
- Hot: Application/booking page → Strategy call → Close
- Alternative: VSL funnel (Video Sales Letter) → Application → Call

## Key Metrics and Benchmarks
- CPL (Cost Per Lead): R$20-100 / $10-50
- Cost Per Booked Call: R$100-500 / $50-200
- Show-up rate: 60-80% (with reminder sequences)
- Close rate: 15-30% of qualified calls
- Target ROAS: 5-20x (high margins)

## Creative Best Practices
- Founder/coach face and voice are essential in all video content
- Long-form video (5-15 min) builds enough trust for high-ticket decisions
- Client transformation stories with specific, verifiable results
- Authority markers: media features, book authorship, credentials
- Application-based funnels (not "buy now") signal exclusivity

## Key Takeaways
- Trust is the bottleneck — every touchpoint must build trust
- Video is the primary trust-building format for high-ticket
- Application funnels qualify leads before expensive sales calls
- Remarketing windows should be longer (30-90 days) for high-ticket
- The sales team's close rate is as important as the traffic CPA
- Brazilian market: WhatsApp is often the booking/closing channel
