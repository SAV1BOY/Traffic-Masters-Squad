# Infoproduct Launches Traffic Playbook
> **Industry**: Digital Products, Courses, Coaching
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03

## Key Characteristics
- High margins (80-95%), digital delivery, scalable
- Launch-based or evergreen sales models
- Audience trust and authority are critical for conversion
- Brazilian market (Sobral methodology) is a major reference

## Recommended Channel Mix
- **Meta Ads** (60-70%): Primary acquisition channel for leads and sales
- **YouTube** (15-20%): Authority building, long-form content ads
- **Google Search** (10-15%): Brand terms, competitor terms, topic terms
- **TikTok** (5-10%): Awareness and list building for younger demographics

## Launch Model (Sobral 8-Phase)
1. Pre-Launch: content distribution, audience warming
2. Capture: lead gen campaigns (CPL target: R$3-15 depending on niche)
3. Warm-Up: engagement campaigns to registered leads
4. Open Cart: maximum reach to all warm audiences
5. Sustain: testimonials, objection handling
6. Urgency: countdown, bonus deadlines
7. Close Cart: final push with highest budget
8. Post-Launch: debrief, evergreen setup

## Key Metrics and Benchmarks
- CPL (Cost Per Lead): R$3-15 (BR market), $2-10 (US market)
- Registration-to-sale rate: 2-5% (webinar), 5-15% (challenge)
- ROAS target: 5-20x (high-margin products)
- Email open rate: 25-40% during launch window

## Creative Best Practices
- Authority-building content ads (tips, insights, mini-trainings)
- Student testimonial videos (transformation stories)
- Founder/instructor face is essential — personal brand drives sales
- Behind-the-scenes content builds authenticity
- Urgency-driven creative for open/close cart phases

## Key Takeaways
- List size determines launch revenue — invest heavily in capture phase
- 80% of sales come from open cart day and close cart day
- Personal brand of the instructor is the strongest conversion lever
- Evergreen funnels complement launches for consistent revenue
- Brazilian launch market is mature — differentiation through content quality
