# Fiscal Compliance Guide — Brazil

## Purpose
Compliance requirements for running paid traffic operations in the Brazilian market.

## Tax & Fiscal Requirements

### Nota Fiscal
- Digital services and products require nota fiscal (NF-e or NFS-e)
- Ad spend invoicing follows Brazilian fiscal calendar
- International platform payments (Meta, Google) have specific tax treatment

### IOF & Currency Exchange
- IOF (Imposto sobre Operacoes Financeiras) applies to international payments
- Dollar-denominated ad spend subject to exchange rate variations
- Document exchange rates used for accounting

### ISS (Imposto Sobre Servicos)
- Digital advertising services subject to ISS
- Rate varies by municipality (2-5%)
- Applies to agency service fees

## Advertising Regulations

### CONAR (Conselho Nacional de Autorregulamentacao Publicitaria)
- Self-regulatory body for advertising in Brazil
- Guidelines on truthfulness, decency, and social responsibility
- Complaints can result in ad removal

### CDC (Codigo de Defesa do Consumidor)
- Consumer protection code applies to advertising
- Misleading or abusive advertising is prohibited
- Clear pricing and conditions required

### LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados)
- Brazilian data protection law (similar to GDPR)
- Consent required for data collection
- Data processing must have legal basis
- Users have right to access, correct, and delete data

## Compliance Checklist
- [ ] Nota fiscal process configured
- [ ] Tax treatment for international ad spend documented
- [ ] LGPD consent mechanisms in place
- [ ] CONAR guidelines reviewed for ad content
- [ ] CDC requirements met for offers and pricing
- [ ] Cookie/privacy policies updated for Brazilian users
