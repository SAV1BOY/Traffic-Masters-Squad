## 1. Full-Funnel Campaign Architecture

**Category:** Strategy
**When to Use:** Setting up a new account, restructuring an existing account, or planning a comprehensive campaign strategy.

**Description:** Defines how campaigns should be organized across the buyer journey — from awareness through conversion and retention. Maps campaign objectives, audience types, ad formats, and success metrics to each funnel stage.

**Key Components:**
- TOFU: Awareness and reach campaigns targeting cold audiences
- MOFU: Consideration campaigns targeting engaged/warm audiences
- BOFU: Conversion campaigns targeting high-intent audiences
- Post-purchase: Retention, upsell, and referral campaigns

**Outputs:** Campaign architecture diagram, stage-specific KPIs, audience-to-campaign mapping

**Related Agents:** Strategy, Audience, Launch
**Related Frameworks:** Audience Layering, Channel Mix Optimization

---

## 2. Budget Allocation Model

**Category:** Budget
**When to Use:** Initial budget planning, quarterly budget reviews, or when adding/removing campaigns or channels.

**Description:** Provides a structured approach to distributing budget across campaigns, channels, and funnel stages based on business goals, historical performance, and marginal efficiency.

**Key Components:**
- Goal-based allocation (awareness vs. acquisition vs. retention splits)
- Historical performance weighting
- Test budget carve-out (recommended 10-20% of total)
- Platform-specific minimum spend thresholds
- Seasonal adjustment factors

**Outputs:** Budget allocation plan, spend distribution by channel/campaign, test budget allocation

**Related Agents:** Budget, Strategy
**Related Frameworks:** Marginal Efficiency Analysis, Channel Mix Optimization

---

## 3. Marginal Efficiency Analysis

**Category:** Budget
**When to Use:** Deciding where to increase or decrease spend, evaluating scaling potential, or optimizing cross-campaign allocation.

**Description:** Analyzes the marginal cost of acquiring an additional conversion at different spend levels to identify the optimal budget for each campaign.

**Key Components:**
- Cost curve analysis by campaign
- Diminishing returns identification
- Incremental CPA/ROAS calculation
- Saturation point detection
- Reallocation recommendation engine

**Outputs:** Marginal efficiency charts, optimal spend recommendations, reallocation plan

**Related Agents:** Budget, Scaling
**Related Frameworks:** Budget Allocation Model, Campaign Scaling Framework

---

## 4. Creative Testing Framework

**Category:** Creative
**When to Use:** Testing new creative concepts, optimizing existing creative, or establishing a structured testing cadence.

**Description:** Defines a systematic approach to testing creative elements (hooks, angles, formats, visuals, copy) with statistical rigor.

**Key Components:**
- Testing hierarchy: Concept > Format > Hook > Visual > Copy
- Minimum sample size requirements
- Test duration guidelines
- Winner criteria and confidence levels
- Iteration rules (winning elements carry forward)

**Outputs:** Test plans, creative testing roadmaps, test result analyses, winning creative profiles

**Related Agents:** Creative Strategist, Experimentation, Copy
**Related Frameworks:** Creative Fatigue Detection, Offer Testing Framework

---

## 5. Audience Layering Framework

**Category:** Audience
**When to Use:** Building audience strategies for new campaigns, expanding reach, or refining targeting.

**Description:** Structures audience targeting across layers — from broad to specific — to maximize reach while maintaining relevance.

**Key Components:**
- Layer 1: Broad / algorithmic targeting
- Layer 2: Interest and behavioral targeting
- Layer 3: Lookalike audiences (tiered by percentage)
- Layer 4: Custom audiences (website, engagement, CRM)
- Layer 5: Retargeting audiences (time-windowed)
- Exclusion management across layers

**Outputs:** Audience architecture, targeting specifications, exclusion lists, overlap analysis

**Related Agents:** Audience, Strategy
**Related Frameworks:** Full-Funnel Campaign Architecture, Campaign Scaling Framework

---

## 6. Campaign Scaling Framework

**Category:** Growth
**When to Use:** When a campaign is performing well and the goal is to increase spend while maintaining efficiency.

**Description:** Provides a phased approach to scaling campaigns with guardrails to prevent efficiency degradation.

**Key Components:**
- Scaling readiness checklist (minimum data, stable performance, creative depth)
- Phase 1: Vertical scaling (increase budget 20-30% per phase)
- Phase 2: Horizontal scaling (new audiences, placements, formats)
- Phase 3: Cross-platform scaling (new channels)
- Efficiency monitoring thresholds (acceptable CPA/ROAS degradation bands)
- Rollback triggers

**Outputs:** Scaling plan, phase-gated milestones, efficiency monitoring dashboard

**Related Agents:** Scaling, Budget, Audience
**Related Frameworks:** Marginal Efficiency Analysis, Audience Layering Framework

---

## 7. Attribution Framework

**Category:** Measurement
**When to Use:** Setting up attribution for a new account, resolving cross-channel attribution questions, or evaluating attribution model changes.

**Description:** Guides the selection, implementation, and interpretation of attribution models for accurate performance measurement.

**Key Components:**
- Attribution model comparison (last-click, first-click, linear, data-driven, MTA)
- Platform-specific attribution window configuration
- Cross-platform attribution reconciliation
- Incrementality testing methodology
- Attribution reporting standards

**Outputs:** Attribution model recommendation, configuration guide, cross-platform reconciliation method

**Related Agents:** Tracking, Reporting
**Related Frameworks:** Channel Mix Optimization, Performance Diagnosis Framework

## 8. Competitive Positioning Framework

**Category:** Intelligence
**When to Use:** Market entry, competitive shifts, strategic planning, or when differentiating messaging.

**Description:** Maps the competitive landscape to identify positioning opportunities and inform creative and messaging strategy.

**Key Components:**
- Competitor identification and tiering
- Ad presence analysis (platforms, volume, formats)
- Messaging and offer comparison
- Audience overlap estimation
- Differentiation opportunity mapping

**Outputs:** Competitive landscape map, positioning recommendations, messaging differentiation guide

**Related Agents:** Intelligence, Strategy, Creative Strategist
**Related Frameworks:** Offer Testing Framework, Full-Funnel Campaign Architecture

---

## 9. Performance Diagnosis Framework

**Category:** Diagnostics
**When to Use:** Investigating performance declines, anomalies, or underperformance against targets.

**Description:** A systematic diagnostic process for identifying root causes of performance issues across all campaign dimensions.

**Key Components:**
- Symptom identification and severity classification
- Diagnostic tree: Creative > Audience > Bid/Budget > Tracking > External
- Hypothesis generation and testing
- Root cause confirmation criteria
- Remediation planning and monitoring

**Outputs:** Diagnostic report, root cause analysis, remediation plan, monitoring criteria

**Related Agents:** Diagnostics, Optimization, Tracking
**Related Frameworks:** Creative Fatigue Detection, Attribution Framework

---

## 10. Channel Mix Optimization

**Category:** Strategy
**When to Use:** Multi-channel budget decisions, adding or removing channels, or quarterly channel performance reviews.

**Description:** Optimizes the distribution of spend across advertising channels based on incremental contribution and efficiency.

**Key Components:**
- Channel performance benchmarking
- Incremental contribution analysis
- Audience reach deduplication
- Cross-channel synergy identification
- Diminishing returns by channel

**Outputs:** Optimized channel mix, reallocation recommendations, cross-channel efficiency report

**Related Agents:** Strategy, Budget, Integration
**Related Frameworks:** Budget Allocation Model, Attribution Framework

---

## 11. Creative Fatigue Detection

**Category:** Creative
**When to Use:** Ongoing monitoring of creative performance, planning creative refresh cycles.

**Description:** Identifies early signals of creative fatigue before they significantly impact performance, enabling proactive creative rotation.

**Key Components:**
- Fatigue indicators: CTR decline, frequency increase, CPA increase, engagement drop
- Threshold definitions per metric (e.g., CTR down 20% from peak)
- Alert triggers and escalation
- Refresh timing recommendations
- Lifecycle stage classification (ramp, peak, plateau, decline)

**Outputs:** Fatigue alerts, creative lifecycle reports, refresh recommendations, rotation schedules

**Related Agents:** Creative Strategist, Optimization
**Related Frameworks:** Creative Testing Framework, Campaign Scaling Framework

---

## 12. Bid Strategy Selection

**Category:** Optimization
**When to Use:** Campaign setup, bid strategy changes, or when current bidding is underperforming.

**Description:** Decision framework for selecting the optimal bid strategy based on campaign maturity, data volume, and objectives.

**Key Components:**
- Decision tree based on conversion volume, stability, and goals
- Strategy comparison: lowest cost, cost cap, bid cap, target CPA, target ROAS, manual
- Learning phase requirements and management
- Strategy transition guidelines
- Performance monitoring criteria per strategy

**Outputs:** Bid strategy recommendation, transition plan, monitoring criteria

**Related Agents:** Optimization, Budget, Launch
**Related Frameworks:** Campaign Scaling Framework, Performance Diagnosis Framework

---

## 13. Landing Page Optimization

**Category:** Conversion
**When to Use:** Low conversion rates, high bounce rates, or when launching new landing pages.

**Description:** Framework for optimizing the post-click experience to maximize conversion rates.

**Key Components:**
- Message match (ad-to-page consistency)
- Page speed and technical performance
- Above-the-fold optimization
- Social proof placement
- Form optimization
- Mobile-specific considerations
- A/B testing methodology for page elements

**Outputs:** Page audit, optimization recommendations, test plan, performance benchmarks

**Related Agents:** Optimization, Tracking, Experimentation
**Related Frameworks:** Creative Testing Framework, Performance Diagnosis Framework

---

## 14. Offer Testing Framework

**Category:** Strategy
**When to Use:** Testing different value propositions, pricing, promotions, or lead magnets.

**Description:** Structured approach to testing offers and value propositions to identify the most compelling proposition for each audience segment.

**Key Components:**
- Offer type categorization (discount, trial, lead magnet, bundle, guarantee)
- Test design with audience isolation
- Metrics beyond conversion rate (LTV, retention, quality)
- Sequencing strategy (test TOFU offers before BOFU)
- Statistical requirements and duration

**Outputs:** Offer test plan, results analysis, winning offer profiles, segment-specific recommendations

**Related Agents:** Strategy, Experimentation, Copy
**Related Frameworks:** Creative Testing Framework, Full-Funnel Campaign Architecture

---

## 15. Retention & LTV Framework

**Category:** Growth
**When to Use:** Post-acquisition strategy, reducing churn, increasing repeat purchase, or improving LTV.

**Description:** Framework for building campaigns and strategies focused on customer retention, repeat purchase, and lifetime value maximization.

**Key Components:**
- Customer segmentation by value and behavior
- Retention campaign architecture (welcome, winback, loyalty, upsell)
- LTV-based acquisition targeting (optimize for value, not volume)
- Cohort analysis methodology
- Retention metric framework (repeat rate, churn, LTV:CAC ratio)

**Outputs:** Retention campaign plan, LTV analysis, cohort reports, segment-specific strategies

**Related Agents:** Strategy, Audience, Reporting
**Related Frameworks:** Full-Funnel Campaign Architecture, Audience Layering Framework
