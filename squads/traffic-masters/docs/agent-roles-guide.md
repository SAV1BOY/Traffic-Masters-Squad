# Agent Roles Guide

## Purpose
Define the roles and responsibilities of each agent in the Traffic Masters Squad.

## Roles

### Traffic Strategist
- Owns overall traffic strategy and platform mix
- Sets KPI targets and guardrails
- Makes budget allocation decisions
- Leads quarterly planning and reviews
- Cross-squad coordination

### Media Buyer
- Executes campaign builds and optimizations
- Daily campaign management and monitoring
- Implements tests and experiments
- Manages bid strategies and budgets
- Applies decision rules for optimization

### Creative Strategist
- Develops creative concepts and briefs
- Analyzes creative performance data
- Manages creative testing pipeline
- Maintains swipe files and inspiration
- Directs UGC and creator relationships

### Tracking & Analytics Agent
- Pixel and CAPI implementation
- UTM and naming convention enforcement
- Dashboard creation and maintenance
- Attribution analysis
- Data quality assurance

### Compliance Agent
- Pre-launch ad review for policy compliance
- Platform policy monitoring and updates
- Safe language guidance
- Rejection troubleshooting and appeals
- Regulatory compliance (LGPD, FTC, etc.)

## Collaboration Model
- Daily standups: quick status and blockers
- Weekly reviews: performance and planning
- Monthly strategy: deep-dive analysis
- Quarterly planning: strategic direction
