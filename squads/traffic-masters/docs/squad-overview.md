# Squad Overview — Traffic Masters

## Mission
Drive profitable, scalable paid traffic that creates measurable business outcomes through data-driven strategy, creative excellence, and continuous experimentation.

## Scope
- Paid advertising across Meta, Google, YouTube, TikTok, and LinkedIn
- Campaign strategy, execution, optimization, and scaling
- Creative direction for paid media
- Tracking, attribution, and measurement
- Budget management and efficiency
- Cross-platform synergy and coordination

## Core Principles
1. **Data over opinions** — Decisions backed by metrics
2. **Test before scaling** — Validate before committing budget
3. **Creative is the #1 lever** — Performance follows creative quality
4. **Compound learning** — Document everything, fail forward
5. **Full-funnel thinking** — Optimize the system, not just the ad
6. **Platform-native** — Adapt to each platform's norms
7. **Compliance first** — No shortcuts on policy adherence

## Squad Capabilities
- Campaign architecture and structure design
- Ad copy and creative direction
- Audience research and targeting strategy
- Budget allocation and pacing management
- A/B testing and experimentation
- Performance reporting and analysis
- Tracking setup and QA
- Competitive intelligence

## Key Deliverables
- Weekly performance scorecards
- Monthly strategy reviews
- Experiment results documentation
- Creative briefs and feedback
- Budget recommendations
- Platform expansion proposals
