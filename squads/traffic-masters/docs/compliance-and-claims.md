# Compliance & Claims Guide

## Purpose
Ensure all advertising content complies with platform policies and regulations.

## Pre-Launch Compliance Checklist
- [ ] Ad copy reviewed against policy-safe language guide
- [ ] Claims substantiated with evidence
- [ ] Disclaimers included where required
- [ ] Personal attribute assertions removed (Meta)
- [ ] Landing page matches ad promises
- [ ] Industry-specific regulations checked
- [ ] Targeting complies with restricted categories

## Platform Policy Quick Reference

### Meta
- No asserting personal attributes ("Are you overweight?")
- Health, finance, and political ads have special requirements
- Before/after images need disclaimers
- Special Ad Categories must be declared

### Google
- No misleading claims or clickbait
- Superlatives require third-party verification
- Restricted content categories need approval
- Trademark usage has specific rules

### TikTok
- No body shaming or unrealistic expectations
- Age-appropriate content only
- Financial claims require substantiation
- Product safety claims regulated

## Rejection Response Process
1. Read the specific policy cited in rejection
2. Identify the offending element
3. Revise using policy-safe alternatives
4. Resubmit with changes documented
5. If wrongly rejected, submit appeal with explanation

## Documentation
- Log all rejections and resolutions
- Track patterns in rejections (systemic issues)
- Update policy-safe language guide with new learnings
- Review platform policy updates monthly
