## 1. Strategy Agent

**Domain:** Campaign Planning & Strategy

**Capabilities:**
- Develop full-funnel campaign strategies aligned with business objectives
- Define KPI frameworks and success criteria
- Create media plans with channel mix recommendations
- Map customer journeys to campaign architecture
- Provide strategic recommendations based on performance data

**Inputs:** Business goals, budget parameters, audience data, competitive intelligence, historical performance
**Outputs:** Campaign strategies, media plans, KPI frameworks, strategic recommendations
**Quality Gates:** Strategy alignment with business goals, feasibility assessment, risk evaluation

**When to invoke:** New campaign planning, quarterly strategy reviews, major pivots, business goal changes

---

## 2. Diagnostics Agent

**Domain:** Account Health & Issue Diagnosis

**Capabilities:**
- Perform full account audits across all dimensions
- Identify performance anomalies and root causes
- Diagnose campaign, ad set, and ad-level issues
- Assess tracking and attribution health
- Generate prioritized issue lists with severity ratings

**Inputs:** Account access, performance data, historical benchmarks, tracking configuration
**Outputs:** Audit reports, diagnostic findings, root cause analyses, prioritized action items
**Quality Gates:** Data completeness verification, hypothesis validation, severity classification

**When to invoke:** Account onboarding, performance drops, periodic health checks, pre-scaling assessment

---

## 3. Budget Agent

**Domain:** Budget Allocation & Financial Optimization

**Capabilities:**
- Allocate budgets across campaigns, channels, and audiences
- Perform marginal CPA/ROAS analysis for reallocation decisions
- Forecast spend and return scenarios
- Monitor pacing and alert on over/under-delivery
- Recommend bid strategy adjustments

**Inputs:** Current budget, performance data by campaign, business constraints, KPI targets
**Outputs:** Budget allocation plans, reallocation recommendations, pacing reports, forecasts
**Quality Gates:** Budget reconciliation, pacing accuracy, efficiency threshold checks

**When to invoke:** Budget planning, reallocation cycles, pacing issues, scaling decisions

---

## 4. Audience Agent

**Domain:** Audience Research, Building & Optimization

**Capabilities:**
- Research and define target audience segments
- Build lookalike, custom, and interest-based audiences
- Analyze audience performance and overlap
- Recommend audience expansion or refinement
- Manage exclusion lists and suppression

**Inputs:** Customer data, CRM lists, platform audience insights, conversion data
**Outputs:** Audience definitions, segment recommendations, overlap reports, exclusion lists
**Quality Gates:** Audience size validation, overlap thresholds, privacy compliance checks

**When to invoke:** Campaign setup, audience fatigue, expansion initiatives, retargeting optimization

---

## 5. Creative Strategist Agent

**Domain:** Creative Strategy & Performance Analysis

**Capabilities:**
- Develop creative briefs and concepts
- Analyze creative performance by format, hook, angle, and element
- Identify creative fatigue and recommend refresh timing
- Build creative testing roadmaps
- Map creative to funnel stage and audience segment

**Inputs:** Performance data by creative, brand guidelines, audience insights, competitive creative
**Outputs:** Creative briefs, performance analyses, fatigue alerts, testing plans, rotation schedules
**Quality Gates:** Brand compliance, creative diversity, fatigue threshold monitoring

**When to invoke:** Creative planning, fatigue detection, performance reviews, new campaign creative

---

## 6. Copy Agent

**Domain:** Ad Copy Creation & Testing

**Capabilities:**
- Write ad copy across all formats (headlines, descriptions, primary text, CTAs)
- Generate copy variations for testing
- Adapt copy to platform-specific requirements and character limits
- Apply phrase libraries and proven formulas
- Optimize copy based on performance data

**Inputs:** Creative briefs, brand voice guidelines, audience profiles, phrase libraries, performance data
**Outputs:** Ad copy sets, headline variations, CTA options, copy test plans
**Quality Gates:** Character limit compliance, brand voice adherence, platform policy pre-check

**When to invoke:** New ad creation, copy testing, creative refresh, platform-specific adaptation

---

## 7. Launch Agent

**Domain:** Campaign Setup & Activation

**Capabilities:**
- Set up campaigns, ad sets, and ads according to specifications
- Configure targeting, bidding, scheduling, and placements
- Implement tracking parameters (UTMs, pixels, events)
- Perform pre-launch quality checks
- Activate campaigns with appropriate monitoring

**Inputs:** Campaign strategy, creative assets, audience definitions, budget allocation, tracking plan
**Outputs:** Configured campaigns ready for activation, pre-launch checklists, launch confirmation
**Quality Gates:** Pre-launch checklist completion, tracking verification, settings validation

**When to invoke:** New campaign launches, campaign restructuring, platform migrations

---

## 8. Optimization Agent

**Domain:** Ongoing Campaign Optimization

**Capabilities:**
- Monitor and optimize live campaigns
- Adjust bids, budgets, targeting, and placements based on performance
- Identify and act on optimization opportunities
- Manage creative rotation and pausing underperformers
- Implement A/B test results

**Inputs:** Live performance data, KPI targets, optimization rules, test results
**Outputs:** Optimization actions, performance improvements, optimization logs
**Quality Gates:** Learning phase protection, change frequency limits, performance trend validation

**When to invoke:** Daily monitoring, weekly optimization cycles, performance threshold triggers

---

## 9. Reporting Agent

**Domain:** Performance Reporting & Insights

**Capabilities:**
- Generate daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly performance reports
- Create executive summaries and detailed breakdowns
- Visualize data trends and comparisons
- Provide context and insight beyond raw metrics
- Prepare client-ready deliverables

**Inputs:** Performance data, KPI targets, historical benchmarks, reporting templates
**Outputs:** Reports (various cadences), scorecards, trend analyses, insight summaries
**Quality Gates:** Data accuracy verification, metric consistency, insight quality

**When to invoke:** Scheduled reporting cadences, ad-hoc analysis requests, stakeholder presentations

---

## 10. Tracking Agent

**Domain:** Tracking Setup, Auditing & Attribution

**Capabilities:**
- Audit tracking implementations (pixels, CAPI, GTM, UTMs)
- Diagnose tracking discrepancies and data loss
- Recommend attribution models and configurations
- Validate conversion event setup
- Monitor data integrity over time

**Inputs:** Tracking configuration, platform data, analytics data, website/app access
**Outputs:** Tracking audits, discrepancy reports, attribution recommendations, implementation guides
**Quality Gates:** Data match rate thresholds, event firing validation, cross-platform consistency

**When to invoke:** Account onboarding, data discrepancies, platform migrations, attribution reviews

---

## 11. Intelligence Agent

**Domain:** Competitive & Market Intelligence

**Capabilities:**
- Monitor competitor ad activity and creative strategies
- Analyze market trends and industry benchmarks
- Identify competitive threats and opportunities
- Research emerging platforms and ad formats
- Curate competitive swipe files

**Inputs:** Competitor lists, industry data, ad transparency tools, market reports
**Outputs:** Competitive analyses, trend reports, benchmark data, opportunity briefs
**Quality Gates:** Source verification, data recency, competitive coverage completeness

**When to invoke:** Strategic planning, competitive shifts, market entry, quarterly reviews

---

## 12. Experimentation Agent

**Domain:** Test Design, Execution & Analysis

**Capabilities:**
- Design statistically valid A/B and multivariate tests
- Calculate required sample sizes and test durations
- Monitor test progress and statistical significance
- Analyze test results and determine winners
- Document learnings and update knowledge base

**Inputs:** Test hypotheses, current performance baselines, sample size data, business constraints
**Outputs:** Test plans, progress reports, result analyses, learning documentation
**Quality Gates:** Statistical validity, minimum sample sizes, test isolation verification

**When to invoke:** Hypothesis testing, creative testing, audience testing, landing page testing

---

## 13. Scaling Agent

**Domain:** Campaign & Account Scaling

**Capabilities:**
- Assess scaling readiness and constraints
- Develop scaling plans with budget, audience, and creative expansion
- Monitor scaling impact on efficiency metrics
- Identify diminishing return thresholds
- Manage phased scaling approaches

**Inputs:** Current performance, budget capacity, audience potential, creative inventory
**Outputs:** Scaling plans, readiness assessments, phase-gated scaling recommendations
**Quality Gates:** Efficiency threshold maintenance, learning phase management, risk assessment

**When to invoke:** Growth initiatives, post-testing scale-up, budget increases, new market entry

---

## 14. Compliance Agent

**Domain:** Ad Policy Compliance & Risk Management

**Capabilities:**
- Pre-screen ads for platform policy violations
- Monitor for ad disapprovals and account restrictions
- Ensure compliance with industry regulations (FTC, GDPR, HIPAA, etc.)
- Provide policy-compliant alternative messaging
- Track policy changes across platforms

**Inputs:** Ad copy, creative assets, platform policies, industry regulations, claim substantiation
**Outputs:** Compliance reviews, risk assessments, policy-compliant alternatives, policy update alerts
**Quality Gates:** Platform policy alignment, regulatory compliance, claim substantiation

**When to invoke:** Pre-launch review, ad disapprovals, regulated industry campaigns, policy updates

---

## 15. Integration Agent

**Domain:** Cross-Platform & Cross-Squad Coordination

**Capabilities:**
- Coordinate workflows across Copy Squad and Brand Squad
- Ensure consistency across platforms and channels
- Manage data flow between systems
- Align messaging and creative across squads
- Facilitate handoffs and shared resources

**Inputs:** Cross-squad requests, shared assets, platform configurations, integration requirements
**Outputs:** Coordination plans, integration documentation, handoff confirmations, alignment reports
**Quality Gates:** Cross-squad consistency, data integrity, handoff completeness

**When to invoke:** Cross-squad projects, multi-platform campaigns, system integrations, asset sharing

---

## 16. Swipe File Agent

**Domain:** Creative Inspiration & Resource Curation

**Capabilities:**
- Curate and organize ad swipe files by platform, industry, and format
- Identify trending creative approaches and formats
- Categorize and tag creative examples for easy retrieval
- Maintain freshness by rotating and updating collections
- Extract patterns and insights from collected examples

**Inputs:** Ad examples, platform libraries, competitive creative, performance data
**Outputs:** Organized swipe files, trend reports, pattern analyses, creative inspiration briefs
**Quality Gates:** Recency, diversity of examples, accurate categorization

**When to invoke:** Creative ideation, trend research, onboarding new team members, creative refresh
