# Cross-Squad Integration Guide

## Purpose
How the Traffic Masters Squad integrates with other squads.

## Integration Points

### Content Squad
- **Input from Content:** Blog posts, videos, lead magnets for promotion
- **Output to Content:** Performance data on content-driven ads
- **Shared:** Audience insights, messaging that resonates
- **Cadence:** Weekly sync on content calendar and traffic needs

### Sales Squad
- **Input from Sales:** Lead quality feedback, close rates by source
- **Output to Sales:** Lead volume forecasts, campaign timing
- **Shared:** CRM data for audience building, attribution
- **Cadence:** Bi-weekly review of lead quality and volume

### Brand Squad
- **Input from Brand:** Brand guidelines, approved messaging
- **Output to Brand:** Performance data by brand vs DR creative
- **Shared:** Creative assets, brand voice alignment
- **Cadence:** Monthly creative review

### Analytics / Data Squad
- **Input from Analytics:** Attribution data, cohort analysis, LTV data
- **Output to Analytics:** Campaign data, UTM standards
- **Shared:** Dashboard access, tracking requirements
- **Cadence:** Weekly data quality check

### Product Squad
- **Input from Product:** New features, product updates, launch timing
- **Output to Product:** Customer acquisition insights, demand signals
- **Shared:** Product-market fit signals from ad performance

## Communication Protocol
- Use dedicated cross-squad Slack channels
- Share weekly traffic summary with relevant squads
- Attend monthly cross-squad planning meetings
- Document shared decisions in respective logs
