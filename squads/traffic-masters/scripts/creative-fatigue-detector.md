# Creative Fatigue Detector — Script Guide

## Purpose
Identify creative fatigue before it significantly impacts campaign performance.

## Fatigue Indicators

| Indicator | Warning Threshold | Critical Threshold |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------|
| CTR decline from peak | -15% | -30% |
| CPA increase from best | +20% | +40% |
| Frequency (7-day) | 2.5 | 4.0+ |
| CPM increase | +15% (no market cause) | +30% |
| Relevance/Quality score | Drop of 1 point | Drop of 2+ points |

## Detection Process

### Daily Check
1. Pull CTR trend for last 7 days per creative
2. Compare current frequency to threshold
3. Flag any creative hitting warning thresholds

### Weekly Analysis
1. Rank creatives by CPA (current week vs best week)
2. Identify creatives with 3+ consecutive days of decline
3. Check frequency distribution across audience

### Monthly Review
1. Creative lifecycle analysis (launch > peak > decline)
2. Average creative lifespan calculation
3. Creative pipeline health assessment

## Response Actions

| Fatigue Level | Action |
|--------------|--------|
| Early Warning | Prepare replacement creative |
| Warning | Reduce budget 30%, launch replacement |
| Critical | Pause creative, activate replacement |

## Prevention Strategies
1. Maintain 3-5 active creatives per ad set
2. Launch new creative every 2 weeks proactively
3. Use creative iteration pattern for fresh variations
4. Set frequency caps where platform allows
5. Rotate messaging angles to prevent message fatigue
