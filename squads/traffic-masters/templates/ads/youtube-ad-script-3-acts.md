# YouTube Ad Script - 3 Acts Framework

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: ads
> **Used by tasks**: video-ad-scripting, youtube-campaign-creation, creative-production
> **Filled by agents**: copywriter-agent, creative-strategist-agent

## Purpose
Provides a narrative-driven script template using the classic 3-act storytelling structure for YouTube ads, creating emotional engagement that drives action through setup, confrontation, and resolution.

## Usage Notes
- 3 Acts works best for emotional or story-driven products and brands.
- Act II should take the most screen time; it carries the persuasion.
- Test different Pre-Hooks with the same body to optimize skip rate.

## Example
A fitness app ad: Act I shows someone struggling with inconsistent workouts. Act II shows failed gym attempts, then discovering the app. Act III shows them transformed, confident, with results. CTA: "Download free for 7 days."
