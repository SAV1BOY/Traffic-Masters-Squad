## Purpose
Structures LinkedIn ad copy and targeting for B2B campaigns, optimized for the professional context with appropriate tone, targeting precision, and lead-generation focus.

### Creative Specs
**Single image**: 1200x627 (landscape) or 1080x1080 (square)
**Video**: MP4, 3s-30min (recommend 15-30s), square or vertical
**Carousel**: 2-10 cards, 1080x1080 per card
**Document**: PDF upload, native slide viewer format
**Image guidelines**:
- Use brand colors for recognition
- Include a human face when possible (higher engagement)
- Text on image should be minimal and readable on mobile
- Avoid stock photo cliches

### B2B Tone Guidelines
- Write as a peer, not a vendor
- Lead with insight, not product features
- Use industry-specific language, avoid consumer jargon
- Respect the professional context; no clickbait or hype
- Focus on business outcomes: revenue, efficiency, risk reduction
- Quantify value wherever possible
- Avoid exclamation marks; use confident, measured language

**Bid type**: [auto / max delivery / target cost / manual]

## Usage Notes
- LinkedIn CPCs are 3-10x higher than other platforms; ensure lead quality justifies cost.
- Test single image vs. document ads; document ads often get higher engagement.
- Use Lead Gen Forms for highest conversion rate; website forms for highest intent.

## Example
A B2B SaaS targeting VP-level marketing leaders at companies with 200+ employees. Single image ad offering a "State of Marketing Automation" report download via Lead Gen Form.
