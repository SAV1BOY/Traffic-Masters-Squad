> **Type**: Template

## Purpose
Provides copy and creative templates organized by retargeting window, ensuring messaging escalates appropriately from gentle reminder to urgency-driven offer as time since last engagement increases.

### Window 1: 1-3 Days (Reminder)
**Audience**: Users who visited in the last 1-3 days
**Intent level**: High - they remember you
**Tone**: Helpful, gentle nudge

**Primary text A**: "[Remind them what they were looking at. 'Still thinking about [product]? Here's why customers love it...']"
**Primary text B**: "[Address the most common reason for not buying yet. 'Questions about [product]? Here's what you need to know.']"
**Headline A**: [Product name or category they viewed]
**Headline B**: [Simple benefit reminder]
**CTA**: [Complete Your Order / Continue Shopping / Learn More]
**Creative approach**: Show the exact product or category they viewed (DPA/dynamic)
**Notes**: Keep it simple. They know you. No need to re-educate.

### Window 2: 4-7 Days (Social Proof)
**Audience**: Users who visited 4-7 days ago
**Intent level**: Medium-high - starting to forget
**Tone**: Confidence-building, proof-heavy

**Primary text A**: "[Lead with a testimonial or review. '[Customer name]: This changed how I [outcome]...']"
**Primary text B**: "[Lead with a number. 'Join [X]+ customers who chose [product] this month.']"
**Primary text C**: "[Feature a case study. 'How [customer] achieved [result] with [product].']"
**Headline A**: [Star rating or review count]
**Headline B**: [Specific customer result]
**CTA**: [See Reviews / Read the Story / Shop Now]
**Creative approach**: Customer testimonial video, review screenshots, before/after images
**Notes**: Social proof reduces perceived risk. Show real people and real results.

### Window 3: 8-14 Days (New Angle)
**Audience**: Users who visited 8-14 days ago
**Intent level**: Medium - need re-engagement
**Tone**: Fresh perspective, different value proposition

**Primary text A**: "[Present a new angle they haven't seen. Different benefit or use case.]"
**Primary text B**: "[Address a secondary pain point. 'Did you know [product] also helps with [problem]?']"
**Primary text C**: "[Comparison or education angle. 'Why [your approach] beats [alternative].']"
**Headline A**: [New benefit they haven't heard]
**Headline B**: [Question that reopens curiosity]
**CTA**: [Discover More / See How It Works / Learn the Difference]
**Creative approach**: Different format than earlier windows (carousel if image before, video if static before)
**Notes**: If the first angle did not convert them, this window tests a new persuasion path.

### Window 4: 15-30 Days (Special Offer)
**Audience**: Users who visited 15-30 days ago
**Intent level**: Low - need incentive to return
**Tone**: Generous, urgency-driven

**Primary text A**: "[Direct offer. 'We saved [X]% off [product] just for you. But only for [time limit].']"
**Primary text B**: "[Bundle or bonus offer. 'Come back and get [bonus item] free with your order.']"
**Primary text C**: "[Last chance framing. 'Your cart is about to expire. Here's a little something to sweeten the deal.']"
**Headline A**: [Discount percentage or amount]
**Headline B**: [Limited time urgency statement]
**CTA**: [Claim Your Offer / Get X% Off / Redeem Now]
**Creative approach**: Offer-focused creative with clear discount/value callout, countdown or urgency element
**Notes**: Only use discounts in this window to avoid training audiences to wait for deals.

## Usage Notes
- Set up separate ad sets per window for clean measurement.
- Exclude converters from all retargeting windows.
- Refresh creative monthly; retargeting audiences see ads frequently.
- Monitor frequency; if above 4x/week, narrow the window or add exclusions.

## Example
Ecommerce brand: Day 1-3 shows DPA of viewed products, Day 4-7 shows customer review video, Day 8-14 presents a lifestyle use-case carousel, Day 15-30 offers 15% off with countdown.
