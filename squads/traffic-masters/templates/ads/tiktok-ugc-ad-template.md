# TikTok UGC Ad Template

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: ads
> **Used by tasks**: ugc-ad-creation, tiktok-campaign-setup, creative-production
> **Filled by agents**: copywriter-agent, ugc-coordinator-agent, creative-strategist-agent

## Purpose
Structures UGC-style ads optimized for TikTok's native format, ensuring content feels organic while delivering strategic messaging with strong hooks, relatable storytelling, and clear CTAs.

## Template

### Ad Metadata
**Ad name**: [following naming convention]
**Duration**: [15s / 30s / 45s / 60s]
**Creator type**: [in-house / external UGC / influencer]
**Shooting style**: [selfie-cam / tripod / handheld / screen-record]
**Sound**: [original audio / trending sound / voiceover]

### Hook [0-3 seconds]
**Purpose**: Stop the scroll within the first second. This determines everything.

**Hook Option A**: "[Bold opening statement or question]"
- Visual action: [describe what happens on screen]

**Hook Option B**: "[Controversial or surprising claim]"
- Visual action: [describe what happens on screen]

**Hook Option C**: "[Direct callout of target audience]"
- Visual action: [describe what happens on screen]

**Text overlay**: [on-screen text that reinforces or adds to the verbal hook]
**Hook best practices**:
- Start mid-action, not with an intro
- No "Hey guys" or "So I just..." openings
- Movement in the first frame
- Text on screen within first second

### Problem / Relate [3-8 seconds]
**Purpose**: Make the viewer feel seen. Describe their situation.
**Script**: "[Words that describe the problem in the audience's own language]"
**Visual**: [relatable scenario, facial expression, environment]
**Text overlay**: [reinforce the pain point]
**Emotional target**: [frustration / FOMO / curiosity / insecurity]
**Notes**: Use first person. "I used to..." or "When I realized..." format works well.

### Solution / Product [8-18 seconds]
**Purpose**: Introduce the product as a natural discovery, not a pitch.
**Script**: "[Natural transition into the product - 'then I found...' or 'this changed everything']"
**Visual**: [product in use, unboxing, demonstration, close-up]
**Text overlay**: [product name, key feature callout]
**Key points to cover**:
1. [What it is in one sentence]
2. [How it works or how they use it]
3. [What makes it different]
**Notes**: Show, don't tell. The product should be visible and in context.

### Proof / Results [18-25 seconds]
**Purpose**: Build belief with evidence of results.
**Script**: "[Share a specific result, before/after, or reaction]"
**Visual**: [results screenshot, before/after, reaction shot, testimonial]
**Text overlay**: [stat, result, or social proof number]
**Proof types to use**:
- Personal result: "In just [time], I [achieved result]"
- Social proof: "[X] people are already using this"
- Visual proof: Before/after comparison
- Authority: Featured in [publication] or recommended by [expert]

### CTA [25-30 seconds]
**Purpose**: Tell the viewer exactly what to do next.
**Script**: "[Clear, specific action - 'Click the link in bio' / 'Use code X for Y% off']"
**Visual**: [point to link, show offer screen, product glamour shot]
**Text overlay**: [CTA text, offer code, URL]
**CTA urgency**: [limited time / limited stock / exclusive offer]
**End frame**: [hold product, show offer, or loop-friendly ending]

### Native Format Guidelines
**Aspect ratio**: 9:16 (vertical only)
**Resolution**: 1080x1920 minimum
**Safe zones**: Keep text and key visuals away from top 15% and bottom 20%
**Captions**: Burned-in, large font, centered or bottom-third
**Music**: Use trending sounds when possible; ensure licensing
**Transitions**: Native TikTok transitions (jump cuts, zoom, swipe)
**Filters**: Minimal or trending filters; avoid over-produced look

### UGC Authenticity Notes
- Film in real environments (home, car, office, outdoors) not studios
- Use natural lighting; avoid ring light "influencer" look
- Imperfect is better than polished; slight messiness builds trust
- Speak conversationally, not like reading ad copy
- Show genuine reactions and emotions
- Leave in minor hesitations or natural speech patterns

## Usage Notes
- Produce at least 3 hook variations per ad body for testing.
- Recut winners into 15-second versions for broader distribution.
- Tag all creatives with taxonomy-tags.md for performance tracking.

## Example
Creator in their kitchen discovers a meal prep service. Hook: "Nobody talks about how expensive eating healthy actually is." Shows the product, demonstrates a meal, reveals the price comparison. CTA: "Link in bio for $20 off your first box."
