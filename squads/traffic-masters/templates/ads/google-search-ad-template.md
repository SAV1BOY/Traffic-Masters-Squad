## Purpose
Structures Responsive Search Ads (RSAs) with all required headlines, descriptions, and extensions following Google Ads best practices for maximum ad strength and relevance.

### RSA Best Practices
- Include keywords in at least 3 headlines
- Use all 15 headline slots and all 4 description slots
- Pin sparingly; only pin when specific messaging must appear
- Make each headline and description unique and standalone
- Include at least one headline with numbers or statistics
- Test emotional vs. rational messaging across headlines
- Ensure ad strength rating reaches "Good" or "Excellent"
- Include the primary keyword in at least one description

## Usage Notes
- Create at least 2 RSAs per ad group for testing.
- Review Google Ads recommendations for ad strength weekly.
- Pause underperforming asset combinations after 30 days of data.

## Example
A SaaS project management tool targeting "project management software": H1 pins keyword, H3 highlights "Save 10 Hours/Week", D1 expands on time-saving with free trial CTA.
