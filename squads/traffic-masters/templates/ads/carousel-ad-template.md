# Carousel Ad Template

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: ads
> **Used by tasks**: carousel-ad-creation, creative-production, campaign-setup
> **Filled by agents**: copywriter-agent, creative-strategist-agent

## Purpose
Structures multi-card carousel ads with a deliberate narrative flow, ensuring each card serves a strategic purpose from hook through story to final CTA.

## Template

### Carousel Metadata
**Platform**: [Meta / LinkedIn / TikTok]
**Number of cards**: [3-10, recommend 5]
**Aspect ratio**: 1:1 (standard) or 4:5 (Meta feed optimized)
**Resolution**: 1080x1080 or 1080x1350
**Primary text**: [copy that appears above the carousel]

### Primary Text (Above Carousel)
**Version A**: "[Hook that compels the viewer to swipe through all cards]"
**Version B**: "[Alternative hook with different angle]"
**Notes**: The primary text must create a reason to swipe. Tease the story arc.

### Card 1 - Hook
**Purpose**: Stop the scroll and invite the swipe.
**Image**: [Bold visual, intriguing question, or pattern interrupt]
**Headline**: [Max 40 chars - provoke curiosity to swipe]
**Description**: [Max 20 chars - "Swipe to see..." or benefit tease]
**Design notes**: Use an arrow or visual cue pointing right to encourage swiping.

### Card 2 - Story / Problem
**Purpose**: Establish the problem or begin the narrative.
**Image**: [Visual representing the problem, challenge, or before state]
**Headline**: [Max 40 chars - describe the pain or situation]
**Description**: [Max 20 chars - supporting detail]
**Design notes**: Maintain visual continuity with Card 1 (colors, style, layout).

### Card 3 - Benefits / Solution
**Purpose**: Present the solution and key benefits.
**Image**: [Product in action, transformation moment, or key benefit visual]
**Headline**: [Max 40 chars - primary benefit statement]
**Description**: [Max 20 chars - supporting benefit]
**Design notes**: This is the pivot card; visually shift from problem to solution.

### Card 4 - Proof
**Purpose**: Build credibility and trust.
**Image**: [Testimonial, review, result, before/after, or social proof]
**Headline**: [Max 40 chars - proof statement or customer quote]
**Description**: [Max 20 chars - attribution or stat]
**Design notes**: Use real photos, screenshots, or data visuals for authenticity.

### Card 5 - CTA
**Purpose**: Drive the click with a clear call to action.
**Image**: [Product hero shot, offer summary, or action-oriented visual]
**Headline**: [Max 40 chars - clear CTA with urgency or offer]
**Description**: [Max 20 chars - reinforce the offer]
**URL**: [landing page with UTMs]
**CTA button**: [Shop Now / Learn More / Sign Up / Get Offer]
**Design notes**: Make the CTA card visually distinct; use a contrasting color.

### Additional Cards (if using 6-10)
**Card 6**: [Extended benefit, use case, or feature highlight]
**Card 7**: [FAQ answer, objection handler, or comparison]
**Card 8**: [Additional testimonial or case study]
**Card 9**: [Bonus or value stack]
**Card 10**: [Final CTA reinforcement]

### Flow Guidelines
**Narrative flow options**:
1. **Problem to Solution**: Pain > Agitate > Solution > Proof > CTA
2. **Listicle**: "5 Reasons Why..." - one reason per card
3. **Story Arc**: Setup > Conflict > Resolution > Result > CTA
4. **Product Showcase**: Hero > Feature 1 > Feature 2 > Feature 3 > CTA
5. **Before/After**: Before > The Change > After > Proof > CTA

**Swiping psychology**:
- Each card must create enough curiosity to earn the next swipe
- Never put the full message on one card; distribute value across the arc
- Use visual continuity (consistent colors, fonts, style) to feel cohesive
- End each card with an incomplete thought or visual tease

### Per-Card Checklist
- [ ] Image meets platform specs (size, ratio, file type)
- [ ] Headline is under character limit
- [ ] Description is under character limit
- [ ] URL is set (per-card or carousel-level)
- [ ] Visual hierarchy guides the eye to the right (swipe direction)
- [ ] Text on image is readable on mobile

## Usage Notes
- Test carousel vs. single image on the same audience to measure lift.
- Cards 1 and 5 are most critical; test variations of these first.
- Monitor per-card click-through to identify where users drop off.

## Example
Fitness app carousel: Card 1 "What if 15 minutes changed everything?", Card 2 shows gym intimidation problem, Card 3 shows the app solution, Card 4 shows transformation testimonial, Card 5 "Start your free trial today."
