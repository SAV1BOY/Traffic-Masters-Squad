# Audit Report Template

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: reports
> **Used by tasks**: account-audit, performance-review, client-onboarding
> **Filled by agents**: auditor-agent, strategist-agent

## Purpose
Structures audit findings into a prioritized report with account overview, categorized findings, quick wins, and a 30/60/90 implementation plan with estimated performance impact.

**Examples of critical findings**:
- Conversion tracking broken or misconfigured
- Significant budget spent on non-converting campaigns
- Missing exclusions causing audience overlap or waste
- No CAPI implementation with declining match rates

## Usage Notes
- Present findings in person or via video call for maximum impact.
- Lead with quick wins to build momentum and trust.
- Follow up at 30, 60, and 90 days to track implementation progress.

## Example
Ecommerce brand audit: 8 findings (2 critical, 3 important, 3 nice-to-have). Critical: Meta pixel firing duplicate Purchase events, Google Shopping campaigns missing negative keywords. Quick wins: add purchaser exclusions (est. 10% CPA reduction), pause 3 zero-conversion ad sets. Projected 25% CPA improvement in 90 days.
