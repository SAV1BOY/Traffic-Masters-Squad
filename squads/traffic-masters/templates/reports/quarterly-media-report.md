# Quarterly Media Report

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: reports
> **Used by tasks**: quarterly-review, strategic-planning, executive-reporting
> **Filled by agents**: strategist-agent, analyst-agent

## Purpose
Provides a high-level quarterly summary of paid media performance, comparing budget vs. actual spend, identifying channel and creative trends, delivering strategic recommendations, and outlining the next quarter's plan.

## Usage Notes
- Present within 10 business days of quarter end.
- Include visual charts for trend data in the presentation version.
- This report drives budget and strategic decisions; ensure accuracy.

## Example
Q1 2026: $126k spent (98% of $129k budget). Blended ROAS 3.6x vs. 3.5x target. Meta drove 70% of conversions with UGC outperforming polished by 2x. Google Search stable. TikTok tested but inconclusive. Q2 recommendation: increase Meta by 20%, scale TikTok test, maintain Google.
