# Monthly Growth Report

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: reports
> **Used by tasks**: monthly-review, strategic-planning, stakeholder-reporting
> **Filled by agents**: analyst-agent, strategist-agent

## Purpose
Delivers a comprehensive monthly analysis of unit economics, channel performance, creative performance, and funnel metrics, providing the strategic context needed to adjust plans and allocate resources for the coming month.

## Usage Notes
- Deliver by the 5th business day of the following month.
- Include visualizations (charts, graphs) in the presentation version.
- Separate platform-reported data from blended/MER data clearly.

## Example
March report: $42k spent, $168k revenue, MER 4.0x (target 3.5x). CAC dropped 12% MoM to $38. Meta drove 65% of conversions. UGC video format had 2.3x higher ROAS than static. Funnel drop-off highest at checkout (41% abandon rate). Next month: focus on checkout optimization and scale Meta UGC by 30%.
