> **Type**: Template

## Purpose
Conducts a structured review of a completed campaign, comparing goals to results, analyzing what worked and what did not, identifying root causes, extracting learnings, and defining next steps to improve future campaigns.

## Usage Notes
- Conduct the post-mortem within 1 week of campaign end while details are fresh.
- Involve all team members who worked on the campaign.
- Focus on learning, not blame. Every failure is a data point for improvement.
- Add key learnings to the learnings-log-template.md for institutional knowledge.

## Example
Black Friday campaign post-mortem: Hit 92% of revenue target but overspent budget by 8%. UGC creative outperformed everything (3.8x ROAS). Google Shopping underperformed due to inventory issues. Key learning: align inventory planning with ad scaling schedule. Next steps: create inventory-check process in pre-launch checklist.
