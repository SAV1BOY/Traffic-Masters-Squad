> **Type**: Template

## Purpose
Analyzes creative performance across the portfolio, identifying top performers by CTR, CVR, and ROAS, detecting fatigue signals, extracting winning and losing patterns, and providing specific iteration recommendations.

## Usage Notes
- Produce this report bi-weekly or monthly depending on creative volume.
- Cross-reference with taxonomy-tags.md for accurate angle and format classification.
- Feed winning patterns back into creative-brief.md for future production cycles.

## Example
March analysis: 24 creatives analyzed across Meta and TikTok. UGC video with pain angle and stat hooks had the highest ROAS (4.2x). 3 creatives showing fatigue signals (CTR down 35%+ from peak). Key pattern: short-form UGC (under 30s) outperforms all other formats for TOFU. Recommendation: produce 4 new UGC variations with different stat hooks.
