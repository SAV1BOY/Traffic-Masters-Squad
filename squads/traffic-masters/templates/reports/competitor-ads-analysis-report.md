## Usage Notes
- Conduct this analysis monthly or before major campaign launches.
- Long-running ads (active 30+ days) likely indicate strong performers worth studying.
- Never copy competitor ads directly; use insights to inform original creative development.

## Example
Analyzed 4 competitors in the meal-kit space. All use UGC-heavy Meta ads with price-comparison angles. None are using objection-handling hooks about cooking skill. Opportunity: produce "even if you can't cook" angle in UGC format. Competitor A has a 90-day running advertorial worth studying for structure.
