> **Type**: Template

## Purpose
Standardizes budget requests for paid media spend, providing justification, expected ROI, risk assessment, and approval chain documentation so financial decisions are transparent, data-driven, and properly authorized.

## Usage Notes
- Submit budget requests at least 2 weeks before the planned spend start date.
- Include historical data whenever available to support projections.
- For budget increases above [X]%, executive approval is required.
- Track actual vs. approved spend monthly using invoice-reconciliation-template.md.

## Example
Request for $15k/month Meta Ads budget increase (from $30k to $45k). Justified by 3 consecutive months of 3.5x+ ROAS at current spend. Expected to generate $157k in additional revenue at 3.5x ROAS. Conservative scenario: 2.8x ROAS, still profitable. Stop-loss at 2.0x ROAS.
