# Creative Production Plan

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: plans

## Purpose
Manages the creative production pipeline from concept backlog through production, review, and launch, ensuring a steady flow of fresh creatives to prevent fatigue and support scaling.

## Usage Notes
- Review the backlog weekly and replenish with new concepts.
- Prioritize iterations of winning creatives alongside net-new concepts.
- Scale UGC creator pool as ad spend increases to maintain creative volume.

## Example
March production plan: 16 creatives across 4 weeks. 6 videos (2 UGC, 4 in-house), 6 static images, 4 carousels. 3 UGC creators briefed. Weekly review every Monday at 10am. All creatives tagged and launched within 3 days of approval.
