# 30-60-90 Growth Plan

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: plans
> **Used by tasks**: account-onboarding, post-audit-implementation, growth-strategy
> **Filled by agents**: strategist-agent, media-buyer-agent

## Purpose
Structures a phased 90-day improvement roadmap with clear priorities, owners, and deadlines across three phases: fundamentals (30 days), performance optimization (60 days), and growth scaling (90 days).

## Usage Notes
- Present this plan to stakeholders at kickoff for alignment.
- Conduct formal reviews at each 30-day checkpoint.
- Adjust timelines based on data but maintain the phase structure.

## Example
Post-audit implementation for an ecommerce brand spending $20k/month. Phase 1 fixes broken CAPI and restructures Meta account. Phase 2 launches creative testing and retargeting. Phase 3 scales to $35k/month with proven campaigns.
