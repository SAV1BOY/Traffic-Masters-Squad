# Seasonal Campaign Plan

> **Type**: Template

## Purpose
Plans campaigns around seasonal events, holidays, and peak periods, using historical data, competitor intelligence, and adjusted budgets to maximize performance during high-opportunity windows.

## Usage Notes
- Begin seasonal planning at least 6 weeks before the event window.
- All creative must be produced and approved 2 weeks before launch.
- Monitor CPM trends daily during peak and adjust budget allocation dynamically.

## Example
Black Friday campaign: 5-phase plan over 4 weeks. Pre-season starts Nov 1 building audiences, early bird launches Nov 15 with VIP access, peak runs Nov 24-28, extension through Cyber Monday, post-season retargeting through Dec 5. Total budget: $45k, 3x normal monthly spend.
