# Media Plan Template

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: plans
> **Used by tasks**: media-planning, budget-allocation, campaign-strategy
> **Filled by agents**: strategist-agent, media-buyer-agent

## Purpose
Defines the complete media plan including channel selection, budget allocation, timeline, KPI targets, audience strategy, creative strategy, measurement approach, and risk mitigation for a defined planning period.

### Risks
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creative fatigue | High | Medium | Maintain 2-week creative pipeline |
| CPM increases (seasonal) | Medium | High | Front-load spend before peak, diversify channels |
| Platform policy change | Low | High | Diversify across 2+ platforms |
| Tracking disruption | Medium | High | CAPI backup, manual QA checks |
| Budget cut | Low | Medium | Prioritize highest-ROI channels first |

## Usage Notes
- Review and adjust the media plan monthly based on performance data.
- Use this plan as the source of truth for budget conversations.
- All campaign briefs should reference and align with this media plan.

## Example
Q1 media plan: $90k total across Meta ($50k), Google ($30k), TikTok ($10k). TOFU 60%, MOFU 15%, BOFU 25%. Target blended ROAS of 3.5x. 12 new creatives per month with bi-weekly refresh cycle.
