# Application Page Template

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: landing-pages
> **Used by tasks**: high-ticket-funnel-build, application-funnel, lead-qualification
> **Filled by agents**: copywriter-agent, funnel-builder-agent

## Purpose
Structures an application-style landing page for high-ticket offers that qualifies leads through the page copy and form questions, filtering for fit and creating perceived exclusivity.

## Template

### Page Metadata
**Page URL**: [slug]
**Offer type**: [coaching / consulting / done-for-you / mastermind / enterprise]
**Price range**: [high-ticket indicator, not necessarily displayed]
**Target application rate**: [percentage from page visitors]
**Target qualification rate**: [percentage of applicants who qualify]

### Section 1: Qualification Headline
**Pre-headline**: "[Exclusivity signal: 'By Application Only' or 'Limited Spots Available']"
**Main headline**: "[Who this is for + outcome: 'For [audience] ready to [achieve result]']"
**Subheadline**: "[What they're applying for: 'Apply for [program/service name] and [benefit]']"
**Notes**: The headline must simultaneously attract qualified leads and repel unqualified ones.

### Section 2: Who This Is For
**Section headline**: "This is for you if..."
**Qualifier 1**: [Describe an attribute of the ideal applicant]
**Qualifier 2**: [Describe their current situation or stage]
**Qualifier 3**: [Describe their mindset or commitment level]
**Qualifier 4**: [Describe their budget readiness without stating price]

**Section headline**: "This is NOT for you if..."
**Disqualifier 1**: [Describe who should not apply]
**Disqualifier 2**: [Describe a mindset that does not fit]
**Disqualifier 3**: [Describe a situation that makes them a poor fit]

**Notes**: Disqualifiers increase perceived value and reduce unqualified applications.

### Section 3: What You Get
**Section headline**: "What's included in [program/service name]"
**Deliverable 1**: [Name] - [brief description of what it is and what it achieves]
**Deliverable 2**: [Name] - [description]
**Deliverable 3**: [Name] - [description]
**Deliverable 4**: [Name] - [description]
**Bonus (optional)**: [Name] - [description, available for approved applicants only]
**Duration / timeline**: [program length or engagement period]
**Format**: [1:1 / group / hybrid / done-for-you / self-paced + support]
**Notes**: Focus on outcomes per deliverable, not just features. Each item should answer "so what?"

### Section 4: Application Form
**Form headline**: "[Apply now / Start your application / See if you qualify]"
**Form intro text**: "Answer a few questions so we can determine if [program name] is the right fit for you."

**Qualifying questions**:
1. Full name: [text field, required]
2. Email address: [email field, required]
3. Phone number: [phone field, required for callback]
4. "[What is your current [revenue/role/situation]?]" [dropdown or text]
5. "[What is your biggest challenge with [topic]?]" [text area]
6. "[What have you tried so far to solve this?]" [text area]
7. "[What would achieving [result] mean for your business/life?]" [text area]
8. "[Are you ready to invest in a solution in the next 30 days?]" [yes/no/maybe]
9. "[How did you hear about us?]" [dropdown: ad, referral, social, search, other]

**Submit button text**: [Submit My Application / Apply Now / Request a Call]
**Post-submit message**: "Thank you for applying. We review applications within [X] business days."
**Notes**: Questions should qualify on budget, authority, need, and timeline (BANT).

### Section 5: Social Proof
**Section headline**: "[Results from past [clients/members/students]]"
**Case study 1**: [Name, starting point, result achieved, timeframe, photo]
**Case study 2**: [Name, starting point, result achieved, timeframe, photo]
**Case study 3**: [Name, starting point, result achieved, timeframe, photo]
**Video testimonials**: [embed if available - highest trust format]
**Aggregate stat**: "[X] clients served, $[Y] in results generated, [Z]% satisfaction rate"
**Notes**: High-ticket buyers need more proof. Prioritize detailed case studies over short quotes.

### Section 6: Scarcity
**Scarcity type**: [limited spots / cohort-based / capacity constraint]
**Scarcity text**: "[We accept only [X] new clients per [month/quarter] to maintain quality.]"
**Application deadline**: [date or rolling]
**Notes**: Scarcity must be real. State why the limit exists (quality, capacity, attention).

### Page Design Guidelines
**Layout**: Long-form, single column, clean and premium feel
**Typography**: Professional, readable, generous spacing
**Color palette**: Muted, premium tones with one accent for CTAs
**Images**: Professional photography, real client photos, no stock
**Mobile**: Form must be thumb-friendly; long text areas need adequate height
**No navigation**: Remove all menu links; only action is the application form
**Page speed**: Under 3 seconds

### Tracking Requirements
**Pixel fires**: PageView on load, Lead on form submission
**GA4 events**: page_view, form_start, application_submit
**UTM capture**: Store in hidden fields for attribution
**CRM integration**: Push applications to [CRM name] with source data

## Usage Notes
- Application pages convert lower than standard lead gen but produce higher-quality leads.
- Follow up within 24 hours; speed-to-lead matters for high-ticket.
- A/B test the number of qualifying questions; too many reduces volume, too few reduces quality.

## Example
A marketing agency accepting 5 new clients per month. Page qualifies on current revenue ($500k+), biggest marketing challenge, and investment readiness. Includes 3 detailed case studies with revenue results.
