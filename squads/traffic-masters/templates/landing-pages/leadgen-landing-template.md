# Lead Generation Landing Page Template

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: landing-pages
> **Used by tasks**: landing-page-creation, funnel-build, conversion-optimization
> **Filled by agents**: copywriter-agent, funnel-builder-agent

## Purpose
Structures a high-converting lead generation landing page with all essential sections from hero through final CTA, designed to capture contact information in exchange for a valuable offer.

## Template

### Page Metadata
**Page URL**: [slug]
**Traffic source**: [Meta / Google / YouTube / email / organic]
**Offer type**: [lead magnet / consultation / demo / free trial / webinar / quiz]
**Target conversion rate**: [percentage]
**Mobile-first**: [yes - priority design for mobile]

### Section 1: Hero
**Headline**: "[Clear, benefit-driven headline. What they get and why it matters.]"
**Subheadline**: "[Expand on the headline. Address who this is for and the transformation.]"
**CTA button text**: [Get Free Access / Download Now / Book My Call / Start Free Trial]
**CTA button color**: [high contrast to background]
**Hero image/video**: [product mockup, result visualization, or short explainer video]
**Trust indicators**: [logos, "As seen in", rating stars, user count]
**Notes**: Everything above the fold must answer: What is it? Who is it for? Why should I care?

### Section 2: Problem
**Section headline**: "[Identify the pain point - 'Are you struggling with...?']"
**Pain point 1**: [Specific problem statement with emotional weight]
**Pain point 2**: [Second problem statement]
**Pain point 3**: [Third problem statement]
**Closing line**: "[Bridge from problem to solution - 'There's a better way.']"
**Visual**: [icon set, illustration, or relatable image showing frustration]

### Section 3: Solution
**Section headline**: "[Introduce your solution - 'Introducing [product/offer]']"
**What it is**: [One clear paragraph describing the offer]
**Key benefits**:
1. [Benefit 1 - outcome-focused, not feature-focused]
2. [Benefit 2]
3. [Benefit 3]
**Visual**: [product screenshot, mockup, or demo GIF]
**CTA button**: [Repeat primary CTA]

### Section 4: Proof Stack
**Section headline**: "[Why people trust this - 'Join X+ who already...']"
**Testimonial 1**: "[Quote, name, title/company, photo]"
**Testimonial 2**: "[Quote, name, title/company, photo]"
**Testimonial 3**: "[Quote, name, title/company, photo]"
**Results/stats**: [Specific numbers - "87% of users report...", "Average ROI of 3.2x"]
**Logos**: [Client or media logos if applicable]
**Notes**: Use real names and photos. Video testimonials convert higher than text.

### Section 5: Form
**Form headline**: "[Action-oriented - 'Get your free [offer] now']"
**Form fields**:
- First name: [required]
- Email: [required]
- [Optional field 3]: [phone / company / role - each extra field reduces conversion]
**Submit button text**: [Same as primary CTA, not "Submit"]
**Privacy text**: "We respect your privacy. Unsubscribe anytime."
**Form placement**: [embedded in page / sticky sidebar / popup on exit]
**Notes**: Fewer fields = higher conversion. Only ask for what you absolutely need.

### Section 6: Objection Handling
**Section headline**: "[FAQ or common concerns]"
**Q1**: "[Most common objection]"
**A1**: "[Clear, reassuring answer]"
**Q2**: "[Second objection]"
**A2**: "[Answer with proof point]"
**Q3**: "[Third objection]"
**A3**: "[Answer addressing risk]"
**Q4**: "[Fourth objection]"
**A4**: "[Answer with guarantee or social proof]"

### Section 7: Final CTA
**Headline**: "[Urgency or summary - 'Don't miss out' or 'Ready to [achieve result]?']"
**Subtext**: "[Reinforce the value and what they'll get immediately]"
**CTA button**: [Same primary CTA]
**Scarcity/urgency**: [if applicable - limited spots, time-limited offer]
**Guarantee badge**: [money-back, satisfaction, risk-free]

### Footer
**Elements**: [Privacy policy link, terms link, company name, contact email]
**Notes**: Minimal footer. No navigation links that could leak traffic away from the form.

### Tracking Requirements
**Pixel fires**: [PageView on load, Lead on form submit]
**GA4 events**: [page_view, form_start, form_submit]
**UTM capture**: [store UTM params in hidden form fields]
**Thank you page redirect**: [URL of thank you page]

## Usage Notes
- Remove all navigation menus; the only action should be form submission.
- A/B test headlines first, then CTAs, then form length.
- Ensure page loads in under 3 seconds on mobile.

## Example
A marketing agency offering a free "Ad Account Audit Checklist" PDF. Hero states the benefit, problem section lists 4 common audit mistakes, proof section shows client results, form asks for name and email only.
