# UGC Creator Brief

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: briefs
> **Used by tasks**: ugc-coordination, creative-production, influencer-management
> **Filled by agents**: creative-strategist-agent, ugc-coordinator-agent

## Purpose
Provides clear instructions to UGC creators so they deliver on-brand, on-strategy video content that can be used as paid ad creative with minimal revisions.

## Template

### Creator Profile
**Creator name / handle**: [name]
**Platform presence**: [TikTok / Instagram / YouTube]
**Niche**: [fitness / beauty / tech / parenting / business / etc.]
**Audience demo**: [age, gender, interests of their followers]
**Why selected**: [authenticity fit, past performance, style match]

### Product / Service
**Product name**: [name]
**What it does**: [one-sentence description]
**Key differentiator**: [what makes it unique]
**Price**: [retail price]
**Shipping details**: [will product be sent, timeline]

### Key Messages
**Primary message**: [the one thing the viewer must remember]
**Supporting messages**:
1. [Message point]
2. [Message point]
3. [Message point]
**Proof points to mention**: [stats, results, awards, ingredients]

### Dos and Don'ts
**Do**:
- [Speak naturally, as if recommending to a friend]
- [Show the product in use, not just unboxing]
- [Include personal experience or genuine reaction]
- [Film in well-lit, clean environment]
- [Use vertical format 9:16]

**Don't**:
- [Do not read from a script word-for-word]
- [Do not mention competitor names]
- [Do not make medical or income claims]
- [Do not use copyrighted music]
- [Do not include other brand logos in frame]

### Format
**Content type**: [testimonial / demo / routine / day-in-the-life / review / unboxing]
**Number of deliverables**: [count]
**Variations requested**: [e.g., 2 different hooks on same body]

### Length
**Target duration**: [15s / 30s / 45s / 60s]
**Hook must land within**: [first 3 seconds]
**Core message by**: [first 15 seconds]

### Hook Requirement
**Hook option 1**: [suggested opening line or action]
**Hook option 2**: [alternative opening]
**Hook option 3**: [alternative opening]
**Note**: Creator may propose their own hook if it aligns with the angle.

### Delivery Specs
**File format**: [MP4 / MOV]
**Resolution**: [minimum 1080x1920]
**Orientation**: [vertical 9:16]
**Audio**: [clear voice, no background noise]
**Raw footage**: [yes/no - deliver raw clips in addition to edited]
**Delivery method**: [Google Drive / Dropbox / platform DM]
**Deadline**: [date]

### Usage Rights
**License type**: [paid ads / organic / both]
**Platforms**: [Meta / TikTok / YouTube / all]
**Duration**: [3 months / 6 months / 12 months / perpetual]
**Exclusivity**: [exclusive / non-exclusive]
**Whitelisting**: [yes/no - permission to run ads from creator handle]
**Editing rights**: [may we re-edit, add text overlays, trim]

## Usage Notes
- Send this brief along with the product shipment.
- Include 2-3 reference videos showing the style and energy expected.
- Allow creators to add their personality; over-scripting kills authenticity.

## Example
Fitness creator receives a protein powder sample. Brief requests a 45-second morning-routine video showing the product in use, with 3 hook options. Whitelisting approved for Meta and TikTok for 6 months.
