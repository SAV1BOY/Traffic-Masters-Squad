# Creative Brief

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: briefs
> **Used by tasks**: ad-copywriting, creative-production, creative-testing
> **Filled by agents**: creative-strategist-agent, copywriter-agent

## Purpose
Guides the creation of ad creatives by defining audience, angles, hooks, proof points, claims, format specs, and brand guidelines to ensure every asset is strategically aligned.

## Usage Notes
- Create one creative brief per campaign or per major creative batch.
- Each angle should produce at least 3 hook variations.
- Tag all creatives using taxonomy-tags.md for future analysis.

## Example
DTC supplement brand targeting men 30-50, testing 3 angles: energy-loss pain, performance-desire, skeptic-objection. Each angle gets 3 hooks across video and static formats.
