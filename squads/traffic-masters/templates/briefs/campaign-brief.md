# Campaign Brief

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: briefs
> **Used by tasks**: campaign-setup, ad-creation, tracking-setup
> **Filled by agents**: strategist-agent, media-buyer-agent

## Purpose
Provides a single source of truth for a specific campaign, capturing objective, hypothesis, targeting, budget, creative direction, and success criteria before any build work begins.

## Template

### Campaign Objective
**Objective type**: [awareness / traffic / leads / purchases / app-installs]
**Business goal this supports**: [reference from acquisition strategy brief]
**Funnel stage**: [TOFU / MOFU / BOFU]

### Hypothesis
**Statement**: "We believe that [action] targeting [audience] with [creative approach] will achieve [metric] of [target] because [rationale]."
**Confidence level**: [high / medium / low]
**Evidence**: [data, past results, or competitor intel supporting this]

### Platform
**Primary platform**: [Meta / Google / YouTube / TikTok / LinkedIn]
**Campaign type**: [search / display / video / shopping / feed-based]
**Account ID**: [platform account identifier]

### Audience
**Audience name**: [descriptive name]
**Targeting method**: [interest / lookalike / custom / keyword / retargeting]
**Audience size estimate**: [range]
**Exclusions**: [who to exclude]
**Geo**: [locations]
**Language**: [language(s)]

### Budget
**Daily budget**: [amount]
**Total budget**: [amount over campaign duration]
**Bid strategy**: [lowest-cost / cost-cap / bid-cap / target-CPA / target-ROAS]
**Bid target**: [amount if applicable]

### Schedule
**Start date**: [date]
**End date**: [date or ongoing]
**Dayparting**: [specific hours or all day]
**Learning phase plan**: [how to handle the learning phase]

### Creative Approach
**Number of creatives**: [count]
**Formats**: [image / video / carousel / collection]
**Angles to test**: [list angles]
**Reference creatives**: [links or descriptions of inspiration]

### Tracking Requirements
**Pixel**: [pixel ID]
**Conversion event**: [primary event to optimize for]
**CAPI**: [yes/no, endpoint details]
**UTM parameters**: [full UTM string]
**GA4 events**: [list custom events]

### Success Criteria
**Primary KPI**: [CPA / ROAS / CPL / CTR]
**Target value**: [number]
**Secondary KPIs**: [list with targets]
**Minimum sample size**: [conversions or spend before evaluation]
**Decision date**: [when to evaluate]

## Usage Notes
- One brief per campaign. Multiple ad sets within a campaign share the same brief.
- Link this brief to the creative brief for detailed creative specs.
- Update the brief if mid-flight pivots occur and note the change date.

## Example
Meta Conversions campaign targeting LAL 1% of purchasers in US, $200/day budget, testing 3 video ads with pain-point angles, optimizing for Purchase at $45 CPA target.
