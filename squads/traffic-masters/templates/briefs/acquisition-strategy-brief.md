# Acquisition Strategy Brief

> **Type**: Template
> **Category**: briefs
> **Used by tasks**: strategy-development, media-planning, campaign-launch
> **Filled by agents**: strategist-agent, media-buyer-agent

## Purpose
Defines the overall paid acquisition strategy for a business, aligning ICP, offer, funnel, channels, and budget into a cohesive plan that all downstream tasks reference.

## Template

### Business Overview
**Business name**: [Company / brand name]
**Industry**: [Industry vertical]
**Business model**: [ecommerce / SaaS / lead-gen / info-product / agency]
**Revenue stage**: [pre-revenue / early / growth / scale]
**Current monthly revenue**: [amount or range]

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) / Avatar
**Demographics**: [age, gender, location, income]
**Psychographics**: [values, interests, pain points, desires]
**Buying triggers**: [events or situations that prompt purchase]
**Objections**: [top 3-5 reasons they hesitate]

### Offer
**Core offer**: [what the customer gets]
**Price point**: [price or range]
**Guarantee / risk reversal**: [details]
**Unique mechanism**: [what makes this different]

### Funnel Type
**Funnel model**: [direct-to-checkout / lead-magnet / webinar / VSL / application / hybrid]
**Key pages**: [list all funnel pages in order]
**Expected conversion rates**: [per step]

### Channels
**Primary channel(s)**: [Meta / Google / YouTube / TikTok / LinkedIn / other]
**Secondary channel(s)**: [list]
**Rationale**: [why these channels fit this audience and offer]

### Budget
**Total monthly budget**: [amount]
**Allocation per channel**: [breakdown]
**Testing reserve**: [percentage held for experiments]

### Timeline
**Start date**: [date]
**Phase 1 duration**: [testing phase length]
**Phase 2 duration**: [optimization phase length]
**Phase 3 duration**: [scaling phase length]

### KPI Targets
**North-star metric**: [MER / blended ROAS / CAC / CPL]
**Target CAC**: [amount]
**Target ROAS**: [ratio]
**Target CPL**: [amount]
**Target volume**: [leads or purchases per month]

### Measurement Plan
**Attribution model**: [last-click / data-driven / blended / MER]
**Reporting cadence**: [daily / weekly / monthly]
**Tools**: [GA4, platform pixels, CAPI, third-party]

### Constraints
**Regulatory**: [any compliance requirements]
**Brand**: [brand safety or tone restrictions]
**Technical**: [platform or tracking limitations]
**Competitive**: [known competitor pressures]

## Usage Notes
- Complete this brief before launching any new acquisition initiative.
- Review and update quarterly or when business fundamentals change.
- Share with all agents involved in the campaign lifecycle.

## Example
A DTC skincare brand targeting women 25-45, running Meta + Google Shopping with a $30k/month budget, using a direct-to-checkout funnel with a 3x blended ROAS target.
