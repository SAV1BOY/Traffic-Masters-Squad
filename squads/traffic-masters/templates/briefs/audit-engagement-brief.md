## Purpose
Captures all information needed to conduct a paid media audit, including account access, current performance, goals, and expected deliverables so the audit team can work efficiently.

## Usage Notes
- Send this brief to the client or stakeholder before beginning any audit work.
- Ensure all access is granted and verified before the audit start date.
- Use the audit-report-template.md for structuring findings.

## Example
Ecommerce brand spending $50k/month across Meta and Google. Full audit requested covering 90-day lookback. Deliverables include audit report, quick wins, and 30/60/90 plan. Two-week turnaround.
