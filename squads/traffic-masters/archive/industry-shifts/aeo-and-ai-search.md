# AEO and AI Search Impact on Ads

## Overview
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and AI-powered search (Google SGE/AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity) are changing how users find information and how ads are discovered.

## Key Developments

### AI Search Features
- **Google AI Overviews:** AI-generated answers above search results
- **ChatGPT + Search:** Conversational search with web results
- **Perplexity:** AI-powered answer engine with citations
- **Microsoft Copilot:** AI search integrated into Bing

### Impact on Paid Search
- Fewer clicks on traditional search results
- AI answers may satisfy queries without ad clicks
- Ad placement positions shifting with AI results
- Brand mentions in AI responses becoming important

### Impact on Display/Social Ads
- Less direct impact (discovery-based, not search-based)
- But user behavior shifting toward conversational queries
- Product research happening in AI tools before ad exposure

## AEO Strategy for Traffic
1. **Structured data:** Help AI understand your content for inclusion
2. **Brand presence:** Ensure brand appears in AI training data
3. **Content quality:** AI sources authoritative, well-structured content
4. **Paid search adaptation:** Monitor ad placement changes in AI results
5. **Diversification:** Don't depend solely on search traffic

## Opportunities
- AI-powered ad targeting improvements
- Conversational ad formats (emerging)
- AI-assisted creative at scale
- Better audience understanding through AI tools

## Threats
- Reduced organic search traffic affecting funnel top
- AI answers reducing click-through on informational queries
- Increased competition for remaining ad inventory
- Brand safety concerns in AI-generated contexts

## Key Takeaway
AI search is reducing dependency on traditional search while creating new opportunities. Diversified traffic strategies and strong brand presence across the web are increasingly important.
