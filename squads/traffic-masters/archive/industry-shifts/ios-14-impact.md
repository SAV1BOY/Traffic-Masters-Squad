# iOS 14 Impact on Paid Traffic

## The Event
Apple's iOS 14.5 update (April 2021) introduced App Tracking Transparency (ATT), requiring apps to ask permission before tracking user activity across other apps and websites.

## Impact on Advertising

### Immediate Effects
- ~80% of iOS users opted out of tracking
- Facebook/Meta advertising effectiveness dropped 20-40%
- Attribution accuracy degraded significantly
- Retargeting audience pools shrank dramatically
- CPMs increased as optimization signals weakened

### Platform-Specific Impact
- **Meta:** Hardest hit; lost ~$10B in ad revenue (2022)
- **Google:** Less impacted (first-party search intent data)
- **TikTok:** Growing during disruption, benefited from advertisers diversifying
- **Snapchat:** Significant revenue impact

### Advertiser Response
1. Adopted Conversion API (CAPI) for server-side tracking
2. Shifted to broader targeting (less reliance on precise retargeting)
3. Increased creative quality investment (creative as targeting)
4. Diversified across platforms
5. Adopted first-party data strategies
6. Used blended metrics (MER) instead of platform attribution

## Long-Term Industry Changes
- Privacy-first advertising became the norm
- Creative quality became the #1 performance lever
- First-party data became a strategic asset
- Attribution evolved from precision to triangulation
- AI-driven optimization replaced manual targeting

## Key Takeaway
iOS 14 permanently changed digital advertising. The winners adapted quickly to creative-first, privacy-compliant strategies with diversified platform presence.
