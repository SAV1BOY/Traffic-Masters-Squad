# Rise of UGC in Advertising

## Overview
User-Generated Content (UGC) has become the dominant creative format for direct response advertising, outperforming professional production in many contexts.

## Timeline
- **2015-2018:** Early UGC ads on Facebook (customer testimonials)
- **2019-2020:** TikTok normalized authentic, phone-shot content
- **2021-2022:** UGC became a formal content category; creator marketplaces emerged
- **2023-2024:** AI-generated UGC tools; professional "UGC creators" (not influencers)
- **2025-2026:** UGC is default format for DTC and ecommerce ads

## Why UGC Works
1. **Authenticity:** Feels like a recommendation, not an ad
2. **Platform-native:** Matches organic content in the feed
3. **Trust:** Real people > brand messaging for credibility
4. **Cost:** Cheaper to produce than professional production
5. **Speed:** Can produce 10-20 UGC videos in the time of 1 professional shoot
6. **Performance:** Typically 20-50% lower CPA vs professional creative

## UGC Creator Economy
- Distinction: UGC creators make content for brands to use in ads
- They are NOT influencers (don't post on their own channels)
- Typical cost: $100-500 per video
- Marketplaces: Insense, Billo, Trend.io, Collabstr

## UGC Best Practices
- Brief creators clearly but don't over-script
- Provide talking points, not word-for-word scripts
- Let creator personality come through
- Test multiple creators for same product
- Combine UGC with professional editing (hybrid)

## Key Takeaway
UGC has shifted from a trend to a foundational creative strategy. Every brand running paid social should have UGC in their creative mix.
