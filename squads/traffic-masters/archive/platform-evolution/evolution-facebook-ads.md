# Evolution of Facebook Ads

## Timeline

### 2007-2012: Early Days
- Self-serve ad platform launched
- Basic demographic and interest targeting
- Right-column ads only, then News Feed ads (2012)
- Facebook Exchange (FBX) for retargeting

### 2013-2016: Growth Era
- Custom Audiences and Lookalike Audiences introduced
- Video ads launched
- Conversion tracking pixel introduced
- Carousel ads, Lead Gen ads, Canvas (Instant Experience)
- Dynamic Product Ads for ecommerce

### 2017-2019: Optimization Era
- Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO)
- Automated rules and bid strategies
- Stories ads launched
- Value-based optimization
- Facebook and Instagram merged ad platform

### 2020-2021: Disruption
- iOS 14 privacy changes devastated tracking
- Aggregated Event Measurement introduced
- Attribution windows shortened
- CPMs increased significantly
- Conversion API (CAPI) became essential

### 2022-2024: AI & Automation
- Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns
- Advantage+ Audience (broad targeting)
- AI-driven creative optimization
- Reduced manual targeting controls
- Meta verified for business

### 2025-2026: Current State
- AI-first campaign creation
- Broad targeting as default
- Creative quality as primary optimization lever
- Multi-channel attribution challenges continue
- Increasing focus on Reels and short-form video

## Key Takeaway
The platform has steadily shifted from manual control to AI-driven automation. Creative quality has become the most important differentiator.
