# Evolution of Attribution

## Timeline

### Pre-2012: Simple Attribution
- Last-click attribution dominant
- Google Analytics as primary measurement tool
- Limited cross-channel visibility
- Cookie-based tracking reliable

### 2012-2017: Multi-Touch Era
- Multi-touch attribution models gained adoption
- Platform-specific attribution (Facebook 28-day click/1-day view)
- Cross-device tracking challenges emerged
- Attribution vendors proliferated (Visual IQ, Convertro)

### 2018-2020: Complexity Increases
- Data-driven attribution models (Google)
- Facebook Attribution tool launched
- Incrementality testing gained interest
- Media mix modeling resurgence for large advertisers

### 2021: Privacy Disruption
- iOS 14.5 ATT devastated tracking
- Facebook attribution window reduced to 7-day click/1-day view
- SKAdNetwork for iOS attribution
- Data gaps became significant

### 2022-2024: Adaptation
- Server-side tracking (CAPI, Enhanced Conversions) essential
- Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER) as blended metric
- Incrementality testing as source of truth
- Triple Whale, Northbeam, Rockerbox for independent attribution
- Geo-based lift studies for channel measurement

### 2025-2026: Current State
- No single source of truth — triangulation approach
- First-party data critical for accurate measurement
- AI-driven attribution models improving
- Privacy regulations continue expanding (GDPR, LGPD, state laws)
- Consent Mode and Privacy Sandbox evolving

## Current Best Practice
Use triangulation: platform data + independent attribution + incrementality testing + blended metrics (MER, nCAC). No single method is sufficient alone.

## Key Takeaway
Perfect attribution no longer exists. Smart advertisers use multiple measurement approaches and focus on directional accuracy rather than precision.
