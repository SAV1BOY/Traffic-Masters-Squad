# Evolution of Privacy & Tracking

## Timeline

### Pre-2018: Open Tracking Era
- Third-party cookies universal and reliable
- Cross-site tracking standard practice
- Minimal user consent requirements
- Platform pixels tracked everything accurately

### 2018: GDPR
- EU General Data Protection Regulation enacted
- Consent requirements for data collection
- Right to be forgotten, data portability
- Cookie consent banners became mandatory
- First significant impact on ad tracking

### 2020: CCPA
- California Consumer Privacy Act
- US privacy regulation begins
- Opt-out rights for data selling
- Inspired other state privacy laws

### 2021: iOS 14.5 (ATT)
- App Tracking Transparency required opt-in for tracking
- ~80% of iOS users opted out
- Facebook advertising effectiveness dropped significantly
- SKAdNetwork as limited iOS attribution alternative
- Forced adoption of server-side tracking

### 2022-2023: Adaptation
- Server-side tracking (CAPI, Enhanced Conversions) became standard
- First-party data strategies prioritized
- Consent Mode introduced by Google
- Contextual targeting resurgence
- Privacy Sandbox development (Chrome cookie deprecation)

### 2024-2026: Current Landscape
- Third-party cookie deprecation timeline evolving
- Privacy Sandbox APIs rolling out
- LGPD enforcement increasing in Brazil
- More US state privacy laws enacted
- AI-based modeling filling data gaps
- Consent rates and management crucial

## Impact on Media Buying
1. Attribution became less precise — triangulation required
2. Server-side tracking non-negotiable
3. First-party data is the new competitive advantage
4. Broad targeting + AI optimization replacing manual targeting
5. Creative quality became even more important (can't rely on targeting precision)

## Key Takeaway
Privacy changes have fundamentally shifted media buying from targeting-dependent to creative-dependent. First-party data and server-side tracking are essential infrastructure.
