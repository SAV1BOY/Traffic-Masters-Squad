# Evolution of YouTube Ads

## Timeline

### 2007-2012: Early Days
- YouTube In-Stream ads (pre-roll)
- TrueView launched (2010) — pay only when viewer watches
- Companion banners introduced
- Basic demographic targeting

### 2013-2016: Format Expansion
- Bumper ads (6 seconds, non-skippable)
- TrueView for Shopping
- Custom affinity and custom intent audiences
- YouTube Director (creative tools)

### 2017-2019: DR Performance
- TrueView for Action (conversion-focused)
- Video Action Campaigns
- Custom intent audiences (search-based targeting)
- Machine learning bid optimization

### 2020-2022: Shorts & Connected TV
- YouTube Shorts launched (competing with TikTok)
- Connected TV (CTV) advertising growth
- Video Action Campaigns expanded
- Shorts ads placement available

### 2023-2024: AI & Multi-Format
- Demand Gen campaigns (YouTube + Discover + Gmail)
- AI-powered creative tools
- Shorts ads becoming significant format
- Advanced audience matching
- Cross-device attribution improvements

### 2025-2026: Current State
- Shorts as primary growth format
- CTV representing significant watch time
- AI-generated video assets available
- Multi-format campaigns (in-stream + Shorts + CTV)
- YouTube as both awareness and DR platform

## Key Takeaway
YouTube has evolved from an awareness-only platform to a full-funnel performance channel. Shorts and CTV have expanded reach, while improved measurement makes DR viable.
