# Evolution of Google Ads

## Timeline

### 2000-2010: Foundation
- Google AdWords launched (2000)
- CPC auction model introduced
- Quality Score system developed
- Content Network (later Display Network)
- Google Analytics integration

### 2011-2016: Expansion
- Enhanced campaigns (device, location, time)
- Shopping campaigns launched
- YouTube ads integration
- Remarketing lists for search ads (RLSA)
- Customer Match introduced

### 2017-2019: Automation Wave
- Rebranded to Google Ads (2018)
- Smart Bidding matured (tCPA, tROAS)
- Responsive Search Ads (RSAs) introduced
- Discovery campaigns launched
- Data-driven attribution default

### 2020-2022: AI & PMax
- Performance Max campaigns launched
- Broad match + smart bidding recommended
- Expanded text ads sunset (2022)
- RSAs become only search ad format
- Privacy Sandbox development begins

### 2023-2024: AI-First
- AI-generated ad creative features
- PMax becomes primary campaign type
- Demand Gen campaigns (Discovery rebrand)
- Enhanced Conversions for better measurement
- Consent Mode for privacy compliance

### 2025-2026: Current State
- PMax + Search as primary campaign types
- AI handles most targeting and bidding decisions
- Creative assets and audience signals are primary inputs
- Cross-channel measurement increasingly important
- Search Generative Experience affecting ad placements

## Key Takeaway
Google has moved from keyword-centric to signal-centric advertising. Providing quality creative assets and conversion data is now more important than manual keyword management.
