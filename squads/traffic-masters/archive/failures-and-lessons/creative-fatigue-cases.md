# Creative Fatigue Cases — Lessons Learned

## Purpose
Document creative fatigue scenarios and how they were resolved.

## Case 1: Hero Ad Dependency
- **Situation:** Single video ad driving 80% of conversions for 6 months
- **What happened:** CTR dropped 50% in 2 weeks; CPA doubled
- **Cause:** Audience saw the ad 8+ times; complete message fatigue
- **Resolution:** Emergency creative sprint (10 new ads in 1 week)
- **Lesson:** Always have backup creatives ready BEFORE fatigue hits

## Case 2: Format Fatigue
- **Situation:** All ads were static images with similar layouts
- **What happened:** Gradual CTR decline over 8 weeks across all campaigns
- **Cause:** Format fatigue — audience became blind to the format itself
- **Resolution:** Introduced video and carousel formats
- **Lesson:** Diversify formats, not just messaging

## Case 3: Angle Fatigue
- **Situation:** All ads led with pain angle for 3 months
- **What happened:** CTR stable but conversion rate dropped
- **Cause:** Audience tired of problem-focused messaging
- **Resolution:** Switched to desire and proof angles
- **Lesson:** Rotate angles regularly, not just hooks and visuals

## Case 4: Retargeting Fatigue
- **Situation:** Same retargeting ads running for 60 days
- **What happened:** Frequency hit 12.0; negative feedback increased
- **Cause:** Small audience, no creative rotation
- **Resolution:** 3 creative rotations on 2-week cycles + frequency cap
- **Lesson:** Retargeting audiences fatigue 3x faster than prospecting

## Prevention Framework
1. Monitor fatigue indicators daily (CTR, frequency, CPA trend)
2. Maintain 3:1 ratio of ready creative to active creative
3. Set alerts at frequency 2.5 (warning) and 4.0 (action)
4. Proactively launch new creative every 2 weeks
5. Diversify across format, angle, and hook type simultaneously
