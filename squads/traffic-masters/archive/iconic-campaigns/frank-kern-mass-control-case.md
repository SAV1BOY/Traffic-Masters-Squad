# Frank Kern — Mass Control Case Study

## Overview
- **Marketer:** Frank Kern
- **Campaign:** Mass Control product launch (2008)
- **Result:** $23.8M in 24 hours
- **Significance:** Pioneered the "launch formula" approach for digital products

## Campaign Breakdown

### Pre-Launch (Seed Launch)
- Free video content series building anticipation
- Each video delivered value while creating desire for the full system
- Email list building through content opt-in
- Community building in anticipation of cart open

### Launch Sequence
- 3-4 free videos over 7-10 days (pre-launch content)
- Each video addressed objections and built social proof
- Cart open with limited-time bonuses
- Scarcity through genuine closing deadline

### Key Innovation
- "Results in advance" — give value before asking for sale
- Social proof accumulation during pre-launch
- Urgency through real deadlines (not fake scarcity)
- List segmentation based on engagement level

## Lessons for Modern Media Buyers
1. Pre-launch content campaigns build audiences before spending on conversion
2. "Results in advance" earns the right to sell
3. Launch sequences create event-based urgency
4. Email list + paid traffic amplification = compounding reach
5. This model became the basis for Brazilian "lancamento" strategies

## Legacy
- Inspired Jeff Walker's Product Launch Formula
- Basis for Erico Rocha's Formula de Lancamento (Brazil)
- Core principles still used in challenge funnels and webinar funnels
- Evolved into modern launch sequences with paid traffic amplification
