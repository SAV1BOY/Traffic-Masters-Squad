# Harmon Brothers — Cases Collection

## Overview
- **Agency:** Harmon Brothers (Provo, Utah)
- **Specialty:** Long-form DR video ads
- **Combined Client Results:** $1B+ in attributable revenue

## Key Campaigns

### Squatty Potty (2015)
- Unicorn pooping rainbow ice cream — humor + education
- 170M+ views, $33M first-year sales
- Lesson: Humor makes taboo topics accessible

### Chatbooks (2016)
- Mom-humor driven, highly relatable
- Proved subscription DTC works with video ads
- Lesson: Relatability > aspiration for some audiences

### FiberFix (2014)
- Physical demonstration of product strength
- Destruction testing as entertainment
- Lesson: Demonstration is the strongest proof

### Lume Deodorant (2018+)
- Founder-led, addressing taboo personal care topic
- Science-backed claims delivered with confidence
- Lesson: Brave creative direction in sensitive categories wins

### Purple Mattress (2016)
- Raw Egg Test demonstration
- Named mechanism (Hyper-Elastic Polymer)
- Lesson: Named mechanisms create differentiation

## The Harmon Brothers Formula
1. Pattern interrupt hook (0-5s)
2. Problem establishment with humor (5-30s)
3. Product introduction with demonstration (30-60s)
4. Mechanism explanation (60-120s)
5. Social proof (120-150s)
6. CTA with offer (final 30s)

## Key Takeaways
1. Invest heavily in one great ad vs many mediocre ones
2. Humor must serve the selling message
3. Physical demonstration is the most persuasive element
4. Characters and stories create emotional investment
5. Long-form works when entertainment value is high
