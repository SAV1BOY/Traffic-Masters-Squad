# Old Spice: "The Man Your Man Could Smell Like"

## Campaign Overview

**Brand:** Old Spice (Procter & Gamble)
**Launch Date:** February 8, 2010 (Super Bowl adjacent)
**Agency:** Wieden+Kennedy Portland
**Talent:** Isaiah Mustafa
**Primary Channels:** Television, YouTube, social media (Twitter, Facebook)
**Result:** #1 all-time most viewed branded channel on YouTube; 107% increase in body wash sales

---

## Strategic Context

### Market Challenge
Old Spice was perceived as "your grandfather's brand." The body wash category was growing rapidly, and competitors like Axe/Lynx owned the younger male demographic with provocative advertising. Old Spice needed to modernize without alienating its existing base.

### Key Consumer Insight
Women purchase 70% of men's body wash. The target audience was not young men but the women who shopped for them. This single insight reframed the entire creative strategy.

### Strategic Objectives
1. Reposition Old Spice as modern and culturally relevant
2. Drive body wash sales (shifting from deodorant-only perception)
3. Own social media conversation in the personal care category
4. Speak to female purchasers while making the brand aspirational for men

## Creative Breakdown

### The Original Spot: "The Man Your Man Could Smell Like"

**Format:** 30-second television commercial (extended 60-second online version)
**Concept:** Isaiah Mustafa addresses women directly, transitioning through increasingly absurd scenarios in what appears to be a single continuous shot.

**Script Flow:**
1. Opens in a bathroom: "Hello, ladies. Look at your man. Now back to me."
2. Transitions to a boat: "Now look at me. I'm on a horse."
3. Diamonds pour from his hand, tickets appear, the background shifts
4. Each transition escalates the absurdity while maintaining a deadpan delivery
5. Closing: "I'm on a horse." (callback to setup)

### Creative Mechanics

**1. Direct Address**
Speaking directly to women broke the fourth wall and created intimacy. The audience felt spoken to, not marketed at. This technique is now standard in UGC and creator-led advertising.

**2. Continuous Shot Illusion**
The seamless transitions between locations created a sense of magic and craftsmanship that rewarded repeated viewing. The "how did they do that?" factor drove shares and discussions.

**3. Absurdist Escalation**
Each scene was more ridiculous than the last, maintaining attention through novelty. The escalation pattern kept viewers watching to see what came next, a principle that directly applies to video ad retention curves.

**4. Deadpan Confidence**
Mustafa's delivery was the inverse of Axe's approach. Instead of desperate pursuit of women, Old Spice projected effortless confidence. The humor came from the gap between the absurd scenarios and the completely serious delivery.

**5. Female Gaze**
The ad was designed for the female gaze, a deliberate inversion of most men's grooming advertising. This alignment between creative strategy and purchasing behavior was the campaign's foundation.

## Phase 2: Social Media Response Campaign

### The Concept
In July 2010, Wieden+Kennedy launched a real-time social media campaign where Isaiah Mustafa recorded personalized video responses to fans, celebrities, and brands on Twitter and YouTube.

### Execution
- 186 personalized video responses recorded over 3 days
- Responded to real tweets and comments in near real-time
- Targets included Alyssa Milano, Demi Moore, Kevin Rose, Perez Hilton, and everyday fans
- Each response maintained the character and tone of the original ad

### Why It Worked
- **Speed:** Near real-time responses created a sense of live interaction
- **Personalization:** Each video was unique, making recipients feel special
- **Celebrity engagement:** Celebrities sharing their personalized responses amplified reach exponentially
- **Platform native:** Short-form video responses were native to YouTube and embeddable on Twitter
- **Earned media:** Every response was a piece of content that could be shared independently

### Social Media Results
- 40 million YouTube views in the first week of the response campaign
- Twitter followers increased 2,700%
- Facebook interaction increased 800%
- The campaign became the #1 all-time most viewed branded YouTube channel

## Paid Traffic Lessons

### 1. Know the Actual Buyer
The campaign's success was rooted in the insight that women buy men's body wash. Modern paid traffic managers must distinguish between the user and the buyer. Targeting and creative should address the decision-maker, who may not be the end user.

**Application:** For products purchased as gifts, by parents for children, or by procurement teams for companies, ensure your targeting and messaging addresses the actual purchaser.

### 2. Social Media Is a Conversation, Not a Billboard
The response campaign demonstrated that social platforms reward interaction. Brands that talk at audiences underperform brands that talk with audiences.

**Application:** Design ad creative that invites response. Use comment sections actively. Consider response videos to high-performing UGC. Monitor and engage with comments on paid posts.

### 3. Earned Media Multiplies Paid Spend
Every dollar of paid media was amplified by organic sharing and press coverage. The creative was so strong that it generated its own distribution.

**Application:** When evaluating creative concepts, estimate the earned media potential. Creative that generates conversation, sharing, and press coverage effectively reduces your CPM by expanding reach beyond paid distribution.

### 4. Character Consistency Enables Serialization
Isaiah Mustafa's character was consistent enough to span hundreds of videos. This consistency built familiarity and anticipation.

**Application:** Develop consistent creative characters or formats that can be serialized across dozens of ads. This builds recognition, reduces creative production costs, and creates a library of testable variations.

### 5. Real-Time Marketing Requires Preparation
The response campaign appeared spontaneous but required a production team, a set, writers, and a talent standing by for three days. "Real-time" marketing is planned spontaneity.

**Application:** Prepare response templates, creative frameworks, and production capacity for reactive moments (trends, comments, cultural events). Speed without preparation produces low-quality content.

### 6. Humor Reduces Defensive Processing
Humorous ads bypass the mental defenses consumers have against advertising. People share funny content because it provides social value to their networks.

**Application:** Test humorous creative against serious creative. In many categories, humor outperforms on both engagement and conversion metrics because it lowers the perceived cost of attention.

## Replication Framework

### For Paid Traffic Campaigns

1. **Identify the true buyer** and design creative that speaks to them directly
2. **Create a distinctive character** or spokesperson who can carry a series
3. **Design for shareability** by embedding humor, surprise, or spectacle
4. **Plan for two phases:** broadcast (paid reach) followed by engagement (personalized interaction)
5. **Prepare production capacity** for rapid response to engagement
6. **Measure beyond immediate sales:** track branded search, social following growth, and earned media value
