# Dollar Shave Club — Case Study

## Overview
- **Brand:** Dollar Shave Club
- **Launch Year:** 2012
- **Key Campaign:** "Our Blades Are F***ing Great" (YouTube)
- **Result:** 12,000 orders in 48 hours; $1B acquisition by Unilever (2016)

## Campaign Breakdown

### Creative Strategy
- Founder Michael Dubin as spokesperson
- Low-budget, single continuous shot through warehouse
- Humor as primary engagement driver
- Direct attack on overpriced competitors

### Media Strategy
- YouTube as primary distribution channel
- Organic sharing amplified paid reach (viral loop)
- Retargeting website visitors with subscription offers
- Social media amplification of the video

### Key Metrics
- 27M+ views (original video)
- 12,000 orders in first 48 hours
- Customer acquisition cost: estimated $5-8 in early days

## Lessons for Modern Media Buyers
1. Authenticity and personality can outperform production value
2. A single hero creative can build a billion-dollar brand
3. Subscription model + viral creative = compounding growth
4. Humor makes ads shareable, extending reach beyond paid
5. Founder-led creative builds trust rapidly

## What Would Be Different Today
- Multi-platform launch (TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts)
- UGC variations for Meta ads
- Influencer seeding before launch
- More sophisticated attribution tracking
