# Purple Mattress — Case Study

## Overview
- **Brand:** Purple Innovation
- **Key Campaign:** "Raw Egg Test" (2016, Harmon Brothers)
- **Result:** $75M+ in attributable revenue from the campaign

## Campaign Breakdown

### Creative Strategy
- Demonstration-based proof (raw eggs dropped on mattress)
- Goldilocks character as relatable narrative device
- Named mechanism: "Hyper-Elastic Polymer"
- Educational content wrapped in entertainment

### Media Strategy
- YouTube as primary platform (long-form 2-4 min)
- Facebook video ads (cut-down versions)
- Display retargeting for site visitors
- Influencer seeding and reviews

### Key Metrics
- 180M+ views across platforms
- $75M+ in sales attributed to campaign
- CPV significantly below industry average due to high VTR

## Lessons for Modern Media Buyers
1. Product demonstration is the most persuasive ad format
2. Named mechanisms create differentiation and word-of-mouth
3. Investment in one exceptional hero ad can outperform dozens of average ones
4. Long-form content works when entertainment value is high
5. Physical proof (demonstration) > verbal claims

## What Would Be Different Today
- Shorter cuts optimized for Reels and TikTok
- UGC reaction videos to the demonstration
- Creator partnerships for unboxing and testing content
- More sophisticated multi-touch attribution
