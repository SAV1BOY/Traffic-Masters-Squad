# BOOM! by Cindy Joseph — Case Study

## Overview
- **Brand:** BOOM! by Cindy Joseph
- **Founder/Marketer:** Ezra Firestone (Smart Marketer)
- **Niche:** Cosmetics for women 50+
- **Result:** $30M+/year revenue, built primarily through Facebook Ads

## Campaign Breakdown

### Audience Strategy
- Underserved market: women 50+ ignored by beauty industry
- "Pro-age" positioning (not anti-aging)
- Content-first approach to build trust

### Creative Strategy
- Real women, not models
- Authentic before/after with natural lighting
- Educational content about skincare for mature skin
- Cindy Joseph as authentic brand ambassador

### Funnel Strategy
- Ad > Blog post/advertorial > Product page > Purchase
- Content marketing integrated with paid traffic
- Email nurture sequences for non-converters
- Retargeting with customer testimonials

### Key Metrics
- $30M+/year at peak
- Scaled primarily on Facebook/Instagram
- Advertorial funnel drove 2-5x better conversion vs direct

## Lessons for Modern Media Buyers
1. Underserved markets = lower competition = lower CPMs
2. Content-first funnels build trust before the ask
3. Authentic representation resonates deeply with target audience
4. Advertorial pre-sell pages dramatically improve conversion
5. Consistency in brand voice compounds over time
