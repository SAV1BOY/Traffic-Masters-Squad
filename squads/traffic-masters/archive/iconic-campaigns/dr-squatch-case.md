# Dr. Squatch — Case Study

## Overview
- **Brand:** Dr. Squatch (natural men's soap)
- **Launch:** 2018 (major ad push)
- **Result:** Scaled to $100M+ revenue; Super Bowl ad 2021

## Campaign Breakdown

### Creative Strategy
- Strong spokesperson character with distinct personality
- Identity-based messaging: "You're not a dish. You're a man."
- Humor throughout, attacking "chemical soap" competitors
- Consistent character across all ads (brand universe)

### Media Strategy
- YouTube as primary platform (long-form hero ads)
- Facebook/Instagram for retargeting and shorter cuts
- Cut one hero ad into 15s, 30s, 60s versions
- UGC variations from customers and creators

### Scaling Strategy
- One hero creative validated, then iterated
- Hero ad cut into platform-specific formats
- Seasonal and limited edition angles for re-engagement
- Subscription model for recurring revenue

### Key Metrics
- Scaled from startup to $100M+ in ~3 years
- Hero ad ("You're Not a Dish") ran for years with iterations
- Super Bowl 2021 ad marked mainstream brand status

## Lessons for Modern Media Buyers
1. Identity-based messaging creates deeper emotional connection
2. A strong spokesperson character becomes a brand moat
3. One validated hero concept can be repurposed infinitely
4. Humor makes ads shareable, reducing effective CPA
5. Consistent creative universe builds brand equity over time
