# Creative Performance Metrics

## Purpose
Track ad creative performance to identify winners, detect fatigue, and guide iteration.

## Performance Tiers

| Tier | Criteria | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| Winner | Top 20% by CPA, 500+ conversions | Scale spend, create iterations |
| Performer | Top 50% by CPA, 100+ conversions | Maintain, test variations |
| Average | Middle 30% by CPA | Monitor, prepare replacements |
| Underperformer | Bottom 20% by CPA, 100+ spend | Pause, analyze why |

## Fatigue Detection

| Signal | Threshold | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| CTR decline | -20% from peak | Prepare new creative |
| CPA increase | +30% from best | Reduce spend, introduce new |
| Frequency | >3.0 / 7 days | Cap frequency, rotate |
| Relevance score drop | Below 5/10 | Refresh or pause |
