# Cohort Exports

## Purpose
Track and analyze customer cohorts from paid traffic for LTV and retention insights.

## Cohort Definitions

### By Acquisition Source
- Meta cold traffic cohort
- Google Search brand cohort
- Google Search non-brand cohort
- TikTok acquisition cohort
- YouTube acquisition cohort

### By Time Period
- Monthly acquisition cohorts (Jan, Feb, Mar, etc.)
- Campaign-specific cohorts
- Seasonal cohorts (Q4 holiday, back-to-school, etc.)

### By Offer/Funnel
- Lead magnet cohort
- Webinar cohort
- Direct purchase cohort
- Free trial cohort

## Key Questions Per Cohort
1. What is the payback period?
2. Which source produces highest LTV?
3. How does retention differ by acquisition channel?
4. At what point does the cohort become profitable?

## Export Schedule
- Monthly: Pull cohort data from CRM/analytics
- Quarterly: Full LTV analysis by source
- Ad-hoc: Post-campaign cohort analysis

## Data Sources
- CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.)
- GA4 cohort reports
- Platform conversion data
- Revenue/billing systems
