# Learnings Log — Metrics

## Purpose
Track data-driven learnings from campaign performance analysis.

## Entry Template

### [Date] — [Learning Title]
- **Source:** Campaign/test/observation that produced this learning
- **Data:** Key metrics supporting the finding
- **Insight:** What the data tells us
- **Action:** What we changed or should change
- **Impact:** Result of implementing the action (if applicable)

## Learnings Archive

### Categories
- **Creative:** Learnings about ad creative performance
- **Targeting:** Learnings about audience and targeting
- **Bidding:** Learnings about bid strategies and optimization
- **Budget:** Learnings about budget allocation and pacing
- **Funnel:** Learnings about funnel performance and conversion
- **Platform:** Learnings about platform-specific behavior
- **Seasonal:** Learnings about timing and seasonal patterns
