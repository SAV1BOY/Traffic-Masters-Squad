# Avatar & ICP Research — Index

## Purpose
Store and organize ideal customer profile (ICP) and buyer avatar research.

## Contents

### Avatar Documents
- Customer avatar worksheets
- Demographic profiles
- Psychographic profiles
- Behavioral data summaries

### ICP Definitions
- B2B ICP criteria (company size, industry, revenue, tech stack)
- B2C avatar details (age, interests, pain points, media habits)
- Negative personas (who to exclude)

## File Naming Convention
`[client-or-project]_[avatar-name]_[date].md`

Example: `acme-saas_mid-market-cto_2026-03.md`

## Research Sources
- Customer interviews and surveys
- CRM data analysis
- Platform audience insights (Meta, Google, LinkedIn)
- Analytics demographic reports
- Sales team input

## Template Fields
- Name/label, demographics, psychographics
- Pain points (top 3), desires (top 3)
- Objections (top 3), current solutions
- Media consumption habits
- Buying triggers and decision factors
