# Hook & Angle Research — Index

## Purpose
Store research and testing data on ad hooks and messaging angles.

## Contents

### Hook Research
- Hook testing results by platform
- Hook formulas and templates
- Video hook analysis (first 3-5 seconds)
- Text hook analysis (headlines, primary text openers)

### Angle Research
- Angle testing results
- Pain vs desire vs proof angle comparisons
- Platform-specific angle performance
- Seasonal and contextual angle variations

## File Naming Convention
`[client]_[hook-or-angle]_[platform]_[date].md`

## Research Template
- Hook/angle text or description
- Platform tested on
- Audience targeted
- Creative format
- Performance metrics (CTR, hook rate, CPA)
- Control comparison
- Statistical significance
- Winner/loser determination
- Learnings for next iteration

## Analysis Dimensions
- By platform (Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn)
- By funnel stage (cold, warm, hot)
- By audience segment
- By creative format (video, static, carousel)
- By time period (seasonal trends)
