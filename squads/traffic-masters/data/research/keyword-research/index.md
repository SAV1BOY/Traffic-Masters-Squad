# Keyword Research — Index

## Purpose
Store keyword research for Google Ads, YouTube, and SEO-informed paid strategies.

## Contents

### By Campaign Type
- Search campaign keyword lists
- Shopping campaign search term analysis
- YouTube keyword targeting lists
- Negative keyword master lists

### By Research Type
- Seed keyword expansion
- Competitor keyword gaps
- Long-tail discovery
- Search query mining reports

## File Naming Convention
`[client]_[campaign-type]_keywords_[date].md`

## Research Template
- Keyword / phrase
- Match type (broad, phrase, exact)
- Monthly search volume
- CPC estimate
- Competition level
- Intent classification (informational, commercial, transactional)
- Priority (high, medium, low)
- Associated ad group

## Tools Used
- Google Keyword Planner
- SEMrush / Ahrefs keyword tools
- Google Search Console (for organic insights)
- Search term reports (from active campaigns)
- SpyFu competitor keywords

## Maintenance Schedule
- Monthly: Review search term reports, add negatives
- Quarterly: Full keyword research refresh
- Ad-hoc: New campaign launches, market changes
