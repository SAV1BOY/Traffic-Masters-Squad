# Competitor Ads Research — Index

## Purpose
Organize competitive ad intelligence gathered from spy tools and ad libraries.

## Contents

### By Platform
- `meta/` — Facebook and Instagram competitor ads
- `google/` — Search, Display, Shopping competitor ads
- `youtube/` — Video ad competitor analysis
- `tiktok/` — TikTok competitor creative
- `linkedin/` — LinkedIn competitor ads

### By Competitor
- Individual competitor folders with all their ads across platforms

## File Naming Convention
`[competitor]_[platform]_[date].md`

Example: `competitor-x_meta_2026-03.md`

## Analysis Template
- Competitor name and URL
- Platforms active on
- Ad formats in use
- Key messaging themes
- Offer and CTA patterns
- Estimated creative volume
- Longest-running ads (likely winners)
- Gaps and opportunities for us

## Collection Schedule
- Monthly: Full competitive sweep
- Weekly: Quick check on top 3 competitors
- Ad-hoc: When launching new campaigns or entering new markets
