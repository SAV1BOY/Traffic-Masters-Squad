# Project Template: Seasonal/Promotional Campaign

## Project Overview

Planning and executing campaigns around seasonal events, holidays, sales periods, or promotional windows (Black Friday/Cyber Monday, holiday season, back-to-school, Prime Day, product launches, flash sales). Seasonal campaigns require advance planning, creative preparation, budget allocation, and post-season analysis.

---

## Objectives

1. Maximize revenue/leads during the defined promotional window
2. Achieve target ROAS/CPA during the promotional period
3. Build pre-season audience pools for retargeting during the event
4. Capture seasonal demand without cannibalizing non-promotional performance
5. Document learnings for future seasonal planning

---

## Timeline

**Total Duration:** 6-8 weeks (4 weeks pre-event, event period, 1-2 weeks post-event)

| Phase | Duration | Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1: Planning | Weeks 1-2 (T-4 weeks) | Seasonal plan approved |
| Phase 2: Pre-Season Warming | Weeks 2-4 (T-2 weeks) | Audiences warmed, creative tested |
| Phase 3: Event Execution | Event period (variable) | Campaigns live and optimized |
| Phase 4: Post-Event Analysis | 1-2 weeks after event | Analysis completed, learnings documented |

## Phases and Deliverables

### Phase 1: Planning (T-4 Weeks)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Seasonal strategy document:
  - Event dates and promotional window defined
  - Offer/promotion details confirmed (discounts, bundles, free shipping, etc.)
  - Budget allocation: pre-season warming, event period, post-event
  - Revenue/lead targets for the promotional period
  - CPA/ROAS targets (may differ from non-promotional periods)
  - Channel allocation plan
  - Audience strategy: who to target before, during, and after
- [ ] Historical analysis (if prior seasonal data exists):
  - Previous year's performance by channel, creative, and audience
  - CPM trends during the seasonal period (expect 20-50% CPM increases)
  - Conversion rate changes during promotional periods
  - What worked and what failed in previous seasons
- [ ] Creative brief for seasonal assets:
  - Promotional messaging (sale amount, urgency, exclusivity)
  - Format requirements (static, video, stories, search ads)
  - Pre-season creative (warming/anticipation)
  - Event-period creative (promotional, urgency-driven)
  - Post-event creative (extension/last-chance)
- [ ] Landing page requirements defined:
  - Promotional landing page specs
  - Mobile optimization verified
  - Load speed tested (critical during high-traffic periods)
  - Tracking verified on promotional pages
- [ ] Operational readiness confirmed:
  - Inventory levels sufficient for projected demand
  - Fulfillment capacity for order volume spike
  - Customer service staffing for increased inquiries
  - Website performance under load tested

**Quality Gate:** Strategy approved. Budget confirmed. Offers finalized. Creative brief completed. Operational readiness confirmed.

### Phase 2: Pre-Season Warming (T-2 Weeks)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Audience warming campaigns launched:
  - Increase top-of-funnel spend 2-3 weeks before the event
  - Build video viewer and engagement audiences for event retargeting
  - Drive email/SMS sign-ups for event notifications
  - Warm website traffic audiences for retargeting during the event
- [ ] Creative testing completed:
  - Test promotional creative before the event period (when CPMs are lower)
  - Identify winning formats, hooks, and messaging
  - Prepare multiple creative variants for event period
- [ ] Retargeting audiences built:
  - Website visitors (30, 14, 7 day windows)
  - Video viewers (25%, 50%, 75%, 95% completion)
  - Engagement audiences (ad interactions, social engagement)
  - Email/SMS subscriber audiences uploaded
  - Cart abandoner audiences configured
- [ ] Event-period campaigns built (not yet launched):
  - Campaign structure finalized
  - Creative assigned to campaigns
  - Budgets configured
  - Bid strategies set (may use higher targets during promotional period)
  - Scheduled activation dates set (if platform supports)
- [ ] Promotional landing pages live and tracked
- [ ] Email/SMS alignment confirmed (coordinated messaging)

**Quality Gate:** Warming campaigns running 2+ weeks before event. Retargeting audience pools at target size. Event-period campaigns built and ready to activate. All promotional pages tracked.

### Phase 3: Event Execution (Event Period)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Campaigns activated at event start
- [ ] Real-time monitoring plan executed:
  - Check performance every 2-4 hours during peak days
  - Monitor spend pacing (ensure budget is not exhausted early)
  - Watch for ad disapprovals or delivery issues
  - Track conversion rates and CPA in real-time
  - Monitor website performance (load times, error rates)
- [ ] Dynamic budget allocation:
  - Shift budget to highest-performing campaigns in real-time
  - Scale winning creative aggressively during the event window
  - Reduce spend on underperforming elements immediately
  - Maintain retargeting budgets (cart abandoner recovery critical during sales)
- [ ] Creative rotation:
  - Rotate creative messaging through the event (anticipation, urgency, last-chance)
  - Update countdown messaging as event deadline approaches
  - Launch "extended" messaging if the promotion is extended
- [ ] Daily performance reports during event:
  - Revenue/leads vs. targets
  - Spend vs. budget
  - CPA/ROAS vs. targets
  - Top-performing creative, audiences, and channels
- [ ] Post-event wind-down:
  - Reduce budgets gradually after event ends
  - Pause promotional creative
  - Transition retargeting to non-promotional messaging
  - Launch "thank you" or post-purchase nurture campaigns

**Quality Gate:** Event revenue/leads within 80% of target. Budget fully utilized (95%+). No critical delivery issues during event period. Real-time adjustments executed based on performance data.

### Phase 4: Post-Event Analysis (1-2 Weeks After)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Comprehensive event performance report:
  - Revenue/leads vs. targets (overall and by channel)
  - Spend vs. budget (by channel and campaign)
  - CPA/ROAS vs. targets
  - New vs. returning customer breakdown
  - Average order value during event vs. non-event periods
  - Conversion rate during event vs. non-event periods
- [ ] Channel performance analysis:
  - Performance by platform (Meta, Google, TikTok, etc.)
  - Performance by campaign type (prospecting, retargeting, search)
  - Incrementality assessment (did seasonal spend drive incremental revenue?)
- [ ] Creative analysis:
  - Top-performing creative assets during the event
  - Creative themes that resonated (urgency, value, exclusivity)
  - Format effectiveness (video vs. static vs. UGC)
- [ ] Audience analysis:
  - Pre-warmed audiences vs. cold audiences performance
  - Retargeting contribution to event revenue
  - New customer acquisition vs. existing customer reactivation
- [ ] CPM and competitive analysis:
  - CPM trends during the event (how much did costs increase?)
  - Competitive intensity observations
  - Optimal timing and pacing observations
- [ ] Lessons learned and recommendations:
  - What worked (continue next time)
  - What did not work (avoid or modify)
  - What to try next time (new ideas)
  - Recommended changes for next seasonal event
  - Budget recommendations for next event
- [ ] Updated seasonal playbook for future reference

**Quality Gate:** Complete analysis delivered within 2 weeks of event conclusion. Learnings documented for future reference. Recommendations actionable and specific.

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPMs spike higher than planned | High | Medium | Budget buffer of 20-30%; adjust targets for CPM inflation |
| Website crashes during peak traffic | Medium | Critical | Load test pre-event; have CDN and caching ready |
| Inventory stock-out during event | Medium | High | Confirm inventory before event; have backup offers ready |
| Ad disapprovals during event | Medium | High | Pre-submit creative for review; have backup creative ready |
| Competitor launches competing promotion | High | Medium | Monitor competitive landscape; differentiate on value |
| Attribution confusion during high-volume period | High | Medium | Rely on blended metrics during event; detailed attribution post-event |
