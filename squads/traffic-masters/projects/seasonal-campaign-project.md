# Seasonal Campaign Project

## Objective
Plan and execute campaigns around seasonal events and peak demand periods.

## Timeline: 6-8 Weeks (start 6 weeks before event)

### Week 1-2: Planning (T-6 weeks)
- [ ] Identify seasonal event and opportunity
- [ ] Review past performance data (if available)
- [ ] Set revenue and ROAS targets
- [ ] Plan budget (expect higher CPMs during peak)
- [ ] Brief creative team on seasonal themes

### Week 3-4: Build (T-4 weeks)
- [ ] Create seasonal creative (multiple variations)
- [ ] Build seasonal landing pages/offers
- [ ] Pre-build audiences for warm-up
- [ ] Set up tracking for seasonal campaigns
- [ ] Plan email/SMS coordination

### Week 5: Warm-Up (T-2 weeks)
- [ ] Launch awareness campaigns to build audiences
- [ ] Tease upcoming offers in content
- [ ] Build retargeting pools from engagement
- [ ] Final QA on all tracking and pages

### Week 6: Peak (Event Week)
- [ ] Activate full seasonal campaigns
- [ ] Maximum budget deployment
- [ ] Daily optimization (potentially twice daily)
- [ ] Real-time creative rotation
- [ ] Coordination with email/SMS/organic

### Week 7-8: Wrap-Up
- [ ] Extend offers for late buyers
- [ ] Post-season retargeting
- [ ] Full performance analysis
- [ ] Document learnings for next year
- [ ] Archive seasonal creative

## Key Seasonal Events

| Event | Region | Peak Period | Prep Start |
|-------|--------|------------|------------|
| Black Friday/Cyber Monday | Global | Late Nov | Early Oct |
| Q4 Holiday Season | US/EU | Nov-Dec | September |
| Mother's Day | US | May | March |
| Dia das Maes | Brazil | May | March |
| Carnival | Brazil | Feb/Mar | January |
| Back to School | US | Aug/Sep | July |

## Budget Considerations
- CPMs typically 20-50% higher during peak seasons
- Increase budgets 4-6 weeks before to warm audiences
- Reserve 30% of seasonal budget for peak days
- Plan for higher than normal daily budgets
