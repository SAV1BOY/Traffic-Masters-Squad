# Project Template: Client Onboarding

## Project Overview

End-to-end onboarding of a new client, from initial discovery through account audit, strategy development, tracking setup, campaign launch, and establishment of ongoing management cadence. This project ensures a structured, professional onboarding experience that sets the foundation for a successful long-term engagement.

---

## Objectives

1. Understand the client's business, goals, and competitive landscape
2. Audit existing advertising accounts and tracking infrastructure
3. Develop a comprehensive paid traffic strategy aligned with business objectives
4. Set up or optimize tracking across all platforms
5. Launch initial campaigns within the defined timeline
6. Establish ongoing communication and reporting cadence

---

## Timeline

**Total Duration:** 4-6 weeks (discovery to first campaigns live)

| Phase | Duration | Milestone |
|---|---|---|

## Phases and Deliverables

### Phase 1: Discovery and Access (Days 1-5)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Kickoff meeting conducted:
  - Introductions and team alignment
  - Communication preferences established (Slack, email, calls)
  - Meeting cadence agreed upon
  - Project timeline reviewed
- [ ] Client discovery questionnaire completed:
  - Business overview: products/services, revenue, margins, growth stage
  - Target audience: demographics, psychographics, buying behavior
  - Customer journey: average purchase cycle, decision factors, objections
  - Competitive landscape: top 3-5 competitors, differentiators
  - Historical advertising: what has been tried, what worked, what failed
  - Current goals: revenue targets, lead targets, CAC/ROAS targets
  - Brand guidelines: voice, tone, visual identity, restrictions
  - Compliance requirements: industry regulations, legal restrictions
  - Seasonal patterns: peak and off-peak periods
  - Key stakeholders: decision-makers, approval processes
- [ ] Platform access secured:
  - Ad platform access (Meta Business Manager, Google Ads, TikTok, etc.)
  - Analytics access (GA4, third-party attribution tools)
  - E-commerce/CRM access (Shopify, Salesforce, HubSpot, etc.)
  - Tag Manager access (GTM)
  - Website/landing page access
  - Creative asset access (brand folder, design files)
- [ ] Key business documents received:
  - Customer list (email/phone for Custom Audiences)
  - Product catalog/feed (for e-commerce)
  - Brand guidelines document
  - Existing creative assets
  - Previous reports and performance data
- [ ] Internal team briefing completed

**Quality Gate:** All discovery information collected. All platform access secured. Team aligned on client context and goals.

### Phase 2: Audit and Analysis (Days 5-12)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Account audit completed (reference: Audit Project template):
  - Tracking audit: pixel, CAPI, conversion events
  - Account structure audit: campaigns, ad sets, naming
  - Performance audit: CPA, ROAS, trends, by channel and campaign
  - Creative audit: current assets, performance, fatigue status
  - Audience audit: targeting, audiences, overlap, exclusions
- [ ] Competitive analysis completed:
  - Meta Ad Library review of top competitors
  - Google Ads Auction Insights (if data available)
  - Competitor creative themes and messaging
  - Competitor offer analysis
- [ ] Market research:
  - Platform CPM benchmarks for the vertical
  - Industry CPA/ROAS benchmarks
  - Audience size estimates on target platforms
- [ ] Audit findings presentation:
  - Executive summary of account health
  - Key issues identified (critical and important)
  - Key opportunities identified
  - Quick wins (implementable immediately)
  - Strategic recommendations preview
- [ ] Audit findings reviewed with client

**Quality Gate:** Comprehensive audit delivered. Client aligned on current state. Quick wins identified for immediate implementation.

### Phase 3: Strategy Development (Days 10-17)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Paid traffic strategy document:
  - **Goals and KPIs:**
    - Primary KPI (CPA, ROAS, CPL) with specific targets
    - Secondary KPIs (conversion volume, new customer rate, LTV)
    - Budget allocation across channels and objectives
  - **Channel strategy:**
    - Platforms selected with rationale
    - Role of each platform (prospecting, retargeting, search capture)
    - Budget allocation by platform
  - **Audience strategy:**
    - Targeting approach per platform (broad, interest, lookalike, custom)
    - Audience segmentation for retargeting
    - Exclusion strategy
  - **Creative strategy:**
    - Creative angles to test (3-5 distinct angles)
    - Format mix (static, video, UGC, carousel)
    - Creative production pipeline and refresh cadence
  - **Funnel strategy:**
    - Traffic flow: ad > landing page > conversion
    - Landing page recommendations
    - Post-conversion experience recommendations
  - **Measurement strategy:**
    - Attribution model selection
    - Reporting metrics and dashboards
    - Incrementality testing plan
    - Third-party tools to implement
  - **Budget and timeline:**
    - Monthly budget allocation
    - 90-day roadmap with milestones
    - Scaling triggers and criteria
- [ ] Strategy presented and approved by client
- [ ] 90-day roadmap agreed upon

**Quality Gate:** Strategy document approved by client. Budget confirmed. 90-day roadmap agreed upon.

### Phase 4: Setup and Build (Days 15-25)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Tracking implementation/optimization:
  - Tracking issues from audit resolved
  - Server-side tracking implemented (if not already)
  - Conversion events verified
  - GA4 configuration confirmed
  - UTM parameters standardized
- [ ] Account restructure (if needed):
  - Legacy campaigns paused or archived
  - New campaign structure built per strategy
  - Naming conventions applied
  - Audience exclusions configured
- [ ] Creative production:
  - Initial creative assets produced per creative strategy
  - Minimum 5-8 creative assets per primary platform
  - Ad copy written (multiple variants)
  - Creative approved by client
- [ ] Campaign build:
  - Campaigns built per strategy document
  - Targeting configured
  - Budgets set
  - Bidding strategies selected
  - Creative assigned
  - UTMs configured
  - Internal QA completed
- [ ] Reporting setup:
  - Dashboard configured (Looker Studio, platform dashboards, or third-party)
  - Automated reporting scheduled (weekly and monthly)
  - Reporting template designed and approved by client
  - Baseline metrics documented

**Quality Gate:** Tracking verified. Campaigns built and QA'd. Creative approved. Reporting configured. Ready to launch.

### Phase 5: Launch and Establish Cadence (Days 25-35)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Campaigns launched:
  - Staged launch sequence (tracking campaigns first, then full campaigns)
  - Launch-day verification (ads delivering, tracking firing, no disapprovals)
- [ ] First week monitoring:
  - Daily performance checks
  - 48-hour launch report to client
  - Week 1 performance report
  - Initial optimization actions taken
- [ ] Ongoing management cadence established:
  - **Daily:** Performance check, anomaly detection
  - **Weekly:** Creative performance review, optimization actions
  - **Bi-weekly:** Client status call (or as agreed)
  - **Monthly:** Comprehensive performance report with insights and recommendations
  - **Quarterly:** Strategy review and 90-day planning
- [ ] Communication norms established:
  - Reporting format and delivery schedule confirmed
  - Escalation procedures defined (when to alert client)
  - Approval workflows confirmed (creative, budget changes)
  - Client feedback loop established
- [ ] Onboarding completion meeting:
  - Review of everything set up
  - Confirm ongoing management plan
  - Address any remaining questions
  - Transition from onboarding to ongoing management

**Quality Gate:** Campaigns live and delivering. Tracking confirmed with real conversions. Ongoing cadence established and first reports delivered. Client satisfied with onboarding experience.

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Client delays in providing access | High | High | Send access checklist Day 1; follow up persistently |
| Client delays in approving creative/strategy | Medium | Medium | Set clear approval deadlines; batch approvals |
| Previous agency left account in poor state | Medium | Medium | Plan for additional cleanup time; set expectations |
| Business verification delays (Meta, Google) | Medium | High | Start verification process immediately |
| Unrealistic client expectations | Medium | High | Set expectations clearly in kickoff; reference learning phase timelines |
| Internal team capacity constraints | Low | Medium | Confirm team availability before kickoff |
| Existing campaigns disrupted during transition | Medium | High | Maintain existing campaigns until new ones are stable |
