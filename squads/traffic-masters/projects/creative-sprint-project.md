# Project Template: Creative Testing Sprint

## Project Overview

A focused, time-boxed sprint dedicated to producing, testing, and identifying winning creative assets. This project is used when current creative is fatiguing, performance is declining due to creative staleness, or the team needs to build a fresh creative library. The sprint produces a high volume of creative variants, tests them systematically, and identifies the next generation of winning assets.

---

## Objectives

1. Produce 20-40 new creative assets across multiple angles and formats
2. Test all assets systematically with controlled methodology
3. Identify 5-10 winning creative assets for scaling
4. Document creative insights (winning hooks, angles, formats)
5. Refresh the active creative library and build a tested reservoir

---

## Phases and Deliverables

### Phase 1: Research and Briefing (Days 1-3)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Current creative performance audit completed:
  - Top 10 performing creative assets (all time and last 30 days)
  - Common elements in winners (hooks, formats, angles, colors, talent)
  - Common elements in losers
  - Creative fatigue analysis (what is declining and why)
- [ ] Competitive creative audit completed:
  - Meta Ad Library review of top 5 competitors
  - TikTok Creative Center review of top-performing category ads
  - YouTube ad research for category
  - Document competitor creative themes, formats, and angles
- [ ] Creative angle matrix developed:

| Angle | Hook Approach | Format | Target Segment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem-solution | "Tired of [problem]?" | Video testimonial | Pain-aware audience |
| Social proof | "[X] people switched" | Static with stats | Skeptical buyers |
| Demonstration | "Watch what happens" | Product demo video | Feature-driven buyers |
| Identity | "For [audience] who..." | Lifestyle static/video | Aspirational buyers |
| Comparison | "Why this beats [alternative]" | Side-by-side video | Comparison shoppers |

- [ ] Creative briefs written for each angle (3-5 angles minimum)
- [ ] Creator/UGC briefs sent (if using external creators)
- [ ] Production schedule finalized

**Quality Gate:** Creative audit completed. Minimum 4 distinct angles identified and briefed. Production schedule confirmed with all team members.

### Phase 2: Production (Days 3-8)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Static ads produced:
  - 3-5 variants per angle (minimum 12-15 total)
  - Formats: 1:1, 9:16, 1.91:1
  - Variations: different headlines, different images, different layouts
- [ ] Video ads produced:
  - 2-3 variants per angle (minimum 8-12 total)
  - Formats: 9:16 (stories/reels), 1:1 (feed), 16:9 (YouTube if applicable)
  - Length: 15s, 30s, and 60s versions where appropriate
  - Hook variations: 3+ different hooks per video concept
- [ ] UGC content received and edited (if applicable):
  - Creator content reviewed for quality
  - Edited with captions, music, and branding
  - Multiple cut-down versions created
- [ ] Ad copy variants written:
  - 3-5 primary text variants per angle
  - 5+ headline variants
  - 3+ description variants
  - CTAs tested across variants
- [ ] All assets reviewed for:
  - Brand compliance
  - Platform policy compliance
  - Technical specifications (resolution, file size, duration)
- [ ] Client approval received on all assets

**Quality Gate:** Minimum 20 unique creative assets produced. All formats and placements covered. All assets approved and ready for upload. No policy violations identified.

### Phase 3: Testing (Days 8-18)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Testing campaign structure built:
  - Separate testing campaign with dedicated budget
  - Consistent audience across all tests (broad or proven audience)
  - Each creative asset gets equal budget allocation initially
  - Conversion-optimized bidding (consistent across all tests)
- [ ] Testing methodology:
  - Budget: $20-50 per asset per day (depending on total budget)
  - Duration: minimum 3 days per asset, maximum 7 days
  - Kill criteria: assets below minimum CTR/hook rate threshold after 1,000+ impressions
  - Win criteria: CPA within target range with statistical significance
- [ ] Daily monitoring during test period:
  - Day 1: verify all ads delivering, no disapprovals
  - Day 2: first performance check (CTR, hook rate, CPM)
  - Day 3: kill obvious underperformers, reallocate budget
  - Day 5: identify leading performers, begin second round of cuts
  - Day 7: final performance ranking
- [ ] Performance data collected for all assets:
  - CTR
  - Hook rate (video: 3-second view rate)
  - CPC
  - CPA/ROAS
  - ThruPlay rate (video)
  - Frequency
  - Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares, saves)

**Quality Gate:** All assets have received minimum viable impressions (1,000+). Statistical confidence in winner identification. Testing budget spent within plan.

### Phase 4: Analysis and Handoff (Days 18-21)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Creative performance report:
  - Ranking of all assets by primary KPI (CPA or ROAS)
  - Top 5-10 winners identified with performance data
  - Bottom performers identified with hypotheses for failure
  - Performance by angle/concept (which themes won?)
  - Performance by format (static vs. video vs. UGC)
  - Performance by hook type (which hooks stopped the scroll?)
- [ ] Creative insights document:
  - Winning patterns identified (what do winners have in common?)
  - Losing patterns identified (what to avoid)
  - Hook effectiveness ranking
  - Format effectiveness ranking
  - Angle effectiveness ranking
  - Recommendations for next sprint
- [ ] Handoff to scaling:
  - Winning assets flagged and organized for scaling campaigns
  - Recommended budget allocation across winning assets
  - Suggested scaling campaign structure
  - Creative fatigue timeline estimates for each winner
- [ ] Creative reservoir updated:
  - Winners moved to "ready to scale" category
  - Promising-but-not-winning assets categorized for iteration
  - Asset library organized and documented

**Quality Gate:** Minimum 5 winning assets identified with clear performance data. Insights documented for future creative development. Scaling recommendations delivered to media buying team.

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | Measurement |

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low win rate (<15%) | Medium | High | Revisit creative strategy; ensure angles are based on customer research |
| UGC creators deliver late | High | Medium | Brief 2x the number of creators needed; have backup in-house production |
| Ad disapprovals during testing | Medium | Medium | Pre-review all creative against platform policies |
| Insufficient testing budget | Low | High | Confirm budget before production; prioritize angles if budget is limited |
| Creative all looks the same | Medium | Medium | Ensure distinct angles, not just visual variations of one concept |
| Client approval delays | Medium | Medium | Set clear approval timeline in advance; use batch approval process |
