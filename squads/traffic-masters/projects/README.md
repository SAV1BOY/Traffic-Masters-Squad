## Purpose

Project templates provide structured frameworks for executing common paid traffic management initiatives. Each template includes objectives, timelines, team roles, phased deliverables, quality gates, success metrics, risk registers, and dependency maps to ensure consistent, high-quality execution.

## How to Use These Templates

### Starting a New Project
1. Select the appropriate template based on the initiative type
2. Copy the template and customize for your specific situation
3. Fill in project-specific details (client name, dates, budget, team members)
4. Review objectives and success metrics with stakeholders before beginning
5. Use phases and quality gates to track progress
6. Refer to the risk register proactively, not reactively

### Template Structure
Each template follows a consistent structure:
- **Project Overview:** What this project accomplishes and why
- **Objectives:** Specific, measurable goals
- **Timeline:** Expected duration and milestone dates
- **Team:** Roles and responsibilities (agents/team members involved)
- **Phases:** Sequential stages of execution
- **Deliverables:** Specific outputs per phase
- **Quality Gates:** Criteria that must be met before advancing to the next phase
- **Success Metrics:** How success is measured at project completion
- **Risk Register:** Known risks with mitigation strategies
- **Dependencies:** External requirements and blockers

### Customization Guidelines
- Timelines are estimates; adjust based on account complexity and team capacity
- Add or remove phases as needed for your specific situation
- Success metrics should be calibrated to the specific account and business context
- Risk registers should be updated as new risks are identified during execution
