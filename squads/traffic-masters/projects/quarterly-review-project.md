# Project Template: Quarterly Business Review

## Project Overview

Preparation and delivery of a comprehensive quarterly business review (QBR) analyzing paid traffic performance over the past quarter, assessing strategy effectiveness, and planning the next quarter's initiatives. The QBR serves as the primary strategic checkpoint for the client relationship.

---

## Objectives

1. Analyze quarterly performance against goals and benchmarks
2. Assess the effectiveness of current strategy and tactics
3. Identify optimization opportunities and growth potential
4. Present actionable recommendations for the next quarter
5. Align stakeholders on strategy, budget, and priorities for the upcoming period
6. Strengthen the client relationship through demonstrated expertise and transparency

---

## Timeline

**Total Duration:** 2 weeks (1 week preparation, 1 week delivery and follow-up)

| Phase | Duration | Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1: Data Collection | Days 1-3 | All performance data gathered |
| Phase 2: Analysis | Days 3-7 | Analysis completed, insights identified |
| Phase 3: Presentation Build | Days 7-10 | QBR deck finalized |
| Phase 4: Delivery and Follow-Up | Days 10-14 | QBR presented, next quarter plan agreed |

## Phases and Deliverables

### Phase 1: Data Collection (Days 1-3)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Performance data exported from all platforms:
  - Meta Ads: campaign, ad set, and ad-level data for the quarter
  - Google Ads: campaign-level data, search term reports, auction insights
  - TikTok (if applicable): campaign-level data
  - Other platforms (as applicable)
- [ ] Analytics data collected:
  - GA4: channel performance, conversion paths, audience data
  - Third-party attribution: cross-channel analysis (if available)
  - Backend data: actual revenue/leads, LTV by cohort, new vs. returning customers
- [ ] Business context gathered:
  - Revenue/lead targets for the quarter
  - Actual business outcomes (total revenue, total leads, total customers)
  - Product launches, pricing changes, or other business events during the quarter
  - Client feedback from the quarter (informal notes from meetings, emails)
- [ ] Competitive data collected:
  - Meta Ad Library competitor review
  - Google Auction Insights data
  - Notable competitor activities during the quarter
- [ ] Previous QBR reviewed:
  - What was planned last quarter
  - What was actually executed
  - What outcomes were expected vs. achieved

**Quality Gate:** All data sources collected and validated. No significant data gaps.

### Phase 2: Analysis (Days 3-7)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Quarterly performance summary:
  | Metric | Q Target | Q Actual | % vs. Target | Q-over-Q Change |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Total ad spend | $X | $X | X% | +/- X% |
  | Total conversions | X | X | X% | +/- X% |
  | CPA | $X | $X | X% | +/- X% |
  | ROAS | X.Xx | X.Xx | X% | +/- X% |
  | New customers | X | X | X% | +/- X% |
  | Blended CAC | $X | $X | X% | +/- X% |
  | Revenue attributed | $X | $X | X% | +/- X% |

- [ ] Channel performance analysis:
  - Performance by platform (spend, CPA, ROAS, volume)
  - Channel contribution to total program
  - Channel-level trends over the quarter (improving, declining, stable)
  - Incrementality observations per channel
  - Budget efficiency by channel (marginal ROAS at current spend levels)
- [ ] Campaign-level analysis:
  - Top 5 performing campaigns with analysis of why
  - Bottom 5 performing campaigns with analysis of why
  - Campaign type performance (prospecting vs. retargeting vs. search vs. shopping)
  - Budget allocation efficiency
- [ ] Creative performance analysis:
  - Top 10 performing creative assets
  - Creative themes and patterns in winners
  - Creative fatigue analysis (how quickly did creative fatigue this quarter?)
  - Creative volume assessment (was there enough creative throughput?)
  - Format performance comparison (video vs. static vs. UGC vs. carousel)
  - Recommendations for next quarter creative direction
- [ ] Audience performance analysis:
  - Performance by audience type (broad, interest, lookalike, retargeting)
  - Audience saturation assessment
  - New audience opportunities identified
  - Geographic performance analysis
  - Device and placement performance
- [ ] Competitive landscape analysis:
  - Competitor advertising activity changes
  - Competitive positioning shifts
  - Market share observations (where possible)
  - Implications for next quarter strategy
- [ ] Year-over-year comparison (if data available):
  - Same quarter comparison to prior year
  - Growth trends and trajectory
  - Seasonal adjustment observations

**Quality Gate:** Complete analysis with data-backed insights. Clear identification of wins, losses, and opportunities.

### Phase 3: Presentation Build (Days 7-10)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] QBR presentation deck:

  **Section 1: Executive Summary (2-3 slides)**
  - Quarter at a glance: key metrics vs. targets
  - Top 3 wins
  - Top 3 challenges
  - Strategic assessment: on track, needs adjustment, pivot required

  **Section 2: Performance Deep Dive (5-8 slides)**
  - Overall performance trends (with charts)
  - Channel-by-channel performance
  - Campaign performance highlights
  - Creative performance insights
  - Audience insights

  **Section 3: Key Learnings (2-3 slides)**
  - What we learned this quarter
  - Tests conducted and results
  - Hypothesis validated or invalidated
  - Strategic implications

  **Section 4: Competitive and Market Context (1-2 slides)**
  - Competitive landscape changes
  - Platform updates affecting strategy
  - Industry trends relevant to the account

  **Section 5: Next Quarter Plan (3-5 slides)**
  - Strategic priorities for the next quarter
  - Budget recommendations (allocation by channel, by objective)
  - Creative plan (themes, volume, formats)
  - Testing roadmap (what to test next quarter)
  - Key milestones and dates
  - Expected outcomes and targets

  **Section 6: Discussion Points (1 slide)**
  - Open questions for client input
  - Decisions needed
  - Dependencies requiring client action

- [ ] Supporting data appendix prepared (detailed data tables available if needed)
- [ ] Internal review of presentation completed (second set of eyes)
- [ ] Presentation rehearsed

**Quality Gate:** Presentation reviewed internally. Story is clear and data-backed. Recommendations are specific and actionable. Presentation is professional and client-ready.

### Phase 4: Delivery and Follow-Up (Days 10-14)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] QBR meeting conducted:
  - Presentation delivered (45-60 minutes)
  - Discussion and Q&A facilitated
  - Client feedback captured
  - Decisions documented
  - Action items agreed upon
- [ ] Meeting notes distributed within 24 hours:
  - Key discussion points
  - Decisions made
  - Action items with owners and deadlines
  - Open items requiring follow-up
- [ ] Next quarter plan finalized:
  - Budget approved
  - Strategy adjustments confirmed
  - Testing priorities agreed upon
  - Creative production schedule aligned
  - Key dates and milestones confirmed
- [ ] QBR deck shared with client for reference
- [ ] Internal debrief conducted:
  - Client feedback discussed
  - Team alignment on next quarter priorities
  - Process improvements for next QBR identified

**Quality Gate:** QBR delivered successfully. Client feedback positive. Next quarter plan approved. Action items assigned and tracked.

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poor quarterly performance creates difficult conversation | Medium | Medium | Lead with transparency; focus on learnings and recovery plan |
| Client stakeholders unavailable for QBR meeting | Medium | High | Schedule QBR 3+ weeks in advance; confirm attendance |
| Data discrepancies between sources | Medium | Medium | Reconcile data before presentation; note any discrepancies |
| Client has expectations not aligned with results | Medium | High | Preview key numbers before the formal QBR if possible |
| Scope creep (QBR becomes full strategy overhaul) | Medium | Medium | Set clear agenda; park strategic deep-dives for separate sessions |

## QBR Quality Checklist

Before delivering the QBR, verify:
- [ ] All numbers are accurate and cross-referenced
- [ ] Charts are clearly labeled with proper date ranges
- [ ] Year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter comparisons are included
- [ ] Wins are celebrated (clients need to see value)
- [ ] Challenges are honestly addressed with solutions proposed
- [ ] Recommendations are specific with expected impact and timeline
- [ ] The presentation tells a coherent story (not just a data dump)
- [ ] The deck is professionally formatted and free of errors
- [ ] Discussion points and decision items are clearly identified
- [ ] The team is prepared to answer tough questions
