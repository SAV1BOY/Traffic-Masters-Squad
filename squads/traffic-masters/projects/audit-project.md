# Project Template: Comprehensive Account Audit

## Project Overview

A thorough audit of an existing ad account (or multi-platform advertising program) to assess performance, identify issues, find opportunities, and produce actionable recommendations. Audits are conducted when inheriting an account, when performance has plateaued, or as a scheduled health check.

---

## Objectives

1. Assess current account health across all dimensions (structure, tracking, creative, audiences, bidding)
2. Identify performance issues, inefficiencies, and missed opportunities
3. Benchmark performance against industry standards and historical trends
4. Produce a prioritized action plan with expected impact
5. Establish baseline metrics for measuring future improvements

---

## Timeline

**Total Duration:** 2-3 weeks

| Phase | Duration | Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1: Data Collection | Days 1-3 | All data gathered and access confirmed |
| Phase 2: Technical Audit | Days 3-7 | Tracking, structure, and settings audited |
| Phase 3: Performance Audit | Days 5-10 | Performance analysis completed |
| Phase 4: Creative and Audience Audit | Days 8-12 | Creative and audience analysis completed |
| Phase 5: Report and Recommendations | Days 12-15 | Audit report delivered |

## Phases and Deliverables

### Phase 1: Data Collection (Days 1-3)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Access confirmed to all ad platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, etc.)
- [ ] Access confirmed to analytics (GA4, third-party attribution)
- [ ] Access confirmed to e-commerce platform/CRM backend
- [ ] Historical performance data exported (last 90 days minimum, last 12 months preferred)
- [ ] Client questionnaire completed:
  - Business goals and KPIs
  - Current pain points and concerns
  - Historical context (what has been tried, what worked, what failed)
  - Competitive landscape
  - Upcoming plans or launches
  - Budget constraints and expectations
- [ ] Data sources documented and validated

**Quality Gate:** Complete access to all required platforms and data sources. Client questionnaire completed.

### Phase 2: Technical Audit (Days 3-7)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Conversion tracking audit:
  - Pixel/tag installation verified on all pages
  - Conversion events firing correctly (verified with test transactions)
  - Server-side tracking (CAPI/Enhanced Conversions) status
  - Event deduplication functioning
  - Event match quality scores documented
  - Conversion values passing correctly (e-commerce)
  - Attribution settings reviewed (windows, models)
- [ ] Account structure audit:
  - Campaign naming conventions assessed
  - Campaign objective alignment (correct objectives selected)
  - Ad set structure efficiency (consolidation opportunities)
  - Audience overlap analysis
  - Budget allocation assessment (campaign-level vs. ad set-level)
  - Redundant or conflicting campaigns identified
- [ ] Platform settings audit:
  - Billing and payment method status
  - Account limits and restrictions
  - Placement settings reviewed
  - Bid strategy appropriateness
  - Exclusion lists reviewed (negative keywords, audience exclusions)
  - UTM parameter consistency

**Quality Gate:** All technical issues documented with severity ratings (critical, important, minor). No critical tracking issues left unresolved.

### Phase 3: Performance Audit (Days 5-10)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Overall performance analysis:
  - Spend trend (last 90 days, last 12 months)
  - CPA/ROAS trend over time
  - Conversion volume trend
  - Blended metrics (total revenue / total ad spend)
- [ ] Platform-level performance:
  - Performance by platform (Meta, Google, TikTok, etc.)
  - Platform contribution to total conversions
  - Platform-level CPA/ROAS comparison
  - Cross-platform attribution analysis
- [ ] Campaign-level performance:
  - Performance ranking of all campaigns
  - Spend vs. performance efficiency by campaign
  - Campaigns consuming budget without adequate returns
  - Campaigns with unexploited potential
- [ ] Channel and objective analysis:
  - Prospecting vs. retargeting performance and budget split
  - Branded vs. non-branded search performance (Google)
  - Shopping vs. Search vs. Display performance
  - Video vs. static vs. carousel performance
- [ ] Geographic and demographic analysis:
  - Performance by geography
  - Performance by age and gender (where available)
  - Performance by device
  - Performance by placement
- [ ] Benchmarking:
  - Current metrics vs. industry benchmarks
  - Current metrics vs. historical account performance
  - Identification of above/below benchmark areas

**Quality Gate:** Comprehensive performance analysis complete with clear identification of strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities.

### Phase 4: Creative and Audience Audit (Days 8-12)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Creative performance audit:
  - All active creative assets ranked by performance
  - Creative fatigue analysis (age of active creative, frequency trends)
  - Creative diversity assessment (formats, angles, hooks in use)
  - Creative volume assessment (enough creative for current spend level?)
  - Ad copy analysis (messaging themes, CTA effectiveness)
  - Landing page review (conversion rate, load speed, mobile experience)
- [ ] Audience audit:
  - All active audiences cataloged
  - Audience size and reach analysis
  - Audience overlap analysis (internal cannibalization)
  - Custom audience health (list sizes, upload dates, match rates)
  - Lookalike audience configuration review
  - Exclusion list completeness
  - Retargeting audience segmentation assessment
- [ ] Competitive analysis:
  - Meta Ad Library review of top competitors
  - Google Ads competitive landscape (Auction Insights)
  - Competitive creative trends
  - Share of voice assessment (where possible)

**Quality Gate:** Creative and audience analysis complete. Specific recommendations formulated for each area.

### Phase 5: Report and Recommendations (Days 12-15)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Executive summary (1-2 pages):
  - Overall account health score
  - Top 3 strengths
  - Top 3 issues
  - Top 3 opportunities
  - Estimated impact of implementing recommendations
- [ ] Detailed audit report:
  - Technical audit findings
  - Performance analysis with charts and data
  - Creative audit findings
  - Audience audit findings
  - Competitive landscape summary
- [ ] Prioritized action plan:
  | Priority | Action | Expected Impact | Effort | Timeline |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | [Critical fix] | High | Low | Immediate |
  | 2 | [Key optimization] | High | Medium | 1-2 weeks |
  | 3 | [Growth opportunity] | Medium | Medium | 2-4 weeks |

- [ ] Quick wins identified (implementable within 48 hours)
- [ ] 30-60-90 day roadmap for implementing recommendations
- [ ] Audit presentation delivered to stakeholders

**Quality Gate:** Report reviewed internally before client delivery. All recommendations are specific, actionable, and tied to expected outcomes. Client presentation completed with Q&A.

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Incomplete platform access | Medium | High | Request all access Day 1; follow up immediately |
| Insufficient historical data | Low | Medium | Work with available data; note limitations in report |
| Client disagrees with findings | Medium | Medium | Present data-backed findings; frame diplomatically |
| Audit reveals critical tracking errors | Medium | High | Flag immediately; do not wait for final report |
| Scope creep (audit expanding beyond plan) | High | Medium | Define scope clearly in Phase 1; manage expectations |
