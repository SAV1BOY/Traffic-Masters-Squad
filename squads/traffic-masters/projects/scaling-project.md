# Project Template: Scaling Project

## Project Overview

Scaling an ad account from its current profitable spend level to a significantly higher spend level (typically 2-5x increase) while maintaining acceptable CPA/ROAS. This project requires coordinated effort across media buying, creative production, tracking, and operations to ensure sustainable growth rather than premature scaling failure.

---

## Objectives

1. Increase ad spend from current level to target level over a defined timeline
2. Maintain CPA/ROAS within acceptable range (defined per account)
3. Scale creative production to support higher spend
4. Validate operational readiness for increased volume
5. Establish a sustainable structure that can maintain the higher spend level

---

## Phases and Deliverables

### Phase 1: Assessment and Planning (Week 1)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Current performance baseline documented:
  - Current daily/weekly/monthly spend
  - Current CPA/ROAS by campaign, platform, and audience
  - Current creative performance (top performers, fatigue status)
  - Current audience utilization (sizes, frequencies, overlap)
  - Marginal CPA analysis (what is the CPA of the most recent 20% of spend?)
- [ ] Scaling readiness assessment:
  - Creative: How many winning assets? How fast can new ones be produced?
  - Data: Is conversion tracking complete and accurate?
  - Audiences: How large are targetable audiences? Room for expansion?
  - Operational: Can fulfillment, CS, and inventory handle 2-5x volume?
  - Financial: Is budget confirmed and funded for the scaling period?
- [ ] Scaling plan developed:
  - Target spend level and timeline
  - Budget increase schedule (20-30% every 3-5 days)
  - CPA/ROAS guardrails (maximum acceptable CPA at each stage)
  - Rollback triggers (when to slow or pause scaling)
  - Creative production schedule aligned with scaling timeline
  - Channel diversification plan (if applicable)
- [ ] Scaling plan approved by stakeholders

**Quality Gate:** Scaling plan approved. Current baseline documented. All readiness criteria assessed. Guardrails defined.

### Phase 2: Foundation Strengthening (Weeks 2-3)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Tracking audit and optimization:
  - Verify pixel and CAPI functioning correctly
  - Optimize event match quality (target 8+/10 on Meta)
  - Confirm conversion events are firing accurately
  - Set up enhanced monitoring for the scaling period
- [ ] Creative preparation:
  - Produce 10-20 new creative assets before scaling begins
  - Test new assets in current campaign structure
  - Identify 5+ proven creative assets ready for scale
  - Establish ongoing production pipeline (5-10 new assets per week)
- [ ] Account structure optimization:
  - Simplify campaign structure for algorithmic efficiency
  - Consolidate audiences where overlap exists
  - Remove legacy campaigns that may compete in auction
  - Ensure ad set budgets can support 50+ conversions/week
- [ ] Operational readiness verification:
  - Fulfillment capacity confirmed for projected volume
  - Customer service capacity confirmed
  - Inventory levels sufficient
  - Website load tested for projected traffic increase
- [ ] Measurement framework established:
  - Third-party attribution or blended metrics baseline set
  - Incrementality test designed for key channels
  - Dashboard configured for scaling-period monitoring

**Quality Gate:** Tracking verified. 5+ tested creative assets ready. Account structure optimized. Operational readiness confirmed by all stakeholders.

### Phase 3: Controlled Scaling (Weeks 3-8)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Progressive budget increases executed:
  - Increase 20-30% every 3-5 days
  - Monitor CPA/ROAS against guardrails at each increment
  - Document performance at each budget level
- [ ] Scaling log maintained:

  | Date | Budget Level | CPA | ROAS | Notes |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Week 3 | $X/day (+20%) | $Y | Z.Zx | Performance held |
  | Week 4 | $X/day (+20%) | $Y | Z.Zx | CPA increased 10%, within range |

- [ ] Creative refresh cycles executed:
  - New creative introduced every 1-2 weeks during scaling
  - Fatiguing creative paused and replaced
  - Creative performance tracked relative to spend level
- [ ] Channel expansion (if part of plan):
  - New platform campaigns launched with dedicated budgets
  - Learning phase monitored and supported
  - Cross-channel budget optimization based on performance
- [ ] Weekly scaling progress reports delivered:
  - Spend vs. plan
  - CPA/ROAS vs. guardrails
  - Creative status (active, fatiguing, new)
  - Audience health (frequency, reach)
  - Operational metrics (fulfillment times, CS queue)

**Quality Gate:** Budget increases proceeding on schedule. CPA/ROAS within guardrail ranges. No operational bottlenecks. Creative pipeline keeping pace with demand.

### Phase 4: Optimization and Stabilization (Weeks 8-10)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Target spend level reached
- [ ] Performance stabilized at target spend for 2+ consecutive weeks
- [ ] Marginal efficiency analyzed (CPA of incremental spend vs. average CPA)
- [ ] Budget allocation optimized across campaigns and platforms
- [ ] Incrementality assessment completed for key channels
- [ ] Creative library fully refreshed for sustained performance at new level
- [ ] Long-term creative production cadence established
- [ ] Ongoing optimization playbook documented

**Quality Gate:** Target spend maintained for 14+ consecutive days. CPA/ROAS within acceptable range (may be higher than pre-scaling baseline, as expected). Marginal CPA within defined limits.

### Phase 5: Handoff and Documentation (Weeks 10-12)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Scaling retrospective completed:
  - What worked, what did not
  - Unexpected challenges and how they were resolved
  - Final performance metrics vs. original targets
- [ ] Ongoing management plan documented:
  - Daily, weekly, monthly optimization routines
  - Creative refresh schedule
  - Budget reallocation rules
  - Escalation procedures for performance issues
- [ ] Knowledge transferred to ongoing management team (if different)
- [ ] Final scaling report delivered to stakeholders

**Quality Gate:** Documentation complete. Ongoing management team briefed and capable. Stakeholders satisfied with results.

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPA exceeds guardrails during scaling | High | High | Pause scaling, stabilize, diagnose before resuming |
| Creative fatigue at higher spend | High | High | Maintain aggressive creative production pipeline |
| Algorithm instability during budget changes | Medium | Medium | Increase budget gradually; avoid other changes during budget increases |
| Fulfillment bottleneck | Medium | High | Pre-confirm capacity; have backup fulfillment plan |
| Audience saturation | Medium | Medium | Monitor frequency; expand to new audiences/platforms as needed |
| Competitor response to market presence | Low | Medium | Monitor competitive landscape; prepare defensive creative |
| Conversion tracking degradation at scale | Low | High | Continuous tracking monitoring; server-side backup |
