# Fiscal Compliance Setup Project — Brazil

## Objective
Ensure full fiscal and regulatory compliance for traffic operations in the Brazilian market.

### Week 2: Implementation
- [ ] Set up nota fiscal issuance process

## Key Compliance Areas

### LGPD Requirements
- Legal basis for data processing documented
- Consent mechanisms active on all touchpoints
- Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) process in place
- Data Processing Impact Assessment completed
- DPO (Data Protection Officer) appointed or identified

### Fiscal Requirements
- Nota fiscal process for all billings
- IOF documentation for international payments
- ISS compliance for service fees
- Exchange rate documentation
- Parcelamento (installment) tax treatment

### Advertising Standards
- CONAR guidelines applied to all creative
- CDC (Consumer Code) requirements met
- Clear pricing and terms in all offers
- Disclaimers in Portuguese

## Success Criteria
- All LGPD requirements met and documented
- Nota fiscal process operational
- Tax obligations identified and tracked
- Advertising content CONAR-compliant
- Team trained on BR compliance requirements
