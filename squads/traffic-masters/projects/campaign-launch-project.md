# Project Template: Campaign Launch

## Project Overview

Launching a new campaign from strategic planning through creative production, build, launch, and initial optimization. This template covers a single campaign initiative (new product launch, new offer, new audience expansion) within an existing account with established tracking.

---

## Objectives

1. Define campaign strategy aligned with business goals
2. Produce campaign-specific creative assets
3. Build and launch campaigns across target platforms
4. Achieve defined performance targets within the first 30 days
5. Establish ongoing optimization cadence for the campaign

---

## Timeline

**Total Duration:** 3-4 weeks (1 week prep, 1 week build, 2 weeks launch and optimize)

| Phase | Duration | Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1: Strategy and Planning | Days 1-5 | Strategy document approved |
| Phase 2: Creative Production | Days 3-10 | Creative assets delivered and approved |
| Phase 3: Campaign Build | Days 8-12 | Campaigns built, reviewed, scheduled |
| Phase 4: Launch and Optimization | Days 12-28 | Campaigns live, first optimizations made |

## Phases and Deliverables

### Phase 1: Strategy and Planning (Days 1-5)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Campaign brief completed:
  - Business objective (revenue, leads, awareness)
  - Target audience definition
  - Offer/product positioning
  - Budget allocation
  - KPI targets (CPA, ROAS, CPL, volume)
  - Platform selection
  - Timeline and key dates
- [ ] Competitive landscape reviewed (competitor ads, messaging, offers)
- [ ] Audience strategy defined (targeting approach per platform)
- [ ] Funnel/landing page reviewed and approved
- [ ] Measurement plan established (what to track, how to attribute)
- [ ] Creative brief developed with specific angles and formats needed
- [ ] Strategy document shared and approved by stakeholders

**Quality Gate:** Strategy document approved by stakeholders. Budget confirmed. Landing page ready. Creative brief finalized.

### Phase 2: Creative Production (Days 3-10)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Creative concepts developed (minimum 3 distinct angles)
- [ ] Static image ads produced (minimum 3-5 variants)
- [ ] Video ads produced (minimum 2-3 variants, if applicable)
- [ ] Ad copy written (minimum 3 primary text variants, 5 headlines)
- [ ] Creative formatted for all required placements:
  - 1:1 (feed)
  - 9:16 (stories/reels/shorts)
  - 16:9 (YouTube in-stream, if applicable)
  - Search ad copy (if applicable)
- [ ] Creative reviewed for brand compliance
- [ ] Creative approved by client/stakeholders

**Quality Gate:** Minimum creative volume met (5+ unique assets). All formats and sizes produced. Client approval received. Creative aligned with campaign brief.

### Phase 3: Campaign Build (Days 8-12)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Campaign structure built per strategy document
- [ ] Audience targeting configured
- [ ] Budget allocated across campaigns and ad sets
- [ ] Bidding strategy selected and configured
- [ ] Creative assets uploaded and assigned to correct ad sets
- [ ] Ad copy assigned to correct ads
- [ ] Exclusions applied (existing customers, irrelevant audiences)
- [ ] UTM parameters configured for all ads
- [ ] Internal QA review completed:
  - Targeting verified
  - Budget verified
  - Creative/copy matches correct ad set
  - Landing page URLs correct and tracked
  - Conversion events correctly selected
- [ ] Campaign scheduled or set to launch

**Quality Gate:** Full QA checklist passed. All campaign elements match strategy document. Second reviewer has approved the build.

### Phase 4: Launch and Optimization (Days 12-28)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Campaigns launched at planned time
- [ ] 24-hour launch check: ads delivering, no disapprovals, tracking firing
- [ ] 48-hour performance check: CPM, CTR, CPC within expected ranges
- [ ] Day 3-5: first creative performance analysis
- [ ] Day 7: Week 1 performance report
  - Key metrics vs. targets
  - Creative performance ranking
  - Audience performance comparison
  - Spend pacing
  - Initial insights and optimization plan
- [ ] Day 7-14: first optimization cycle
  - Pause underperforming creative
  - Adjust budgets based on performance
  - Test new audience segments (if applicable)
  - Refresh ad copy (if applicable)
- [ ] Day 14: Week 2 performance report
- [ ] Day 14-21: second optimization cycle
  - Introduce new creative (if needed)
  - Scale winning elements
  - Refine targeting based on data
- [ ] Day 28: Month 1 campaign report
  - Full performance analysis
  - Creative insights
  - Audience insights
  - Budget utilization
  - Recommendations for month 2

**Quality Gate:** Campaign achieving performance within 120% of target CPA/ROAS by Day 14. If not, escalation and strategy review triggered.

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| CPA/CPL | At or below target | Platform + third-party attribution |
| ROAS (e-commerce) | At or above target | Platform + blended calculation |
| Conversion volume | Meets minimum volume target | Platform reporting |
| Creative winner identification | 2+ winning creative assets identified | Performance ranking |
| Budget utilization | 90%+ of allocated budget spent | Platform pacing |
| Learning phase exit | All ad sets exited learning within 7 days | Platform status |

---

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creative disapprovals | Medium | Medium | Pre-review creative against platform policies |
| Learning phase stall | Medium | Medium | Ensure sufficient budget per ad set for 50+ conversions/week |
| Landing page performance issues | Medium | High | Test landing page before launch (speed, mobile, conversion flow) |
| Audience saturation (small audiences) | Low | Medium | Have backup broader audiences ready |
| Competitor launches competing offer | Low | Medium | Monitor competitive landscape; prepare reactive messaging |
| Budget pacing issues | Medium | Low | Set appropriate daily budgets; monitor pacing daily |
