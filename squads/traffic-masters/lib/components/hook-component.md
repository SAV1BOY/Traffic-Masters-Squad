# Hook Component

## Purpose
Standardized format for creating and testing ad hooks.

## Hook Types
1. **Question Hook:** Opens with a question the audience must answer
2. **Bold Claim Hook:** Specific, surprising statement
3. **Curiosity Hook:** Opens information gap
4. **Authority Hook:** Leads with credentials or results
5. **Social Proof Hook:** Leads with crowd validation
6. **Pattern Interrupt Hook:** Breaks expected pattern
7. **Story Hook:** Opens with narrative element
8. **Contrarian Hook:** Challenges conventional wisdom

## Component Fields
- `hook_id`: Identifier
- `type`: Hook category (from list above)
- `text`: The hook text/script
- `platform`: Target platform
- `format`: Text / Video / Audio
- `target_emotion`: What feeling it creates
- `audience_stage`: Cold / Warm / Hot

## Hook Scoring Rubric

| Criteria | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|----------|---|---|---|---|---|
| Specificity | Vague | Somewhat | Moderate | Specific | Very specific |
| Relevance | Off-target | Tangential | Related | Relevant | Bullseye |
| Emotion | None | Mild | Moderate | Strong | Powerful |
| Curiosity | None | Slight | Moderate | Strong | Irresistible |
| Scroll-stop | Weak | Below avg | Average | Strong | Exceptional |

## Testing Protocol
1. Write 5+ hook variations per concept
2. Test with same body and CTA
3. Measure: hook rate (video), CTR (static), CPA
4. Minimum 1,000 impressions per hook before judging
5. Winner becomes control for next round
