# Competitive Intelligence Collector Component

## Purpose
Reusable template for systematically gathering, organizing, and analyzing competitor advertising activity to inform paid traffic strategy.

## Intelligence Collection Sources

### Free Tools

| Source | What You Can Find | URL | Update Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta Ad Library** | All active Meta/Instagram ads for any advertiser | facebook.com/ads/library | Real-time |
| **Google Ads Transparency Center** | Active Google Ads for any advertiser | adstransparency.google.com | Real-time |
| **TikTok Ad Library** | Active TikTok ads (select markets) | library.tiktok.com | Real-time |
| **LinkedIn Ad Library** | Active LinkedIn ads | linkedin.com/ad-library | Real-time |
| **Google Search (manual)** | Ad copy, extensions, landing pages | Search target keywords | On-demand |
| **SimilarWeb (free tier)** | Traffic estimates, channel mix | similarweb.com | Monthly |
| **BuiltWith** | Tech stack, pixels, analytics | builtwith.com | On-demand |

### Paid Tools

| Tool | Capabilities | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **SEMrush** | Search ad history, keyword overlap, display ads, spend estimates | Google Ads intelligence |
| **SpyFu** | Competitor keyword lists, ad history, spend estimates | Search PPC |
| **Adbeat** | Display and native ad intelligence | Programmatic/display |
| **Pathmatics** | Cross-platform spend estimates, creative analysis | Enterprise-level intel |
| **BigSpy / AdSpy** | Social ad databases, searchable by keyword/advertiser | Social ad creative research |
| **iSpionage** | PPC competitor analysis, keyword tracking | Search campaigns |
