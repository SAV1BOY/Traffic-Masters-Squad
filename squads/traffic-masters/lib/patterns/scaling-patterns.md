# Scaling Patterns

## Purpose
Proven approaches to scaling ad spend while maintaining performance.

## Pattern 1: Vertical Scaling (Budget Increase)
- Increase budget by 20-30% every 3-5 days
- Monitor CPA for 48-72hrs after each increase
- If CPA rises >20%, hold and let algorithm stabilize
- Never double budget overnight (algorithm shock)

## Pattern 2: Horizontal Scaling (New Ad Sets)
- Duplicate winning ad sets with new audiences
- Keep winning creative, change targeting variable
- Expand lookalike % (1% > 3% > 5% > 10%)
- Add new interest categories or behaviors

## Pattern 3: Creative Scaling
- Create iterations of winning creative (same concept, new hook)
- Test new angles with proven audiences
- Launch 3-5 new creatives per week during scaling
- Replace fatigued creative before performance drops

## Pattern 4: Platform Scaling
- Take winning creative concept to new platforms
- Adapt format to platform native (not copy-paste)
- Start with testing budget, validate, then scale

## Pattern 5: Geographic Scaling
- Expand to new markets with proven offer and creative
- Localize copy and creative for new markets
- Adjust CPA targets for market differences

## Scaling Decision Framework
| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| CPA stable, ROAS above target | Increase budget 20% |
| CPA rising, ROAS declining | Hold, refresh creative |
| One audience saturating | Horizontal expand |
| All audiences saturating | New platform or market |

## Common Scaling Mistakes
1. Scaling too fast (>50% budget increase)
2. Scaling without creative pipeline
3. Ignoring frequency increases
4. Not adjusting CPA targets for higher scale
