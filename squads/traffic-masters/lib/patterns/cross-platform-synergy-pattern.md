# Cross-Platform Synergy Pattern

## Purpose
How to create compounding effects by coordinating across multiple ad platforms.

## The Pattern

### Awareness Layer (Top)
- **YouTube:** Long-form educational/entertaining video
- **TikTok:** Short-form native content
- **Meta:** Reels and stories for broad reach
- Goal: Build awareness and audience pools

### Capture Layer (Middle)
- **Google Search:** Capture demand generated by awareness
- **Google Shopping:** Product-level capture
- **Meta Retargeting:** Re-engage video viewers and visitors
- Goal: Convert awareness into consideration

### Conversion Layer (Bottom)
- **Meta BOFU:** Retarget high-intent visitors
- **Google Brand:** Capture branded search from other channels
- **Email/SMS:** Close leads generated by paid
- Goal: Maximize conversions from warmed audiences

## Synergy Tactics
1. YouTube awareness drives Google branded search volume
2. TikTok virality creates Meta retargeting pools
3. Meta prospecting builds Google remarketing lists
4. Content from one platform repurposed across others

## Measurement Approach
- Attribution: Multi-touch or data-driven
- Incrementality tests: Geo-based lift studies
- Brand search volume: Leading indicator of awareness impact
- Blended ROAS: Holistic efficiency across platforms

## Key Principles
1. No platform operates in isolation
2. Each platform has a role in the funnel
3. Creative adapts to platform norms (not copy-paste)
4. Unified measurement across all platforms
5. Budget allocation based on marginal contribution
