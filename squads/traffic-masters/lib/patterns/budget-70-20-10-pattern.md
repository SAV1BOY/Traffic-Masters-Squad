# Budget 70-20-10 Pattern

## Purpose
Framework for allocating budget between proven, promising, and experimental.

## The Pattern

### 70% — Proven Winners
- Campaigns and creatives with demonstrated performance
- Minimum 2 weeks of data at target CPA/ROAS
- Stable, predictable performance
- Focus: Scale and maintain

### 20% — Promising Tests
- Campaigns showing early positive signals
- Passed micro-test phase, in validation
- Has potential to become a "proven" winner
- Focus: Validate and graduate

### 10% — Experimental
- Brand new ideas, platforms, or approaches
- No performance data yet
- High risk, potentially high reward
- Focus: Learn quickly, fail cheaply

## Implementation

| Category | Budget | Min Duration | Success Criteria |
|----------|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Proven | 70% | Ongoing | CPA within target |
| Promising | 20% | 7-14 days | Trending toward target |
| Experimental | 10% | 3-7 days | Any positive signal |

## Graduation Rules
- **Experimental > Promising:** Shows positive signal within 7 days
- **Promising > Proven:** Hits target CPA for 5+ consecutive days
- **Proven > Demoted:** CPA exceeds 1.5x target for 7+ days

## Monthly Review
1. Calculate actual spend distribution vs 70-20-10
2. Review graduation/demotion of campaigns
3. Ensure experimental bucket is always funded
4. Adjust based on pipeline and seasonality

## Common Mistakes
1. 100% on proven = no innovation, eventual fatigue
2. Too much experimental = unstable performance
3. Not graduating promising winners fast enough
4. Keeping "proven" campaigns that have fatigued
