# Retargeting Sequence Patterns

## Purpose
Proven retargeting sequences organized by window and intent level.

## Pattern 1: Time-Based Sequence

| Day | Audience | Message | Creative |
|-----|----------|---------|----------|
| 1-3 | Site visitors | Reminder + value recap | Product-focused |
| 4-7 | Engagers | Social proof / testimonial | Customer story |
| 8-14 | Non-converters | Objection handling | FAQ / comparison |
| 15-30 | Still interested | Incentive / offer | Discount or bonus |
| 30+ | Cool audience | Re-education / new angle | Fresh creative |

## Pattern 2: Action-Based Sequence

| Trigger | Message | CTA |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Page view only | "Want to learn more?" | Content piece |
| Product view | "Still interested in [product]?" | Product page |
| Add to cart | "You left something behind" | Checkout |
| Checkout abandon | "Complete your order + free shipping" | Checkout |
| Purchase | "You might also like..." | Cross-sell |

## Pattern 3: Content Sequence (Lead Gen)

| Stage | Content | Next Step |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Blog reader | Related deeper content | Lead magnet |
| Lead magnet | Case study or webinar | Offer |
| Webinar viewer | Testimonials + offer | Application |

## Frequency Management
- Cap at 3-4 impressions per week per person
- Rotate creative every 2 weeks
- Exclude converters immediately
- Monitor frequency vs performance correlation

## Key Rules
1. Always exclude previous stage converters
2. Message must match intent level
3. Creative fatigue hits faster in retargeting (smaller audience)
4. Dynamic product ads for ecom, sequential for lead gen
