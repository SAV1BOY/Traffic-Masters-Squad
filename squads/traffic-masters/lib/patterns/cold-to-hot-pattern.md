# Cold-to-Hot Audience Pattern

## Purpose
Framework for warming cold audiences through structured touchpoint sequences.

## The Pattern

### Stage 1: Cold (Unaware)
- **Goal:** Make them aware of the problem or brand
- **Content:** Educational, entertaining, value-first
- **Platforms:** Meta broad, YouTube in-stream, TikTok broad
- **Metric:** CPM, reach, video view rate
- **Budget:** 60-70% of total

### Stage 2: Warm (Problem-Aware)
- **Goal:** Build consideration and trust
- **Trigger:** Engaged with Stage 1 content (video view, click, engagement)
- **Content:** Testimonials, case studies, demos, comparisons
- **Metric:** CTR, engagement rate, time on site
- **Budget:** 15-20% of total

### Stage 3: Hot (Solution-Aware)
- **Goal:** Drive conversion
- **Trigger:** High-intent action (ATC, lead form start, pricing page)
- **Content:** Offers, urgency, risk reversal, final social proof
- **Metric:** CPA, ROAS, conversion rate
- **Budget:** 10-20% of total

## Audience Building Timeline
- Week 1-2: Build cold audiences, accumulate pixel data
- Week 3-4: Warm audiences reach minimum viable size
- Week 5+: Full funnel active with retargeting at scale

## Key Principles
1. Each stage earns the right to ask for more
2. Content matches intent level (don't hard-sell cold traffic)
3. Exclusion lists prevent stage overlap
4. Pixel events define stage transitions
5. Creative must feel different at each stage
