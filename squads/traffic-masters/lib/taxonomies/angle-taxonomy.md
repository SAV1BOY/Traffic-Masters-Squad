# Angle Taxonomy

## Purpose
Classification system for ad messaging angles.

## Primary Angles

### 1. Pain Angles
- **1.1 Financial Pain** — Cost, waste, lost revenue
- **1.2 Time Pain** — Slow, manual, inefficient
- **1.3 Status Pain** — Falling behind, embarrassment
- **1.4 Emotional Pain** — Frustration, anxiety, overwhelm
- **1.5 Opportunity Cost** — What they're missing out on

### 2. Desire Angles
- **2.1 Achievement** — Goals, milestones, success
- **2.2 Lifestyle** — Freedom, flexibility, enjoyment
- **2.3 Status** — Recognition, respect, leadership
- **2.4 Security** — Stability, predictability, safety
- **2.5 Belonging** — Community, connection, tribe

### 3. Proof Angles
- **3.1 Social Proof** — Numbers, reviews, popularity
- **3.2 Authority Proof** — Experts, media, certifications
- **3.3 Data Proof** — Statistics, research, case studies
- **3.4 Demonstration Proof** — Live demo, before/after
- **3.5 Testimonial Proof** — Customer stories, quotes

### 4. Mechanism Angles
- **4.1 Named Mechanism** — Proprietary process/technology
- **4.2 Scientific Mechanism** — Research-backed explanation
- **4.3 Process Mechanism** — Step-by-step methodology
- **4.4 Contrarian Mechanism** — Why common approach fails

### 5. Objection Angles
- **5.1 Price Objection** — ROI, value framing
- **5.2 Time Objection** — Quick results, easy setup
- **5.3 Trust Objection** — Guarantee, risk reversal
- **5.4 Relevance Objection** — "This is for you because..."
- **5.5 Comparison Objection** — Why this vs alternatives

## Usage
Tag every ad creative with primary and secondary angle from this taxonomy.
