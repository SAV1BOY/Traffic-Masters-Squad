# Hook Taxonomy

## Purpose
Classification system for ad hooks across all formats and platforms.

## Hook Categories

### 1. Question Hooks
- **1.1 Pain Question** — "Are you still struggling with...?"
- **1.2 Curiosity Question** — "Did you know that...?"
- **1.3 Challenge Question** — "Can you do this in 5 minutes?"
- **1.4 Rhetorical Question** — "What if I told you...?"

### 2. Statement Hooks
- **2.1 Bold Claim** — "We increased ROAS by 400%"
- **2.2 Contrarian** — "Everything you know about X is wrong"
- **2.3 Confession** — "I wasted $50K before learning this"
- **2.4 Prediction** — "By 2027, every brand will..."

### 3. Story Hooks
- **3.1 Origin** — "Two years ago, I was..."
- **3.2 Failure** — "I almost lost everything when..."
- **3.3 Discovery** — "I accidentally found..."
- **3.4 Transformation** — "From $0 to $1M in..."

### 4. Pattern Interrupt Hooks
- **4.1 Visual** — Unexpected image or action
- **4.2 Audio** — Surprising sound or silence
- **4.3 Behavioral** — "Stop scrolling" / "Wait"
- **4.4 Format** — Breaking expected ad format

### 5. Social Hooks
- **5.1 Celebrity/Authority** — "[Expert] says..."
- **5.2 Crowd** — "10,000 people just discovered..."
- **5.3 Peer** — "Other [role/title] are doing this"
- **5.4 Trending** — "Everyone's talking about..."

## Tagging Convention
Use format: `HOOK-[Category#].[Sub#]` (e.g., HOOK-2.1 for Bold Claim)
