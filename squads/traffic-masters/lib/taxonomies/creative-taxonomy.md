# Creative Taxonomy

## Purpose
Standard taxonomy for classifying creative assets by format, angle, hook type, visual style, and performance characteristics. Enables consistent creative analysis and reporting.

---

## Classification Hierarchy

```
Level 1: Format
  Level 2: Angle / Theme
    Level 3: Hook Type
      Level 4: Visual Style
        Level 5: Production Type
```

---

## Level 1: Creative Formats

| Format | Code | Description | Best Platforms | Typical Duration/Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Static Image** | `img` | Single image ad | Meta, Google Display, LinkedIn | 1080x1080, 1200x628, 1080x1920 |
| **Short Video** | `vid-15s` | Video under 15 seconds | TikTok, Meta Reels/Stories, YouTube Shorts | 9:16 or 1:1 |
| **Medium Video** | `vid-30s` | Video 15-30 seconds | Meta Feed, TikTok, YouTube | 1:1, 9:16, or 16:9 |
| **Long Video** | `vid-60s` | Video 30-60 seconds | YouTube, Meta Feed | 16:9 or 1:1 |
| **Extended Video** | `vid-long` | Video 60+ seconds | YouTube, Meta Feed | 16:9 |
| **Carousel** | `car` | Multi-card swipeable format | Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok | 3-10 cards, 1:1 per card |
| **Collection** | `coll` | Instant Experience + product grid | Meta | Full-screen mobile |
| **UGC Video** | `ugc` | User-generated or creator content | TikTok, Meta Reels | 9:16 |
| **Slideshow** | `slide` | Image sequence as video | Meta | 1:1 or 16:9 |
| **GIF / Animation** | `gif` | Animated graphic | Meta, Google Display | 1:1 or banner sizes |
| **Responsive Display** | `rsp` | Multi-asset responsive format | Google Display | Multiple sizes auto-generated |
| **Text Ad** | `txt` | Text-only search ad | Google Search, Bing | Headline + description |
| **Shopping Ad** | `shop` | Product listing ad | Google Shopping | Product image + price |
| **Lead Form** | `lead` | In-platform lead capture form | Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok | Form fields |
| **Playable** | `play` | Interactive mini-experience | Meta, TikTok | Interactive |

## Level 2: Creative Angles / Themes

| Angle | Code | Description | When to Use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pain Point** | `pain` | Highlights the problem the audience faces | Cold audiences, problem-aware prospects | "Tired of wasting money on ads that don't convert?" |
| **Benefit-Led** | `benefit` | Leads with positive outcomes and results | Warm audiences, solution-aware | "Get 3x more leads with half the effort" |
| **Social Proof** | `social-proof` | Uses testimonials, reviews, or user counts | All funnel stages | "Join 50,000+ marketers who trust us" |
| **Authority** | `authority` | Positions brand as the expert | Cold-warm, high-consideration | "The #1 rated platform by G2" |
| **Curiosity** | `curiosity` | Creates information gap to drive engagement | Cold audiences, content-first | "The secret most agencies won't tell you" |
| **Comparison** | `comparison` | Compares product to alternatives or competitors | Warm-hot, consideration stage | "Why 5,000 businesses switched from [X]" |
| **How-To / Educational** | `how-to` | Teaches something valuable while demonstrating product | Cold-warm, value-first | "3 steps to double your conversion rate" |
| **FOMO / Urgency** | `fomo` | Creates fear of missing out or time pressure | Hot audiences, retargeting | "Sale ends tonight -- 40% off everything" |
| **Transformation** | `transform` | Shows before/after or journey | All stages | "From struggling to scaling: our customer's story" |
| **Unboxing / Demo** | `demo` | Shows product in action | Warm-hot, consideration | Product demonstration or unboxing experience |
| **Behind the Scenes** | `bts` | Shows process, team, or making-of | Brand building, trust | "Here's how we make our product" |
| **Listicle** | `listicle` | Numbered list of benefits or tips | Cold-warm, educational | "5 reasons you need [product]" |
| **Contrarian** | `contrarian` | Challenges conventional wisdom | Cold audiences, pattern interrupt | "Stop doing [common practice] -- here's why" |
| **Aspirational** | `aspirational` | Paints a picture of the ideal future state | Brand, lifestyle | "Imagine a life where [desired outcome]" |
| **Problem-Solution** | `prob-sol` | States problem then presents solution | All stages | "The problem with X is... We fixed it." |

## Level 3: Hook Types

| Hook Type | Code | Description | Best For | Avg. CTR Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Question** | `question` | Opens with a question to the audience | Engagement, curiosity | Medium-High |
| **Bold Statement** | `bold-claim` | Strong declarative statement | Attention, authority | High |
| **Statistic** | `stat` | Leads with a compelling data point | Credibility, curiosity | High |
| **Testimonial** | `testimonial` | Opens with a customer quote | Trust, social proof | Medium |
| **Call-Out** | `call-out` | Directly addresses a specific audience | Relevance, targeting | High |
| **Problem Agitation** | `problem` | States a relatable problem | Empathy, connection | Medium-High |
| **Demonstration** | `demo` | Shows the product in action immediately | Product clarity | Medium |
| **Controversy** | `controversy` | Makes a provocative or unexpected claim | Pattern interrupt | High (variable) |
| **Story Opening** | `story` | Begins a narrative | Engagement, retention | Medium |
| **Visual Surprise** | `visual` | Unusual or striking visual element | Thumb-stop | High |
| **Before/After** | `before-after` | Immediate transformation visual | Impact, proof | Medium-High |
| **User Address** | `user-address` | "Hey [audience type]" direct address | Personal connection | Medium |

## Level 4: Visual Styles

| Style | Code | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Polished / Studio** | `polished` | Professional production quality | Brand campaigns, luxury products |
| **Native / Organic** | `native` | Looks like organic platform content | Social media, TikTok |
| **UGC / Creator** | `ugc-style` | Filmed by real users or creators | Trust, authenticity |
| **Editorial** | `editorial` | Magazine or article style | Content marketing, authority |
| **Lifestyle** | `lifestyle` | Product in real-life context | Aspirational, brand |
| **Product-Focused** | `product` | Clean product shots, features | E-commerce, consideration |
| **Text-Heavy** | `text-heavy` | Bold text dominates the visual | Offers, announcements |
| **Meme / Cultural** | `meme` | References internet culture | Younger demographics, TikTok |
| **Motion Graphics** | `motion` | Animated graphics and text | Explainers, SaaS |
| **Split Screen** | `split` | Side-by-side comparison layout | Comparisons, before/after |
| **Talking Head** | `talking-head` | Person speaking directly to camera | Trust, education, UGC |
| **Screenshot / Screen Record** | `screen` | Shows app or website interface | SaaS, digital products |

---

## Level 5: Production Types

| Type | Code | Description | Cost | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **In-House** | `inhouse` | Created by internal team | Low | Fast |
| **Agency** | `agency` | Created by creative agency | High | Medium |
| **Creator / UGC** | `creator` | Commissioned from creators | Medium | Fast |
| **AI-Generated** | `ai` | Generated or assisted by AI tools | Low | Very Fast |
| **Stock-Based** | `stock` | Built from stock assets | Low | Fast |
| **Customer-Sourced** | `customer` | Real customer content (reviews, photos) | Free-Low | Variable |

## Creative Naming Convention

```
[Format]_[Angle]_[HookType]_[CTA]_[CreativeID]_[Version]
```

**Examples:**
```
vid-30s_pain_question_shop-now_CR001_v1
img_social-proof_testimonial_learn-more_CR015_v2
ugc_benefit_demo_get-offer_CR022_vA
car_comparison_bold-claim_shop-now_CR030_v1
vid-15s_fomo_stat_buy-now_CR045_v3
```

## Performance Benchmarks by Format

| Format | Avg. CTR | Avg. CPA Index | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Static Image | 0.8-1.5% | 1.0x (baseline) | Quick production, testing angles |
| Short Video (< 15s) | 1.0-2.0% | 0.85x | TikTok, Reels, Stories |
| Medium Video (15-30s) | 0.8-1.5% | 0.90x | Meta Feed, general prospecting |
| Long Video (30-60s) | 0.5-1.0% | 0.95x | YouTube, education, high-consideration |
| Carousel | 0.8-1.5% | 0.90x | Product showcases, storytelling |
| UGC | 1.2-2.5% | 0.80x | Social platforms, trust-building |

*CPA Index: < 1.0 = more efficient than baseline. > 1.0 = less efficient.*

## Creative Tagging for Reporting

When uploading or logging creatives, apply these tags for aggregate analysis:

```
Tags:
  format: {{FORMAT_CODE}}
  angle: {{ANGLE_CODE}}
  hook: {{HOOK_CODE}}
  style: {{STYLE_CODE}}
  production: {{PRODUCTION_CODE}}
  platform: {{PLATFORM}}
  funnel_stage: {{TOFU/MOFU/BOFU}}
  audience_temp: {{COLD/WARM/HOT}}
  launch_date: {{YYYY-MM-DD}}
  status: {{ACTIVE/PAUSED/RETIRED}}
```

This enables analysis like: "What is the average CPA for UGC videos with pain-point angles and question hooks targeting cold audiences?"
