# Paid Traffic Glossary

## Purpose
Complete glossary of paid traffic, digital advertising, and performance marketing terminology. Contains 150+ terms organized alphabetically.

---

## A

**A/B Test** -- An experiment comparing two variants (A and B) to determine which performs better on a specified metric. Only one variable should differ between variants.

**ABO (Ad Set Budget Optimization)** -- Budget allocation method where each ad set has its own daily or lifetime budget, giving the advertiser direct control over spend distribution.

**Above the Fold** -- The portion of a webpage visible without scrolling. Critical for landing page optimization as it determines first impressions.

**Ad Account** -- The container within an advertising platform that holds campaigns, billing information, and reporting data.

**Ad Copy** -- The text content of an advertisement, including headlines, body text, descriptions, and calls to action.

**Ad Creative** -- The visual and textual elements of an ad, including images, videos, copy, and design.

**Ad Fatigue** -- Decline in ad performance over time due to repeated exposure to the same audience. Indicated by falling CTR and rising CPA.

**Ad Rank** -- Google Ads metric determining ad position, calculated from bid amount, Quality Score, and expected impact of extensions.

**Ad Relevance** -- A platform's assessment of how well an ad matches the intent and interests of the target audience.

**Ad Set** -- A group of ads within a campaign (Meta terminology) that shares the same targeting, budget, schedule, and placement settings. Equivalent to "Ad Group" in Google Ads.

**AOV (Average Order Value)** -- Total revenue divided by total number of orders. Indicates the average amount spent per transaction.

**API (Application Programming Interface)** -- A set of protocols for building software that interfaces with advertising platforms to automate data retrieval and management.

**ASC (Advantage+ Shopping Campaign)** -- Meta's automated campaign type for e-commerce that uses machine learning to optimize across audiences and placements.

**Attribution** -- The process of assigning credit for conversions to specific marketing touchpoints along the customer journey.

**Attribution Window** -- The time period after a click or view during which a conversion can be credited to an ad. Common windows: 1-day view, 7-day click, 28-day click.

**Audience Network** -- Advertising placements on third-party apps and websites within a platform's partner network (e.g., Meta Audience Network).

---

## B

**Bid** -- The maximum amount an advertiser is willing to pay for a specific action (click, impression, conversion).

**Bid Strategy** -- The approach used to set bids in auctions. Types include manual CPC, target CPA, target ROAS, maximize conversions, and maximize clicks.

**BOFU (Bottom of Funnel)** -- The final stage of the marketing funnel where prospects are ready to make a purchase decision.

**Bounce Rate** -- The percentage of visitors who leave a website after viewing only one page without taking any action.

**Brand Lift** -- A measurement of the increase in brand awareness, ad recall, or purchase intent caused by an advertising campaign.

**Broad Match** -- A Google Ads keyword match type that shows ads for searches related to the keyword, including synonyms and related concepts.

**Budget Cap** -- A maximum spending limit set at the campaign or account level to control total ad spend.

---

## C

**CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)** -- The total cost of acquiring a new customer, including all marketing and sales expenses divided by new customers acquired.

**CAPI (Conversions API)** -- Server-side tracking solution (Meta) that sends conversion events directly from the server to the ad platform, bypassing browser limitations.

**CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization)** -- Budget allocation method where the budget is set at the campaign level and the platform distributes it across ad sets based on performance.

**Click-Through** -- The action of a user clicking on an ad to visit the advertiser's destination (website, app, etc.).

**Contextual Targeting** -- Placing ads on web pages whose content is relevant to the ad's product or message, rather than targeting based on user data.

**Conversion** -- A desired action completed by a user after interacting with an ad. Examples: purchase, lead form submission, app install, phone call.

**Conversion Rate (CVR)** -- The percentage of users who complete a desired action after clicking an ad. Calculated as conversions divided by clicks times 100.

**CPA (Cost Per Acquisition/Action)** -- The average cost to acquire one conversion. Calculated as total spend divided by total conversions.

**CPC (Cost Per Click)** -- The average cost each time a user clicks on an ad. Calculated as total spend divided by total clicks.

**CPL (Cost Per Lead)** -- The average cost to generate one lead. A specific form of CPA where the conversion event is a lead.

**CPM (Cost Per Mille)** -- The cost per 1,000 impressions. Calculated as (spend / impressions) x 1,000.

**CPV (Cost Per View)** -- The cost each time a user views a video ad (typically counted as a view of a specified duration or percentage).

**Creative Fatigue** -- The phenomenon where ad performance declines as the target audience has seen the creative too many times.

**CRM (Customer Relationship Management)** -- A system for managing customer data and interactions. In advertising, CRM data is used for custom audience creation and LTV analysis.

**Cross-Sell** -- Marketing additional or complementary products to existing customers.

**CTA (Call to Action)** -- A prompt that tells the user what to do next (e.g., "Shop Now," "Learn More," "Sign Up").

**CTR (Click-Through Rate)** -- The percentage of impressions that result in clicks. Calculated as clicks divided by impressions times 100.

**Custom Audience** -- An audience built from first-party data (customer lists, website visitors, app users, or engagement data) for targeted advertising.

---

## D

**Data-Driven Attribution (DDA)** -- An attribution model that uses machine learning to distribute conversion credit across touchpoints based on their actual contribution.

**Dayparting** -- Scheduling ads to run during specific hours of the day or days of the week to optimize performance.

**DCO (Dynamic Creative Optimization)** -- An automated system that assembles ad variations from a library of creative components (images, headlines, CTAs) and tests combinations.

**Deep Link** -- A link that takes users directly to a specific page within an app, rather than the app's home screen.

**DMA (Designated Market Area)** -- A geographic region in the US used for targeting and media planning.

**DSP (Demand-Side Platform)** -- A platform that allows advertisers to buy digital ad inventory programmatically across multiple ad exchanges.

**Dynamic Ads** -- Ads that automatically show relevant products to users based on their browsing behavior (e.g., Meta Dynamic Product Ads, Google Dynamic Remarketing).

---

## E

**Engagement Rate** -- The percentage of people who interacted with an ad (likes, comments, shares, saves, clicks) relative to those who saw it.

**Enhanced Conversions** -- A Google Ads feature that supplements existing conversion tags by sending hashed first-party customer data to improve measurement accuracy.

**Event** -- A user action tracked by a pixel or SDK, such as PageView, AddToCart, Purchase, Lead, or CompleteRegistration.

**Exact Match** -- A Google Ads keyword match type that shows ads only for searches that have the same meaning or intent as the keyword.

---

## F

**First-Click Attribution** -- An attribution model that gives 100% of conversion credit to the first touchpoint in the customer journey.

**Frequency** -- The average number of times each user has seen an ad. Calculated as impressions divided by reach.

**Frequency Cap** -- A limit on how many times a single user can see an ad within a given time period.

**Full-Funnel Strategy** -- An advertising approach that addresses all stages of the customer journey, from awareness through conversion and retention.

**Funnel** -- A model representing the customer journey from initial awareness to final conversion, with each stage narrowing the pool of prospects.

---

## G

**GA4 (Google Analytics 4)** -- Google's analytics platform for measuring website and app performance, user behavior, and conversions.

**Geo-Targeting** -- Targeting ads to users in specific geographic locations (countries, regions, cities, or radius around a point).

**GTM (Google Tag Manager)** -- A tag management system that allows managing and deploying marketing tags on a website without modifying code.

---

## H

**Halo Effect** -- The indirect positive impact of advertising on one channel on another channel's performance (e.g., social ads increasing branded search volume).

**Hook** -- The opening element of an ad (first line of copy, first 3 seconds of video) designed to capture attention and stop the scroll.

**Hook Rate** -- The percentage of video viewers who watch past the first 25% of the video, indicating the hook's effectiveness.

---

## I

**Impression** -- A single instance of an ad being displayed on a user's screen.

**Impression Share** -- The percentage of eligible impressions that an ad actually received (Google Ads metric).

**Incrementality** -- The measurement of the true additional value generated by advertising that would not have occurred without it.

**In-Market Audience** -- An audience of users who are actively researching or considering purchasing products/services in a specific category (Google Ads feature).

**Interest Targeting** -- Targeting users based on their declared or inferred interests and behaviors on a platform.

---

## K

**KPI (Key Performance Indicator)** -- A measurable value that demonstrates how effectively a campaign is achieving its objectives.

**Keyword** -- A word or phrase that triggers an ad to appear in search results (Google Ads, Bing Ads).

---

## L

**Landing Page** -- The web page a user arrives at after clicking an ad. Optimized to encourage a specific conversion action.

**Last-Click Attribution** -- An attribution model that gives 100% of conversion credit to the last touchpoint before conversion.

**Learning Phase** -- A period after campaign launch or significant edit where the platform's algorithm collects data and optimizes delivery. Typically requires 50 conversions.

**Lifetime Budget** -- A total budget for the entire duration of a campaign or ad set, as opposed to a daily budget.

**Linear Attribution** -- An attribution model that distributes conversion credit equally across all touchpoints.

**Lookalike Audience (LAL)** -- An audience built by a platform's algorithm to find users who are similar to a source audience (e.g., existing customers).

**LP (Landing Page)** -- See Landing Page.

**LTV (Lifetime Value)** -- The total revenue a business expects from a customer over their entire relationship.

---

## M

**Manual Bidding** -- Setting bid amounts manually for each keyword, ad set, or placement rather than using automated bid strategies.

**Marginal CPA** -- The cost per acquisition for the next incremental conversion, used to assess whether scaling spend is still efficient.

**Match Type** -- The setting in Google Ads that determines how broadly or narrowly a keyword matches to search queries (Broad, Phrase, Exact).

**MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio)** -- Total revenue divided by total marketing spend (across all channels). A blended measure of marketing efficiency.

**MOFU (Middle of Funnel)** -- The consideration stage of the marketing funnel where prospects are evaluating options.

**MTA (Multi-Touch Attribution)** -- Attribution models that distribute conversion credit across multiple touchpoints in the customer journey.

---

## N

**Native Advertising** -- Ads that match the form and function of the platform they appear on, appearing as organic content.

**Negative Keyword** -- A keyword that prevents an ad from showing for specific search queries (Google Ads).

---

## O

**Objective** -- The primary goal of an advertising campaign, such as awareness, traffic, engagement, leads, or conversions.

**Optimization Event** -- The specific conversion event that a platform's algorithm optimizes toward when delivering ads.

**Organic** -- Non-paid traffic or content that reaches users through search engine results, social media feeds, or other unpaid channels.

---

## P

**Phrase Match** -- A Google Ads keyword match type that shows ads for searches that include the meaning of the keyword.

**Pixel** -- A small piece of code placed on a website that tracks user actions and sends data back to the advertising platform for measurement and optimization.

**Placement** -- The specific location where an ad appears (e.g., Facebook Feed, Instagram Stories, Google Search, YouTube Pre-Roll).

**PMax (Performance Max)** -- A Google Ads campaign type that uses machine learning to serve ads across all Google properties (Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Discover, Maps).

**PPC (Pay Per Click)** -- An advertising model where advertisers pay each time a user clicks on their ad.

**Programmatic** -- Automated buying and selling of digital advertising using algorithms and real-time bidding.

**Prospecting** -- Targeting new potential customers who have not previously interacted with the brand. See also: TOFU.

---

## Q

**Quality Score** -- A Google Ads metric (1-10) that rates the quality and relevance of keywords, ads, and landing pages. Higher scores lead to lower costs and better positions.

---

## R

**Reach** -- The total number of unique users who saw an ad at least once during a given period.

**Retargeting (Remarketing)** -- Showing ads to users who have previously visited a website, used an app, or engaged with content.

**ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)** -- Revenue generated divided by ad spend. Expressed as a multiple (e.g., 5.0x).

**ROI (Return on Investment)** -- Net profit from an investment divided by the cost of the investment, expressed as a percentage.

**RLSA (Remarketing Lists for Search Ads)** -- A Google Ads feature that allows targeting or adjusting bids for past website visitors when they search on Google.

---

## S

**SaaS (Software as a Service)** -- A software distribution model relevant in advertising for subscription-based products with LTV-focused acquisition strategies.

**Search Term** -- The actual query a user types into a search engine, which may differ from the keyword an advertiser is bidding on.

**Seed Audience** -- The source audience used to create a lookalike audience. Typically composed of customers, leads, or high-value users.

**Segmentation** -- Dividing a target audience into smaller groups based on shared characteristics for more targeted advertising.

**SERP (Search Engine Results Page)** -- The page displayed by a search engine in response to a user's query, containing both organic and paid results.

**Smart Bidding** -- Google's automated bid strategies that use machine learning to optimize for conversions or conversion value.

**Social Proof** -- Evidence that others have used and approved of a product (reviews, testimonials, user counts), used in ad creative to build trust.

**Split Test** -- See A/B Test.

**SSP (Supply-Side Platform)** -- A platform used by publishers to sell their ad inventory programmatically.

---

## T

**Target CPA** -- An automated bid strategy where the platform aims to achieve a specified average cost per acquisition.

**Target ROAS** -- An automated bid strategy where the platform aims to achieve a specified return on ad spend.

**Thumb-Stop Rate** -- The percentage of impressions that result in a 3-second video view on mobile, indicating the ad's ability to stop scrolling.

**TOFU (Top of Funnel)** -- The awareness stage of the marketing funnel where the goal is to reach new audiences who may not know the brand.

**Tracking Pixel** -- See Pixel.

---

## U

**UGC (User-Generated Content)** -- Content created by customers or creators (not the brand) used in advertising. Often outperforms polished brand content due to authenticity.

**Upsell** -- Marketing higher-value products or upgrades to existing customers.

**UTM Parameters** -- Tags added to URLs (source, medium, campaign, content, term) to track the effectiveness of marketing campaigns in analytics platforms.

---

## V

**View-Through Conversion** -- A conversion that occurs after a user saw (but did not click) an ad. Credited within the view-through attribution window (typically 1 day).

**VTR (View-Through Rate)** -- The percentage of users who saw a video ad and completed a specified view threshold (e.g., 25%, 50%, 100%).

---

## W

**Warm Audience** -- Users who have had prior interaction with a brand (website visitors, email subscribers, social engagers) but have not yet converted.

**Winning Creative** -- An ad creative that outperforms other variants in a test, achieving better performance on the primary success metric.

---

## Numbers and Symbols

**1% Lookalike** -- A lookalike audience matching the top 1% of users most similar to the seed audience. Most precise but smallest reach.

**3-Second View** -- A video view counted when a user watches at least 3 seconds of a video ad. Used to calculate Thumb-Stop Rate.

**7/1 Attribution** -- Short for 7-day click, 1-day view attribution window. The default attribution window for Meta Ads.

**70-20-10 Rule** -- Budget allocation framework: 70% on proven tactics, 20% on iterative improvements, 10% on experimental approaches.
