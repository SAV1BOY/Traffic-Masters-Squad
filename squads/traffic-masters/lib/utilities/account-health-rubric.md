# Account Health Rubric

## Purpose
Standardized scoring for ad account health across platforms.

## Health Dimensions

### 1. Policy Compliance (Critical)
- No active policy violations
- No restricted ad categories without approval
- Clean account history (no bans or suspensions)
- Score: Green (clean) / Yellow (warning) / Red (violation)

### 2. Pixel & Tracking Health
- All pixels firing correctly
- Server-side tracking active (CAPI)
- Conversion events verified
- Event match quality score > 6/10 (Meta)
- Score: 1-5

### 3. Campaign Structure
- Logical organization by objective/funnel stage
- No audience overlap > 30%
- Proper exclusion lists in place
- Naming conventions followed
- Score: 1-5

### 4. Creative Health
- Active creative count per ad set (3-5 minimum)
- Creative refresh rate (new creative every 2 weeks)
- No creatives with frequency > 4.0
- Winning creative has active iterations
- Score: 1-5

### 5. Budget Efficiency
- Pacing within 90-110% of target
- No campaigns stuck in learning phase > 14 days
- Budget allocation follows 70-20-10 rule
- No wasted spend on non-converting segments
- Score: 1-5
