# Platform Comparison Matrix

## Purpose
Quick reference for comparing advertising platforms on key dimensions.

## Platform Overview

| Dimension | Meta | Google Search | YouTube | TikTok | LinkedIn |
|-----------|------|--------------|---------|--------|----------|
| Audience Size | 3B+ | Intent-based | 2B+ | 1.5B+ | 900M+ |
| Targeting | Broad+Interest | Keyword intent | Interest+Custom | Interest+Behavior | Job title+Company |
| Best For | B2C, Ecom, Lead Gen | Intent capture | Awareness, DR video | Young demo, UGC | B2B, High-ticket |
| Avg CPM | $8-15 | N/A (CPC) | $6-12 | $5-10 | $30-80 |
| Avg CPC | $0.50-2.00 | $1-10+ | $0.10-0.50 | $0.30-1.00 | $5-15 |
| Avg CTR | 1-2% | 3-8% | 0.5-1% | 0.8-2% | 0.4-0.8% |
| Min Budget | $5/day | $1/day | $5/day | $20/day | $10/day |
| Learning Phase | 50 events/week | 30 conv/month | Varies | 50 events/week | Varies |
| Creative Format | Image/Video/Carousel | Text/Shopping | Video | Video | Image/Video/Doc |

## Platform Selection Guide

| Scenario | Primary Platform | Secondary |
|----------|-----------------|-----------|
| B2C Ecommerce | Meta | Google Shopping |
| B2B SaaS | Google Search | LinkedIn |
| Mobile App | Meta + TikTok | Google UAC |
| Local Business | Google Search | Meta |
| Course/Coaching | Meta + YouTube | TikTok |
| DTC Brand | Meta | TikTok + Google |

## When to Add a Platform
1. Primary platform hitting diminishing returns
2. Target audience concentrated on new platform
3. Budget supports minimum viable spend on new platform
4. Creative can be adapted (not just repurposed)
