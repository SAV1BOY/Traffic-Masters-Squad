# Policy-Safe Language Guide

## Purpose
Alternative phrasings that comply with ad platform policies.

## Health & Wellness

| Risky Claim | Safe Alternative |
|-------------|-----------------|
| "Cures [condition]" | "Supports [wellness goal]" |
| "Lose 30 lbs in 30 days" | "Supports healthy weight management" |
| "Clinically proven" | "Backed by research" (with citation) |
| "Doctors recommend" | "Formulated with expert guidance" |
| "Eliminates pain" | "Supports comfort and mobility" |

## Income & Financial

| Risky Claim | Safe Alternative |
|-------------|-----------------|
| "Make $10K/month guaranteed" | "Our students have achieved [result]" |
| "Get rich quick" | "Build a sustainable income stream" |
| "Financial freedom" | "Work toward your financial goals" |
| "Passive income" | "Build income streams" |
| "Replace your salary" | "Create additional revenue" |

## Results & Guarantees

| Risky Claim | Safe Alternative |
|-------------|-----------------|
| "Guaranteed results" | "Results may vary. See testimonials." |
| "100% success rate" | "Proven framework used by X people" |
| "Never fail" | "Designed for consistent performance" |
| "Works for everyone" | "Designed for [specific audience]" |

## Before/After Claims
- Always include "Results not typical" disclaimer
- Show realistic timeframes
- Avoid implying guaranteed transformation
- Use "individual results may vary"

## Platform-Specific Notes
- **Meta:** Strict on personal attributes ("Are you...?"), health, finance
- **Google:** Strict on superlatives, misleading claims, restricted content
- **TikTok:** Strict on body image, financial claims, health products
- **LinkedIn:** Professional standards, B2B appropriate language
