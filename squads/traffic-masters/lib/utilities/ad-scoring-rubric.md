# Ad Scoring Rubric

## Purpose
Standardized scoring system for evaluating ad creative before launch.

## Scoring Dimensions (1-5 each)

### 1. Hook Strength
- 1: Weak, generic, easy to ignore
- 3: Decent attention-grabber, relevant
- 5: Exceptional pattern interrupt, impossible to scroll past

### 2. Message Clarity
- 1: Confusing, unclear value proposition
- 3: Clear enough, could be stronger
- 5: Crystal clear, instantly understood

### 3. Audience Relevance
- 1: Too broad, not targeted
- 3: Relevant to target, not deeply personal
- 5: Feels like it was made specifically for the reader

### 4. Emotional Impact
- 1: No emotional response
- 3: Mild interest or curiosity
- 5: Strong emotional trigger (desire, fear, excitement)

### 5. CTA Effectiveness
- 1: No clear CTA or irrelevant CTA
- 3: Clear CTA, standard approach
- 5: Compelling, low-friction, irresistible CTA

### 6. Visual Quality
- 1: Poor quality, off-brand
- 3: Professional, acceptable
- 5: Outstanding, platform-native, scroll-stopping

### 7. Compliance Safety
- 1: High risk of rejection
- 3: Low risk with minor concerns
- 5: Fully compliant, no risk

## Launch Threshold
- Score 3.5+: Approved for launch
- Score 2.5-3.4: Revise and re-score
- Score <2.5: Reject and rebuild
